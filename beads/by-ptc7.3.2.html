Description

Why

A generated scenario asserts almost nothing itself. Its content is the final snapshot the dump appends (rust/crates/beryl_scenario/src/run/snapshot.rs, json.rs with_snapshot), which the oracles compare and which the mutation check compares between the card and a mutant. The snapshot has life, zone sizes, public cards by name, permanents, tapped state, power and toughness of a unique creature, counters and face-down counts. A mutant that changes only a color, a type, a keyword, damage marked or what is on the stack is not seen. Plan: Designs E3, "A wider snapshot".

What to build

  • Add to the snapshot, for each permanent: its colors, card types and subtypes, its keywords, damage marked, what it is attached to, and power and toughness for every creature (named by card and by battlefield order where names repeat). Add each player's mana pool, and the stack's contents where the scenario ends with a non-empty stack.
  • The snapshot may now be taken at a position that is not quiet, where the scenario ends there.
  • The exporters read the same fields: tools/src/beryl_tools/xmage.py and xmage_java.py (the assert* family of CardTestPlayerBase: assertColor, assertType, assertSubtype, assertAbility, assertDamageReceived), forge.py and tools/oracles/BerylForgeHarness.java. A field an oracle cannot assert is left out for that oracle with a reason in the code, never guessed.
  • New divergences are settled by the rules: a real oracle fault goes through just card diverge, an exporter gap into data/oracle-exporter-gaps.json.

Run the full oracle sweep (just oracle with no cards) before and after and report the change in passes, failures and unsupported scenarios.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria
