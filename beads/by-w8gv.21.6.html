Close reason

End Turn's pass-on stops for an opponent's spell or ability; the session takes a lone opposing spell as target; two or more spells open unpicked (staging 15d3793c)

Description

by-w8gv.21.1 settled two rules in docs/ui-conventions.md (data/ui-conventions.json, Settled as Arena) that today's code contradicts, both outside the stage's motion work. Human review of the rulings is by-hhdr (non-blocking): if he overrules one there first, follow him instead.

  1. Passing to the end step (by-sqo). Arena's "End Turn" passes until the end step but stops when the opponent puts a spell or ability on the stack: the player gets priority with "Resolve", then "Pass" after it resolves (SswBO8aOOgc 21:00, WQiYBv23buw 37:52, jD7VqaT8VC8 45:29). by-sqo's fix made the pass-on continue through opponent stack objects. Code: rust/crates/beryl_session/src/game.rs (pass_on, pass_on_target, passes_on around lines 1512-1545, 2020, 2098), protocol.rs pass_on / pass_to; tests in rust/crates/beryl_session/tests/stop_places.rs. The pass-on should end when an opposing object is put on the stack (the player's own objects and triggers keep it going).
  2. Targeting a spell (by-2lwc, by-69ps). With exactly one spell on the stack, a spell that targets a spell takes it with no prompt and no Confirm. With more, the choice opens with nothing preselected (vPdoSA5H5xU 21:36.5, qF4GF-vfc8Y 23:58.5, jD7VqaT8VC8 17:54.5; SN9QFe2kM4Y 26:54.0). Commit 940cf1eb added the preselect-and-Confirm: ui/src/game/Board.tsx, ui/src/components/Stack.tsx, route.ts, ui/src/screens/Game.tsx, its board in rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/boards.rs and checks in ui/scripts/interactions.mjs. Prefer taking the lone target in the session (auto-choosing a single legal target) over UI logic; Board.tsx is the presentation track's and is rebuilt at the cut-over, so keep UI changes minimal.

Acceptance criteria

End Turn's pass-on stops with priority when the opponent puts a spell or ability on the stack, covered by a session test; a spell-targeting spell with one spell on the stack takes it without a Confirm, and with two or more nothing is preselected, covered by a test or fixture; just check passes.
