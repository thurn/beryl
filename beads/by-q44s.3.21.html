Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Objects and placement' ('Marks are separate quads') and 'The frame' (bands 2 and 3); the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/table/scene.rs (Table::write), src/frame/draw.rs (sprites::MOON, WEDGE, CRESCENT), a new file src/table/own.rs. Web: ui/src/components/Card.tsx (the header comment: 'summoning sickness as a large moon over the text box, leaving the art clear', 'a token's coin badge', 'a stolen permanent's violet edge'; CombatRole), ui/src/stage/light.ts (Fighter: a creature in combat, 'the wedge an attacker wears and the crescent under a blocker', since when it has fought and since when it is blocked; SWELL; lightOf), ui/src/stage/layout.ts (chevron: how far past its frame the tip of an attacker's chevron is). Protocol: ObjectView::summoning_sick, combat, token, controller and owner in rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/protocol/view.rs.

These say what the card's record says, so they need no span and no step: they are drawn from the record the table holds, and change as the step that carries a new record begins.

Build, in src/table/own.rs, called from Table::write for each permanent:

  • Summoning sickness: sprites::MOON over the lower half of the card.
  • A token: sprites::COIN at the place the web's coin badge has.
  • A permanent its owner does not control: the sprites::OUTLINE quad in violet.
  • An attacker: sprites::WEDGE past the card's far edge, pointing at what it attacks, its tip chevron card widths past the frame. A blocker: sprites::CRESCENT under the card's near edge. Each swells in as SWELL has it from the frame its record first says so; the table keeps that time for each slot in an array sized at startup.
  • Colors come from the web's CSS for each (ui/src/design/components) into src/tokens.rs; sizes and offsets go under own in data/beryl3d/space.json, as shares of a card.

Do not build: the combat lines (by-q44s.3.19) or a prompt's marks (by-q44s.3.20).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/own.rs: a summoning-sick creature has one more record than the same creature without it, inside the card's corners; an attacker's wedge lies past the edge of the card nearer the opponent from the viewer's seat and nearer the viewer from the opponent's; a blocker's crescent is in the table's band; the wedge's opacity rises over SWELL's time and stays; a record that stops attacking has no wedge in the next frame; a frame with 255 attackers allocates nothing.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d png <a recording with combat> --at <attackers declared>; just b3d gate; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

The tests pass; a comment names a recording with declared attackers and the path of a just b3d png of it; just b3d gate passes, the commit body giving old and new counts if a limit rose.
