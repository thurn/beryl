Description

Milestone 4 of Project 50k (plan K4, epic by-w8gv.7). The Effects store built by by-w8gv.7.2 affects objects. This bead adds a player and the game as affected sides and the rule-change payload, and moves the two kinds of rule change the engine has onto it: the one a resolved ability makes ("until your next turn, you may cast sorcery spells as though they had flash") and the ones static abilities make (Rule).

Read first

  • docs/project-50k.md: decisions K4, K6, K11, and "State" and "Kernel rules" under Target architecture.
  • rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs: the kernel's contract. Its "Stores" section (written by by-w8gv.7.2) is the design this bead follows: kinds, shapes, capacities and byte allotments, expiry, indexing, capability bits, and what a store implements once. The same numbers are in a comment on by-w8gv.7.2, and enact brief shows that bead's handoff. Where the code proves the contract wrong, change its text in the same push and say so on the bead.
  • rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/legacy.rs: the table of what M4 deletes. Delete each Legacy method this bead replaces; the struct itself goes in by-w8gv.7.10.
  • docs/rules-coverage.md §10 (player-level rule changes) and Principles 1 and 2.

Need not read: the Memos, Trackers and counters code (by-w8gv.7.6 to .9), beryl_session beyond what a grep for the names below finds, ui/.

Scope

  1. A player and the game as affected sides, and the rule-change payload, as the contract shapes them. Add a side only with its first user.
  2. Player::sorceries_as_flash goes. It is a rule change affecting one player that lasts until their next turn. Where it lives at staging 08d1a377:
    • kernel: the field and getter in player.rs, new.rs, relayout.rs (impl Relayout for Player), off_board in board.rs, Legacy::set_sorceries_as_flash;
    • engine: EffectCtx::allow_sorceries_as_flash in effect.rs (called by beryl_cards/src/azorius/teferi_time_raveler.rs), the clear in turn::begin_turn, and the reads in cast::instant_speed and mana.rs (s.player(player).sorceries_as_flash()). It becomes one entry with duration UntilYourNextTurn(seat), ended by the same expiry call that ends every other effect of that duration (turn.rs already makes it): no clear of its own.
  3. Static rule changes resolve through the store. Rule (beryl_card/src/continuous.rs) is read through a u8 mask: rule_bit, FaceFacts::rules, GameTable::rules and may_have_rule in beryl_kernel/src/table.rs, Copiable::has_rule in query.rs, and cast::has_rule, which cast::sorcery_speed_limiter calls for every permanent on the battlefield and the loyalty timing check calls beside ENTERED_THIS_TURN. After this bead:
    • one query answers "does this rule change apply to this player (or this permanent) now", whether a static ability or a resolved effect makes it, as the contract's item 6 says;
    • the list of rule kinds has no cap of 8 and grows by a list entry, not a wider mask written by hand;
    • when no card of the game can make a rule change of a kind, the check is one AND (a GameTable capability bit); when one can, the answer is kept current with the layers (State::rederive, beryl_engine/src/layers.rs) rather than found by walking the battlefield, if the count allows it. Record what you measured either way.
    • "can't" still beats "can" (CR 101.2, cited at sorcery_speed_limiter).
    • cast::has_rule reports the ability to the coverage feature (crate::coverage::hit): keep that hit.
  4. data/card-names-allowlist.json, "mechanics": remove Player::sorceries_as_flash. Rule::OpponentsCastAsSorceries and Rule::InstantLoyaltyTheTurnItEntered leave the list only if this bead makes them something more than one card's sentence (not required: the card port is milestone 5).

Gotchas

  • by-w8gv.7.8 moves ENTERED_THIS_TURN, the other half of the loyalty timing check in cast.rs. Whichever lands second rebases a three-line conflict.
  • The static's rule change follows its permanent's controller after continuous effects (Derived::controller), and a face-down permanent has no abilities (CR 708.2): Copiable::has_rule answers for the face that is up.
  • Teferi's two rule changes meet in one game: his static limits the opponent, his +1 frees his controller. The scenarios in teferi_time_raveler.rs and the_wandering_emperor.rs cover both: cargo test -p beryl_cards teferi and wandering, and just oracle "Teferi, Time Raveler" "The Wandering Emperor".

Measurements and decisions (by-w8gv.7.3), base staging f4156d44.

COUNT (just count-gate, rollout_counts rollouts subject, same VM, back to back):

  • before: 402,475,964
  • after: 398,897,988 (-0.89%). The gate reported a drop past its tolerance against the bar of 405,276,238 and asked for a lower bar, so count-gate.toml is lowered to 398,897,988 (a lowering, not a bump).
  • By profile: the old read walked the battlefield for a sorcery-speed limit at each priority decision, about 7.05M instructions (cast::has_rule 3.74M, the Situation's OnceCell 3.31M). What replaces it: layers::player_rules 1.33M plus 0.37M of coverage hits, run only in a recompute with such a permanent on the battlefield; the reads themselves (State::rule_applies inlined into cast_options and decision) are under 0.4M.
  • First attempt, rejected: 410,363,089 (+1.96%). Two causes. (1) The benchmark is built with beryl_engine's coverage feature (dev-dependencies of beryl_cards and beryl_search turn it on), so the read-time coverage hit, which found the ability again on the battlefield at every read, ran on the counted path: 4.8M. (2) A table load per permanent in the layers' main loop to find rule-changing permanents, behind Capability::RuleChanges: about 5.2M with the callee not inlined.

SIZE: size_of::<State<Small>>() is 20,040, unchanged (allotment +16, used 0). Player went 20 -> 18 bytes without the flag; the four bytes of rule sets (two sets a player, one byte each while the list has at most eight rules) took what that gave back.

DECISIONS, with the contract's text changed to match (beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs, "Stores"):

  1. Two sets of rules per player, not one. State::static_rules is what the layers found for static abilities; Effects keeps its own index of its entries on players. State::rule_applies ORs them. With one set, an effect ending would have to dirty the layers to rebuild it; with two, each owner checks its own (the store's invariant; reference.rs compares the statics' against a fresh recompute).
  2. No Capability::RuleChanges: removed. A card whose static ability changes a rule for players has a bit in its listener mask (event::kind::RULE_CHANGE, bit 15, no event has it), which the layers already fold from every permanent for the listeners. So finding rule changers costs the layers no read of its own, and a game with such a card pays nothing while none is on the battlefield. The acceptance's "one capability-bit AND" is the AND of the player's rule sets at the read, and one AND of the folded mask in the layers.
  3. The game as an affected side is not built: no card changes a rule for every player, and the bead says to add a side only with its first user. The contract says it joins with its first user.
  4. Coverage: a player's rule (Teferi's static) is now reported as the layers put it in force, as a layer-changing static is, not at each read. Reading-time reporting needs the ability found again by walking the battlefield, which is what cost 4.8M. A permanent's rule about itself (The Wandering Emperor's) is still reported at the read, which needs no walk. This weakens what "exercised" means for Teferi's static: a scenario with him on the battlefield under an opponent now counts, whether or not a spell was refused.
  5. Rule is generated by a macro_rules! list with its words (as Purpose is); Rules is [u8; ceil(count / 8)], so a ninth rule widens every set (beryl_card test a_set_of_rules_has_room_for_every_rule_of_the_list). Rule::whose says whether a rule is for players (You / AnOpponent of what makes it) or about the permanent.
  6. Saves: a checkpoint's lingering words are compared when a save replays, and two frozen saves hold "player N casting sorceries as though they had flash until their next turn". The store says a player's rule change in those words and in the old place in State::off_board's order (after the permissions): State::rule_change_lingering. No migration, no breaks entry.
  7. corpus_never_changes is re-pinned (6,486,009,062,369,685,475). A state's hash changed for three reasons, none of them the games: Player no longer hashes the flag, the entry takes a timestamp as every effect does (CR 613.7), and the hashed listener mask carries the new bit. rollouts_never_change holds unchanged (12,769 decisions, same answers and ends from every corpus position).
  8. Rule::SorceriesAsFlash replaces Player::sorceries_as_flash on the card-names mechanics list: it is as much one card's sentence as its two siblings there.
  9. EffectCtx::add_effect takes impl Into<Affects<L>> (an ObjectRef or Affects::Player), so effect.rs shrank (1215 -> 1212) and cast.rs too (1795 -> 1772): the rule reads moved to beryl_engine/src/rule_change.rs. The Effects store's tests moved to beryl_kernel/src/effects/tests.rs (effects.rs was at 801 lines with them).

Gotchas for by-w8gv.7.4 and .5:

  1. Saves compare the words of State::off_board. Its order is: entries on objects (Effects::lingering), then the permissions queue, then a rule change on a player (State::rule_change_lingering). When permissions become entries, keep "player N's permission to cast X" in that middle place and those words, or two frozen saves stop replaying (cargo test -p beryl_session --test corpus).
  2. The benchmark build has beryl_engine's coverage feature on. Anything behind #[cfg(feature = "coverage")] on a hot read is counted.
  3. A state's hash covers listening and static_rules. corpus_never_changes moves whenever a hashed field or an entry's timestamp does; rollouts_never_change is the pin that says the games are the same.
  4. State::rule_applies for a player debug-asserts the layer results are current, as State::derived does.
  5. effects.rs is at 566 lines; a payload over 15 bytes or a side over 4 trips the 24-byte assert.

A reviewer should check: decision 4 (Teferi's static is reported to coverage as the layers put it in force, no longer at the read that refuses a spell); that reusing a listener-mask bit for "changes a rule for players" is acceptable against a table read of its own (measured: the read cost about 5M, 1.3%); the lowered bar against whatever the other M4 lanes land.
