Description

While a resolution is paused after a hidden pick (Effect::Choose with hidden, CR 101.4a; Show and Tell), the other player's view shows the picked card still in the picker's hand, among the others, so nobody can see which card was picked. CR 101.4a says each player must clearly indicate which face-down card they are choosing.

Build: beryl_session's PlayerView marks the slots the paused resolution's hidden picks picked (beryl_kernel::state::Resolving::hidden_picks, read from the committed state's pending; the slot is public, the card is not, so the picker sees their card and the other player a card back). The stage (ui/src/stage, both desktop and phone layouts, src/stage/phone.ts) draws such a card set apart from the hand, face down for a viewer who does not know it, using poses the stage already has (no new animation; list it in UI Conventions under 'Not seen in Arena' if Arena shows nothing for it: check with the arena-video skill first).

Files: rust/crates/beryl_session/src/protocol/{view.rs,build.rs}, ui/src/generated/protocol.ts (regenerate), ui/src/stage/. Test: a spec moment of Show and Tell at the second player's question (just specs --shots 'Show and Tell') passes the generic UI checks from both seats.
