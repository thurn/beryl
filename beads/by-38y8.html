Derek Thurn2026-11-14

Reproduced: on staging b5d536d9 a card dragged from the hand (live game via just ui-live) moves with node transform translate/scale only: no lean, no shadow. Cause: the stage rewrite (8e6fc07c) deleted game/physics.ts and nothing replaced it; not a perf decision. Arena reference: _IBqPx0gcag 4:09.85-4:10.32 at 30 fps (Baleful Strix dragged from hand): the card tips in 3D toward where it is pulled while moving and is flat within a few frames of stopping; its shadow is not readable over Arena's dark water. Fix: ui/src/stage/heft.ts ports the old integrator (lift spring, tilt spring driven by smoothed acceleration+drag, edge clamp) for held/settling cards only; dom.ts appends the lean after the node's pose transform and moves a pre-blurred shadow element (heft.css) in the card layer, transform/opacity only, rAF only while held or settling, none under reduced motion. Verified in headless WebKit: moving drag -> rotateX 0.147 rotateY 0.123 rad, scale 1.06, shadow opacity 0.55 offset (10,45); held still -> tilt 0, still lifted; let go -> node back to plain pose transform and shadow removed within 1.5 s. Differences from Arena left: Beryl draws a soft shadow (its own look, which the report asks for); the tilt magnitudes are the old physics.ts tuning, not measured off Arena. Tests: ui/scripts/stage-heft.test.mjs (rise, lean direction, flat when still, settles and stops, shadow direction/fade). No fixture/spec moment: a held card is pointer state, not a view the audits can replay. Frame cost: only compositor properties change per frame (the ui/AGENTS.md rule); no drag perf harness exists to measure it.
