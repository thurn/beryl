Close reason

Fast check plays changed cards' scenarios in XMage and Forge (--no-sim, 10 s lock wait then skip); 22-33 s beside rust lane (bee4e0ad)

Description

Check a changed card's scenarios against the oracles in the fast check, before the push (plan K19; docs/project-50k.md, Target architecture, Checks, "Selection": "A changed card's scenarios run against the oracles before the push"). Step two of the CI track (by-w8gv.20).

Measured (by-w8gv.20.5)

just oracle Counterspell (2 scenarios), warm: 33.5 s and 40.9 s at load 21-34 on 18 cores. About 8 s is the scenario dump (cargo run), 17 s XMage then Forge on the scenarios, and 16 s Forge's 3 AI sim games, which oracle() plays on every call (SIM_GAMES). The full 260-scenario sweep takes 27-79 s in slow CI. oracles.locked() is one machine-wide lock on the oracle work dir, so a fast-check run would wait behind slow CI's oracle lane, which holds it for up to 80 s.

What

  • by oracle --no-sim <cards> skips the sim games. Slow CI keeps them.
  • Fix the lock wait: give a card run its own work dir and log dir so it does not take the shared lock, if the oracle builds are read-only during a run. Otherwise wait at most 10 s and log the step skip with the reason, never failing on it.
  • In checks.py fast(): when a card file under rust/crates/beryl_cards/src/<color>/ changed, add an oracle lane running that card's scenarios. Map file to card through the file's name: names::X and names.rs, or by the dump's card field. The lane's rerun (failed_cards) and runlog ids (oracle:<card>/<scenario>/<engine>) are unchanged.
  • If it cannot fit: measure it in a fast check beside the rust lane on a card change. If the 60 s budget cannot hold it even with --no-sim, keep it out of the fast check and record the measured cost on this bead and on the epic (the plan allows this).

Files

tools/src/beryl_tools/oracles.py (oracle, locked, root, log_dir, dump_scenarios, sim_forge, register), checks.py (fast, oracle_lane, failed_cards).

Tests

pytest for the file-to-card mapping and for --no-sim reaching oracle(). By hand: a one-line change to counterspell.rs in a worktree; show just check's oracle lane and total seconds, then the same while just oracle runs in another worktree.

Acceptance criteria

A change to a card file runs that card scenarios against XMage and Forge (no sim games) in just check within 60 s without waiting on slow CI oracle lock, or the bead and epic record the measured cost and why it stays out; pytest covers the file-to-card mapping.

Lock: not lock-free, because a run writes its XMage test classes and surefire reports into the shared XMage checkout and runs Maven there; so the fallback (wait 10 s, skip, pass). The dump now runs before the lock wait into a temp file, which also shortens every run's hold by the dump (~8 s). Deviation: the oracle lane is added only where both oracles are built at their pins, so a machine without just oracle-setup is not blocked.
