Description

Part of the Arena Powered Cube retarget (epic by-0w2): Beryl's V1 cube is now data/arena-powered-cube-2025-10-30.csv and the mirror match target is the 40-card main deck in docs/sample-decks/2025-11-18-17lands-cb8d5d5f-wu-control.txt (30 distinct cards, 17 of them new). Read the epic's description and design field first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md). Follow the existing card files in rust/crates/beryl_cards/src (for example blue/mana_drain.rs, white/overlord_of_the_mistmoors.rs) for layout and scenarios.

Implement: Abhorrent Oculus, with the additional cost of exiling six cards from your graveyard (CR 601.2b, 601.2h) and manifest dread (CR 701.62): manifest (CR 701.40) and face-down permanents (CR 708), including turning a manifested creature card face up for its mana cost and what a face-down 2/2 reveals to each player in the PlayerView.

Edge cases for scenarios: casting impossible with five cards in the graveyard; manifest dread with one or zero cards in library; a manifested noncreature card that cannot be turned face up; turning a creature face up (a special action, CR 116.2b); a face-down permanent leaving the battlefield and being revealed (CR 708.9); the trigger on each opponent's upkeep only.

Acceptance criteria

  • Each card listed has one file under its color directory with its CardDef, bodies and scenarios; every ability is exercised by at least one scenario, including the edge cases named; every body cites the CR.
  • All scenarios pass under the strict runner, and just oracle <these cards> passes on XMage and Forge, or each disagreement is in data/oracle-divergences.json with its CR citation.
  • Fuzz games including these cards raise no invariant (debug, at least 1,000 games on demand; the just ci count still passes).

Delivered 92a5aeb..f1fddfb. Engine: FaceIx::DOWN resolves to card::FACE_DOWN (2/2, no text/name/cost) through CardDef::face, so layers/triggers/filters need no special cases; knowledge: controller only (layers insert on control), revealed on leaving (zone.rs, CR 708.9); Observation keeps DOWN visible; collapse never merges face-down permanents (else candidate counts leak identity). Turning face up: Opt::TurnFaceUp special action + Pending::TurningFaceUp for plan/manual payment. Manifest dread: EffectCtx::manifest_dread, Ask::ManifestDread. Graveyard exile cost: Face::exile_from_graveyard, CastStep::Exile generalized with left/spec; picks bottom-up (window) so each set is one answer sequence; Ask::ExileCards{zone,count} for the UI. Protocol: ObjectView.facing (enum, avoids 4th bool), Candidate::TurnFaceUp, AskView::ManifestDread/ExileCards. Found and fixed: Fractured Identity copying a face-down permanent leaked the card and could be turned up (docs/bugs.md). Corpus pin moved only because Subtype::Eye shifted bit positions (verified by moving Eye last: old hash returns). Oracles: XMage 9/9 pass; Forge 8 pass, 1 unsupported (Forge AI pays exile costs itself, so scripted graveyard choice can't be replayed). Fuzz 1000 games clean (manifest hit ~850x, face-up ~140x). UI: face-down permanents draw as card backs for both players (no P/T badge) - minimal.
