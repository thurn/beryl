Deviations from the bead, with reasons:

  1. A click on an item sends Click::Drop { on, pile: 0, at: 0 }, not Click::On. The session's Order::click (rust/crates/beryl_session/src/prompt/order.rs) ignores Click::On, the web's OrderRow sends drop(on, 0, 0) for a click, and shape-order's recording holds exactly that drop. Rule 5 forbids changing the session.
  2. The bead's test said dragging the leftmost item to the right end gives at = the last slot. In the session's and the web's numbering, at counts from the first to resolve, which is at the right, so the right end is at 0 and the left end is at = len. The recording answers only Drop on ability 1 at 0, so the inspect test drags the leftmost to the right end and expects that drop. A unit test in prompts/order.rs (an_item_let_go_past_either_end_lands_at_that_end) covers both ends and the middle.
  3. Tests live in beryl_inspect/tests/all/prompts_order.rs, like the other prompt beads' tests, because beryl_scene/tests/prompts.rs doesn't exist and the recordings are read from beryl_inspect.
  4. A waiting trigger is not on the stack, so it has no scene object. The row is drawn in the flat layer with Shown::Face (the ability's plate face, or its source's face when candidate.text is set), as the web draws it as a flat element. A carried item is a float (floats::CARRIED_ITEM) moved by FloatLook, so a carry allocates nothing after it starts.
  5. Items are named '<place>. <source>' (e.g. '1. Currency Converter'), because the duplicate check fails two sibling flat elements that share a role and a name, and two triggers of one source are common.
  6. Table::cover_for_prompt makes the table take no press while an order is open, as the web's coversTable does. Without it, the reachable check failed on a battlefield card under the row.
  7. The ends' words are 600 weight but not italic, because the text system has no italic style. Timeline parity: shape-order/you, shape-order/opponent, shape-order-theirs/you and shape-order-theirs/opponent had no entry before and have none after (0 -> 0); the order prompt makes no steps of its own. just b3d test b3d:shape-order: 16 tests, 0 faults.
