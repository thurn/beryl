Derek Thurn2026-11-14

What the player wants: when the AI opponent casts Stock Up, the time from the cast to its two cards reaching the opponent's hand and the spell resolving should be short. They call the animation itself fine. Acceptance: (1) the flights stay as they are; (2) the wait goes down substantially; (3) the fix applies to every effect that orders cards onto the bottom of a library, not just Stock Up.

Evidence: the report's build 1468ae45 predates 1afd8305, which replaced the five-card lift with Arena's drop-and-rise flight. The current spec film (stock-up takes-two-of-five-...-0, theirs seat) plays the flight in about 1.2 s of stage time. The real wait came from the AI's thinking. In ui.json the cast arrived at 04:19:44.80, the cards reached the hand at 47.44 and the spell resolved at 50.04. searches.json shows four Deep Searcher searches in that span: picks for the hand at 1487 ms and 1145 ms, then the bottom order of the three cards left at 1542 ms and 1069 ms (the last card was forced). Those gaps line up with the gaps between views, so no stage or session delay is involved.

Fix (8e66fd3e): Prompt::orders_library_bottom (beryl_card prompt.rs) is true for a split prompt's Part::Second sent to Place::Bottom. That part takes every card left, so only their order is open. LongSearch and Mcts answer it with Choice(0) unsearched. This removes about 2.6 s of the 5.2 s wait. The cards still reach the hand at about +2.6 s, and the spell now resolves right after. Scry is untouched, because its bottom pick is Part::First, which chooses which cards go to the bottom. Test: beryl_search tests/long.rs neither_search_thinks_about_the_order_of_cards_for_the_library_bottom uses real bottom-order positions from greedy mirror games. It failed before the fix and passes after; the full beryl_search and beryl_card suites pass.

Arena: no motion changed, so I didn't run arena-pixel-match. I compared the timing instead (just video card 'Stock Up'): human Arena opponents' casts show 4 to 14 s before the next event (0VsYGs88NXA #1 14 s, 3sEoP_s4DQE #3 8 s, FGsR_FGChOU #15 4 s). Kept as it is: the two hand picks are still each searched (about 2.6 s), because which two cards it takes matters to its play.
