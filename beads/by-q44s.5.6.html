Description

Read first: the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md; docs/beryl-3d.md 'Text', 'Threads', 'The frame' (the Glyph and Panel materials). Code: beryl_scene src/text/mod.rs (Text, Block, measure, block), src/text/glyphs.rs and src/text/write.rs (moved there by the contract), src/text/fonts.rs (Font; the committed fonts include fonts/mana.ttf); beryl_assets src/glyphs.rs (the rasterizer and its pages); beryl_host's worker threads (src/threads.rs) and where the app asks for glyphs (Client::wanted_glyphs). Web behavior: ui/src/components/Mana.tsx (ManaSymbol, ManaCost, RulesText: {1}{U} in text becomes symbols from the Mana font) and ui/src/design/components/mana-symbols.css (.mana-symbol: the coloured disc and glyph of each symbol, and its shadow inside a button or a title).

Build:

  1. Styled spans (beryl_scene src/text): Text::block takes the element's spans, and lays them out with parley's style runs in one paragraph, each with its type style and color. The cache key includes the spans.
  2. Inline mana symbols: {X} tokens in a text (the same pattern RulesText matches) become parley inline boxes the size of the line's em, so they flow and wrap with the words. Each is written by text::write_block as a Panel record (the disc, full radius, the symbol's color from mana-symbols.css, which this bead adds to data/design-tokens.json under flat as mana.w, mana.u, ... and builds with just tokens) and the mana font's glyph for the symbol in the Glyph material. The Icon material is not needed for this; leave it to the icons bead. Symbols are rasterized with the other glyphs.
  3. Glyphs at a new scale: when Input::Surface changes the scale, the wanted glyphs at the new size go to the host as now; until their page arrives, text::write_block draws each glyph from its old-size cell, scaled to the new size. Layout does not change when the page arrives (test it). In beryl_assets the rasterizing already runs on a worker; check that a rescale's batch does not block the frame thread, and fix it if it does.
  4. The 'the text track' notes on Element::spans go.

Do not build: text editing (the field bead).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/flat_layer.rs: a text with a bold span lays out the span's glyphs with the bold style's font weight; Pay {1}{U} lays out two inline boxes after 'Pay', each as wide as the line's em, and the same text in a narrow box wraps before a symbol, not inside one; after a scale change, the frame draws the old cells scaled and no element's rectangle moves when the new page arrives; the 'text fits' check passes on a prompt whose title has symbols.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_assets; just b3d lane.
