Description

Card

Teferi's Protection: "Until your next turn, your life total can't change and you gain protection from everything. All permanents you control phase out. Exile Teferi's Protection."

What to build

Plan: Designs 7, "Phased-out permanents"; Designs 6, "A player"; decision R11.

  1. Phasing (CR 702.26).
    • State::phase_out(set) and State::phase_in(set). A phased-out permanent leaves the battlefield queue, every battlefield bitboard and the layer results, and joins a bitboard of phased-out slots. Its zone stays the battlefield, its incarnation does not change, and its counters, memos, effects and attachments stay as they are. So nothing that walks the battlefield sees it, and nothing triggers as though it left or entered.
    • What is attached to it phases out with it and comes back attached (702.26g). It is removed from combat. A spell or ability targeting it has an illegal target.
    • In the untap step, before untapping (CR 502.1), the active player's phased-out permanents phase in and their permanents with phasing phase out, at once.
    • A token that phases out stays a token and phases in.
    • A new ObjectFlags bit; invariants for a slot being in exactly one of the two sets; Observation shows phased-out permanents as public.
  2. A player's rules. Rule changes on a player, lasting until their next turn:
    • "life total can't change": State::gain_life, lose_life, damage_player and the cost check for paying life ask. Damage is still dealt (so lifelink's gain happens to the other player), and a cost of life can't be paid at all (CR 119.8).
    • protection from everything (CR 702.16j): the player can't be targeted, damage to them is prevented (by-bajq.3.9), and nothing can be attached to them.

Scenarios

Phase out with an Equipment attached; a creature phased out in response to a removal spell; the opponent's attack into an empty board dealing no damage; Griselbrand's controller unable to pay 7 life; phasing in at the untap step, untapped or not as it was.
