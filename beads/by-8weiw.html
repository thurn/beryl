enact next decides under the ownership lock even when it only waits

Description

Symptom: on Beryl's Mac, enact next held Enact's ownership lock (.git/enact/ownership.lock) for 3,492 s of the 6,197 s of all lock holds between 02:00 and 04:15 UTC 2026-11-16: 347 polls, about 10 s each. Every enact claim, start, close, push and worktree create waits behind it (14,700 s of summed waits in that window, 11 give-ups).

Partly fixed: Enact e74328e removed 7.5 s of each poll (recover_deliveries checked 237 dead delivery receipts with git). What is left is schedule() in ~/enact/scripts/enact.py, which is @serialized as a whole. Each call does fetch (0.4 s), recover_deliveries, register_run (bd show), close_finished_epics (its own beads.all, about 2 s), beads.all again (about 2 s), bd ready (0.8 s) and justiciar_state (git ls-remote, 0.3 s), measured at load 30 with the Beads store uncontended; contention on Dolt's store lock makes each bd call several times slower. Almost every poll ends in action wait (capacity, pool busy, nothing ready) and writes nothing.

Proposed: run schedule once without the lock in a probe mode in which each write site (recover_deliveries finding a receipt to finish, register_run finding a changed route, close_finished_epics finding an eligible epic, the justiciar transition, the claim loop, start_ci) raises instead of writing; on that, take the lock and run it again from fresh reads. claim() already re-checks the bead and its run holds after a sync, and the per-vizier next-*.lock keeps one vizier's polls apart, so a decision made unlocked is safe for the wait paths. Also pass the graph that schedule reads into close_finished_epics' eligibility check so one poll reads the graph once. Deliver through Enact's own worktree/push flow with a unit test in tests/test_unit.py.

Measure with .git/enact/lock-waits.jsonl (held_s per command; group by the enact verb). Done when next's share of lock hold time is under 10% and its median hold under 1 s.
