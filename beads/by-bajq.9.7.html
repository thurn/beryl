Close reason

Both oracle bridges play controlled turns: Mindslaver 7/7 in XMage and Forge

Description

Mindslaver's scenarios (rust/cards/cards_2003/mrd/mindslaver.rs) in which P0 controls P1's turn are reported unsupported by both oracles, so XMage and Forge check only 4 of its 7 scenarios. Beryl's scenario trace records the turn's controller (beryl_scenario Position::controller), and tools/src/beryl_tools/forge.py (export) and xmage.py (act) raise Unsupported(CONTROLLED_TURN) for any action in such a turn.

Why: both harnesses look an action's cards and permanents up among the acting player's own. XMage's BerylScenarioTest.tap (tools/src/beryl_tools/BerylScenarioTest.java) taps 'an untapped Plains of PlayerA' when PlayerA, the controller, pays for PlayerB's spell; XMage's TestPlayer refuses 'attack' commands from a player whose turn it is not (Mage.Tests TestPlayer.java, 'can't attack on turn'), though XMage's own Mindslaver test (TakeControlWhileSearchingLibraryTest.test_Mindslaver) has the controller cast the controlled player's spell. Forge's harness (tools/oracles/BerylForgeHarness.java) looks the cast card up in the actor's hand ('p0 has no Oust').

Fix: in each bridge, while a position has a controller, look cards up among the controlled (active) player's resources and route attacks and payments as each oracle expects, then remove the Unsupported raise. Run just card check Mindslaver; record a real disagreement with just card diverge.

Acceptance criteria

just card check Mindslaver plays all 7 scenarios in XMage and Forge with none unsupported for a controlled turn (or each remaining one is a recorded divergence).
