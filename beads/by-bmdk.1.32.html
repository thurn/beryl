Close reason

Preserved public labels and projected durable ability previews for each seat in 6770658e.

Description

The table currently redacts public notes, named counters and proposal labels by substring against every hidden card and other seat’s deck. A normal charge counter can disappear from a view or entry when the other seat loads Charge. Also, an ability marker left on the stack loses its source identity and text for every seat when the source leaves the table or gets a new id on entering a hand. In rust/crates/beryl_table/src/redact.rs and apply/cards.rs, use explicit visibility for labels and ability source information. Keep the design’s hidden-card rule for structured actions, changes and proposal text. Coordinate the source contract with the table-session view and ability menu beads. The representation crosses the core and adapter boundary, so it needs its own verified change.

Acceptance criteria

A public charge counter and its log label survive a hidden card with the same name without disclosing the hidden card. A public ability keeps its display source and text after the source bounces or is removed. A marker or proposal about a source the viewing seat cannot identify carries no private name or text. Exercise both seats and save/replay with focused tests in the fast check.
