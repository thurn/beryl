Description

Part of the ladder epic by-97g. Read docs/ai-strategy.md first: §2.2, §6.4 and §9. Start this only when every rung's gate bead is closed.

Design the tree of turns and write the design into this bead's design field. Build nothing. It needs judgement and is not tier:light.

The question

The search's unit is one priority decision; a turn is 10 to 20 of them, and 720 rollouts a tree never see a whole turn. §9 outlines the answer: at the root, enumerate what is left of the player's own turn with the opponent passing, merge the sequences that reach the same position, take combat from the planner, score each end-of-turn position by rollouts, and plan again when the opponent acts.

Where to look

  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs: Tree::iterate, keys (how a candidate is told apart across worlds), Method, Family.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/combat.rs: the planner, after rung 4.
  • docs/ladder.md: what each rung measured.

The design must answer, with numbers measured on this code

  1. How many distinct end-of-turn positions does a turn of the WU mirror have? Measure it over 200 positions of champion games: enumerate with the opponent passing and count after merging. Give the median and the 95th percentile.
  2. How are sequences merged? Name the state hash and what it leaves out.
  3. What does a plan say about the opponent's turn: which mana stays up, and for what? hold settles this by rule today.
  4. How is hidden information handled: one enumeration a world, or one over what the player sees?
  5. What is the new family name part, and what is its first challenger?
  6. What would make us drop it?

Design

Measurement

On Cygnus at staging b5d536d9, I replayed the saved choices of the ten distinct shuffle seeds from the November 12 anchor+guard@3M self-match (D:/beryl-cache/ladder/champion-2026-11-12). The logs agreed with the engine on the deciding seat and legal candidate index at every choice. I sampled 200 evenly spaced precombat-main starts from the 211 eligible turns, ordered by pair and then turn (index floor(i * 210 / 199)).

The probe branched over the active player's legal choices under Profile::SMART apart from combat declarations, where the generic combat planner chose for both seats. The opponent always passed priority and used greedy for other required answers. Each root used its logged deal and the engine's Replay randomness. A leaf is the first state at the next turn boundary after cleanup or a game outcome during the active turn. The probe used DefaultHasher over State::hash for distinct leaves and intermediate states and skipped intermediate merging while a combat declaration plan could supply the answer. It stopped each root at 100,000 distinct intermediate decision states or depth 512. The count therefore includes rules-relevant distinctions such as which mana sources tapped and keeps the logged deal fixed. It is a measurement of this branching surface, before the proposed cross-world policy or pruning.

122 roots completed and 78 hit the intermediate-state cap. The median was 3,490.5 distinct leaves (the middle counts 3,471 and 3,510 are exact because every capped run had already found more). The nearest-rank 95th percentile is at least 95,727. An exact 95th percentile was beyond this probe's cap. Ten roots used more than the entire 3M budget in returned and silent decisions alone, excluding copies, world deals and leaf scoring. One uncapped root used 369,094,183 such decisions because combat evaluation also runs simulations. Full enumeration does not fit the ladder budget. The probe and CSV are in the Cygnus session's system temp directory as beryl-by-97g.31-probe.rs and beryl-by-97g.31-probe.csv. No product code was changed.

First challenger

Add a family method named turn72, initially with the same 72 worlds and other settings as the champion. At the current champion that is pimc72/hidden/rollout/tuned/anchor+guard -> turn72/hidden/rollout/tuned/anchor+guard. Only use it on an active player's own turn, starting at a priority decision with an empty stack. Keep the existing PIMC search for other roots. The controller returns only the first currently legal Choice. At the next decision it plans again from the actual observation, including after an opponent action or newly visible card. It never submits a queued sequence.

Deal worlds from Observation::determinize as the existing hidden search does. Generate a bounded frontier separately in each dealt world. Group prefixes across those worlds by the observation available at the time of the choice and the candidate identity from long::keys, including its duplicate ordinal. At a shared observation all worlds must follow the same continuation. Only split a continuation when the root player could observe the difference, for example after drawing a card. Opponent priority always passes. The generic combat planner handles declarations for both seats and the rollout policy handles the opponent's required noncombat choices. A private opponent choice may change the world, but the root policy can only distinguish the result once the root player can see it. When an observation does not occur in the proposal sample, finish its hypothetical turn using the observation-only rollout policy. Never pick the highest scoring continuation independently in every world and average those maxima.

Deduplicate within one dealt world using State::hash with exact State equality inside hash buckets. Include any pending combat plan or only merge at points where the pending plan is empty. Keep distinct first actions in the search's accounting even if their paths meet. The kernel hash omits the keyed-slot index, which is derived from the stores. It includes RNG and library order, knowledge, priority, pending choices, object incarnations, effects and engine limits. Do not merge states on visible board equality or discard budget fields in the initial build. Across worlds use observation equivalence to share a decision, without merging the underlying rules states.

Start with one eighth of the 3M or 30M work budget for proposing continuations and use the balance for scoring, with 24 proposal worlds and 48 separate scoring worlds. Charge decisions, silent decisions, state copies and world deals as long.rs does, including nested combat work. Use a budget-aware combat call that stops at a plan boundary. Order expansions by rollout policy score, keep at most eight partial lines per first action per world, and reserve a pass line and the greedy line. Score completed lines with the existing softmax rollout, same turn discount and same observation policy across the scoring worlds. Use a common fixed world order and seeds for competing first actions. Every scored first action must cover the same set of worlds before it can displace the guarded default. A root that runs out of proposal budget before producing a comparable alternative uses the guarded default within the original total work budget.

Opponent's turn

The plan says which untapped sources remain and which known instants or activated abilities they can pay for. Use the engine payment candidates and solver. Equal available mana totals can differ in color or in the abilities they leave available. Preserve these distinctions in both the state and the frontier. Reserve mana by retaining lines that pass with a payable response, rather than requiring a particular card or reserving a fixed number of lands. Price that reserve through the champion's existing rollout policy on the opponent's turn, where opposing plays and actual priority windows can occur. Replan with the ordinary search when priority returns on that turn. This first challenger adds no hold option and has no precommitted response.

Decision

Drop the full enumerator based on the measured branching and work. Test the bounded challenger once as a Search rung. It must pass just gate with no claims, then the paired 3M stronger bar in docs/ai-strategy.md section 4. A failed suite, rejected match or undecided 2,000-game cap leaves the champion in place and ends this rung. Before running that gate, require deterministic checks that the work limit includes nested planner calls and that hidden swaps cannot change the chosen action under a fixed observation and seeds.
