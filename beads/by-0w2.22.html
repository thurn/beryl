Close reason

Blockers face their attackers from any row position; combat-blockers keeps Trinket Mage second (e7ce8c3)

Description

Found by by-0w2.20. When the attacker is first in the attacking player's row and its blocker is second in the defender's row, the table leaves the blocker in its own row position instead of moving it across from the attacker. Repro: in rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/boards.rs, swap TRINKET_MAGE and SNAPCASTER_MAGE in the combat-blockers board's P0 battlefield (so Trinket Mage, the blocker, is second), then run just ui-test. 'a blocker stands across from its attacker, covering no other card' fails: Trinket Mage's centre is 145 px from Beza's, over the 120 px the check allows. The fixture currently lists both first in their rows, as the old seeded fixture happened to.

Acceptance criteria

  • A blocker stands across from its attacker whatever their positions in their rows, and the rest of its row makes room.
  • With the combat-blockers board's blocker second in its row, just ui-test passes 'a blocker stands across from its attacker'. Keep that order in the board, or add a board for it.

Derek Thurn2026-11-06

Cause: the defending row in combat-blockers is packed from ROOM.start to ROOM.end, so a blocker can only take a slot. The row was ordered by wanted left. The blocker's want leans toward the table's middle (ACROSS_LEAN), so Snapcaster's natural slot 0 sorted before it, and settle pushed the pinned blocker into slot 1 (127 px off Beza's middle in stage units). Fix (layout.ts arrange): try each split of the unpinned groups around the pinned ones (in order) and keep the one that leaves pinned groups nearest their unleaned 'face' left (straight across), with ties going to the natural side. The lean still applies when the row has slack. Checked by probe: mirror case (attacker last, blocker 4th of 5) takes the last slot; a roomy row still leans. combat-blockers now lists Trinket Mage second. Pushed e7ce8c3.
