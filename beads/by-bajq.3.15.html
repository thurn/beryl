Description

Why

Lightning Bolt was escalated (data/card-escalations.json, commit 20c230eb; by-bajq.3.7): "Lightning Bolt deals 3 damage to any target." A TargetSpec (beryl_card/src/target.rs) has one TargetKind, and the only kinds are Permanent, Spell, SpellOrPermanent, GraveyardCard and Player; nothing accepts a permanent or a player in one target. Recipients (beryl_card/src/effect/reference.rs) is either Players(..) or Permanents(..), so Effect::DealDamage has no form for "the target, whichever kind it is".

Bolt is the red damage source for Mother of Runes (by-bajq.3.9), Circle of Protection: Red and Furnace of Rath (by-bajq.3.10), and Fork (by-bajq.6.4).

What to build

  • "Any target" (CR 115.4): a target that is a creature, a player, a planeswalker or a battle. Add a TargetKind for a permanent or a player (the filter applies to the permanent side; a constructor such as TargetSpec::any_target() gives creature-or-planeswalker-or-battle permanents and any player). Planeswalkers and battles may not exist in the engine yet: accept what the type line says, so the kind is right once they do.
  • The casting machine offers both kinds of candidate in the same target step (beryl_rules/src/cast/targets.rs), the AI's options list both, and the check on resolution (CR 608.2b) handles either.
  • A Recipients form for "that target", so DealDamage deals damage to a player or to a permanent through the existing damage functions. Damage to a creature is marked on it; damage to a player is life loss (CR 120.3).
  • Render "any target" (beryl_card/src/render/) and accept it in the table check (beryl_card/src/check.rs). The view and the UI already show a player or a permanent as a target; check that a target arrow to either works in just specs for the card bead.

Tests

Unit tests in beryl_rules/src/tests/ with a test-only definition: the target step offers both players and the creatures; damage to a player lowers their life; damage to a creature is marked and the creature dies to state-based actions; a target that became illegal (left the battlefield) makes the spell do nothing.

Files

beryl_card/src/{target.rs, effect/reference.rs, effect/build.rs, check.rs, render/}, beryl_rules/src/{cast/targets.rs, damage.rs, interpret/}.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate; R2 in the plan). The count is exact, so report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust). A change to what the WU mirror's rollouts do is a bug in this epic.
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (root AGENTS.md); just citations.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill: just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), every instruction carried out by its own scenarios, and just specs <card> passing the generic UI checks from both seats. A disagreement with an oracle is settled by the rules and recorded in data/oracle-divergences.json.
  • Read the card's rulings before writing its scenarios (just card brief <name> prints Scryfall's). Each statement in a ruling that a scenario can show is a scenario of the card. Where one cannot be shown yet, the close reason names the ruling and why.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry.
  • If the design in the plan is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.

Acceptance criteria

A TargetSpec can say 'any target', the target step offers players and permanents together, and DealDamage can deal damage to that target whichever kind it is; unit tests for both kinds and an illegal target. Count gate unchanged.
