Acceptance criteria

No white or light pixels appear in the corner regions of any ability or trigger stack item (stack, ordering tray, chooser faces, game-over card); the top corners are rounded to the same radius as a card. Checked on the reported fixture by sampling the four corner pixels against the dark background.

Notes

What the player saw: the triggered/activated ability stack item (here an ability card for Jace, Wielder of Mysteries) has a white square corner at the top-left (and top-right) poking out of its dark rounded frame (screenshot.png). Cause: .stack-item.stack-item-scanned (ui/src/design/components/card-frame.css:458-466) rounds only the bottom corners (border-radius: 0 0 50% 50% / 0 0 18% 18%) with overflow: hidden, so the source card's scan (white card border) shows square at the top. What they want: no white corners on ability/trigger indicators. Fix: round the top corners to the card frame radius (about 4.75% / 3.5%, as .stage-way-face uses) and/or paint the scan's corner area dark. Scope: every stack item with a scan (abilities and triggers on the stack, tray order row, the game-over culprit card, chooser ability faces in Choose.tsx). Triage kind: skin. Reproduce: fixture route, just review "<folder>/replay.json" --turn 16. Verify with /arena-pixel-match. Follow the bugfix skill's 'Worker: fixing one report' section.
