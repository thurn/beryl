Rules for every shape

  • The session holds the prompt's state (K26). The UI draws the marks and button states it is sent and sends clicks as commands through ui/src/ipc.ts. No prompt logic in the UI: nothing there decides what is eligible, selected or allowed.
  • Wording is Arena's exactly and comes from the session (K22; AGENTS.md, UI and Workflow). Exception 3 stands: never ask a question before a choice the next screen can decline.
  • Cards in a prompt are the stage's own nodes, moved by verbs: one visual per object, no borrowed copies.
  • Checks: shape:<name>/<seat>/<layout> in ui/scripts/shapes.mjs (made by by-w8gv.21.26, reporting and selected as ui/scripts/verbs.mjs is), from both seats and in both layouts: the chooser's seat answers the prompt by clicks, the other seat sees what an opponent may see. One check per shape with its purposes as cases, never one per card. A hand-written text snapshot per shape (K24) of the marks and buttons after each click, in beryl_session's tests if milestone 6 wrote none.
  • Today's files are the list of cases; the cut-over deletes them.

Build

  • Marks from the session onto stage nodes, with the looks by-w8gv.21.22 defined once.
  • The prompt bar: title, buttons and their states. Today that is Prompt in Board.tsx (about lines 2905 to 3133), passButton and HALT_BUTTON in layout.ts (about 1930 to 1960), FloatPrompt.tsx.
  • Clicks and drags from by-w8gv.21.16's interface become commands.
  • ui/scripts/shapes.mjs, the shape suite.

  • Priority: playable cards, casting by dragging from hand (the held card), a permanent's abilities (ActionMenu and menuEntries, Board.tsx from about 3377), passing and the pass-on buttons.

  • Any other purpose milestone 6 gave this shape.

FILES

  • ui/src/stage/prompt.ts (new): Prompts. One per page (playback.ts pageStage().prompts). It stands between the feed and the stage (Feed is given the Prompts, whose play/skip/shown/subscribe pass through), and is the stage pointer's one listener. asked() is what the screen draws: { decision, prompt, sending, answered, error }. Opens the prompt of a view once its script has played out (stage.shown.view, not busy); press(button), on(subject) and a click or drop on a node send ipc.click(decision, revision, click); the reply is the next state. Marks are mark steps played as scripts of their own (stage.play(view, { beats: [steps] })), a diff against what it has drawn; they end in a script of their own before the next view's script. onTable(prompt) says what the stage holds; subjectOf(name); shut() for the motion captures; clicks(name) lets a board keep a touch (the phone's hand); listen(events) hands hover, lift and unused clicks on to chrome.
  • ui/src/stage/PromptBar.tsx (new): usePrompt, usePromptKeys (Space/Enter the button put forward, Shift for pass-on, Escape cancels a cast), PromptDock (desktop: title, the primary button as the turn button with its caption, pass-on as the round button, the others stacked, Cancel from decision.cancellable and prompt.words.cancel), PhonePromptDock and PhonePromptOver (title pill and the other buttons over the strip), PlayerTarget (the hit area on a life total the prompt marks).
  • ui/src/stage/StageBoard.tsx, PhoneStageBoard.tsx, Chrome.tsx (Controls takes prompts and the dock as children; Hud takes orb), switch.tsx (useAsked), playback.ts (prompts, window.berylPrompts, berylBehind now also covers marks coming up), feed.ts (behind = a view has not played out), spans.ts (keyOf exported), mobile/chrome.tsx (Orb children), mobile/phone.css.
  • ui/src/screens/Game.tsx: waiting includes the stage's answered click; a failed click is told as any failed command.
  • ui/src/ipc.ts and rust/crates/beryl_session/src/ipc.rs: a fixture response may carry its request; the transport refuses a click that is not the recorded one.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/fixture.rs, fixture/clicks.rs (new): a definition's clicks (grammar in Written::parse and ui/fixtures.json's comment). boards/shapes.rs (new): shape-pick, shape-pick-theirs.
  • ui/scripts/shapes.mjs (new), ui/shapes.json (new): shape:<shape>/<seat>/<layout>. ui/scripts/stage-prompt.test.mjs (new, npm test). tools: selection.py (SHAPE_SCRIPT, shape_checks, shape_fixtures, scripts), shots.py, coverage.py.
