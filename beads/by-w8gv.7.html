D
Derek Thurn2026-11-12

M4 Mason audit (by-w8gv.8.1, staging 86ee9715): where the milestone as built differs from K4 and the plan's "State" section. Each is recorded in beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs "Stores" with its reason; listed here so the epic holds them. None needs work before M5.

  1. Four stores, not three: the counters table (counters.rs) is its own store beside Effects, Memos and Trackers. Counters are the rules' own (CR 122), but all kinds past the three the rules read cannot be fields of 255 objects.
  2. "A per-game capability bit makes an absent kind cost one AND": Effects and Memos use a mask of the kinds live in the store instead, which is exact where a GameTable bit could only bound it (a Rust body is opaque to the table). Only Trackers use table::Capability. Measured cost per hot read is in the contract's table.
  3. Trackers are "this-turn and this-game counts" in the plan; built are two facts keyed by permanent, both of this turn. No card of the pool reads a count; a kind kept for a player or the game joins with its first reader.
  4. "The game" as an affected side is designed and not built: no card changes a rule for everyone.
  5. "Each store implements saving once": Store::lingering says what a checkpoint would lose, but a checkpoint (beryl_session/src/checkpoint.rs) records memos kind by kind and Memos::lingering is None. Changing it breaks frozen saves and no card needs it (Warden, by-w8gv.7.11).
  6. "The one-card state fields and variants removed": the fields are gone. Nine one-card vocabulary entries remain on data/card-names-allowlist.json (mechanics), Rule::SorceriesAsFlash new among them in place of Player::sorceries_as_flash, and the kernel mirrors one (LayerChange::PowerIsCreaturesYouControl). M5's scope replaces them with composable data; noted on by-w8gv.9.1.
  7. State<Small> is 20,176 bytes of 20,480; the contract's target was at most 20,256. Object stayed 24 bytes.
