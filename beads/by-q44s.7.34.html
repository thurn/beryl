Description

Context: by-q44s.7.29 grouped the phone reachable faults of just b3d test --phone-only by what covers each card (a reachable fault now ends with "under ..."). The phone matrix cannot become the default (by-q44s.7.33) until it is green, and no phone fault allowances may be added to data/beryl3d/test-faults.json. Read docs/beryl-3d.md "The table", "Verbs", "Sizes" and "Interface checks" and rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. The reachable rule is beryl_scene src/agent/checks.rs reachable; it judges every frame in which no segment moves a card (motion.rs Track::moving: a segment that holds a card still does not count). Reproduce each fault with the just b3d tree <recording> --seat <seat> --layout phone --size WxH --at Tms line it prints, and just b3d png the same moment.

Symptom: about 20 reachable faults in the named recordings come from the strike verb (beryl_scene src/verbs/strike.rs) holding a creature still at two poses: at its poise, reared back 0.6 card widths (verbs.strike.rear in data/beryl3d/space.json) into its own back row, and on its landing, where a blow at a player stops at their life total in the opponent's strip, under the opponent's hand of card backs. Examples at 375x647: verbs-combat/you t=2904 (The Wandering Emperor under the rearing Beza), mother-of-runes/an-attacker-with-protection-from-white-cant-be-blocked-by-a-white-creature/you t=548 (the attacking Wetland Sambar at 146,61 under the opponent's hand), late-game/opponent t=8369 and t=8462, combat-attackers/opponent t=24565 and t=45225, combat-attackers/you t=120561 at 393x759, mother-of-runes furnace-of-rath and protection-from-white-prevents at t=1250. On the desktop the same rear leaves the back row's cards a square because they are about 100 points tall; on the phone they are 75-80. Fix in strike.rs, using step.places.layout().bands (Some on the phone) for the phone's room: a smaller rear on the phone, and a blow at a player that stops inside the battlefield (beryl_scene src/table/phone.rs battlefield) and is drawn above the opponent's hand. Keep the desktop's motion unchanged; record what the phone does in docs/beryl-3d.md 'Verbs'.

Acceptance criteria

No reachable fault whose covering card is a striking creature in just b3d test --phone-only and --every-tick at all three sizes for verbs-combat, verbs-combat-theirs, late-game, combat-attackers and mother-of-runes; desktop just b3d test, just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene and just b3d gate pass.

Re-validated on staging ec7bda7cd. The strike faults this bead describes no longer reproduce. Commit 9978acc6d (fix: count a strike's held apex as motion) landed about an hour before this bead was filed. It adds motion.rs Track::paused, and scene.rs draws a card that its step holds still (a strike's poise and landing) as moving, so reachable skips those frames. I ran just b3d test b3d:<r> --phone-only --every-tick for verbs-combat (which includes verbs-combat-theirs), late-game, combat-attackers and mother-of-runes, and no reachable fault is under a striking creature. The ones that remain belong to by-q44s.7.29: (a) in verbs-combat and verbs-combat-theirs the coin region over Beza or Elite Spellbinder (7.29 item 2); (b) in combat-attackers and late-game, back-row permanents (Jace, the Mind Sculptor; Teferi, Time Raveler; Portable Hole; Currency Converter) about 45% covered by the prompt controls in the viewer's back row (7.29 item 1). mother-of-runes has one reachable fault, which is not a strike. The strike's 0.6-card-width rear on the phone still overlaps the back row while it is held, but it is drawn as motion there, as on the desktop. I made no code change.
