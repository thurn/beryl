DECISIONS AND DEVIATIONS (by-w8gv.11.7)

  1. One title per prompt, as the wire has. Arena's browsers have a heading and a line under it: the title is the line that asks, its sentence of rules text ("Put two of them into your hand.") or its heading where the line only says how to drag ("Scry", "Order Triggers", "Assign Damage", "Explore", "Return 1 Card").
  2. Buttons are Arena's families: a pick confirms with "Submit {picked}" (confirm and counted alike), an arrangement with "Done", a may with "Take Action" / "Decline", optional picks decline with "Decline" (search, look) or "Submit 0" (up to one target, untap). The old UI now fills {picked} on every confirm (choices.ts, tablePick.ts) and a new state hole {s} ("{picked} Attacker{s}").
  3. Arena words many prompts from the asking card's text ("Target a creature.", "Search for a basic land card."). The record has no such text, so those entries are Arena's any-card form or composed from it; listed in data/ui-conventions.json (three not-seen entries named "Words: ...") and on the human bead by-w8gv.11.14. Matching Arena exactly needs the effect's own text on the record: not built here.
  4. Holes: {Count}, {Whose}, {land} removed (unused once Arena's titles replaced "Top Four", "Opponent's Hand" and the land's name); {most:words} added; Holes.cost is an Option so a cost of no mana falls to the unnamed title ("Pay {0}."). place_word(place) became pile_word(purpose, place): a scry's piles are BOTTOM / TOP, an ordering's Bottom / Top.
  5. Cancel: cancel_word(purpose) ("Cancel", "Cancel Attacks", "Cancel Blocks"), sent as Words.cancel (one new wire field; protocol.ts regenerated). The old UI still draws its own Cancel.
  6. Candidate labels (protocol/candidates.rs) are prompt buttons too: "Keep" is Arena's "Keep 7" (the cards the hand keeps), "Play first" / "Draw first" are "Play" / "Draw" (composed).
  7. The old UI's " for <source>" after a target's title became Arena's "(<source>) " before it (decisions.ts prompt()): with Arena's full stop the old form read "Choose a target. for Oust".
  8. NOT in the table: the pass button's labels. by-w8gv.11.8 is adding pass_words(PassLeads) to wording.rs with the old UI's strings and is its only consumer; a second table here would have been dead code on staging. Arena's labels and captions with evidence are handed over in the run's notes folder (by-w8gv.11.7-arena-wording-for-11.8.md) and by message, and noted on the Warden by-w8gv.11.12. The combat dock (Attack With All, Clear, N attacking) is still worded by the old UI; the table has Arena's words for it (All Attack as Attack's auto, Cancel Attacks).
  9. One composed title was shortened for the overlay: "Put two cards on top of your library." overflowed choice-jace's heading (ui-test, 'choice text fits its box'), so it is "Put two cards on top." No Arena string was shortened.
  10. Evidence: every entry cites <recording> <time> beside it in wording.rs; found through the text index of all 26 recordings and one still (the X prompt's Pay button).
