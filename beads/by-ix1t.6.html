Description

Part of the AI ladder epic by-ix1t. Read docs/ai-plan.md first: §2.3 and §6.5.

Below the root the tree visits every child about equally, so a node's mean is the value of answering that decision at random. Tree::select uses UCB1 with EXPLORATION 0.7. At the 15 to 600 visits a node gets, the exploration bonus is 0.07 or more, and real differences between moves are 0.01 to 0.05. The effect is visible in by-97g.34's notes: at beza-trinket-into-blockers, holding back was scored as the average of 11 follow-up plays, among them a pass rated 0.48, and the attack was scored against blocks spread 15, 20, 18 and 17 visits over four ways to block. order (rung 7 of the old plan, +6 Elo over 1,652 games) only changed which child is tried first, which cannot matter when every child ends up visited equally.

This bead adds the option prior: a child's exploration is weighted by the policy's score of it.

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs, Tree::select: it takes a scores closure that returns the policy's score of each candidate (order uses it for the first visit), and computes bound = n.reward / visits + EXPLORATION * (ln(n.avail) / visits).sqrt(). n.avail counts the times the parent was visited with this action available.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/rollout.rs, Softmax: a rollout draws a candidate with weight exp((score - top) / temperature), and a candidate scored None is handled there. Use the same weights, normalised, as P.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/why.rs: just why ENTRY --a LABEL --b LABEL --tree --family FAMILY prints the tree below two candidates and the paired rollouts' lead.

Build

  1. Add Options::prior, named prior, last in the fixed order.
  2. With prior set, in select:
    • the first visit goes to the unplayed child with the highest P;
    • after that a child's bound is mean + C * P(child) * sqrt(avail) / (1 + visits). A candidate the policy does not score takes an equal share of P. Compute the scores once a node visit, not once a child.
  3. Choose C. Try three values an octave apart. For each, run just why --tree --family FAMILY --work 3M at beza-trinket-into-blockers, manifest-into-samurai, spirits-stay-home and skip-teferi-plus-one (the labels to compare are in by-97g.34's description). Take the C whose tree lead is closest to the paired rollouts' lead over the four. Paste the twelve pairs of leads into the notes.
  4. Tests:
    • A child the policy scores far below its siblings gets fewer visits than under UCB1 at the same budget and seed.
    • At beza-trinket-into-blockers, a seeded search with prior rates the attack less than 0.02 above holding back.
    • The name round-trips, and the champion's moves do not change.
  5. The benchmark's instruction count must not move with the option off: keep the new branch out of line (docs/ai-plan.md §7).

This costs a policy scoring at every tree node, which order already pays on first visits. Measure rollouts a second with and without prior at 3M and write both in the notes.

Acceptance criteria

prior parses and is off by default; the chosen C and the twelve lead comparisons are in the notes; the Beza entry's tree lead is under 0.02 in a test; just test-rust, just check and the count gate pass.
