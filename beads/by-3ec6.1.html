Close reason

Phone prompt pill moved to the middle band, clear of permanents; overlap check now flags a prompt pill over a permanent

Description

On the phone layout, the pill with what the open prompt asks (PhonePromptOver in ui/src/stage/PromptBar.tsx, .phone-pill in ui/src/mobile/phone.css, placed at top - 30 above the player strip) is drawn over the player's own land row. During a pick made on the battlefield, such as Teferi, Hero of Dominaria's 'Untap up to two lands.', the player cannot see which lands are picked (gold) or eligible (cyan). Seen with the fixtures choice-teferi and choice-teferi-lands at 393x759 (shot phone-untap-teferi-lands). just ui-play reports 0 findings for both, so the generic overlap check misses a pill over a card in play (it is pointer-events: none). Arena (desktop) puts the words on a line across the middle of the table, clear of both players' permanents.

Acceptance criteria

At choice-teferi-lands and choice-teferi on the phone, no part of the prompt pill covers a permanent of either player; the generic overlap check is extended so that a prompt pill over a card in play is a finding, and it fails on the current layout.
