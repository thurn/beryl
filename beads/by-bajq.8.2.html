Description

Card

Animate Dead: "Enchant creature card in a graveyard. When this Aura enters, if it's on the battlefield, it loses 'enchant creature card in a graveyard' and gains 'enchant creature put onto the battlefield with this Aura.' Return enchanted creature card to the battlefield under your control and attach this Aura to it. When this Aura leaves the battlefield, that creature's controller sacrifices it. Enchanted creature gets -1/-0."

What it needs

Plan: Designs 7, "An Aura on a card in a graveyard".

  • A target in a graveyard for an Aura spell, and Object::attached_to naming an object in another zone. The zone move's clearing of attachments must still be right.
  • Which enchant ability is in force is a layer 6 result (by-bajq.2.2: LoseAbility and a grant). The state-based action for an Aura attached to something illegal (CR 704.5m) reads the one in force and the attached object where it is.
  • "Put onto the battlefield with this Aura" is a memo naming the creature (Memo, by slot and incarnation), written as it is returned.
  • Return and attach: a zone-change instruction that puts a card onto the battlefield under a named player's control, then Effect::Attach. The census's effect:ChangeZone is this family: write the general instruction (from a zone, to the battlefield, under whose control, tapped or not) and not one for this card.
  • An Aura that enters without being cast chooses what it enchants as it enters, by its enchant ability, and it is not a target (CR 303.4f); with nothing legal it stays where it was (303.4g). This is a rewrite of entering (group 3). Worldgorger Dragon returning Animate Dead uses it, and so does Show and Tell (group 8).
  • The leaves trigger looks back (by-bajq.1.2) and names "that creature" through the memo.
  • The intervening "if it's on the battlefield".

Scenarios

Reanimating a creature; Animate Dead destroyed (the creature is sacrificed); the creature blinked (Animate Dead falls off as a state-based action); Animate Dead under Containment Priest (the creature is exiled, the Aura goes to the graveyard); the creature leaving in response to the enters trigger.

Decisions: (1) An enchant ability is the Aura's spell ability target; may_enchant asks every one the Aura has now via State::abilities (CR 702.5c), so LoseAbility removes one and a granted spell ability (now allowed by listener::read_when_granted) adds one. (2) 'When this Aura leaves the battlefield' sits in the enters ability's paragraph, so it is a delayed trigger the enters ability creates (first ruling calls it delayed; XMage and Forge agree): delayed triggers may now wait for their source to leave (delayed_triggers::fire_on_leaving). (3) The creature is named by Memo::PutOntoBattlefieldWith written by a linked Effect::PutOntoBattlefield, read by TargetSpec::put_with_this and Objects::PutOntoBattlefieldWithThis (the delay carries it). (5) Unattaching from a card leaving a graveyard lives in event::move_batch_to behind the table's Aura test: in the kernel's zone::leave it cost the WU corpus +280K instructions. (6) Added Tormod's Crypt for scenarios; the Forge harness falls back to a card's only activated ability when Forge's older description doesn't match.
