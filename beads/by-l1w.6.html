It should just highlight the permanents it will tap when you drag it out, and then tap those permanents on play, or let you pick different ones, not give you a prompt."

In ui/src/game/decisions.ts, ui/src/game/choices.ts, ui/src/game/ChoiceOverlay.tsx, ui/src/game/Board.tsx, ui/src/screens/Game.tsx (and beryl_session protocol if the UI needs more, e.g. which permanents a payment plan taps):

  • The playable highlight marks exactly the hand cards with a legal candidate. Interchangeable objects collapse to one candidate (architecture §7.2: two untapped basics are one candidate), so the UI must map a collapsed candidate to every identical card. Both Plains light up.
  • Group a card's cast candidates (alternative and optional costs such as kicker) with its X into one cast step: one compact panel or drag gesture where the player sets kicker and X together, then the UI sends the engine's sequence of picks. The engine's candidate surface stays as is.
  • Never show "Payment N" options. Pick a default payment plan, highlight the permanents it will tap while the card is dragged or the cast is being set up, tap them on play, and let the player choose different permanents directly (click lands) instead of a prompt. Where plans must be shown, describe each by the permanents it taps.
