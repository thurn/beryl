Description

On the phone layout (just b3d check stress-255 --layout phone --size 393x759, also 375x647 and 430x932), 9 to 18 step-shown faults: permanents that fly onto a crowded battlefield row are 'seen in 5 of 25 frames ... mostly covered', and some 'moved 7 points in 0 ms of the 420 ms' of their fly step. The desktop run of stress-255 has none. Cause, as far as by-q44s.7.19 looked: the phone's back row (lands from the left, others at the right; beryl_scene src/table/phone.rs, layout.phone in data/beryl3d/space.json) holds only two or three cards before it scrolls (src/table/rows.rs lay_row, least_strip; src/table/scene/scroll.rs), so a card entering a scrolled row rests at the row's nearer end under the cards in view, and its flight starts and ends mostly covered, then snaps when the row's window moves. Fix it in placement or the fly verb (src/verbs/fly*.rs, docs/beryl-3d.md 'How a row scrolls': a card out of view is drawn while its own step's verb moves it) so the flight is visible, without changing the desktop's timelines (just b3d timeline-parity --fixtures). Tests: a phone-size test in beryl_scene tests/all/scroll.rs that flies a card into a full scrolled row and checks the step-shown rule (agent::steps) passes.
