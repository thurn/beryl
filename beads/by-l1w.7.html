Close reason

Attacks and blocks drafted on the table in any order, twins glow, sent as engine picks; readable block links

Description

Owner: "The UI for declaring blockers is impossible to understand, I legitimately am still not sure how you block or declare multiple blockers"; "The interface for declaring multiple attackers is really weird, it kind of seems like e.g. I need to pick tokens to attack with in a specific order? All eligible attackers do not get the blue highlight or respond to clicks together."; "The attacker/blocker arrows are also really hard to understand visually".

Engine combat declarations are sequential picks, and identical tokens collapse to one candidate (architecture §7.2, §11), which is why the UI forces an order. Rework in ui/src/game/choices.ts, ui/src/game/decisions.ts, ui/src/game/Board.tsx, ui/src/screens/Game.tsx:

  • Attackers: every eligible attacker highlighted at once; clicking any toggles it, in any order; an "attack with all" shortcut; a choose-defender step only when there is a choice; confirm sends the engine's picks in whatever order it needs.
  • Blockers: an obvious interaction (e.g. select a blocker, then click the attacker it blocks; or drag), multiple blockers on one attacker, changing your mind before confirming, a clear on-screen instruction, and a visible pairing of each blocker with its attacker (placement next to the attacker and/or a clear connector).
  • Combat pairing visuals readable when cards are close together.
