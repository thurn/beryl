What the player wants: after clicking Keep, the hand should visibly move down into its place on the table instead of appearing there. Acceptance: (1) on desktop, when the opening closes each kept card starts where the opening screen showed it and glides down to its place in the hand; (2) same on phone from the opening's rows; (3) no frame where the hand is in place before moving; (4) reduced motion still jumps.

Cause: under the opening the desktop table holds the hand 'away' (420 px below the stage; the phone hides it with CSS). When the opening closed, StageBoard relaid the table with no motion (ScriptStage.relayout), so the hand snapped into place in the same frame the opening vanished.

Arena (arena-video): Keep in 3SEgIYXJgdQ 13:33.95, 2VW6ZzmS8U4 6:56.25 and 14:07.75. The opening screen vanishes in one frame; the hand, still fanned mid-screen, drops into the hand at the bottom, ~40% down after one frame of 30, ~85% after two, at rest after four (~130 ms, ease-out).

Fix: Opening notes its hand cards' page rects as it unmounts (ui/src/stage/arrive.ts noteOpeningHand); useKeptHand (desktop StageBoard and PhoneStageBoard) converts them to stage poses in the commit that closes the opening and calls new ScriptStage.arrive, which glides each hand entry home over KEPT (130 ms easeOut; SNAP under reduced motion).
