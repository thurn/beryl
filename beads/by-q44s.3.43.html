Close reason

Row strips sized from perspective, pile copies pressed via top, mid-script rest state; row reachable faults gone

Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Interface checks' ('Reachable') and 'The table' (rows); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md (test-faults.json). Symptom: since cards became interactive, the 'reachable' check faults permanents in crowded battlefield rows: cards overlap so much that one keeps no uncovered square of audit.target_points (24 points) where a press reaches it. At 1600x900, lands of one name overlap about 27 points apart and creature rows about 25 points apart, and a tapped row (y about 450) covers part of the untapped row above it, e.g. creatures at x=165-317, y=269-451 and lands at x=140-290, y=572 and y=150. About 60 of 261 sampled reachable faults across the 1600x900 tests (recordings such as combat-attackers, crowded-max) are in rows. They are listed in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json. Code: rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/place.rs (battlefield_row, row_of) and src/table/rows.rs; the gap and row values under layout.desktop in data/beryl3d/space.json. Compare the web's row packing in ui/src/game/battlefield.ts (battlefieldPlacements). Fix: pack each row so every card keeps at least audit.target_points square uncovered, as the web's rows do, and so a tapped row does not cover the row behind it. Lower test-faults.json with just b3d test --update. Run: just b3d test b3d:combat-attackers; just b3d test --update; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

No reachable fault in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json is on a battlefield row card that a stack item does not cover; just b3d test --update lowers entries and raises none; the tests pass.
