Close reason

Last attacker's planeswalker line begins at the tap beat (6dd06095)

Description

Found in by-w8gv.9.14 (staging 3764b1bc). beryl_session's scripts (src/script) begin the attack line (+point attack) of the last attacker declared only after combat ends when that attacker attacks a planeswalker, instead of when its destination is chosen. With the last attacker at the player, the line begins at the tap beat as it should.

Repro: in rust/crates/beryl_cards/src/azorius/teferi_time_raveler.rs, scenario "combat damage removes Teferi while other attacks remain", change the 2/1's Action::answer(P0, DecisionKind::AttackTarget, Pick::DefendingPlayer) to Action::choose(P0, DecisionKind::AttackTarget, props::PLANESWALKER) (and its assertions: the planeswalker's loyalty 1, P1 at 20 life). Then SCRIPT_DUMP="Teferi, Time Raveler|combat damage removes Teferi while other attacks remain" cargo test -p beryl_session --test script_build dump -- --ignored --nocapture shows script 9 ending in set turn 3 you endOfCombat then +point attack #2 > #259: the line begins after the attack is over and never ends, and no set #2 ... attacking #259 precedes it. tests/script_spans.rs's a_combat_line_is_begun_once_and_never_swings states the rule it breaks.

The scenario was left with its last attacker at the player so it passes; the fix should add the broken state as a spec moment or fixture for the generic audits (AGENTS.md, Checks).

Acceptance criteria

With the last declared attacker at a planeswalker, its attack line begins in the beat its destination is chosen (or the tap beat, as for the player) and ends at most once; a scenario or fixture in that state is held by the span tests or the generic audits.

Derek Thurn2026-11-12

Handoff: cause was Builder::tap (and Builder::attack) only filling an attacker's combat record when it was None; the last attacker's record from the view before was already 'attacking' with its target pending, so the planeswalker only reached the table in settle (record() amends the tap step via touched), and the line began in close(). Fix: new rust/crates/beryl_session/src/script/attack.rs (tap, attack, declared moved out of build.rs, which was at 810 lines) — declared() replaces a record that says only 'attacking' (no planeswalker, no departed) with the view's when they differ. Also covers a non-tapping (vigilance) last attacker via attack(). Held by a new Teferi scenario 'combat damage removes Teferi while the other planeswalker is attacked' (spec moments in script-check) and tests/script_spans.rs a_combat_line_is_begun_once_and_never_swings (verified failing without the fix). Known gap, not fixed: a last attacker whose planeswalker leaves within the same script as its declaration gets no line at all, since the protocol hides a departed target (target_departed) and GameEvent::Attack carries no target.
