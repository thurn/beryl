Description

Not built by by-isv.6 (its comment: the view carries no reason a card cannot be played).

  • Repro: seed 101 vs Expert, turn 2: click Counterspell in hand with no spell to counter (later, Teferi, Time Raveler holds you to sorcery speed).
  • Actual: a 320 ms +/-7 px shake (SHAKE in CardLayer.tsx) and the usual inspector; nothing says whether mana, timing or targets is the cause (D1/020-g1-click-unplayable-150ms.png, D1/022-g1-click-unplayable-1200ms.png).
  • Expected: the inspector of a refused card names the reason, as an icon plus at most a few words (no mana, no target, sorcery speed and the card imposing it). The reason comes from the engine (beryl_session view), not a client guess, so flash and alternate costs are right.

Found by the by-isv.13 ui-review on staging 4c67340 (WU Control mirror both seats; seeds and opponents per item). Reproduce each item first with just ui-live (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md) or the named fixture; drop any that does not reproduce and say so in the close reason.

Design rules (epic by-isv): follow the skill's design philosophy (one gesture for the common case, the client does the bookkeeping, ask once in context, Arena first, show don't make them infer, few words) and add no standing words to the table. Every acceptance check is an agent's: just ui-test, just ui-shots, Rust tests or ui-live probes.

Acceptance criteria

  • beryl_session tests: the view gives a refusal reason for a hand card with no legal target, one lacking mana, and one held to sorcery speed by Teferi, Time Raveler.
  • A ui-test check: clicking a refused card shows its reason in the inspector.

Executor: engine gains beryl_engine::refusal(obs, table, slot) -> Option<Refusal> (Timing{by}, LandPlayed, Mana, Cost, Target). It reuses the same checks as castability: cast.rs castable() became refused() returning the first failed check (timing, mana, exile cost, targets, in that order), so the reason can never disagree with the offered candidates. Across a card's faces and casting options the reason reported is the one that got furthest (e.g. Force of Will with a blue card to exile but no spell says No target). Timing names the opponent's sorcery-speed limiter (Teferi) only when a face could otherwise be cast at instant speed. beryl_session: DecisionView.refused: Vec<Refused{slot, reason}> at priority (and halts). UI: a click on a refused hand card puts an icon + 1-3 words under the inspector's scan (No target / No mana / Can't pay / Land played / Sorcery speed[: Teferi]); hover alone shows nothing, and it clears when the pointer leaves. Tests: beryl_session tests/refused.rs (target, mana + land played, Teferi), ui-test 'clicking a refused card shows why in the inspector' (menu-choices: Counterspell, No target). Repro note: menu-choices' Counterspell reproduces the seed-101 no-target case; not re-run through ui-live. Pre-existing: ui-test 'Space during the opponent's turn shows the rest of it at once' fails on untouched base 190578a too (commented on by-5f0).
