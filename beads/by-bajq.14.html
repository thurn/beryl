Description

Symptom

BERYL_FUZZ_GAMES=1000 just fuzz 1000 stops in its engine fuzz (beryl_search tests/games.rs, random_games_keep_the_invariants) before the session fuzz runs:

crates/beryl_search/tests/games.rs:46: seed 613: a capacity ran out

The game played Outcome::Stalled(StallReason::Capacity): a fixed-capacity list in the Large state representation filled up, or a Full was not handled by switching from Small to Large (seed 613 is seed % 4 == 1, so it starts in Small). It happens on staging at b916e3ba1 without by-bajq.13's change. It reproduces in about 13 seconds with cd rust && BERYL_FUZZ_GAMES=614 CARGO_PROFILE_RELEASE_DEBUG_ASSERTIONS=true cargo test --release -p beryl_search --test games random_games_keep_the_invariants -- --include-ignored.

What to do

Play seed 613 alone, find which list reports Full and where it is not widened, then fix the capacity or the widening. Add a deterministic test of that state and a docs/bugs.md entry if it is a rules engine bug.

Files: rust/crates/beryl_search/tests/games.rs, the Full propagation in rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/ and rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/.

Acceptance criteria

Seed 613 plays to a win, a draw or a non-capacity stall; a deterministic test covers the state; BERYL_FUZZ_GAMES=1000 just fuzz 1000 passes its engine fuzz (or fails only on a new, filed seed).

Handoff: Seed 613's 'capacity ran out' was a Small game whose memo table (32 entries) filled: Worldgorger Dragon and a copy of it exiled 31 permanents, each linked by a Memo::ExiledWith. That is the designed point to move to Large, but the fuzz harness never widened. Both fixes landed on staging from other work while I was working: fc10cea9e (common::widening::play_widening in the fuzz) and 2fef7d987 (the together.rs fix, a Worldgorger scenario and a bugs.md entry). Verified: BERYL_FUZZ_GAMES=1000 just fuzz 1000 passes on staging 89e48b1b3 plus this test (0 of 1000 games stalled, session fuzz ok).
