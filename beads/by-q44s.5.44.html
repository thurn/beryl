Description

In just b3d test b3d:opponent-turn/you/1600x900 (and 1920x1080), the "every step shown" check fails on step:16.0.1, the second card of an opponent's two-card draw (fly, case "opponent draw"): card #261 is seen in only 6 of 14 frames, mostly covered, while it leaves the opponent's library at the top right of the screen. Run just b3d tree opponent-turn --seat you --size 1600x900 --at 5161ms to see it. The fault became visible when the client started counting its own prompt marks in Client::rested, which moves each recorded reply 250 ms later and so moves which frames the check samples. The flight itself did not change. It is listed in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json under this bead.

Look at how verbs/fly.rs starts a draw from a pile (table/pile.rs, the library's top two cards and its side record) and why the drawn card stays under the pile's top card for its first frames. The web interface's draw (ui/src/stage/tracks.ts) lifts the card off the pile before it travels. Done when the entry is removed by just b3d test --update.
