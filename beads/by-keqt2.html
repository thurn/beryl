Guard builds against low disk while a backup snapshot pins churn

  • Description
  • Acceptance criteria
  • Related1
  • Comments1
  • Changelog

Description

Symptom: on 2026-11-15, jarvis's Data volume hit 100% and enact push fast checks failed with ENOSPC writing rust/beryl3d/target (incident by-g2xcc). Why: an Arq backup takes an APFS snapshot of the Data volume and holds it for hours. While it runs, every block that cargo rewrites or deletes in the ~10 worktree slots and in $BERYL_CACHE stays allocated, so deleting caches frees almost nothing. Fix: have tools/beryl_tools/cargo_pool.py (and the b3d sweep in tools/) check free space before starting a build, and wait or prune (warm.prune, caches.tidy_target) when it is below a threshold, for example 40 GiB. Also cap how many worktree slots keep a full rust/beryl3d/target.

Acceptance criteria

A build started with less than the threshold free waits or prunes instead of hitting ENOSPC, covered by a unit test that fakes shutil.disk_usage.
