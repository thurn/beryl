Close reason

Low-disk prune-caches now prunes each idle bisection tree's rust/target (incremental, tauri, phone targets, then warm.prune's budget), skipping a tree whose bisection lock is held and never touching the checkout. Tests in tools/tests/test_caches.py cover low-space, room, lock-held and dry-run. Landed in a2622936 on staging.

Description

Cygnus slow CI hit rustc LLVM output errors because its 120 GiB Dev Drive filled. The registered bisection worktree retained 50.8 GiB under rust/target, including 18.5 GiB of debug incremental artifacts. The normal low-disk cleanup in tools/src/beryl_tools/caches.py only prunes the shared target, and the retained bisection worktree in tools/src/beryl_tools/bisect.py bypasses warm.save. See incident by-nh81.

Acceptance criteria

Reclaim rebuildable bisection artifacts under disk pressure without touching a live bisection or its checkout. Cover the low-space and lock-held paths in the Python cache or bisection tests.
