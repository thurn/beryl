Close reason

TURN_DISCOUNT 0.98 default in LongSearch (f36ae31, master 5aa953b): decided-position seed agreement 60% -> 78%, contested unchanged. beryl_cli agreement measures it.

Description

Derek's playtesting: the deep agents play randomly when far ahead or far behind. In run-003, 45% of pimc18/omniscient/rollout/tuned@10M's searched decisions (1,866 of 4,118) are in positions it rates above 0.95 or below 0.05, and in 89% of those the runner-up is within 0.01 of the best: every move wins (or loses) in nearly every rollout, so noise picks the move.

Make it default behavior in beryl_search::LongSearch (not a family option, per Derek): a finished game scores for the winner by how soon it ends, discounting each turn past the tree's root, so that a decided position still has a best move: close the game out when ahead, prolong it when behind. Evaluations at cut and eval leaves are discounted alike so the scales agree. Mcts (the Milestone 1 agents) is unchanged.

Measure before and after with the same positions and seeds: how often two searches from different seeds agree on the move, in decided and in contested positions from run-003's logs. Note: every LongSearch family plays differently after this, so run-003 cannot adopt it (its --upgrade check refuses); a new arena run is needed to measure it there.

Notes

2026-11-07 results (beryl_cli agreement, run-003, pimc18/omniscient/rollout/tuned@10M, 60 decided + 60 contested positions, two seeds each at 10M): seeds agree on decided positions 60.0% undiscounted, 78.3% at 0.98, 80.0% at 0.95; contested 78.3%, 76.7%, 90.0%; chance ~33%. Choosing the best mean among well-visited moves instead of the most visited added ~3 points (noise) and was dropped. Chose 0.98: the stronger discount trades more of a close game's chances for speed. Also fixed: serde_json float_roundtrip in beryl_cli, since discounted rewards exposed an ulp drift in resumed longthink runs (caught by stops_cost_nothing_but_time).
