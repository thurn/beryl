Description

Card

Pithing Needle: "As this artifact enters, choose a card name. Activated abilities of sources with the chosen name can't be activated unless they're mana abilities."

What it needs

Plan: Designs 6, "Prohibitions".

  • Governed gains activating the abilities of the sources a filter accepts, with an exception for mana abilities; When gains "never" (When::Never), a restriction, which beats any permission (CR 101.2).
  • The filter is Filter::ChosenName (by-bajq.3.3), read for the Needle: a rule change is numbered once for the game, and what it says depends on the memo of each permanent that makes it. rule_change::maker finds the makers only once the kind is in force, and the check then reads each maker's chosen name.
  • A source is named by its copiable values: a copy of the named card is stopped, a face-down permanent has no name, and a card in a graveyard or hand with an activated ability (none here yet) is a source too.
  • Loyalty abilities are activated abilities. Abilities already on the stack are not affected.
  • Naming "another card" is legal and stops nothing in this game.

Scenarios

Needle naming Mother of Runes; naming a planeswalker; naming a land with a mana ability and another ability (Strip Mine: the mana ability works, the other does not); a token copy of the named creature made by Fractured Identity; naming "another card".
