Close reason

Failed steps rerun once, narrowed to named checks; flaky: lines on a pass; build failures not rerun (79ac3430)

Description

Rerun a failed check once before a run fails (plan K21), with no dependency on the run log, so one flaky check stops failing slow CI and filing Steward beads as early as possible. Split out of by-w8gv.20.3, which keeps verdict inference and quarantine and later logs what this prints.

  • In tools/src/beryl_tools/checks.py (run_lane, Steps): when a step fails, rerun it once before the lane is marked failed, narrowed to the checks that failed where the step can name them. ui-test: the titles on its FAILED: <title> lines, passed as title filters (shots.py ui_test, args.only). shots: the failing shot names (ui_shots, args.only). rust tests: nextest's failed tests, as a filter expression in rust_tests_step. oracle: the failing card names. Any other step reruns whole.
  • If the rerun passes, the step and lane pass and the output names each check on a line starting flaky: , with the lane, so by-w8gv.20.3 can log it and people can grep .git/enact/ci.log for it meanwhile. If it fails again, the failure stands and the output shows both runs.
  • Never rerun a step killed at the deadline or one that failed to build or start (a compile error, clippy, fmt, tsc, a UiError): those are not flakes. One rerun only.
  • The rerun's seconds count toward the budget as any step's do. Both by check and by ci do this.
  • pytest in tools/tests with a fake step that fails once then passes, one that fails twice, and one that names its failed checks.

Acceptance criteria

A step that fails once then passes passes its lane with a flaky: line; one that fails twice fails; a build failure is not rerun; just check and just ci stay within 60 s and 300 s.

Handoff: commit 79ac3430. checks.py: Lane steps are now Step(name, cmd, cwd, failed); Lane.step(..., rerun=<Failed>) opts a step into one rerun. run_lane's attempt() reruns a failed step with cmd + the names its parser returns (failed_tests / failed_shots / failed_titles / failed_cards; whole() or an empty list reruns it whole). Never rerun: a deadline kill (code None), exit CANNOT_RUN=125 (rust-tests build failure, ui-shots/ui-test UiError or record_fixtures failure, smoke's tauri build, oracle before scenarios dumped incl. not built at pin), and steps with no rerun (just, markdown, black, pyre, build-paths, fmt, clippy, prettier, tsc, card-names, mobile-check). Outcome.flaky holds the checks that passed on rerun (the step name for a whole rerun); run_lanes prints 'flaky: <lane> <check>' after the per-lane times — by-w8gv.20.3 can log from Outcome.flaky. rust_tests_step(*only) takes '<binary> <test>' names, builds a binary_id/test(=) filterset, skips allocations; nextest now runs --no-fail-fast so a narrowed rerun cannot skip tests fail-fast cancelled. ui-test narrows only when no 'page errors:'/'sound:' lines. Gotcha for later: oracles.apply_gaps counts every EXPORTER_GAPS entry as stale under a card filter; harmless while EXPORTER_GAPS is empty.
