Motion is Beryl's own: exception 8; by motion holds the stage to each verb's own values with Arena's beside, no waivers (e460b75d)

Description

The user's decision (2026-11-11), which changes K22 and K27 for motion: "it kind of seems like we are following the duration of the arena animations without looking at anything else about what makes those animations work? animations are not only about duration" and "independent aesthetic judgement is needed here, our cards do not work the same way".

Beryl's cards are not Arena's: full cards that tap (exception 2), our own skin, frames and sizes, and different distances on the table. Copying Arena's numbers onto them does not reproduce what makes Arena's motion work, and holding a verb to Arena's duration within 15% made the stage look faithful while straight paths, missing effects and jostling batches passed every check.

The rule after this bead

  • Arena stays the reference for what a motion communicates and when: what changed, who did it, where it went, what happens in what order, what holds still while it plays, where the eye is led. Its flow, sequencing and wording stay Arena's (K22 is unchanged for those).
  • How a motion looks on Beryl's cards (path, easing, duration, depth, rotation, effects, how batches and neighbours move) is designed for Beryl, by aesthetic judgement, starting from Arena's clip and numbers (data/arena-motion.json) and its prose description of each verb, never cloning them.
  • A verb is done when an independent motion review passes it (by the reviewer that the motion-review bead builds), not when its numbers fall inside Arena's range.

Do

  1. data/ui-conventions.json: add a numbered exception, "Motion is Beryl's own", saying the above with the user's words, and regenerate docs/ui-conventions.md (just conventions). Rewrite AGENTS.md's UI section to match: Arena for flow, timing of events relative to one another, layout and wording; motion's look designed for Beryl's cards and reviewed independently. Keep the paragraph short.
  2. docs/project-50k.md, K22 and K27: note the change and the date in one or two sentences each (the Archivist folds it in later).
  3. tools/src/beryl_tools/motion.py and tests/test_motion.py: the values check and the stepped capture stop holding verbs to Arena's range. They hold each verb to Beryl's own committed values in data/ui-conventions.json (a regression guard: the stepped capture's measures of the stage against the values the verb declares, within the same frame-or-share tolerance), and print Arena's summary beside each as reference with no verdict. Drop waivers (nothing to waive) and keep data/arena-motion.json as the reference data it is. The run-log ids motion:<verb>/<layout> stay.
  4. Comment on by-w8gv.21.37 (stack card 3.4x, Arena 1.9x): it is judged by the motion review on Beryl's merits, not by Arena's ratio.

Files: data/ui-conventions.json, tools/src/beryl_tools/conventions.py, tools/src/beryl_tools/motion.py, tools/tests/test_motion.py, AGENTS.md, docs/project-50k.md. Read by-w8gv.21.18 and by-w8gv.21.19's handoffs first.
