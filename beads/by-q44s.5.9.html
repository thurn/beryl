Decisions and deviations: (1) The highlighted row of a list or menu is the focused element, so Return reuses focused-press and the row is drawn by its FOCUSED state (a gold tint, .table-menu-row[data-active]). Layer::navigate (layout/menu.rs) handles up/down (wrapping), right/Return to open a submenu, left to close it, and Return to press; it returns Navigation::{Ignored,Moved,Press} so a Layer-level test can see the Press::Menu the client would send (the client still has no arm for Press::Menu/Dismiss: no screen opens a menu yet). (2) Placement needed three new Anchor variants: Menu(point) (kept flat.menu_edge=8 points inside the window, flipping as Menu.tsx place() does), Submenu (beside the parent menu, left when no room right, level with its row) and Tip. Node gained placed/shown; Layer gained submenu/pointed/tip. Layer::hit now finds anchored descendants outside their ancestors' boxes (a submenu sits outside its menu). (3) MenuRow gained submenu: Vec<MenuRow> and MenuRow::new; rows of submenus are numbered after all of the menu's own rows. MenuRow.key holds the hint's words, written by the new element::describe (shortcuts.ts describe), because menu() does not know the platform. (4) tooltip(text, of) keeps the contract stub's naming, 'tooltip <of>'; the layer finds the element named <of> under the pointer, waits flat.tooltip_delay=500 ms (space.json, as the bead said) counted from the next frame's now, then shows it centred above (or below) with flat.tooltip_gap=8. Client::frame wakes at the due time. The 120 ms fade of .tip is not drawn. (5) A press outside an open context menu, or Escape, sends Press::Dismiss(Dismiss::Menu). (6) Not built: the menu header's 'whose' line and group headings (MenuGroup has none yet), the sheet presentation (milestone 4), menu max-height scrolling. (7) Four new type styles (MENU, MENU_TITLE, MENU_HINT, TIP): beryl_assets' latin_glyphs_cover_every_type_style now counts type_style::ALL instead of a literal 3. (8) Gate: on staging before this change, just b3d gate already failed (stress-255/1024 total_records over by 101, stress-verbs under); identical numbers with and without this change.
