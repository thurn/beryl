Close reason

Force of Will (alternative cost; 3 mana under Trinisphere, agreeing with XMage and Forge) and Gurmag Angler (delve after the cost floor). Count 395,691,316 -> 396,023,701 (+0.08%). Oracle delve support filed as by-bajq.7.7

Description

Cards

  • Force of Will: "You may pay 1 life and exile a blue card from your hand rather than pay this spell's mana cost. Counter target spell."
  • Gurmag Angler, 5/5: "Delve (Each card you exile from your graveyard while casting this spell pays for {1}.)"

What they need

  • Force of Will's alternative cost is a casting option with two parts, life and the exile of a card from hand that a filter accepts. Subtlety's evoke cost already exiles a card of a color from hand: reuse its cost part. The color is a word (group 4): under Painter's Servant with blue chosen, every card in hand is blue.
  • Delve (CR 702.66): cards exiled from the graveyard pay for generic mana as the cost is paid (CR 601.2h), after the total cost is locked in. So delve can pay for the mana a floor adds, and never for colored mana. The payment decision offers it as a resource whose use is a real choice (which cards leave the graveyard matters to Tarmogoyf and to escape): follow D7's rule for payments that consume resources, and let the player choose the cards.

Scenarios

Force of Will for its alternative cost under Trinisphere costs three mana as well as the life and the card; Force of Will for mana under Thalia; Force of Will exiling a card that is blue only because of Painter's Servant (an interaction scenario); Gurmag Angler with seven cards in the graveyard, with three, and under Trinisphere with a full graveyard.

Handoff: Force of Will is CastingOption::Alternative {cost, parts} (beryl_card cost.rs; CastingKind::Alternative), with CostPart::Life now payable on spells (kernel payable.rs pays_for_spell, rules cast/parts.rs spell_payable and pay). Delve is Keywords::DELVE behind Capability::Delve. In beryl_rules cast/parts.rs it is a static DELVE part (Exile from graveyard, Measure::ForGeneric(u8::MAX)) addressed by DELVE_PART = u8::MAX in part_at. steps.rs begin_payment runs parts::pay_substitutes for spells when the table can delve. total_cost subtracts Casting::substituted for spells too. legal.rs moved restricted mana and delve into one cold pays_otherwise. Delve's question is DecisionKind::ChooseUpTo with Purpose::Delve (Shape::Pick, wording 'Delve', 'Submit N'), not the waterbend ManaPayment: the session's Pay prompt (prompt/pay/taps.rs) reads every offered object as a permanent and panicked on a graveyard card. Cards are offered bottom-up (graveyard_from), the chosen ones stay in the graveyard until end_substitutes exiles them together, and no mana activations are offered among the picks. Known gap: x_options does not count delve, which matters only for an X spell with delve, and no such card exists. Oracles: Forge passes all Force of Will scenarios (one unsupported: the exile choice); XMage passes all 7. Gurmag Angler is unsupported in XMage (delve is a special action there) and partly in Forge (the delve choice); follow-up by-bajq.7.7. Generator: a CARD_TO_EXILE witness (derive/witness.rs) adds Timetwister to the hand for a card whose casting option exiles from hand. Count gate: 395,691,316 on 36cf4c73 vs 396,023,701 with the change (+0.08%); rollouts unchanged; State size unchanged. Rulings: Force of Will's 'mana value is always 5' and Gurmag Angler's 'doesn't change mana value' are not shown, because no implemented card reads a spell's mana value on the stack.
