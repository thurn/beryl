Enhanced lighting: key, fill, computed ambient, moving sheen; shadow scales the lit color (4aa388d1d, 1f9984937)

Description

Read first: the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md; docs/beryl-3d.md 'The Enhanced tier' (per-pixel lighting) and 'The Baseline tier' (colors are blended as stored).

Cards and the table lit by the key light and a soft fill, with a faint sheen that moves across a card's face as it tilts. Edit only src/enhanced/light.rs and src/enhanced/light.wgsl in beryl_render, the tests named below, and data/beryl3d. The Baseline shader does not change: the_baseline_shader_does_not_change keeps passing.

What exists: in light.wgsl, shade_card_lit(face, mesh_uv, tint, params, normal, view, shadow) and shade_table_lit(tint, normal, view, shadow), stubs returning the Baseline shade_card and shade_table colors times shadow; light::lighting(frame) in light.rs filling Lit::key, eye and fill; normal is the record's transform's third column and view the direction from the point to Lit::eye. The shadow map bead supplies shadow::key_direction(); until it lands, keep the stub's straight-down key.

Build:

  • Add enhanced.light to data/beryl3d/space.json: ambient: 0.62, key: 0.38, fill: 0.12 (from the side opposite the key, at 30 degrees above the table), sheen: 0.10, sheen_power: 24.0. The values are chosen so that a card lying flat and lit by the key alone, unshadowed, is drawn within 3/255 of its Baseline color: ambient + key * dot(up, key_direction) == 1 for the key direction the shadow map uses. Compute ambient from that rule in lighting rather than storing a number that can drift from it, and state that in the data's comment key.
  • lighting(frame): key from shadow::key_direction(), fill from the values above, eye from the inverse of frame.view_projection.
  • shade_card_lit: the Baseline color times ambient + key * max(dot(n, key), 0) * shadow + fill * max(dot(n, fill_direction), 0), plus the sheen, a white highlight of sheen * pow(max(dot(n, half), 0), sheen_power) * shadow where half is the normalized sum of the key direction and view, added after the multiply and kept under the face's dissolve and opacity as the Baseline branch keeps its color. n is the face's normal, flipped for the back (the Baseline branch's front_facing rule). shade_table_lit the same without the sheen.
  • Colors stay sRGB values, as stored, as in the Baseline tier.

Tests, in beryl_render/tests/all/light.rs, single pixels of hand-built frames in Enhanced:

  • a card lying flat, unshadowed, is within 3/255 of its Baseline color at its centre;
  • the same card tilted 25 degrees toward the key light is brighter at its centre than tilted 25 degrees away;
  • the sheen moves: on a card tilted so that the highlight is on its face, the brightest of nine pixels sampled down its middle is at a different one of them when the tilt changes by 10 degrees;
  • a card's back is lit by the same rule as its face. Report the costs just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_render prints for card_lit and table_lit in a comment, and lower their entries under materials in data/beryl3d/gates.json to them.

Must not build: shadows, bloom, any change to shader.wgsl.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_render; just b3d png verbs-zones --at 1.5s --tier enhanced (look at it and say in a comment what you saw); just b3d lane.

Beads named above: the contract bead (the tier input and the check) is by-q44s.11.2; the renderer skeleton bead is by-q44s.11.3; the shadow map bead by-q44s.11.4; the lighting bead by-q44s.11.5; bloom by-q44s.11.6; particles by-q44s.11.7; the tier choice bead by-q44s.11.8; the settings row by-q44s.11.9; the limits bead by-q44s.11.10; the settings screen bead by-q44s.5.27. Every bead of this milestone depends on by-q44s.4, the review gate before it.

Derek Thurn2026-11-17

Handoff: Enhanced lighting landed in 4aa388d1d (feat) and 1f9984937 (fix after rebasing onto the shadow map).

Files: beryl_render src/enhanced/light.rs (lighting(): key from shadow::key_direction(), fill from the opposite side at enhanced.light.fill_elevation degrees, ambient computed), light.wgsl (diffuse(), shade_card_lit with the sheen, shade_table_lit), comments only in mod.rs and entry.wgsl for Lit; data/beryl3d/space.json enhanced.light; tests/all/light.rs (4 tests); enhanced.rs and shadow.rs adjusted.

Deviations from the bead, with reasons:

  • Packing: Lit has no free slots, so key.xyz = key direction * strength with ambient in key.w, fill.xyz = fill direction * strength with sheen_power in fill.w, and sheen strength in eye.w. Lit stays 112 bytes. The comments in mod.rs and entry.wgsl were updated to match (comment-only edits outside the two named files).
  • ambient is not stored in space.json. It is computed in lighting() as 1 - key*key.z - fill*fill.z: the fill also lights a flat card (0.12 * sin 30 = 0.06), so the rule has to include it or a flat card would be 6% brighter. With the current key it comes to about 0.60. space.json values must be numbers (the build script panics on strings), so the rule is documented in light.rs rather than in a JSON comment key.
  • The shadow scales the whole lit color, not only the key term. Scaling only the key would darken a full shadow by about 0.19, but the shadow map bead's tests (and Baseline parity) need enhanced.shadow.darkness = 0.55.
  • The sheen (0.1 white at peak, power 24) is broad. The desktop camera sits near where flat cards mirror the key light, so flat battlefield cards gain up to about 25/255 of even white. twenty_cards_draw_in_enhanced_as_in_baseline_away_from_edges now holds the table points exact and card centres to Baseline plus at most an even sheen. the_flat_layer_is_drawn_after_the_resolve puts its panel over a table-material rectangle. a_card_lying_flat_does_not_shadow_its_own_face checks that the face is not darker and at most the sheen brighter. A reviewer might want a narrower or weaker sheen; the values are the bead's.
  • Shader costs went up, not down (the stubs did no lighting): card_lit 280 expressions, table_lit 224 (5 samples each), which are the gate values now.

Looked at just b3d png verbs-zones --at 1.5s --tier enhanced next to Baseline: the table is unchanged; flat cards are slightly lighter from the sheen; hand cards tilted toward the camera are lighter; faint key shadows lie along the near edges of piles and the Snapcaster stack; no positions differ.

Ran: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_render (47 passed), just b3d clippy, just b3d lane (passed via enact push's fast check).
