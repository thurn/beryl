Found and fixed (3d0f951):

  1. Layout bug: a blocker was moved after layout to stand opposite its attacker, on top of whatever stood there. In combat-blockers, Thundertrap Trainer covered Thieving Skydiver completely (130 px of a ~190 px card), so a creature you might still need to see or click disappeared. The defending row is now laid out with the blockers' wanted positions and settled (layout.ts settle()), so the rest of the row makes room; the post-hoc facing() is gone. New ui-test check (fixture combat-blockers) fails on the old layout and passes now.
  2. abilityText counted only paragraphs starting When/Whenever/At, but the engine lists keyword triggers (offspring, impending's countdown) first. Thundertrap Trainer's offspring trigger on the stack showed its look-at-the-top-four text, and Overlord's countdown showed the Insect text. Now a paragraph whose reminder text holds a trigger counts too; checked against every deck card's text.

Left alone: Game.tsx's plan queue and choices.ts plans match the engine's question order (checked against EffectCtx::scry, choose_up_to); during combat a squeezed attacking row with combat z still nearly hides a noncombatant between two attackers (Mana Crypt in combat-blockers): that is row spacing, left to pixel fidelity by-b0q.3.18, where the rows are already noted as too close.
