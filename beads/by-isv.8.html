Close reason

Menus draw mana/loyalty symbols and light the ability's paragraph; MDFC lands play/pay by land face; target prompts leave on-table namesakes to the board; pay-life defaults by need over the life orb; Take Nothing secondary. Dropped: Thundertrap trigger text (already fixed by a85df33).

Description

  • Raw costs in menus: ability menus show costs as raw text: "{T}, Sacrifice this", "+1 / −3", and literal braces ("with impending 4, {2}{W}{W}"). Render costs with the mana and loyalty symbols. Identify each ability by highlighting its paragraph on the card while its option is hovered, rather than adding effect text.
  • Double-faced lands: "Play Sink into Stupor" plays Soporific Springs, and the pay-life prompt then shows the instant face. The option and prompt should show the face that is played.
  • Same-named choices: prompts list identical names without side or state: two "Jace, Vryn's Prodigy" (yours and theirs), two "Insect Token" (tapped and untapped), two "Plains". Pick on the board where possible; otherwise show thumbnails that carry side and state.
  • Trigger text: Thundertrap Trainer's enters trigger shows its Offspring reminder paragraph on the stack; re-check after a85df33.
  • Pay life: the prompt defaults to paying even when entering tapped costs nothing this turn. Default by context, and point at the life orb rather than printing the total.
  • "Take Nothing": it is the primary button even when an eligible card is worth taking.

Files: ui/src/game/ChoiceOverlay.tsx, ui/src/game/choices.ts, ui/src/game/CastStep.tsx, ui/src/components/Mana.tsx, ui/src/components/Stack.tsx.

Before changing anything, reproduce each item on current staging. Use just ui-live, as the ui-review skill describes (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md), or a fixture. The findings come from reviews of the build before the 2026-11-05 UI fixes, so some may already be fixed. Drop those, and list them in the close reason.

Design rules (see the epic): follow the skill's design philosophy, and add no standing words to the table. What a player needs at a glance is shown as icons, badges, numbers, colour or position. Everything else goes behind disclosure: hover, the inspector, a pile's browser, or the game log.

Acceptance criteria

  • Shots show a planeswalker's menu with loyalty symbols and highlighted paragraphs, and a double-faced land option showing the land face.
  • A just ui-test check: a target prompt with two same-named objects is answered on the board, or shows them apart by side.
  • No option label contains a literal {.
