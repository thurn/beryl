Close reason

Staging carries ordered undo blockers over MemoryLink and converts them to the local IPC reply.

Description

An undo refusal delivered by local table_act carries blocked_by, which ui/src/table/tableLog.ts consumes to highlight the blocking rows. The linked RefusalReason in rust/crates/beryl_link/src/wire.rs is fieldless, and host.rs / guest.rs only exchange that reason, so a guest would lose the blocker list even with the same table_act IPC type. Before integrating host and guest, give the independent link envelope a typed refusal payload that preserves the ordered entry numbers of an Invalid refusal. Keep permission and stale distinct. Provide one conversion at the session boundary so both local and linked replies keep the existing beryl_session::ipc::TableActReply wire shape for the UI. Keep beryl_link independent of table and session, and make the change visible to the wire fingerprint used by by-bmdk.5.3.

Acceptance criteria

A deterministic MemoryLink test sends a refusal with two blocking entry numbers through the real host and guest loop and asserts exact numbers and ordering. The session mapping yields the same TableActReply as a local invalid action. Permission and stale still round-trip. No update is emitted for a refusal.
