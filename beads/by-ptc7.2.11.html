Description

Circle of Protection: Red omits a red card referred to by an ability on the stack. CR 609.7a allows the player to choose such a source even when it cannot deal damage. The 2004-10-04 Circle ruling c066d7fe22be records this case. Reproduce in the Circle card file with a scenario called Circle of Protection: Red can choose a Bolt referred to by a trigger. On P1 turn 2 precombat main, P1 controls a Hill Giant and two Islands, has Snapcaster Mage in hand and Lightning Bolt in the graveyard. P0 controls the Circle and a Plains. P1 casts and resolves Snapcaster, then passes with its trigger targeting the only graveyard card. P0 activates the Circle in response. Beryl automatically chooses the Hill Giant and moves on to attackers. The Circle controller should instead be able to choose either the Hill Giant or the Lightning Bolt. In rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/damage.rs, sources only searches battlefield and stack zones. Settling the rule also needs to distinguish original source identities and preserve a permanent spell shield after it resolves (CR 400.7c). Review rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/effects/prevention.rs, stack references and the damage replacement evaluator.

Acceptance criteria

Run the Snapcaster Mage scenario in Beryl and the two oracles. Include a scenario where the Circle chooses a red permanent spell and shields the permanent it becomes. Update the Circle ledger entry and docs/bugs.md, citing the regression guard. Run the count gate before and after engine changes.
