Close reason

Long press opens the table menu, a bottom sheet with the card on phones; on staging at b31eb0b7f

Description

Beryl 3D's manual table opens a card's, pile's, life total's or the table's context menu on a right click only (beryl_scene src/client/manual.rs open_table_menu, called from src/client/input.rs point). The web opens it on a long press too, and on a phone as a bottom sheet with the full card above the actions (data/ui-conventions.json, the manual table context-menu rule near line 963; ui/src/table/drag.ts longPress; ui/src/table/TableMenu.tsx). Build the long press for touch: a touch held still on a target past a hold time opens the menu (on the phone layout as the bottom sheet), and a touch that moves first does not. The hold time must be a data value the session already reads, or one added by the bead allowed to change data/ui-conventions.json; look for the web's value in ui/src/table/drag.ts first. Read src/manual/menus.rs (OpenMenu, open) and src/screens/table.rs (where the menu is shown).

Acceptance criteria

A test in beryl_scene tests/all/manual.rs holds a touch on a permanent past the hold time and finds its menu; a touch that moves first opens none; just b3d lane passes.

Handoff: beryl_scene src/client/manual.rs: Hold (follow_hold on each pointer event, hold_table_menu at the start of Client::frame, Manual::hold_due feeds Wake). The hold time is the new space.json pointer.long_press_ms = 500, the web's LONG_PRESS_DELAY in ui/src/stage/pointer.ts, which is a TS constant and not data. A move of DRAG_THRESHOLD_POINTS cancels the hold. Opening the menu calls the new Pointer::cancel and clears pressed/viewing.down, so lifting the finger clicks nothing. src/screens/table.rs bottom_sheet: on Window::phone() (new method, which replaces private copies in hud/steps.rs and inspect/inspector.rs) the menu is Anchor::Menu'd at the bottom-left so it stands on the bottom edge, full width up to flat.sheet.max_width 430, with max_height and Overflow::Scroll, and a 144-point Full face of a face-up card first (sheet_face is requested with want_full in client/screen.rs). Gap: submenus still open beside the sheet; filed as by-q44s.5.51. Tests: tests/all/manual.rs, three new long press tests (the phone test uses an Island because the stack spell covers both Serra Angels at 393x759).
