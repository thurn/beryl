Description

The docs/ui_mockups images are generated references, not a pixel spec: they are not consistent with one another, and earlier pixel-matching work fitted code to each one separately. This bead removes one such fit. Prefer one rule that holds in every state over matching any single mockup. just ui-compare verdicts for the affected regions may get worse; that is expected, so do not tune values back toward the mockups.

In ui/src/game/ChoiceOverlay.tsx, HELD = { width: 297, height: 358 } gives a card held mid-drag in the choice tray (scry, put-back) an aspect of 0.83, against 0.716 (ASPECT.full) for every other card. Picking a card up makes it wider and shorter, copied from scry.png's mid-drag frame. A held card should keep the full frame's aspect: scale it up uniformly (a width, or a scale factor) and keep the tilt.

Acceptance criteria

A held card in the choice tray has ASPECT.full's aspect. just check and just ci pass, with no new constants tuned to a single mockup.
