Close reason

Small holds 3 copy exceptions: the copies corpus has no capacity stall (was 1 of 24); State<Small> 20,184; count gate +0.14%, passing; copies ratio 1.000

Description

The present-cost corpus of the copies family (by-bajq.6.5, just count-gate --family copies) has 1 of 24 rollouts ending in Stalled(Capacity): rollout 2, turn 20, after 63 steps, with 23 permanents. The list that filled is the Additions list (beryl_kernel/src/additions.rs:131, AdditionList::push), which holds 2 entries in Small (layout.rs:208) and 8 in Large. The stalled rollout lost about 87 of the corpus's roughly 3,600 steps, around 2.4%, which Large would have played. That is over the one-in-a-hundred bar. Decide whether Small needs more room (State<Small> must stay within 20,480 bytes) or whether the overflow should move the game to Large instead of stalling it. Re-measure with just count-gate --family copies and family_steps.

Acceptance criteria

The copies corpus has no rollout ending in Stalled(Capacity), or the share of steps in Large is recorded under 1 in 100. State<Small> within 20,480 bytes; just count-gate holds with no bump.

Derek Thurn2026-11-15

Decision: Small's Additions list goes from 2 to 3 entries rather than making the bench rollout widen to Large. Instrumenting the overflow showed the one stall was a real copy of a copy of a copy (additions [card 26, card 25] plus card 25 again: a Phantasmal Image copying a Phantasmal Image that copied a Quicksilver Gargantuan copy), not a loop. Real games already widen to Large on Full (Widening in beryl_search, Boards in beryl_session), so only the Small-only bench stalls. Cost: State<Small> 20,128 -> 20,168 (+40, Copiable 6 -> 8 bytes). This is within by-bajq.6.1's +72 allotment (it landed +32), so the 20,192 bound in beryl_kernel's lib.rs still holds. The copies family isn't on staging yet (by-bajq.6.5 is in progress), so I measured with its commit a89489430 cherry-picked locally and did not push it. Separate problem filed as by-bajq.6.11: Thespian's Stage targeting itself adds its exception again each time, without bound.
