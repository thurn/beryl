Description

The Cavern of Souls ruling 7288823ce2c0 includes spending its colored mana solely on kicker while a spell is cast without paying its mana cost. Malcolm, Alluring Scoundrel with three chorus counters can attack and discard Thieving Skydiver on the fourth hit, with Cavern on Merfolk as the only mana source. Beryl offers Pass plus plain and kicked casts, but beryl_scenario/src/run.rs treats Action::Cast as a Priority answer, and Pick::Card cannot distinguish the two CastOffer options. Extend scripting and oracle exporters to distinguish offered casts by casting options.

Acceptance criteria

Run a Cavern scenario that selects the kicked free cast, uses Cavern's colored mana and proves Counterspell cannot counter it. Record the scenario under both dates for 7288823ce2c0 and check it with Beryl and both oracles.
