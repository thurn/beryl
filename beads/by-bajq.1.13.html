Close reason

Description

Bloodghast (escalated in data/card-escalations.json, by-bajq.1.10) needs two entries beryl_card lacks:

  1. An instruction that returns a card from a graveyard to the battlefield under its owner's control. Effect has only ReturnToHand and ReturnTransformed (rust/crates/beryl_card/src/effect/mod.rs). by-bajq.3.14 adds the instruction that returns a card from exile to the battlefield under its owner's control, and this bead runs after it. Extend that instruction so it can take a card from a graveyard (for example, by letting it name the zone or accept any Objects that is a card outside the battlefield). Do not add a second effect with the same meaning. The returned card enters through the same entering path, so replacement effects of entering (Containment Priest, by-bajq.3.5) apply to it.
  2. A rule that a creature can't block (CR 509.1a). RuleChange and Governed in continuous.rs cover only casting spells and loyalty abilities. Add a restriction that combat.rs reads when it lists the legal blockers. Write it as a general restriction ("[objects] can't block"), not as a keyword special to one card.

The graveyard trigger itself already exists: Ability::in_graveyard with a Trigger::of(TriggerEvent::Enters, Subject::A(land you control)).

Prove both with unit tests in beryl_rules/src/tests/ using test-only definitions. Do not write Bloodghast here: by-bajq.1.10, reopened, is that card's bead and runs after this one.

Files: beryl_card/src/{effect/, continuous.rs, check.rs, render/}, beryl_rules/src/{interpret/, combat.rs, rule_change.rs, event.rs}, beryl_kernel/src/rule_changes.rs.

Acceptance criteria

beryl_card can say 'return [card] from a graveyard to the battlefield under its owner's control', by extending by-bajq.3.14's instruction (not by adding a second one), and '[objects] can't block'. Unit tests with test definitions show both. just count-gate unchanged; State<Small> within 20,480 bytes.

Deviation: the bead said a graveyard trigger on 'a land you control enters' already existed, but the card table refused it: beryl_kernel::listener::listens_to matched Enters only as About::This (Unmatched::Subject for another object entering). trigger::fires already reads a filtered subject for Enters, so I widened the listener to About::Either and added tests. ReturnToBattlefield (by-bajq.3.14) already took any source zone, so part 1 is a render change: an ability that works only in a graveyard calls its card 'this card', and ReturnToBattlefield(This) from such an ability renders 'return this card from your graveyard to the battlefield'. Can't block is RuleChange{players: You, what: Governed::Block(Objects::This), when: When::Never} (new Governed::Block and When::Never), kind RuleKind::CantBlock asked of the creature like LoyaltyAsInstant; other shapes (other creatures, effects, casting Never) are refused with Unchecked::Rule/Players until a card needs them.
