Decisions and deviations (by-w8gv.21.16).

Pointer. ui/src/stage/pointer.ts is the one interface: PointerEvents { hover(name | undefined), click(name, at), lift(name | undefined), drop({ name, start, at, pose, onto }) }, told to one listener (stage.pointer.listen; the last to listen wins). Names are the stage's (a slot, or a<id> for an ability); onto is { name } or { player }. The page's events become the model's calls in one place, DomSurface.point (dom.ts): stage pixels from the layer's box, the node by data-stage, what a drop is over by elementsFromPoint as CardLayer's under() did. A touch has no hover. Nothing may be picked up until a listener sets stage.pointer.holdable: no shape is here yet, so in the app nothing is dragged; the unit test drags.

The held card. It enters the model as data, not as a track: pointer.held is { name, start, at } and the stage computes heldPose(pose at pickup, start, at) wherever it computes a pose (#poseOf), so stage.pose returns it, the clock does not move it, and a step that moves the card starts its glide from where it is held. It is upright and unleaned over everything (depth 1000, CardLayer's DRAGGING_Z), the pressed point kept under the pointer. Let go: the drop's listener returns true for a drop it took, and the card then stays where it was let go until what the drop did plays (a step that moves it glides from there; if none does it goes home when that script is over); any other drop glides home on the stage's clock in stage.release (the fly verb's timing; none under reduced motion). No physics (physics.ts's tilt and spring are browser-timed): the cut-over deletes them or a verb bead gives the held pose a lean from the pointer's speed. Tests: ui/scripts/stage-pointer.test.mjs (8, no browser).

Announcements. Shown.notices lists the announce steps under way, so banners follow the stage's steps and not a timer: the turn banner (TurnAnnouncement, split out of TurnBanner.tsx, which keeps the old board's timer), the coin's banner and the scry pill (Arena's words, "Scry: 1 top, 1 bottom."). The combat banner ("Combat / Blockers declared") is no verb in the script (Notice is turn, scry, coin), so it keeps the old board's 2.5 s timer (useBannerUp) on both boards: by-w8gv.21.23 decides whether it becomes a notice. The turn banner's fade is still CSS keyframes over the step's duration.

Chrome moved out of Board.tsx, shared by both boards, behaviour unchanged: game/Corner.tsx (Corner, Settings, ConcedeButton, UndoButton, Gear, FullControlIcon), game/Life.tsx (Life, Incoming, lifeId), game/StepBanner.tsx, game/StackPeek.tsx, game/Inspector.tsx (InspectAt, inspectorPlace). Board.tsx fell from 3,506 to 3,096 lines.

Desktop (stage/Chrome.tsx, StageBoard.tsx). Hud and Controls read stage.shown.table for what a step changes (life, incoming damage, mana, piles, stack peeks, the step stops) and the played view for what answers the player (undo, thinking, the combat banner). Not on the stage's board, as prompts': the turn button and dock, targets on the orbs, the mana pool's pulse, a click on a card (the exile-with viewer, menus). Life totals show the table's number at once: the old Life counts over 0.7 s of browser time, and the count verb's track is by-w8gv.21.21's. The hand's raise on hover is not here (a pointer-driven pose like the held card's; by-w8gv.21.26 has casting from hand). The layout takes what stands around the table (desktopWith({ hold, logOpen })): the hand tucks under a screen and rows clear the open log, by a relayout with no motion.

Hover inspection: the same rule as the old board's (not a card under a pile's top or being handled; the place from inspectorPlace by the card's resting x and whether the stack holds the right). A card on the stack is inspected, as the old board does; the settled entry says Arena shows none there, which I left as the acceptance says "where the old board opens it".

Phone (stage/PhoneChrome.tsx, PhoneStageBoard.tsx, mobile/phone.css): composed from the same components and listed in data/ui-conventions.json under "The phone". The menu button opens a sheet with the log, the bug report and the desktop's settings; undo and full control's mark at the end of the middle band; mana beside the orb, incoming damage on its rim, the spinner right of the opponent's orb; a touch on a graveyard or a known library lists its cards; a touch on a card inspects it (the stage's click), a touch beside it puts it away; banners scaled over the table. No stack peek on the phone: its stack shows every item, and the peeks are arrows' anchors (by-w8gv.21.22). No step stops on the phone (the old phone board has none either).

Checks. Generic, no scripted check: ui/shots.json gains a shows key (selectors that must be on screen after the click and hover) and 12 shots under ?stage (7 desktop: own turn, opponent's turn, stack, game over, hover, graveyard viewer, open log; 5 phone). Two audits were made exact rather than excused: text that takes no pointer and is painted over a card is not "under" it (clippedText tests with the pointer given back), and a dialog's buttons may overlap cards (crowdedControls).
