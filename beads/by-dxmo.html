Generated oracle scenarios break whenever the card pool changes

Close reason

Generator draws cards by rendezvous hashing, so a new card changes few positions; Drudge Skeletons back in the pool. Both exporters pay with Beryl's taps (XMage tapSource by card name; Forge floats them before its AI pays). Generator skips chance draws after a shuffle. Full just oracle + 2x just oracle Generated passed; slow CI passed at 5a4894ad.

Description

Symptom: adding any card to the generator's pool (rust/crates/beryl_cards/src/generate.rs, Pools::new) renumbers every 'Generated N' scenario. Entries in data/oracle-exporter-gaps.json are keyed by scenario name, so they go stale, and new positions show payments that the XMage and Forge AIs make differently from Beryl. This failed slow CI in by-6zyi (Drudge Skeletons). The steward kept the card out through OUTSIDE_CUBE, which is a hand-kept list that also misses Leonin Scimitar. Fix one or both: (1) have each exporter pay mana from the trace's 'taps' (the sources Beryl tapped), so oracle AI payment never diverges; that is the 'payment the harness leaves to Forge' gap class; (2) make a scenario's seed independent of the pool, for example by drawing each position from a stable list of cards per seed. Files: generate.rs, tools/src/beryl_tools/xmage.py, tools/src/beryl_tools/forge.py, tools/oracles/BerylForgeHarness.java.

Acceptance criteria

Adding a card to the generator pool no longer causes oracle failures or stale gaps in Generated scenarios; demonstrate by removing Drudge Skeletons from OUTSIDE_CUBE with just oracle Generated passing three runs.
