Close reason

Tokens wear a pewter frame, piles of twins a ×N badge and split on any difference or stack reference, stolen permanents a violet edge with the owner in the inspector; tokens fixture/shot and ui-test checks added. Already fixed before: enchanted tokens stood outside piles, and twins shared their candidate.

Description

  • Tokens: they are marked only by their name. Give them a token frame or badge, with no words.
  • Identical permanents: group them with a ×N count badge, as Arena does. A group must split on any difference: tapped state, attachments, counters or a type change. Reviewers saw an enchanted Insect token, by then a Vehicle, stacked under its identical twin. In a stack of Map tokens, the visible top token was not the usable one; the buried one was. The card a click uses must be the one on top.
  • Stolen permanents: a permanent controlled by the other player sits on its controller's side with no sign of its owner (the reviewers stole a Mana Crypt). Mark it with a subtle border or accent, and name the owner in the inspector.

Files: ui/src/game/CardLayer.tsx, ui/src/game/layout.ts, ui/src/components/Card.tsx.

Before changing anything, reproduce each item on current staging. Use just ui-live, as the ui-review skill describes (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md), or a fixture. The findings come from reviews of the build before the 2026-11-05 UI fixes, so some may already be fixed. Drop those, and list them in the close reason.

Design rules (see the epic): follow the skill's design philosophy, and add no standing words to the table. What a player needs at a glance is shown as icons, badges, numbers, colour or position. Everything else goes behind disclosure: hover, the inspector, a pile's browser, or the game log.

Acceptance criteria

  • A fixture shot shows grouped identical tokens with a count badge, an enchanted or otherwise changed token outside its group, and a stolen permanent with an owner marker.
  • A just ui-test check: clicking the top card of a group acts on an eligible object of that group.
  • No new words stand on the table.

Design: tokens get a pewter frame body in place of black; the top card of a pile wears a cream ×N badge over its art's top right; a stolen permanent wears a violet line inside its black frame (a span, since WebKit ignored negative outline-offset), and the inspector's marks list says 'Owner: You/Opponent' beside the same violet. Piles now group any identical face-up permanent (not only tokens), keyed on name, token, owner, tapped, sickness, types and P/T, with nothing attached to or beneath it; twins() also compares owner and types, matching the engine's signature. Land piles keep no count badge (their edges show it, by the earlier design).
