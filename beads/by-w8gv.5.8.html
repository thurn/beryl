Close reason

State's last fields (stack, trigger queues, pending, legacy queues) are kernel-private behind mutators; the flashback rule is written once (cast::exiled_instead); counts 404,243,458 -> 404,879,191 before the rebase

Description

Milestone 3 (plan K6): the last of State's fields become private: the stack, the trigger queues and the engine's pending state. After this bead a rules module cannot name a field of State.

Read first

docs/project-50k.md: Decisions K6, K7, K11 and K12, and "Crates", "State" and "Kernel rules" under Target architecture. rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs: its rustdoc is the kernel's API contract (who writes, mutator and getter naming, events, the board door, the legacy accessor, sets and orders); follow it, and change it in the same push when the code proves it wrong. enact brief shows the handoffs of the beads this one depends on.

Scope

  • stack (raw uses in rust/crates/beryl_engine/src when this was decomposed: cast.rs 10, interpret/run.rs 2, zone.rs 2, profile.rs 2 and single uses in effect.rs, engine.rs, resolve.rs, sba.rs, trigger.rs, turn.rs). The stack is two structures, the zones.stack bitboard and the stack queue, which each caller keeps in step by hand. Mutators: put a spell or ability on the stack (CR 601.2a, 603.3b), take the top entry to resolve it (CR 608), change an entry's targets (CR 115.7), and the one below.
  • A spell leaves the stack without resolving (Mason note 6 on by-w8gv.5.1). "It is exiled instead if it was cast with flashback (CR 702.34a)" is written four times: cast::leave_stack, EffectCtx::return_to_hand, EffectCtx::put_spell_into_library and Run::unstack (interpret/run.rs:351, a raw state.stack.retain). One kernel mutator takes objects off the stack and returns their entries; the flashback rule is applied once by its one caller in beryl_engine.
  • triggers and delayed (trigger.rs 9, engine.rs 2): queue a trigger, take the next to put on the stack in APNAP order (CR 603.3b), register and fire a delayed trigger (CR 603.7).
  • pending (engine.rs 15, combat.rs 14, cast.rs 12, start.rs 6, resolve.rs 5, setup.rs 5, face_down.rs 3, sba.rs 2, turn.rs 2): the engine's state machine. Its payloads (Casting, Resolving and the rest) are records per the contract's section c. cast.rs mutates Pending::Casting in place across the steps of casting (CR 601.2): give it a scoped way to do that which cannot leave pending inconsistent with Resolving::knowledge, rather than a mutator per field.
  • The legacy queues: effects (turn.rs 6, layers.rs 2, effect.rs, event.rs), permissions (cast.rs 3, turn.rs 3, effect.rs 2, mana.rs) and entered_x (event.rs 4) are rebuilt by milestone 4 as the Effects and Memos stores. They and CastPermission's extra, exile and flashback get no typed mutator: reach them raw through the legacy accessor (contract, section e). Complete that accessor's rustdoc: the list of everything behind it, each with the store that replaces it, from by-w8gv.7.1.
  • relayout.rs and the invariants follow the fields.

Constraints

  • decision.rs and relayout.rs are being edited by the prompts milestone (by-w8gv.11), which reshapes Ask and how a paused resolution is stored. Keep the diff to both small: privacy and accessors, no restructuring of Pending::Resolving. Rebase over it.
  • cast.rs is 1,838 lines on the file-length allowlist. Do not grow it; if a mutator replaces enough code to let a section move out, lower its entry.
  • Run just bench-count before and after and record both.

Parity and checks

Parity (plan K11) at the push: just check passes; the frozen save corpus replays (cargo test -p beryl_session --test corpus); rollouts_never_change holds (cargo test -p beryl_bench rollouts_never_change); slow CI's counts lane (just count-gate, bar 405,276,238 instructions, 1% tolerance, rust/crates/beryl_bench/count-gate.toml) passes with no bump to the bar. No behavior changes: no rules fix, no new card, no new mechanic. Work the card-names and file-length allowlists down as files are touched (data/card-names-allowlist.json, data/file-length-allowlist.json; an entry moves with its file and its count only falls; a new file is under 800 lines). Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules (AGENTS.md); keep the citations of code you move.

Acceptance criteria

State has no pub or pub(crate) field that beryl_engine reads or writes directly: stack, triggers, delayed, pending, effects, permissions and entered_x are private; one kernel mutator takes objects off the stack (the zones.stack bitboard and the stack queue together) and returns their entries, and cast::leave_stack, EffectCtx::return_to_hand, EffectCtx::put_spell_into_library and Run::unstack call it with the flashback rule (CR 702.34a) written once in beryl_engine; effects, permissions, entered_x and CastPermission's extra, exile and flashback fields are reachable only through the legacy accessor, whose rustdoc lists everything milestone 4 deletes; just bench-count before and after is recorded on the bead; parity holds.

Counts (just bench-count, rollouts): before 404,243,458 (staging 00f3c0ec), after 404,879,191 (+635,733, +0.157%); bar 405,276,238. The rise is not one function: callgrind's per-function diff shows inlining moving between engine::next (-806k), engine::perform_or_stall (+997k), cast::cast_options (+281k), engine::decision (-82k) and mana::Groups::pays (+120k), with every call count unchanged and memcpy unchanged. Three experiments gave the identical count (so the code is the same): #[inline(always)] on ask, settle_question and pause_resolution; #[inline] on pending(), stack(), triggers(), permissions(), effects(); matches! in place of == Pending::None in the priority loop. I stopped there.

Deviations from the bead:

  1. The kernel's State::take_off_stack(spell) takes the entry out of the stack queue only. The stack zone's bitboard is the zone move's (State::move_object's leave), and the card must still be in the stack zone when the rules read where it goes; removing it there too would leave an object whose zone says Stack outside the stack's set. The module doc of stack.rs states the rule (entry on after the card arrives, off before it leaves), put_on_stack debug-asserts its half and the invariant check holds the other.
  2. The flashback rule (CR 702.34a) is written once as cast::exiled_instead(entry, to), which also carries the permission's 'exile instead of the graveyard'. cast::leave_stack (resolve and counter), cast::unstack (EffectCtx::return_to_hand, put_spell_into_library) and Run::unstack call it. leave_stack does not itself call take_off_stack: at resolution the entry is already off the stack (State::take_top_of_stack), so only cast::counter and cast::unstack take it.
  3. 'A scoped way' for cast.rs's Casting: cast.rs never mutated Pending in place; it copies the Casting out, changes it and stores it whole, so State::ask(Pending) covers it. A resolution's pause is the one payload with an invariant (Resolving::knowledge), so it has its own mutators: pause_resolution(record, question) keeps the knowledge of the first pause, record_answer(index) appends in place (no clone of the 400-byte record as before), and ask debug-asserts it is never handed a Resolving.
  4. The legacy queues got four named writes rather than raw &mut queues, in the style by-w8gv.5.4 began (start_effect, end_effects): Legacy::add_permission, end_permissions_of_turn, remember_x. No &mut to a queue leaves the kernel.

Handoff: staging 2162bce0. Rebased over c1143436 (the prompts milestone's 'a decision carries its prompt'), which conflicted only in data/file-length-allowlist.json. Count on the rebased tree: 405,148,597 (bar 405,276,238; this bead's own delta, measured before the rebase, is +635,733, see the earlier comment).

Every field of State is pub(crate) now: stack, triggers, delayed, pending, effects, permissions and entered_x were the last. A rules module cannot name a field of State.

Kernel (rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src):

  • stack.rs: State::put_on_stack(entry), take_top_of_stack() (resolution, and CR 603.3d's removal of an untargetable trigger), take_off_stack(spell) -> Option<StackEntry> (a spell leaving without resolving), announce_x(x) and announce_target(target) on the entry being cast (they replace cast.rs's top_mut), queue_trigger, take_trigger(index), delay_trigger, fire_delayed(due: FnMut(&PendingTrigger) -> bool), delayed() (read).
  • pending.rs: State::ask(Pending), settle_question(), pause_resolution(record, question), record_answer(index).
  • legacy.rs: Legacy::add_permission, end_permissions_of_turn, remember_x; the struct's rustdoc is the table of everything milestone 4 deletes with the store that replaces each.
  • state.rs: pending(), stack(), triggers(), permissions(), effects() are #[inline]. lib.rs: three table rows and the legacy paragraph amended.

Engine (rust/crates/beryl_engine/src):

  • cast.rs: exiled_instead(entry, to) is the one statement of CR 702.34a; unstack(s, spell, to) -> Option<Zone> takes a spell off and says where its card goes; leave_stack now takes the destination Zone (was a bool). take_spell and top_mut are gone.
  • effect.rs return_to_hand and put_spell_into_library call cast::unstack; interpret/run.rs Run::unstack(objects, to) loops State::take_off_stack over the spells among its objects (it was a raw stack.retain).
  • engine.rs, combat.rs, face_down.rs, sba.rs, start.rs, turn.rs, resolve.rs: s.pending = X became s.ask(X) or s.settle_question(); reads are *s.pending().
  • trigger.rs fire_delayed passes its 'due' rule to State::fire_delayed as a closure.

Gotchas: (1) take_off_stack leaves the stack zone's bitboard to the zone move that must follow (deviation 1 above). (2) Run::unstack keeps the old behavior that a flashback spell being exiled moves with Reason::Effect and the rest with Reason::Exile. (3) ask debug-asserts it is not given Pending::None or Pending::Resolving. (4) data/file-length-allowlist.json: cast.rs 1825, effect.rs 1242, engine.rs 834.

A reviewer should check: exiled_instead against the four old sites (leave_stack used exile || entry.exile; the three others entry.flashback alone); pause_resolution's knowledge against the old knowledge.unwrap_or(s.knowledge()); Legacy::end_permissions_of_turn and remember_x against the loops they replace; the count delta (no single cause found). Verified: clippy, the whole workspace's tests on the rebased tree (corpus replay, rollouts_never_change, corpus_never_changes), the fast check at push. Not run by me: slow CI. beryl_cli's a_second_lock_names_the_first_holder failed twice under load during my runs and passed alone and on the last run: by-w8gv.20.8 already tracks it.
