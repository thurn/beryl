Close reason

marks-on check lands; fixed marks lagging tray/box cards and player marks off the orb core

Description

Symptom: in the desktop app, the outlines that mark playable cards sat to the right of their cards. No check compares a mark with the thing it marks: light::marks::write draws glow quads into the 3D scene, and the interface checks look only at the flat layer's elements (by-q44s.9.39).

Change: add an interface check (beryl_scene/src/agent/checks.rs) that projects each open mark's quad (beryl_scene/src/light/marks.rs, Draw::glow with sprites::OUTLINE, or the player mark's rectangle) to the window and reports a fault when its centre is more than a small tolerance from the centre of its card's or player's projected bounds, or when the mark does not contain them. Make it run at scale 2 too, once the scale bead lands.

Files: beryl_scene/src/light/marks.rs, agent/checks.rs, agent/hit.rs, agent/fault.rs, frame/draw.rs, data/beryl3d/gates.json (audit thresholds).

Acceptance criteria

A new interface check faults when a mark's projected quad is off its card or player; a test with a deliberately shifted mark fails it; just b3d test passes or lists the real faults.

Handoff: staging 73c43a969, 4fc56f9a4, c5a4eaa38.

Check (4fc56f9a4): new interface rule marks-on (Rule::MarksOn, agent/checks.rs marks_on). Each outline that light::marks::write draws goes through FrameBuilder::ring, which records the outline draw's model with what it rings (frame::Ring, Ringed::Element(ElementId) for cards/abilities, Ringed::Player(Side)) in Frame::rings. The check projects sprites::OUTLINE_LINE through Frame::view_projection and faults when the line's centre is more than audit.mark_points (2, gates.json) from the thing's box centre, or the thing's box is not inside the line grown by 2 points. Players compare against hud::players::core_box (the orb core inside the orb art element found by hud::players::orb_name). Test: verbs_mark::the_marks_check_faults_an_outline_moved_off_its_card_or_narrower_than_it (passes the real outline, faults one moved 10 mm and one drawn at 0.7 width, the shape of the by-q44s.9.36 bug). Docs: docs/beryl-3d.md Interface checks, rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md (mark_points), b3d skill.

Real faults the check found and that I fixed here:

  1. 73c43a969: Show::reach (marks, arrows, effects) ignored the table's slide, tray-move and shown-box adjustments, so eligible outlines waited at the scry tray's slots while cards flew there (aangs-iceberg scry asks, 344 failing tests) and stayed in the hand while cards stood in the trigger-order / put-on-bottom box (choice-trigger-order, greedy-turn-9, opening-bottom). New Table::drawn_pose applies slide_look, tray_look, shown_look; Show::reach uses it. Show::pose (pointer) is unchanged.
  2. c5a4eaa38: after the orb became the web's art, a player's target outline sat 2.1 points above the life-total core. light::marks moves it down by art::orb::face_offset.

Evidence: just b3d test 8312 tests, 0 faults, marks-on judged 25404 outlines over 11112 frames (scale 1 and 1600x900@2). Phone run: no marks-on faults; its 20 reachable faults are unrelated to marks. Pictures: aangs-iceberg/lands-tapped --at 133ms --scale 2 before the 9.36 fix showed the narrowed outline the check flagged; choice-trigger-order --at 7514ms --scale 2 after the fix shows outlines round each boxed card. Not compared with the web picture.

Reviewer: the table's own stolen-permanent outline (table::own) and effects::light glow still use builder.draw, so they are not checked.
