Description

These flows ask more than once, or ask out of context:

  • Up to one target: it asks "How many targets? 0 / 1" (on 42×23 px buttons) before the target. Make it one targeting step with a skip.
  • Attacking past a planeswalker: with an opposing planeswalker, every attacker needs an attack-target prompt. Default to the player, and change the target by dragging to the planeswalker.
  • Adeline's token: its trigger asks "Choose what it attacks" before the token is on screen. Show the token first.
  • Explore: it asks Yes/No without showing the revealed card. Show the card, and mark the choices by their result.
  • Time Spiral: "untap up to six lands" lists 12 lands from both players, all lit alike, though only 7 can be chosen. Pick on the board, with eligible lands lit; whose land each is follows from where it sits.
  • Crew: show a running power meter, not text.
  • Waterbend: don't ask when lands alone cover the cost; otherwise show the remaining cost shrinking as mana symbols.
  • Loot: Malcolm and Jace show the drawn card only after the discard. Show the draw before asking for the discard.
  • Discard and cleanup: the count needed is unclear. Show it as slots, or a number on the confirm button, not a sentence.

Some of these may need beryl_session changes, such as event order for loots or defaults for attack targets.
