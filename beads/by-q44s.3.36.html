Handoff: Turn (table/scene/motion.rs) is a per-card time at which the card switches from the record the table showed before the script to the one the script leads to; StepOut::turn writes it (play/step/out.rs), Compiled::turns keeps it, play/relay.rs shift() moves it with its step, and Table::object_at reads it (min over the card's turns). Only strike writes turns today; any verb whose act changes a card's record mid-step (the web's Entering::cover) can use it. Pose::upward is the transform for what is drawn on a card's face (badges, own marks). notice::Fade carries each notice kind's fade shares from space.json notices. Gotcha: a card struck twice at different times shows all its damage from the first blow. Staging's just b3d gate is red independently of this change (see the report).
