DECISIONS AND DEVIATIONS (by-w8gv.11.11)

  1. Where the code is. rust/crates/beryl_session/src/prompt/: pay.rs (the pay shape: Pay, its Outlook after each click, and Plans, a cost the engine takes as one of its payment plans), pay/taps.rs (a cost the engine takes one permanent at a time: what each permanent can pay, which sets of picks pay with no wasted tap, the default for the rest, each played ahead on a copy), declare.rs (Declare, and Aims, the run step that declares and aims). held.rs: Shaped::Pay and Shaped::Declare, Step::Aimed, Want::Mana / Pool / Plan, named and alike now match Opt::Attack, Opt::Block and Opt::Defender, and Run::over puts a run under any already under it (a payment's picks, then the plan pick of the Pay / mana decision that follows). The session now holds every shape; Held::open returns None only for a payment whose plans tap the same permanents.
  2. A click is made in the game. Held::click takes the state, the table and the open decision (Game::click passes its board's): a payment asks the engine what the picks leave after each click, which no table built at opening can say. The other shapes ignore them. Tests that click a Held held apart from a game pass the position they built it in.
  3. One pay shape, no mechanic in its code. taps.rs reads the decision, not the purpose: a candidate that is a mana ability pays its mana (mana_contribution), a candidate that is an object pays one generic mana where the record has a mana cost and, where the record counts (count and most), whatever the engine's next pick says it took off what is left. So crew, waterbend, convoke-like costs and manual mana are one module. preview.rs's Contribution, waterbend_roles, waterbend_sources, automatic_waterbend and waterbend_plan moved there under general names (Contribution, roles, Due::of, automatic, line) with their behaviour; its two unit tests are tests/payments.rs's now, through clicks. The one-card test in the default (Subtype::Treasure) became "a permanent whose own mana would use it up" (mana_contribution's consumed).
  4. Confirming. A purpose with a confirm word (crew: Arena's "Submit n") is answered by its button once the picks pay; one without (mana, waterbend) by the pick that pays the cost in full, as the old UI's waterbend did. Auto ("Auto Pay") answers with the picks and the default for the rest, enabled when the default can pay.
  5. Crew has an Auto Pay (UI Conventions, "Paying with permanents"; Arena has none). Its default is new: the creatures that cost least to tap, those that could not attack this turn before those that could, then least attacking power, then fewest (taps.rs cover). A judgement call: human bead filed.
  6. No wasted pick. For a cost mana can finish, a pick is refused when the set would overpay by a whole pick (the old preview's rule, kept). For a cost permanents alone pay, picks are taken in order until the cost is met, as the engine asks them, so two small creatures and then a large one is allowed (the old rule would have left the player unable to finish). Every pick is also played ahead: one the engine will not take (a Plains for {U}) is neither marked nor accepted.
  7. left. A counted cost says the number left; mana says the cost less each unit paid, a unit of mana paying a symbol of its own kind before generic. A payment plan pays its cost whole, so all of it is left until the picks make a plan.
  8. Plans (Pay / mana / Plan). The player picks permanents; the plans that tap them all stay open, the first is the default (suggested), and the pick that makes one answers with it as the cast's continuation (Run::continuing), as the old UI's castPlan sends it. A plan that taps nothing (floating mana pays) is the default and Auto's.
  9. Manual mana (Pay / mana / Pick) is reached by no game of the app: no session profile sets Payment::Manual. It is tested from a position under that profile with Held::opening (tests/payments.rs mana_is_paid_one_source_at_a_time_by_picking_them). Floating mana pays first, unasked; the engine offers one source of each class, so only that source and the permanents interchangeable with it can be picked.
  10. Declare. Words are the table's (Arena's): with nothing declared, auto "All Attack" (primary) and decline "No Attacks" / "No Blocks"; with a declaration, confirm "n Attacker(s)" / "n Blocker(s)" and decline as the cancel word ("Cancel Attacks", "Cancel Blocks"), which takes the declaration back rather than answering. The design had Confirm "Attack" / "Block": .11.7's wording replaced those. All Attack declares every creature as it lies on the table; with a planeswalker to attack they all await one aim. A blocker that can block one attacker is paired at once, where "one" counts attackers alike as the player sees them (two alike are two to click, though the engine asks nothing).
  11. Aims fixes a flaw the old UI's blockPlan has: when the engine takes a twin for the attacker clicked, later blockers aimed at that attacker follow it, and blockers aimed at the twin now go to the other one (the map is kept both ways).
  12. The run's end is left in for a declaration: when no creature is left to declare the engine asks for no end, and the next decision is never the same purpose's pick, so the step is dropped with the run.
  13. The old UI. ui/src/game/waterbend.ts is on the click command: its hook sends each pick and Auto Pay as clicks and draws from the marks. Every permanent picked carries the tap mark now (the convention's look for paying), not only those tapped in place of mana. The boards and Game.tsx change by one argument and a dead branch; Board.tsx stays at its allowance. Crew, plans and the declarations keep their own state and choose calls there.
  14. StackItem::Ability's wire field waterbend is tapsToPay (beats.ts reads it); DecisionView::waterbend, PREVIEW_WATERBEND, WaterbendRequest, WaterbendPreview, WaterbendMana and Game / Sessions / Session::preview_waterbend are gone, with the Tauri command and the servers' arms.
  15. Known limits, for the Warden: a declaration taken up mid-run (an old-UI save or an undo of one of its picks) opens with only the creatures still to declare and "No Attacks", though some attack already; an effect-time payment plan whose plans' taps the preview cannot tell apart is not held; CandidateView::blocks, PlayerView::pending_attackers and preview.rs blocks stay for the old UI.
  16. Engine untouched: no file under beryl_engine, beryl_kernel, beryl_card, beryl_cards or beryl_search changed.

FOR by-w8gv.21.29 (the stage), PER PURPOSE

PAY: state { shape: "pay", cost, left }, both as the screen writes them ("{3}" and "{1}"; a crew cost "5" and "3"). Marks: paying on what the player picked (the convention's look: turned, a tap mark, no outline), suggested on what the default would tap for the rest (shown on hover or long press of Auto Pay, UI Conventions "Paying with permanents"), eligible on what else a click can add. A permanent has one mark: one the default would tap is suggested, not eligible, and can still be clicked. Click { kind: "on", on: card }: picks it, or takes a pick back; a click with no use returns the state unchanged. Buttons: auto "Auto Pay", enabled when the default can pay what the picks leave, primary then; and for a crew, confirm "Submit n" (n permanents picked), enabled and primary once they pay.

  • waterbend: no confirm; the pick that pays the cost in full answers, and Auto answers with the picks and the default. Title "Tap permanents to Waterbend". Opens with nothing picked. Scenarios BEND, SOURCES, ALIKE (tests/payments.rs); boards and fixtures waterbend-tap (mana cannot pay: Auto disabled until two permanents are picked) and choice-scry (lands pay: Auto enabled at once); /specs/aangs-iceberg (ability-1 and the two waterbend scenarios).

  • mana, a plan: opens with the default plan's permanents suggested and those only another plan taps eligible; a pick narrows the plans; the pick that makes a plan answers; Auto takes the default. DECLARE: state { shape: "declare", arrow: "attack" | "block", pairs: [{ from, to }], aiming: [slots] }. to is a card, or { kind: "player", player: "opponent" } for the defending player. Draw a line of arrow for each pair. Marks: eligible on each creature that can be declared and is not; selected on each declared (paired or aiming); while aiming is not empty, eligible on what they can be aimed at (the player's mark is on Subject player). Clicks: on a creature declares it or takes it back; on an aim while some await one pairs them all.

  • attack: buttons with nothing declared: auto "All Attack" (primary; declares every creature, as they lie) and decline "No Attacks" (answers). With some declared: confirm "1 Attacker" / "n Attackers" (enabled and primary once none awaits an aim) and decline "Cancel Attacks", which takes the declaration back and does not answer. With nothing but the player to attack a creature is paired at once; while the defending player controls a planeswalker it joins aiming, and a click on the player or a planeswalker aims every creature aiming.   • block: decline "No Blocks" (primary) with nothing declared; then confirm "1 Blocker" / "n Blockers" and decline "Cancel Blocks".
