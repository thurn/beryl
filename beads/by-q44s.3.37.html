Description

Review beryl_scene's src/pointer and src/table (place.rs, rows.rs, scene.rs, shadows.rs, badges.rs, own.rs, tray.rs) and the abilities on the stack, against docs/beryl-3d.md 'Objects and placement', 'Motion the pointer drives', 'Space and camera' and 'Interface checks', the epic's rules, and the web interface's files each bead named (ui/src/game/handLayout.ts, fanArc.ts, stackLayout.ts, pileLook.ts, ui/src/stage/tray.ts, pointer.ts, heft.ts, raised.ts, prompt.ts). Fix what you find, with a test or a stress board for each.

Look hardest at:

  • a placement function that reads more than the view, the layout, the camera, the scroll offsets, the open lift and the tray;
  • a hand, a stack, a pile or a tray that leaves the window, or covers a card's last uncovered strip, at any size of the desktop matrix from either seat: try 0, 1, 2, 7, 20 and 60 cards in a hand, 1, 10 and 50 on the stack, and 12 in a tray;
  • a card that is interactive and cannot be reached, or can be reached and is not interactive; count the cards 'reachable' judged on verbs-zones, pay-default and stress-255 from both seats and say so;
  • the lean of a held card differing between 60 and 120 frames a second by more than the test's tolerance, or after a frame gap;
  • pointer state that is a function of when an input arrived instead of where the pointer is;
  • a drop that sends the session something other than what Prompts in ui/src/stage/prompt.ts sends for the same gesture: compare #dropped and #lifts with their ports line by line, and replay three recorded prompts with a drag;
  • a held or hovered card that the checks on cards at rest judge, or a card at rest that they skip;
  • a hover that flickers between two cards of a fan as the raised card moves under the pointer;
  • a scroll that leaves a card resting where placement does not put it, or a script that flies a card from out of view through the table;
  • a shadow that lies under the cards beneath it, or a card on the table that has one;
  • a pile whose records grow with its height, or whose hidden cards lost their poses;
  • an ability's element id, or a revealed face's, colliding with a card's or a flat-layer element's;
  • quadratic work in placement at 255 and 1,024 objects (just b3d gate --counts).

Report in a comment the counts above and what you changed.

Run: just b3d cargo-test; just b3d test; just b3d gate; just b3d lane.
