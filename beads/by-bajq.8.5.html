Description

Card

Gemrazer, 4/4: "Mutate {1}{G}{G} (If you cast this spell for its mutate cost, put it over or under target non-Human creature you own. They mutate into the creature on top plus all abilities from under it.) Reach, trample. Whenever this creature mutates, destroy target artifact or enchantment an opponent controls."

What is wrong today

One slot is one card or token, and a permanent's card is its slot's. A merged permanent is one object represented by two or more cards.

What to build

Plan: Designs 7, "Merged permanents"; decisions R7 and R11.

  1. The object stays. The permanent that is mutated onto is the same object: its slot, incarnation, counters, attachments, timestamp and summoning sickness do not change, and nothing enters (CR 731.2c). The merging card's slot goes to a zone state of its own (a new Zone value for "part of a permanent") with a memo naming the permanent and its place in the pile. It is in no battlefield list or bitboard.
  2. Characteristics. The permanent's copiable values are the top card's face plus one addition per card under it meaning "every ability of this face" (by-bajq.6.1). If the top card is not the slot's own, Memo::Really (by-bajq.6.1) remembers the slot's card. So the layers, the listener masks and a copy of the permanent need nothing new. More additions than Small holds is Full.
  3. Leaving (CR 731.3). When the permanent leaves the battlefield, the move expands to every card of it: each goes to the destination as its own object in the one event. Into a graveyard or library their owner orders them. event::move_batch_to finds the pile behind the keyed bit, so a permanent with nothing under it costs nothing.
  4. Mutate (CR 702.140): a casting option with a target (a creature spell with a target). As it resolves, if the target is illegal it is an ordinary creature spell and enters; otherwise its controller chooses over or under (the Mode shape) and State::merge joins it. A trigger event for "this creature mutates", which every "whenever this creature mutates" ability of the pile sees.
  5. A Capability for the game having a card that merges.

Scenarios

Gemrazer over and under a creature with an ability; the merged creature dying (both cards go to the graveyard, a dies trigger from under it triggers once); bounced (both cards to hand); the target leaving in response (Gemrazer enters as a creature, no mutate trigger); Phantasmal Image copying the merged creature.
