Description

AI ladder: the policy plays, the search overrules

The plan is docs/ai-plan.md (2026-11-14). Read it first. It replaces docs/ai-strategy.md and the closed epic by-97g.

Why this epic exists

The ladder of 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-13 (by-97g) tried twelve rungs and promoted one. The champion's strength and blunder count did not move, because nine rungs were stopped by the suite gate before any match:

  • hold raised the suite's passed runs from 90 to 141 of 200 and was stopped because one entry passed 17 of 20 runs against the champion's 20. The champion's own 20-seed check of that entry passed 37 of 40.
  • combat took two attack entries from 0 and 1 of 20 to 16 and 17, with no entry lower, and was recorded as "fixed none of its claims" because a claim needed 18.
  • No two rungs were ever tried together, and nobody read the tables.

Two findings shape the new design (docs/ai-plan.md §2.3):

  • A blunder is a dominated play worth about 0.001 of a win. The search measures win rate with an error of 0.005 to 0.01, so it cannot see dominance at any budget. The default that plays when the search has no opinion must be right.
  • The tree invents leads. Below the root, UCB1's exploration bonus (0.07 or more) is larger than any real difference (0.01 to 0.05), so every child is visited about equally and a node's mean is the value of answering it at random. Paired rollouts in the same worlds do not show those leads.

The design

The policy (greedy with general rules, plus the combat planner at combat) answers every decision. The search overrules it only when paired rollouts confirm its lead. The champion is the app's Deep Searcher (beryl_search::DEEP_SEARCHER), today pimc72/hidden/rollout/tuned/anchor+guard@30M.

Decisions (Derek, 2026-11-14)

  • Run instruction from Derek: Cygnus can do all builds and their verification. Read every instruction in this epic or docs/ai-plan.md that assigns builds or their verification to the Mac as applying to Cygnus for this run. The measurement chain still runs on Cygnus.
  • Who judges. The session that works a measure bead judges its result. On Cygnus that is a Codex run (GPT Codex 6.1 Sol). There is no mechanical gate: just gate reports and the session decides.
  • Fitting. Rungs may fit the numbers the policy already has (card values, rule constants). No new model is trained in this epic. A distilled policy model is docs/ai-plan.md §9, for a later epic.
  • Suite entries come only from Derek's own games. No bead adds an entry from match transcripts, and no bead adds a class he has not reported. fractured-identity-own-crypt was retired as a sound play on 2026-11-14.
  • Forge's and XMage's AI hints are not used (AGENTS.md: no card data or design from Forge).

Rules for every bead here

  • A rung is two beads. Build adds the change as an option that is off by default, with tests, and pushes it; the champion's moves do not change. Measure runs docs/ai-plan.md §4.3 on Cygnus, judges and records.
  • Measure beads are chained by dependencies, so no two matches run at once. A match takes 6 to 18 hours. Build and build-verification beads can run on Cygnus.
  • Stack what is independent. A measure bead may play two options in one match when each passed the suite and they change different things. If the stack fails, each is played alone.
  • A measure bead may reorder or retry when a result suggests it, and says why in its notes. It may not add a suite entry or change a target.
  • No bead waits for a person, and none files a bead asking one to look.
  • General by data. A rule reads what a card's definition says and the game's state. It names no card. Per-card numbers live only in values.rs and the per-deck overlay.
  • Record everything in docs/ladder.md: one row for each match, audit or dropped rung. Logs, transcripts and position files are never committed.
  • Keep docs/ai-plan.md true. A promotion updates §3 and §5. A bead whose result contradicts the doc corrects the doc in the same push.

Code map

  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs: the long-think searcher. Family, Options (with to_name and FromStr), LongSearch::choose_traced (the root), grow (how a decision's work is spent), check and AnchorCheck (the override test), guarded (where the default is held), Tree::select (UCB1), value and discounted (how a finished game is scored). It is exempt from the file-length check, but put new code in a module under long/.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long/turn.rs: the turn search (turn72).
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/rollout.rs and rollout/policy.rs: the rollout policy. RolloutPolicy::with_rules, Rules { hold, waste }, Policy::action, Softmax, soft_rollout_within.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/combat.rs: the combat planner (Sim, Plan, General, handles).
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/values.rs and overlay/: card values.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/lib.rs: the agent registry (AGENTS, agent), DEEP_SEARCHER.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/: blunders.rs (the suite), gate.rs (just gate), ladder.rs and ladder/ (just ladder, the sequential test, the row), screen.rs, why.rs (paired rollouts and the tree below a move), match_log/ (what a match records for each decision).
  • data/blunders.json: the suite's entries.
  • Tests: just test-rust. The suite: just blunders [agent] --seeds N --work 3M,30M --only NAME. On the Mac, tournaments and suites at 30M use the whole machine: run the suite at --work 3M --seeds 1 there and leave the rest to Cygnus.
