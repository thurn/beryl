Respect native safe area and keep text at least 11 points and controls at least 44 square. Reuse existing viewer, settings, choice and tray elements and messages. Leave opening/game result composition and non-game sheets to the other screen bead; keep those dispatch branches additive so the two beads can land independently.

Tests in beryl_scene tests/all/game_phone.rs: idle game furniture exists without an open prompt; a stack or board target occupies the middle without obscuring prompt targets; prompt actions send the existing decisions; open and dismiss a pile and log sheet; safe interactive extents at all three sizes including on resize. Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene --test all game_phone; just b3d test b3d:pay-default --phone-only; just b3d tree b3d:pay-default/you/375x647 --at end. Use the stock recordings on both seats wherever offered.
