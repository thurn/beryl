Description

Symptom: on the desktop app's start of game, the life total was drawn over the "on the play" text. The overlap check (beryl_scene/src/agent/checks.rs overlap) only compares interactive elements, so two texts drawn over each other never count as a fault. The same goes for floats such as life totals, pile counts and notices (by-q44s.9.39).

Change: extend overlap, or add a sibling rule, so that the laid-out lines of text (frame.lines) and floats do not intersect other text or interactive elements unless one contains the other in the tree. Seed allowed faults in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json only for overlaps that are on purpose, each with a reason.

Files: beryl_scene/src/agent/checks.rs, agent/hit.rs, agent/fault.rs, layout/write.rs, hud/players.rs, data/beryl3d/test-faults.json.

Acceptance criteria

Overlapping text lines and floats are faults; a test with a life total over a text line fails; allowed faults are listed with reasons.

Handoff: staging 17366ae4e. New interface rule text-overlap (Rule::TextOverlap) in beryl_scene/src/agent/text_overlap.rs, a sibling of overlap rather than an extension, so the existing overlap stays about interactive elements. It takes every Frame::lines line (floats such as life totals and pools are laid out as text, so they are included), cut to its element's clip, skipping elements with opacity <= audit::TRANSPARENT, shrinks each box by 1 point (a line box includes its leading), and faults a pair of a line with another element's line, or with an interactive non-card flat-layer element, that intersect unless hit::is_within relates them. One fault per element pair. checks.rs's fault/subject/interactive are now pub(super) for it. Judged counts frame.lines. Test: agent::a_life_total_over_a_line_of_text_overlaps_it_and_a_label_inside_its_toggle_does_not (life total over 'On the play' faults; over a toggle faults with the toggle and its label; clear passes).

Allowed faults: just b3d test found real overlaps in 211 tests (462 faults), listed in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json with --raise. None is on purpose. by-q44s.9.51 owns the incoming combat damage drawn over the step toggles (about 290 of them; web draws it as the .life-incoming badge on the orb's rim, seen in a compare at scale 2: adeline-resplendent-cathar/adelines-human-attacks-a-planeswalker-ask-3 --at 0ms). by-q44s.9.52 owns the rest (pile counts colliding, life change over the Combat notice, log over pool/library counts, abilities over Attackers declared, scry TOP over library count).
