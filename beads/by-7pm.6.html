Moot, 2026-11-12: just longthink is retired (AGENTS.md, Tournaments; docs/ai-strategy.md section 8), so its QoS no longer matters. Not done.

Description

Measured (by-7pm.2.2 Sage, 2026-11-08, load avg ~48 on 18 cores): the long-think arena (just longthink, run-003, running 41 h in the user's tmux) uses ~14 cores at default QoS, only nice -n 10 (justfile awake := "caffeinate -i nice -n 10"). The justfile's own comment above low says nice does not leave the CPU to other work on macOS, and 22303247 moved play/review/tournament to taskpolicy -c utility for that reason; longthink was left at nice.

Probe (a Python pool burning CPU for 6 s per worker):

  • 18 workers at default QoS beside the arena: 7.5 effective cores of 18 (42%). Every session's cargo build, test, smoke and slow CI runs at about 40% of the machine. Slow CI: 63-76 s load-adjusted, 185-255 s on the clock.
  • 18 workers at utility QoS beside the arena: 0.2 cores (1%). macOS strictly prefers the higher QoS, so just tournament, just play and just review --agent (all {{ low }}) almost stop while the arena runs. A Warden's gating tournament would effectively hang.

Change: run longthink at utility QoS like the other long compute, keeping caffeinate: on macOS awake := "caffeinate -i taskpolicy -c utility" (Windows unchanged: by low --awake --). Update the justfile comment on awake and the AGENTS.md "Tournaments are expensive" paragraph (the longthink sentence, line ~141) so it says longthink yields too. On an idle machine utility work still gets every core, so the arena loses throughput only while other work runs, which is what --power light is for today.

Expected gain: sessions and checks get ~18 of 18 cores instead of 7.5 while the arena runs, about 2.4x faster wall clock on parallel builds and tests. Tournaments, play and review also run again beside the arena. Side effect to note on the bead, not fix: check budgets count load-adjusted seconds, and the load average still includes the arena, so a check beside a utility-QoS arena gets up to 2.7x budget credit it no longer needs.

Do NOT stop or restart the live arena: it runs in the user's tmux (ttys002) with a terminal UI. The change applies when the user next restarts it (Ctrl-C, then just longthink, which resumes run-003 and costs at most the decisions being searched). Say that in the close reason.

Files: justfile (lines ~20-30, the low/awake variables, and the longthink recipe at ~203), AGENTS.md. Verify with just --evaluate awake, and just longthink --status must still work (it goes through the same recipe).

Acceptance criteria

justfile runs longthink under taskpolicy -c utility on macOS (caffeinate kept, Windows unchanged); AGENTS.md and the justfile comment say so; the live arena is left running; the close reason tells the user to restart it in tmux to apply.
