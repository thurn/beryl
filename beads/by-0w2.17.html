Description

Part of epic by-0w2. The mirror switch (by-0w2.12) left the greedy rollout policy (rust/crates/beryl_search/src/rollout.rs, values.rs) weak in the new WU mirror: just tournament greedy random --games 200 --seed 1 gives 68.0% [61.2, 74.1], against 98.0% on the old PrinceJools deck (still available as decks::VINTAGE_WU_CONTROL). Measured causes, from just play greedy random --seed N transcripts (seeds 1-30): nine of ten losses are greedy drawing from an empty library at 9-24 life, and games run 22 turns (old deck 17). (1) No deck-out awareness: greedy activates every draw ability each turn (Currency Converter loot, Teferi, Hero of Dominaria +1, Jace, Wielder of Mysteries +1, Jace, Vryn's Prodigy, Staff, JTMS 0) and casts Thirst for Discovery / Stock Up regardless of library size. An experiment guarding listed draw abilities while the library holds 5 or fewer (unless we control a WinInsteadOfDrawingFromEmptyLibrary permanent) raised it to 74.5%. (2) Greedy never pressures the player: AttackTarget sends every attacker at the most valuable planeswalker until the opponent is at 6 life (seed 8: the random player is at 20 on turn 22); attacking only walkers of loyalty 3 or less moved it to 76%. (3) values.rs hand-tunes only the old deck's 29 cards; the 17 new cards get estimates (Counterspell, Damn, Winds of Abandon 13; Portable Hole 9). The expert (expert/mod.rs) has the same old-deck card knowledge (Timetwister, Mana Drain, draw_safe) and should be checked against the new cards too. greedy_beats_random in rust/crates/beryl_search/tests/greedy.rs was lowered to 75% of its 40 seeded games by by-0w2.12; restore it to 90%.

Done. greedy v random, 200 games: seed 0 (the acceptance command's default) 96.0%, seed 1 96.5%, seeds 0-3 together 768/800 = 96.0% (was 67-68%). greedy_beats_random asserts 90% again (36 of 40).

Changes (rollout.rs, values.rs), each measured over seeds 0-3:

  • Deck-out guard: DRAWS lists draw abilities and spells (Converter loot, JTMS 0, JVP loot, Staff, THoD +1, Reprieve, Stock Up, Thirst) plus Abhorrent Oculus, whose manifest dread takes two cards a turn. All are worth 0 at library <= 10 unless we hold a wheel or control a WinInsteadOfDrawingFromEmptyLibrary permanent. 5, 7 and 10 measured within 1%; kept 10.
  • Jace, Wielder of Mysteries' -8 only when it wins (library <= 7). It used to be a 200-score ult that decked us at library 8+. This was the single biggest gain (+5 points).
  • Mana Crypt is never cast and valued 0. Its coin flips killed greedy in 20+ turn games. Casting it only before land N measured worse at every N (0/1 best: +6 points).
  • An overloaded sweeper (Damn is BB, so effectively overload-only) needs the opponent's matching permanents to outweigh ours by REMOVAL_MIN.
  • Attacks skip a planeswalker the attackers already declared will kill; otherwise unchanged. Capping walker targets at loyalty 3, as the bead suggested, measured 2 points worse.
  • Hand-tuned values for all 17 new cards.

Expert: left alone (docs call it frozen). Its losses to random are not self-decking (3 of 60 P0 games, none from its own draws). expert v greedy went from 58.0% to 51.5% because greedy got stronger; expert v random went from 86% to 88%.

Re-pinned: rollout corpus hash; greedy perf-count bands (games are longer, up to 511 decisions); the MCTS redeterminization position, now [23,5].

UI: 10 fixtures re-seeded and 9 shot views remapped. The combat-declare test now finds its twin tokens and lone attacker in the recording instead of naming Spellbinder, Malcolm and Insects. pay-default plays vintage-wu-control because the mirror without Mana Crypt rarely offers a payment choice. game-log-counter now uses arrow-player's countered Stock Up.

Surprises: (1) On origin/staging and at 23f24d3, combat-blockers (seed 29) failed to record ('game ended before first priority decision'), so staging's ui-shots was likely red; this push re-seeds it. (2) At be17bae, debug and release builds recorded different fixture games. On current staging they agree byte for byte, so this is already fixed. (3) With a 4-card hand, the selected center card rises over the player's life total (shots text-cut check). game-cast-step now clicks the leftmost card.
