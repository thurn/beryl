The finding (round 3, Depth, phone only, both seats)

The reviewer asked that the striking card be drawn above every other card for its whole lunge and return.

What was done about it, to check

ui/src/stage/tracks.ts depths and lunge: until STRIKE.seen (0.57 of its step, when its blow has been seen) a striker is over every card and over the strikes after it; from then it is still over what rests, under the strikers still on their way, and over the earlier ones drawing back; a creature is over the one that strikes it back throughout. So a later strike whose way crosses an earlier fight goes over it both ways (the builder's own look at phone/yours/combat-19 strip +282 to +349 and +582 to +649 showed it over Beza). The two requirements pull against each other: round 2 asked that the fight's cards not be covered at their blow by a neighbour that has begun its own strike, round 3 that a later striker never go under the earlier fight. The rule gives each striker the top until its blow has been seen. If the reviewer still finds a layer change in a frame, the other way out is time: a blocked attacker's stagger (data/ui-conventions.json, strike, "blocked attacker", 400 ms) long enough that the fight is home before the next strike crosses it.
