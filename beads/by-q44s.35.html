Close reason

enact 5a48890: busy slots (dirty, held by an open bead) never reassigned; push checks the branch

Description

What happened: after the Claude app crashed and restarted, the vizier asked by-q44s.3.19's session to resume. Its worktree was /Users/dthurn/beryl/.worktrees/slot-08 with uncommitted work. Enact then also assigned slot-08 to by-q44s.29 (bd show by-q44s.29: enact_worktree = .worktrees/slot-08), and that session checked out enact/by-q44s.29 there while 3.19 was in the middle of a rebase (git reflog in slot-08: 'checkout: moving from 96709ae5d to enact/by-q44s.29' between 'rebase (start)' and 'rebase (finish)'). The rebase finished with no commit of 3.19's, and the next amend rewrote by-q44s.29's tip commit with 3.19's message. 3.19's commit survived only in the reflog (6a54b8ca5); it was moved to .worktrees/by-q44s.3.19 by hand and slot-08 was put back on enact/by-q44s.29. No work was lost, but about half an hour went into finding and undoing it, and it could easily have lost uncommitted work.

Why (to confirm): slot allocation seems to treat a slot as free when its owning session is not running, as during an app restart, without checking that the slot has an in-progress rebase, uncommitted changes or a branch that is still the bead's enact_branch.

Build: in ~/enact (scripts/enact.py, the worktree slot allocator), never hand out a slot whose current bead is still in progress or whose tree is dirty or mid-rebase; and have enact push refuse to run when the checked-out branch is not the bead's enact_branch. This bead is filed under by-q44s because the run hit it; the fix lands in the enact repository.

Acceptance criteria

A test in ~/enact shows that a slot holding an in-progress bead's worktree with uncommitted changes, or with a rebase in progress, is not given to another bead, even when its session is gone; enact push in a worktree whose branch is not the bead's enact_branch exits 2 with a message saying so.
