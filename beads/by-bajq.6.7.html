Amount::X exists and Thieving Skydiver pays X in a kicker cost. Check whether a spell's own mana cost may hold {X} and whether the cast asks for its value (CR 601.2b). If the vocabulary cannot say it, escalate in data/card-escalations.json with what is missing and say so in this bead; by-bajq.6.4's session then builds it before its own scenarios.

Follow the card-writing skill. Scenarios: X is 2 and the target player draws two cards; X is 0 and nothing is drawn; the opponent is the target; X larger than the target's library (the player draws what is there and loses at the next state-based action check, CR 704.5b).
