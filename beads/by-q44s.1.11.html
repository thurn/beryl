Close reason

Work counters, just b3d gate (lane step) and the allocation gate on staging at 092c600b4

Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Performance gates'. Read docs/benchmarking.md ('Determinism') and tools/src/beryl_tools/ui_counts.py with data/ui-counts.json, the nearest existing gate on counted work.

  • In beryl_scene, fill Frame::work each frame: elements visited, segments evaluated and draw records written. Layout nodes and text runs are the flat-layer slice's.
  • The gated boards are stress-255 and stress-1024, at rest and during their flight of
    1. The last bead of the milestone adds the recorded fixtures.
  • data/beryl3d/gates.json, under frames.<name>: limits on the worst frame's and the total of each counter; on draw calls, upload bytes and blended area for the Baseline tier (start from 16 draw calls, 0.75 screens and 327,680 bytes, and set each from the measurement); and on allocations made in Client::handle. Counters are exact, so their tolerance is 0.
  • just b3d gate (tools package, with --help): plays the gated boards natively and fails on a count over its limit, naming the board, the moment and the counter. --update lowers limits to what was measured and never raises one.
  • The allocation gate: a test binary with a counting global allocator plays every gated board and fails on an allocation inside Client::frame after the game has started, printing a backtrace of the first.
  • rank(name) -> [usize; 8]: the 8 frames that the counters rank highest, pinned by a test, for the count gates to use.
  • gate is a step of the beryl3d lane.

No limit here needs anyone's approval to rise: the epic's rule 8 says when one may, and the commit states it.

Run: just b3d gate; just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene -p beryl_inspect; just b3d lane.

What the prototype found (rust/prototypes/beryl3d, 2026-11-14; read its code for a working example, then build it properly):

  • Blended area: stress-1024 reached 0.73 screens with shadows alone, 3% under the 0.75 limit, before marks, glows or the flat layer. Expect the real boards to exceed it, and set the limit from a measurement.
  • A shadow drawn at one fixed height hides under taller stacks (a 1,024-card grid rises 41 mm). Place a shadow at the height of what lies under it.

Acceptance criteria

just b3d gate passes with limits set from measurement for both stress boards; a planted Vec push in the frame path fails the allocation gate; a planted extra pass over the elements fails a counter's limit; the gate is a step of the lane.
