Description

Symptom: the play-or-draw buttons look disabled and the life orbs look nothing like the web's. Both show at scale 1 in just b3d png opening-play --at prompt:1: the Play button is a darker orange with grey text, unlike the start screen's Start button, and the orb is a small plain circle. Agents and Wardens closed by-q44s.5.16, 5.20 and the Wardens 5.39 to 5.41 without putting the picture beside the web's at the same moment. Decision C5 forbids comparing whole pictures, and nothing took its place (by-q44s.9.39).

Change:

  1. just b3d compare <recording> --at <moment> writes one PNG with the web interface's picture of the same fixture (exported with just b3d export and shot the way just ui-shots shoots it) beside b3d png at the same window size, so that a session can see both in one read.
  2. A furniture parity check that runs on every push, comparing drawn properties rather than whole pictures: an enabled primary button's fill and label colour equal the web's tokens for an enabled button (data/design-tokens.json), a disabled one's equal the disabled tokens, and the life orb's core, bezel width and ring colour equal LifeOrb's in ui/src/components/Player.tsx and life-orbs.css. Read the values from the frame's draws (beryl_scene/src/frame), not from pixels, so the check holds at every scale and tier.

Files: beryl_pictures/src/main.rs, tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/cli.py, beryl_scene/src/widgets/button.rs, hud/players.rs, data/design-tokens.json, beryl_inspect/tests/all/hud.rs.

Acceptance criteria

just b3d compare writes a side-by-side picture of web and Beryl 3D at one moment; a check on every push compares button and life orb draw properties with the web's tokens and fails on the disabled-looking Play button until it is fixed.
