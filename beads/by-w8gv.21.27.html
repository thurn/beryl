Rules for every shape

  • The session holds the prompt's state (K26). The UI draws the marks and button states it is sent and sends clicks as commands through ui/src/ipc.ts. No prompt logic in the UI: nothing there decides what is eligible, selected or allowed.
  • Wording is Arena's exactly and comes from the session (K22; AGENTS.md, UI and Workflow). Exception 3 stands: never ask a question before a choice the next screen can decline.
  • Cards in a prompt are the stage's own nodes, moved by verbs: one visual per object, no borrowed copies.
  • Checks: shape:<name>/<seat>/<layout> in ui/scripts/shapes.mjs (made by by-w8gv.21.26, reporting and selected as ui/scripts/verbs.mjs is), from both seats and in both layouts: the chooser's seat answers the prompt by clicks, the other seat sees what an opponent may see. One check per shape with its purposes as cases, never one per card. A hand-written text snapshot per shape (K24) of the marks and buttons after each click, in beryl_session's tests if milestone 6 wrote none.
  • Today's files are the list of cases; the cut-over deletes them.

Shapes and today's code

  • Order: trigger order (TriggerOrder, Board.tsx about 3200), cards put back in an order.
  • Split into destinations: scry, surveil, look at the top N and keep some (ui/src/screens/Scry.tsx; ui/src/game/ChoiceOverlay.tsx, 895 lines; choices.ts, 766).
  • Pick from a zone: search a library with a way to find nothing (exception 3), discard, a card from a graveyard (Piles.tsx ZoneViewer picks; untapChoice.ts).
  • choiceFlight.ts borrows a table card into an overlay and returns it (useBorrowedCard, takeMovedCard): on the stage the same node flies by a verb, there and back.

The scry flight

One of the five repeated themes, 7 reports (bd list --label bug-report --all --limit 0): the prompt's cards rise from the library, are sorted, and fly to the top or the bottom as by-w8gv.21.20 built those flights. A named case here, citing the reports, from both seats (The local player's scry; Cards the opponent looks at).

Decisions and deviations (executor, staging base f5034c58).

  1. How a prompt's cards get onto the stage. A tray: the cards the session lists (a pick's state.tray, a split's piles) are the stage's own nodes, rested by the layout in a tray over a shade (ui/src/stage/tray.ts; desktop.ts and phone.ts give it a space). They get there by a lift step of the stage's own (table.ts Hold: verb lift with a tray in place of a handling), which the prompt layer plays as it plays a prompt's marks: begun as the prompt opens, begun again (sorted) as each reply moves cards, and ended inside the first beat of the answering view's script that is about one of the cards, or its first beat. So a kept card flies from the tray and the rest go home; nothing is borrowed or copied. The stage's step type is widened to the session's acts plus this one (table.ts Act, Step, Script); no session act was added. Timings are two cases of lift in data/ui-conventions.json (tray 300 ms, sort 200 ms).
  2. The scry flight follows UI Conventions, 'The local player's scry' (Arena: the browser closes at once, nothing flies large to top or bottom, a pill says what happened), not the bead's 'fly to the top or the bottom as by-w8gv.21.20 built those flights': .21.20's own lift check holds that your own look lifts nothing in the session's script, and the script that follows a scry has no step about its cards. On the stage the cards rise from the library into the tray, are sorted there, and go back to the pile as the pill shows; the library's new order then lays them. The case (ui/scripts/shape-cases.mjs scryFlight) holds one node a card at every tick, the library's zone throughout, the face shown until a card is home, top over bottom at the end, and the pill up and then gone, each against its report. It caught a real fault on the way: a card let go was dressed as a back while it glided home (by-1mrh again); a lift's card now keeps its face until it lands (tracks.ts shownUntil).
  3. Order. The triggers are no nodes of the table, so the screen draws them: a row of ability faces, first to resolve at the right, between 'Last' and 'First', as Arena's browser has them (UrGalw_f35w 56:30.5). The two words are Arena's and were missing from the session's table: added as the order's words.piles (wording.rs end_words). A drag sends drop {on, pile 0, at}; a click sends the same with at 0 (put first), so the shape is answered by clicks alone too.
  4. Fixtures. A board with clicks used to hand its seat over when its scripts ended, so a prompt reached only through another shape's prompt could not be a click fixture's stop (Aang's Iceberg's waterbend is a pay prompt, by-w8gv.21.29's). The recorder now hands over at the stop itself and the stand-in answers what comes between (beryl_cli fixture.rs). New click words: drop <pile> <at> <card>, move <item> <at>.
  5. The old choice overlay no longer opens under ?stage for a prompt the stage holds (Game.tsx staged).
  6. Snapshots: milestone 6 wrote them (beryl_session tests/splits.rs for split and order, tests/zone_picks.rs for zone picks: the marks, piles and buttons after each click, by hand). None added.
  7. Not built here: the Revealed Cards pile (by-w8gv.21.39's note). Nothing emits showcase, so it is session script work plus a pile and a browser: filed as by-w8gv.21.55, which by-w8gv.21.31 and the step's Warden now wait on.
