Pay particular attention to:

  • Reconciliation: time categories summing to window lengths, dollar splits summing to totals, and agreement with just ci-value and just ci-report.

  • Collection must stay free for agents: no hook, wrapper or prompt text may be added to any session, and ingest must never run inside another command.
