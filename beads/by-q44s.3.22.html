Code: beryl_scene src/frame/draw.rs (sprites: the cells of the atlas's page 0 that the contract bead by-q44s.3.2 named; Draw::glow, Draw::streak, Draw::icon); beryl_assets src/glyphs.rs (the glyph pages, numbered from 0 today), src/packer.rs (PAGE_SIZE 1024), src/lib.rs (Prepared, Upload); beryl_render src/textures.rs (placeholder_atlas and GLOW_SPRITE, one soft disc), src/shader.wgsl (shade_glow, and the switch on the material in fs), src/renderer.rs, tests/render.rs (tests that sample single pixels), tests/shader_cost.rs; data/beryl3d/gates.json (materials). Web, for how each shape looks: ui/src/stage/glow.ts (Glow: the fragment shaders of a ribbon, a flare and a spark), ui/src/stage/flare.ts (SETTLE, BEAM, TRAIL, STREAK), ui/src/stage/effects.css.

Build:

  • In beryl_assets, a new file src/light.rs: pub fn light_page() -> Vec<u8>, the atlas's page 0, 1024 by 1024, single channel like a glyph page, drawn in code with no image file and no randomness: in the cells of sprites, a soft disc, a burst with 12 thin rays, a ring, a cone, a comet (a bright head with a tail that thins), a ribbon (a bar that is soft across and uniform along), a card-shaped outline (a rounded rectangle ring of a card's proportions), an arrowhead, a wedge, a crescent, a tap mark (a quarter-turn arrow), a moon, a plaque (a filled rounded rectangle) and a coin. Each is white with its shape in the opacity, and leaves a 2-texel empty border in its cell.
  • The glyph worker's pages start at 1 (GlyphWorker's first page number), and the host uploads light_page() as page 0 before the first frame. Find where pages are uploaded from Upload in beryl_host and beryl_3d, and do it there, on the same path.
  • beryl_render: placeholder_atlas and GLOW_SPRITE are deleted; the renderer's own tests upload light_page(). shade_glow keeps its meaning. Add shade_streak(sprite, mesh_uv, tint, params): the sprite's opacity, cut to nothing ahead of the head (params.x) and behind the tail (params.x - params.y) along the quad's length, fading toward the tail, added to what is beneath it like a glow. Add the STREAK case to fs. One texture sample per fragment, the one fs already takes.
  • data/beryl3d/gates.json: a streak entry under materials, set from the measured counts by just b3d gate --gpu --update; the doc's table of materials is unchanged.

Do not build: any effect's records (by-q44s.3.23), the dissolve in shade_card (by-q44s.3.24), or bloom.

Tests: beryl_assets/tests/light.rs: each cell's border texels are 0, the disc's centre is 255 and its opacity never rises with distance from the centre, the ring's centre is 0, the outline's centre is 0 and its edge is 255, and two calls return equal bytes. beryl_render/tests/render.rs: a white Draw::glow with sprites::DISC over a black target is brighter at its centre than half way out, within a tolerance of 4 in 255; a Draw::streak with head 0.5 and tail 0.25 lights a pixel at 0.4 of its length and leaves pixels at 0.1 and 0.8 black. tests/shader_cost.rs holds streak to its limit.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_assets; just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_render; just b3d gate --gpu; just b3d smoke; just b3d lane.

The trail draws sprites::COMET as one streak quad with head 1.0 and tail 1.0 and its head at local +x (u = 1), as streaks.rs does. If the sprites are drawn the other way round, flip the quad in src/effects/light.rs rather than the sprite.
