Description

Review the workflow as built, against Designs E of the plan, and fix what you find.

  • The bar is only as strong as the mutants. Take five converted cards with no scenarios module and, in a scratch branch, make a change to each definition that a player would call a different card (a wrong target filter, a missing "may", a trigger on the wrong event, a static ability on the wrong set, a cost one mana cheaper). Does some check fail for every one? Each that passes is a missing mutant kind or witness: add it.
  • Prelude helpers: do the functions make their callers clearer? Check that names and parameters expose meaningful behavior such as optional targeting and the choice of owner or controller. Look for fixed aliases of straightforward builder calls.
  • The drafter: can it emit a definition that passes just card check and differs from the card? Try five cards with unusual wording.
  • The rewritten skill against the tools: follow it literally on one new card and note every place it is wrong or assumes knowledge.
  • The sample of by-ptc7.3.11: is there any change that tests no card it could break?
  • The pilot's numbers: are they measured as the bead says, and do they support decision P2, or did most cards need hand-written scenarios after all? Say so plainly in the close reason and in the plan.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Findings fixed or filed as children of this milestone. The close reason says whether the generated bar and the draft hold on real cards, with the numbers.
