Close reason

Seven creatures with tokens, scenarios and engine support on staging 36f6fdc; 10k fuzz games pass

Description

Part of Milestone 1 (epic by-b0q): a rules-exact engine, game UI and AI opponents for a mirror match of the 40-card WU Control main deck in docs/sample-decks/2026-10-17-PrinceJools-wu-control.txt (29 distinct cards). Read the epic's description (plan, card table) and design field (decisions) first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md).

Implement these cards in beryl_cards (epic card table): Thundertrap Trainer (offspring token copy), Thieving Skydiver, Malcolm, Alluring Scoundrel, Overlord of the Mistmoors (impending and the Insect tokens), Elite Spellbinder, Adeline, Resplendent Cathar (the Human tokens), Sphinx of False Conclusions. Jace, Vryn's Prodigy is in the planeswalker bead.

Edge cases for scenarios: Thundertrap Trainer with and without offspring, and when no card qualifies; Thieving Skydiver kicked for X against a Map token, Mana Crypt and a Swift-Reconfigured creature, and unkicked; Malcolm's fourth chorus counter casting the discarded card free; Overlord cast for impending (counters removed at end step, becomes a creature) and in full; Elite Spellbinder's card cast for {2} more by its owner; Adeline's power changing and her token arriving attacking; Sphinx dying as a card and as a token copy.

Acceptance criteria

  • Each card listed has one file under its color directory with its CardDef, bodies and scenarios; every ability is exercised by at least one scenario, including the edge cases named; every body cites the CR.
  • All scenarios pass under the strict runner.
  • Fuzz games including these cards raise no invariant (debug, at least 1,000 games on demand; the just ci count still passes).

From by-b0q.1.7 (events/triggers/layers, on staging 235cd13): triggered abilities that target (Thieving Skydiver, Elite Spellbinder) need targets chosen as they go on the stack (CR 603.3d); trigger::put_on_stack doesn't do that yet, so wire it to the casting work's target selection. Token copies: EffectCtx::create_tokens_of(lki.card, n, TokenEntry{one_one}) for Sphinx and offspring; dies-if-not-token via Ability.condition reading info.lki.token. Adeline: Modification::PowerIsCreaturesYouControl + Trigger::YouAttack + TokenEntry{tapped, attacking}. Impending: put_onto_battlefield(.., Some((Time, n))) + StaticCondition::HasCounter(Time) RemoveTypes(CREATURE).

D
Derek Thurn2026-11-05

From Combat (by-b0q.1.8): a creature put onto the battlefield attacking (TokenEntry/Entry.attacking) always attacks the defending player; Object.combat stays NONE. CR 508.4 lets its controller choose a planeswalker the defending player controls instead. Adeline's Human token needs that choice when the opponent has a planeswalker: ask in the body (it can pause now) and set the token's combat = planeswalker slot plus ObjectFlags::ATTACKS_PLANESWALKER. Crew: Ability::crew(n) and Cost.crew exist; Swift Reconfiguration can grant Ability::crew(5).

D
Derek Thurn2026-11-05

Delivered in 74b28d2 (scenario runner: Cast, Activate, Resolve, more Picks and assertions, shared with 1.9/1.11), 9ea6754 (the seven creatures, Human/Insect tokens, engine support) and 36f6fdc (edge-case tests).

Engine additions: enters triggers carry their spell's X (CR 107.3m) into the stack entry; Filter::ManaValueAtMostX evaluated with the entry's X for targets; StaticCondition::Impending and Ability::{offspring, impending, impending_countdown} keyword helpers; EffectCtx::{top_of_library, look_at, discard, discard_a_card, may_choose_object, put_on_bottom_in_random_order, create_attacking_tokens (CR 508.4 choice of player or planeswalker)}; zone::rebind (shuffle_library now uses it, same RNG order). 15 creature subtypes.

Two engine bugs found and fixed: (1) a body that paused left turn.passes == 2, breaking the invariant check; now reset at the pause. (2) knowledge a paused run gained (looks, draws) was dropped with the scratch state, so a determinized state replayed to a different question (e.g. Spellbinder's choice over a hand its controller had looked at). resolve::keep_what_was_seen keeps it when the slot's identity is unchanged; test a_question_after_a_look_keeps_the_look fails without it.

Deviation: Malcolm's chorus count is read only while he is on the battlefield; LKI counters would need ~320 bytes of State (TriggerInfo in 160 queue slots) against 216 bytes of headroom under the 20 KiB cap, so if he leaves before his trigger resolves the free cast isn't offered.

Not covered here because the cards are 1.11's: Skydiver against a Swift-Reconfigured creature, and Adeline's Human attacking a planeswalker (the choice itself is covered by engine test a_token_created_attacking_may_attack_a_planeswalker). Commented on 1.11. Random games end sooner with creatures, so the short-game guards in beryl_search tests went from 50 to 40 decisions. just fuzz 10000 passes.
