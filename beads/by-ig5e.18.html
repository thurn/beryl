Close reason

Fixed zombie double count in cpu sampler (16c07b632); pid identity is ps lstart

Description

On jarvis, 19 of 175 sampled minutes in cpu/samples.jsonl (2026-11-15) sum to more CPU seconds than 18 cores can do in the row's seconds: one minute reached 120.8 cores, with desktop/desktop credited 8,314 CPU seconds, and peak_cores reached 969.7. The efficiency dashboard (efficiency/src/beryl_efficiency/machine.py) now counts such a minute as all cores busy and reports how many there were (over_cores), but core minutes per bead and shared CPU still include the overcount. Find why the sampler (tools/src/beryl_tools/cpu.py, Sampler.step and the reaped-children accounting) charges a process far more than its lifetime could use in one minute, likely a long-lived process first seen mid-run or a parent's reaped-children total jumping, and fix it. Test with a synthetic process table in tools/tests (the sampler's existing tests).

Acceptance criteria

A test reproduces the overcount with a synthetic process table and passes after the fix; on jarvis, after an hour of normal work, just efficiency machine --since 1h shows 0 minutes sampled over the cores.

Derek Thurn2026-11-15

Cause: zombies. A child that has exited but not been reaped often can't be read by proc_pid_rusage (probe: every rusage failure on this user's processes was a zombie or an exiting process), so its CPU came from ps, which prints 0:00.00 for a zombie. Sampler._growth treated the fall as pid reuse and set last[pid] to 0, so when the child disappeared, _forget owed its parent ~0 and the parent's reaped-children growth charged the child's whole lifetime again. Long-lived children produce the big single-key spikes (desktop 8,314 s, session/tool 5,298 s); many short compile children produce the milder 18-26-core minutes. Fix: identity is ps lstart (new Process.start); a changed start = exit + new process; a falling figure for the same process adds nothing and last keeps the max. Rejected source-switch noise (ps vs rusage precision) as the main cause: it is under a second.

Tests: tools/tests/test_cpu.py test_a_zombie_child_is_not_counted_twice_when_reaped (fails before the fix: the 8,000 s child is charged again on reap) and test_a_pid_with_a_new_start_time_is_a_new_process.
