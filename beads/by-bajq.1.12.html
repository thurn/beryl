Description

Cerebral Vortex, {1}{U}{R} instant: "Target player draws two cards, then Cerebral Vortex deals damage to that player equal to the number of cards they've drawn this turn."

Why

Amount::CardsDrawn, the per-player count of cards drawn this turn (by-bajq.1.4, State::counted), is proved only by the test definition Test Tally in rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/tests/trackers.rs. Chains of Mephistopheles and Notion Thief (by-bajq.3.4) read whether a player drew in their draw step, not the number of cards drawn, and no other card of the epic reads it.

It needs a Mountain, which by-bajq.1.8 adds; this bead depends on it.

Scenarios

  • Cast in the opponent's upkeep before their draw: 2 damage. Cast in their main phase after their draw step: 3 damage.
  • A draw from an empty library is not counted: with one card in library, the target draws one and takes damage for one (plus earlier draws).

Follow the card-writing skill: just card brief <name>, just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), scenarios for each instruction and each ruling a scenario can show, and just specs <card> from both seats. A clause the vocabulary cannot say is escalated in data/card-escalations.json. Epic bars hold: just count-gate with no bump (report the count before and after), State<Small> within 20,480 bytes, just test-rust (frozen saves, rollouts_never_change).

Acceptance criteria

Cerebral Vortex's scenarios pass on Beryl, XMage and Forge, with the damage following the cards drawn earlier in the turn.
