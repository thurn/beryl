Description

Review the other half of family I as built (sagas, classes, cases, levelers, morph, Illusionary Mask, the sideboard, Burning Wish and the companion), against Designs I of the plan and CR 702.37, 702.87, 702.139, 708, 711, 715, 717 and 720, and fix what you find.

  • Hidden information: the leak checklist of docs/engine-architecture.md §13 for a face-down spell and a face-down permanent, in the paused position, the save file and the view sent to each seat. Two face-down objects of one controller: can anything tell them apart that the opponent sees or a save records? The reveal when one leaves, and at the end of the game.
  • The new turn-based actions and state-based actions (a Saga's lore counter and its sacrifice, a Case's solve check): each behind a capability, each with no cost when absent, each at the place in the turn the rule gives.
  • State a card creates is in a store (K4 of the architecture): the class level, solved, prepared, the companion's once-a-game use, Lurrus's once-a-turn use. Is any a new field on Object, Player or State?
  • Abilities that exist only at a level or once solved, under ability removal (Humility, Dress Down) and under a copy.
  • Outside the game: a wished card in a pool slot is a card for every rule (the invariants, the "card" filters, the legend rule, a graveyard). The pool running out. A sideboard card's tokens are in the table.
  • Slot order: a wished card's slot, and a face-down spell's, must not order anything a player or a save sees. Run the keystones' scenarios under the three slot numberings.
  • The count and the test definitions, as every Warden of this epic reports them.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Findings fixed or filed as children of this milestone. The close reason gives this half's total change to the count and State<Small>'s size.
