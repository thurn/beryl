Milestone 3's prompt beads depend on this bead: a prompt draws its own marks through Client::lit_mut.

Code: beryl_scene src/verbs/mark.rs and src/light/marks.rs (stubs: look and write), src/light/mod.rs (MarkSpan, Lit), src/table/scene.rs (Look), src/frame/draw.rs (sprites::OUTLINE, sprites::TAP). Web: ui/src/stage/spans.ts (Marked, Spans), ui/src/components/Card.tsx (Highlight: the outline a card wears), ui/src/stage/tracks.ts (PAYING, paying: a permanent picked to pay turns and carries a tap mark), ui/src/stage/face-mark.ts (LIT, faceMark: the mark on a player, drawn on their life total), ui/src/stage/prompt.ts (how a prompt begins and ends marks from its state). Data: marks in data/ui-conventions.json (eligible: a cyan outline; selected: gold; suggested: an ember ring; paying: the permanent turns and carries a tap mark, with no outline; friendly-target: green; hostile-target: red) and the cases of mark (eligible, selected, target, paying, end).

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build:

  • verbs/mark.rs: Span::Begin and Span::End push a SpanChange for Span::Mark(MarkSpan { mark, on }). It moves nothing.
  • light::marks::look sets the state bits of a marked card: ELIGIBLE for eligible, SELECTED for selected, TARGET for the two target marks; and for paying it turns the card's pose toward tapped by PAYING's share over the mark's fade.
  • light::marks::write draws, for each open mark on a card or an ability, one sprites::OUTLINE quad a little larger than the card (light.outline_grow, in millimetres), moving and turning with it, tinted by the mark, at the opacity of its fade, in Band::TableMarks for a card lying on the table and Band::RaisedMarks otherwise; a suggested mark uses the same sprite in the ember color; paying draws sprites::TAP at the card's middle instead. A mark on a player is the same outline round the player's life total (Scene::reach of the player). Add the six colors to src/tokens.rs from the web's CSS variables the Highlight classes use.
  • Two marks on one thing are both drawn, the later begun outside the earlier.

Do not build: any rule for which mark a card gets: the session's scripts and prompt state say. The marks of a card's own record (by-q44s.3.21).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/verbs_mark.rs, on views and scripts built by hand with testing::ViewBuilder, ScriptBuilder and testing::timing: for each case of the verb in data/ui-conventions.json (the five above), a script with that case's duration and delay plays to its end with every object at its placement (agent::steps::rest_equals_placement finds nothing), no_jumps finds nothing, and step_times finds nothing. A card marked eligible has the eligible state in the tree from the mark's begin to its end, across scripts; its outline's corners enclose the card's at every time of a flight; a marked card under another card has its outline in the table's band, so the depth test hides it; a paying permanent is turned and has no outline; a mark on a player is drawn at the anchor; a frame with 255 marks allocates nothing.

When it is done: set BUILT = true in the verb's file, so that Client::next_unbuilt stops reporting it; run just b3d test --update and just b3d timeline-parity --update if those commands exist on staging by then, and commit the lowered lists.

Handoff (0588fdfff):

  • verbs/mark.rs is BUILT: Begin and End write a SpanChange for Span::Mark over the step's own interval. It moves nothing.
  • light/marks.rs: look() sets ELIGIBLE, SELECTED or TARGET while a mark stands (from begins.from until ends.to). For paying it turns the card a quarter toward tapped as the mark fades in, unless the card is already tapped, and lifts it light.paying_lift mm. write() draws one Draw::glow quad per open mark: sprites::OUTLINE, light.outline_grow mm beyond the card's reach half-size, each later mark on the same thing growing by one more step; paying draws sprites::TAP (light.tap_size) instead. A mark on a player is sized from light.player_half_pixels, the web life orb's face in stage pixels, converted at the anchor's depth. The pub fn band() picks TableMarks for a card below light.on_table_below mm and RaisedMarks otherwise. A fade eases out, the curve of both mark cases that take time.
  • Colors: tokens::color::MARK_*, taken from the .stage-mark-* CSS. Values: the new light object in data/beryl3d/space.json.
  • Gotchas: outlines follow Show::reach, so they do not include the paying turn that look() applies (paying draws no outline anyway). Suggested sets no state bit, because StateBits has none for it.
  • Tests: tests/all/verbs_mark.rs (6 tests) and a 255-mark case in tests/frame_allocation.rs. The gate and test-faults.json are unchanged. timeline-parity --update listed card-scenario recordings that would rise; it changed nothing, and the lane's --fixtures run passes.
