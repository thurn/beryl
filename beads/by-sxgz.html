Description

Why this epic exists

Beryl's card checks were built for 48 cards and have let wrong definitions through. Five card definitions did less than their Oracle text said and every check passed (the first entry of docs/bugs.md): Damn shipped without "can't be regenerated". In the authoring pilot (by-w8gv.15), Spellseeker was right and still could not land, because XMage is wrong about it and nobody may record that.

Adding cards at scale needs checks that show a definition says everything its card says, and that do not stop a correct card for a reason outside it.

What is in it

  • by-ilmp: regeneration shields in the engine, and Drudge Skeletons as a real card that regenerates. Today no test can tell Damn's "can't be regenerated" from a plain destroy.
  • by-rq1t: generated scenarios built from witnesses (positions in which one field of a vocabulary entry changes the outcome), held to a mutation check that replaces the test of which instructions a scenario reaches. Blocked by by-ilmp, which gives it its first witness.
  • by-0ebu: a command that records a fault in XMage or Forge only when the other of the two agrees with Beryl and the rule cited is in the pinned release.

Rules the children share

  • A clause is tested with a real card implemented for the purpose (the card-writing skill, "Cards for scenarios"). Nothing is left untested until a deck needs it.
  • Card definitions stay typed effect data in Rust, and scenarios stay the const builder data they are. Neither gets a separate syntax, and Oracle text is not parsed into definitions. The Oracle text diff (just card render) stays the check that a whole sentence is missing.

Related work outside this epic

  • by-w8gv.16.4 (tests stop depending on which cards exist), by-w8gv.16.3 (agent randomness per decision) and by-w8gv.16.8 (overlay tunings) remove the unrelated test failures that stopped the pilot's other cards.
  • by-gdn3.6 (slow CI runs only the checks a change can affect) has a note on what a card-only change should select.
