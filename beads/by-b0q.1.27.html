Description

Part of Milestone 1 (epic by-b0q): a rules-exact engine, game UI and AI opponents for a mirror match of the 40-card WU Control main deck in docs/sample-decks/2026-10-17-PrinceJools-wu-control.txt (29 distinct cards). Read the epic's description (plan, card table) and design field (decisions) first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md).

Design decision 16. Meticulously reproduce the mockup parts listed below, one part at a time:

  1. Measure the part from the PNG with just ui-probe: sizes, corner radii, border widths, colors, shadow offset and blur, glow color and falloff, gradient stops, letter size, weight and spacing.
  2. Implement or adjust the real component and its styles.
  3. Run just ui-compare on the part and study the side by side, onion skin, difference and flip outputs, zoomed.
  4. Repeat until the part is indistinguishable from the mockup.

Compare every mockup a part appears in, not just one. In replicas the art windows hold the mockup's own art, and masked regions (the MAGIC card back's artwork, the mouse cursor) don't count. Garbled mockup text (for example "Mounstain") is matched in style, not spelling. Changes go into the components and styles the game uses, never into replica-only overrides. If the region file lacks a part or draws it wrong, fix the region file.

Done for a part: a fresh reviewer subagent (not your own judgment), given the part's side by side, onion skin and difference images, calls it indistinguishable from the mockup at a glance. No numeric thresholds. Post each part's verdict, and anything the reviewer accepted as out of reach (painted lighting, texture noise), as a comment on this bead.

Parts: every card face at every size it appears, in all five mockups.

  • Battlefield cards: the name bar colored by card color, the art window, the black frame and rounded corners, the P/T badge, the flying-ability icon badge. The large Inferno Titan 6/6 in section 04 of ui_system.png.
  • Tapped and untapped lands (rotation, the vertical name bar of a tapped card), and the cards inside land stacks and the graveyard at small size.
  • Hand cards in the fan: name bar, art, the type line peeking at the bottom.
  • Full cards: Counterspell in the scry tray (mana cost symbols, type line, rules box) and the Preordain source card (scry.png, ui_system.png section 06).
  • The triggered-ability stack card: Inferno Titan, "Triggered Ability", the rules text, the rounded bottom (trigger_on_stack_with_target.png, section 05).
  • States: source (gold glow), target (cyan glow), and the lifted, tilted, cyan-glowing card being dragged in the scry tray.
  • The Project Beryl card back at library, opponent-hand and scry-tray sizes, against scry.png.
  • Choose the font family for card names, type lines, rules text and P/T numerals with the font sheets.

Acceptance criteria

  • Every part listed has a reviewer verdict of indistinguishable at a glance, posted as a comment on this bead with any accepted residuals.
  • The changes live in the game's own components and styles.
  • npm run typecheck, prettier, just smoke and just check pass; no screenshots, crops or comparison outputs committed.

Reviewer verdicts (fresh subagents, five rounds; each judged ui-compare's in-place side-by-side/onion/diff plus an aligned copy with placement owned by other beads cancelled out). Final state at staging 44861e7.

INDISTINGUISHABLE at a glance (37 of 47):

  • battlefield: opponent-strix, opponent-beast, you-goblin-guide, you-angel, opponent-island-stack, opponent-swamp-stack, you-mountain-stack, you-plains-stack, hand-bolt, hand-swords, hand-mountain, hand-plateau, opponent-graveyard, opponent-library, you-library (libraries judged on shape/size/shadow: MAGIC back masked)
  • combat: opponent-strix, opponent-beast-blocker, you-goblin-guide-attacker (tapped), you-angel-attacker
  • trigger: opponent-beast, you-angel, you-mountain-stack-tapped, stack-trigger
  • scry: tray-counterspell, source-card
  • ui_system: 03-graveyard, 03-lands-stacked, 03-library, 04-titan, 04-tapped, 04-untapped, 04-target, 05-attacker, 05-blocker, 05-source, 05-target, 06-source-card Accepted residuals named by reviewers: painted/marbled rim texture and frame bevel, leather and parchment grain, the mockups' slightly darker red rims, P/T badge tint.

Still DIFFERS (10), left to the follow-up bead:

  • trigger/opponent-strix-target, trigger/you-titan-source, ui/04-source: glow strength. The mockups disagree (ui_system 04 wraps the rim in light; the trigger screen keeps a black frame inside a thick neon ring); one round's fix flipped the other's verdict.
  • scry/tray-island, ui/06-island: the full-art Island's frame reads silver where the mockup's reads blue-lined chrome; the mockup's glow is a crisper blue line.
  • scry/tray-backs, ui/06-backs: the back looks washed out where the dragged Island's halo overlaps it; crest/wordmark contrast.
  • ui/05-trigger: rules text face/size and line breaks; mockup's box edge darker.
  • ui/04-hand: mockup crops the three cards at their type lines and its names are bolder.
  • ui/06-counterspell: section 06 is the real overlay scaled down; the mockup's sketch draws that card squatter with larger text and mana symbols.

Decisions: card font Crimson Pro (bundled, OFL) for names, type lines, rules text and P/T, chosen by ui-fonts sheets; one blue-black P/T ink because the mockups disagree (blue on battlefield.png, near black elsewhere); names fit their bars by measuring overflow (ResizeObserver, follows the tap animation); multicolored cards: gold rim with the first color as accent (Baleful Strix); full-art basic land (scry Island) as a CardFace flag; dual lands keep the pale land frame; the hand fixtures and the gallery's mini hand show cards without mana costs because the mockups draw them that way; region file: gallery art windows and land art windows corrected (they were too short or included rim slivers). Out of scope and flagged to by-b0q.1.28: the opponent's hand turns its backs 180° (the mockup shows their bottoms).
