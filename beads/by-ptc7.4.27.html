Description

With BERYL_FUZZ_GAMES=2000 and release debug assertions enabled, beryl_search tests/games.rs panics with "derived read while dirty" in random_games_keep_the_invariants (game 118), agents_decide_the_same_on_a_redeterminized_state and a_color_given_in_every_zone_shows_nothing_hidden. All three reproduce at 42b04e9f1 and on 679bf2933. Their backtraces go through rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/drawing.rs::draw at the call to replace::draw, where replace/draws.rs reads a permanent controller to decide which replacements apply. The shrink fuzzer also reported the panic after 114 choices at seed 3842646118. Identify what dirties the layers in the failing position and refresh before readers that need the result, keeping the grouped draw events and the fast path for games without draw replacements.

Acceptance criteria

Add a deterministic regression for the reduced failing draw position. With BERYL_FUZZ_GAMES=2000 and release debug assertions and overflow checks enabled, the games and shrink test binaries pass. Document the defect and guard in docs/bugs.md and preserve frozen saves, rollout pins and the count gate.
