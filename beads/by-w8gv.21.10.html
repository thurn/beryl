What moves to the builder

  • Target arrows and the line from an ability to its source: Board.tsx boardArrows (about lines 2636 to 2760) derives them from the stack's items and their targets (protocol.rs TargetRef, StackItem); ui/src/components/Stack.tsx Arrows draws them.
  • Combat lines: attackers to the player or planeswalker they attack, blockers to what they block (protocol.rs Combat, PlayerView.pending_attackers; ui/src/game/combat.ts).
  • Mana: ui/src/game/ManaFlow.tsx manaMotes (about line 127) decides which mana pays for which spell or ability (GameEvent::Mana, ResolutionPayment).
  • Marks a script shows outside a prompt, as by-w8gv.21.8 listed them.
  • Follow the settled entries and exceptions in data/ui-conventions.json: A line from an ability to its source (ships as Arena's always-on thin line), Two items on the stack, Attacking a planeswalker, Mana into what it pays for, exception 4 (target arrows by relationship), exception 7 (own-target warning).
  • A span left open when a script ends (an arrow from a spell still on the stack) is carried by the session into the seat's next script: hold the open spans per seat beside the last view.

Rule invariants

script/check.rs, as debug assertions beside the replay check:

  • no arrow or line points at an object that is moving or has left;
  • an arrow is ended before its target moves;
  • one visual per object, unless the verb says otherwise in by-w8gv.21.8's table (Arena's cast chooser shows a card twice);
  • every beginning has an end, in this script or carried.

These are what two of the five repeated themes come to: arrows on cards that moved (6 reports) and the combat line that flickers (5). Find the reports with bd list --label bug-report --all --limit 0 and write each theme's failing sequence as a test the invariants pass.

Decisions (spans, mana and the rule invariants)

Code: rust/crates/beryl_session/src/script/ — check.rs (Open, carried: the rule invariants), spans.rs (wanted: the arrows and lines a table calls for; Builder::part, sync, lower, close), mana.rs (streaks and pool counts), infer.rs (Pays, drains, making: the guesses about mana), build.rs (Screen.spans and broken; emit ends and begins spans around every step). Tests: tests/script_spans.rs, tests/script_build.rs, tests/common/mod.rs.

  • Arrows and lines are the table's, by difference. After every step the builder compares the spans open with the ones its working table calls for (each stack item's targets, each ability's source on the battlefield, each blocker's attacker, each attacker's destination where a planeswalker could be attacked) and writes the Begins and Ends. A screen holds its open spans beside its table and its next script ends them. A reset's beats are the Begins of its view's spans, in one beat.
  • An End goes in a beat of its own before the beat that moves either end of the arrow, never in that beat: "ended before its target moves" is then checkable, and a batch (a sweeper) stays one beat. A Begin joins the beat of the step that calls for it, or the first beat after one that moved an end. One exception, from Arena (IEXcjBn3BXk 20:39.3, the line "from the frame it lifts"): a spell or ability going on the stack takes its own arrows with it in that beat.
  • What moves: fly, appear, vanish and emerge. Tap, flip, strike, count and set leave a thing in place, so a block line stays through the strike and ends when combat's roles clear or a creature leaves. showcase is the verb that shows a card twice, and is no move.
  • An arrow ended because its target changed zones, or by the doing of its own spell or ability, is not drawn again while that item is on the stack (CR 400.7). The view still names the card's slot as the target, which is how the UI came to point at graveyards; Screen.broken remembers the pair until the item leaves.
  • A combat line begins mid-script only if the view's end has it too. Found by a soak of 80 random games through Game: a record's part in combat can be the view before's until the script settles, and the line pointed at the player before the planeswalker (by-m0wf again). No Block event exists and Attack has no targets, so a combat that begins and ends inside one script shows no line: step two (by-w8gv.21.5) can give them.
  • No mark is emitted outside a prompt. All six marks are a prompt's (by-w8gv.21.8: Arena puts no glow on a stack item's target or an ability's source). check.rs holds Mark spans to the same rules, for the session's prompt state (K26, by-w8gv.11.1) to use.
  • Lift: ended in a beat of its own as the script begins (before what the look did shows), begun in the last beat.
  • Mana: each Mana event is a streak from its source into the spell or ability it pays for, in the beat of the tap; into an ability not yet on the stack, the streaks wait for its emerge. Mana nothing is paid with counts in the pool, as far as the view's end still has it there; what a pool lost streaks from the player into the first thing they cast, activate or pay into (not seen in Arena: listed in data/ui-conventions.json as "Mana spent from a pool", with "A target that moves").
  • Two items on the stack: the top one gets its arrow at the one under it (the settled entry), which today's boardArrows still skips.

Rule invariants (check.rs)

carried(table, open, script) -> the spans left open, or the first step that breaks a rule. Run in debug beside the replay check for every script, with an assertion that what the script leaves open is what the screen holds. (1) a span begins only on things on the table that are not moving in that beat; (2) nothing moves while a span is on it, or in the beat its span ended; (3) one visual per thing: nothing moves twice in a beat, nothing appears that is there, no span begins twice; (4) an End ends only what is open; what is left is carried, and a reset drops it.

Deviations

  • The test harness now gives each view the decision open at its state (two passes over the scenario: visit for the decisions, watch for the events), as a waiting player's view: pending attackers and card handling are then real. beryl_scenario is untouched: run.rs is at its file-length allowance, so no combined runner was added.
  • The fix for the transient combat line is in my own code from this session and is not a rules engine bug: no docs/bugs.md entry.

Measured

  • Soak, not committed: 80 seeded WU Control games with random answers and undos, 70,663 steps of which 9,214 begin or end a span: no assertion fails, and no span ends and begins again within a script.

For the stage (by-w8gv.21.14) and its verbs:

  • A reset's beats are the Begins of the view's spans: show them at once. Otherwise keep every span drawn from its + step to its - step, across scripts.
  • An End always sits in a beat of its own just before the beat that moves either end of the arrow, so a beat of only - steps should take no visible time of its own beyond the arrow going. A + step joins the beat of the step that calls for it; its from-end may be a spell or ability travelling to the stack in that same beat (the source line stretches from the frame the ability lifts), never anything else in motion.
  • point attack goes to a planeswalker (#slot) or to a player; it exists only while the defender controls a planeswalker. point source goes from the ability to its permanent: hiding it during a prompt is the stage's (UI Conventions, A line from an ability to its source).
  • streak with from = a player is mana out of that player's pool; from absent is a source with no place left on the table (a sacrificed Treasure).
  • No mark step is built: marks come with the session's prompt state.

For step two (by-w8gv.21.5): the guesses are infer::payments and making (which mana pays for what), and in spans.rs the rule that a combat line begins only if the view's end has it (Builder.lines). With causes and a Block event, a combat that starts and ends inside one script can show its lines, and Screen.broken can become the engine's own word that a target is a new object.

For tests: common::views(card, scenario, tweak) gives both seats' views with scripts; steps(card, scenario, part) in script_spans.rs gives every step whose text has part with its script's number. Table::of(view) has unnumbered abilities, so carried() by hand works on a spells-only table (the Counterspell scenario) or after replaying a reset.

A reviewer should check: the definition of a move in check.rs (tap, flip and strike are not); that an End in the same beat as the move is a violation and the builder never writes one (Builder::part splits the beat); wanted() against boardArrows (the two-items case now draws its arrow, by the settled entry); the pool arithmetic in mana.rs (pool only counts up to what the view's end has).
