Description

Part of Milestone 1 (epic by-b0q): a rules-exact engine, game UI and AI opponents for a mirror match of the 40-card WU Control main deck in docs/sample-decks/2026-10-17-PrinceJools-wu-control.txt (29 distinct cards). Read the epic's description (plan, card table) and design field (decisions) first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md).

Design decision 16. Meticulously reproduce the mockup parts listed below, one part at a time:

  1. Measure the part from the PNG with just ui-probe: sizes, corner radii, border widths, colors, shadow offset and blur, glow color and falloff, gradient stops, letter size, weight and spacing.
  2. Implement or adjust the real component and its styles.
  3. Run just ui-compare on the part and study the side by side, onion skin, difference and flip outputs, zoomed.
  4. Repeat until the part is indistinguishable from the mockup.

Compare every mockup a part appears in, not just one. In replicas the art windows hold the mockup's own art, and masked regions (the MAGIC card back's artwork, the mouse cursor) don't count. Garbled mockup text (for example "Mounstain") is matched in style, not spelling. Changes go into the components and styles the game uses, never into replica-only overrides. If the region file lacks a part or draws it wrong, fix the region file.

Done for a part: a fresh reviewer subagent (not your own judgment), given the part's side by side, onion skin and difference images, calls it indistinguishable from the mockup at a glance. No numeric thresholds. Post each part's verdict, and anything the reviewer accepted as out of reach (painted lighting, texture noise), as a comment on this bead.

Parts: everything around the cards, in all five mockups.

  • Life orbs: active (22, glow, silver wings) and inactive (17); the player badges ("Opponent", "You": diamond icon on a dark band).
  • Zones: the library pile's depth and shadow; the graveyard pile; land stacks (offsets, the ×N badge); the opponent's hand of card backs along the top edge; the arc, overlap and rotation of the player's hand fan.
  • Combat: the attacker's angular gold chevron and the blocker's rounded mark (section 05, combat.png), the attack and block lines with their end dots, the gold arc from an unblocked attacker to the life orb, and the "COMBAT, Blockers declared" banner with its ornaments.
  • Stack arrows: the gold source-to-ability curve with dot ends; the cyan ability-to-target curve with its arrowhead and glow.
  • The scry overlay shell: the dimmed, blurred battlefield backdrop, the bordered tray, the BOTTOM and TOP labels, and how the tray fans its cards.

Acceptance criteria

  • Every part listed has a reviewer verdict of indistinguishable at a glance, posted as a comment on this bead with any accepted residuals.
  • The changes live in the game's own components and styles.
  • npm run typecheck, prettier, just smoke and just check pass; no screenshots, crops or comparison outputs committed.

INDISTINGUISHABLE AT A GLANCE:

  • Player badges: battlefield and combat Opponent/You. Residuals: name ~2px low, hand-painted notch in the mockup's diamond.
  • Life orbs: trigger You (inactive); ui_system 03 Active and Inactive. Accepted design conflicts: the sheet omits the screens' rays and under-glow, and fans its blades flatter.
  • Libraries: battlefield opponent and You, ui_system 03. Residual: warm brown tint on the mockup's edges.
  • Land stacks: battlefield Island, Mountain, Plains. Residual: copies step down 1-4px differently (onion only).
  • Opponent hand (battlefield); player hand fan (battlefield).
  • Scry: tray fan, dimmed/blurred backdrop, BOTTOM and TOP labels. Residual: the mockup paints its middle back taller than a card.
  • Combat: block link, attack arc, COMBAT banner. Cyan stack arrow (borderline: glow slightly paler).
  • ui_system 05 attacker and blocker marks: accepted as design conflicts (the sheet draws a double nested chevron and a detached arc; the screens a single chevron with a light fan and a notched crescent hugging the card, which the game follows).

NOT YET at the last review (fixes for several were pushed after it and are unreviewed):

  • Opponent life orbs (battlefield/combat/trigger): rim brightness, under-glow softness, blade warmth/crossing. Active You orbs (battlefield/combat): thin hot ray cores and a white top bloom (bloom added after review).
  • Badges in trigger and the sheet: those mockups paint the emblem bright gold with a notch, the battlefield and combat ones dim; no game state distinguishes them, so the majority look is kept (mockup inconsistency).
  • Graveyards (table and sheet): size/depth refits pushed after review.
  • Swamp stack: cards ~7% smaller than painted (card size shared with the Island stack, which passes). Tapped Mountain stack: the turned card's footprint (card-face geometry, by-b0q.1.27's area).
  • ui_system 03 lands stacked: x3 badge restyled and moved after review.
  • Trigger hand: outer cards ~2 degrees flatter (the battlefield fan with the same constants passes).
  • Attacker chevrons and the Beast blocker mark: reviewers contradicted each other across rounds (concave vs straight vs bowed arms; pale vs saturated gold). Current: near-straight arms with ray streaks, gold crescent with a white-hot edge.
  • Gold stack arrow, ui_system 05 link and arrows: arrowhead, dot size and S-curve fixes pushed after review.
  • ui_system 06 tray and labels: design conflict. The sheet shows the live overlay at 44%; its painted tray is squarer with larger cards than the screen's, so one component cannot match both.

Notes: reviewer measurements were frequently contradictory between rounds (e.g. Mountain copy spacing 11 vs 20px; hand height), so later fixes were made only where a reviewer's claim matched my own probe measurements.
