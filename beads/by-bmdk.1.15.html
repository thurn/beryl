Handoff: Reviewed beryl_card_store facts and prefix-first, case-insensitive search, plus all five table assists. A throwaway pass ran the text readers on every committed store record (34,071 records, 34,928 faces). Compared accepted and rejected examples and checked the before/after differences. Tapped entry now requires unconditional text about the card itself, so a player handles conditions manually.
