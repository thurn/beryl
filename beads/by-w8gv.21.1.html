Description

Settle what the first audit left open, and put the design language into data (plan K22, K28). No UI code changes here.

Context: docs/ui-conventions.md was rewritten by hand on 2026-11-10 from an audit of 180 bug reports against the Arena recordings. Most findings rest on one instance; ten items are under "Still open". Use the arena-video skill (.agents/skills/arena-video/SKILL.md): find moments in the text index, then look at stills or short filmstrips. The recordings are cube drafts, so look for the same kind of event on any card.

  1. Each item under "Still open": find it in at least two recordings, and record what Arena does with the recording id and time. Arena wins by default. Move the item to "Settled as Arena", or to "Not seen in Arena" when the recordings cannot show it. The most important is whether "pass to end step" keeps passing when the opponent acts (by-sqo).
  2. Re-check on at least two instances the single-instance findings behind "Settled as Arena": tokens appearing at their slot, permanents dissolving in place, the cast chooser, a countered spell leaving first, the arrow between two stack items, the size of cards the opponent looks at, the additional-cost chooser opening before mana taps (seen for Delve only). Measure Arena's land-stacking threshold for exception 5, and explain the red curve at UrGalw_f35w 48:36 against exception 4.
  3. Three recordings are unindexed (krskVfn2v5Q, bJQDi6NIrZA, I3Te0m1bBFw). Index them if they are needed, one at a time (just video index takes every core; never inside a check).
  4. Design-language data: one file under data/ holding every entry of docs/ui-conventions.md (kind: exception, settled, no-equivalent, not-seen, open; number for exceptions; the rule; the report beads; what Arena does with its evidence). A by command generates docs/ui-conventions.md from it, and the repo lane fails when the file is stale, the way ui/src/generated/protocol.ts is checked. Later beads add verb values and marks to the same data. Code in tools/src/beryl_tools (cli.py for the command, checks.py for the lane step); pytest for the generator.
  5. Where Arena turns out to differ from what a report of the user's asked, or contradicts a ruling already made, do not stop and do not ask: apply Arena, and list every such case in ONE bead labelled human (title, what he asked, what Arena does, the still's path). Nothing depends on that bead.

Never crawl ~ with find. Commit no stills or filmstrips.

Acceptance criteria

Every item under Still open is settled with a recording id and time, or says why the recordings cannot settle it; the single-instance findings are re-checked on two instances; docs/ui-conventions.md is generated from the data file and the repo lane fails when it is stale; one bead labelled human lists every case where Arena differs from a request or a ruling; just check passes.
