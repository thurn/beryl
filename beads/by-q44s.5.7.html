Frame thread: no icon is rasterized there. icons::rasterize, the only rasterizing function, is called only from Atlas::add_icons on the glyph worker's thread, and it counts into a thread-local (icons_rasterized_on_this_thread). beryl_pictures' the_prompt_bar_draws_text_rasterized_on_another_thread asserts the count is 0 on the drawing thread after Headless has asked for icons and drawn, and beryl_assets' every_icon_rasterizes_to_a_cell_partly_inked_on_another_thread asserts the same for the asking thread. just b3d gate passed without --update (stress-255, stress-1024, verbs-zones, pay-default). Deviations: (1) icons share the glyph worker's thread and pages instead of a separate worker or page: GlyphWorker::icons(scale) packs cells into fresh shelf space, so a rescale never overwrites the old cells the client still draws. (2) The client's table is keyed by (IconId, size in points), and a cell is drawn over its box in points, so old cells draw scaled until the new ones replace them. (3) Single channel: the phase icons' dark fills are drawn at coverage scaled by their luma against the cream outline (0.18 / 0.24 / 0.38), full control's dark fill at 0.24. (4) PLUS and MINUS have no SVG in the web (text there): strokes 2 wide, the phone menu's weight. (5) Sizes follow the web: 22 for icon buttons, step stops and pass-on; 28 for full control; 32 for phases. (6) Step ids: outlined after each filled one (11..34), phases moved to 35..39. (7) The shader's ICON case added 2 naga expressions to the fragment entry, so every material limit rose by 2 (numbers in the commit). Doc said tiny-skia 0.12.0; corrected to 0.11.4, which the bead pins and the lockfile already had.
