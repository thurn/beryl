Description

Why

Decision R14: no strength bar, but an agent that cannot answer a new kind of decision, or that loops, makes a card unplayable against the AI and poisons rollouts.

What to do

  1. Run just fuzz. It takes only a number of games and deals each seed's two decks from every implemented card (beryl_cards::decks::fuzz_deck), so the keystones are in its decks already. To play the cards of one family together, add a test beside the fuzzer's (beryl_search/tests/games.rs) that deals with decks::fuzz_deck_from over that family's cards and the basic lands, one test per present-cost family. Every invariant failure is a bug to fix here or, if it is a rules bug in a group's code, in that code with a docs/bugs.md entry.
  2. Play seeded games of each of beryl_search::AGENTS against each other with those decks (beryl_cli; read "Tournaments" in rust/AGENTS.md first and keep it small: a few games per pairing, at utility priority). No game may end Stalled for a reason other than a budget, and none may panic.
  3. The card values agents derive from definitions (beryl_search/src/{values.rs, estimate.rs}, beryl_card/src/facts.rs) must give every new card a value: a new instruction or modification that facts.rs does not read is a hole.
  4. The expert system (beryl_search/src/expert/) must answer every new DecisionKind and Purpose with something legal; its exhaustive matches say where.
  5. just exhaust to depth 3 from each new card's scenarios (the fast check does this; run depth 5 on the keystones once).

Scenarios that cross groups

Two scenarios need cards of groups 5 and 7 together, and this is the first bead behind both. Write them in Stifle's card file (by-bajq.8.1):

  • Stifle on Dark Depths' state trigger: the trigger is countered and triggers again.
  • Stifle on the storm trigger of Tendrils of Agony: no copies are made.

Record in the close reason: games played per pairing, stalls by reason, and the fuzzer's count of abilities random play never reached. Also record, for each family's games, how many left State<Small> for the slower Large representation and which list filled. A family in which more than one game in ten does is a tier:opus bead under that family's group, with the list and the count.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate; R2 in the plan). The count is exact, so report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust). A change to what the WU mirror's rollouts do is a bug in this epic.
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (root AGENTS.md); just citations.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill: just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), every instruction carried out by its own scenarios, and just specs <card> passing the generic UI checks from both seats. A disagreement with an oracle is settled by the rules and recorded in data/oracle-divergences.json.
  • Read the card's rulings before writing its scenarios (just card brief <name> prints Scryfall's). Each statement in a ruling that a scenario can show is a scenario of the card. Where one cannot be shown yet, the close reason names the ruling and why.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry.
  • If the design in the plan is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.

Acceptance criteria

No panic, no invariant failure and no stall other than a budget across the runs listed. Every new card has a derived value.

Decisions so far:

  • The Stifle-on-Dark-Depths scenario already exists in Dark Depths' file ("countered, it triggers again", from the copies group), so only the storm one was added, to Stifle's file. Its stack(2) check after casting Tendrils was dropped: XMage reports a stack of 1 there while Beryl and Forge have 2; the life totals show no copy was made, and all three engines pass the scenario as written.
  • beryl_search::AGENTS' two deep agents spend 30M engine steps a decision, too much on a machine at load 50-80. The tournaments play their exact families as long-think entrants at 300k (pimc18/omniscient/rollout/tuned/anchor@300k, pimc72/hidden/rollout/tuned/anchor+guard+hold+combat+sure+paired@300k): the same decision code, a smaller search. mcts-hidden plays at --iterations 50 --trees 1. The retired expert and greedy agents play too, since step 4 asks about the expert.
  • Card values: every card gets a value under both knowledge tiers (values::shown and estimate::estimate are total over definitions). The estimate's features ignore reanimation (Animate Dead, Show and Tell), life drain (Tendrils), turn control (Mindslaver) and phasing (Teferi's Protection); those cards are valued from mana value, stats and the features they do have. Adding features would need weights the tuned cards can't fit (they have none of these), so no change.
  • Expert: its rules all play for d.player; under Mindslaver the decider is another player, so it now defers to the policy, which picks the worst for the controlled player as the other agents do. Every other kind falls back to the exhaustive policy, and best returns a candidate index, so answers are always legal.
  • Random games that outgrow Small now continue in Large in the fuzzer (as the app does) instead of failing; the identity family's Worldgorger Dragon exiles every other permanent its controller has with an ExiledWith memo each, which outgrows Small's 32 memos.
