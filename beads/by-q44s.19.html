Close reason

Worst frame 26.8M -> 13.3M (stress-1024), 6.7M -> 3.4M (stress-255); targets not reached, remainder recorded and filed as by-q44s.22

Description

Symptom: after the change that re-places only the groups a step touched (commit 7618d7b49), just b3d gate --counts measures the worst frame of stress-1024 at 26,838,750 instructions and stress-255 at 6,713,316, against the targets of 4,000,000 and 2,000,000 in docs/beryl-3d.md ('What it is held to'). The frame is still frame 30, where Client::handle compiles the second view's script of 40 flights, each moving one permanent to the end of the battlefield.

Why (callgrind self cost of ranked.stress_1024_0 in beryl3d-bench, ~/gungraun/b3d-gate/beryl_inspect/frame_counts/frames/): the cost is now spread out. About 25% is malloc and free. Each step still places a whole row of about 256 cards: members in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/place.rs scans the whole battlefield, and lay_row in table/rows.rs sorts the row by name (by_name, about 3%), lands_in_order sorts again, and piles allocates one Vec per group. Motion::affected in play/compile.rs sorts the affected slots (about 4%). Played::leave (rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/script/table.rs) runs retain over the 1,024-entry battlefield for each step. compile also places the whole table twice (the view before, and finals), and FrameBuilder::finish (frame/order.rs) is about 3%.

Possible fixes, cheapest first: (1) make piles return groups as ranges into one index vector instead of a Vec per group, and let lay_row reuse its buffers; (2) keep each row's name order between steps so a step that moves one card updates it instead of sorting the row again; (3) let Motion start from the table's current scene objects instead of placing the view before again. Keep every segment exactly as now: just b3d gate, just b3d lane and the scripts' recorded values must not change (with records kept, a debug build already asserts that incremental placement equals a full one).

Then run just b3d gate --counts --update (about 30 minutes in beryl3d-bench) and commit the lowered limits in data/beryl3d/gates.json with the old and new counts in the commit body.

Acceptance criteria

The worst frame of stress-255 is under 2,000,000 instructions and of stress-1024 under 4,000,000 in just b3d gate --counts, or the bead records what stops it with the measured numbers; gates.json holds the lowered limits; segments are unchanged (just b3d cargo-test, just b3d gate and just b3d lane pass).
