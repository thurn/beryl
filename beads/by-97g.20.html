Description

Part of the ladder epic by-97g. Read docs/ai-strategy.md first: §2.2 and §4.7.

just why ENTRY --a LABEL --b LABEL [--rollouts 2000]: what the champion's rollouts do after each of two candidates of one suite entry. Two incidents ended "cause not found"; this is the tool that finds causes.

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/blunders.rs: how an entry is replayed to its position (the function that returns the game and the decision open there) and how a decision's candidates are labeled (crate::transcript::labels).
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/rollout/play.rs: soft_rollout and its loop, which answers every decision with the softmax policy.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs: ROLLOUT_TEMPERATURE, and Tree::deal for how a hidden world is dealt from an observation (obs.determinize).
  • New file rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/why.rs; main.rs; the justfile (copy the blunders recipe).

Build

  1. Replay the entry to its position. --a and --b are candidate labels as just blunders prints them (for example "attack with Beza, the Bounding Spring" and done). An unknown label is an error that lists the labels.
  2. For each of N rollouts (default 2,000): deal one hidden world from the acting player's observation, with one seed. Copy it twice. Apply A to one copy and B to the other. Play each out with the champion's softmax rollout policy from the same seed. While playing, record each decision of the next two turns as (player, decision kind, label of the choice made).
  3. Print:
    • the win rate of the acting player after A and after B, and their difference with its standard error over the pairs;
    • the 30 recorded (player, kind, label) events whose count per 1,000 rollouts differs most between A and B, with both counts;
    • three full transcripts of pairs where A won and B lost, and three where B won and A lost, each pair side by side or one after the other.

Tests

  • With A and B the same label, the win rates are equal and the event table is empty.
  • On the entry oust-own-token, --a "cast Oust" --b pass runs and prints the three parts.

Done

Pushed with enact push. Run just why spirits-stay-home --a "attack with Spirit Token" --b done and paste the win rates and the top ten events into the notes.
