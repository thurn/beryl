Close reason

Regeneration shields in engine and vocabulary; Drudge Skeletons; Damn scenarios tell Forbidden from Allowed; oracle exporters handle cards in no deck

Description

What was seen

Damn's Oracle text says "A creature destroyed this way can't be regenerated." Its definition says so too (destroy_without_regeneration, rust/cards/cards_2021_h1/mh2/damn.rs), but no test can tell that definition from a plain destroy. Changing Regeneration::Forbidden to Regeneration::Allowed in Damn changes the outcome of no scenario. The clause was left out of Damn once already and every check passed (the first entry of docs/bugs.md).

Why

The engine has no regeneration shields. rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/actions/destroy.rs (the doc comment near line 42) says the regeneration field of Effect::Destroy is not read, because every destruction already behaves as "can't be regenerated" does. No implemented card regenerates, so no scenario can put a shield in front of Damn.

The project rule is that a clause is tested with a real card implemented for the purpose, never skipped until a deck needs it (the card-writing skill, "Cards for scenarios"). A card writer cannot do that here, because it needs a vocabulary entry and engine work.

just census counts effect:Regenerate on 266 Forge scripts; it is the only unmapped kind on 114 of them.

Change

  1. Vocabulary (rust/crates/beryl_card): an Effect::Regenerate(Objects) entry in src/effect/mod.rs, a builder in src/effect/build.rs, its case in src/walk.rs, and an English template in src/render/effects.rs ("regenerate [objects]"). Add effect:Regenerate to data/census-coverage.json.
  2. Engine (rust/crates/beryl_rules): a regeneration shield as CR 701.19a describes it: "The next time [permanent] would be destroyed this turn, instead remove all damage marked on it and its controller taps it. If it's an attacking or blocking creature, remove it from combat." The shield is state a card creates, so it goes in the Effects store, not in a field on Object. It is used up when it applies and ends at end of turn.
    • actions/destroy.rs applies a shield unless the destruction carries Regeneration::Forbidden (CR 701.19c: such effects "cause regeneration shields to not be applied").
    • The state-based destructions for lethal damage and deathtouch damage in sba.rs are destruction, so a shield applies to them. A creature with toughness 0 and a sacrifice are not destruction, so it does not.
    • Removal from combat uses what combat.rs already has.
    • CR 701.19b (regeneration from a static ability) needs no card yet. Do not build it, but do not shape the entry so that it could not be added.
  3. A real card that regenerates: Drudge Skeletons ({1}{B}, "{B}: Regenerate this creature."), which is in the card store and in no deck. Write it with the card-writing skill once steps 1 and 2 have landed. Its doc comment says it was implemented for scenarios.
  4. The XMage and Forge exporters must be able to say the new entry, so that just card check "Drudge Skeletons" plays its scenarios on all three engines (data/oracle-exporter-gaps.json lists what the exporters cannot say).

Default taken: no new UI for a shield beyond what an Effects-store entry already shows. Record any other choice on this bead.

Tests

Scenarios, each with its CR citation:

  • Drudge Skeletons with a shield takes lethal combat damage: it stays on the battlefield tapped, with no damage, and is removed from combat.
  • Get Lost (a plain destroy) on Drudge Skeletons with a shield: it stays.
  • A shield applies once: a second destruction in the same turn destroys it.
  • A shield made this turn is gone next turn.
  • Damn on Drudge Skeletons with a shield, targeted and overloaded: it is destroyed both times. This is the scenario that tells Forbidden from Allowed.
  • A sacrifice ignores the shield.

Must not change

just count-gate holds with no bump (a game with no shield pays one capability test). rollouts_never_change and the frozen saves hold.

Acceptance criteria

Effect::Regenerate exists with a render template; destroy.rs reads the regeneration field; Drudge Skeletons passes just card check on Beryl, XMage and Forge; a scenario shows Damn destroying a creature with a regeneration shield, and changing Damn to Regeneration::Allowed makes that scenario fail; count gate holds with no bump.
