Description

What to build

Plan: Designs 2, "Land types", "Dependencies" and "Timestamps"; decision R5.

  1. Land types (CR 305.6, 305.7). Modification::SetLandTypes(types) sets a land's basic land types in layer 4 and, with it, removes the abilities its rules text gives it (the mask of by-bajq.2.2). The mana ability of a basic land type comes from the derived subtypes: mana/sources.rs and the payability prefilter (beryl_kernel/src/payable.rs) read them. GameTable's per-card mana facts stay the fast path behind a flag the recompute stores: no land's types differ from its card's. AddSubtype on lands (Urborg) gives the mana ability the same way.
  2. Dependencies. In a layer with two or more effects, A depends on B when applying B changes whether A exists, what A applies to, or what A does to it (CR 613.8a).
    • Each LayerChange declares what it reads to find its set, what its existence rests on (its source's abilities, for a static ability) and what it writes, as sets of characteristics (types, subtypes, supertypes, colors, abilities, controller, P/T). A pair whose sets do not meet is never tried: that is the common case and must cost a few ANDs.
    • For a pair that might, apply B to a scratch copy of the Derived rows and compare A's existence, set and resolved change before and after. Do not write a table of card pairs or of kinds that "are known to depend".
    • Apply next the earliest effect by timestamp that depends on nothing still waiting. A loop of dependencies falls back to timestamps (CR 613.8b). Find the order again after each application (CR 613.8c). Effects of characteristic-defining abilities are compared only with each other.
  3. Timestamps (CR 613.7). A new timestamp as an Aura or Equipment becomes attached (613.7e) and as a permanent turns face up or transforms. Objects entering together (613.7m) are ordered by a decision only when two of them have effects in one layer that the prefilter above says could interact; otherwise in State::in_game_order. When the u16 counter would overflow, renumber every live timestamp by rank, out of line.

Cards

  • Blood Moon: "Nonbasic lands are Mountains."
  • Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth: "Each land is a Swamp in addition to its other land types."

  • Scenarios: Blood Moon with Urborg in both orders of entering (nonbasic lands are Mountains, nothing is a Swamp); Dryad Arbor under Blood Moon is a 1/1 green Mountain Dryad that taps for red; a land that enters tapped unless a condition holds, under Blood Moon, waits for by-bajq.2.5.

Handoff: Commits 231be8a6 (engine), 113c940c (cards), 5cbd7b77 (XMage exporter), 7b19e55d (allowlist).

  • Vocabulary: Modification::SetLandTypes(BasicLandTypes) and the BasicLandTypes bitset (beryl_card characteristics.rs, continuous.rs). Rendering is in render/abilities.rs and render/modify.rs (staging moved modify there). It sits beside staging's new SetSubtype, which does not yet give a land intrinsic mana under CR 305.7; nothing uses it on a land today.
  • Mana from land types: Derived rows have no spare byte, so mana abilities that layer effects give are bits in Derived::keywords (Keywords::PLAINS_MANA..FOREST_MANA). Mana from a face's own basic land types is FaceFacts::land_mana in the card table. State::intrinsic_mana combines the two. The flag Flags::mana_changed, set in Rederive::finish, gates State::makes_card_mana, so the table's mana fast paths in mana/sources.rs stay valid. pay.rs activates an intrinsic ability as INTRINSIC_MANA[color] with index u8::MAX.
  • Dependencies: beryl_rules/src/layers/dependency.rs. may_depend filters on Characteristics read/write sets, with an early exit when nothing a layer writes is READABLE. apply_ordered and in_loop use fixed queues, because the game loop must not allocate. Work.dependency_trials counts trials, and beryl_search games.rs asserts zero trials in the WU mirror.
  • Timestamps: beryl_kernel/src/timestamps.rs. take_timestamp renumbers by rank before the u16 counter runs out. Staging had added its own take_timestamp in effects.rs, and I removed it while rebasing.
  • Rules bug fixed: an existence check at the head of a layer, recorded in docs/bugs.md. Designs 2 in docs/rules-coverage.md is updated to what was built.
  • XMage exporter: the scenario dump now marks abilities with changes_land_types, and the exporter leaves a payment to XMage when any card in the scenario has one. Before, it named Plains' {W} where Beryl had tapped them for {B} under Urborg.
  • Checks: the count gate went from 391,440,097 to 391,357,373. State<Small> is unchanged at 20,336 bytes. rollouts_never_change holds, with pins re-pinned only because of the new flag in the state hash. All three cards pass just card check on XMage and Forge, and Blood Moon/Urborg agree in both orders. just specs --shots passes for Blood Moon and Urborg. Dryad Arbor has no file and no spec.
  • Rulings no scenario shows yet: Blood Moon on a land that 'enters tapped' needs by-bajq.2.5. Urborg's 'not on the battlefield' rulings (cards in other zones are not Swamps) need no code, because layer effects only apply on the battlefield.
  • Gotchas: the Jace, the Mind Sculptor mutation survivors also happen on unchanged staging, so they are not from this change. Follow-up by-bajq.2.10 covers CR 613.7m (timestamps of objects entering together).
