Description

Part of the Arena Powered Cube retarget (epic by-0w2): Beryl's V1 cube is now data/arena-powered-cube-2025-10-30.csv and the mirror match target is the 40-card main deck in docs/sample-decks/2025-11-18-17lands-cb8d5d5f-wu-control.txt (30 distinct cards, 17 of them new). Read the epic's description and design field first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md). Follow the existing card files in rust/crates/beryl_cards/src (for example blue/mana_drain.rs, white/overlord_of_the_mistmoors.rs) for layout and scenarios.

Point everything that reads the Vintage list at data/arena-powered-cube-2025-10-30.csv: tools/src/beryl_tools/cards.py (CUBE), rust/crates/beryl_cards/src/tests.rs (CSV and the generated header), the regenerated rust/crates/beryl_cards/src/names.rs, ui/scripts/interactions.mjs (the longest-names check), tools/tests, and docs/engine-architecture.md where it names the list.

The six Omenpaths cards keep their Arena names (epic design decision 2). Pin their om1 printings in data/card-printings.csv so by cards --cube resolves them, keyed by the Arena name.

Aang's Iceberg, Swift Reconfiguration and Sphinx of False Conclusions are not in the Arena list but WU_CONTROL and its fixtures still use them until the mirror bead. Keep them compiling (for example a short retired-names list in the generator, marked for deletion by the mirror bead); do not touch WU_CONTROL.

  • just check and just ci pass.
