Close reason

Oust, Get Lost and Map scenarios run on Elite Spellbinder and Jace TMS; stand_ins.rs removed (a89fa35)

Description

Part of Milestone 1 (epic by-b0q). by-b0q.1.9 implemented Oust, Get Lost and the Map token, but no creature or planeswalker card existed yet, so their edge cases run against stand-in cards in rust/crates/beryl_cards/src/stand_ins.rs (test-only, not in SCENARIOS, so the oracle dump lacks them). The cards' own SCENARIOS only show they can't be cast without a target.

Once the creatures (by-b0q.1.10) and planeswalkers (by-b0q.1.11) are in CARDS, move these to real-card scenarios in white/oust.rs and white/get_lost.rs: Oust on a library of zero, one and two cards (second from the top, controller gains 3); Get Lost on a creature and on a planeswalker (its controller gets two Maps); a Map exploring a land (to hand), a nonland (+1/+1 counter, kept on top or put in the graveyard) and an empty library (counter). Then delete stand_ins.rs. Note: a planeswalker placed by the scenario setup has no loyalty counters, so cast it in the scenario (or use a setup with counters if 1.11 adds one).

Acceptance criteria

  • white/oust.rs and white/get_lost.rs SCENARIOS cover the edge cases listed, with cube cards only; stand_ins.rs is gone.
  • just check passes.
