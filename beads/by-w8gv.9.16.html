Description

Milestone 5 of Project 50k (plan K5 and K3; epic by-w8gv.9): the rules library and the card port. Line numbers and counts below were read at staging f5034c58; the code as it stands wins over this text, and a deviation is a comment on this bead.

Second of three port beads, after the first (see its handoff in enact brief for the entries it added and the pattern it set). These cards look at cards, choose among them, search and shuffle. All paths are under rust/crates/beryl_cards/src.

The cards

  • blue/stock_up.rs: "Look at the top five cards of your library. Put two of them into your hand and the rest on the bottom of your library in any order."
  • blue/thundertrap_trainer.rs: "look at the top four cards of your library. You may reveal a noncreature, nonland card from among them and put it into your hand. Put the rest on the bottom of your library in a random order." With Stock Up, one entry: look at the top N, put up to K that a filter accepts somewhere (revealed or not), the rest somewhere in an order (CR 401.4, CR 701.20).
  • blue/jace_the_mind_sculptor.rs: +2 "Look at the top card of target player's library. You may put that card on the bottom of that player's library"; 0 "Draw three cards, then put two cards from your hand on top of your library in any order"; -12 "Exile all cards from target player's library, then that player shuffles their hand into their library." The -1 is data already.
  • blue/thirst_for_discovery.rs: "Draw three cards. Then discard two cards unless you discard a basic land card." An Unless whose cost is an action, not mana (CR 118.12).
  • blue/trinket_mage.rs: "you may search your library for an artifact card with mana value 1 or less, reveal it, put it into your hand, then shuffle" (CR 701.23, CR 701.24).
  • white/winds_of_abandon.rs: "Exile target creature you don't control. For each creature exiled this way, its controller searches their library for a basic land card. Those players put those cards onto the battlefield tapped, then shuffle", and the overload half over each creature you don't control (CR 702.96). A count for each player of what an instruction did to their permanents.
  • blue/subtlety.rs: "choose up to one target creature spell or planeswalker spell. Its owner puts it on their choice of the top or bottom of their library": a choice made by a player other than the controller.
  • blue/timetwister.rs and blue/time_spiral.rs: "Each player shuffles their hand and graveyard into their library, then draws seven cards" (CR 101.4 for the order), and Time Spiral's "Exile Time Spiral" and "You untap up to six lands". wheel is a helper one card file exports to the other today: as data each card says its own sentence, or the shared effect is a constant in the library.
  • azorius/teferi_hero_of_dominaria.rs: +1 "Draw a card. At the beginning of the next end step, untap up to two lands" (a delayed triggered ability made by an effect, CR 603.7) and -8 "You get an emblem with ..." (CR 114). The -3 is data already.

New entries this bead owns

Looking at cards of a library and distributing them; choosing objects as an instruction is reached ("two of them", "a card from among them"), by a named player; searching a library; shuffling a zone into a library; a cost that is an action; a count for each player; untapping up to N of a filter; a delayed triggered ability made by an effect, in whatever form the vocabulary lane's trigger bead recorded; an emblem. The third port bead uses the delayed trigger and the emblem: it runs beside this one, so write those two first and push them on their own before the cards, and read its bead for what it adds.

Asking

These bodies ask the most questions of any cards: Stock Up and Jace's 0 ask for one card at a time (Part::First, Part::Second); Trinket Mage asks whether to search before opening the library. The library lane's asking bead recorded every place where today's sequence is not what the shape would ask (its comment in enact brief). The data must ask exactly today's sequence: the frozen corpus and beryl_cards/tests/prompts.rs hold it.

How a card is ported

  • Start from just card brief <name>: Oracle text, rulings, matching vocabulary entries, the nearest implemented cards, the XMage class.
  • The card file takes the form the vocabulary lane's last bead set (see its handoff in enact brief and the fifteen data-only card files it converted): built with the const builders, every ability's behavior effect data, the definition check beside it, the vocabulary imported from beryl_card. Its Rust bodies, bodies!, conditions!, may_pause, needs and .removal() go, since the data now says them.
  • A new vocabulary entry is designed from its rule, not from the card's sentence: it takes Objects, Players, Amount, Condition or a Filter wherever the rule has a parameter, and it says what the card says (K7: "its controller", "each opponent"). It carries its Comprehensive Rules citation with a quotation, on the entry and on the library function. The interpreter's arm and the library function go where the contract at the top of rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/lib.rs says; Effect::asks, Effect::takes and the definition check are extended; one hand-written, CR-cited scenario for the entry goes where the contract says library scenarios live (K3). The library lane already made the functions general: most entries only name one.
  • A body stays only where the vocabulary cannot say the card and an entry for it would be one card's sentence again. Write it against Game and Resolution as the contract says, and say why in a comment on the body and on this bead. If a second card needs it, it moves into the library (plan, A card file).
  • Two cards that say the same thing use the same entry. Helpers shared by two cards live in the library, never in a card file (AGENTS.md).
  • The card's existing scenarios are the proof of parity: they pass unedited. Do not change a scenario to fit the port. Saves and rollouts replay each decision, so a ported ability asks the same questions in the same order with the same options, makes the same events in the same order and draws the same random numbers.
  • Port what the body does today. Where a body does less than the Oracle text (a clause the engine has no mechanic for), the data does the same and no more (K14: no new mechanic): list each such clause on this bead.
  • Delete each EffectCtx shim method that no body calls any more.
  • just oracle <each card> passes (the fast check runs it for changed card files).

What the reader of this bead should know

  • The WU Control mirror the benchmark plays holds many of these cards: interpreting data where a body ran is on the counted path. just bench-count before and after, and after each group of cards if the count moves.
  • The M2 Mason audit, item 6: beryl_cards/src/tests/effect_data.rs keeps five Rust bodies as a reference for the interpreter. Leave it; the closing bead deletes it.
  • The readings of each card below were taken from its body at staging f5034c58 and are a starting point, not a specification: the Oracle text in the card file's header and the brief decide.

Held by checks (AGENTS.md, Code structure): no card name in generic code; no source file over 800 lines (data/file-length-allowlist.json only shrinks, and a new file starts under the limit); rules code and card bodies ask the state for a player's opponents and never name the other of two; they change the state through a mutator, never the board door; a push whose bound is argued takes Queue::push_bounded and any other propagates Full; a count or position that grows with the layout is never narrowed with as. No procedural macro, and a macro_rules! only where a const fn cannot remove the repetition. beryl_card, beryl_kernel and beryl_rules take no runtime dependency. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a yawgatog link and a quotation; keep the citations of code you move, and check new ones against the pinned release with just citations. Forge is a test oracle only (K16): no entry, name or design comes from its scripts. beryl_search/src/expert and beryl_search/src/frozen are exempt from the project: edit them only where a call stops compiling, giving the answer the old read gave. Decisions and deviations (staging 44a1e43e; first slice b92a517b).

All ten card files have no Rust body, no bodies!, no may_pause/needs/.removal(). No body remains. wheel is gone: Timetwister and Time Spiral each write shuffle_into(EACH_PLAYER, hands and graveyards) then draw(EACH_PLAYER, 7). Stock Up and Thundertrap Trainer are both Effect::Dig.

New entries (beryl_card effect/mod.rs, effect/terms.rs, effect/reference.rs, effect/amount.rs), each with its CR citation, check arm and builder:

  • Effect::Dig { player, look, take: Taken::Put(n) | Taken::MayReveal(filter), rest: LibraryOrder::Any | Random } (CR 401.2, 401.4, 701.20a). The two takes ask as their bodies did: Put counts the cards looked at and asks nothing with n or fewer; MayReveal's prompt counts the number told. The two combinations no card uses (Put+Random, MayReveal+Any) compose the same parts and are untested.
  • Effect::Fateseal { player, library, count } (CR 701.29a): one card only (Malformed::Fateseal), of any player's library, since the frozen question is a yes/no where a scry of one asks a pick.
  • Effect::PutBack { player, count } (CR 401.4): top only.
  • LibraryPosition::TopOrBottom on Effect::PutIntoLibrary: each card's owner is asked (Subtlety). A choice by a player other than the controller is said by the position, not by a general chooser.
  • Effect::Search { player, filter, count, found: Found::RevealToHand | OntoBattlefieldTapped } (CR 701.23): searches, moves, shuffles. A count of zero neither searches nor shuffles (the 'for each creature exiled this way' reading). Effect::May asks Purpose::MaySearch when its effect is a Search, which is Trinket Mage's frozen 'Search?' question.
  • Effect::ShuffleInto { player, cards } (CR 701.24a, 701.24d).
  • Effect::Unless's cost is a Cost (was a ManaCost): mana alone, or CostPart::discard(filter), one card from hand (Malformed::Cost for any other). Where the effect is itself a discard by that player the question is the discard's Part::Instead with most = that count, and with one card in hand nothing is asked: both are Thirst for Discovery's frozen sequence, written as a rule of the arm (costs::unless_discards), not of the card.
  • Amount::ThoseOf(players): of what the instruction before took, how many each player controlled as it did (Winds of Abandon's count for each controller). Run::remember now records controllers for every removal and refreshes the layers first (it reads a derived value), so it returns Result and its callers add ?.
  • Objects::CardsIn { players, zones }: several zones as one set (their hand and graveyard). by-w8gv.9.17 landed Objects::InZoneOf (one zone, with a filter) meanwhile; Jace uses that, the wheel uses CardsIn because both zones must move as one batch. The two overlap: folding them into one is a candidate for the closing bead or the Warden.
  • From the first slice: Effect::Delay, Effect::GetEmblem, Effect::UntapUpTo (see the comment above).

Structure: Effect::operands() (effect/operands.rs) is the one place that lists an entry's players, objects, amount and filter; the check, the filter walk and names_paid read it, so a new entry is listed once there plus any rule of its own in check.rs's instruction. check.rs's tests moved to check/tests.rs and the entries' parameter types to effect/terms.rs for the 800-line limit.

Clauses where the data, like the body, does less than the Oracle text: Winds of Abandon has each player search, put onto the battlefield and shuffle in turn, where the text has all players put their cards onto the battlefield together and then shuffle (one player searches in a two-player game). Nothing else.

EffectCtx lost: discard_one_of, put_spell_into_library, move_all, shuffle, reveal, top_of_library, put_on_bottom_in_random_order, put_library_card_on_bottom, search_library_up_to, search_library, put_onto_battlefield_tapped, untap_up_to_lands.

Parity: the ten cards' scenarios and beryl_cards/tests/prompts.rs pass unedited; corpus and rollouts_never_change pass; just oracle on the ten: XMage 42 pass, Forge 46 pass, 0 fail (the unsupported and the one allowlisted Trinket Mage divergence were there before). just bench-count, rollouts: 387,518,179 before -> 388,141,150 after on base b92a517b (+0.16%); 388,988,143 after the rebase over by-w8gv.9.17 (bar 389,343,295, 1% tolerance, no bump). The rise is the layer refresh and controller record in Run::remember and the second table call.

One library test changed (not a card scenario): beryl_rules tests/library.rs's untap test passes the permanents as a set, since tap::untap_up_to takes Objects' set now.
