Close reason

The fast check already runs every listed check before each push that could break it (Python tests on tools/ or card-store changes, scale tests on card changes, script checks on their inputs), the same changes that start these lanes in slow CI. 3ebcdfdc adds the whole steps python:pytest, scale:pytest and script:script-check to verdicts.FAST_ONLY, so slow CI leaves them to the fast check; it keeps black and pyre and skips the now-empty scale and script lanes. The 'move to the nightly' checks with catches stay covered by the fast check. Slow CI passed on 3ebcdfdc.
