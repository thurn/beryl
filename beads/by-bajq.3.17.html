Description

The count gate (just count-gate) measures 393,587,948 instructions at staging b7a23543, +0.84% against the bar of 390,293,470, and the epic shares one 1% tolerance, so groups 4 to 8 have about 0.16% left. Group 3 (replacement and prevention) added about +0.55% by the sum of its beads' close reasons. Three beads rose more than 0.05% without naming where the instructions went, though the WU mirror uses none of their mechanisms:

  • by-bajq.3.2 (replacement choice mid-step, Life from the Loam; commits 2d82c218 a8e2d1b9 3a3fced4 d8942349): 392,509,701 -> 392,992,478 (+0.12%). Suspects: engine::pass and turn.rs split into next_step / end_step_before_actions / step_actions; resolve.rs run() vs run_answered and the Run struct; perform_priority's #[inline(always)] (its author asked a reviewer to check it); Sink::asking and save_scopes in every resolution.
  • by-bajq.3.9 (damage proposal, protection; commit c00e2222): 392,579,811 -> 392,970,964 (+0.10%). Suspects: damage::deal building replace::Damage and calling replace::damage on every damage event; protection::live tests in cast/targets.rs domain, combat::can_block and sba::doomed; layers::recompute's Applied struct OR-ing Replaces::PROTECTION.
  • by-bajq.3.8 (Griselbrand, life costs; commit 1e8e8a91): 391,269,527 -> 391,894,716 by its own figures (+0.16%; its close reason says +0.05% against another tip). Suspect: cast/legal.rs and payable.rs checks of CostPart::Life on every activation.

Method: profile the corpus with just bench-count before and after each commit and compare per-function counts with callgrind_annotate (docs/benchmarking.md, Profile). Move what a game without the mechanism runs behind a GameTable capability or the state's replacing mask, out of line with #[cold], or remove it.

Per-function evidence (just bench-count on the rollouts subject, callgrind_annotate exclusive Ir grouped by function, each commit against its parent in detached worktrees):

by-bajq.3.2, 2d82c218 (parent 3e2ccf73): 392,097,034 -> 392,579,811 (+482,777). The WU mirror runs no dredge, so the rise is not new work. It is the call structure that the step split made: turn::step_actions +3.88M, engine::pass (now out of line) +1.83M, turn::next_step +0.95M, resolve::body +1.09M, against turn::begin_step -3.44M, resolve::run -0.99M, turn::end_step -0.94M, engine::perform -0.72M, combat::assignment::deal -0.64M and trigger::match_event -0.50M (inlined into other functions now). That nets about +0.42M in the step functions and +0.10M in the resolve split. The only new instructions on the absent path are replace::may_ask (three table ANDs) on each empty-stack pass and each resolution, and the Run record check in resolve::run, about 0.1M in all. Two experiments restoring the old shape moved the cost without removing it: forcing step_actions and next_step inline gave -41K; also forcing pass inline, with begin_step and end_step out of line, gave -2K. Forcing step_actions inline copies it into paused_step::run as well, so untap, combat::may_block and combat::may_attack get two call sites and are outlined (+1.24M, +0.89M, +0.88M).

by-bajq.3.9, c00e2222 (parent 6a4988c3): 393,234,661 -> 393,618,866 (+384,205): cast::targets::domain +204K, engine::questions::decision +134K, engine::advance +114K, others below 60K. domain's rise is spread over its inlined helpers (the spells iteration, Option handling, a second recursive copy of domain), not one removable test. The protection gate itself was cheapened: protection::live read State::replacing(), which first tests whether the layer results are stale. It now reads the bit directly, through State::may_protect, as State::protection already did (-35K, mostly sba inside advance and damage::deal in pass and step_actions; domain unchanged).

by-bajq.3.8, 1e8e8a91 (parent 89935bd6): 391,686,082 -> 391,894,716 (+208,634): engine::questions::decision +164K, the rest below 25K. The line count of the new test in activatable (Cost::pays_life_or_chooses) is 92,182 (46K activations at 2 instructions each), the same as the chooses() test beside it. The rest is code layout in decision, which is 19% of the benchmark. Merging the flag tests into one did not help. With chooses|life|this out of line, the WU mirror's many abilities with chosen or this parts paid for the call (-17K net). Merging chooses|life inline made decision +228K worse.

Conclusion: of the three rises (+1.08M), about 0.3M is new work on the absent path, and each part is the one-AND gate the design calls for (may_ask, Run check, the protection gate, the life flag). The rest is inlining and register allocation in the large inlined functions (decision, domain, the step functions). Reshaping them by hand moves the count by tens of thousands either way. Landed: the -35K protection gate.
