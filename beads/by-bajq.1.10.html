Bloodghast written and passing in Beryl, XMage and Forge; added haste and life-total conditions; escalation removed

Description

Bloodghast, 2/1: "This creature can't block. This creature has haste as long as an opponent has 10 or less life. Landfall — Whenever a land you control enters, you may return this card from your graveyard to the battlefield."

Why

Triggered abilities that work in a graveyard (Ability::in_graveyard, trigger::away, by-bajq.1.3) are proved only by test definitions in rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/tests/abilities_away.rs: Test Ghost (a draw trigger that works in a graveyard) and Test Graveyard Mourner (a dies trigger that works in a graveyard). No card of the epic has a triggered ability that works in a graveyard: Underworld Breach grants an activated one, and dredge is a replacement.

Scenarios

  • A land enters under its owner's control while Bloodghast is in their graveyard: the trigger, and accepting returns it.
  • The same land while Bloodghast is on the battlefield or in hand: no trigger.
  • Declining the return leaves it in the graveyard.
  • It can't be declared as a blocker; it has haste only while an opponent is at 10 life or less.

Follow the card-writing skill: just card brief <name>, just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), scenarios for each instruction and each ruling a scenario can show, and just specs <card> from both seats. A clause the vocabulary cannot say is escalated in data/card-escalations.json. Epic bars hold: just count-gate with no bump (report the count before and after), State<Small> within 20,480 bytes, just test-rust (frozen saves, rollouts_never_change).

Acceptance criteria

Bloodghast passes just card check (Beryl, XMage and Forge), including a land entering while it is in the graveyard and, separately, on the battlefield, and its entry is removed from data/card-escalations.json. Escalating again is not an outcome of this bead: build what is missing or file a bead under by-bajq and block this one on it.

Notes

Reopened 2026-11-13: the first attempt escalated the card (commit aeee13ef) because nothing returned a card from a graveyard to the battlefield and no rule said a creature can't block. by-bajq.1.13 builds both; this bead runs after it.
