Trinket Mage, Beza and Currency Converter with library search, linked exile, discard triggers and Treasure (d96c3e8)

Description

Part of the Arena Powered Cube retarget (epic by-0w2): Beryl's V1 cube is now data/arena-powered-cube-2025-10-30.csv and the mirror match target is the 40-card main deck in docs/sample-decks/2025-11-18-17lands-cb8d5d5f-wu-control.txt (30 distinct cards, 17 of them new). Read the epic's description and design field first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md). Follow the existing card files in rust/crates/beryl_cards/src (for example blue/mana_drain.rs, white/overlord_of_the_mistmoors.rs) for layout and scenarios.

Implement: Trinket Mage; Beza, the Bounding Spring (Treasure and 1/1 blue Fish tokens); Currency Converter (2/2 black Rogue and Treasure tokens).

Edge cases for scenarios: Trinket Mage finding Mana Crypt or Portable Hole and finding nothing (shuffle still happens, CR 701.24); Beza with each comparison true and false checked on resolution, including ties; Currency Converter's optional exile on discard (from Thirst for Discovery or its own loot), its {T} choosing among several exiled cards, the land/nonland split, and the cards staying linked to that Converter only (CR 607).

Acceptance criteria

  • Each card listed has one file under its color directory with its CardDef, bodies and scenarios; every ability is exercised by at least one scenario, including the edge cases named; every body cites the CR.
  • All scenarios pass under the strict runner, and just oracle <these cards> passes on XMage and Forge, or each disagreement is in data/oracle-divergences.json with its CR citation.
  • Fuzz games including these cards raise no invariant (debug, at least 1,000 games on demand; the just ci count still passes).

Executor notes (a37c73f):

  • Oracle text wins over the bead: Currency Converter costs {1}, triggers per discarded card ('Whenever you discard a card'), and makes a Treasure for a land, a 2/2 Rogue for a nonland.
  • Engine: EffectCtx::search_library (CR 701.23, look at all, ChooseUpTo with Done = find nothing), exile_linked/exiled_with_source (CR 607.2a; ObjectFlags::EXILED_WITH, links cleared when a new object enters the linker's slot or a token slot frees, so an ability still on the stack after its source left finds its cards but a returned Converter does not), Trigger::YouDiscard (matched on every ZoneChange with Reason::Discard), Treasure (beryl_cards/src/tokens.rs): sacrifice mana abilities are never spent by the solver; they are offered as Opt::Mana at priority in every profile and during manual payment.
  • Portable Hole and Thirst for Discovery are not on staging (by-0w2.2): Trinket Mage is tested with Mana Crypt (Staff of the Storyteller as the MV-2 artifact it must not find); Converter discards come from its own loot and Jace, Vryn's Prodigy's. Thirst's discards will trigger it as long as they use Reason::Discard. A cleanup-discard scenario was dropped: the Forge harness does not script cleanup discards.
  • Shuffle after finding nothing is checked by a Rust test on knowledge (tests.rs), since oracles shuffle on their own.
  • Oracles: all cards pass on XMage and Forge; one XMage divergence allowlisted (Trinket Mage cannot fail to find in XMage, CR 701.23b); the Treasure-for-mana scenario is unsupported on both harnesses (no priority mana activation in either).
  • New subtypes renumber the subtype bits, so beryl_bench's corpus pin moved; with the subtypes appended instead, the old pin held, so the mirror plays identically.
  • 1,000 debug fuzz games (just fuzz 1000) passed.
