Design notes: the field frame is landscape (1.3:1), so a full-size frame turned 90° is 30% taller than its row and reached into the next row (covering land name bars and P/T badges, failing bead 3's clear-of-lands ui-test). Tapped battlefield frames therefore turn whole (clockwise, same 1.3 aspect, art never reshaped) and are drawn at 1/1.3 size, so the turned frame stands exactly as tall as the row; layout reserves the turned (narrower) width. Tapped lands now form their own pile beside the same name's untapped pile. Exiled-until cards (exiledBy) are placed as a new 'beneath' look under their host, peeking up-left so the whole name bar shows, shaded, hoverable for the Inspect preview; the row reserves their peek. New fixture 'exiled-beneath' (seed 24 greedy: Aang's Iceberg exiling The Wandering Emperor, mixed tapped lands, tapped creatures) via a new beryl_cli fixture condition 'exiled beneath a permanent'; shot game-tapped-beneath; ui-test checks frame aspect/turn/footprint and the beneath peek + hover preview.
