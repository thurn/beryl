Mason audit of milestone 2 (and the late part of milestone 1), on staging e07e319fe. Doc fixes pushed in 7df1e125f. Paths are under rust/beryl3d/crates unless they say otherwise.

MILESTONE 2

  1. Is each verb one implementation that reads its values from data? Yes. beryl_scene/src/verbs/mod.rs play(verb) maps each of the 16 verbs to one play function in its own file. Durations, delays and waits come only from the step's StepTiming through beat (rule 7b holds). Spatial values come from data::space (space.json). A grep for literals in verbs/, light/, effects/, pointer/ and table/ finds only geometry constants (Bezier 3.0, shake's sine periods), a few unnamed tunings (fly.rs PUSHED_FROM 0.2, vanish.rs CLOSES 0.4, hands.rs SPARE 8.0, shadows.rs HALF_DIAGONAL 54.1, camera.rs near and far planes) and the player anchors in table/mod.rs:286-291. These are small and do not justify a bead. One exception to "from data": fly's path is chosen by matching StepTiming::case against the string literals "draw" and "draw of several" (verbs/fly.rs:178), and otherwise by re-deriving from the zones what the case already says (see 4).
  2. Is placement a pure function of the view and the window, with rest equal to placement on every fixture? Yes. table/place.rs place(view, abilities, layout, camera, scroll) is pure. Scroll and the prompt's handled cards are explicit inputs. Scripts place incrementally (play/step/placement.rs re-places only the groups that a step marks dirty, and place_reusing copies the groups that did not change). Wherever the checks keep records, play/step.rs:380 asserts after every step that this equals placing every group, and agent/steps.rs (Rule::RestPlacement) checks on every recording that each object ends its script at the place compile gives it. That place is Table::placement of the final view, or place_reusing of it.
  3. Do the interface checks know anything about a particular card or screen? The rules in beryl_scene/src/agent do not. The runner does: beryl_inspect/src/stress.rs audited exempts "stress-verbs" from crowded and step-shown, and pages.rs and gate.rs drive "stress-hand" and "stress-stack" through name comparisons, with the stack's wait implemented twice. Filed by-q44s.4.4.
  4. Is anything in the client inferring what the script already states? Two places. (a) verbs/fly.rs path_of derives the flight's path from the zones, the controller and Why (draws(), (Stack, Battlefield), (Hand, Battlefield)), which duplicates the case the session sends ("opponent draw", "permanent resolves", "land played"). It still needs the zones for flights that have no case, such as a creature put onto the battlefield from a hand, so this is lower value and not filed. A typed case parsed once would be the fix if it ever bites. (b) sound.rs is a hand port of ui/src/audio/steps.ts stepCues, and no parity check covers it. Filed by-q44s.4.5.
  5. What will the flat layer need from the scene that is not there? Moot: milestone 3 (by-q44s.5) closed and built it, including floats (layout/float.rs) and scene objects in element boxes (table/shown.rs).

MILESTONE 1, THE PART AUDITED HERE

  1. Are the frame gate's worst frames chosen deterministically, and would the gate catch one slow frame in a long recording? Yes and yes, but only within the gated boards. beryl_inspect/src/gate.rs rank sorts by (weighted work descending, frame index), so ties break by frame, and the counters are exact. Since by-q44s.16's fix, b3d-frame-counts (src/bin/frame_counts.rs) counts every frame under Callgrind, so the worst frame is a maximum over all frames, not over the 8 ranked ones. counts.py fails when the frame with the most instructions is not among the 8 ranked. The native gate holds each counter's worst frame on every tick. A single slow frame in a recording that is not one of the 7 boards is not gated. rank's weights (gate.rs:356) are a hand fit; the ranking check catches it when they go stale.
  2. Did the measurements confirm the doc's budgets? No, and the gap has grown. On 2026-11-15, stress-255, stress-1024 and verbs-zones missed the 2M target and pay-default met it. On staging the limits are 3.27M, 9.28M, 4.82M and 1.26M, and stress-verbs is at 2.64M. Running just b3d gate --board verbs-zones today shows frame 980 compiling a 34-step script that places 738 objects. Six beads cut the cost per step, and by-q44s.3.65 showed that 2M cannot be reached by placing fewer cards. The decision to revisit is compiling a whole script in the handle call that receives it. Filed by-q44s.4.3 (P1) to compile over several frames, ahead of each step's start. The re-pin churn of the limits themselves is by-q44s.80, already in progress, and is not refiled.
  3. What share of pay-default's worst frame is taffy's and parley's? As measured on 2026-11-15: taffy 7,000 instructions (2.0%), parley 56,508 (15.8%), or 129,618 (36.3%) including the font crates parley calls, out of 357,443 (docs/beryl-3d.md, 'Measurements'). pay-default's worst frame is now frame 0 (50 layout nodes and 20 text runs, the HUD laid out once) at a limit of 1.26M, after milestone 3's step banner and log. Text shaping is the larger share of first-layout cost. It is still within target, so nothing is filed. by-q44s.78's handoff notes an O(n^2) count in hud/log.rs log(), which is a candidate if pay-default grows.
  4. What did the phone spike find on each graphics path, and does it change 'Rendering' or 'Hosts'? From by-q44s.1.23: iOS Simulator on Metal played verbs-zones with no late frame and needed no renderer change. Android on Vulkan was software (llvmpipe), so its timings say nothing. It needed a display handle for the surface and wgpu's DEBUG instance flag off, because the emulator's driver crashed on object names. Android on GLES (SwiftShader through ANGLE) needed exactly one backend chosen before any surface exists. The doc's 'Hosts' had the first two but not the backend choice; added in 7df1e125f. Nothing changes 'Rendering': every path drew within downlevel_webgl2 limits. No decision moves.

RULES FOR EVERY BEAD, checked against the code

  • 2 (purity): no HashMap, HashSet, Instant, SystemTime, thread or Mutex in beryl_scene/src; the scene-purity check runs.
  • 3 (no allocation in frame): the allocation gate (frame_allocations 0) runs in the lane.
  • 6 (values as data): holds apart from the small literals in item 1.
  • 11 (unsafe): only beryl_host, beryl_render/src/surface.rs, the beryl_3d ffi, and test or bench binaries that install allocators or call Valgrind client requests. Within the rule, given its spirit.
  • 12 (wgpu): only beryl_render names wgpu.
  • 13: no bead or plan reference in code; no source file over 800 lines (the largest is client/mod.rs at 799, and play/step.rs and driver/mod.rs are at 787 and 788, so they will need splitting soon); tests/all/manual.rs at 1306 lines is exempt as a test file.
  • Load-sensitive tests: the beryl_inspect prompt tests use Duration only to step the simulated clock (page.step), which is deterministic. Only ffi.rs and faces.rs have real-time limits, 120 s, and those only catch hangs.
  • CI budget: already covered by open beads by-br5xr, by-48w9, by-ba3oh and by-yz2gx (b3d over its 60 s budget, record killed at 180 s), so not refiled.
