Close reason

Enact slots with a stopped rebase/merge or unmerged paths are no longer claimed (enact 357d4ed, slow CI green)

Description

Seen twice on 2026-11-15 by the executor of by-q44s.3.16. Each time, enact push stopped on rebase conflicts in a slot worktree, leaving HEAD detached. Within a minute another session checked out its own branch in that slot, which threw away the uncommitted conflict resolution and left that session in someone else's rebase state.

  • slot-05: my rebase started at 16:07:58; another session checked out enact/clippy-own-target at 16:13:39.
  • slot-03: my rebase started at 16:35:38; another session checked out enact/by-q44s.3.13-redo at 16:36:12 (git reflog in each slot).

Likely cause: in ~/enact/scripts/enact.py, worktree_bead and owns_slot identify a slot's owner by git rev-parse --abbrev-ref HEAD. During a rebase that prints HEAD, so the slot looks free to claim_slot. A slot whose git dir holds rebase-merge, rebase-apply, CHERRY_PICK_HEAD or MERGE_HEAD, or has unmerged files, is in use.

Workaround I used: git reset --hard origin/staging on the bead branch, then git cherry-pick <commit>, which keeps HEAD on the branch while resolving.

Acceptance criteria

A slot with a rebase, cherry-pick or merge in progress, or with unmerged paths, is never claimed by another bead or ad hoc worktree; a test in Enact's suite covers a detached-HEAD slot mid-rebase.

Handoff: fixed in the Enact repo (~/enact), commit 357d4ed on Enact staging; Enact slow CI passed. scripts/enact.py: new slot_in_use() (rebase-merge, rebase-apply, CHERRY_PICK_HEAD, MERGE_HEAD, REVERT_HEAD or unmerged paths) excludes a detached slot from claim_slot's free list; new paused_branch()/tree_branch() read the branch from the rebase's head-name so worktree_bead, slot_on_branch and verified_worktree still match the owner mid-rebase; the sweep reports such a slot as 'slot with a stopped rebase or merge'. Test: tests/test_integration.py test_a_slot_stopped_mid_rebase_is_never_handed_to_another_tree (fails without the fix). Decision, recorded in Enact docs/design.md: a slot abandoned mid-rebase stays busy until someone aborts it; the pool makes another slot or named dir instead. This beryl checkout needed no change.
