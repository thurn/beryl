Fix (bf276fb3):

  • The raised card grows upright about its top center and stands with its foot 6 px above the stage bottom (game/raise.ts raisedPose; RAISE.scale 1.5). I matched Arena's table fractions rather than its ratio to a resting card. Arena's raised card is 0.29-0.31 of the table height wide with its top at 0.58; Beryl's is 0.30 and 0.57. That is 1.5x Beryl's resting hand card, because Beryl draws its resting hand cards larger than Arena does (182 of 1600 vs 175 of 1920). Matching the ratio (1.8x) made the card visibly too big for the table.
  • No inspector opens for a hand card (Chrome.tsx), except after a refused click, so the refusal reason still shows. Otherwise the 'clicking a refused card shows why' check would lose its reason.
  • In a prompt that marks cards to pick, the card grows only as far as it stays clear of them (keptClear, now exported), and with no room it rises only as far as stays clear. This keeps by-isv.17's rule that a card shown whole never covers a target. Arena would cover them, and this is the only difference from Arena that I kept on purpose. It extends an existing rule, so I added no numbered exception.
  • The hit box keeps its old reach, clamped clear of your lands (handLifts), so a land under the raised card stays pointable.
  • The card may cover your own life orb, as Arena's covers the avatar.
  • Phone: no change. The phone has no hover, and touching a hand card already opens its own whole-card view, so there is no side panel beside a raised card there.

Tests: scripts/hand-raise.test.mjs (size, top, foot, clear of targets, no room). In kept-pointer, the 'hovering a hand card' check now requires no inspector, the foot on the window's bottom edge and more than 1.3x the fan width. It FAILED on the old code (foot at 927 of 900). I updated the target-step, crowded-sweep, refusal and artist checks for no inspector on hand cards. All hover, crowded, pass-on button, 16:10 and visible-strip checks pass. Convention 'Hover inspection' rule and evidence updated.

Side-by-side (scratch size-pair2.png: three Arena tables over three Beryl tables): width, top and foot match within 1% of the table.
