Close reason

Beryl 3D leaves a self-exiled spell off the view's stack, so it rests in exile after its vanish step (7f1a849f5)

Description

Symptom: in the recording fork/copies-time-spiral-which-exiles-the-copy-first (both seats, every desktop window), the original Time Spiral (#258) stands on top of the stack while it resolves and then, when script 5 ends, jumps about 1 m to its owner's exile pile with no step. just b3d test b3d:fork/copies-time-spiral-which-exiles-the-copy-first/you/1600x900 reports it as a rest-placement fault: 'ended the script 999 mm from its resting place'.

Why: while a spell resolves, the game takes it off view.stack but its card stays in the stack zone. Beryl 3D now places such a card as the stack's top item (on_stack in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/stack.rs); before, it had no place and was simply not drawn. Time Spiral exiles itself as it resolves, but the session's script for that resolution has no step (fly or vanish) for the card's move from the stack to exile, so nothing moves it and the end of the script snaps it to the exile pile.

What to do: find out whether the session's script builder (rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/script, rust/crates/beryl_session) omits the step for a spell that moves itself to exile as part of its own resolution, and how the web interface shows it (just b3d timeline, ui/scripts/timeline.mjs). If the script lacks the step, that is a session change, which reaches both clients (epic rule 7b). If the step is there and Beryl 3D drops it, fix the client. The fault is listed in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json (rest-placement, +1 in each of the 12 tests of the two fork/copies-time-spiral recordings) until this is fixed.

Acceptance criteria

just b3d test b3d:fork/copies-time-spiral passes with the rest-placement entries of those tests lowered by one (just b3d test --update), and the original Time Spiral is seen moving from the stack to exile in just b3d film fork/copies-time-spiral-which-exiles-the-copy-first --from 9700ms --to 10200ms.

Handoff: The session's script was right: it already has a vanish step (why exile) for the card going stack→exile (step 5.1.0 at ~5.2s, not 9.7–10.2s as the acceptance guessed). The cause was in Beryl 3D: the view drawn at Time Spiral's untap choice still lists the spell in view.stack although its card's zone is exile, and Zones::of_view (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/place.rs) placed every listed spell on the stack, so the end-of-script rest was the stack. It now drops a listed spell whose card is not in the stack zone, as Table::of and the web stage's tabled() do. The card now fades on the stack in the vanish step and rests on the opponent's exile pile. The same fix removed the copy #512's rest-placement fault and every rest-placement entry of the Time Spiral and choice-untap tests (36 entries in test-faults.json). New test: table::a_spell_that_exiled_itself_rests_in_exile_while_the_view_still_lists_it (fails without the fix). Commit 7f1a849f5.
