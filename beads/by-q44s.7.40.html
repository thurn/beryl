Description

Symptom: on Beryl 3D's phone layout, cards in the viewer's back row (lands and noncreature permanents) are fully covered by the hand, a neighbouring row or a prompt control, so no 44-by-44 point area of them takes a press. just b3d test --phone-only reports these reachable faults (test, time):

  • Hand over the back row: table-crowded/you/393x759 t=9523, 10238, 10564 (Plains, Spara's Headquarters, Portable Hole, The Wandering Emperor, Currency Converter, Tundra under Stock Up, Reprieve, Counterspell, Thirst for Discovery and the Teferis); walkers/opponent/393x759 t=2959 (Islands and Jace, Telepath Unbound under the hand cards); walkers/opponent/375x647 t=0 (Jace, the Mind Sculptor under Island and Counterspell); stress-verbs/you at 393x759 and 375x647 t=0 (Stress 037, 038, 041, 042, 045, 049).
  • A crowded back row covering itself: shape-mode/opponent/375x647 t=7539 (Currency Converter under Mana Crypt).
  • The life total: table-crowded/you/375x647 t=10890 (Island under Damn, Teferi, Time Raveler and player "You 20").
  • Prompt controls: phone-attack/you/375x647 t=516 (Teferi, Hero of Dominaria under the "No Attacks" button and its more region); verbs-place/you/375x647 t=6389 (The Wandering Emperor under the stepper region and its Less button).

The hand cases look like the hand's rows reaching up over the back row once it holds three or more rows, with the back row not moved up or the hand not capped below it. by-q44s.7.31 made the hand show only as many cards as keep 44-point strips under its ceiling; check that the ceiling leaves every back-row card a 44-point area. by-q44s.7.29 moved the stacked buttons off the back row (RightEnd::moved in prompts/bar.rs); the attack prompt's "No Attacks" and the stepper did not follow.

Read: rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/phone.rs (bands, your_life, hand ceiling), src/table/hands.rs (Fan::new, Fan::shown), src/table/rows.rs (lay_row), src/prompts/bar.rs (phone_bar, RightEnd), src/hud/phone.rs (clear_at_right), src/agent/checks.rs (reachable), tests/all/table_phone.rs, data/beryl3d/space.json layout.phone; docs/beryl-3d.md "The table", "Sizes", "Interface checks".
