Description

The engine's decisions carry a shape and a purpose in place of Ask (plan K10). After this bead a card's question needs no edit to relayout.rs.

Start from the design bead's handoff and table (the first bead of this lane: its types are in rust/crates/beryl_card, its decisions in its comments).

Change

  • rust/crates/beryl_engine/src/decision.rs: Decision::ask becomes the design's record; Ask and its 22 variants are deleted. Add the size assertion: Decision<Small> is no bigger than before.
  • rust/crates/beryl_engine/src/relayout.rs, about 325 to 370: the per-variant match over Ask goes; the record converts between layouts generically.
  • The ask sites: effect.rs (ask, ask_options, choose_yes_no, choose_up_to, choose_or_done, choose_object, ask_object, about 229 to 360, and their callers: grep Ask::, 10 sites), engine.rs (7), cast.rs (5, in the decision builder about 1530 to 1630), combat.rs (4), face_down.rs, interpret/run.rs. Eight card files pass an Ask (grep Ask:: in rust/crates/beryl_cards/src): each passes a purpose instead, and no card names a prompt of its own.
  • Readers: rust/crates/beryl_search/src/rollout.rs (one site, about 206), expert/mod.rs and frozen/expert/mod.rs (five each). The frozen and expert code gets mechanical renames only: every agent answers exactly as before.
  • rust/crates/beryl_session: the wire does not change in this bead. ask_view (protocol.rs about 1596) builds today's AskView from the new record, and preview.rs (3 sites) and game.rs (1) read the record. The wire bead of lane:prompt-state deletes AskView and that conversion.
  • DecisionKind and Opt do not change here: the next bead of this lane removes their mechanic-specific members.

DECISIONS AND DEVIATIONS (by-w8gv.11.3)

Where the code had moved: decision.rs and relayout.rs are in rust/crates/beryl_kernel now (the kernel milestone moved them); the bead's line numbers were for beryl_engine.

  1. tests/asks.rs could not pass unedited. It imported beryl_engine::Ask and read d.ask in three places to find the decision to look at, and this bead deletes Ask. Those three engine-side reads now read d.prompt / d.about; every assertion about the wire (AskView, CardHandling, pending attackers) is untouched. Proof the wire did not change: a scratch test dumped, for all 751 decisions the cards' scenarios reach, the DecisionView JSON (ask, source, candidates and the rest) for the deciding seat and the handling and pending attackers for both seats, on the base commit and on this one. The two dumps are byte-identical. generated/protocol.ts is unchanged.
  2. A look keeps its object set through its pick. Design 1 says about is empty at an ordinary pick. may_choose_object passes its candidates as about at the Whether and again at the pick that follows: today's wire sends Look.eligible at that chooseObject, and the pick's own candidates are collapsed (one of each group of interchangeable cards), so the set cannot be had from them. beryl_card/src/prompt.rs ("The object set") says so now. A consumer following design 1 is not harmed: it may ignore about at a pick.
  3. most where today's wire carries a constant: search and untap keep most = the n asked for through the run (AskView::Search/Untap max is that constant). Scry's most falls with the cards left at Part::First and is 0 at Part::Second. A look at a hand has count 0 in the record (design row 28); ask_view recomputes the hand's size for the wire. Arrange at Part::Second carries the cards still to place as count, as today's Bottom { count } did. Target at Part::Count has most = the spec's count.
  4. card_handling (what the opponent sees) is derived from the record, the zone and owner of the first card of about or else of the candidates, EXCEPT that a prompt of shape YesNo or Mode gives handling only for fateseal and mulligan. Design 4 wanted no purpose test there. Without it three things the opponent sees would change: an explore (about is the revealed card on top of a library: a lift of the top card would appear), a pay-life land (about is the land, still in hand: the hand would lift) and a cast offer of a card in hand. Parity won; by-w8gv.11.8 owns what the opponent sees and can settle it (a "the cards are looked at, not public" bit in the record would do it).
  5. Row 6: EffectCtx::discard_cards(player, n) is new: n discards as the picks of one prompt, most falling from n. The interpreter's Discard and Thirst for Discovery use it; discard_a_card and discard_one_of ask with most 1.
  6. File lengths: the ask helpers of effect.rs (ask, ask_options, choose_yes_no, choose_up_to, choose_or_done, choose_object, ask_object, the discards, may_choose_object) moved to rust/crates/beryl_engine/src/effect/ask.rs, since the new parameters pushed effect.rs past its allowance; the allowlist entry fell from 1417 to 1257. Decision::objects, Decision::options and Decision::about (kernel) are small constructors that kept engine.rs under its own. ask_view moved to rust/crates/beryl_session/src/protocol/ask.rs, a file the wire bead deletes whole; protocol.rs fell from 2465 to 2408. The eight Ask:: entries left data/card-names-allowlist.json.
  7. choose_yes_no takes (player, prompt, about); choose_object and choose_up_to take a Prompt; may_choose_object sets the parts itself.

What changed

  • beryl_kernel/src/decision.rs: Decision { player, kind, candidates, prompt: Prompt, about: Bitboard<L::Slot> }; Ask is deleted; const assertion size_of::<Decision<Small>>() == 312; constructors Decision::objects, Decision::options and .about(set). relayout.rs copies prompt and converts about with bits: no per-prompt match.
  • beryl_engine: every ask site says its record as design 2's table has it (engine.rs decision(), cast.rs decision(), combat.rs, face_down.rs, effect.rs, interpret/run.rs). The ask helpers live in the new effect/ask.rs: choose_yes_no(player, prompt, about), choose_object(player, cards, prompt), choose_up_to(player, cards, n, prompt), may_choose_object (sets Part::Whether, then Part::Pick, both with about = the candidates), discard_a_card / discard_one_of (most 1) and the new discard_cards(player, n) (most falls from n).
  • beryl_cards: the eight card files build a Prompt with the const builders; none names a prompt of its own. New tests/prompts.rs holds the record's rules over every scenario decision (part fits shape, about only where the shape and part say, a split names its first place).
  • beryl_search: rollout.rs reads d.prompt.purpose == Purpose::Search; expert and frozen expert read the purpose, and a look's or fateseal's player and zone from the first object of about.
  • beryl_session: protocol/ask.rs (new) is ask_view(obs, &Decision) -> AskView, the whole conversion from the record, to delete with AskView (by-w8gv.11.6). protocol/build.rs: card_handling and pending_attackers read the record. protocol/decide.rs: decision_source reads Purpose::PayLife and about. preview.rs and game.rs read the purpose.
