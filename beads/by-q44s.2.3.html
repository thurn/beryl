Close reason

StepTiming carries room and strike phases; both clients read them; staging 4dd7369e

Description

Symptom. Rule 7b of the epic says beryl_scene never chooses how a step is timed: every case, duration, delay, wait and effect comes in the StepTiming the session sends (rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/script/timing.rs, which moved there from beryl_session). Some step timing is still chosen in each client, separately and differently:

  • When a card joins or leaves a hand, the web stage moves the hand's other cards to their new places in REGROUP = 170 ms from the moment the act is applied (ui/src/stage/tracks.ts REGROUP, used in carried). Beryl 3D moves them over the whole step (verbs::slide over own in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/play/compile.rs step), which for a draw is about 1,050 ms; tracks.ts's comment says that "read as a slow reshape of the hand". Milestone 2's bead by-q44s.3.16 plans to copy the 170 into data/beryl3d/space.json as slide.regroup_ms, a timing value in Beryl 3D's spatial data file.
  • What makes room in a tap waits until the tapping card has turned (tracks.ts WAITS: tap: TURNED, vanish: 0), a share of the step that only the web knows.
  • A strike's phases (poise 0.32 and hit 0.376 of the step, under strike in data/ui-conventions.json) are read by the session's timing (Conventions::strike in timing/conventions.rs) but not sent. The web reads them straight from the JSON (tracks.ts STRIKE), and by-q44s.3.15 plans for beryl_scene's build script to read data/ui-conventions.json too, the only timing value a verb would take from data instead of from its StepTiming.
  • tracks.ts also exports CLOSED, read from vanish.closes in the JSON; nothing uses it since StepTiming::closes arrived.

Proposed design. The session decides these and sends them, as it does every other part of a step's timing:

pub struct StepTiming {
    // ... as now ...
    /// How what this step makes room among (the cards whose resting place it changed but
    /// that it does not move itself) gets to its new place.
    pub room: Room,
    /// For a verb whose motion has named phases (strike), each phase's start as a share of
    /// the step, in the data's order.
    pub phases: Vec<Phase>,        // Phase { name: String, at: f64 }
}
pub enum Room {
    /// Over the step, as now (the default).
    Step,
    /// For `ms` from when the act is applied, easing out (a hand regrouping: `regroup` in
    /// the data, 170).
    For { ms: f64 },
    /// From this share of the step to its end (what makes room in a tap waits until the card
    /// has turned).
    From { share: f64 },
}

opens and closes keep their meanings and take precedence as they do now. The values go in data/ui-conventions.json beside the verbs they belong to (a regroup value, the tap's room share), never in code. The session's rules (timing/rules.rs) choose Room per step exactly as tracks.ts carried chooses today: For when a hand card regroups because another card joins or leaves its hand (not for vanish), From for a tap, Step otherwise.

Then both clients read them: the web stage's carried, makingRoom and STRIKE take them from the step's played timing (ui/src/stage/timing.ts playedOf) and the constants REGROUP, WAITS, CLOSED and the JSON reads of strike.phases go; beryl_scene's compile.rs step uses timing.room for the room interval instead of own. ui/src/generated/protocol.ts is regenerated (its header says how). This is a change to the session's protocol and the web stage, which the owner allowed for moving step timing into the session (the epic's 'Decisions and defaults from the plan review': "Step timing moves into Rust", and doc 'Timing a script'); the doc's 'Checks and isolation' lists it among the exceptions.

What it eliminates: the last step timing chosen in either client; the 170 ms in space.json and the build script's read of ui-conventions.json that by-q44s.3.16 and by-q44s.3.15 would add; a source of timeline-parity differences (by-q44s.3.35) between the clients.

Behavior that must not change: the web stage plays every fixture with the same timeline as before (just motion and the timing-parity check pass unchanged). Beryl 3D's verbs-zones and pay-default play with no fault (just b3d check).

After it lands, edit by-q44s.3.16 and by-q44s.3.15 so they read room and phases from StepTiming instead of space.json and ui-conventions.json, and correct docs/beryl-3d.md 'Timing a script' (the StepTiming listing).

Files: rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/script/timing.rs, timing/rules.rs, timing/conventions.rs, timing/tests.rs; data/ui-conventions.json; ui/src/stage/timing.ts, tracks.ts; ui/src/generated/protocol.ts; rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/play/compile.rs, src/verbs/mod.rs; docs/beryl-3d.md.

Acceptance criteria

A test in timing/tests.rs shows a draw's StepTiming with room For { ms: 170 }, a tap's with room From { share } equal to the web's TURNED, and a strike's phases poise 0.32 and hit 0.376. A beryl_scene test shows that after a draw the hand's other cards are at rest 170 ms after the act is applied. grep finds no REGROUP, WAITS, CLOSED or strike.phases read in ui/src/stage, and no read of data/ui-conventions.json in rust/beryl3d. just motion, the timing-parity check, just b3d check verbs-zones and just b3d lane pass.

Handoff: StepTiming gains room (Room::Step | For { ms } | From { share }) and phases (Vec<Phase>) in beryl_protocol script/timing.rs; room_of/touches_hand/phases_of choose them, and the trays' own timings send For. Values: fly's regroup (170) and tap's room (0.6) in data/ui-conventions.json, read by conventions.rs regroup()/turned(). Web: carried regroups on room.kind === 'for' (still only hand-fanned entries, and still ahead of opens/closes there, as before), makingRoom takes the room (vanish keeps its own wait-from-0 and CLOSES, which this bead left in the client), struck/lunge take StrikePhases from Timing.phases, and audio's strike cue reads phases.hit. REGROUP, WAITS and CLOSED are gone; TURNED stays for the tapping card's own turn and must equal tap's room. Beryl 3D: compile.rs room_of gives the From interval and the regroup interval (applied to slots that lie in a hand after the step); Motion::push now cuts segments an earlier step left that begin after the new one (Track::cut_after). Without that cut, three simultaneous taps made just b3d check verbs-zones report no-jumps and rest-placement faults. Deviations: the timing-parity check was deleted by by-q44s.1.26, so just motion is the web parity check (it passed). rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_inspect/src/pack/sources.rs still lists ui-conventions.json, but only as a cache key for rebuilding the recordings pack, not as timing data. Edited by-q44s.3.16 and .3.15 to read room/phases from StepTiming; updated docs/beryl-3d.md's StepTiming listing.
