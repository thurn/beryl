Reproduction: replayed the report's fixture (8 cards, turn 2) and by-kgn7's (10 cards) and by-pic0's (8 cards, discard) in ui-live at the report's 1512x949 window. Before the change, the outer cards turned 14-18 degrees and sank 73-121 units, as in each report's window.png. Cause: handPlacements turned each card 4 degrees and dropped it 6*(index from middle)^2 units whatever the spacing. At 1440 stage width the dock and the piles leave room for only 75 units per card at 8 cards, so the fan curled at its ends and dropped about twice as far as its own turn implied.

arena-pixel-match: I measured Arena's resting hand on whole-table frames at 5, 6, 7, 8, 10 and 18 cards (nBj2ikeYCtk 1:07, sybcO8S7x6A 24:27 and 51:04, FGsR_FGChOU 2:25, kvVLJ_cS_KE 6:24, uzg_P976qhc 17:20, _IBqPx0gcag 12:00). Arena's cards lie on one circle: a card's turn is about 0.0275 degrees per pixel from the middle at 1920 wide, with cards 180 px wide. The outer card turns about 11.5 degrees at 8 cards and 14-15 degrees at 10 and 18. Cards sit about 0.95 of a card apart until the hand reaches the turn button.

Fix: game/fanArc.ts onArc puts each card's top center on a circle 14 card widths in radius and turns the card to lie along it. The held hand, the raised hand (no longer used by StageBoard) and the phone fan (mobile/layout.ts) all use it. After the change, 8 cards at 1440 have outer turns of about 6 degrees and a sink of 7 units, and 10 cards at 1600 have outer turns of about 7 degrees. Every name shows. Pair sheet: Arena 8 and 10 cards beside Beryl's c4f4 and kgn7 moments at 1600x900 (scratch, not committed).

Difference table after the fix:

  • Turn at a given distance from the middle: matches Arena's circle within 3 degrees (fan-arc.test.mjs checks Arena's 8/10/18-card values).
  • Drop relative to turn: follows the circle, as in Arena. No card drops further than its neighbor's turn (tested).
  • LEFT: overlap and spacing. Beryl's 8 cards sit 0.42-0.54 of a card apart against Arena's 0.95, so its outer turn is 6-8 degrees against Arena's 11.5. Beryl's hand cards are 182 of 1600 (11.4% of the width) against Arena's 9.4%, and the room Beryl's dock and three bottom-left piles leave is 689 units against Arena's about 983. Matching would mean smaller hand cards or moving the dock and piles: a table-layout change beyond this report. by-kgn7 also asks for more legibility, which smaller cards work against.
  • LEFT: height. Arena's middle card top sits at about 0.87 of the table height and Beryl's at 0.81, so Beryl shows more of each card. That keeps names and types readable (by-kgn7) and was not changed.
  • LEFT: Arena floats each card's mana cost above the fan, so costs show past the overlap. Beryl doesn't. This bears on by-kgn7's cost visibility. Tests: ui/scripts/fan-arc.test.mjs; the new hand-ten board, fixture and game-hand-ten shot (ui-shots passes); npm test 204/204; typecheck.
