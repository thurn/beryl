Description

The presentation track of Project 50k: decisions K22 to K30 in docs/project-50k.md (What the audit found, UI reports; Decisions; Target architecture, Presentation and Prompts; Milestones; Acceptance; Risks; Measurements, UI reports). Read those and docs/ui-conventions.md before any bead here. It is the sixth track: it starts after milestone 1 (by-w8gv.1), runs beside the engine chain, and must cut over before the final gate (by-w8gv.16).

Why

180 bug reports in three days (2026-11-08 to 11-10), about 150 on the UI; about 40 re-filed five themes. One motion, a trigger lifting out of its card, took 12 reports and 7 commits (66eb208b, cdf249e4, 8c8f581e, a4905960, b023882a, c4305819, 1c242875). Each fix verified the batching patterns it knew by sampling bounding boxes in headless WebKit. The motion is what Arena does. Three causes:

  • No reference an agent could measure: prose to interpret and its own pixel numbers to verify against.
  • The UI rebuilds a timeline from snapshots and a flat event list (ui/src/game/beats.ts) and guesses causes the engine knew (tokenOrigins in Board.tsx).
  • Several animation mechanisms share one screen, sequenced by fixed waits: HOLD in beats.ts; layout effects in CardLayer.tsx, Piles.tsx, Stack.tsx and ManaFlow.tsx; trigger flights and boardArrows in Board.tsx.

Decisions from the user (design interview, 2026-11-10)

  • Arena everywhere: flow, motion, timing, table layout, and wording exactly. Skin stays Beryl's: "I like some of our current choices. The phase icon design currently is nice. I like that we use full cards that actually tap, not just crops of the art. We don't need to go redraw icons and frames and stuff, no."
  • Seven Beryl exceptions stand and 14 earlier requests follow Arena; docs/ui-conventions.md lists both. His bug report wins over Arena and over an earlier report, and is recorded as an exception.
  • Rust builds a per-seat script and the UI only plays it. The script owns every visible change: arrows, combat lines and marks included.
  • Per card, invariants (the replay check and rule invariants), not snapshots. Snapshots exist only where a person wrote the expectation: per verb, per prompt shape, and the rules library's hand-written scenarios.
  • An imperative DOM stage; not a canvas, not reactive React components. Built behind a switch and cut over once.
  • A prompt's state lives in the session; marks come from a closed list.
  • One measuring tool for an Arena recording and a Beryl capture; Arena's motion is measured as reference; each verb's look is Beryl's own (2026-11-11, by-w8gv.21.42). No review gates a verb: the user judges motion through his bug reports (2026-11-12).
  • The design language is data; docs/ui-conventions.md is generated from it.
  • What the recordings do not show is composed from existing verbs and shapes and listed. The phone shares the stage and keeps its own layout.
  • Reports about motion, arrows and combat lines are banked from the track's start until the cut-over.
  • Agents keep closing their own UI reports.
  • "please don't give me approval steps as part of the plan, you can file human beads asynchronously, just not blocking things." No bead here waits on him. Proceed on Arena or the nearest existing verb, and file a bead labelled human saying what to look at.
