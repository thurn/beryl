Description

Fix the game screen's catastrophic UI problems, reported by the owner after playing the current build (2026-11-05). Light run: one final Warden, no per-bead review, no Mason/Sage/Archivist.

The owner's report (verbatim list, the scope of this run)

  • Clicking "start" shows "no game in progress" with a link. It then jumps into the game after around 5 seconds unless you click the link in which case you are SOL
  • Everything in the UI jumps around crazily at all times. Hovering over a card in your hand makes the whole hand jump and play an animation. Hovering a card in play does the same thing.
  • The highlights on cards in your hand seem to be applied more or less at random. I think this highlight is maybe supposed to be "cards you can play"? But I have two plains in hand and only one of them is highlighted.
  • If I drag a plains out to play it, it freezes the UI for around 5 seconds, and then suddenly it's my second turn and the opponent has played a land.
  • The experience in general of my turn suddenly jumping ahead if I have no plays is super confusing. I understand that I technically can't do anything, but you would want at least an "end turn" button and indication of what the opponent is doing via animations.
  • Really just literally any indication that the AI is doing something would be great
  • The opponent countered my card with mana drain, there is no indication that this happened except mana drain appeared in their graveyard
  • Cards in graveyards can still be hovered, and there isn't obviously a way to open the graveyard to view its contents
  • My graveyard is covered by my hand, so I can't view its contents
  • Tapped and untapped lands are incredibly difficult to visually distinguish
  • The way prompts work in general is super weird, like why do I have "cast thieving skydiver" and "cast thieving skydiver with kicker" as two separate options and THEN I pick the X?
  • I don't really feel like you need to give me priority when my own thieving skydiver is resolving by default
  • The source and target arrows for cards and abilities are really visually hard to parse, especially when the cards are close together
  • The attacker/blocker arrows are also really hard to understand visually
  • Why is everything on the battlefield off-center? It all shifts towards screen right
  • "choose how to pay" just gives me "payment 1" and "payment 2". What are these options? Nobody knows!. It should just highlight the permanents it will tap when you drag it out, and then tap those permanents on play, or let you pick different ones, not give you a prompt.
  • Why is the tapped state so weird looking? Tapped creatures render like a squashed version of their art
  • Something like Aang's Iceberg should show the card it's exiling underneath it
  • The UI for declaring blockers is impossible to understand, I legitimately am still not sure how you block or declare multiple blockers or anything
  • Bunch of issues with text being truncated, e.g. the popup I get for stacking triggers cuts off the text with the card names
  • Can we maybe just auto-stack triggers intelligently?
  • The interface for declaring multiple attackers is really weird, it kind of seems like e.g. I need to pick tokens to attack with in a specific order? All eligible attackers do not get the blue highlight or respond to clicks together.

The owner also welcomes fixing plainly poor aesthetic choices met along the way.

Root cause found while planning

beryl_session emits a PlayerView only at the human's decisions (session.rs run, game.rs advance plays every agent decision in one blocking loop). So the UI hears nothing while the AI thinks or acts: the freezes, the sudden jumps to the next turn, the invisible Mana Drain and the "No game in progress" screen (the AI deciding play/draw before the first view) all follow from it.

Decisions (made with the owner in pre-flight)

  • Turn flow: the session streams a view after every agent action and every step change. The UI plays them back at a readable pace (about 0.5 to 1 s each) with an "opponent is thinking" indicator and a game log, and animates what happened (casts, counters, resolutions, attacks). Under smart stops the session always halts the human at their own first main phase (with an End turn button) and at the opponent's end step, even when they have no legal action; these halts are session-level pauses: the engine's Stops::Smart and the AI's profiles stay unchanged (MCTS uses Stops::Smart, and set_stops notes the state holds one profile for both seats).
  • Run size: light.
  • Defaults recorded by the vizier (no owner input needed):
    • Under smart stops the human auto-passes priority while only their own spell or ability is on top of the stack and nothing new happened since they cast it (Arena-like). Session-level, human-only; exact stops still offer it.
    • Under smart stops the human's simultaneous triggers are ordered automatically by a deterministic heuristic; exact stops still ask, with untruncated text.
    • Payment is never a "Payment 1 / Payment 2" prompt: a default plan is picked, the permanents it taps are highlighted while dragging, they tap on play, and the player can choose different permanents directly.
    • A card's cast options (alternative and optional costs such as kicker) and its X are chosen in one cast step, not a list of near-duplicate options followed by a separate X prompt. The engine's candidate surface (architecture §7.2) stays; the UI groups it.
  • No human QA, now or later. Acceptance is checks an agent runs.

Whole-run acceptance

  • Every bullet above is fixed or has a recorded reason on its bead why it changed shape.
  • just check and just ci pass; each bead adds the fixture states (ui/fixtures.json, ui/shots.json) and just ui-test interactions that show its fix.
  • The final Warden reviews held-out screenshots captured after the last change (the visual-review skill), passes when no Bug-severity findings remain on the listed problems, and after two fix rounds files what remains as separate P2 beads outside this epic instead of looping.
