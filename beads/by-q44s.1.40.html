Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Test speed' and decision C14; 'Interface checks'; 'Checks and isolation'. Then tools/src/beryl_tools/script_checks.py and runlog.py (how each check is named and logged in batches), and the driver's check_times and check_all.

Build the thing that makes interface tests cheap enough to run all of them before every push of a Beryl 3D change. All of it is in beryl_inspect, which has no GPU dependency.

  • beryl_inspect::run(filter, mode) runs every test on every core with std::thread::scope and a shared atomic index. A test is a recording, a seat and a window from the matrix, played through Page with the checks applied at its check times. No engine, no renderer and no process per test. The matrix is the doc's four desktop sizes; milestone 4 adds the three phone sizes.
  • just b3d test [prefix] [--every-tick]. Tests are named b3d:<recording>/<seat>/<width>x<height>. Faults print as check prints them, the same fault in several tests once with a count, at most 20, then one closing line with the tests run, the faults and the time. Each test is logged to the run log in batches, as the script lane's are.
  • The differential test: on a fixed sample of 200 tests, plant each kind of fault at a seeded random tick (the planting functions exist) and require the check times to find it. A miss names the fault and the tick.
  • Limits in data/beryl3d/gates.json under tests: the frames a test evaluates, median and maximum over all tests. A unit test fails over them.
  • The lane and the sweep, both in the tools package: test is a step of the beryl3d lane, and test --every-tick a step of the sweep. If the whole run does not fit the fast check's budget in docs/checks.md, do not cut tests or raise the budget: report the numbers on the epic and file a bead under milestone 1 to bring the cost down.

Report in a comment, each as the median of five runs with the load beside it: the number of tests, CPU milliseconds per test, tests per CPU-second, and wall seconds for all of them at their check times and on every tick. Put them in the doc's 'Test speed' table in place of the targets.

Run: just b3d test; just b3d test b3d:verbs-zones; just b3d test --every-tick; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

Every fixture and card spec moment runs from both seats over the desktop window matrix in one process with no engine and no renderer; the differential test passes; a failing test's name, given to just b3d tree, shows that test's recording; the whole run is a step of the beryl3d lane, or a bead says what stops it; the measured numbers are on the bead and in the doc.

Measurements (debug build, as just b3d builds it), after rebasing onto staging 5545734c1, medians of five runs at load 86 to 101: 7,616 tests (1,904 recordings from a seat x 4 desktop windows); at the check times 9.1 s on the clock, 77.7 CPU-s, 10.2 CPU-ms a test, 98 tests per CPU-second, frames median 103 / most 1,704 (b3d:first-target/you/2560x1080); on every tick 9.9 s, 87.3 CPU-s, 11.5 ms a test, frames median 166 / most 2,889. Slowest test: stress-1024 at about 1.7 s. 1,869 tests fail. Before the rebase the same run took 1,381 s and 4,955 CPU-s (stress-1024 alone 1,363 s); by-q44s.1.53, worked by another session meanwhile, removed that. The table is in docs/beryl-3d.md 'Test speed'. Deviations: (1) test is not a step of the beryl3d lane and --every-tick not a step of the sweep: 1,869 tests fail today, so either step would fail every run. by-q44s.1.54 adds both once by-q44s.3.8 lists the faults. (2) The frame limits (gates.json tests) are held by just b3d test itself (both over a whole run, most_frames over a prefix), not by a cargo unit test, because a unit test would replay every recording inside cargo-test; a unit test covers Limits::over. (3) The differential test found 17 faults only every tick found, all 'every step shown'; I changed Page::check_all (beryl_scene driver/check.rs) to sample judged steps' subjects at every tick in both modes, which is outside beryl_inspect but was needed for the acceptance check. (4) The differential sample leaves out the stress boards.
