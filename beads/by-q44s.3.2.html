Handoff (e723f507d):

  • verbs/<verb>.rs: 16 files, each with BUILT and play(&StepIn, &mut StepOut); fly, appear and set are built. verbs::play(verb) and built(verb) match over them. verbs::segment is gone.
  • play/step.rs now holds Motion (moved out of compile.rs), StepIn, Places and StepOut. Motion::step calls the verb at the subject's slot inside the affected-slot loop, so segment records keep their order; a step with no card subject calls its verb after the loop. StepOut::instant() skips a no-op change, as the old code did. Places::pose and rest handle Object and Player; Ability returns None.
  • StepIn::to is the rest pose, or from faded out when the act takes the card off the table (the old behavior). StepIn::was clones the subject card's record before the act. That raised handle_allocations by 7 to 96 per board; gates.json was raised and the commit body gives the old and new numbers.
  • Compiled gains reset, spans, streaks, notices and effects. The first three are sorted by start time, because steps in a beat can start out of order and Show gives the records out in order.
  • light/: Span, SpanChange, StreakRun, Open and Lit. Streaks also live in Lit (Lit::streak, Lit::streaks); the bead had not said where they go. Show::frame feeds Lit (and Show.effects) at each record's real time, including records of a script that ends inside the frame, and clears Lit when a reset script starts. Show::lit, Show::lit_mut and Client::lit_mut exist.
  • effects/: Effect (ALL, name, from_name; a test checks the list against the data file) and EffectRun, with stubs card::look, light::write and shake::offset.
  • table: Look, Dress, Layout::players (PlayerAnchor; the NDC values are in DESKTOP, converted from desktop.ts), Table::player_pose and Table::layout. The per-card drawing is now Table::write_card, which keeps staging's rule that fading cards go in Band::TableMarks. pointer/mod.rs holds Pointer and a look stub.   • frame: Sound { id, theirs }, Vec<Sound> with room for 16, FrameBuilder::sound and shake, and frame::sprites with 14 named 256x256 cells on page 0.
  • Test hook: Planted::Mark (not a fault) puts a selected mark on a step's card, for tests of how spans reach Lit (tests/all/light.rs, tests/play_allocation.rs).
