Build, in src/prompts/split.rs:

  • The split's piles are the tray's piles, each with a box and its label drawn in the flat layer under the cards, as TrayUnder draws them, with the place's words.
  • A click on a card sends Click::On (it goes to the other pile); a card dragged and dropped into a pile sends Click::Drop { on, pile, at } with the slot landing gives. A pile with closed takes no drop.
  • OwnStep::TrayLifts { sorted: true } moves cards within a tray already up after a reply.
  • The pile shape: the token pile's top token is the click target, and its count is drawn on it.

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/prompts.rs: shape-split's two pile boxes carry the session's labels; dragging a card from one pile to the other sends Click::Drop with that pile's index; a click on a card in shape-pile sends Click::On for the pile's top token.   • prompts::screen gives the flat-layer elements for the prompt from the PromptView alone: the dock (the prompt bar, with its buttons in the session's order, the primary at the foot as PromptDock in ui/src/stage/PromptBar.tsx draws it) and whatever else the shape draws in the flat layer. The client keeps no prompt state of its own: every click goes to the session as Press::Button or Press::Prompt(Click), and the session's reply (ClickReply::Open) brings the next state.
  • prompts::own begins and ends the marks, arrows and tray lifts the prompt shows, through Lit::begin and Lit::end (milestone 2's src/light) and the tray of src/table/tray.rs, timed by the entry of own_timings() for each OwnStep (beryl_protocol src/script/timing/own.rs); where after_ends is set, fill after with the arrow's ends that are not players.   • Timeline parity: run just b3d timeline-parity --update after the shape plays, and say in a comment how many entries of data/beryl3d/timeline-parity.json your shape's recordings had before and after. The count may only go down.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d test b3d:shape-split; just b3d timeline-parity; just b3d lane.
