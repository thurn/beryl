Close reason

push watch_ci and enact_unowned in Enact 078ca14; slow CI green

Description

This change is made in the Enact repository at ~/enact (scripts/enact.py, tests in tests/test_unit.py and tests/test_integration.py), not in Beryl. Read ~/enact/AGENTS.md first and follow that repository's workflow; record design decisions in ~/enact/docs/design.md. Keep changes backward compatible: projects and live sessions on every machine run this repository directly.

Why

When Enact cannot tell whether a vizier will repair a slow-CI failure, the two mistakes cost very different amounts. A watch_ci notice that was not needed costs one session some waiting. A missing one left Beryl's master stuck for three hours on 2026-11-13. So Enact should assume nobody is watching unless it has fresh evidence otherwise.

What to change

  • enact push prints watch_ci unless a fresh vizier lease (from the lease bead this depends on) covers the pushed bead's run, or the bead belongs to a vizier's run and that vizier's lease is fresh.
  • When enact ci files a Steward bead and no fresh local vizier lease covers any of its enact_runs, it sets enact_unowned=true on the bead and says so in the bead's description, so a session or scheduled routine looking for unowned failures finds it without reasoning about runs. Clear the flag when someone claims the bead.

Done when

Tests cover a push with no live vizier printing watch_ci, a push under a live vizier not printing it, and a Steward bead filed with no live vizier carrying enact_unowned.
