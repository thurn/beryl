Bloom on glows and streaks: half-size additive draw, 9-tap separable blur, composite before the flat layer; tests pass (ac0526808)

Description

Read first: the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md; docs/beryl-3d.md 'The Enhanced tier' (bloom, multisampling) and 'Effects' (which effects the Enhanced tier gives bloom: glow, settle, impact, trail, flash, beam).

Bloom on glows and streaks, from a half-resolution blur. Edit only src/enhanced/bloom.rs and a new src/enhanced/bloom.wgsl in beryl_render, the tests named below, and data/beryl3d. The renderer skeleton bead made the four-sample main pass with its resolve, and calls bloom::apply between the resolve and the flat layer's pass; that bead's multisampling test covers the multisampling, and this bead adds one check of it from the gated boards (below).

What exists: bloom::apply, a stub; Frame::bands, where each band starts in draws, so frame.bands[Band::Glows as usize]..frame.bands[Band::Glows as usize + 1] are the glow and streak records (glow, impact, flash, beam, pulse, trail, the burst of settle, and the streaks of paying).

Build:

  • Add enhanced.bloom to data/beryl3d/space.json: scale: 0.5 (the blur's target, of the frame's size), taps: 9, sigma: 3.0 (in the half-size target's pixels), strength: 0.6.
  • Bloom, made with Enhanced: two half-size color targets of the target format, remade only when the frame's size changes; a pipeline that draws records into the first with the Baseline shader's vs and fs, one sample, additive blending, no depth; a pipeline for a separable Gaussian blur (blur in bloom.wgsl, a full-screen triangle with no vertex buffer, the direction a uniform) run across and then down between the two targets; and a pipeline that adds the blurred target times strength onto the frame's resolved view.
  • apply records nothing, and costs no call, when the Glows band is empty. Otherwise four passes: the band's records into the first target (the runs of runs that fall in the band's range), the blur across, the blur down, the composite. The flat layer is drawn after it, so text and panels never bloom.

Tests, in beryl_render/tests/all/bloom.rs, single pixels of hand-built frames:

  • a glow record of Sprite::DISC: a pixel just outside the sprite's drawn extent is brighter in Enhanced than in Baseline, and a pixel 60 pixels away is equal in both;
  • a streak record blooms along its length, not past its head (the a_streak_lights_only_between_its_tail_and_its_head test of render.rs, in Enhanced, with the bloom's spread allowed);
  • a frame with no Glows records draws the same number of calls as the skeleton bead's and the same pixels;
  • a panel record over a glow has the same color in both tiers. And in tests/all/enhanced_boards.rs, every_gated_board_draws_in_enhanced_on_a_full_device: each board of beryl_inspect::gate::BOARDS at its ranked frames (as downlevel.rs plays them, with Playback::with_tier(Tier::Enhanced)) draws on a Limits::Default device with no validation error.

Must not build: shadows, lighting, particles, any gate limit.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_render; just b3d film verbs-zones --tier enhanced (around a step that plays flash or settle; say in a comment what you saw); just b3d lane.

Beads named above: the contract bead (the tier input and the check) is by-q44s.11.2; the renderer skeleton bead is by-q44s.11.3; the shadow map bead by-q44s.11.4; the lighting bead by-q44s.11.5; bloom by-q44s.11.6; particles by-q44s.11.7; the tier choice bead by-q44s.11.8; the settings row by-q44s.11.9; the limits bead by-q44s.11.10; the settings screen bead by-q44s.5.27. Every bead of this milestone depends on by-q44s.4, the review gate before it.
