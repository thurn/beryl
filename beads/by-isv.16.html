Description

Follows by-isv.6 (cancel until the cost is paid), which left two items unbuilt and missed one decision.

  • Evoke exile cost cannot be cancelled. Seed 101 vs Expert, turn 21: the opponent casts Beza; at your stop click Subtlety (casts with evoke). A full-screen "Exile / Choose a card to exile." prompt opens; the decision is exileCost with cancellable: true. Escape, right-click and dragging the Subtlety stack card back (via View Battlefield) send nothing; the only buttons are a disabled Exile and View Battlefield. The title does not say it is Subtlety's evoke cost. Shots: D1/243-g1-evoke-next.png, D1/247-g1-esc-exilecost-after.png, D1/248-g1-exilecost-viewbf.png.
  • Drag back does not cancel. In the cast step (Subtlety: Cast or Evoke, turn 20) dragging the card back into the hand leaves the prompt open; while choosing a target (Fractured Identity on the stack, cancellable: true, turn 22) dragging the stack card back does nothing. Escape, right-click and Cancel work in both.
  • "Next" stays live during the cast step and drops the cast. Turn 20: click Subtlety (orange Cast button), then the larger orange "Next / To Main 2": priority passes and the cast silently disappears. Two orange primaries are on screen at once. Shots: D1/207-g1-next-during-caststep-before.png, D1/210-g1-next-during-caststep-after.png.

Design rules (epic by-isv): follow the skill's design philosophy (one gesture for the common case, the client does the bookkeeping, ask once in context, Arena first, show don't make them infer, few words) and add no standing words to the table. Every acceptance check is an agent's: just ui-test, just ui-shots, Rust tests or ui-live probes.

Acceptance criteria

  • just ui-test checks, on fixtures or scripted boards: an exileCost decision marked cancellable is cancelled by Escape, right-click and a Cancel control; dragging a card in its cast step, or a cancellable stack card, back to the hand cancels the cast.
  • While a cast step or cancellable cost is open, the turn button is not a second primary (disabled, or it confirms the cast); a check asserts one primary action.
  • The exile-cost prompt names the card whose cost it is (by its card, not a sentence).
