Beryl 3D manual table: shake a refused flat-layer button

  • Description
  • Acceptance criteria

Description

A refused table action shakes the card a click sent it from (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/pointer/refusal.rs, Pending::from in src/manual/mod.rs, Client::take_table_reply in src/client/manual.rs), but not a flat-layer element: the web's shakeRefusal (ui/src/stage/Refusal.tsx) also shakes a refused mana or player counter button (manual::pools) and a life total. Extend Pending::from to name the pressed element and offset it in the flat layer for 240 ms through the same OFFSETS. Test in tests/all/manual_controls.rs beside a_refused_click_shakes_the_card_clicked_and_an_accepted_one_does_not.

Acceptance criteria

A refused counter-button press shakes that button; a test covers it; just b3d lane passes.
