Review findings: rewinding before cards were removed forgot what seats had learned, a seat’s own deck list omitted cards with names shared by hidden objects or another deck, rolls inside batches disappeared from projections, and borrowing a hidden zone allowed a seat to clone a card it could not identify. Fixed each with a targeted regression. The seeded history test now uses 1,024 actions and still asserts that every supported action kind was exercised; the redaction test retains its 2,000 actions. Follow-up by-bmdk.1.32 covers public labels that disappear when a hidden card has the same name and ability markers whose source moves out of view. Both need a stable visibility contract with the adapter.
