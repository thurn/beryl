Close reason

beryl_card_records holds the card records with no engine; only beryl_3d and beryl_record link it; cargo-test after a session edit 49.2 s -> 28.0 s

Description

Symptom. After by-wzq9, a warm just b3d lane with no main-workspace change takes about 15 load-adjusted seconds. But enact push rebases onto staging first, and when staging has changed an engine crate, the lane's cargo-test step recompiles and relinks most of Beryl 3D's tests. In by-wzq9's own push the lane took 73 load-adjusted seconds (cargo-test 53 s) against the fast check's 60 s budget.

Why. beryl_assets depends on beryl_session (and beryl_card_store) for one import, beryl_session::card_records::{CardRecordsRequest, card_records, record} in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_assets/src/faces.rs. That puts the whole engine (beryl_session, beryl_cli's dependencies, the card crates) under beryl_assets, and so under beryl_render, beryl_host and beryl_3d, and under every one of their test binaries. beryl_scene's tests also take beryl_session as a dev-dependency, for card_records in tests/all/checks.rs and tests/all/faces.rs. beryl_assets' dev-dependency on beryl_cards adds the card crates.

What to do. Give the card-record code a home that has no engine dependency (for example a module of beryl_card_store or beryl_protocol, or a small crate of the main workspace), make beryl_assets and beryl_scene's tests use it, and drop their beryl_session and beryl_cards dependencies. Only beryl_3d and beryl_record should still link the engine. The purity check (tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/purity.py, scene-purity) may be able to enforce this.

Measure: after a one-line edit to a main-workspace engine crate (e.g. beryl_session), just b3d cargo-test on the clock, median of three with the load, before and after.

Acceptance criteria

beryl_assets, beryl_render, beryl_host and beryl_scene (including its tests) build without beryl_session or the card crates in their dependency trees (cargo tree -e normal,dev shows none). After an edit to beryl_session, just b3d cargo-test recompiles only beryl_3d, beryl_record and their tests among the Beryl 3D crates. Before and after timings recorded on the bead.

Timings: a one-line edit appended to rust/crates/beryl_session/src/lib.rs, then just b3d cargo-test, a warm-up run and then three runs, each with a different edit. Before (base 8169355): about 64 s (estimated from log times), 49.2 s and 45.4 s, so a median of 49.2 s at load 60-72. After: 28.0 s, 28.3 s and 27.5 s, so a median of 28.0 s at load 34-47. The loads differ, so part of the gap is load. Each run includes a re-recording (record, 7-23 s), because the recordings key hashes the engine sources. That is expected: beryl_record links the engine. After the edit, cargo test --no-run in rust/beryl3d compiles only beryl_3d and beryl_record among the Beryl 3D crates. cargo tree --all-features -e normal,dev shows no beryl_session, beryl_cards or beryl_engine under beryl_assets, beryl_render, beryl_host, beryl_scene, beryl_pictures or beryl_inspect.

Deviation from the plan: the card-records code went into a new crate, rust/crates/beryl_card_records, which depends on beryl_card_store and beryl_protocol. The token tie-break used beryl_cards::CARDS, so it now takes a TokenMakers argument. beryl_session passes the engine's cards (engine_makers) and keeps its old API as wrappers. decklist_faces passes the decklist's own cards. Beryl 3D tests that have no maker list use TokenMakers::NONE, which shows the store's first printing. The beryl_assets tests parse docs/sample-decks/...wu-control.txt instead of using beryl_cards::decks::WU_CONTROL. The scene-purity check now enforces the rule through engine_use() in purity.py.
