Cross-host contention also cost by-bmdk.1.4 and by-bmdk.1.7 about 9 and 8 minutes before checks began. In Enact scripts/enact.py, land checks ownership after running the fast check, but the on-success state that feeds ENACT_CHECKED is local to land and is discarded when run_queue reacquires a lease. Trace the lost-lease path and retain proof of the passed tree across reacquisition, within the same integrator. Reuse only for the identical tree and batch, or use the existing ENACT_CHECKED lane invalidation for a changed checked tree. Expected gain is one redundant full fast check for a safely retryable lease loss, about 6–8 minutes on the observed host at this load. This change belongs in the Enact repository and must land through its own flow.
