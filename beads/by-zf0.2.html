Close reason

Saves record choices by meaning (v2), with explicit journaled leniency; v1 still replays

Description

Part of the durable-saves epic (read it first). Depends on the resume bead.

Record every choice by meaning instead of by candidate index, so a save survives candidate reordering, card fixes that leave the chosen action legal, and added or removed prompts.

Build

  • A save format v2 in beryl_session (replay.rs or a new save module) with a format_version field. Each choice entry, the human's and the agent's alike, is a semantic action. Keep the index too, as a hint used only to break ties and to report drift.
  • Vocabulary: start from beryl_scenario's Action and Pick (lib.rs) and its matcher answer() (run.rs, about line 1037), which turn "cast Lightning Bolt", "the card named X", "player 1" into a Choice. beryl_scenario is only a dev-dependency of beryl_session today: move the shared vocabulary somewhere both can use (keep scenarios' &'static str const builders working) rather than depending on the test crate.
  • Gaps to close:
    • Same-named objects in different states (a tapped and an untapped Island, two Bears with different counters). Pick::Card takes the first match; a save needs a stable per-game instance name, e.g. one assigned when the card was first revealed (the reveal bead fills this in) or a public slot handle that never changes meaning.
    • Activations by AbilityIx index: confirm they're stable against card edits, or describe the ability by its cost/text.
    • Every DecisionKind and Opt must have a description; a compile-time-exhaustive match so a new one can't be forgotten.
  • Replay leniency, explicit and journaled: a decision the log doesn't have with exactly one candidate (or a smart-stop auto answer) is answered; a recorded action whose decision no longer appears is skipped only when the rules make it moot (say, a priority pass). Anything else stops the replay at that step with a clear reason.
  • The app writes v2; Game::from_replay and resume read v1 and v2 (the migrations bead turns v1 into v2 later). Bug reports (bugs.rs) and just review keep working.
  • Update docs/engine-architecture.md §7/§14 where they say a replay is (decklists, seed, choices).

Tests

  • Every existing session test that round-trips a replay passes through v2.
  • A v2 game replays identically when its candidates are permuted before matching (the full perturbation suite comes later; this is the smoke test).
  • One test per leniency rule, and one where a recorded action is illegal and replay stops with the step named.

Derek Thurn2026-11-08

Decisions and deviations: (1) The vocabulary lives in beryl_session::save (Chosen {index, what: Option<Described>}, Described {player, turn, step, kind, act}, and Act/Obj/Kind/StepName/ZoneName/Color/CastingName/AbilityRef). Matching moved to the engine for both crates: State::name and Opt::object_named, which beryl_scenario's run.rs now uses. Scenario Action/Pick stay as authoring types, since a save needs owned names and every Opt. (2) An answer records who decided and the turn and step. Without them a missing pass shifted every later identical pass a decision early; the test caught it. That added a third leniency rule: a priority the record lacks, before the turn and step of its next answer, is passed, because the original game passed it unasked. Identical passes by one player within one step are still ambiguous. (3) Obj is {name, zone, controller, tapped}. Same-named objects that differ only in counters or damage tie, and the index hint breaks the tie. That gap is for the reveal bead's instance names. (4) Activations: AbilityIx::Own(i) shifts when a static or triggered ability is added to the card, so an ability is recorded by its ordinal among the face's activated and mana abilities. Granted abilities keep their grant index. Payment plans keep their index, since plans differ only by consequences the engine computes. Casting options are recorded by keyword. (5) v1 and v2 share one path. Chosen deserializes from a bare index (v1, matched by index as before) or an object (v2). formatVersion defaults to 1. The old log equality check is replaced by a journaled drift count. (6) save::reverse_candidates is a doc-hidden thread-local test hook (beryl_session isn't covered by the engine's banned-types test) and is the start for the perturbation bead.
