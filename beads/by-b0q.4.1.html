Mason audit of M1b at staging 2532e87. Ranked summary.

Conformance with docs/engine-architecture.md: sound. Agents see only Observation (the one escape, Observation::unmasked_root, is used only by mcts-omniscient and is labeled cheating); MCTS follows decision 5 (choice-only nodes replayed from the root, iteration budgets, root-parallel trees summed in tree order, ensemble determinization); the greedy agent is the rollout policy; beryl_app is thin (Tauri wrappers around beryl_session::Sessions plus art.rs, command names checked at compile time); no engine bugs were fixed in M1b (by-b0q.3.21 was a transcript misread; docs/bugs.md has no entries). CI: slow 66 s of 300 s (rust 50 s under lane contention, shots 57 s, oracle 30 s); warm cargo test --workspace 19 s, slowest test 5.5 s (replay equivalence); no wall-clock thresholds, sleeps or Steward flake beads; perf gates count instructions/work, not time.

Filed (gate children, role:executor, review:inline):

  1. by-b0q.4.3 Resolution prompts say what they ask. The engine's resolution decisions are generic (ChooseUpTo/ChooseObject/YesNo), so the UI (choices.ts: 7 card-name constants, LOOKS, TIME_SPIRAL_LANDS, HAND_SIZE, an in-library scry guess) and the expert (Jace +2 vs Map explore by name) recover their meaning from card names; the UI also copies §15's smart stops (layout.ts nextStop) and guesses sources. Proposal: an Ask purpose on Decision, forwarded by DecisionView with Rust-computed passTo and source. Matters most for the cube: every new look/scry/explore card would otherwise need TS and agent edits keyed by name.
  2. by-b0q.4.4 Typed faces, casting options and mana colors (P3). by-b0q.2.4 left face: u8, costs: u8 bitmask and color: u8 in Opt/StackEntry; M1b copied the costs & (1 << i) decode into agents, CLI and protocol (8 sites, 6 crates) and faces[usize::from(face)] to 44 sites.

Considered, not filed:

  • expert/mod.rs is 2558 lines of name-keyed rules (138 names:: uses) mixing counter discipline, main-phase play, removal aim, walkers and targets. Splitting it by Timing would only move code; it is M1-specific card knowledge that the cube's search-based agents will not reuse. Revisit only if the expert is carried to the cube.
  • UI Intent closures (decisions.ts wants/alike closures parked in a ref and replayed on view change, JSON.stringify option equality): works and is covered by ui-test; plain-data intents would be cleaner, low value now.
  • Board.tsx (849 lines) mixes gesture-to-act mapping, HUD and arrows; a pure useBoardInput hook would aid testing. Candidate kinds are re-inspected ad hoc and slotOf has a silent default arm (no assertNever).
  • Slot vs CandidateIndex are both plain number in generated TS; branded ts-rs types would be cheap.
  • The replica harness hand-builds PlayerViews (replica/games.ts) and re-composes Game's Stage; sharing a GameStage component would stop drift.
  • RolloutPolicy's expert: bool flag routes valuation into crate::expert::appraise; a valuation trait would be tidier, not simpler.
  • ui-test asserts geometry (drop at 800,380, 20 px drag-follow tolerance), so layout-preserving refactors that move the hand fan will need test edits; acceptable for an interaction test.
  • Agent seed derivation differs between beryl_cli (xor-salted stream) and beryl_session (direct seed); harmless, both reproducible.
