Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Sound' ('A sound is triggered by the client core') and 'The frame' (sounds); the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/frame.rs (Sound, SoundId, FrameBuilder::sound, from by-q44s.3.2), src/play/mod.rs (Show::frame, Playing), src/play/step.rs, build.rs. Web: ui/src/audio/steps.ts (Cue: a sound, the edge of its step it is asked for at, and into, a share of the step after that edge; CUES: one function for each verb; moving; stepCues; sideOf and controllerOf: whose doing a step is; THEIRS_DB; soundSteps: a sound asked for more than once at one stage time plays once), ui/src/audio/manifest.json (sounds: the cues and their files), ui/src/audio/sound.ts (Sound: the cue names).

Build, in a new file beryl_scene/src/sound.rs:

  • The build script reads the keys of sounds in ui/src/audio/manifest.json, in the file's order, and writes them as sound::NAMES: [&str; N]; SoundId(n) is the n-th. It reads that file and writes nothing under ui/.
  • pub fn cues(act: &Act, found: &Found) -> impl Iterator<Item = Cue>: a port of CUES, verb by verb, with the same sounds, edges and into shares, where Found is what the web's is: the script's table as the step found it and the steps of its beat before it. Port moving and sideOf with it. A match over Verb with no wildcard arm, so that a new verb does not build until it has a line.
  • As a script is compiled, each step's cues become SoundRun { sound: Sound, at: u64 } in Compiled, at the step's start or end plus into of its length, with theirs set when sideOf says the opponent. Show::frame passes each to FrameBuilder::sound in the first frame whose now is at or after its real time, once; two of the same sound at one time are one. A script with a reset still sounds its steps.
  • No playback: milestone 3 plays what the frame lists.

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/sound.rs: for verbs-zones' first three scripts built by hand from its recorded messages (a fixture file under tests/fixtures is not needed: build the acts with testing::ScriptBuilder), each step that cues gives a sound has that sound in the frame that first reaches its time and in no other frame; a cast by the opponent has theirs; two cards drawn at one instant sound once; frames at 60 and at 30 a second list the same sounds in the same order; the frame that sounds allocates nothing. In beryl_inspect/tests/recorded.rs: playing verbs-zones from each seat, every sound's frame time is within one tick after its step's start or end (Page::steps).

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_inspect; just b3d gate; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

The tests pass; sound::NAMES has as many entries as sounds in the manifest (a test); cues has an arm for each of the 16 verbs and no wildcard; just b3d gate passes without --update.
