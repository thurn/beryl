Close reason

ui-test runs on a paused clock by default with fonts loaded; 15 clean runs under load

Description

Make the browser harness deterministic by default (plan K21). Nine of 34 Steward beads were a check racing the wall clock or font loading; all 212 checks in ui/scripts/interactions.mjs install a fake clock (check(), line 149) and only 23 pause it.

  • check() pauses the clock once the page is ready unless the check opts out with a named option; time then moves only through clock.runFor and friends (322 uses exist).
  • The ready wait includes document.fonts.ready (by-xzx).
  • Repair the checks that relied on running time; prefer advancing the clock over opting out. A check that needs wall time says why in a comment.
  • Change no product code and delete no check here: pruning is the backfill bead's.

Acceptance criteria

ui-test passes five full runs in a row while just test-rust loops beside it; no check waits on unpaused wall time without a comment saying why.

Notes

2026-11-10: by-6sbc names two checks to use as cases: 'an ETB trigger flies directly from the settled permanent (opponent, stop)' (first requestAnimationFrame sample) and 'bug report thaw preserves later player arrows' (REST_MS in Stack.tsx). Keep repairs cheap: most of this suite is expected to be replaced by a per-verb and per-prompt-shape suite when the planned UI presentation track builds its stage. A check that the paused clock does not fix in a few minutes is deleted with a line in the commit body, not rewritten. 2026-11-10, from the presentation-track session (its reading of the code; the failure was not reproduced): a paused clock does not cover timers registered in a React useEffect. Passive effects flush in real time, so on a loaded machine the effect can register its timer after the test has advanced the clock, and a fixed clock.runFor(N) past the duration then lands before the timer exists. Seen once: 'opponent scry plays consecutive results and lifts the new top cards' (the expiredScry effect in ui/src/game/Board.tsx, 1,800 ms) failed on a docs-only commit although it pauses its clock. Repair on staging as 3f0c7e4e (by-k0h4): untilGone(page, selector, limit, step) beside until() in ui/scripts/interactions.mjs steps the clock until nothing matches and fails past the limit. In scope here: replace 'gone after runFor(N)' assertions on effect-set timers with untilGone, starting with the sibling 'opponent scry fans out...' checks (fixed runFor(1650)); the same stepping pattern applies to 'appears after runFor(N)'. Do not move product timers out of effects: the stage (by-w8gv.21, K25) replaces them.
