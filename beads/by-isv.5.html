Description

Reviewers measured these on busy boards:

  • Covered permanents: with 6–8 or more nonland permanents a side, P/T badges, loyalty and whole permanents were covered (probe.covered), and names were cut to about 5 letters. A click at a card's centre hit its neighbour, and a Wandering Emperor that was a legal target was fully covered. The nonland row piled up on the right with about 580 px empty on the left; re-check after 4685165.
  • The stack: when it appears it compresses and reflows the battlefield by 100–290 px, and it lies over permanents. See also by-ash, the lifted stack covering the opponent's piles.
  • The hover inspector: it opens over the stack and the newest permanents, and stays up after its card moves. Hovering a centre hand card hides your own life total.
  • Banners: the COMBAT and coin-flip banners overlap the life orb and stay up for a turn.
  • Attacking: declaring an attacker re-sorts the row by up to 700 px, so the next click misses; re-check after f66b4ec.
  • Small text: rules text is 6–10 px in overlays and on the opening hand, and cards there cannot be enlarged. Hover should open the inspector everywhere a card appears, overlays included.

Files: ui/src/game/layout.ts, ui/src/game/Board.tsx, ui/src/game/CardLayer.tsx, ui/src/components/Stack.tsx.

Before changing anything, reproduce each item on current staging. Use just ui-live, as the ui-review skill describes (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md), or a fixture. The findings come from reviews of the build before the 2026-11-05 UI fixes, so some may already be fixed. Drop those, and list them in the close reason.

Design rules (see the epic): follow the skill's design philosophy, and add no standing words to the table. What a player needs at a glance is shown as icons, badges, numbers, colour or position. Everything else goes behind disclosure: hover, the inspector, a pile's browser, or the game log.

Acceptance criteria

Add just ui-test checks, using the measuring approach of ui/scripts/drive.mjs's probes, on a late-game fixture with at least 8 nonland permanents a side and a spell on the stack:

  • no P/T badge, loyalty badge or card name is covered;
  • sweeping the pointer across each row moves nothing but the hovered card;
  • the stack appearing and resolving moves no battlefield card by more than 4 px;
  • the inspector never covers the stack or a life total.

Hovering a card in an overlay opens the inspector (a check).
