Description

Why

The machine runs at load 35-55 on 18 cores most of the time (many agent sessions, slow CI, browser runs). Measured before this pass:

  • About 1 in 5 fast checks that ran the rust lane had its tests step killed at the 120 s deadline (37 of ~196 in 3 days), several on a quiet machine; sessions then reran the whole push, adding load.
  • Headless WebKit used about 11 of 18 cores. Its page and GPU processes are started by launchd at priority 46-55, above every build (31); taskpolicy -c utility on the run reaches Playwright.app but not them.
  • enact push held the host's ownership lock through rebase, fast check and push, so every claim, close, meta and sync on the host waited out other sessions' checks and failed "ownership operation busy" after 180 s. enact sync took 101 s wall for 6 s of work.
  • Each bd command took 1.6-2.7 s under load: the embedded Dolt journal had grown to 410 MB and is never compacted. verdicts.Beads.history read 123 ci-check beads with bd show, about a second each, and timed out at 120 s, so the prune and quarantine judged without verdicts.

What changed

Beryl:

  • 53f7e97e/ad51d97b ci: eight game-playing tests run in slow CI only (about 160 test-seconds a fast check).
  • 930c34d4 perf: ci-check verdicts read with one bd export (2 s for every bead) instead of bd show.
  • daa1b4b3 refactor: slow-only checks in their own module (verdicts.py was at 802 lines).
  • bfd6d628 fix: lanes the deadline killed rerun once with a fresh deadline on the same clock (and within slow CI's wall limit); their failures wait for no verdict. Vite and spec-server waits use the load-adjusted clock.
  • 16a3c720 perf: browser pages hold machine-wide slots (ui/scripts/slots.mjs): half the cores' worth in all, checks may use all of them, any other run shares a third.
  • 3c78fe57 docs: AGENTS.md says so.
  • 15da9f6e perf: the fast rust lane runs clippy and tests only on the changed packages and their dependents (rust_tests.affected_packages); workspace files, the lock file and the card store still check everything.
  • 9d51c502 perf: the python lane's pytest runs in four processes (pytest-xdist), about half the time.
  • 4d35594a perf: a recheck after a lost race runs only the lanes the commits landed since also select; the what-changed helpers move to changes.py (checks.py was at 801 lines).

Enact:

  • 3d5645f Hold the ownership lock only for push bookkeeping (first ownership check; final check, push and delivery record).
  • 95e7dee Sync compacts an embedded Beads store with dolt gc once its journal passes 64 MiB (history untouched; bd gc would squash commits the remote has). Ran once by hand: store 418 MB to 136 MB, bd show 1.1 s to 0.18 s.
  • fe1ec33 A recheck after a lost race sets ENACT_CHECKED to the commit the last pass checked. Staging takes about 20 pushes an hour, so a 2-minute check often loses the race.

Decisions (defaults taken, no approval asked)

  • Browser slots: half the cores in all; non-check runs a third. Slow CI alone runs about ten pages and its ui-test lane is its critical path (280-380 adjusted s against 300), so a tighter cap would push slow CI into budget failures.
  • Not done: clamping WebKit after launch (taskpolicy -b -p works but pages live seconds, so it means chasing pids, and darwinbg would distort timing checks); a machine-wide cargo jobserver (a step killed at the deadline would leak its tokens and starve every later build); bd server mode (experimental in bd 1.3.2, and Enact's conflict resolution needs embedded mode); lowering slow CI's priority (its strict budget would fail more often).
  • A deadline kill reruns regardless of load: a slow lane after a rebase fails the same way on a quiet machine, and a hung one hangs again and fails.
