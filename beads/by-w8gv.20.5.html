Description

Decompose step two of the CI track against the run log and verdicts as built, following the epic's decisions and model policy. File beads under this epic for:

  • Coverage recording: a nightly Chromium run of ui-test and shots that records, per check, the functions and CSS rules it executes, mapped to source lines, into $BERYL_CACHE. Decide what runs the nightly job.
  • Selection in the fast check (checks.py fast): the browser checks whose recorded lines meet the diff, time-boxed, worst failure record first; the log's chosen field says why. First measure the fixed cost of recording fixtures, starting Vite and launching the browser: it must leave room inside 60 s.
  • Oracles for the changed cards' scenarios before the push, if they fit the budget; report the measured cost if not.
  • Bisection in by ci: rerun the failed checks over the commits since master, and end the log with the checks, the culprit commit and each check's record, short enough for Enact's log tail. A run over budget on a loaded machine or a cold cache reruns and files nothing. The selection-miss verdict.
  • The weekly prune: a recurring tier:sonnet bead that runs just ci-report and applies the rule, honoring keep comments.
  • A measurement bead for the acceptance bar, two weeks after the rest lands, and a Warden for the step.
