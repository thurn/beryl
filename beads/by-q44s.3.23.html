Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Effects' (the table: what the Baseline tier draws for each) and 'The frame' (band 4, the glow and streak materials); the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/effects/light.rs (the stub write(runs, now, scene, builder)), src/effects/mod.rs (Effect, EffectRun), Scene::reach, src/frame/draw.rs (Draw::glow with sprites::DISC, BURST, RING and CONE; Draw::streak with sprites::COMET). Web: ui/src/stage/effects.ts (Effects, Effect, Drawn, Layer: under or over the card), ui/src/stage/flare.ts (FLARES, flaresOf; SETTLE, BEAM, TRAIL: sizes and how each spreads and fades with progress), ui/src/stage/effects.css (the sizes and colors of glow, impact, flash and pulse), and each effect's shows text and layer in data/ui-conventions.json.

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build effects::light::write. For each run under way whose subject has a place this frame (Scene::reach; a run on a player is at the player's anchor), write, in Band::Glows:

  • glow: one DISC under the card, a card and a half wide at first, spreading to twice that and fading with the run's progress.
  • impact: one BURST over what was struck, about one and a half of its widths across, spreading a little as it fades.
  • flash: one BURST over the spell, spreading to about twice the card's width.
  • beam: one CONE from the side of the ability toward the middle of the table, reaching nearly two card widths in a quarter of its time, then narrowing and fading.
  • pulse: one RING round the card's frame.
  • trail: one Draw::streak with COMET behind the dashing card, along the way it has come in the last effects.trail.frames sixtieths of a second (its pose at now and at now less that time, both from Scene::reach), gone as the run ends.

A record's params[0] is the run's eased progress. Sizes are shares of a card under effects.<name> in space.json, copied from SETTLE, BEAM, TRAIL and effects.css, and colors are constants in src/tokens.rs from the same rules. A light that the web draws under the card is placed 0.1 millimetres below the card's height and one drawn over 0.1 above it, so the depth test orders it. An effect moves nothing.

Do not build: settle, dissolve, dim, blanch, cover or shake (by-q44s.3.24); loss and gain (by-q44s.3.14); bloom or particles (milestone 6).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/effects_light.rs, on hand-built scripts whose timings carry each effect: each of the six has exactly one record in every frame inside its run and none outside it; the record's centre follows a card in flight; glow's record is below the card's height and flash's above; no resolved element's corners differ between a frame with the effect and the same frame of the same script without it; a frame with 40 effects allocates nothing; Frame::load.blended_area for one glow is under 0.02 screens at 1600 by 900.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d film verbs-zones --from 0ms --to 3s --frames 12; just b3d gate; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

The tests pass; just b3d gate passes, the commit body giving old and new counts where a limit rose; a comment gives the path of a just b3d film in which a settle or glow record is visible, or says that by-q44s.3.22 has not landed and the records draw as nothing yet.
