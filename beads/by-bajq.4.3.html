Description

What is wrong today

size_of::<State<Small>>() is 20,344 bytes (measured at staging 8c868ebb), 136 under the 20,480 limit (R4 in docs/rules-coverage.md). The work of groups 4 to 8 plans to add more than that, and no bead has an allotment:

About 250 to 330 bytes in all.

Where the room is

state::Object (beryl_kernel/src/object.rs) is #[repr(align(4))] and 24 bytes, but its fields sum to 22 (card 1, incarnation 2, zone 1, owner 1, base controller 1, flags 1, damage 1, counters 3, attached_to 1, timestamp 2, face 1, combat 5, battlefield_index 2). With an alignment of two it is 22 bytes, 510 fewer over the 255 slots. Find out why the alignment was set (git log -S 'repr(align(4))' -- rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/object.rs) and whether the count holds without it. If it does not, other candidates: CombatRole (5 bytes) packed smaller, or a rare structure moved to Large only (principle 3: a game that first needs it stalls into Large).

What to build

  1. Free at least 300 bytes of State<Small> with the count gate holding with no bump.
  2. Write the allotment of each bead above into the kernel contract's table of allotments (beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs, "Shapes, capacities and bytes"), and comment each bead with its allotment, as by-w8gv.7's beads had.

What must not change

Every state hash a test pins only through State's own Hash, which feeds fields in a fixed order; rollouts_never_change; the frozen saves.

Acceptance

size_of::<State<Small>>() before and after in the close reason, at most 20,180; the count before and after (just count-gate), no bump; the allotment table in the contract.

Acceptance criteria

State<Small> at most 20,180 bytes with the count gate holding (no bump); each growing bead of groups 4-8 has an allotment in the kernel contract and a comment.
