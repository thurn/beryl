Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Verbs', 'Effects' and 'Interface checks' ('No jumps', 'Every step shown'); the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/verbs/vanish.rs and emerge.rs (stubs), src/verbs/mod.rs (StepIn, StepOut), src/play/step.rs (how what makes room for a step slides: StepTiming::closes). Web: ui/src/stage/enter.ts (entering, and Entering: an ability lifting out of its source), ui/src/stage/desktop.ts (emerging(source, rest): the small copy on the source that travels to the stack), ui/src/stage/tracks.ts (dissolve: the card stays where it was and never travels; fade; CLOSES and roomIn: what the card left room for closes up after it has gone; CLOSED; shownUntil; closedBy). Data: the cases of vanish (destroyed, countered, resolved instant or sorcery, exiled, bounced, sacrificed, ability finished) and emerge (triggered ability, activated ability) in data/ui-conventions.json.

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build:

  • vanish: the subject stays at from for the whole step, and at the step's end it is at to with no path, or gone when the act carries no record. Its opacity over the step is the web's dissolve: 1 - eased progress squared, as a new Shape::Fading of pose::Shape. The look of its going (ash, white light, a glow) is the step's effect, which by-q44s.3.24 draws; this bead draws none. An ability that finishes (duration 0) is cut at once.
  • emerge: the ability starts as a copy on its source, at the pose emerging gives (copy its size as verbs.emerge.start_scale and its offset on the source as verbs.emerge.start_offset), and travels to its place on the stack over the step along the step's curve, growing. An ability with no source on the table fades in at its place. Its source does not move.

Do not build: the beam effect, or where the stack's items stand.

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/verbs_leaving.rs, on views and scripts built by hand with testing::ViewBuilder, ScriptBuilder and testing::timing: for each case of the verb in data/ui-conventions.json (all nine above), a script with that case's duration and delay plays to its end with every object at its placement (agent::steps::rest_equals_placement finds nothing), no_jumps finds nothing, and step_times finds nothing. An emerging ability moves at least 4 logical points over at least 150 ms (step_shown finds nothing), and a vanishing card's opacity at half its step is 0.75 with a linear curve.

When it is done: set BUILT = true in the verb's file, so that Client::next_unbuilt stops reporting it; run just b3d test --update and just b3d timeline-parity --update if those commands exist on staging by then, and commit the lowered lists.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d check verbs-zones; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

The tests pass; Client::next_unbuilt reports neither verb on verbs-zones (a test); just b3d check verbs-zones reports no no-jumps, rest-placement or step-time fault for a vanish or emerge step.
