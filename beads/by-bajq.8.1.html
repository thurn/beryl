Description

Card

Stifle: "Counter target activated or triggered ability. (Mana abilities can't be targeted.)"

What is wrong today

A target is an object or a player. An ability on the stack has no slot and no identity: the kernel's cause tags name a stack entry by its place (StackIx), and the architecture document says why the entries have no field for it ("a field there is copied in every rollout").

What to build

Plan: Designs 7, "Abilities as targets"; decision R10.

  1. state::Target gains an ability, named by its place on the stack counted from the bottom. TargetKind gains "an activated or triggered ability", with a filter by kind.
  2. Repair. Only an entry above an ability can name it. Where the kernel removes an entry from below the top (State::take_off_stack), it walks the entries above: a target that named the removed entry becomes "gone" (an illegal target, CR 608.2b), and one that named a higher place moves down by one. Removing the top entry repairs nothing. An invariant checks that every ability target names an entry below its own.
  3. actions::counter on an ability removes its entry (CR 701.6a). The source is untouched. A countered delayed trigger does not wait again.
  4. Legal targets: every activated or triggered ability on the stack, delayed ones included. Mana abilities never are on it.
  5. A save describes such a target by the ability's source and its index among the source's abilities, and by its place among like ones (beryl_session::save).
  6. The view marks the entry as a target, as it marks a spell.

Scenarios

Stifle on a fetchland-style ability if one is implemented, otherwise on Strip Mine's; on an enters trigger; Stifle's target countered by something else first. Two more need cards of group 5: Dark Depths' state trigger (it triggers again) and the storm trigger of Tendrils of Agony. Those two are by-bajq.9.4's to write, which runs after both groups: do not wait for them here.
