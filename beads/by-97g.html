Description

AI improvement ladder

The plan is docs/ai-strategy.md, rewritten 2026-11-11. Read it first. One fair champion, the app's Deep Searcher (beryl_search::DEEP_SEARCHER, starting as pimc72/hidden/rollout/tuned/anchor@30M), is improved one challenger at a time. A challenger is promoted by the gate of §4.3 and by nothing else.

What changed on 2026-11-11

  • Rungs 1 and 2 of the old plan failed at the suite and no match was played.
  • Greedy alone gets 11 of the 20 suite positions right; the champion is fixed on
    1. So the root first keeps greedy's answer where greedy is right (guard), then policy rules repair what greedy gets wrong (hold, waste), then the expert's combat planner is lifted out for the search (combat), then the search rungs (mid, 18 worlds, order).
  • Everything runs on Cygnus, in one lane, written for small models.

Rules for every bead here

  • Every bead carries lane:ladder (the games ledger aside). One session works them in dependency order. No two matches run at once.
  • All suites, screens, matches and audits run on Cygnus (BERYL_CACHE=D:\beryl-cache; positions at D:\beryl-cache\ladder\positions).
  • A rung is two beads: build (the change as an option that is off by default, with tests, pushed) and gate (§4.3, mechanical, tier:light).
  • A gate bead follows its numbered steps exactly. It may not ask for more than §4.3 does: fixing any one claimed entry passes the first condition.
  • A step that fails in a way the bead does not cover: write it in the notes, close the bead as failed, go on. Never wait for a person; never file a bead asking one to look.
  • Results go in the bead's notes and as a row in docs/ladder.md. Logs, transcripts and positions are never committed.

Order

Tools: rules plumbing, just gate, the bars, just why. Then the rungs, each a build bead and a gate bead: guard (root), hold and waste (policy), combat (root: the combat planner), then mid, 18 worlds and order (search). Dependencies hold the order. Last, by-97g.31 designs the tree of turns (§9).

One yardstick is measured once, after guard: the champion anchored to the expert's answers (by-97g.32 builds it, by-97g.33 measures; §6.6). It is never promoted.

Closed without a match, as parked or absorbed (docs/ai-strategy.md §8): old rung 3 (decided positions), old rung 4 (now hold, waste and combat), old rung 7 (many worlds for once-a-game draws), old rung 9 (turn discount), old rung 10 (90M), and by-97g.15 (now hold).

Done

The four tools exist, the seven rungs have each run their gate, passed or not, and docs/ladder.md has a row for each. The mirror itself is done when a human finds a new blunder in fewer than one game in five (§1).
