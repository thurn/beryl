What the player saw: the thin line from the opponent's The Wandering Emperor ability on the stack to the planeswalker (on the opponent's row, right above and left of the stack card's middle) fell from the planeswalker's bottom straight down across the ability's text and came up into the card's bottom. Want: the line never crosses the ability card. Acceptance: (1) a source above the stack card gets a line that never passes over the stack card; (2) every earlier arc shape (by-olby, by-2e5a, 41cb0459: Arena-fitted hang, bounded sag, short curve into a source right under the stack) stays exactly as it was. Cause: hung() in ui/src/stage/lines.ts only special-cased a source BELOW the stack card. Arena's stack stands beside the battlefield, so Arena never shows a source over the stack card. Arena check: looked at the stills the hang was fitted to (5UHzpVvUkfI 19:48.9, 2VW6ZzmS8U4 32:16.0) and The Wandering Emperor's dump (just video card): Arena's lines leave the source bottom, hang low and come up into the stack card's bottom, never over it; no Arena instance has a source above the stack card (Arena has no such moment). Expectation that stands in: the mirror of Beryl's existing 'source right under the stack card' rule, a short curve from the stack card's top up into the source's bottom, used only where the hanging curve would cross the card. Verification: new stage-spans test (fails on old code, passes now) sweeps the far row and checks that no line crosses its card. Old-vs-new sweep over about 480k positions and 3 card sizes: identical curves everywhere except the 16,900 where the source lies wholly above the card and the hang crossed it. All 22 stage-spans tests pass (including the Arena-fitted 0.9-sag, far-row-near and right-under tests). Reported fixture replayed in ui-live at 1728x1084: the line now goes from the stack card's top up into The Wandering Emperor's bottom (/Users/dthurn/Library/Caches/beryl/ui-live/by-52mf/20261113-204737/001-after-fix.png). The UI Conventions text for the source line was updated. No generic UI audit can say 'a line crosses its own end', so the unit sweep holds the property. Handoff: ui/src/stage/lines.ts hung() + crosses(); test in ui/scripts/stage-spans.test.mjs; commit eb956fdb.
