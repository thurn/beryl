Description

Follows by-isv.6 (single-use acts on click) and by-isv.8 (menus).

  • Payment plans: seed 101 vs Expert, turn 10, Mana Crypt and lands untapped: clicking Jace, Vryn's Prodigy opens "Cast Jace... / Tap these, or click a glowing permanent: [Plains, Island] [Mana Crypt, Island] / Cast / Cancel" (2 gestures, D1/140-g1-click-jace-1200ms.png). Dragging the same card casts at once with the first plan (D1/146-g1-drag-jace-field-100ms.png). Click and drag should agree: the client picks the plan (as by-isv.12's auto-pay ranking does), shows the lands it tapped, and lets the player change it, in one gesture.
  • Planeswalkers: turn 6, clicking your Teferi, Time Raveler opens +1/-3; turn 8, at loyalty 1, the same click activates +1 at once (also Jace, Wielder of Mysteries' +1, turn 22). A loyalty activation cannot be undone and Arena always shows the abilities: a planeswalker's click always opens its ability menu, with unaffordable abilities shown disabled.
  • Menu entries say nothing: the ability menu shows only cost icons ("+1", "-3"; Jace, the Mind Sculptor "+2 / 0 / -1") with blank space where an effect would be (D1/069-g1-click-my-teferi-500ms.png, D1/070-g1-hover-minus3.png, D1/352-g1-jtms-menu-300ms.png); the effect appears only in an inspector elsewhere. Subtlety's cast step offers "Cast" and "Evoke, exile a blue card" without the {2}{U}{U} (D1/198-g1-subtlety-caststep.png). Each option must show what it does and costs, compactly (symbols, the lit paragraph beside the menu).

Design rules (epic by-isv): follow the skill's design philosophy (one gesture for the common case, the client does the bookkeeping, ask once in context, Arena first, show don't make them infer, few words) and add no standing words to the table. Every acceptance check is an agent's: just ui-test, just ui-shots, Rust tests or ui-live probes.

Design (deviation from 'lets the player change it'): click and drag both cast at once paying the session's ranked first plan (Arena's auto-tapper); the payment's permanents glow while the spell tops the stack (during targets and while the opponent answers). Choosing another plan is Arena's way: under Full control (exact stops) a click or drop opens the cast step with the plans, as before. Manual mana tapping isn't offered under plan payment, so full control is the change path. Menu casts now follow the click rule too (no redundant cast step). Planeswalkers: click always opens the menu; loyalty paragraphs without a candidate show disabled. Menu lines: cost symbols + effect from the ability's paragraph (reminder text stripped; cycling read from reminder). Cast step: 'Cast' pill shows the mana cost; the chosen option's card words (kicked paragraph, reminder text, or keyword paragraph) below the pills. New board/fixture walker-low (Teferi, Time Raveler at 1). Shot game-pay-plans replaced by game-cast-evoke and game-menu-walker-low (shots cannot set full control); the plan picker is covered by ui-test check on pay-other-exact. Inline review (cold Opus): (1) confirmed — Spara's Headquarters cycling line had no effect (colon only in reminder text): fixed, read from reminder, ui-test check added. (2) The paid-lands glow check freezes pay-default at the payment view; for an untargeted spell the glow lasts while the spell is on the stack (opponent's response window) — kept, noted. (3) Design question on changing the plan under smart stops — answered above (full control, Arena convention).
