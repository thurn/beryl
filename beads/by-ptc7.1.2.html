Description

Why

rust:clippy is the most expensive check in CI (about 76 s a run) and nobody knows which crates the time goes to. The efficiency store has per-check times (cilog.py) and no per-crate times. Plan: Designs D.

What to build

  • The rust lane's clippy and tests steps (tools/src/beryl_tools/rust_tests.py, rust_lane) write Cargo's per-crate timings when asked: cargo ... --timings writes target/cargo-timings/cargo-timing.html and a JSON form with -Z unstable-options --timings=json on nightly only, so prefer parsing the units from the stable HTML's embedded UNIT_DATA, or --message-format=json build-finished timestamps. Record crate, mode (check, clippy, test build), seconds and whether the unit was fresh. Slow CI turns it on; the fast check does not pay for it.
  • A timings source in efficiency/src/beryl_efficiency/ (model: the LineSource classes of ingest.py), a migration appended to MIGRATIONS in store.py, and metrics compile.<crate>.check, compile.<crate>.clippy and compile.<crate>.test-build registered in metrics.py.
  • just efficiency compile prints, for the last seven days, each crate's median seconds by mode, largest first, and the share of the lane's total.
  • Also record, from one manual run each, cargo llvm-lines for beryl_session and beryl_search (how much of each is the generic engine instantiated again) and put the top twenty rows in this bead's close reason.
  • just efficiency cards did not finish in two minutes on 2026-11-15. Find out why and fix it, or say in the close reason what it is waiting for.

Tests go in efficiency/tests/ with synthetic data only (efficiency/AGENTS.md). Add an entry to data/efficiency-changes.json only if this change itself saves time.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

just efficiency compile prints per-crate seconds from real slow-CI runs. just efficiency cards answers within 30 s. The close reason has the per-crate table and the llvm-lines rows.
