Derek Thurn2026-11-05

Warden review of by-b0q.1.7 (9a975c8..235cd13), reviewed as on staging 0d9eb55. Acceptance verified: just test-rust passes; every listed trigger, replacement, SBA and layer case has a black-box engine test, and the dirty-derived panic test plus random_games_with_triggers_never_read_derived_dirty together with the invariant show derived is never read dirty. Pushed e510c4e:

  • CR 704.5m was missing. zone::leave never cleared attached_to, so an Aura whose permanent left kept pointing at the old slot: it would enchant whatever reused that slot (a card flickered before SBAs, e.g. Jace's exile-and-return, or a new token) and broke the 'link to an empty slot' invariant once a token ceased to exist. Now leave() detaches, the SBA puts unattached Auras into the graveyard, Subtype::Aura was added, and new invariants require an attachment and an EXILED_UNTIL link to name a permanent on the battlefield.
  • remove_counters called Counters::get_mut even when nothing was removed. For a long-tail kind the object lacked, that hit the debug assert and in release reset its other counters (Story on Staff, Chorus on Malcolm). It also set derived_dirty with no refresh. Now it reads first and returns early.
  • Tests: Aura detached on flicker and on a token; a stolen creature's dies trigger belongs to its last controller (LKI, CR 603.3a); removing an absent counter kind changes nothing. The first and third fail without the fix. Left alone on purpose: the deviations the implementer recorded (no 616.1 ordering choice, legend rule in the repeated check, pay-life asked only on land plays), which are fine for the 29 cards; the Entry/TokenEntry overlap and the AbilityKind-vs-field redundancy, which are style; tokens created attacking always attack the player (combat's 508.4 choice, by-b0q.1.8's area). No test is timing-sensitive; the engine suite runs in 0.2 s.
