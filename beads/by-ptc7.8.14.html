Face-down spells and morph: Exalted Angel and Gathan Raiders

Description

Why

A permanent can be face down (manifest and manifest dread put one onto the battlefield, actions/manifest.rs; face_down.rs turns one up for its front face's mana cost if it is a creature card with a nonzero cost). A spell cannot: become_spell and the casting code never take FaceIx::DOWN. Morph, megamorph and disguise are on hundreds of cards. Plan: Designs I, "Face-down spells"; CR 702.37 and 708.

What to build

  • CastingOption::Morph(cost): the card may be cast face down for {3} from where it could be cast. The spell is a 2/2 creature spell with no name, text, subtypes or mana cost (CR 708.2; FACE_DOWN in definition.rs is that face). Its mana value is 0. Only its controller may look at it; determinization and the observation treat its identity as hidden, as they do a face-down permanent's. It enters face down.
  • Turning it up is the special action that exists (Opt::TurnFaceUp, face_up_cost), generalised: the cost is the morph cost, which may be any Cost (Gathan Raiders: discard a card), and it is offered for a face-down permanent whose card has a way to be turned up. A manifested card keeps its rule (its mana cost, if it is a creature card). "When this is turned face up" triggers.
  • A face-down spell or permanent is revealed when it leaves the stack or the battlefield and when the game ends (CR 708.9), and a face-down spell that is countered is revealed. An instant or sorcery card cannot be turned face up; if one would be, it stays down (CR 708 says what happens).
  • Face-down objects are indistinguishable to opponents: two face-down creatures of one controller must not be told apart by slot, by order of a list or by a save (docs/bugs.md has four entries of this class from copies and manifests: read them). Candidates, the script and the saves name them by battlefield order.
  • Exalted Angel (flying; lifelink-like trigger "whenever this deals damage, you gain that much life"; morph {2}{W}{W}) and Gathan Raiders (hellbent; morph, discard a card).

Write each card with the card-writing skill. Read its rulings first (just card rulings <name>): the card joins data/rulings-ledger.json with every ruling a scenario or a reason, and joins the keystone fuzz pool (beryl_cards/src/decks.rs). Where XMage or Forge cannot play the card, its scenarios are reported as unsupported and the ledger is the outside check, so be thorough with it.

Scenarios: cast face down, attack as a 2/2, turn up before damage; turn up in response to removal; countered face down (revealed); dies face down (revealed); a face-down spell is colorless with mana value 0 for filters; Gathan Raiders turned up by discarding with an empty hand afterwards (hellbent applies); two face-down creatures and what the opponent's view shows of each.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Both cards pass just card check and are in the ledger and the pool. The information-independence test and the save perturbation tests pass with face-down spells in play.
