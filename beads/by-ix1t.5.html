Description

Part of the AI ladder epic by-ix1t. Read docs/ai-plan.md first: §3 and §6.4.

The champion holds the policy's answer at four kinds of decision only (an attacker, a choice of up to a number of objects, a discard, and a cast or activation the policy scores at 0 or less). Everywhere else the search's most visited move is played, and in the 62% of decisions where the search's lead is under 0.01 that is a coin flip. skip-teferi-plus-one is the case: greedy takes Teferi's +1, the search prefers to pass by 0.03, paired rollouts prefer the +1 by 0.029, and nothing guards a pass chosen over greedy's action.

This bead adds the option first: the default is held at every kind of decision, and the paired test of rung 2 decides each override.

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs, guarded: it returns search != default for every kind when options.expert is set. first does the same with the policy's answer, or the planner's at combat with combat. Read its doc comment: it explains the never_cast exemption (Mana Crypt), where greedy passes by tuning and the search keeps its say.
  • LongSearch::choose_traced: where default and guards are built.
  • long/turn.rs, choose_turn: the turn search calls guarded too.

Build

  1. Add Options::first, named first, after paired in the fixed order. It requires anchor and paired: without the paired test it would repeat the expert yardstick, where the tree's own test overruled 10 of 18 right answers.
  2. With first set, guarded holds the default at every decision kind, mulligans and play-or-draw included. Keep the never_cast exemption: a cast of a card the policy is told never to cast is not guarded.
  3. Tests:
    • The name round-trips; first without paired is rejected.
    • At skip-teferi-plus-one, a seeded search with anchor+guard+paired+first at 300,000 steps takes the +1.
    • guarded returns true for one decision of every DecisionKind when the search's choice differs from the default, and false when they agree.
  4. The champion's moves do not change.

On Cygnus, run just blunders CHAMPION+paired+first --work 3M --seeds 1, with CHAMPION from docs/ladder.md, and paste the table into the notes. It is one sample an entry, so read it only for entries that are plainly wrong.
