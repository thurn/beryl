Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Objects and placement' ('Hands, the stack and cards being read keep their place on screen'), 'The Baseline tier' ('Shadows are single quads') and 'The frame' (band 1); the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/table/place.rs (hand: a fan with a fixed spacing and a turn of HandData::fan radians for each place from the middle; stack), src/table/mod.rs (HandData, Layout::stack and stack_step), src/table/camera.rs (Camera::screen_pose), src/table/scene.rs (Table::write), src/frame/draw.rs (Draw::contact_shadow), build.rs. Web: ui/src/game/fanArc.ts (FAN_RADIUS, onArc: Arena's hand lies on one circle whatever its size, so a crowded hand turns and sinks less for each card), ui/src/game/handLayout.ts (handPlacements: the viewer's fan; heldPlacements: the opponent's held cards; RAISED_HAND), ui/src/game/stackLayout.ts (STACK, stackPlace: where the stack's items stand, and how they close up as the stack grows), ui/src/components/Stack.tsx (StackItem), ui/src/stage/heft.ts (LIGHT: shadows fall toward the viewer and a little right; SHADOW: its darkness and the height over which it comes in; how it spreads with height).

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build:

  • The hands, in place::hand: each card stands on a circle of hand.fan_radius card widths (FAN_RADIUS), turned as the circle's tangent, at the spacing handPlacements gives for the number of cards (full spacing until the hand's width is reached, then closer). The opponent's hand is laid out by the same rule with heldPlacements' values, face down, along the top edge. Move the numbers of HandData for both hands into space.json under hand and opponent_hand, in normalized device coordinates and card widths, and read them from the build script's constants.
  • The stack, in place::stack: every item (spells and, once by-q44s.3.10 has landed, abilities) at the place stackPlace(index, count) gives, relative to the camera at the right of the table, the newest nearest the viewer, closing up as STACK says when the stack is tall. Values under stack in space.json. A stack of 50 keeps every item on screen.
  • Contact shadows, in a new file src/table/shadows.rs called from Table::write: a card whose height above what lies under it is more than 1 millimetre (in flight, in a hand, on the stack, held up) gets one Draw::contact_shadow in Band::ContactShadows, on the table at the height of what lies under it there (the top of a pile or a row's cards, which placement knows; 0 elsewhere) plus 0.1, displaced toward the viewer and to the right by LIGHT times its height, with a blur and an opacity that grow and fall with height as SHADOW has them. A card lying on the table gets none. Values under shadow.

Do not build: the hand card's rise under the pointer (by-q44s.3.27), piles or trays (by-q44s.3.26), the row that scrolls (by-q44s.3.29).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/table.rs and tests/shadows.rs: hands of 1, 5, 7, 10 and 60 cards lie inside the window from both seats at each size of the desktop matrix, each card keeping an uncovered strip at least audit::TARGET_POINTS wide; a hand's cards all lie on one circle within 0.5 millimetres; the turn of a card grows with its distance from the middle; a stack of 1, 3, 10 and 50 items is on screen with the newest nearest; every card in a hand or on the stack has exactly one shadow record and no card on the battlefield has one; a shadow under a card in flight over a 60-card library is above the library's top card; placement of the same view twice is equal; a frame with a 60-card hand allocates nothing.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d png verbs-zones --at end --tree; just b3d check verbs-zones --seat opponent; just b3d gate; just b3d lane.

Decisions and deviations:

  • Hand strip: a 60-card viewer's hand cannot give every card a 24-point strip in windows under about 1600x900 points (59 strips of 24 points plus a card's width exceed the window's width). The spacing never closes past layout.strip unless the hand would then run past the window's sides; the table test checks strips only where they fit (95% of the window width), and checks every size stays inside the window.
  • Opponent's hand: not the viewer's to press, so it closes up within heldPlacements' reach (no strip floor). Widening it pushed its outer cards above the top edge. It is laid out by the same circle rule, with a radius matching heldPlacements' parabola (36.2 back widths), upside down, 44 points showing.
  • Stack and hands use NDC/card-width units (camera-relative), not stage pixels to millimetres; values in space.json under hand, opponent_hand, stack and shadow (moved out of layout.desktop).
  • Resolving spell: a card in the stack zone missing from view.stack (resolving) is now the stack's top item (on_stack). Before, it had no place and vanished with no step; staging's checks missed it because stack items never moved. Exposed a missing self-exile step in fork/copies-time-spiral, filed as by-q44s.3.44.
  • verbs/fly.rs: a spell cast from a hand no longer rises verbs.fly.arc along the table's z. Both ends sit near the camera, and the rise pushed the card up the screen against its bow (upstream's on-screen bow test failed).
  • Shadow: oblique projection of the card along heft.ts LIGHT onto (highest resting pile/row card under it)+0.1, spread by PENUMBRA*height, alpha SHADOW.alpha*min(1,h/emerging). Hand and stack shadows land off the bottom of the screen (the cards are about 1 m above the table), which follows the light as specified; flight and lifted-card shadows show on the table.
  • Revealed Cards pile faces cast no shadow (only write_card and write_ability call write_shadow).
