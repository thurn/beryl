Wake idle Enact workers when their background job ends

Description

Symptom: during the by-q44s run, worker sessions often ended their turn while a long background job ran (a Callgrind count, the GPU gate in the VM, a sweep, slow CI) with a note like 'waiting on the VM counts; I'll continue when it reports'. When the job finished, nothing woke the session. It sat idle until enact next flagged it as stalled after 90 minutes and the vizier sent a nudge. Over 2026-11-15 this happened about a dozen times (by-q44s.1.12, .1.15, .1.16, .1.40, .1.49, .17, .19, .22, .23, .50), each costing 90 to 180 minutes of a worker slot.

Why: the 90-minute stall rule in enact next (Enact commit ce65f4a) cannot tell a session that is idle while waiting on its own finished job from one doing long legitimate work. The background-task notice that should wake the session is lost when the turn ends or when the job runs outside the harness (VM, bench machine).

Fix, in ~/enact (scripts/enact.py, skills/enact/roles/common.md):

  • Make workers wait on long jobs in the foreground or with a harness-tracked background command whose exit wakes them, never by ending the turn on a detached job. State this in common.md.
  • In enact next, flag a worker sooner (for example 15 minutes) when its session is idle and its bead has had no update, keeping 90 minutes for a session whose turn is still running.

Acceptance: a test in Enact shows an idle worker with a stale bead flagged within the short threshold, and a running one not flagged until 90 minutes. common.md tells workers how to wait on long jobs.
