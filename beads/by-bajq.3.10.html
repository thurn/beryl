Description

Cards

  • Circle of Protection: Red: "{1}: The next time a red source of your choice would deal damage to you this turn, prevent that damage."
  • Furnace of Rath: "If a source would deal damage to a permanent or player, it deals double that damage to that permanent or player instead."

What they need

Plan: Designs 3, "Prevention" and "Regeneration".

  • A shield is an entry of the Effects store (effects::Payload, at most 15 bytes): the kind of proposal it replaces, what is left of it (an amount, or "the next time"), and the source it was chosen for as an ObjectRef. It is used up as it applies. The source is chosen as the ability resolves and is not a target (CR 609.7): any source, a permanent, a spell on the stack, or a card in another zone that an ability names.
  • Doubling is a rewrite that multiplies the amount.
  • With a shield and Furnace of Rath both applying, the player dealt the damage chooses the order (by-bajq.3.2): prevent then nothing to double, or double then prevent it all, for "prevent that damage"; with a shield of a fixed amount the two orders differ in number. Write a test shield of "prevent the next 3" so the difference is a scenario.
  • Regeneration is by-ilmp, in epic by-sxgz, not this group: a shield in the Effects store that actions::destroy and the lethal-damage SBAs apply (CR 701.19). It may land before this bead. If it has, bring its shield into the replacement pipeline as a destruction proposal here rather than building regeneration again, and keep Drudge Skeletons' and Damn's scenarios passing. If it has not landed, build no regeneration here: say so in the close reason, and comment on by-ilmp that its shield is to be a destruction proposal in beryl_rules/src/replace.rs.

Files

The two card files, beryl_kernel/src/effects.rs, beryl_rules/src/{replace.rs, damage.rs, actions/destroy.rs, continuous.rs}.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate; R2 in the plan). The count is exact, so report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust). A change to what the WU mirror's rollouts do is a bug in this epic.
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (root AGENTS.md); just citations.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill: just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), every instruction carried out by its own scenarios, and just specs <card> passing the generic UI checks from both seats. A disagreement with an oracle is settled by the rules and recorded in data/oracle-divergences.json.
  • Read the card's rulings before writing its scenarios (just card brief <name> prints Scryfall's). Each statement in a ruling that a scenario can show is a scenario of the card. Where one cannot be shown yet, the close reason names the ruling and why.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry.
  • If the design in the plan is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.

Acceptance criteria

Both cards pass just card check; the ordering of a shield and a doubling is a scenario with two different outcomes.

Decisions: (1) The test shield is Mending Hands (a real card, prevent the next 4) rather than an invented 'prevent the next 3'; the Furnace ruling itself uses Mending Hands. (2) A prevention that stops all the damage applies without asking, since every order then deals 0 and it uses up the fewest shields; only a multiplier against a shield of an amount is asked. (3) Spec boards skip the moments where P0 responds to their own Bolt and the order-choice moments, the same existing limit Mother of Runes' Bolt moments hit; no generic UI check failed. (4) Pre-existing on staging and untouched: beryl_cards tests every_ability_of_a_generated_card (Builder's Blessing) and scenarios_name_only_their_card (Vigilance, Life and Limb, Wild Nacatl) fail locally under just test-rust; slow CI evidently doesn't run them.
