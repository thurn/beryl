Close reason

Objects are written only by beryl_kernel: object_mut and State::objects are crate-private, typed mutators and getters, the board door and the legacy accessor are in; counts 404,482,019 -> 404,476,069

Description

Milestone 3 (plan K6): only the kernel writes a field, and each mutator keeps bitboards, caches, links and knowledge consistent. This bead does that for objects, the largest cluster and the one behind most of docs/bugs.md.

Read first

docs/project-50k.md: Decisions K6, K7, K11 and K12, and "Crates", "State" and "Kernel rules" under Target architecture. rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs: its rustdoc is the kernel's API contract (who writes, mutator and getter naming, events, the board door, the legacy accessor, sets and orders); follow it, and change it in the same push when the code proves it wrong. enact brief shows the handoffs of the beads this one depends on.

Scope

  • Object<L> (beryl_kernel, from state.rs): flags, damage, counters, attached_to, linked, timestamp, face, combat, base_controller, zone, incarnation, card, battlefield_index. State::objects becomes private and object_mut private to the kernel.
  • Write sites when this was decomposed, by object_mut calls in rust/crates/beryl_engine/src: setup.rs 12, combat.rs 10, zone.rs 6, event.rs 6, state.rs 4, effect.rs 4, turn.rs 2, start.rs 2, chance.rs 2, cast.rs 2, resolve.rs 1, observation.rs 1, face_down.rs 1; tests: events.rs 9, combat.rs 5, casting.rs 3, invariants.rs 3, rules.rs 2, cast.rs 1. Several of those files are in the kernel now. Make object_mut private and let the compiler list what is left.
  • Mutators, by what the rules do: tap and untap (CR 701.26), summoning sickness (CR 302.6), mark and remove damage (CR 120.3, 514.2), put and remove counters of the kinds that stay (+1/+1, -1/-1, loyalty; CR 122), attach and unattach (CR 701.3), the exile link, turn face up or down and transform (CR 708), the combat flags and pairings (attacking, blocking, blocked, struck first; CR 506 to 510), control (CR 108.4), the per-turn resets. event.rs's primitives (deal_damage, add_counters, remove_counters, set_tapped) keep their replacement and trigger logic and call the mutator for the write, in whichever shape the contract's section b settled.
  • Getters on Object for what the rules give an object (zone, owner, tapped, damage, counters of a kind, attachment, face, incarnation and the rest), #[inline]. Reads inside beryl_engine move to them where you touch the line. Fields stay pub for readers in other crates until the by-w8gv.5.9 (readers) bead flips them: no &mut Object leaves the kernel after this bead, so a public field can no longer be written.
  • The seven ObjectFlags milestone 4 moves to Memos (KICKED, OFFSPRING, IMPENDING, EVOKED, EXILED_UNTIL, EXILED_WITH, LOYALTY_ACTIVATED) and Counters' other_kind and other go behind the legacy accessor with no typed mutator. event::Entry::flags carries some of them into the battlefield: keep that path, raw, through the accessor.
  • The board door (contract, section d): move setup.rs's mutators behind it and change their callers: beryl_scenario/src/setup.rs, beryl_session/src/checkpoint.rs, beryl_cli/src/blunders.rs, beryl_search/tests/greedy.rs, beryl_cards/src/tests.rs and beryl_engine/src/tests.

Constraints

  • This is the first cluster on the hot path. Run just bench-count before and after and record both. If the count rises, look for a mutator that lost inlining or re-derives something its caller knew.
  • combat.rs's order of writes is rules-visible (docs/bugs.md, newest entry: attackers tapped in slot order). Do not reorder.
  • effect.rs's ask sites are being edited by the prompts milestone (by-w8gv.11): keep the diff there to the writes.
  • The zone lists, the battlefield queue, knowledge and the derived cache are the next bead's (by-w8gv.5.4 (zones)); leave their fields pub.

Parity and checks

Parity (plan K11) at the push: just check passes; the frozen save corpus replays (cargo test -p beryl_session --test corpus); rollouts_never_change holds (cargo test -p beryl_bench rollouts_never_change); slow CI's counts lane (just count-gate, bar 405,276,238 instructions, 1% tolerance, rust/crates/beryl_bench/count-gate.toml) passes with no bump to the bar. No behavior changes: no rules fix, no new card, no new mechanic. Work the card-names and file-length allowlists down as files are touched (data/card-names-allowlist.json, data/file-length-allowlist.json; an entry moves with its file and its count only falls; a new file is under 800 lines). Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules (AGENTS.md); keep the citations of code you move.

Acceptance criteria

State::object_mut is not callable outside beryl_kernel and State::objects is private; every write to an Object from beryl_engine (non-test and test) goes through a kernel mutator or the board door; Object has an #[inline] getter for every field the rules give it; the seven M4 ObjectFlags and Counters' long-tail kind are reachable only through the legacy accessor; the board mutators are behind the contract's door and beryl_scenario, beryl_session, beryl_cli and the tests build boards through it; the handoff lists each mutator and getter with the field it replaces; just bench-count before and after is recorded on the bead; parity holds.

Combat mutators, arrive, make_token, become_spell, turn_face_up and the resets return nothing; lib.rs now says why (part of a larger announced change, or no Event exists for it).
  • mark_damage does CR 120.3c and 120.3e for a permanent and takes source and combat for the Event. Damage to a player still writes life in event.rs until by-w8gv.5.7.
  • No attach, unattach or set_base_controller mutator: no rules code does any of them on its own. Attachment and base controller are set by arrive (enters attached, under a player's control) and become_spell; a host leaving unattaches inside the zone move. Board::attach and Board::set_controller remain. The contract table is amended.
  • After add or remove counters event.rs still sets flags.derived_dirty from layers::counters_matter, which reads the card: that field is by-w8gv.5.4's and 5.7's.
  • Added State::drop_stale_effects (layers.rs split-borrowed s.objects against s.effects) and chance::adopt_unknown (resolve.rs wrote card identities).
  • event::Entry stays the rules' record; event.rs's arrive() builds a kernel mutate::Arrival from it and sends long-tail counters and Entry::flags through legacy_mut.
  • Inside the kernel the zone move, reset_permanents_for_turn and forget_exiled_with still clear legacy flags directly; everything else, the board included, goes through legacy_mut, so a search for it finds what milestone 4 must move.
  • setup.rs is now board.rs (git mv). off_board, a read, stays on State. Board gained set_entered_this_turn, set_loyalty_activated and swap_cards for tests that wrote those fields, and corrupt(slot, Corruption) for the invariant tests, which must break a state.
  • beryl_cli blunders.rs: set_face is now called for every placed card (a no-op for the front face, the move has already marked derived stale) to keep the file at its allowance of 844 lines.

Handoff: staging b1ac73e1 and 04c19eb1. State::objects is pub(crate) and State::object_mut is pub(crate): nothing outside beryl_kernel can write an Object. Its fields stay pub to read until by-w8gv.5.9.

Files: beryl_kernel/src/mutate.rs (new: the object mutators and the Arrival record), legacy.rs (new: Legacy, from State::legacy_mut), board.rs (was setup.rs: Board, from State::board; off_board stays on State), object.rs (getters, ObjectFlags::LEGACY, Counters::is_long_tail and long_tail; other_kind, other and get_mut are pub(crate)), chance.rs (adopt_unknown), lib.rs (contract amended: Mutators table, Events, Entities, board door, legacy accessor).

Mutators on State, with the Object fields each writes:

  • set_tapped(object, tapped) -> Event::Tap or Untap: flags TAPPED.
  • set_summoning_sick(slot, sick): flags SUMMONING_SICK.
  • reset_permanents_for_turn(active): flags SUMMONING_SICK (the active player's), ENTERED_THIS_TURN, LOYALTY_ACTIVATED, for every permanent. Reads controller, so derived must be current.
  • mark_damage(source, to, amount, combat) -> Event::Damage: damage (a creature), counters.loyalty (a planeswalker).
  • clear_damage(): damage of every permanent.
  • add_counters / remove_counters(object, kind, amount) -> Event::CountersAdded / CountersRemoved: counters.plus_one, minus_one, loyalty. A long-tail kind debug-asserts; use legacy_mut.
  • turn_face_up(slot): face, timestamp, plus zones.next_timestamp, both players' knowledge and derived_dirty.
  • become_spell(slot, face, controller): face, base_controller.
  • make_token(slot, owner, card, one_one): card, owner, base_controller, flags (TOKEN, ONE_ONE).
  • arrive(slot, Arrival): flags TAPPED and ATTACKING, face, base_controller, counters of a common kind, attached_to. Must follow the move onto the battlefield (asserts derived_dirty).
  • link(slot, source): linked.
  • declare_attacker(slot): flags ATTACKING. choose_attacked(attacker, Option<planeswalker>): combat, flags ATTACKS_PLANESWALKER. forget_attacked(attacker): combat.
  • declare_blocker(blocker): flags BLOCKING. block(blocker, attacker): blocker's combat, attacker's flags BLOCKED.
  • mark_struck_first(slot): flags STRUCK_FIRST.
  • remove_from_combat(slot): its flags COMBAT and combat, and combat of whatever named it. end_combat(): flags COMBAT and combat of every permanent.
  • drop_stale_effects(): the effects queue, by each object's incarnation.
  • chance::adopt_unknown(s, run): card of the library slots no player knows.
  • legacy_mut(): set_object_flags(slot, flags, on) for the seven ObjectFlags::LEGACY flags; add_counters, remove_counters, set_counters for counters.other_kind and other.
  • board(): place, place_token, set_exiled_with, set_exiled_until, set_controller, set_sick, attach, set_damage, set_impending, set_entered_this_turn, set_loyalty_activated, swap_cards, add_emblem, set_known, set_counters, set_face, set_tapped, set_life, order_library, set_flip_winner, begin_at, begin_before_turn, reverse_deck_slots, renumber_deck_slots, corrupt.

Getters on Object (#[inline]), with the field each reads: card, incarnation, zone, owner, base_controller, flags, damage, counters, attached_to, linked, timestamp, face and combat read the field of the same name; counters_of(kind) reads counters; is_tapped, is_summoning_sick, is_token, is_one_one, entered_this_turn, is_attacking, is_blocking, is_blocked, attacks_planeswalker and struck_first each read one bit of flags. Counters::long_tail() reads other_kind and other. battlefield_index stays behind State::battlefield_position.

Gotchas: (1) State::set_tapped is the event-returning mutator and Board::set_tapped the silent one; a missed .board() fails to compile on the argument types. (2) s.board() borrows the state, so compute a slot first (let slot = find(&s); s.board().place(slot, ..)). (3) Readers in beryl_engine moved to getters only on lines this bead touched; the rest are by-w8gv.5.9's. (4) Counts: 404,482,019 before, 404,476,069 after.

A reviewer should check: the write order in combat.rs is unchanged (declare_attacker, then choose_attacked; block; remove_from_combat's loop); event.rs's arrive against the fields move_batch used to set; that reset_permanents_for_turn and mark_damage read derived exactly where the engine did; and the amended contract in lib.rs against what is built.

Verified: cargo test for the workspace (corpus replay, rollouts_never_change, corpus_never_changes), clippy, the fast check at push. Not run by me: slow CI (oracle, counts, mobile), running on staging now.
