Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Verbs', 'Determinism' (segments) and the fly entry of data/ui-conventions.json, whose shows text describes every flight; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/verbs/fly.rs, src/pose.rs (Shape, Segment), src/motion.rs (Segment::pose_at), src/verbs/mod.rs (slide), src/play/step.rs. Web: ui/src/stage/tracks.ts (glide and Flown: the one function every flight goes through; carried: the cards that stay in a hand move to their new places in the time the step's room gives; makingRoom, NEAR), ui/src/stage/bows.ts (Bow, BOWS, bowOf, bowed: how a flight leaves the straight line, by what it is: a spell thrown onto the stack bows toward its controller's edge), ui/src/stage/lift.ts (LIFT, lifts, liftAt, lifted: a permanent leaving the stack is thrown up the screen, crosses at that height and drops onto its place, shrinking as it comes down), ui/src/stage/enter.ts (entering: which flight a step gets). Cases of fly: land played, opponent land played, permanent resolves, discard, own spell cast, opponent spell cast (draws are by-q44s.3.17's).

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build:

  • Shape::Bowed { bow }: the path leaves the straight line sideways by bow millimetres at its middle, to the left of travel seen from above, as bowed does. fly picks the bow from the act as bowOf does; copy BOWS into space.json under verbs.fly.bows, one key for each entry.
  • Shape::Thrown: the flight of the case permanent resolves, as lifted has it: the card rises at once to verbs.fly.thrown_height, is half way across after a quarter of its time, and drops onto its place, its scale going from thrown_scale to 1. Copy LIFT's values.
  • Lands: the viewer's glides straight to its place (case land played); the opponent's drops out of their hand into the open table on the side away from where it is going, tilted, and swings back along the table to its place (case opponent land played), as entering and glide have it.
  • A discard and the two casts use Shape::Bowed with the arc verbs.fly.arc they have now.
  • The cards a step makes room among already move as the step's timing says: by-q44s.2.3 made the session send StepTiming::room (Room::For { ms }, 170 from fly's regroup in data/ui-conventions.json, for a step that takes a card into or out of a hand), and compile.rs step slides a hand's cards over that time from when the act is applied. Add no regroup value to space.json.
  • Check times: driver::check's times adds, for a Thrown segment, the time of its greatest height.

Do not build: a drawn card held up or swept past, or a card turning over in flight (by-q44s.3.17); the stack's and the hands' places (by-q44s.3.25).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/verbs_fly.rs, on views and scripts built by hand with testing::ViewBuilder, ScriptBuilder and testing::timing: for each case of the verb in data/ui-conventions.json (the six above), a script with that case's duration and delay plays to its end with every object at its placement (agent::steps::rest_equals_placement finds nothing), no_jumps finds nothing, and step_times finds nothing. A cast's middle pose is bow to the side of the straight line, toward the caster's edge for each seat; a resolving permanent is at its greatest height before a quarter of its time and is half way across at a quarter; the opponent's land ends at its placement having first moved away from it; the hand's other cards are at rest 170 ms after a card leaves it, with the step's timing's room set to Room::For { ms: 170.0 } as the session sends it; a resize in the middle of a thrown flight leaves the pose at that instant unchanged.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d film verbs-zones --from step:<a cast> --frames 12; just b3d check verbs-zones; just b3d lane.

Deviations from the plan, and why:

  • Shape::Bowed carries a Bow { offset: Vec3, sharpness, lean, axis } rather than a scalar bow in mm. The web's bowed() also swings the opponent's land back across the table (BOWS.played.back) and leans the card, which a scalar to the left of travel cannot say.
  • Bows and the thrown rise are worked out in the camera's screen plane (new Camera::axes: right, up, toward the camera), not the table's plane. Both hands sit near the camera (the opponent's hand is at y -495, z 1068), so a bow in the table's xy plane is not sideways on screen. The web's bowed() and lifted() work on the screen, so the screen plane matches them. 'Thrown up the screen' is along the camera's up.
  • verbs.fly.thrown is an object (height, risen, held, scale, shrunk, launch) copied from LIFT, instead of thrown_height and thrown_scale keys. height is a share of the spell's width, as in the web.
  • bowOf gives a discard no bow, so a discard stays a straight line with verbs.fly.arc; only the two casts are Bowed with the arc. The viewer's land and the opponent's land have no arc rise (the web's are straight and bowed only).
  • The opponent's land still turns over in flight. Snapping it face up as it leaves the hand would be a jump, and turning over in flight is by-q44s.3.17's.
  • Found and fixed: the session sends Room::For { ms: 170 } for a 165 ms discard, and the hand's close-up then outlasted the beat, so rest-equals-placement failed at the script's end. The web holds a beat open until its close-ups end (closedBy in ui/src/stage/tracks.ts). Motion now records the latest end of the slides it makes (take_closes), and lay_out extends each beat to it.
  • The tests are in tests/all/verbs_fly.rs, not tests/verbs_fly.rs: rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md keeps every integration test in the one tests/all binary.
  • The old play test held the viewer's land to the 55 mm rise. It now checks that the land stays on the straight line.

Handoff (d6328f1ee):

  • pose.rs: Shape::Bowed(Bow { offset, sharpness, lean, axis }) and Shape::Thrown { rise, aloft }. motion.rs: Segment::along gives the travelled shapes' eased progress, offset and lean; Segment::travelled builds the pose; Segment::peak gives a thrown card's first moment at full height, which driver::check's check times add. Segment::through inverts every travelled shape. For Thrown it solves the start scale before verbs.fly.thrown.shrunk, and after that it solves aloft.
  • verbs/fly.rs picks the path from the act, as the web's bowOf and lifts do. Hand to stack: Bowed, with the cast bow for the viewer and the theirs bow for the opponent, plus the arc. Stack to battlefield: Thrown. Hand to battlefield: straight with no arc for the viewer, the played bow with no arc for the opponent. Anything else: straight with the arc. Bows and the rise use Camera::axes (right, up, toward the camera), because both hands sit near the camera, not on the table.
  • data/beryl3d/space.json verbs.fly: bows.{cast,theirs,played,lifts,drawn,taken}.{share,peak,lean,back} and thrown.{height,risen,held,scale,shrunk,launch}, copied from the web's BOWS and LIFT. by-q44s.3.17 can use drawn and taken.
