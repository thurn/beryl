Warden review of by-b0q.1.6 (1179861). Verified on staging: just test-rust green; the differential test (tests/replay.rs) fuzzed at 400 seeds x 4 profiles = 1600 games, no divergence or invariant failure; acceptance tests present for never-offered unpayable/untargetable spells and plan decisions.

Found and fixed (7dff45d): the solver took or offered plans that tap a source in vain. {3}{W} from 3 Plains + Mana Crypt: Greedy tapped all four (3 Plains + Crypt, floating {C}) instead of Crypt + 2 Plains; KeepMostPayable offered that wasteful plan as a PaymentPlan decision, a decision with no real trade-off (violates D7 / acceptance). Fix: mana::wasteful rejects a plan if removing one tap of any class, with the same floating spend, still pays exactly. Sound for this cube: it has no {C} symbol in any cost, so floating {C} is never worth more than the spared source.

Tests added: a_payment_never_taps_a_source_it_could_leave_untapped (fails without the fix, both profiles); the_solver_pays_exactly_the_costs_brute_force_can_pay, a 300-position randomized check of payable() against brute force, plans non-empty iff payable, distinct remainders, and each plan paying exactly from the tapped sources and pool. Mutation-checked: breaking the Hall check or the floating remainder makes it fail.

Left alone deliberately: (1) plans() returns Full (stall) past 64 distinct floating remainders, reachable only with large mixed floating pools; not in the M1 deck. (2) Sources assume multi-mana simple sources make one kind (debug-asserted); true for M1, but Relic of Sauron and Gilded-style rocks in the V1 cube will need it. (3) Plans are recomputed in begin_payment, decision() and perform(); a perf concern, not correctness. (4) Floating-mana dominance beyond tap minimality (e.g. spending floating {W} plus Crypt vs Crypt alone) is still offered; rare. Rest of cast.rs/resolve.rs/effect.rs reviewed: CR ordering (X before targets, 601.2h non-mana costs first), fizzle/608.2b partial targets, 603.3d trigger targets, 603.4 recheck, 608.2g cast offers, Teferi/Wandering Emperor timing, loyalty once-per-turn, permissions cleanup: no issues found.
