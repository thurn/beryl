Description

Why

Keywords (beryl_card/src/characteristics.rs) has first strike, flash, flying, reach and vigilance. Partner cards of this epic need four more: trample (Gemrazer), lifelink (Griselbrand), indestructible (the Marit Lage token) and landwalk (Dandân, Lord of Atlantis). Other keywords wait for the cards that need them.

What to build

  • Trample (CR 702.19): in combat.rs's damage division, lethal damage to every blocker before any goes to the player or planeswalker. preferred_damage and the distinct outcomes offered change with it.
  • Lifelink (CR 702.15): in damage::deal, the source's controller gains that much life, as part of the damage event and not as a trigger.
  • Indestructible (CR 702.12): actions::destroy and the lethal-damage state-based action do nothing to it.
  • Landwalk (CR 702.14): a keyword with a parameter, the land type. Derived gains the set of basic land types a creature has landwalk for; a creature with it can't be blocked while the defending player controls a land of that type. Hold the land type in the keyword as a plain Subtype for now: group 4 makes such words a private type.
  • Rendering (render/words.rs), and data/census-coverage.json for the keyword kinds now covered.

Each keyword gets a hand-written scenario in beryl_cards' library tree with a real card that has it and nothing else new: pick vanilla-with-keyword creatures, which need no file (rust/AGENTS.md, "complete from the card store").

Files

beryl_card/src/{characteristics.rs, render/}, beryl_kernel/src/object.rs (Derived), beryl_rules/src/{combat.rs, damage.rs, actions/destroy.rs, sba.rs, layers.rs, tests/combat.rs}, rust/crates/beryl_card_build (the keywords a store card may have).

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate; R2 in the plan). The count is exact, so report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust). A change to what the WU mirror's rollouts do is a bug in this epic.
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (root AGENTS.md); just citations.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill: just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), every instruction carried out by its own scenarios, and just specs <card> passing the generic UI checks from both seats. A disagreement with an oracle is settled by the rules and recorded in data/oracle-divergences.json.
  • Read the card's rulings before writing its scenarios (just card brief <name> prints Scryfall's). Each statement in a ruling that a scenario can show is a scenario of the card. Where one cannot be shown yet, the close reason names the ruling and why.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry.
  • If the design in the plan is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.

Decision: landwalk is five Keywords bits (PLAINSWALK..FORESTWALK, Keywords::LANDWALK maps each to its Subtype), not a Subtype on Face plus a byte in Derived. Derived<Small> is 40 bytes with no padding, so one byte made it 48: State<Small> 20,104 -> 21,128 bytes, past the 20,480 limit (the bead's +128 estimate missed the padding). Bits cost nothing, store cards parse 'Islandwalk' with no extra code, and Lord of Atlantis can grant it through Modification::AddKeywords. Plan amended in docs/rules-coverage.md (group 1 keywords bullet, group 4 Words bullet). Trample offers no division with one blocker and only the most damage to the player otherwise (damage past lethal on a blocker changes nothing the pool reads).
