Notes

Cause: the shared rust/target cache (warm.py) evicted the third-party units every build reuses. APFS updates access time only while it is older than mtime, so LRU-by-atime saw long-reused crates as least recently used. A fresh worktree then rebuilt 261 missing units and their dependents: clippy took 48 s with no change (measured), and 64-83 s in fast checks of commits touching no Rust. Fix d95d26fd: after a clone, set each fingerprint file's atime 1 s behind its mtime, so cargo's read records real use (verified: 771 of 1730 units marked after a full clippy). Considered and rejected: limiting fast clippy to touched packages (saves 5 s on a warm tree, misses dependents' lints). Not done: rust tests step (~99 s) still dominates a whole-workspace change; next overrun refiles with fresh data. Cache needs a few saves to refill the evicted units.
