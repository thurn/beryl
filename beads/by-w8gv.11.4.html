Decisions and deviations (executor)

  1. Seven kinds went, as design 3 lists, not three: PutOnBottom, Discard, LegendRule, PayLife, Crew, Waterbend, ExileCost. DecisionKind has 18 members and Opt its 23 (grep -rnE 'DecisionKind::(Crew|ExileCost|Waterbend|PayLife|PutOnBottom|Discard|LegendRule)' rust/crates: 0 lines). They are asked as ChooseObject (crew, exile cost, put-on-bottom, discard, cleanup, legend rule), YesNo (pay life) and ManaPayment (waterbend).
  2. Saves, a deviation from 'a pure rewrite of the kind name': v9_to_v10 renames the kind AND keeps the old name as kindBeforeV10 on the answer (save::Described::before_v10, type save::KindBeforeV10). Two replay paths for old files read the recorded kind and would have gone wrong on the general kind: Game::replay_log's stop at a pre-version-7 cast whose remaining entries are all exile-cost picks, and replay_implicit_waterbend's test that the next recorded answer is itself a waterbend answer (after the rename a recorded waterbend answer and a manual mana payment are both manaPayment, and both can be an Act::Mana). Replay still matches an answer by kind alone (Chosen::find does not read the marker). The reader also accepts the seven old names where a file has them (save/kind.rs KindFile), because v1_to_v2, v2_to_v3 and v6_to_v7 deserialize a ReplayFile mid-chain, before v9_to_v10 runs; the steps before it are untouched.
  3. The scenario trace gained the prompt's purpose: Position::purpose and Forced::purpose (beryl_scenario), serialized as the purpose's name. Scenario actions still name only the kind. The exporters read the purpose from the trace: xmage.py FORCED_COSTS (crew, waterbend, exile-cost) and the waterbend answers; forge.py passes it to the harness as each answer step's 'purpose', and the harness takes a waterbend's picks by it (isAnswerFor, takeFollowingFor). A trace without purposes still exports (the test fixtures are such), as for the other fields added later. A new card with one of these costs needs no entry.
  4. The runner's 'unscripted decision' message names the purpose after the kind: 'player 0 ChooseObject (cleanup) in turn ...'.
  5. The wire: three interim AskView variants as design 3 says (putOnBottom, legendRule, discard), built in protocol/ask.rs. The old UI tells all seven prompts apart by ask.kind; ui/src/game/decisions.ts Intent gained an optional ask so a planned pick still answers only its own prompt (a waterbend's optional Done would otherwise match a manual mana payment). To delete with AskView in by-w8gv.11.6.
  6. Places that tested 'PaymentPlan or ManaPayment' and must not now take a waterbend test the purpose instead: Game::unpaid_cast and preview.rs's payment loop use purpose == Mana (every payment decision but a waterbend carries it: cast.rs, face_down.rs, effect.rs); script/infer.rs making() and ui ManaFlow.tsx exclude the waterbend ask.
  7. Engine: the four rules picks are answered in perform_machine by the pending state (EnterPayingLife, Mulligans with kind ChooseObject, Discard, LegendRule); engine.rs fell to 830 lines.
  8. ui/fixtures.json: nine fixtures stop at 'first chooseObject|yesNo|manaPayment decision' with a where 'ask <kind>' in place of a removed kind.

Not changed, on purpose: v6_to_v7's own test of the wire kind after its replay (PaymentPlan or ManaPayment) now also matches a waterbend decision. It cannot arise (the step only replays a log that ends in exile-cost picks of one cast, and no card has both costs), and the policy is to leave earlier steps alone.
