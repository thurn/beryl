Build:

  • A life total for each player: a float (floats::LIFE) at the player's anchor (Layout::players[side].life), Role::Player, named "You" or "Opponent", whose text is the life the table shows. It changes as the count step of Tally::Life begins, not when the view arrives. This is the plain number milestone 2's arrows, streaks and strikes point at; milestone 3 (by-q44s.5.1, item 6) gives it its orb and avatar.
  • The mana pool, floats::POOL: the unspent mana as text ("2 U 1 G"), shown only while there is some, changed by Tally::Pool.
  • Tally::Counters, NamedCounters, Stats and Damage replace the object's record as the step begins; the badges of by-q44s.3.13 show it. Tally::PlayerCounters changes no drawing here.
  • loss and gain (the cases life loss and life gain): while the run lasts, the life total's text is tinted red or green (FloatLook::tint), and floats::LIFE_CHANGE shows the amount with its sign, sinking 24 logical points for a loss and rising 24 for a gain while its opacity falls from 1 to 0, linearly over the run. Copy the two colors from effects.css into src/tokens.rs.
  • The step holds its beat for its timing's duration, as now.

Do not build: the pulse ring on a card (by-q44s.3.23).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/verbs_count.rs: a script whose second beat takes the viewer's life from 20 to 17 shows "20" until that step begins and "17" from then; during the step the tree holds a text "-3" below the total, lower at each later time, and none after the run; a gain shows "+2" rising; the pool's float is absent with no mana; a frame during a loss allocates nothing; Frame::work.layout_nodes is 0 in every frame of the run after the one that begins it.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d check verbs-zones; just b3d lane.

Decisions and deviations: (1) A life total float is named for the player and the number ("You 20"), not just "You": the tree prints role and name only, so this is how just b3d tree --role player shows the life, as the acceptance asks. (2) The bead allowed layout in the frame that begins a step, but the gate's recorded-game allocation tests forbid any frame allocating, so every value the coming scripts set is laid out in Client::handle (Layer::prepare / show_prepared); no frame of a loss lays out or shapes anything, which the test now holds. (3) Effect runs took their subject from mover_of, which drops a life count's loss/gain; compile.rs now mirrors the web's litBy (life -> player, card tallies -> the card, pool -> nothing). (4) Tally::Counters/NamedCounters/Stats/Damage stay instant changes here: no per-step record replacement exists yet, and by-q44s.3.13 (badges, Fact::Object) is building it; Act::object() already covers both, so its mechanism should cover count tallies too. (5) LOSS and GAIN colors are hand-written in tokens.rs's color module (the build script only reads flat colors that name a token, and effects.css has none). (6) The allocation test is in tests/frame_allocation.rs (a counting allocator needs its own binary), and the other tests are in tests/all/verbs_count.rs. (7) Fixed a float-layout bug from by-q44s.3.3: a float-only layout pass evicted the screen's shaped texts, so later prompt changes re-shaped all their text.
