Description

Cards

  • Chains of Mephistopheles: "If a player would draw a card except the first one they draw in their draw step each turn, that player discards a card instead. If the player discards a card this way, they draw a card. If the player doesn't discard a card this way, they mill a card."
  • Notion Thief: flash; "If an opponent would draw a card except the first one they draw in each of their draw steps, instead that player skips that draw and you draw a card."

What they need

  • The draw proposal is one card. drawing::draw splits a draw of several into single draws while anything replaces draws.
  • "Except the first one they draw in their draw step" reads the tracker of by-bajq.1.4.
  • Chains' rewrite is an Effect done instead, with a discard the player chooses, a condition on whether they discarded, and a draw inside it. Chains does not apply again to that draw: it has had its one chance at the event and at what replaces it (CR 614.5). Another replacement that has not applied (Notion Thief, dredge) still can.
  • Notion Thief's rewrite moves the draw to another player; Chains then applies to that player's draw.
  • With both in play the affected player chooses the order (by-bajq.3.2), and the two orders differ.

Scenarios

A draw-seven under Chains with cards in hand and with none (Timetwister and Time Spiral are implemented); Chains with dredge; Chains with Notion Thief in each order; the first draw of the draw step untouched and the second replaced. These are the cards on which XMage and Forge are most likely to disagree with each other: the rules decide, and each divergence is recorded with its citation.

Files

The two card files, beryl_rules/src/{drawing.rs, replace.rs}, beryl_rules/src/tests/library.rs.

  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust). A change to what the WU mirror's rollouts do is a bug in this epic.
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (root AGENTS.md); just citations.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill: just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), every instruction carried out by its own scenarios, and just specs <card> passing the generic UI checks from both seats. A disagreement with an oracle is settled by the rules and recorded in data/oracle-divergences.json.
  • Read the card's rulings before writing its scenarios (just card brief <name> prints Scryfall's). Each statement in a ruling that a scenario can show is a scenario of the card. Where one cannot be shown yet, the close reason names the ruling and why.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry.
  • If the design in the plan is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.

Chains' and Notion Thief's scenarios must cover: drawing::draw counts each card actually drawn, so a replaced draw and a draw from an empty library are not counted, and the first draw of the draw step is the one Chains lets through. Note that static_ability.rs refuses DrewInDrawStep, so a replacement gated on it needs that path extended.
