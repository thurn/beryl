Cause: not a code regression. The step failed with ENOSPC. Every timeline-parity run leaked a ~400 MB b3d-parity-* export dir in $TMPDIR (734 dirs, 290 GB) plus a 12 MB timeline-*.jsonl in $BERYL_CACHE/b3d/out (700 files) and a web-timelines-<hash>.jsonl per web hash (79, 706 MB). Fix in tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/timeline.py: run_parity rmtree's the export dir in a finally, native_lines unlinks the timeline file after reading, web_lines prunes cache files older than 2 days. Deleted the existing leaked dirs/files older than 2 h by hand to free the disk. Other b3d/out files (png/film outputs, ~2 GB) are left alone: they come from manual png/film commands.
