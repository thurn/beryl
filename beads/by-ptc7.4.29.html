Description

State::clear_damage in rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/mutate.rs iterates only the phased-in battlefield. CR 514.2 explicitly removes marked damage from phased-out permanents too. A damaged permanent can phase back in still carrying damage from a prior turn. The cleanup call is in rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/turn.rs, and the existing phasing tests are in rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/tests/phasing.rs.

Acceptance criteria

A regression phases out a damaged permanent, runs normal cleanup, and phases it in without the old damage. Preserve the cleanup citation and add a docs/bugs.md entry naming the regression guard. The count gate passes.
