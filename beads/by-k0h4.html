Notes

Failure at 1b991d3d (a docs-only commit on a tree that passed at 308ed32b): ui-test 'opponent scry plays consecutive results and lifts the new top cards', caption.count() 1 !== 0 after a fixed clock.runFor(850). The caption's 1,800 ms timer is set in a React effect (Board.tsx, expiredScry), which flushes in real time, so on a loaded machine it can register after the paused clock was advanced. Not reproduced locally; read from the code. Repair 3f0c7e4e: untilGone() steps the clock until the caption is gone and fails past 1,800 ms. Passed 5 filtered runs, fails when the limit is cut to 600 ms, just ci passed in the worktree, slow CI passed on staging at 3f0c7e4e. No product change. The earlier failure this bead was filed for (4aaa4de9: ETB trigger first frame, thaw player arrow) is tracked in by-6sbc.
