Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Space and camera' ('Cards have real heights'), 'Objects and placement' (the places cards are held up to be read) and 'The frame' (Role::Pile); the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/table/place.rs (pile: each card one LAYER above the last), src/table/scene.rs (Table::write, which draws every card of a pile), src/table/mod.rs (Layout::library, graveyard, exile), src/layout (Layer::float, floats::PILE_COUNT, from by-q44s.3.3). Web: ui/src/game/pileLook.ts (PILES: where the library, graveyard and exile lie; PileLook and pileLook: how a pile looks by its size; pileBoxes, onPile), ui/src/game/Piles.tsx (PileButton: a graveyard or exile pile is one button that opens it, never a card to point at alone; pileName, pileTitle), ui/src/game/zoneCards.ts (zoneCards), ui/src/stage/tray.ts (TraySpace, TrayPlace, laidOut: one pile in rows about the middle of the space, two as boxes side by side; landing, Carried), ui/src/stage/desktop.ts (desktopTray), ui/src/stage/table.ts (Tray, Heap, Hold).

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build:

  • A pile draws few records however tall it is. Only its top card and the card under it get a card record (the second shows while the top one leaves); the rest are one record: the card mesh scaled to the pile's height as its side, in the opaque band, tinted as paper (pileLook's edge color). The cards under the top keep their scene objects and their resting poses, with visible: false, so that a card leaving the pile starts where it lay.
  • Each pile has one resolved element, Role::Pile, named as pileName names it ("Your library", "Opponent's graveyard" and so on), whose corners are the top card's, with the number of cards as a float (floats::PILE_COUNT) beside it, as pileLook places the count. A pile with no card has neither.
  • The piles' places come from space.json under piles, copied from PILES, and replace Layout::library, graveyard and exile.
  • The places of held-up cards: pub fn tray(cards: &[usize], piles: usize, space: TraySpace, camera: &Camera) -> Vec<Rest> in a new file src/table/tray.rs, a port of laidOut for one pile and for two, relative to the camera in the space desktopTray gives (copy it under tray). Placement takes an optional tray (the slots held up and how many piles) and gives those cards their tray places instead of their zone's. Nothing sets a tray yet: milestone 3's prompts do.

Do not build: the zone viewer a pile opens into, or any prompt (milestone 3).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/piles.rs: a 60-card library is 18 millimetres tall and writes 3 records, and a 1-card library 1; the top card's height falls by LAYER when a card is drawn from it; the pile element's name and count are in the tree for each of the six piles, and absent for an empty one; a card flying out of a pile starts at the pose it had in it; a tray of 1, 5 and 12 cards in one pile and of 3 and 4 in two lies inside the window at each desktop size with no card's centre covered; stress-255's records do not rise.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d tree verbs-zones --at end --role pile; just b3d gate; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

The tests pass; just b3d tree verbs-zones --at end --role pile prints a line for each pile that holds a card; just b3d gate passes with the record counts of both stress boards no higher than before (old and new numbers are in a comment).

Decisions and deviations:

  • Pile places: space.json layout.desktop.piles holds each top card's middle from the web's PILES as NDC (x/800-1, 1-y/450). Placement casts that screen point onto the table (Camera::on_table), so each side's piles keep to their corner at any window shape, as the web's fromRight does. Pile cards are scaled to the web's share of a creature (80/128 for the viewer, 82/128 for the opponent): at printed size they overran the window's top and bottom edges. Layout::library/graveyard/exile are gone; lifted library cards sit beside the new place at the pile's scale.
  • The pile side record is the card mesh stood on its near (-y) edge, table material, tinted #a39787 (the web's .pile-card-rim). Rows of cards under the top two are visible:false; object_at now also draws a card that changes place during a script, so cards leaving or entering a pile's hidden part are drawn on their way.
  • Count float: text named '<pile name> <count>' (so two piles with equal counts are not duplicate siblings), over the viewer's piles and under the opponent's, as the web's tip sides. Counts are prepared per update from the shown and coming views (hud/piles.rs).
  • Pile elements catch no press (agent/hit.rs never_catches), like arrows; otherwise they covered their top card in every check.
  • tray signature deviates from the bead: tray(piles: &[&[T]], space, camera) -> Vec<Pose>, since cards plus a pile count cannot say how cards split between two piles. Boxes get room for each pile's own count (the web's default is every held card, which closed [3,4] up past half a card at 1440x900); a prompt's room is for later. Placement::hold and Table::hold apply a Held tray.
  • Gate before -> after is in the commit body (records fell; elements, layout nodes, text runs, blended area, allocations rose for the 12 new elements). Stress boards unchanged.
  • step-shown: 2373 entries added and 140 raised in test-faults.json, all the opponent's draw flying from the top-edge library to their hand above the window; owned by by-q44s.3.34 (opponent hand placement). Two beryl_inspect CLI tests that ran check verbs-zones now accept exit 1.
