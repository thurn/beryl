Combat and crew on staging (0d9eb55, c3749fd)

Description

Part of Milestone 1 (epic by-b0q): a rules-exact engine, game UI and AI opponents for a mirror match of the 40-card WU Control main deck in docs/sample-decks/2026-10-17-PrinceJools-wu-control.txt (29 distinct cards). Read the epic's description (plan, card table) and design field (decisions) first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md).

Architecture section 11, CR 506 to 511. Test with test-only CardDefs.

  • Declare attackers one pick at a time, with the defender (a player or a planeswalker, CR 508.1b) offered only when it is a real choice; summoning sickness (CR 302.6); vigilance (CR 702.20).
  • Creatures put onto the battlefield attacking (CR 508.4): never declared, no attack triggers.
  • Declare blockers one pick at a time with evasion pruning (flying and reach, CR 702.9, 702.17).
  • Damage: division among blockers offered only by distinct outcomes; first strike adds a damage step only when present (CR 510.4, 702.7); damage to planeswalkers removes loyalty (CR 120.3c); combat damage events for triggers.
  • Crew (CR 702.122) tapping creatures one pick at a time; a crewed Vehicle is an artifact creature until end of turn.
  • Smart stops after attackers and after blockers are declared.

Acceptance criteria

  • Engine tests cover each rule listed, including a planeswalker attacked and losing loyalty and a first-strike step that appears only when needed.
