Description

Found by the warden by-q44s.3.36, with no comment explaining the differences:

  1. rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/lifted.rs (held_up, the port of handlingPlacements in ui/src/game/layout.ts) has no case for the opening-hand look. The web uses view.turn.number === 0 to lay the looked-at cards out narrower (72 against 104), centred at fromMiddle(465) instead of 800, with top 8.
  2. lifted.rs shows card backs when the owner is the opponent or the card is not face up. The web shows backs when handling.zone === 'pile', when the card's zone is the library, or when the owner is the opponent, so the viewer's own pile taken from a hand shows faces in Beryl 3D and backs on the web. (Check also the web's pile.width * 1.95 against pile_scale.)
  3. rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/light/lines.rs treats a player's anchor as a point (End with half zero), so an arrow to a life total ends at its middle. The web's Reach.round makes the edge an ellipse (hypot) for a life total (ui/src/stage/lines.ts), so the arrow stops at the orb's rim.

Tests: tests/all/verbs_point.rs, and a lift test beside the existing ones for the lift verb.
