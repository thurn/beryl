Slow CI's budget step reads 30 days of raw run log: 16 s, 6.8 GB

Description

Symptom

At the end of every slow CI run on this Mac, budget.py (check_budget) calls runlog.read_log(since, host=...) over check_value.DAYS (30 days). On 2026-11-16 that read 3.67 million records in 16 s wall and peaked at 6.8 GB of memory (/usr/bin/time -l, load ~16). The machine was already at 18 of 18.4 GB swap on 2026-11-14, so a 6.8 GB spike pushes other sessions into swap. by ci-value, by ci-report, bisect.py (DAYS = 30) and nightly_failures.py do the same full read.

Background

Commit 1a2cea7bb split the run log into hour files ($BERYL_CACHE/ci/runs/<YYYY-MM-DDTHH>.jsonl) with per-lane sums for each finished hour and day (ci/sums/, tools/src/beryl_tools/runstore.py). The fast check's per-lane readers (selection.Chooser, deadline.Records) now read runstore.lane_tallies: 0.04-0.31 s instead of 2.3-3.6 s. The readers above still need raw records because check_value.score groups records into runs (a lane's last check_value.RUNS = 10 runs) and bisect/nightly_failures match on commit.

The log grows about 60 MB an hour, mostly per-test pass records from the beryl3d-interface lane (scripts b3d and check: about 400,000 of 487,000 records between 04:00 and 08:00 UTC 2026-11-16), so 31 days of hour files is roughly 40 GB.

Work

  1. check_value.score / budget.py: read hour files newest first (runstore.read_lines in reverse order of hour) and stop once every lane has its last RUNS runs; take the 30-day catch and failure counts from runstore sums (extend the sums with cpu_seconds if the score needs it).
  2. bisect.py: its tally can use runstore.lane_tallies per lane; its misses needs only the commits in the bisected range, so read only the hours since the oldest of them.
  3. Cut volume at the source: a passing interface test (b3d:<recording>/<test>) is one record per test per run. Consider logging one record per recording, or dropping passes' fields that repeat per line (host, commit, tree, script, lane, load, chosen are ~60% of each line). Then lower runstore.RAW_DAYS to what the remaining raw readers need.

Done

check_budget at the end of slow CI takes under 2 s and under 300 MB, independent of how many days the log covers, with the same cuts as before on the same data (test in tools/tests/test_budget*.py). The hour files hold under 10 GB.

Files: tools/src/beryl_tools/runstore.py, runlog.py (read_log), budget.py, check_value.py (score, full_run, cuts), bisect.py (line ~565), nightly_failures.py, tools/tests/test_runstore.py.
