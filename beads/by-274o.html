Notes

The exile-stagger assertion measures positions after 150ms, but page.clock.install() continues wall-time unless paused. Under a busy browser, unpaused time can elapse between Resolve click, waiting for data-zone and bounding-box measurements. Neighboring motion tests pause the clock first and use button.evaluate(el => el.click()) to avoid Playwright actionability depending on live animation frames. The test advanced a running fake clock, with Playwright calls between its time checks. Under contention the live elapsed time used up the last card’s delay before the intermediate assertion. Pause the clock before dispatching Resolve, then explicitly advance it to measure the first departure, the last card waiting, and the last departure. The focused test passes with the production motion and thresholds unchanged.
