Description

Milestone 8 of Project 50k (run epic by-w8gv; plan docs/project-50k.md, "Authoring tools", decisions K9). Read the plan's sections named here and enact brief on this bead before starting. This milestone starts once M7b (by-w8gv.13) closes: read its beads' handoffs for the set crates, the generated registry and characteristics, and the card store as they landed.

Follows the derived card facts and per-deck overlay in the same lane: read that bead's handoff first (CardValues, Knowledge, the overlay in beryl_search/src/overlay/).

Today

estimate(face) in rust/crates/beryl_search/src/values.rs values a card from what its face shows: a creature by 4×(power+toughness) + 3×mana value + 5 for flying, a planeswalker by 15×loyalty, a land 10, anything else 5 + 4×mana value. It is what every card without an overlay entry gets, so it is what the AI knows of the 50,000-card pool.

What to build

  1. An estimate from the definition. Value what the card does, read from its effect data, in the same units as the overlay (a basic land about 10, a game-winning planeswalker about 90): keywords beyond flying, card advantage (cards drawn, tokens made), removal and counterspells by what they can hit, mana abilities, loyalty abilities by what they do, enter and dies triggers, costs that are cheaper or dearer than the mana value says (alternative costs, additional costs). Keep it a pure function of the definition, built once per game, out of the counted rollout loop. Default for the weights: fit them once to the overlay's values with beryl_cli evalfit (rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/evalfit.rs) or a short script, report the fit (rank correlation with the overlay over the 47 cards) on the bead, and write the weights as named constants with a comment that says where they came from. Never tune to the match below.
  2. Derived-only is a first-class agent. Knowledge::Estimate (derived flags, this estimate, no overlay) is selectable by family name in long.rs like tuned; the cheap agents (lib.rs registry) that take a knowledge tier can run it.
  3. One reported-only pairing. Play one match between the same agent family differing only in knowledge (derived-only against tuned), on the WU mirror (decks::WU_CONTROL), on Cygnus, the Windows PC that runs every AI match (docs/ai-strategy.md §4.1: the Mac plays no ladder games, and just tournament decides nothing). Default: the champion's family with only its knowledge changed, at 3M steps, just ladder <family with estimate in place of tuned> --vs <champion family> --work 3M --bar audit --cap 100 --positions D:\beryl-cache\ladder\positions, which plays all 100 games and prints the score with its 95% interval. Nothing else heavy runs beside it, and it is not a ladder rung: it writes no row in docs/ladder.md and changes no champion. Record the score with its 95% interval on this bead and on the milestone epic by-w8gv.15. It has no bar: never rerun it, never tune to it (run epic, "Defaults for anything measured on samples"). Expect derived-only to lose clearly: run-003 found hand-tuned values beat 17lands win rates by about 90 Elo (docs/ai-strategy.md §2.1). The estimate still matters, because it is what the AI knows of any card without an overlay entry, so it is what it will play the first Legacy deck with (docs/ai-strategy.md §8: greedy's card values depend on the deck).

Files

beryl_search/src/values.rs (keep it under 800 lines: move the estimate to its own module if it grows), long.rs, lib.rs, beryl_cli/src/evalfit.rs. Need not read: the expert's internals, mcts, the UI, the tools.

Tests: cargo test -p beryl_search, unit tests that the estimate orders a few hand-picked pairs as a player would (a 2/2 flyer above a 2/2, a counterspell above a vanilla 1/1, a land near 10) without asserting exact numbers; rollouts_never_change (the tuned tier must not move).
