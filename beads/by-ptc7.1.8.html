Description

Why

Five logged bugs were a value narrowed or widened wrongly between State<Small> and State<Large>. The scenario runner plays on Small only, and one test (beryl_search/tests/games.rs::a_widened_game_plays_on_as_the_small_one) compares the layouts on two seeds. Plan: Designs B4.

What to build

  • rust/crates/beryl_scenario/src/run.rs (and setup.rs): plays_alike_in_both_layouts. It plays the scenario on Small and on the widened state (State::<Small>::widen, beryl_kernel/src/layout.rs) in lockstep. At every decision, small.widen() must equal the large state and the two decisions must match after relayout. A scenario that cannot start in Small (Misfit) plays in Large alone.
  • beryl_cards/src/tests.rs: every_card_has_scenarios_and_they_pass calls it beside plays_alike_in_every_slot_order, for hand-written, rules-library and generated scenarios.
  • The fuzzers (beryl_search/tests/games.rs, tests/common/widening.rs) make the same comparison at every decision of a share of their games; keep the fuzz run's time.
  • A failure prints the first decision where the two differ and the field that differs.

This doubles the cost of the per-card test, which the sampling of by-ptc7.3.11 (another bead) later bounds. Measure it and report it. If it puts rust:tests over its budget before that lands, run the comparison on a sample fixed by the commit hash and say so.

A difference found is a bug: fix it with its docs/bugs.md entry, or file it as a child of this milestone.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Every card's scenarios and the generated scenarios pass the both-layout comparison, or each failure is fixed or filed. The close reason gives the test's CPU seconds before and after.
