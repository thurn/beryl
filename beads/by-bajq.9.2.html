Description

Card

Show and Tell: "Each player may put an artifact, creature, enchantment, or land card from their hand onto the battlefield."

The problem

Each player chooses in turn order and the cards are put onto the battlefield at once (CR 101.4). A card chosen from a hand may stay face down as it is chosen (CR 101.4a), so the second player chooses without knowing what the first chose. In Beryl a pick is an index into candidates that are slots, and slots are public, so the second player's view already shows only "a card in slot 17". The leak is in search: when the second player's agent determinizes the first player's hand, it may deal slot 17 a card the first player could never have picked.

What to build

Plan: Designs 8, "Hidden picks"; decision R12.

  1. The instruction. "Each player may put a card a filter accepts from their hand onto the battlefield" composes from what exists: ForEachPlayer of an optional Choose among the cards in that player's hand, then one instruction that puts Objects::Those onto the battlefield together (by-bajq.8.2's zone-change instruction). Choose gains "hidden": the pick is not revealed to the other players as it is made.
  2. The constraint. The answer record of a paused resolution notes, for a hidden pick, which instruction asked it. chance's determinization, for a viewer who does not know the card in a picked slot, deals that slot a card the instruction's filter accepts, and only then deals the rest. If the player picked nothing, there is no constraint.
  3. The test. The information-independence test (docs/engine-architecture.md §13) gains this position: the second player's agent decides the same from the true state and from a redetermined one.
  4. The view of the paused position shows the first player's pick face down, set apart from their hand.
  5. An Aura put onto the battlefield this way chooses what it enchants as it enters (by-bajq.8.2, CR 303.4f).

Scenarios

Both players put a creature (Griselbrand and another); one declines; the first player's pick is not in the second player's view; Animate Dead put onto the battlefield with a creature card in a graveyard; Containment Priest in play (a creature put this way is exiled).

Derek Thurn2026-11-15

Decisions (deviations from the bead's plan): (1) The plan said the record notes which instruction asked a hidden pick. The real leak was larger: a paused resolution records answers as indices into candidates, and which hand cards pass the filter is hidden, so a redealt state could give the first player's question a different candidate list (or none, or one answered silently) and misalign the whole record. So a hidden pick is one ChooseUpTo question (Done + candidates), asked of every player whatever their hand holds, never answered silently, and recorded as slot+1 (0 = none). Resolving gains hidden: u32 (record positions holding such slots) and asks_hidden: bool, both in existing padding: State<Small> stays 20,216, Resolving 424, and Resolving's Hash skips them when zero so pinned hashes hold. (2) Instead of recording an instruction id (no room), the filter comes from the stack top's one hidden pick (Effect::hidden_pick); Malformed::Chosen requires a hidden pick to be optional and among Objects::InZoneOf a hand or library. (3) Players with an empty hand are asked too (a forced Done): XMage asks every player, and hand size is public anyway. (4) Item 4 (pick shown face down, set apart) is filed as by-bajq.9.8: it needs a new stage pose on desktop and phone. (5) XMage exporter: new card fact 'hidden' and Cards.hidden_pick; the question is exported as setChoice yes/no + addTarget, forced answers included.
