Description

A throwaway prototype, built in one session, that a person can run and watch. It comes before milestone 1 so that the feel of the 3D table and the first real performance numbers are known before the foundations are built. Doc: 'The frame', 'The table', 'Rendering' (The Baseline tier), 'Threads', 'Measurements'.

Exit checks:

  • just proto3d opens a window on the Mac in which a real game plays itself on a 3D table with real card images, and the pointer lifts cards in the hand.
  • just proto3d --stats <board> prints, with no window, what a frame costs on a real game and on boards of 255 and 1,024 cards.
  • A comment on the epic compares each number with the doc's budgets and says which of the doc's claims the prototype confirmed, which it refuted and which it could not test.
  • Milestone 1's beads are corrected where the prototype showed them wrong.
