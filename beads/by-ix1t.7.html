Close reason

Added fitted rollout knowledge. Held-out agreement rose from 2.78% to 12.15%, and fitted greedy won 65.1% of 20,000 paired games. Staged as e90312a3.

Description

Part of the AI ladder epic by-ix1t. Read docs/ai-plan.md first: §2.4, §6.7 and §9.

The two largest strength gains on record are both policy quality: hand-tuned card values beat 17lands win rates by about 90 Elo, and the expert as the root's default scored +54 Elo [+17, +92] over 400 games. Both were tuned by hand. Budget and hidden information add nothing measurable at 30M. This bead fits the numbers the policy already has to what the search confirms, and adds no new model.

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/values.rs and overlay/: per-card values, and the per-deck overlay that tunes them. Knowledge in long.rs (Estimate, Tuned) picks the table, and is part of a family's name.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/rollout/policy.rs: the constants of the policy's rules.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/match_log/mod.rs: DecisionRecord (the choice, each candidate's visits and mean) and AnchorRecord (the default, the search's pick, the lead, its error). Every ladder match on Cygnus has these under D:\beryl-cache\ladder\<date>-<challenger>\.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/evalfit.rs: eval-fit replays seeded games to positions and fits weights by logistic regression. Copy how it replays.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/anchor_replay.rs: walks a match directory's decisions.

Steps

  1. Labels. A decision where the paired test overruled the policy says the policy ranked the confirmed move below its own answer. Collect these from every match played since paired reached the champion. Count them and write the count in the notes. If paired was dropped, or there are fewer than 2,000, add a labelling run on Cygnus: at decisions sampled from match games, paired rollouts of the policy's top three candidates against its answer, keeping those where a candidate wins by 0.02 and three standard errors. Size it to finish in a night.
  2. The fit. just fit (beryl_cli fit) replays each labelled game to its decision, scores every candidate with the policy, and searches the numbers one at a time for the setting under which the confirmed move most often ranks first. Hold out a fifth of the games. Penalise distance from the hand-set values, so a number with little evidence stays where it is.
  3. The output is a third knowledge, fitted, beside estimate and tuned: a table checked in next to the tuned one. The challenger is the champion's family with fitted in place of tuned.
  4. A check. Play greedy with fitted values against greedy with tuned values, 20,000 games with seats swapped (just tournament). Write the score in the notes. Under 50%, look for the numbers that moved most and say what they did before going on.
  5. Tests: the fit is deterministic from a seed; on a synthetic set where one card's value is wrong by construction, the fit moves it the right way; the fitted family name round-trips; the champion's moves do not change.

Report in the notes: the labels used, the held-out agreement before and after, the ten numbers that moved most, and the check's score.

A caution

A policy that agrees with the search more does not have to make better rollouts. Only the match of the measure bead says. If held-out agreement does not improve, close this bead with that result and say so: it is a finding for docs/ai-plan.md §9, which depends on it.

Acceptance criteria

Notes

Fit executed on Cygnus with seed 0 from five completed Nov 15–16 ladder match directories: +paired+first at 3M (3,017 labels), +paired reference audit at 10M (1,330), the promotion match at 3M (972), +paired+prior at 3M (348), and +waste+paired at 1M (610). The per-game logs preserve the same anchor and choice fields as decisions.jsonl. All 6,277 accepted paired overrides replayed. No extra labelling run was needed. I kept repeat positions and same-seed shuffles, including swapped seats and repeats across match directories, in one split.

Training 105/5,092 (2.06%) to 661/5,092 (12.98%). Held out 33/1,185 (2.78%) to 144/1,185 (12.15%). A second run with the same seed produced byte-identical Rust output (SHA-256 825819d2b967b92feb6ef39d04b8e745459a21f69f5614b415d6cd8c40a15fd7).

Ten largest changes: Damn 55 to 35, Jace, Vryn's Prodigy 50 to 30, Winds of Abandon 50 to 30, Samurai Token 16 to 34, Jace, Wielder of Mysteries 65 to 82, Rogue Token 16 to 33, Spirit Token 13 to 30, Mana Crypt 0 to 16, Oust 50 to 66, and Subtlety 45 to 29. Removal threshold 25 to 14 and counter threshold 40 to 31. The bonus per positive creature power point rose from 3 to 6 and the bonus per loyalty counter from 4 to 6. The land cap stayed at 15.

To reproduce use just fit with the above match directories, --seed 0 and --out pointing at rust/crates/beryl_search/src/overlay/fitted.rs. The policy-only check is just tournament greedy+fitted greedy --games 20000 --paired --seed 0. For paired blunder tables with the champion's rules, use greedy+hold+waste+combat+fitted and greedy+hold+waste+combat. Policy-only check on Cygnus: just tournament greedy+fitted greedy --games 20000 --paired --seed 0. Fitted won 13,023 games, tuned won 6,976, and one game stalled at the turn limit. Fitted won 65.1% of all games (Wilson 95% interval 64.5% to 65.8%). Of 10,000 swapped-seat seed pairs, fitted swept 3,998, 5,028 split, and tuned swept 974. The two policies used the plain greedy rules for this check. The one-seed policy blunder suite with hold, waste and combat went from 19/20 tuned to 20/20 fitted. The fitted policy sent both attackers at Jace, Wielder of Mysteries on spare-jace-wielder. This is one seed of the policy alone, ahead of the measure bead's search gate.

The completed just test-rust run passed 1,424 tests, the allocation checks and all doctests. On Cygnus, I reran it with the C: Cargo cache, a 16 MiB worker stack, and debug symbols and incremental compilation disabled after stopping the first build when D: fell below 1 GB free.
