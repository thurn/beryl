Close reason

Tucked cards close up so a permanent's group fits its phone row; Worldgorger Dragon fault gone

Description

Symptom: just b3d test --phone-only reports an on-screen fault in b3d:worldgorger-dragon/exiles-your-permanents-until-it-leaves (and -0)/you/375x647: "Worldgorger Dragon #10 (349,308 59x78) is not inside the safe area (0,44 375x569)". At rest the Dragon lies at x=366, past the right edge. Worldgorger Dragon exiles six Mountains until it leaves, and the phone tucks them under it, each peeking out a step further to the left (UI Conventions exception 6). The peek step is peek_step in table/rows.rs, max(beneath_step.x * CARD_WIDTH, rules.strip): on the phone the strip makes each step nearly a whole card width (52 points), so one group is wider than the row (group_width), and a row cannot scroll a single group into view. Make a group whose peeks exceed the row's room fit (closer steps beyond what fits, or scroll the peeks), keeping each tucked card's pressable strip where the reachable rule needs one. Read docs/beryl-3d.md 'How a row scrolls' and 'Interface checks', rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md, and table/rows.rs place_group and Beneath::peek first.

Acceptance criteria

No on-screen fault in b3d:worldgorger-dragon in just b3d test --phone-only and with --every-tick, and no new reachable fault there; desktop just b3d test, just b3d timeline-parity --fixtures and just b3d gate pass.
