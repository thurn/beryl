Close reason

card diverge command, divergences check, oracle-run stale check and Spellseeker landed

Description

What was seen

In the authoring pilot (by-w8gv.15), Spellseeker passed Beryl, Forge (8 of 8 scenarios), the Oracle text diff and the citation check, and was still not committed. XMage's Spellseeker class requires the search to find a card. CR 701.23b says a player searching a hidden zone for a card with a stated quality "isn't required to find some or all of those cards". data/oracle-divergences.json already holds the same XMage fault for Trinket Mage.

Why

The card-writing skill forbids a card writer from adding an entry to data/oracle-divergences.json ("Never weaken a check"), and nothing says who does add it. So a card that is right stops whenever XMage or Forge is wrong about it. With two outside engines and tens of thousands of cards, that will be common.

The rule exists for a good reason: an entry is the easiest way to hide a wrong definition. The fix has to keep that from happening without a person in the loop.

Change

Let the tool decide, from evidence a writer cannot fake.

  1. A command, default name just card diverge "<card>" "<scenario>" <xmage|forge> --cr <rule> --reason "<what that engine does>", in tools/src/beryl_tools/card_check.py beside just card check. It writes the entry only when all of these hold, and refuses with the reason otherwise:
    • The other oracle played the scenario and agrees with Beryl. Two engines of three agree, and the third is the one named.
    • The rule cited exists in the release pinned in data/rules-version.json, and the tool fills in its quoted text itself (the lookup just citations uses).
    • The entry names one card, one scenario and one oracle. No wildcard. When both oracles disagree with Beryl, or the other oracle cannot play the scenario, there is no entry: the definition is treated as wrong, as today.
  2. The skill (.agents/skills/card-writing/SKILL.md, "Never weaken a check" and "Reading failures") says a writer may run that command and may never edit the file by hand. A check in tools/src/beryl_tools/checks.py fails a change by hand: an entry whose quoted text is not the rule's, or whose scenario the other oracle does not pass.
  3. Confirm that the nightly oracle sweep fails an entry whose oracle now agrees with Beryl (tools/src/beryl_tools/oracles.py), and add that if it is missing, so that a fixed XMage or Forge removes its entries.
  4. Proof: write Spellseeker with the skill and land it, with its divergence recorded through the command.

Files

tools/src/beryl_tools/card_check.py, oracles.py, checks.py, tools/tests/, .agents/skills/card-writing/SKILL.md, data/oracle-divergences.json, data/AGENTS.md, rust/AGENTS.md (the just card paragraph).

Tests

Python tests in tools/tests/ for the command: it writes the entry when the other oracle agrees; it refuses when the other oracle disagrees, when the other oracle did not play the scenario, and when the rule number is not in the pinned release.

Acceptance criteria

just card diverge writes an entry only when the other oracle agrees with Beryl and the cited rule is in the pinned release, and refuses otherwise (tested); a check fails a hand-edited entry; the card-writing skill describes the command; Spellseeker is landed with its XMage divergence recorded through the command.
