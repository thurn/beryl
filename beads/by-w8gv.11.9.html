Description

Session-held state for the shapes that work over cards lifted from a zone: order, split into destinations, pick from a zone (plan K26). by-w8gv.21.27 builds them on the stage from this bead's handoff.

Start from the design bead's table and the framework bead's handoff (the bead before this one in this lane).

Shapes, purposes and today's code

Check each against the design's table; the UI files are the list of cases and of the picks each choice plans.

  • Split into destinations, with order: scry (bottom picks, then the order on top); look at the top N, put some in hand and the rest on the bottom in an order (the Take and Bottom asks); cards from the hand onto the library in an order (PutBack). Today: ui/src/game/choices.ts Arrange and Stack and their plan(), ui/src/screens/Scry.tsx, ChoiceOverlay.tsx.
  • Order: the order of waiting triggers (TriggerOrder; Board.tsx about 3200; the session's own trigger_to_stack, game.rs about 2138, which already answers some for the player).
  • Pick from a zone: a library search with a way to find nothing, folding the "may search" yes or no into it (exception 3); a look at a zone that may take one eligible card (Look); discard and the cleanup discard; cards from the hand or graveyard exiled to pay a cost; the card put on the bottom after a mulligan; manifest dread's pick of two. Today: choices.ts Pick, tablePick.ts, ui/src/game/Piles.tsx.
  • What the opponent sees while a seat handles cards (card_handling, the protocol's build module) keeps working for every purpose here.

DECISIONS AND DEVIATIONS (by-w8gv.11.9)

  1. Where the code is. rust/crates/beryl_session/src/prompt/order.rs (the order shape, and the session's own trigger ordering, trigger_to_stack, moved there from game.rs), prompt/split.rs (the split shape), prompt/pick/zone.rs (picks from a hand, a library or a graveyard: Objects::from_zone and the graveyard Pile). held.rs's Shaped has three variants now and Held::open routes by the prompt's shape. game.rs shrank to 2187 lines (allowlist lowered).
  2. The run (held.rs) gained: Step::InTurn (each of these in this order: the next itself, or else one the engine offers in its place; a pick it cannot make ends the step, so the rest opens as a prompt rather than going on in another order); Want::Yes and Want::No (Whether, Instead); Want::Trigger { source, ability } (a waiting trigger named from one decision to the next, as Candidate::Trigger is); and a run that waits (Run::waiting): a trigger-order run is not ended by the Target decision of the trigger it just put on the stack. That prompt is the player's; when a click answers it, its own run goes over the waiting one (Run::over, Run::rest) and the order's next pick is made when its decision arrives. Game::undo and Game::cancel drop a waiting run (game.rs, one line each); play_on already drops it at any step that is not the human's decision.
  3. An order's items are the open decision's candidates, not every waiting trigger: the engine offers one of each set of equivalent triggers (trigger.rs next_to_stack, section 7.2). An item is Subject::Ability whose number is its candidate's index in the open decision (ShapeView::Order's comment says so): a waiting trigger has no script number yet. Nothing is marked. The prompt opens in the order the session itself would use (trigger_to_stack's heuristic, first to resolve first), so Confirm alone gives what the smart stops give. Known limit: with several equivalent triggers and another kind (three emblem triggers and a Converter trigger), the run's picks are one per item, and the order of what is left is asked again in a further prompt. tests/splits.rs shows it.
  4. DEVIATION from the design's Pick summary ("tray empty, most 1, no end offered: the click answers"): no pick from a zone answers by a click. A card of the hand (discard, cleanup, exile for a cost, the mulligan's bottom) is picked where it lies, tray empty, and a button confirms, as the old UI and Arena do: a misclick should not discard. Objects.confirmed says it; answers_by_click's comment is the design in place.
  5. Stand-ins (Table::stand_ins, held.rs) are no longer limited to the battlefield and the viewer's hand: any card the viewer knows by name, in the zone of an offered card of that name, that the engine says is interchangeable with it. A search needs it for the second copy in the library, a look at an opponent's hand for theirs. A card with no name known to the viewer stands for nothing, so nothing is asked of the engine about cards the viewer cannot tell apart; tests/sessions.rs no_view_shows_an_identity_its_viewer_does_not_know still passes. Side effect on .11.8's shapes: a priority or target now also marks a known twin in a graveyard or exile.
  6. Exile for a cost from a graveyard: every card of the graveyard is eligible (tokens excluded, CR 111.6), though the engine offers a window from the bottom up and the lowest of cards alike (cast.rs exile_choices). zone.rs's Pile mirrors that rule to compute the exact picks the engine will ask for (asked), as choices.ts graveyardExile did by name; here kinds are the engine's interchangeable. If the two ever disagree the confirm does nothing (the run's first pick finds no candidate): noted for the Warden.
  7. A run's end pick (Done) is now left out once as many are picked as the prompt takes, as the engine asks for none then (pick.rs run). It was left over before, harmlessly, for the untap.
  8. Wording added (wording.rs): confirm "Done" for trigger-order, scry, arrange, put-back and put-on-bottom (the old UI's button), and pile_words: a split whose second place is None calls that pile after where its cards stay ("Hand", for put-back). by-w8gv.11.7 edits the same table and may reword them.
  9. Split: built from the record alone, no match on purpose: the cards are those laid out where from says (top count of the library, or the hand), all opening in the second pile; the first pile takes exactly most when its picks offer no end; a prompt opening at Part::Second has the first closed. Known limit: opening mid-run (an old-UI save or undo) with a library no larger than the count can list a bottomed twin of an offered card among the top's.
  10. may-search stays a YesNo the session answers yes outside full control (design 1); under full control it is asked, and the search prompt follows. Nothing changed there.
  11. Not done here, by the bead's own list: combat-damage (with .11.11). Left with the old UI as before: its own state and choose calls for all of these purposes; it reads none of the new state.

THE COMMAND is by-w8gv.11.8's: click(decision, revision, click) -> ClickReply. New here are the clicks { kind: "drop", on, pile, at } and the states split and order. Every reply is the whole state; a click with no use returns it unchanged at the same revision. Rust tests drive it with tests/common/play.rs (Play::click for a drop).

FOR by-w8gv.21.27 (the stage), PER PURPOSE. Text forms after each click are in tests/splits.rs and tests/zone_picks.rs; titles and labels are the wording table's (by-w8gv.11.7).

SPLIT: state { piles: [first, second] }, each { place, label, cards (top first), exactly, closed }. Marks: eligible on every card of a pile that is not closed. Buttons: confirm "Done", enabled and primary once each exactly pile holds its number. Click on a card: to the other pile, at its end (refused when that pile is full or closed). Drop { on, pile, at }: into that pile before what is at at now, at = its length for the end; within a pile it reorders.

  • scry: piles bottom "BOTTOM" (opens empty) and top "TOP" (opens with the cards as they lie). Reached by: tests/splits.rs SCRY (Aang's Iceberg); board and fixture choice-scry; /specs of Aang's Iceberg.
  • arrange: piles hand "Hand" exactly N (opens empty) and bottom "Bottom" (opens with all, top first). Title "Put two of them into your hand.". Reached by: tests/splits.rs STOCK; Stock Up's own scenarios (beryl_cards blue/stock_up.rs), so /specs/stock-up. No board has the human cast it.
  • put-back: piles onTop "Top" exactly N (opens empty; first listed is the top card) and none "Hand" (the hand). Reached by: tests/splits.rs PUT_BACK; board and fixture choice-jace.
  • A prompt opened at its second part (a game taken up mid-run) has the first pile closed and empty.

ORDER (full control only; otherwise the session orders): state { items }, first to resolve first, each { kind: "ability", id } where id is the INDEX OF ITS CANDIDATE in the open decision: decision.candidates[id].option is { kind: "trigger", source, ability } and its label and nth say what to draw. No marks. Button: confirm "Done", always enabled. Click: drop { on: that ability, pile: 0, at }. After Confirm the next prompt may be a target (pick shape) for the trigger just put on the stack; the session makes the order's later picks itself once it is answered. With equivalent triggers waiting, a further order prompt can follow for what is left. Reached by: tests/splits.rs ORDER; fixture choice-trigger-order (seeded); signals-full-control for full control.

PICK FROM A ZONE: state pick { tray, least, most, picked, warned: [] }. Marks: selected on the picked; eligible on the rest while more can be picked (with most 1 the others stay eligible: a click replaces the pick). Never answers by a click: confirm "Submit n", enabled and primary when what is picked answers; decline "Decline" where it may answer with nothing, primary while confirm is disabled.

  • search: tray = what it can find, by name; 0..most; decline. A lone card opens picked. Scenario SEARCH (Trinket Mage); board and fixture choice-search.
  • look: tray = the cards looked at (top N, or the opponent's hand); eligible = those it can take; 0..1 with decline when the engine asks whether first, 1 of 1 without when taken up after the yes. A lone card opens picked. Scenarios LOOK (Thundertrap Trainer) and PEEK (Elite Spellbinder); board and fixture choice-look.
  • discard: tray empty, the hand's cards eligible where they lie. "Discard a basic land card or two cards.": the whole hand, confirm enabled at one basic land alone or at two cards. Plain: exactly most. Scenario THIRST; board and fixture choice-thirst (plain, after its yes... its board answers the yes first, so it opens at the one discard), choice-loot (one card).
  • cleanup: as a plain discard, most = cards over the hand size. Scenario CLEANUP; board and fixture choice-cleanup.
  • exile-cost: from the hand, tray empty, exactly most (scenario EVOKE; board and fixture cast-evoke). From a graveyard, tray = the graveyard top first, every card eligible, exactly most (scenario DELVE; boards and fixtures cast-exile-choice, cast-oculus).
  • put-on-bottom: tray empty, the hand eligible, exactly most, confirm "Done", title "Return N Cards". Seeded game in tests/zone_picks.rs to_the_bottom; fixture opening-bottom.
  • manifest-dread: tray = the two cards looked at, 1 of 1. Scenario DREAD; Abhorrent Oculus's own scenarios, so /specs/abhorrent-oculus.
  • combat-damage is not held yet (by-w8gv.11.11).
