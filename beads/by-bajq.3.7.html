Description

Lightning Bolt: "Lightning Bolt deals 3 damage to any target." A red source of damage for the prevention scenarios of this group, and for Fork in group 5.

"Any target" is a creature, player, planeswalker or battle (CR 115.4).

Reopened 2026-11-13: the first attempt escalated the card (commit 20c230eb) because no TargetKind and no Recipients form took a permanent or a player, and no implemented land made {R}. by-bajq.3.15 builds "any target" and by-bajq.1.8 adds Mountain; this bead runs after both. Write the card with them, and remove Lightning Bolt's entry from data/card-escalations.json in the same commit. Follow the card-writing skill. Scenarios: 3 damage to a player, to a creature with toughness 3 (it dies), and to a creature that left the battlefield in response (the spell does nothing).
