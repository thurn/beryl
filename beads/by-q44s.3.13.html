Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Objects and placement' ('Marks are separate quads'), 'The frame' (bands 2 and 3) and 'Verbs'; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/verbs/tap.rs and set.rs, src/table/scene.rs (Table::write), src/table/rows.rs (how a tapped card lies), src/text (how a string is shaped in handle), src/frame/draw.rs (Draw::icon, Draw::glyph, Draw::panel, sprites::PLAQUE). Web: ui/src/stage/tracks.ts (TURNED, turned: a permanent turning as it taps; PAYING, paying), ui/src/stage/enter.ts (entering), ui/src/components/Card.tsx (CardMarks, CounterMark, StatTone and toneClass: the plaque inked up or down against the printed values; Loyalty; MarkBadge and MarkIcon; NamedCounterBadge; the count the top card of a pile of identical permanents wears), and the record's fields in rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/protocol/view.rs (ObjectView::pt, counters, named_counters, damage, tapped).

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build:

  • tap: the card turns in the table's plane from how it lay to how its record says, over the step, about its middle, by the shortest way (turned). The cases are tap and untap.
  • set: stays an instant change; make it BUILT in the sense that every Fact is applied: Fact::Object replaces the record (the badges below then show it), Fact::Library and ShuffledLibrary reorder the pile with no motion, Fact::Forget drops the slots, Fact::Item and Fact::Turn move nothing.
  • Badges, in a new file src/table/badges.rs, called from Table::write for each card on the battlefield: the power and toughness plaque at the bottom right, loyalty in its place for a planeswalker, one badge for each kind of counter with its count, the damage marked, and the count on a pile of identical permanents. Each is a sprites::PLAQUE quad on the card's face, moving and turning with the card, in Band::TableMarks for a card lying on the table and Band::RaisedMarks otherwise, and its number is glyph records. The numbers 0 to 99, '+', '-' and '/' are shaped once, in Client::handle, when the first view arrives; frame only places glyphs it already has. The plaque's tint follows toneClass (raised, lowered or printed) with colors added to src/tokens.rs from the web's CSS (ui/src/design/components, the rules toneClass names).
  • frame::RECORDS_PER_OBJECT rises from 4 to 12, for a card with its edge, its shadow, a plaque and its digits, and two counter badges.

Do not build: the count verb or life totals (by-q44s.3.14); the marks a prompt draws (by-q44s.3.20); summoning sickness and combat marks (by-q44s.3.21).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/verbs_tap.rs, on views and scripts built by hand with testing::ViewBuilder, ScriptBuilder and testing::timing: for each case of the verb in data/ui-conventions.json (tap, untap; set's step begins and library shuffled), a script with that case's duration and delay plays to its end with every object at its placement (agent::steps::rest_equals_placement finds nothing), no_jumps finds nothing, and step_times finds nothing. A tapped card's TAPPED state is set from the middle of its step; a 2/3 creature has a plaque whose glyphs spell 2/3, placed inside the card's corners at every time of a flight; a card with two kinds of counters has two badges; a frame with 255 badged permanents allocates nothing; a Fact::Library leaves every card at its placement.

When it is done: set BUILT = true in the verb's file, so that Client::next_unbuilt stops reporting it; run just b3d test --update and just b3d timeline-parity --update if those commands exist on staging by then, and commit the lowered lists.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d png verbs-zones --at end --tree; just b3d gate; just b3d lane.

Decisions and deviations:

  • Badges are Draw::panel quads (rounded, bordered) rather than sprites::PLAQUE quads: the atlas does not draw PLAQUE yet (by-q44s.3.22 is open) and the Icon material has no shader branch, so a PLAQUE quad would sample glyph texels. Counter badges show their count tinted by kind (stat-up for +1/+1, stat-down for -1/-1, gold otherwise) with no icon, for the same reason. by-q44s.3.22 or the marks beads can swap the panel for the sprite.
  • Only the digits 0-9 and + - / x are shaped (14 pieces), not each number 0-99: shaping 104 strings cost 105 text runs and about 2,400 allocations in Client::handle on every gated board. The card font's numerals are tabular, so a number set digit by digit reads the same.
  • Damage is drawn as the web draws it: toughness shown less damage marked, inked down (frameFace), not as a separate badge.
  • One text style for all badges (Crimson Pro 700), sized per badge from the web's em values in space.json 'badges'.
  • TAPPED on a card on its track is read from its rotation (past half its quarter turn), so it switches mid-turn.
  • set: Motion::step now gives what a set step places again an instant change (Cause::Change) instead of a zero-length slide. This removed every listed no-jumps fault (12,004 entries in test-faults.json).
  • beryl_inspect gate test a_flight_draws_only_the_cards_the_rows_show now counts card elements, not records, since a permanent's records now include its badges.
  • just b3d png verbs-zones --at end has no creature on the table at its end, so no plaque there; adeline-resplendent-cathar/adelines-human-attacks-a-planeswalker --at end shows plaques 2/4 and 1/1 and loyalty 2 (/b3d/out/adeline-resplendent-cathar_adelines-human-attacks-a-planeswalker-you-1600x900-end-51754.png).
