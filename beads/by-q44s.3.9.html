Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Effects', 'Timing a script' (StepEffect), 'Determinism' and 'Test speed' (check times); the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/effects/mod.rs (Effect, EffectRun, and the stubs the contract bead by-q44s.3.2 left), src/play/step.rs and compile.rs (where a step's verb is played), src/play/mod.rs (Show, Playing::real, Show::wake), src/play/record.rs (ScriptRecord), src/driver/check.rs (times). Web: ui/src/stage/effects.ts (Effects: an effect 'moves nothing, holds up no step and outlives the one that began it'), ui/src/stage/stage.ts (the method that begins a step's effects at an edge, near the comment 'Begins the effects the step run has at this edge'), ui/src/stage/overlay.ts.

Build:

  • As a script is compiled, each StepEffect of each step's StepTiming becomes an EffectRun in Compiled::effects, on the script's clock. It is on effect.on, or else on the step's subject; with neither it is dropped. It starts at the step's start for Edge::Start and its end for Edge::End, or with into at start + into x (end - start), and then delay later. It lasts duration, or the length of its step where that is none. A name that is no Effect is dropped, with a debug_assert.
  • As a script starts, Show adds its runs to Show::effects with their times made real (Playing::real). Each frame first drops the runs that have ended, with retain, which allocates nothing. When the list is full (256), the run that ends first is replaced. A script with a reset clears the list.
  • An effect goes on after its script has ended, and never delays Show::settled. Show::wake answers Now while a run is under way and At its start before one begins.
  • ScriptRecord gains effects: Vec<EffectRun> on the script's clock, and driver::check's times adds each run's start, middle and end to the check times.
  • Show::effects(&self) -> &[EffectRun], which the writers of by-q44s.3.23 and by-q44s.3.24 read through Dress and Scene.

Do not build: anything an effect draws.

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/effects.rs: a step from 100 to 500 ms with settle@end of 330 ms gives a run from 500 to 830; with into 0.376 and no duration, one from 250.4 to 500; a run is listed at every frame of its span and at none after; it is still listed 200 ms after its script ended and the prompt opened meanwhile; a reset clears it; the frame that starts a script with 20 effects allocates nothing; wake is Now during a run.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d gate; just b3d lane.
