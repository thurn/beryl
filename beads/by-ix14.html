Derek Thurn2026-11-12

Cause: tests/test_warm.py::test_the_tauri_target_is_cloned_only_for_the_tauri_cli read the host's real free disk. jarvis had 48.3 GB free (< caches.LOW_DISK 60 GB), so warm.ensure's caches.run(only_when_low=True) went into low-disk mode and spare() removed the test cache's target/tauri before the tauri clone. Not caused by any commit in the range (bisect agreed: fails at b0626f4c too). Reproduced by faking shutil.disk_usage free=1 with the old conftest. Fix: tools/tests/conftest.py's autouse own_cache pins caches.free_space to 10x LOW_DISK; test_caches.low_disk_cache now patches caches.free_space instead of shutil.disk_usage. Test kept: the product is right, the test was host-dependent; it guards the tauri-only clone, which nothing else covers. Also annotated a pyre-flagged captured variable in test_warm.py (from cf143106).
