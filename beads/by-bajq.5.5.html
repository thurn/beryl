Description

Cards

  • Underworld Breach: "Each nonland card in your graveyard has escape. The escape cost is equal to the card's mana cost plus exile three other cards from your graveyard. At the beginning of the end step, sacrifice this enchantment."
  • Lion's Eye Diamond: "{T}, Sacrifice this artifact, Discard your hand: Add three mana of any one color. Activate only as an instant."

What they need

Plan: Designs 6, "A class of permission"; Designs 4, "Reading".

  • Escape (CR 702.138) as a casting option from the graveyard: an alternative cost with a part that exiles other cards from the graveyard, chosen as the cost is paid; the permanent or spell remembers it escaped (Memo::Paid).
  • Granting it to cards in a graveyard: Underworld Breach's static ability is an effect that reaches another zone (by-bajq.5.1's list). cast::legal::cast_sources adds the cards of a zone to what a player may cast only while that list or a class permission is not empty, and each such card's casting options include the granted one, with its cost read from the card's own mana cost.
  • Lion's Eye Diamond: a mana ability with a cost that discards the whole hand, and "activate only as an instant", which keeps it from being activated while a spell's cost is paid (the spell is already on the stack and the player does not have priority). It can be activated in response, with the escaped spell's card still castable from the graveyard afterwards.

The loop

With Brain Freeze (by-bajq.6.4): Breach on the battlefield, Lion's Eye Diamond and Brain Freeze, cast and escape them in turn until the opponent's library is empty.

A line to start from (check the arithmetic against the engine, and change it if it is wrong). Start: Breach on the battlefield, two untapped Islands, Lion's Eye Diamond and Brain Freeze in hand, both graveyards empty, 53 cards in the opponent's library.

  1. Cast the Diamond. Cast Brain Freeze with the Islands, targeting yourself, and with it on the stack sacrifice the Diamond for three blue mana. Storm makes one copy: you mill six. Your graveyard holds eight cards with the Diamond and Brain Freeze.
  2. Each round after that: escape the Diamond (no mana, exile three other cards), escape Brain Freeze (two mana, exile three other cards), and sacrifice the Diamond in response for three more. A round uses six cards of the graveyard, returns the two spells to it, gains one mana, and its Brain Freeze has two more copies than the last.
  3. The storm trigger lets each copy have a new target (CR 702.40a). Round 2 (four mills of three) targets yourself. Round 3 (six) sends two at yourself and four at the opponent. Rounds 4 and 5 (eight and ten) target the opponent: 12 + 24 + 30 cards.

Write it as a scenario and as a test that the whole line stays inside the turn's decision budget (Limits, 500). No shortcut is built (decision R13).

Derek Thurn2026-11-15

Design (executor, 2026-11-15):

  • Escape: Underworld Breach's first ability is a static ability with Objects::Everywhere(nonland, yours, Filter::InZone(Graveyard)) and a new Modification::Escape (layer 6, LayerChange::GrantEscape). The layers list it in the elsewhere list like Painter's Servant's color; the kernel answers State::escapes(t, slot) from that list. A grant needs no new field on Object.
  • The escape cost is fixed as 'the card's mana cost plus exile three other cards from your graveyard': Breach is the only printed card that grants escape, so the count is a constant, not a parameter.
  • A cast with escape is marked by a reserved bit of Castings (bit 7, so faces list at most 7 options). Castings::paid yields a synthesized CastingOption::Escape { cost: face.cost, parts: [exile 3 other cards from your graveyard] }, so the total cost, X, the parts step and Memo::Paid (CastingKind::Escape) read it with no special case. A card with both flashback and escape offers both casts, and an escaped cast starts no flashback replacement.
  • cast_sources adds the player's graveyard cards with escape only while the elsewhere list is not empty (one length test otherwise).
  • Lion's Eye Diamond: a new CostPart for 'discard your hand' and Timing::OnlyAsInstant for mana abilities. The solver never taps such an ability; priority offers it as Opt::Mana { slot, color } (one option per color, the path manual payment already uses for floating mana), gated by a new Capability so other games pay nothing. It is never offered during a payment, which is what 'activate only as an instant' rules out.
