Acceptance: (1) the tray's Hand pile lies in the table's hand order after draws; (2) a card moved back from Top returns to its old hand place; (3) both layouts (shared table reducer).

Cause: the session lists the Hand pile in slot order (beryl_session prompt/held.rs laid_out, Hand arm), while the stage fans a hand in arrival order (table.ts Table.order / arranged), drawn cards last.
