Built as the bead asks, with these deviations: (1) Press had no way to open a viewer, so Press::View(Viewed) was added (Viewed::Pile(side, zone) | Viewed::Revealed); a press on a pile's element (which now catches presses) or a non-pressable pile card opens it. (2) Shown objects are applied when the table draws (Table::shown_look, Band::LayerCards), not in Placement, so scripts keep playing from zone rests and rest-equals-placement is unaffected. (3) OwnTimings has no fly entry; moves into and out of boxes use TrayLifts/TrayLetGo, the own steps that carry cards off the table to be looked at (rule 7b). (4) The Revealed Cards pill is a float (floats::REVEALED, prepared once in handle for the first revealing script), not a game-screen piece, because faces appear mid-script and a frame must not lay out; revealed::revealed() returns the pill element. The revealed viewer draws faces flat (Shown::Face) since the faces are second elements, not scene objects. (5) scry_sample takes (window, faces) because faces need FaceIds; the gallery stub does not call it yet. (6) A box scrolled partly out of the grid shows no card (3D cards cannot be clipped). (7) Viewer cards are not interactive (no hover inspect in the viewer yet). Numbers: just b3d test 8220 tests, 0 failed; gate passes with stress-verbs limits raised for the pill (old/new in commit body); beryl_scene 308+ tests pass; just b3d lane passes.
