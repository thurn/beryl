Description

Fiend Hunter, 1/3: "When this creature enters, you may exile another target creature. When this creature leaves the battlefield, return the exiled card to the battlefield under its owner's control."

Why

TriggerEvent::Leaves ("when this leaves the battlefield", by-bajq.1.2) is proved only by the test definition Test Keepsake in rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/tests/triggers.rs. No card of the epic has a leaves-the-battlefield trigger.

Fiend Hunter needs the exiled card linked to the ability and returned under its owner's control, which by-bajq.3.14 builds for Flickerwisp; this bead depends on it.

Scenarios

  • Fiend Hunter enters, exiles a creature; Fiend Hunter is destroyed; the creature returns under its owner's control.
  • Fiend Hunter bounced to its owner's hand also returns the creature (the trigger fires for every zone).
  • The classic ruling: Fiend Hunter leaves while its enters trigger is still on the stack. The leaves trigger resolves first and returns nothing, then the enters trigger exiles the creature for good.

Follow the card-writing skill: just card brief <name>, just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), scenarios for each instruction and each ruling a scenario can show, and just specs <card> from both seats. A clause the vocabulary cannot say is escalated in data/card-escalations.json. Epic bars hold: just count-gate with no bump (report the count before and after), State<Small> within 20,480 bytes, just test-rust (frozen saves, rollouts_never_change).

Acceptance criteria

Fiend Hunter's scenarios pass on Beryl, XMage and Forge, including the leave-in-response ruling.
