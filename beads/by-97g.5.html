Description

Part of the ladder epic (read it and docs/ai-strategy.md §6 rung 1 first). The root plays the most visited candidate; near-ties are then settled by noise, and visits can overrule the means (Jace, Wielder led by 0.015 and lost on visits).

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs: leader (plays by visits), Search::means (each candidate's mean averaged over the trees that visited it), AnchorCheck and mean_and_error (the noise across trees), Options and Family's Display and FromStr (the fifth name part).
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/mcts.rs: Mcts votes by visits too; leave it unless the match passes, then a second bead.
  • docs/incidents/2026-11-07-snapcaster-and-jace-wielder.md, 2026-11-08-searcher-mulligan-means.md: the evidence.

Build

A new option means: at the root, play the candidate with the best tree-averaged mean among those with at least a tenth of the most visited candidate's visits, unless the most visited candidate's mean is within the noise across trees (two standard errors) of the best, in which case keep it. pimc72/hidden/rollout/tuned/anchor+means plays as the champion wherever the vote and the means agree. Moves depend on the seed alone.

Bar (pre-registered)

Plausible: just blunders shows it fixes the Jace, Wielder attack entry and regresses no entry the champion passes; then just ladder pimc72/hidden/rollout/tuned/anchor+means@3M --bar plausible accepts "no change" (elo0 -25, elo1 0, alpha = beta = 0.05, cap 2,000). Run the screen first and write its cost beside the result.

Tests

Crafted trees where the vote and the means disagree choose by the means; where they agree, nothing changes; a losing candidate below the visit floor is never chosen. Family names round-trip with means.

Record

Results go in this bead's notes and as a row in docs/ladder.md; commit no logs. On a pass: change deep_searcher in rust/crates/beryl_search/src/lib.rs to the challenger's settings, name this bead and the match in its doc comment, update the champion table in docs/ai-strategy.md §3, and run just blunders once more on the shipped agent.
