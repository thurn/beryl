Close reason

Flips re-place only their card (placed 2038->1924); frame 30 2,620,279, target 2.0M unreachable by placing fewer cards (measured ~160 instr/slot)

Description

stress-verbs frame 30 measures 2,626,633 instructions on beryl3d-bench after by-q44s.3.64 (Callgrind, all 106 steps compiled). The target was 2,000,000. Read by-q44s.3.63's and by-q44s.3.64's comments first: they give the per-function profile, the per-step slot counts, and two restructurings of the step passes that were measured and rejected because they cost more. The per-slot passes in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/play/step.rs (mark_affected, commit, follow) are already tight. What remains is cutting how many slots each step lays out and follows.

The data: of about 2,000 slots laid in the beat, about 750 do not change. Flip, Tap on a creature, and Appear at the end of a non-centred row each lay a 16-28 card row to change one card. RowChange::between (table/rows/change.rs) returns Layout for any change of tapped, facing or name, so play/step/placement.rs places the whole row again.

Designs to try, largest first: (1) A RowChange case for a change that can move only the card itself: a tap or flip in a row whose layout does not depend on that field for this card (no piles, no lands ordering, and a card width that does not change). Give only that card a new Rest, computed from its old one (rows.rs permanent decides the pose), and lay nothing else. Prove it with the debug full-placement comparison in Motion::commit, which already runs where records are kept. (2) Follow without retargeting each step (the bead before this one called it option 2). About 900 of the 1,200 follow calls only retarget a slide an earlier step of the same beat pushed. Apply the retarget once, when the beat ends or when anything reads or pushes the slot's track. The tracks must come out identical: same segments, causes and closing ends. (3) Fewer allocations and ObjectView clones and comparisons in compile: together they cost about 270K of frame 30. Index::new's same check (play/index.rs) and place_reusing's member comparison (table/place.rs) each compare about 100 ObjectViews.

Profile with Callgrind's fn= self costs (see by-q44s.3.64's comments for why addr2line names mislead in this binary).

Acceptance criteria

On beryl3d-bench, just b3d gate --counts measures at most 2,000,000 instructions for stress-verbs frame 30 with all 106 steps compiled, or the bead records, with measurements, which of the designs were tried, what each saved and why the rest cannot reach it. The count gate, native gate (just b3d gate) and interface tests (just b3d test) pass. Tracks are unchanged (segment counts in just b3d gate, interface tests).

Derek Thurn2026-11-16

Measured on beryl3d-bench (just b3d gate --counts --board stress-verbs, all 106 steps compiled): staging before this bead 2,638,123 (the limit); after design (1) for flips, 2,620,279. The 2,000,000 target is NOT met. Design (1) was done for the change that moves no card in its row: RowChange::Pose (facing or phased_out changed; name, types, tapped and attachment unchanged), given only if no other card of the row has the card's name. It cut frame 30's placed slots from 2038 to 1924 (the 5 flips) and saved only ~18K instructions, about 160 a slot, end to end. That measurement is the main finding: if each laid slot costs ~160 instructions in total (lay, set, mark, commit, follow), the ~750 unchanged slots in the beat cost about 120K together, so no design that only places fewer cards can cut 620K. Not done, and why: (1) for taps and appends. A tap changes the card's width (card_width), and an Appear changes the row's members. Only in an overflowing row whose window and last shown card stay the same does such a change leave the other cards in place. Re-deriving that outside lay_single_cards and lay_row would copy their window and spacing rules, and it would save at most the ~13 x 22 slots x 160 ≈ 45K the flips' rate predicts. (2) retarget once per beat: follow costs 136K, with retarget 69K, so its ceiling is well under 100K, and the tracks would have to stay identical. (3) clones and comparisons: Index::new's same check must compare the previous script's end table with the view, because the two can differ (compile.rs ended()/fallback). Together (1)-(3) are bounded at roughly 300K, short of the 620K needed. Reaching 2.0M needs a cut on the drawing side (~0.6M) or a cheaper per-step structure, not fewer slots.
