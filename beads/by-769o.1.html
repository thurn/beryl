Close reason

Slow CI takes idle tokens up to 3/4 of the pool; waits for tokens extend the lane's deadline and are logged apart (473bd88e)

Description

What we see

Every slow-CI run since 253920fa ("machine-wide CPU token pool") and aedba2ce (by-gdn3.9) has timed out. In the run at 30fc3534 the rust lane logged ci rust clippy: 292.3 s while cargo reported Finished ... in 2.70s, then ci rust tests: TIMED OUT after 7.7 s. During the run at 57f6cd5a the load average was 8-18 on 18 cores, so cores were idle while slow CI waited.

Why

In tools/src/beryl_tools/tokens.py, ceiling("ci", 18) is 9: slow CI may hold half the pool at most, whether or not anything else wants tokens. Ten heavy steps (HEAVY_STEPS) start in parallel lanes and each asks for largest(18) = 12 tokens, so most get one or two tokens or wait. checks.py (Steps.run, around line 230) takes the lease "against the deadline": time spent waiting for tokens counts towards the lane's 600 s deadline and Enact's slow_timeout (1000 s in .enact.toml). With one or two cargo jobs, a cold clippy took 8 minutes.

What to change

  • Slow CI may use any token that no higher class (push, agent) holds or is waiting for. Keep the rule that a higher class's waiter blocks lower classes, so a push still gets cores first. Remove the fixed ci ceiling or make it apply only while a higher class waits.
  • Time spent waiting for a lease does not count towards any step deadline or budget. Record the wait separately in the step's log line.
  • Give each lane's heavy steps leases sized so that the lanes running at once share the pool, rather than ten steps each asking for 12 of 18.

Files and tests

tools/src/beryl_tools/tokens.py (ceiling, largest, Pool.grant, Pool.acquire), tools/src/beryl_tools/checks.py (Steps, step_tokens callers), tools/tests/test_checks.py, and tests for the pool (add tools/tests/test_tokens.py if there is none). Update docs/checks.md.

Done when

A slow-CI run on a staging tip passes or fails on test results alone, with no lane killed by its deadline, and master advances. Close or update the Steward bead by-kaz8 accordingly. The XMage oracle failure on scenario Generated 28 ("Missing CAST/ACTIVATE def for turn 1") seen at 57f6cd5a is a real failure: leave it to the Steward bead.

Derek Thurn2026-11-13

Decisions: (1) Slow CI's ceiling is now the agents' ceiling (3/4 of the pool), not the whole pool, so a push keeps its reserved quarter. Leases are fixed when they start, so with no reserve a push could wait minutes behind a slow-CI clippy; a waiting push or agent still blocks slow CI from taking more. (2) A lane's waits are tracked per thread in Steps.waits and added to that lane's deadline. The wall limit (before Enact's slow_timeout) still applies while waiting, because it's an outside kill. (3) A run's time for its budget is now the longest lane's time with waits left out, not the run's clock time. (4) A heavy step wants ceil(pool / heavy lanes still running), at most largest(); play() counts the lanes before any start. (5) Steps moved to tools/src/beryl_tools/step_runner.py because checks.py went over 800 lines. Steps.lock is now an RLock because over() reads waits under it.
