A walker's unusable abilities show dimmed in its Choose One; staging f5b6db6e

Description

Split from by-w8gv.21.63 (the old check "a planeswalker's click opens its chooser, unaffordable abilities disabled", ui/scripts/interactions.mjs, fixture walker-low).

What Arena does

A click on your planeswalker opens "Choose One" with a frame for EVERY loyalty ability, in the card's order. Those that can be activated wear the cyan outline; one whose cost its loyalty cannot pay is shown without the outline, slightly dim, and takes no click. Seen twice, Liliana of the Veil at 3 loyalty (+1 and -2 outlined, -6 not): 2VW6ZzmS8U4 6:24.5 and TbrYIs2juCw 1:10:20.5 (just video film 2VW6ZzmS8U4 6:23.0 --span 2 --fps 2 --cols 2 --width 960). Not seen: a walker with exactly one usable ability; the old board opened the chooser there too (game/AbilityChooser.tsx: "always opens it, even when only one can be activated now, so that activating is always two clicks"). Proceed on that.

What the stage does

The session's choice among a card's ways (rust/crates/beryl_session/src/prompt/mode.rs Mode::act, opened from prompt/pick/priority.rs click) has a button only for each candidate the engine offers, and a card with one way is answered by the click itself (ways.len() == 1 => answers(ways[0])). So a walker at low loyalty shows only what it can do, and one with a single usable ability activates on one click.

What to build

  • Session: for a permanent with two or more activated abilities that are not mana abilities (the old rule, AbilityChooser.tsx abilityChoices), open the act mode on a click even with one way, and send a disabled button (PromptButton.enabled false) for each such ability the priority does not offer, in the card's order among the offered ones. A disabled way names no candidate: it needs its ability's index (the nth the UI's abilityText takes) and a label. Decide the ButtonId (a new variant, or the option's index made optional) and regenerate ui/src/generated/protocol.ts (its header says how). The engine already knows each activated ability of the object; nothing is worked out in the UI (plan K26).
  • Stage: ui/src/stage/Choose.tsx Ways draws a way from decision.candidates[id.index]; draw a disabled way from the ability's index instead, with no outline and no press (choose.css). The dock's Cancel stays.
  • Check: a case of the mode shape (ui/shapes.json, shape-mode board in rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/boards.rs, ui/scripts/shape-cases-panel.mjs) or a fixture with clicks on the walker-low board; the suite already holds each button's enabled to the session's.
  • data/ui-conventions.json, "Choosing how to cast": add the planeswalker's unusable abilities with the two timestamps above (just conventions renders the page).

Parity as every bead of the track: just check passes, the frozen save corpus replays, the counts lane holds with no bump.

Acceptance criteria

On the stage, on the desktop and the phone, a click on a planeswalker with an ability its loyalty cannot pay opens the choice with every loyalty ability, the unusable ones shown disabled and taking no press, and a click on a permanent with two or more non-mana activated abilities never activates one at once; a shape case or fixture check named in a comment holds it under just ui-test; UI Conventions cites the Arena evidence; just check passes.
