Description

Cards

  • Mind Bend: "Change the text of target permanent by replacing all instances of one color word with another or one basic land type with another. (This effect lasts indefinitely.)"
  • Crystal Spray: "Change the text of target spell or permanent by replacing all instances of one color word or basic land type with another until end of turn. Draw a card."

What to build

Plan: Designs 4, "A text change", "On the stack", "Absent" and "Shown".

  1. The effect. Modification::ChangeText in layer 3 (Layer::Text): a kind of word, from and to, both chosen as the spell resolves (two Catalog picks; the "from" candidates are the words the target's text has, plus any other, since choosing a word that is not there is legal and does nothing). It is an Effects entry on one object, permanent or spell, with its duration.
  2. State::words_of(object) composes the entries on the object in timestamp order. It reads the Effects store behind its kinds mask, so with no text change in force it is one AND.
  3. The layers apply layer 3 before layer 4: a permanent's own type line and its static abilities are read through its words from then on. Changing "Mountains" on Blood Moon to "Islands" changes what Blood Moon does; changing a Forest's type makes it an Island that taps for blue (CR 305.6).
  4. Granted abilities are not changed (CR 612.3): only the object's own face is read through its words.
  5. A spell whose text is changed keeps the change as the permanent it becomes (CR 400.7a): the entry moves with it.
  6. On the stack. An activated or triggered ability keeps the words its source had as it went on the stack. The state holds up to four captured sets, named by one byte in the stack entry, emptied when the stack empties. A fifth is Full.
  7. Shown. The session's rendered rules text and the type line in the view take the object's words.

Scenarios

Mind Bend on a Circle of Protection: Red makes it a circle against another color; on Blood Moon; on a basic land. Crystal Spray on a spell on the stack, and its end at cleanup. Dandân's scenarios are by-bajq.5.4's.

Decisions: (1) Effect::ChangeText {objects, duration} instead of Modification::ChangeText — a Modification changes permanents in the layers and Crystal Spray targets spells; plan section 4 amended. (2) No separate Capability: the Effects store's Kinds bit is the test (words_of is one AND).
