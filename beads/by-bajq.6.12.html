Close reason

Double Major implemented with WithoutSupertypes copy change; Test Twin deleted

Description

The copies group proved 'a copy of a permanent spell becomes a token as it resolves' (CR 608.3f, CR 707.10) only with a test definition, Test Twin ("Copy target creature spell") in rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/tests/spell_copies.rs (a_copy_of_a_creature_spell_becomes_a_token). No card of the epic copies a creature spell, so no scenario checks that path against XMage and Forge.

Implement Double Major ({G}{U} instant: 'Copy target creature spell you control, except it isn't legendary if the spell is legendary. (A copy of a creature spell becomes a token.)') with just card new and the card-writing skill. The copy is Effect::CopySpell (beryl_card effect/mod.rs, builder copy_spell_except; Fork in rust/cards/cards_1993/lea/fork.rs is the model). 'Isn't legendary' is a new CopyChange (a supertype removed), read by layer 1 as CopyChange::Colors and AddSubtype are (State::copiable_base, Copiable::excepted_subtypes in beryl_kernel/src/copiable.rs); if it can't be said, escalate it in data/card-escalations.json, never leave it out.

Scenarios must show: the copy resolves first and is a token creature with the spell's P/T on the battlefield; the original resolves after it as a card; a copy of a legendary creature spell is not legendary, so the legend rule keeps both (use a cheap legendary creature already implemented); the copy is not cast (a 'whenever you cast' trigger sees only the original). Once a scenario covers the token path, delete Test Twin and a_copy_of_a_creature_spell_becomes_a_token from spell_copies.rs.

Acceptance criteria

Double Major passes just card check (Beryl, XMage, Forge) with the scenarios above, and just specs --shots 'Double Major' passes. Test Twin and its test are deleted. The count gate holds (just count-gate).
