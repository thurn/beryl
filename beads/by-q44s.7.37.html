Close reason

Phone exile rests unseen under its graveyard; step-shown skips flights back to the same place

Description

Symptom: just b3d test --phone-only reports six step-shown faults in b3d:shape-zone/opponent at every phone size (step:7.0.0 to 7.0.5, "seen in 13 of 29 frames ... mostly covered"). Cards fly from the opponent's graveyard to their exile, and the phone rests exile cards at the third pile's place in the opponent's strip (table::phone::piles, layout.piles[side][2], used by table::pile::pile_base). The Revealed Cards pile and its button lie on that same place (table/revealed.rs, comment: the phone leaves the third pile's place free), so each flight ends under the flat-layer button. UI Conventions, 'The phone', says an exiled card rests unseen under its owner's graveyard, as the web's ui/src/stage/phone.ts places it.

Change: on the phone, place each side's exile cards at its graveyard's spot, drawn under that pile and never shown (table/pile.rs lay_pile, table/place.rs lay), leaving the third place to the Revealed Cards. A flight from graveyard to exile then starts and ends in one place; agent::steps::step_shown reports 'did not move' for that unless a step whose start and end rest at the same place on the window is treated like a card that stays in its zone. Decide that and record it in the doc's 'Interface checks'. Read docs/beryl-3d.md 'The flat layer' and 'Interface checks' and rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md first.

Acceptance criteria

No step-shown fault in b3d:shape-zone in just b3d test --phone-only and with --every-tick; desktop just b3d test, just b3d timeline-parity --fixtures and just b3d gate pass.
