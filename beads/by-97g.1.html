Description

Part of the ladder epic (read it and docs/ai-strategy.md §4 first). The match runner.

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/tournament.rs: run, Paired (the sign test on swept seeds), game_seed, the Wilson interval.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/game.rs: play, agents, entrant (long-think names, family@budget with options), seat_seeds.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/main.rs: usage and dispatch. The justfile and tools/src/beryl_tools for the just ladder recipe, at utility QoS as just tournament runs (by low).
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs: LongSearch, Settings, Search (root visits, means), for the per-decision record.

Build

beryl_cli ladder <challenger> [--vs NAME] [--work 3M] [--bar stronger|plausible] [--cap N] [--seed N] [--positions DIR] [--out DIR], behind just ladder:

  • The champion defaults to the registry's Deep Searcher family at the proxy budget: pimc72/hidden/rollout/tuned/anchor@3M. Both names are long-think entrants or registered agents, as game.rs already resolves them.
  • Games come in seed pairs, each seed played from both seats, 18 games at a time, one thread each (Budget.parallel false, as tournament.rs does).
  • With --positions, four pairs in five start from a position of the set and one from the shuffle (§4.1); without it, every pair starts from the shuffle, so this bead lands before the position set does.
  • The test is a trinomial SPRT over seed pairs (swept, split, lost), as fishtest's pentanomial one: the generalized log-likelihood ratio of the pair-score distribution under the two Elo hypotheses of the bar. stronger: elo0 0, elo1 +50, alpha = beta = 0.001, cap 2,000 games. plausible: elo0 -25, elo1 0, alpha = beta = 0.05, accepting elo1, cap 2,000. A split pair carries little information by construction; the test does not stop early on splits.
  • The match ends on a decision or at the cap and prints: games, pair counts, the challenger's score, an Elo estimate with a 95% interval, the verdict (pass, fail, undecided) and a ready docs/ladder.md row (date, challenger, champion, budget, games, score, Elo, verdict). JSON beside it.
  • Every game's transcript and replay, with the champion's searched decisions (root visits and means, as the long-think decisions.jsonl records them), go to --out, default $BERYL_CACHE/ladder/<date>-<challenger>/. The screen bead reads them. Nothing under it is committed.
  • A seed reproduces a match's games, as tournament.rs guarantees.

Tests

The SPRT on crafted pair streams: all sweeps accept elo1 within the expected games; all splits never decide; the bounds match alpha and beta. A pair's two games share the seed and swap seats. The row renders. just test-rust, clippy pedantic.
