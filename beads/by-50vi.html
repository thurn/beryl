The run passed every lane and was over budget at 595.6 s on an idle machine (load 2 on 20 cores), so neither the load excuse nor a rerun applied. Cygnus has overrun the same way on unrelated commits: by-r7p9 562 s, by-6h2o 627 s (which passed at 332 s and 297 s on the next runs), with rust (tests 370-503 s, compile included) and oracle (400-600 s) the critical paths each time, against Mac runs of about 130-160 s for the tests.
