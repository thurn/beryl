Description

Card

Cavern of Souls: "As this land enters, choose a creature type. {T}: Add {C}. {T}: Add one mana of any color. Spend this mana only to cast a creature spell of the chosen type, and that spell can't be countered."

What is wrong today

ManaPool is six counts (beryl_kernel/src/player.rs). Mana cannot remember what it may be spent on or what it does to the spell it pays for.

What to build

Plan: Designs 6, "Mana with memory".

  1. A pool gains up to four entries of mana that remembers: a color, a restriction and a rider. A fifth is Full. Restrictions and riders are numbered by the game as rule changes are; a restriction may read a value from the source's memo, captured into the entry as the mana is made (the chosen type's game number), since the source may be gone when the mana is spent.
  2. A Capability says whether any card of the game makes such mana. Without it the pool is six counts and nothing else is read.
  3. The solver (beryl_rules/src/mana/): a source of such mana is a class of its own. It can pay only for a cost its restriction accepts. A plan that spends it differs in outcome from one that does not (the rider), so both are offered (D7).
  4. The rider. As the mana is spent on a spell, "can't be countered" becomes a rule change on the spell (Continuous::Rule on an object, which State::effect_rules_on already reads and no card starts). actions::counter asks it. A spell that can't be countered can still be targeted by a counterspell, which then does nothing to it.
  5. The mana empties with the pool at the end of each step.

Scenarios

A creature spell of the chosen type paid with Cavern mana is not countered by Counterspell; the same spell paid without it is; the colored mana cannot pay for a spell of another type or a noncreature spell; the colorless ability pays for anything.
