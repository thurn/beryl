Cause: 3df489dc's generated registry reordered CARDS, so beryl_cards::generate's 100 random scenarios changed. Four new ones hit exporter/harness gaps, not rules differences: XMage G23 (evoke alternative-cost choice XMage asks when a blue card is in hand; cast_answers gives no 'Cast with no' answer for non-mana alternatives), XMage G28 (xmage.py pay() pre-taps by ability text; '{T}: Add {W}' matched Tundra instead of Plains), Forge G29 (BerylForgeHarness.hasChoice counts Snapcaster flash despite Teferi, Time Raveler's restriction), Forge G100 (harness doesn't replay Beryl's traced taps; Forge AI left Strip Mine untapped). Fix: listed all four in data/oracle-exporter-gaps.json (7b0f30f9). Verified: just oracle Generated passes both oracles; just ci passed locally. Handoff: if CARDS order or the pool changes again, these entries go stale and fail the run; the real fixes are in xmage.py pay()/cast_answers and the Forge harness (replay trace taps, respect casting restrictions in hasChoice).
