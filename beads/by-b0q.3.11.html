Gate rerun on fresh seeds: the 5/5 bar is an artifact of the seeds. Same rubric (verbatim), Sonnet reviewers, notes with the transcript format and the 29 cards' Oracle texts.

  • Round 1, 45d4878, seeds 11/22/33/44/55: only 11 met the bar (44 'reasonable' with 2 blunders; 22, 33, 55 not reasonable).
  • Round 2, after the first fixes, seeds 1-5 and 11-55 (all changed): clean were 3, 11, 22, and 33 (its one claimed blunder did not hold up); 5 and 44 reasonable with blunders; 1, 2, 4, 55 not reasonable. So the code no longer passes on seeds 1-5 either, though it plays better (fixes below).
  • Round 3, d44c086, unseen seeds 101/202/303/404/505: 101 clean; 202 one blunder (fixed in f621913); 303, 404, 505 not reasonable. 21 rounds of fixes on seeds 1-5 tuned the rules to those games: each new seed set turns up new blunders. Filed a follow-up to iterate against unseen seeds each round.

Strength (not seed artifacts): expert v random 197-198/200 on tournament seeds 1 and 77 (98.5-99%); expert v greedy 73.5-80% (was 74-75%).

Fixed (all confirmed in the transcripts):

  • Teferi first when more spells follow and the opponent could counter (seed 22: tapped out into No More Lies with Teferi in hand, dying).
  • Sink into Stupor never on a Swift Reconfiguration they could flash straight back (seeds 22, 44).
  • Get Lost never on a non-token Sphinx of False Conclusions, which returns as a token (seeds 33, 55); test get_lost_leaves_a_sphinx_that_comes_back (fails without the fix).
  • In their combat, removal and Swift Reconfiguration rank attackers by danger (lethal, half our life, a planeswalker they kill), not any attacker first (seed 2: Get Lost on a 1/2 attacker over Overlord; seed 4: Iceberg on a token over a 7/7; seed 33: Swift on a non-attacker).
  • Known counters include one our Elite Spellbinder exiled (castable for {2} more: new Observation::permissions, public per CR 406.3), check colors, and No More Lies counts only when we cannot pay {3}; Teferi and The Wandering Emperor respect them (seed 44: Emperor into an exiled Mana Drain; seed 55: Teferi held all game).
  • No Mana Drain on a late Mana Crypt (seed 55); no Mana Crypt with 7+ lands or nothing to spend it on (seeds 55, 404).
  • A known Mana Tithe holds back any cast of ours, flash ones too (seed 44).
  • A major spell goes first while we can pay No More Lies's {3} (seed 33: Sink then Adeline into it).
  • The last land waits for Jace, Vryn's Prodigy's loot (seed 44); Soporific Springs pays 3 life only when its mana enables a card, colors included (seed 5).
  • A wheel from an empty hand against 3+ cards (seed 2: died holding Timetwister); counter/flash mana kept only for cards we could cast; removal in a window the opponent cannot counter (seed 202).

Open, not fixed (leads for the follow-up): missed multi-step lethals (seed 1 T27, two bounces; seed 505 T20, Emperor +1 precombat); land kept as loot fodder for Malcolm/Sphinx loots (seed 303 T14); Time Spiral cast offer from Malcolm while far ahead (seed 303 T20); Emperor -2 on a tapped flier at 1 life (seed 505 T21); No More Lies on a wheel that refills a far-behind opponent (seed 505 T17, seed 4 T4); Teferi -3 targets (seed 505 T17).

Checked, fine: the expert reads hidden cards only through Observation (masked slots); tests are deterministic counts, no wall-clock; expert tests ~2 s. Note: the fast check took 62 s (budget 60), 49 s of it the rust lane's compile and tests, not the expert tests.
