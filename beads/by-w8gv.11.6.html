Description

The wire says a prompt's shape and purpose, and the old UI reads them (plan K10). After this bead a card's question needs no edit to the protocol, generated/protocol.ts, ui/src/game/choices.ts or the exporters.

Start from the design bead's wire types and table (bd comments on the first bead of lane:prompt-shapes) and the two engine beads' handoffs: the engine's decisions now carry the design's record, and DecisionKind has lost its mechanic-specific members. The protocol is a directory now (rust/crates/beryl_session/src/protocol/).

Change

  • DecisionView (protocol/decision.rs) carries the design's prompt type in place of ask: AskView: shape, purpose, parameters by public slot and relative to the viewer, and its wording. AskView, ask_view and the per-ask conversion are deleted. Put the prompt's types and conversion in modules of their own under protocol/, cut along the shapes, each under 800 lines.
  • Wording: the session sends each prompt's title and button labels from one table keyed by purpose (where the design bead put it). Seed it with today's strings, unchanged: the titles and labels in ui/src/game/choices.ts (choiceOf and its helpers), untapChoice.ts, tablePick.ts and Board.tsx's Prompt (about 2905 to 3133). The wording bead audits them against Arena afterwards.
  • Marks and buttons are not sent yet: the prompt-state beads that follow in this lane fill them, shape by shape. DecisionView's waterbend, self_removal_targets and damage_default stay until those beads replace them.
  • The old UI switches from ask.kind to shape and purpose, and keeps its own state and its plans of picks: choices.ts (choiceOf), tablePick.ts (5 reads), Board.tsx (15), beats.ts (1), combat.ts (1), decisions.ts (1), ui/scripts/interactions.mjs (5) and fixture-views.mjs (1). A switch that named a one-card ask now names a shape and a purpose, so a new card with a known shape and purpose falls into an existing arm. Where the UI shows a title the session now sends, read the session's.
  • rust/crates/beryl_session/tests/asks.rs is rewritten against the prompt type; beryl_cli reads asks in specs (grep ask under rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/specs and fixture.rs) and follows.
  • Regenerate ui/src/generated/protocol.ts (BERYL_UPDATE_TS=1 just test-rust -p beryl_session --test typescript). Fixtures are recorded by the checks, not committed.

DECISIONS AND DEVIATIONS (by-w8gv.11.6)

  1. One object on the wire. DecisionView.prompt is the design's PromptView, extended: shape (ShapeName), purpose (string), part (PartName), title, words, count, most, places, cost, about, from (whose cards and the zone they lie in: the first of about, or else the first object offered), then marks, buttons and state. The design had shape: ShapeView required; it is now shape: ShapeName plus state: Option<ShapeView>, None until the session holds that shape's state (by-w8gv.11.8 to .11.11 fill it shape by shape, with marks and buttons). Reason: the old UI still holds every prompt's state and plans the picks, so it needs the record (the same inputs design 1's "rule for consumers" gives the session), and an opening ShapeView computed here would be each later bead's work done without its tests. When every shape has state, state can lose its Option and shape its duplicate. The record fields exist for the old UI: the stage reads state only, so they go at the cut-over.
  2. Where the code is. The bead said modules under protocol/; the design's types had landed in rust/crates/beryl_session/src/prompt/ and by-w8gv.11.8 puts the state there too, so everything is there: prompt/view.rs (wire types), prompt/wording.rs (the table and its holes), prompt/open.rs (prompt_view: the record to the wire). protocol/ask.rs and AskView are deleted. Nothing is cut along the shapes yet because the conversion has no per-shape code: it copies the record. The per-shape modules come with the state.
  3. Words. const fn wording(&Prompt, zone) -> Wording, exhaustive on the purpose; where a purpose is worded two ways the entry turns on the part, the place or the zone (target at a count, a look at a library or a hand, a move by where it goes, exile-cost from a graveyard or a hand, attack and block at their aim, mana at its plan). Holes: {count} {most} {Count} {cost} {source} {name} {land} {Whose} {from}, filled by the session; {picked} and {of} are the prompt's state and are left for whoever holds it, which is the old UI for now (decisions.ts counted). The wire sends the finished title and words (confirm, counted, decline, yes, no, auto, piles): interim, for a UI that draws its own buttons. PromptView.buttons will carry the session's own.
  4. Strings are today's, with these exceptions, all for by-w8gv.11.7 to settle against Arena: (a) choose and legend-rule have one title each, the board's ("Choose a card", "Choose the legend to keep"); the overlay's "Choose a Card" and "Legend Rule" are gone (the overlay shows only past five candidates; the legend one is unreachable in practice). (b) waterbend's title is the visible "Tap permanents to Waterbend"; the float prompt's aria-label was "Waterbend". (c) never shown and not seeded: PROMPTS' "Cast it?", "Choose" for a yes or no, "Choose cards", ASKED's "Pay life?". (d) Catalog and Divide purposes take the titles of tests/prompt.rs's worked examples, since no card asks them. (e) priority has no title (the turn button passes). (f) "Discard a basic land or N cards" still says basic land for any discard at Part::Instead.
  5. Still built in the old UI, because they depend on its own state or lie outside the files the bead named: " for <source>" after a target's title, "Resolve first" / "Resolve next", "Cancel", "Confirm", the pass button's labels (passButton), and the combat dock (Declare, Finish: "Attack With All", "Clear", "N attacking", "Choose an attacker to block"). The table has attack's and block's titles and their confirm and decline words; the dock does not read them yet.
  6. The old UI: choiceOf switches on shape, then purpose, each shape with a default arm. fateseal, top-or-bottom, explore, may-search and may are one generic yes-or-no (title and answers from the session, cards from about); manifest-dread and choose are one generic pick. tablePick's "basic land" test is now about (the regex over type lines is gone). Intent.ask became Intent.purpose.
  7. beryl_cli: the fixture condition "ask <kind>" is "prompt <purpose> [<part>]" (ui/fixtures.json follows; "prompt discard pick" pins the pick after Thirst's yes or no). Spec labels read "Ask: <purpose> [<part>]"; specs' yes and no actions and review titles come from the prompt's words.
  8. script/infer.rs looked is generic: the top count of the library the prompt's cards lie in, or the card a yes or no sends somewhere. protocol/build.rs shown is shared by card_handling and the prompt.
  9. DecisionView.prompt and ClickReply::Open's prompt are boxed (clippy's large_enum_variant).
