Overload, evoke and granted flashback in the engine; Damn, Winds of Abandon, Subtlety, Snapcaster (+Swamp) with scenarios, oracles pass or unsupported, fuzz 1000 clean (c11f179)

Description

Part of the Arena Powered Cube retarget (epic by-0w2): Beryl's V1 cube is now data/arena-powered-cube-2025-10-30.csv and the mirror match target is the 40-card main deck in docs/sample-decks/2025-11-18-17lands-cb8d5d5f-wu-control.txt (30 distinct cards, 17 of them new). Read the epic's description and design field first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md). Follow the existing card files in rust/crates/beryl_cards/src (for example blue/mana_drain.rs, white/overlord_of_the_mistmoors.rs) for layout and scenarios.

Implement: Damn and Winds of Abandon (overload, CR 702.96), Subtlety (evoke, CR 702.74; a card that is an alternative cost of exiling a blue card), Snapcaster Mage (granting flashback, CR 702.34).

Edge cases for scenarios: Damn cast for {B}{B} needs black mana and for overload destroys every creature, untargeted (hexproof and protection do not stop it); Winds of Abandon overloaded exiling each creature you don't control with each controller fetching one basic per exiled creature, and fetching nothing from a library with no basics; Subtlety evoked (sacrificed on entering, its trigger still resolving) and hard cast, choosing top or bottom, and its target being a creature spell the owner chooses for; Snapcaster's flashback on Counterspell then exiling it, flashback not usable after the turn ends, and the target with no instant or sorcery.

Acceptance criteria

  • Each card listed has one file under its color directory with its CardDef, bodies and scenarios; every ability is exercised by at least one scenario, including the edge cases named; every body cites the CR.
  • All scenarios pass under the strict runner, and just oracle <these cards> passes on XMage and Forge, or each disagreement is in data/oracle-divergences.json with its CR citation.
  • Fuzz games including these cards raise no invariant (debug, at least 1,000 games on demand; the just ci count still passes).
  • just check and just ci pass.

Executor notes (by-0w2.4):

  • Engine: CastingOption::Overload {cost, ability} (alternative; the face lists an untargeted spell ability at index 'ability', picked by Face::spell_ability_with(costs) in entry_ability/castable) and CastingOption::Evoke {cost, exile: Colors} (alternative; CastStep::Exile + DecisionKind::ExileCost chooses the card from hand during payment, CR 601.2h; castable only with another card of that color in hand). Ability::evoke() + ObjectFlags::EVOKED for the sacrifice trigger.
  • Flashback (Snapcaster) is a CastPermission with flashback=true: it excludes the face's own alternative costs (CR 118.9a; tested via Damn: no overload from the graveyard), StackEntry.flashback exiles the spell on resolve/counter and also on return_to_hand (Reprieve) and the new put_spell_into_library (Subtlety) (CR 702.34a). Engine unit test covers the bounce path; a Snapcaster scenario covers it with Reprieve.
  • New primitives: grant_flashback, put_spell_into_library, destroy_all, search_library_up_to (Trinket Mage's search_library now calls it), put_onto_battlefield_tapped. Ask::TopOrBottom (UI: Top/Bottom buttons) and DecisionKind::ExileCost (UI pick from hand).
  • Deviation: added Swamp (black/swamp.rs) so Damn's {B}{B} mode can be exercised; by cards' default list now includes the five basics (cards.json gains Swamp/Mountain/Forest).
  • Deviation: hexproof/protection aren't implemented anywhere in the engine, so 'overload ignores hexproof' is shown as 'overload asks for no target and hits the caster's own creature too', with the CR 702.96b citation.
  • Oracles: all scenarios pass or are unsupported. Exporters learned overload ('X with overload' in XMage, AlternativeCost Overload/Evoke in Forge), evoke, Winds' library search, XMage flashback as activateAbility('Flashback {cost}'). The dump trace now lists cards with flashback per position. Unsupported: flashback cast onto a non-empty stack in XMage (one Beryl Resolve covers Snapcaster + its trigger), and all flashback casts in Forge (the harness finds Forge's flashback SA but fails to play it).
  • XMage note: in the evoked Subtlety scenario XMage failed a mid-resolution 'Subtlety in graveyard' check whichever trigger order it was given; the scenario now asserts the stack count there and the end state, which both oracles pass.
  • Corpus pin in beryl_bench updated (StackEntry/CastPermission gained a hashed field).
