Description

Major design gap: the visible pile depth is decorative rather than the cards. In Player.Pile, edges=min(20,ceil(count/2)); FaceUpEdges invents count=(n-1)*3; hidden library CardLayer objects have opacity 0 and share a position. Graveyard/exile placements all use the same onPile coordinate. This produces slab-like cosmetic edges that cannot individually depart, settle or be browsed. Evidence: 002-converter-zones.png and 007-graveyard-duplicates.png, plus Timetwister/shuffles-graveyards-away film (gather into deck, draw seven). Static pile edges reconfigure immediately with count/depth; they have no motion identity. Build a bounded, efficient physical pile model: visible layers correspond to the cards being manipulated; stable depths/offsets, thickness, shadows and occlusion; move the top object away and reveal the next; settle on arrival. It need not mount every hidden card as a heavyweight card face. Animate collecting/shuffling/cutting and returning to top/bottom instead of modifying a static back/count. Respect hidden-library knowledge and known-top rendering. Where: Player.tsx Pile/Library/FaceUpEdges, layout.ts pileLook/onPile/library and zone placements, Board pile rendering, CardLayer travel, beats/playback, components.css pile-edge. Integrate the browser identity work rather than a second pile system. Verify draw, mill, discard, exile, return, graveyard recycle, bulk draw, empty->one->many, last card removal, both seats. Existing linked exile must remain linked.

Evidence root: /Users/dthurn/Library/Caches/beryl/spec-drive/physical-ui-audit/20261109-162708-35500/; film root: /Users/dthurn/Library/Caches/beryl/spec-film/physical-ui-audit/

Implement general presentation/event behavior, not card-name special cases. Preserve hidden information. Hover previews are explicitly allowed by the updated AGENTS.md; prefer modest in-place enlargement when feasible, never force a huge original-card zoom. Do not dissolve ordinary cards that are moving between zones: carry them to their destination. Use the specs skill and verify both seats; retain evidence outside git. All repository changes use Enact worktrees and CI.

Acceptance criteria

Piles read as discrete objects at game size and each arrival/departure has a physically consistent layer; count/depth changes animate. Timetwister and Jace ultimate visibly collect/redeal with ordered movement. No identity leaks, no new duplicate representations, no unbounded heavyweight library rendering.
