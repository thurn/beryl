Description

Players lack these, and each must be added without standing text:

  • Turn and step: there is no turn number, and the phase tracker is unlabelled icons. Label the icons on hover, and put the turn number in the game log's turn headers and the tracker's hover.
  • Pile counts: the library, graveyard and exile piles now print counts. Move the numbers into disclosure: a pile's browser shows its count, and so does hovering it. Pile thickness scales with the count, so its size reads at a glance. The opponent's hand size reads from its card backs.
  • Floating mana: the mana pool is never shown (Mana Drain's {C}{C} and Mana Crypt's leftover {C} vanished). Show mana-symbol icons by the life orb only while the pool is not empty. Passing with mana floating pulses the pool rather than raising a dialog.
  • Land drop: once it is used, lands in hand lose their playable highlight. That already says it, so add no text.
  • The stack: it shows neither the controller nor an announced X. Show the controller by side or accent colour, and X as a small badge on the card.

Files: ui/src/components/Player.tsx, ui/src/game/Piles.tsx, ui/src/components/Stack.tsx, ui/src/game/Narration.tsx, ui/src/game/log.ts.

Before changing anything, reproduce each item on current staging. Use just ui-live, as the ui-review skill describes (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md), or a fixture. The findings come from reviews of the build before the 2026-11-05 UI fixes, so some may already be fixed. Drop those, and list them in the close reason.

Design rules (see the epic): follow the skill's design philosophy, and add no standing words to the table. What a player needs at a glance is shown as icons, badges, numbers, colour or position. Everything else goes behind disclosure: hover, the inspector, a pile's browser, or the game log.

Acceptance criteria

  • Shots show the tracker's hover label, a pile browser with its count, floating-mana icons, and a stack item with X and controller marking.
  • A just ui-test check: the words on the table outside cards and open prompts (the board's text, minus card text) do not grow compared with the parent commit. They should shrink once the pile counts move out.
