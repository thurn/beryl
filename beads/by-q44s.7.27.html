Handoff: Decision: the strike's apex is treated as mid-verb rather than shortened on the phone, so desktop and phone strikes keep the same path and timelines.

Changes (commit 9978acc6d):

  • Track::paused(t) in beryl_scene src/motion.rs: true when a step's own act holds the card still between two of that step's segments that move it (both segments have the same step, with Follows::Start). A pause between two steps, or after a step's last move, is not a pause.
  • table/scene.rs write/write_card: a paused card gets StateBits::MOVING, so reachable (which judges cards only in frames where no card is MOVING or HELD) skips that frame. Shadows and badge bands still follow under_way (Track::moving). A first attempt widened Track::moving itself, which moved held cards into the raised band and failed just b3d gate (stress-verbs total_records 29800 over 29735, blended area 0.81 over 0.78). Splitting the rule out kept the gate exactly green.
  • Unit test motion::a_card_held_within_its_steps_act_is_paused; docs/beryl-3d.md "Reachable" now says what counts as moving.

Measured (phone-only, with MOST_FAULTS raised locally to list every fault): reachable faults went from 444 to 346 at check times and from 825 to 354 on every tick. None left are on a striking creature: the bead's examples vigilance/... and adeline/...stays-home are clean, and the faults left in mother-of-runes, teferi-time-raveler and verbs-cast-theirs are cards resting in their own row under the stack's ability card. Desktop just b3d test (8264 tests, 0 failed), timeline-parity --fixtures and gate pass.

For a reviewer: a paused card now skips pointer lift (pointer::look returns early on MOVING), which only matters mid-verb. Arrows (play::Scene::moving) are unchanged.
