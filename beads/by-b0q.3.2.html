Derek Thurn2026-11-05

Delivered in fbefb7a, d5fd80c, 83d9706. Decisions and deviations:

  • The dump now carries a trace per action (turn, step, decider, stack, coin flips, taps, forced answers): XMage scripts by turn and step, Beryl by decision, so the exporter places commands from where Beryl actually was. A new Stops::Forced profile stops at questions with one answer (in next and in EffectCtx::ask) so the trace records answers Beryl gives silently but XMage asks (targets, X, crew, waterbend). The runner always uses it; its answers are the engine's own silent ones.
  • Assertions run inside runCode at their position, through XMage's assert* family where it states the same fact and helpers in a generated BerylScenarioTest base otherwise. Beryl Decision assertions and assertions in the middle of a decision are skipped (listed per scenario in the summary).
  • The generated base installs a TestPlayer subclass with passPriority (a command after the other player cast from an empty stack would otherwise run first), resolveTop (XMage's waitStackResolved:1 is consumed at once on an empty stack) and optional choices for forced answers XMage may not ask (they expire after the step Beryl answered them).
  • Mana payments replay the sources Beryl tapped when each has one mana ability (Plains, Island, Mana Crypt, Strip Mine); otherwise XMage pays.
  • Per-card tables translate XMage's different questions: impending/kicker/offspring at cast, Sink into Stupor's targets in the cast command, Time Spiral's 'include tapped lands' shortcut, Spellbinder/Trainer may-choose, JTMS put-back and scry bottom via the target queue.
  • Merged with the Forge exporter that landed meanwhile: one just oracle runs both; the allowlist is its data/oracle-divergences.json (3 XMage entries, CR 118.3) and the summary uses its status names (allowlisted, stale_allowlist).
  • Get Lost {1}{W}{W} bug: found here and by Forge; Forge's fix landed first, I kept a two-lands regression scenario.
