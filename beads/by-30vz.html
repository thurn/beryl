Close reason

56f00adc: the 37 oracle checks named were five cards (Timetwister, Trinket Mage, Thieving Skydiver, Spellseeker, Thundertrap Trainer) from rotating per-commit samples. Cutting them saves nothing, since the next commits sample other cards. The real cause was the estimate: check_value.full_run summed every card sampled in the lane's last 10 runs (~530 CPU s) when one run plays one sample (~137). full_run now counts a SAMPLED lane's median run cost, and cuts never names its checks. No oracle scenario was deleted; the fast check still plays changed cards and the nightly plays all.
