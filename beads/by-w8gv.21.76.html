Verb suite plays each moment once: 207 -> 70 CPU-s user, ui-test lane 195 of 300 s

Description

Slow CI's ui-test lane runs over its 300 s budget whenever the machine is busy: 334, 387 and 478 load-adjusted s on staging on 2026-11-12 (a65a9389 to 93b44b62, .git/enact/ci.log), and 512 s (timed out, 285 of 286 checks ok) in the Warden's worktree at load 38. The cut-over itself measured 261.5 s of 300 s for the whole of just ci on a quieter machine. by-5fnf excuses a rerun killed by the wall limit; it does not make the lane cheaper.

The cost is the verb suite: just ui-test verb: alone is 212 CPU-seconds (64 checks), and under contention for browser slots (ui/scripts/slots.mjs) took 14:44 on the clock; just ci-report --days 1 has verb:point, verb:streak and verb:fly on the phone at 235 to 263 s each on the clock, the costliest checks of the lane. The shape suite is 46 checks in about 30 CPU-seconds and is not the problem.

Why: ui/scripts/verbs.mjs (the loop at about line 690) plays, for every verb, seat and layout, the moment 'whole' (and 'cut' for emerge) plus each moment the verb watches (ui/verbs.json watches, more), at every window of the layout (one on the desktop, three phone sizes from ui/shots.json), at ticks of 16 and 100 ms at the first window. Sixteen verbs replay the same recordings from the same seat at the same window and tick: the play (play(), walk()) does not depend on the verb except for the written marks of verb:mark.

Do: play each (moment, seat, layout, window, tick) once and hold every verb's case (CASES in verb-cases*.mjs) and audit (verb-audit.mjs) to the shared runs, keeping one run-log check verb:<verb>/<seat>/<layout> each (tools/src/beryl_tools/suites.py and selection.py choose among them by name, so a chosen subset must still play only the moments its verbs need). Keep the two tick sizes compared and the three phone windows. Report the suite's CPU-seconds and the ui-test lane's seconds before and after on this bead and to the CI track (by-w8gv.20).

Acceptance criteria

just ui-test verb: passes all 64 checks with the same check ids and uses under half the CPU-seconds it does today (212 s user, measured with time on jarvis); a planted fault still fails the right check (ui/src/stage/enter.ts emerge returning undefined fails verb:emerge on all four); just ci's ui-test lane is inside 300 load-adjusted s in the worktree; ui/scripts/verbs.mjs stays under 800 lines; just check passes.
