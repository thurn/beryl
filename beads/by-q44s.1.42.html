Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'The frame' (the atlas, and the glyph and panel rows of the material table) and 'Rendering'. Read beryl_assets' glyph rasterizer and how the flat-layer slice asks for glyphs (Client::wanted_glyphs, its glyph table, Input::Assets).

In rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_render:

  • The atlas as a 2D array texture of single-channel pages, and upload_page.
  • Two shading functions in the blended pipeline: shade_glyph (coverage times tint) and shade_panel (a rounded rectangle with a border, from params and the border color in uv as the doc's table gives them, antialiased over one pixel).

In beryl_host's Headless and in b3d-render: call prepare_latin before the first frame; each frame drain Client::wanted_glyphs, hand the keys to the rasterizer, pass what comes back to the client as Input::Assets, and upload pages before drawing.

Tests in beryl_render/tests, on the default device and on one with the downlevel limits: an offscreen render of the text "Pay" in white on a black panel has a white pixel inside the stem of the P and a black pixel in its bowl, within tolerance; a panel's corner pixel outside its radius is untouched.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_render; just b3d png pay-default --at prompt:1.

Acceptance criteria

just b3d png pay-default --at prompt:1 shows the prompt bar with readable text and bordered buttons; the pixel tests pass on both devices; no glyph is rasterized on the thread that calls Client::frame; shade_glyph and shade_panel read only the fields the doc's table gives them.

Handoff: beryl_render/src/atlas.rs is new. Atlas is R8Unorm, 8 pages of 1024 by 1024, with no mipmaps. Glyph pages 0..GLYPH_PAGES (7) come from Renderer::upload_page(&Prepared). The last page holds GLOW_SPRITE's disc, which moved from the old RGBA placeholder atlas, so shade_glow now reads sprite.r. shader.wgsl: shade_panel(mesh_uv, mesh_pixel, tint, border=in.rect, params) is an SDF rounded box with a border, antialiased over one pixel. mesh_pixel = fwidth(mesh_uv) is taken before the switch, because derivatives need uniform control flow. shade_glyph(sprite, tint) is coverage times tint. A new flat varying, rect, carries uv to the fragment stage. The gates.json material limits rose because the entry point's cost is added to every material: card 63->72, contact_shadow 77->86, glow 63->72, table 55->64; new glyph 66 and panel 110. beryl_assets: GlyphWorker::flush() blocks until every request before it is sent (a Message enum replaces the plain key Vec), and glyphs_rasterized_on_this_thread() is the test hook. beryl_scene driver/glyphs.rs (new, split out to keep mod.rs under 800 lines): Page::wanted_glyphs and Page::give_glyphs. Page keeps the glyph AssetReady values it was given and replays them in begin() and fresh(), so text survives a seek back. beryl_host Headless owns a GlyphWorker plus a Receiver. new() asks for prepare_latin(1.0). text() runs before frame, pixels and png: it lays out, requests the wanted glyphs, flushes, uploads the pages and gives the client their glyphs. Tests: beryl_render/tests/text.rs ('Pay' stem and bowl; panel corner, border and fill; both limit sets), beryl_assets glyphs flush test, beryl_3d pictures the_prompt_bar_draws_text_rasterized_on_another_thread. Not done: the desktop and phone hosts do not run the glyph worker or upload pages yet. Text at --scale >1 is enlarged from 1x glyphs, because the driver lays out at scale 1. Fast check: beryl3d cargo-test was cut off under load and handed to the sweep; I ran the beryl_render, beryl_scene, beryl_assets, beryl_inspect and beryl_3d pictures tests locally, all green.
