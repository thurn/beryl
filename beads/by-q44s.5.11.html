Build, in src/prompts/pick.rs:

  • A pick's candidates are clicked where they lie: a card or player on the table sends Press::Prompt(Click::On { on }). Picked and warned subjects show the marks the session sends in PromptView::marks, begun and ended by own.
  • A non-empty tray holds those cards up in the tray (OwnStep::TrayLifts), and lets them go when the prompt closes (TrayLetGo). A zone pick (from set) holds the zone's cards up the same way, as the web does for a library search.
  • For purpose target, an arrow from the asking spell or ability to the pointer follows the mouse until picked holds most, as Aim.tsx does (it is not drawn for a touch); picked targets get the session's arrows from the asking object.
  • The tap previews (previews): hovering a card shows which lands it would tap, as PromptBar.tsx's usePaying does for its card.

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/prompts.rs (new): picking a target in shape-pick sends Click::On with the card's subject and the reply's mark appears on it; the tray of shape-zone holds its cards up and lets them go after confirm; the aim arrow's end follows a hover and is gone once most are picked. How every prompt bead works (the contract's src/prompts/mod.rs):

  • prompts::screen gives the flat-layer elements for the prompt from the PromptView alone: the dock (the prompt bar, with its buttons in the session's order, the primary at the foot as PromptDock in ui/src/stage/PromptBar.tsx draws it) and whatever else the shape draws in the flat layer. The client keeps no prompt state of its own: every click goes to the session as Press::Button or Press::Prompt(Click), and the session's reply (ClickReply::Open) brings the next state.
  • prompts::own begins and ends the marks, arrows and tray lifts the prompt shows, through Lit::begin and Lit::end (milestone 2's src/light) and the tray of src/table/tray.rs, timed by the entry of own_timings() for each OwnStep (beryl_protocol src/script/timing/own.rs); where after_ends is set, fill after with the arrow's ends that are not players. The web's version is Prompts in ui/src/stage/prompt.ts (and trayOf, waysOf, coversTable there).
  • The shape's recordings are the fixtures ui/shapes.json names for it. Run each from both seats (--seat you and --seat opponent) under just b3d test, answering with the recorded clicks, which the driver does as the recording plays.
  • Timeline parity: run just b3d timeline-parity --update after the shape plays, and say in a comment how many entries of data/beryl3d/timeline-parity.json your shape's recordings had before and after. The count may only go down.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d test b3d:shape-pick; just b3d timeline-parity; just b3d lane.

Deviations from the bead:

  • The contract's prompts::own(prompt, was, lit, timings, now) became prompts::own(prompt, &OwnIn, &mut Wanted). The arrows need DecisionView::source and the tray needs the table's camera, so the prompt says what it wants and client/own.rs (Owning) diffs that against what is drawn, as Prompts.#mark does. Beats are built in handle, so the frame allocates nothing.
  • Players are picked by a press on their life total float (hud/counts.rs). A separate target element would sit under the float, and the reachable check rejects that.
  • Tap previews outline the payment's taps for the card the pointer carries out of the hand, as prompt.ts #carrying does. The hover is only found during the frame, so the beat could not be built ahead of it.
  • The tests are in beryl_inspect/tests/all/prompts.rs, not beryl_scene/tests/prompts.rs, because only beryl_inspect reads the recordings pack. There are four: a target click and its reply mark; the aim arrow following the pointer and going once the target is picked; the search tray lifting its cards and letting them go after Submit; a player picked through their life total.
  • The driver's Responses now skip recorded clicks of decisions the recording played past, so a test can act at a later prompt.
  • Scripts compile from the zones, not the tray. The first script after a let-go starts each card it carries more than half a card away from the tray (Show::hand_over_tray). Under a tray only its cards can be pressed (the web's shade). Filed by-q44s.5.44 for opponent-turn's step-shown fault on an opponent's draw. It became visible because Client::rested now counts own steps, which moves the reply times.
