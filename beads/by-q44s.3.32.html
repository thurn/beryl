Close reason

Scripts are laid out again as they start (hold and alone), every timed record moves with its step; parity 8116 -> 6684 steps; pushed 3211ac401

Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Timing a script' ('A client lays a beat out in time': two of its inputs are known only to the client), 'Determinism' and 'Timeline parity'; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. The problem. beat (beryl_scene src/beat.rs, a port of laid in ui/src/stage/beat.ts) takes two inputs that depend on when a script starts: whole, since when the span a step ends has stood whole, and passed, until when something passes over the table. A script is compiled when its update arrives, which may be seconds before it starts, so compile.rs passes |_| None and the beat's own start for both. A target arrow's end waits until the arrow has stood whole for 290 ms (StepTiming::hold), and a coin waits until no banner is passing (StepTiming::alone). Without them, those steps start too early and the timeline differs from the web interface's.

Code: beryl_scene src/beat.rs, src/play/compile.rs (lay_out), src/play/step.rs, src/play/mod.rs (Show::frame, where a script starts; Playing), src/light/mod.rs (Lit::whole_since), Show::passed (from by-q44s.3.30; until it lands, the latest end of the notices Show keeps, or the script's start with none). Web: ui/src/stage/stage.ts (the call of laid with this.#over.whole(act) and this.#over.passed()), ui/src/stage/overlay.ts (Overlay::whole, Overlay::passed), ui/src/stage/beat.ts.

Build:

  • Everything Compiled holds that has a time carries the number of the step it belongs to and which of that step's times it follows (its start, its end, or the interval the step leaves for what makes room): segments, effect runs, span changes, streaks, notices and sounds. Add the field where it is missing.
  • Compiled keeps the laid-out beats (Vec<Laid> for the whole script) and a scratch Vec<Laid> of the same length, allocated when it is compiled.
  • When a script starts, in Show::frame: if any of its steps has a hold or is alone, lay every beat out again into the scratch, with whole answered from Lit::whole_since (as real time less the script's start, in milliseconds, counting a span the script itself begins from its own step) and passed from Show::passed. Then move each timed thing by the difference between its step's new and old time, and the script's end with its last beat. Nothing is allocated, and no pose is recomputed.
  • This is exact when the steps that wait move nothing, which the session's scripts guarantee for a span's end (rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/script/mod.rs, 'Spans': 'One ends in a beat of its own before the beat that moves either of its ends'). A coin's announce moves nothing either. Assert it in debug builds: a step whose time changed relative to another step of its own beat has no segment.
  • ScriptRecord shows the times as played, so just b3d timeline prints them.

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/relay.rs: an arrow begun in script 1 and ended in script 2, which starts 100 ms after the arrow stood whole, ends 190 ms into script 2, and every later step of script 2 is 190 ms later than compiled; started 2 seconds after, nothing moves; an arrow begun and ended in one script waits from its own begin; a coin after a turn banner of 1,000 ms starts when the banner ends; the frame that starts such a script allocates nothing; a script with no hold and no alone step is not laid out again (count the calls with a test counter).

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d timeline <a recording with a targeted spell>; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

The tests pass; for a recording with a targeted spell (named in a comment), just b3d timeline shows the target arrow's end step starting no earlier than 290 ms after its begin step ended; if just b3d timeline-parity exists on staging, its list is lowered and committed, and the comment gives the number of recordings before and after.
