Review of by-0w2.10 (92a5aeb..f1fddfb). Checked Oculus against Oracle and CR 701.40/701.62/708/601.2h/118.3, every bead edge case has a scenario, all Rust tests pass. Engine model (FaceIx::DOWN -> FACE_DOWN through CardDef::face) holds: filters/MV/layers read the 2/2; zone moves reset face and reveal (708.9); collapse never merges face-down; determinize resamples face-down deck slots; Observation masks. Warden lead (copiable_values on face-down) is fixed in c1ad83d, with scenario + test. Added: test turning_face_up_asks_for_a_payment_plan (the Pending::TurningFaceUp plan path had no coverage; Island/Island/Mana Crypt, plan choice, face up after). Not oracle-exported because the exporters script no plans. Left alone on purpose: (1) a token copy of a face-down permanent is modeled as FaceIx::DOWN, though CR 707.2 makes it a face-up nameless 2/2. Its characteristics are the same and it can't turn face up; only protocol facing/UI card back differ. Fractured Identity exiles and reveals the original anyway, so nothing leaks. (2) AI: rollout Policy::action and expert main() score Opt::TurnFaceUp 0, and Policy::value prices a face-down permanent at its card's value (the controller's) or 0 (the opponent's), so search rarely turns manifests face up. That's AI strength, not in the 17.3 support bar, so no bead filed. (3) face_up_cost treats a {0} cost as no mana cost. No creature in the cube costs {0} (checked the CSV).
