Description

Why

Decision P4 of the plan: the workflow is proven on about a dozen cards, not on a set. This bead is that proof and its measurement. Plan: Designs E8.

What to do

  1. Run just set plan ths. Take six cards of its drafted tier and six of its composed tier, in the order printed, skipping any that needs {R} or {G} if no implemented land makes it yet (write Mountain's or Forest's mana first if that is all that is missing; they are basic lands with generated definitions).
  2. Write each with the card-writing skill exactly as written, in its own worktree and push, one after another. Do not read engine or vocabulary source unless the skill sends you there; if you had to, say for which card and why.
  3. For each card record: whether the draft was right as drafted, the sentences you composed, prelude functions added, whether just card check passed first time and what failed first, hand-written scenarios needed and why, and the lines of the file.
  4. From the efficiency store (just efficiency cards, just efficiency bead), report tokens, dollars and agent minutes per card for these twelve against the median of the 121 cards written before.

Then write the result into the plan's section E8 as a table, with one paragraph on what would most reduce the cost of the next hundred cards. File what you found broken in the tools as children of this milestone.

If a card turns out to need a vocabulary entry, escalate it as the skill says and take the next card of the tier: that is a result, and it is reported.

GPT-6.1 Luna on drafted cards

Route at least six drafted-card trials to GPT-6.1 Luna in Codex through the tier:haiku mapping added by by-ptc7.3.10. Route the rest through the existing tier:sonnet comparison tier. Record the actual model and effort for each group, then compare tokens, minutes per card, first-time just card check pass rate, and stop-rule outcomes.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason.
  • If the count gate still fails after three attempts, follow the run decision on by-ptc7. Leave this bead unresolved and continue independent ready work.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Twelve cards landed or were escalated through the skill. At least six drafted-card trials used GPT-6.1 Luna. Section E8 compares the two groups by actual model and effort and includes per-card costs, first-pass results, stop-rule outcomes, and the earlier median.
