Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Verbs' (fly) and 'Interface checks' (every step shown); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md.

Symptom: in just b3d test b3d:table-crowded/you/2560x1080, Mana Crypt #33 leaves the middle of a ten-card hand for the battlefield at step:6.0.4 (t=11227ms) and is 99% covered by the hand cards to its right when it starts, 61% covered at 11360ms and 48% at 11400ms, so 'every step shown' finds it seen in 9 of 19 frames. Hand cards each face the camera a little nearer the eye than the card before them (table/hands.rs), so a card that leaves for the table (verbs/fly.rs path_of: (Hand, Battlefield) if mine => Path::Straight { rises: false }) starts farther from the eye than the cards after it and moves farther away as it goes. The hit test and agent::hit::covered judge covering by depth. The web interface draws a card in flight above everything (its flying card is on top), so it never fails the audit.

The fault became visible when hands on a script's table were put in the order of the view the script leads to (table::arrival), which moved Mana Crypt from the right end of the hand, where nothing covers it, to the middle. data/beryl3d/test-faults.json lists it (table-crowded/you/2560x1080, step-shown 2).

Code: rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/verbs/fly.rs (path_of, Path::Straight, the segment built from it), src/table/hands.rs (hand, data.depth, step_depth), src/agent/hit.rs (covered), src/agent/steps.rs (sample). Look up how Arena lifts a card out of the hand as it is played (arena-video skill) before choosing a path.

Acceptance criteria

The table-crowded/you/2560x1080 step-shown entry in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json is back to 1 or less, just b3d test --update raises nothing, and a test in beryl_scene shows a card leaving the middle of a ten-card hand for the battlefield seen in at least audit.seen of its frames.
