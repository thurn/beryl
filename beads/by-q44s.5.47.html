Code: rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/tray.rs (rows, boxes, laid_out, tray_boxes, landing, Held), src/prompts/tray.rs (Piles::of, Piles::held), src/prompts/split.rs (the boxes the flat layer draws). Build: Held says which of the three a tray is (and a pick's dealt order, kept from the tray up before as trayOf's before keeps it); port fan and putBack and their landing (the library column) to tray.rs; split.rs draws no boxes for a fan and the library's backs and edgeless boxes for a put-back.

Tests (beryl_inspect tests/all/prompts_split.rs): Stock Up's five cards lie in one fan with no pile boxes; Brainstorm's put-back drop of Mana Drain lands at the recorded place (pile 0 at 0) when let go over the library's top place.
