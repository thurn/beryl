Description

Cards

  • Phantasmal Image: "You may have this creature enter as a copy of any creature on the battlefield, except it's an Illusion in addition to its other types and it has 'When this creature becomes the target of a spell or ability, sacrifice it.'"
  • Quicksilver Gargantuan: "You may have this creature enter as a copy of any creature on the battlefield, except it's 7/7."

Quicksilver Gargantuan stands in for Phyrexian Metamorph, whose Phyrexian mana the vocabulary cannot say.

What they need

Plan: Designs 5, "Entering as a copy".

  • A rewrite of the entering proposal, in the class of CR 616.1c: optionally choose a permanent a filter accepts (a choice, not a target, so hexproof and protection do not stop it), and enter with a copy effect started as part of the arrival, before anything reads the permanent. After it applies, the replacements of entering are collected again against the copy: a copied permanent's own "enters tapped" or "enters with counters" applies (CR 616.1f).
  • Enters triggers of the copied permanent trigger for the copy.
  • A trigger event for becoming the target of a spell or ability (CR 603.2, as targets are chosen and when a target is changed).

Scenarios

Image copying a creature with an enters trigger; Image copying a Gargantuan that copied Mother of Runes (a 7/7 Illusion Human Cleric with Mother's ability and the sacrifice trigger); Image targeted by an Aura spell; Image declining to copy (a 0/0 that dies); two Images of a legendary creature and the legend rule.

Files

The two card files, beryl_rules/src/{replace.rs, event.rs, cast/targets.rs, trigger.rs}, beryl_card/src/{continuous.rs, trigger.rs}.

  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry.
