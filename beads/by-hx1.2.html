Close reason

Sound for every game event and player action, exhaustive by type; ui-test asserts cues for ~0.2 s; pushed d18b985f

Description

Give every UI event and user action a sound, timed with its animation, and add a cheap CI guarantee that it stays that way. Builds on the audio engine from the previous bead in this lane (ui/src/audio/, its cue log window.berylSounds or equivalent, and the by audio manifest). Read the epic first.

The rule (from the user)

"Anything that happens in the UI gets a sound effect, especially user actions."

  • User actions: every click, drag, drop, key shortcut and confirm/cancel: playing or casting a card, choosing targets, paying mana, declaring attackers/blockers, passing priority, choice overlays (pick, toggle, submit), mode/ability choosers, start screen, concede, bug reporter. An illegal or refused action gets a distinct soft "no" sound.
  • Game events: every GameEvent kind in ui/src/generated/protocol.ts (ability, attack, cast, coinFlip, countersAdded/Removed, damage, draw, gainControl, lifeGain, lifeLoss, lose, mana, stepBegins, tap, tokensCreated, untap, win, zoneChange split by destination: battlefield, graveyard, exile, hand, library, stack), plus the screen's own moments: turn banner, a prompt or decision opening, a spell resolving, a spell struck/countered (HOLD.hit), showcase and landing beats, stack arrows, game over (win/loss music stings).
  • Hovering a card in hand may get a very quiet tick; other hovers stay silent.
  • Sounds fire when the eye sees the thing (in step with playback.ts beats and the animations in CardLayer.tsx/Board.tsx), not when the view arrives. Many identical events in one beat play one cue (or a short stagger), never a wall of noise. Opponent actions may be slightly quieter than the player's own.
  • Select from the staged Kenney packs ($BERYL_CACHE/audio-sources/kenney/): Casino Audio for card moves/shuffles/draws, Impact Sounds for damage, Interface/UI Audio for controls and prompts, RPG Audio for magical/spell flavor. Add them through the manifest and just audio; keep total committed audio at most 12 MB.

CI guarantee (+1 s budget, hard cap)

  • Static, zero runtime cost: map GameEvent["kind"] (and zone-change destinations, beat kinds) to sounds with exhaustive Record<...>/satisfies types, so a new event kind fails tsc until it has a sound (or an explicit, commented null).
  • User actions: make the click sound structural (the shared button path from the previous bead) and, if cheap, a unit-level check that no onClick/onPointerDown handler bypasses it (e.g. a small scan inside an existing check lane, not a new process).
  • Runtime: in ui/scripts/interactions.mjs (just ui-test, already in slow CI), assert after the existing driven interactions and played views that each action and each shown event logged its cue. Reuse existing page loads; add no new pages or fixtures unless trivial.
  • Measure just ci (or at least just ui-test and just check) before and after on a quiet machine; record both timings in the bead notes. If the runtime check costs more than 1 s, cut it down or drop it and say so.

Verify

cd ui && npm run typecheck, just ui-test, just ui-shots, just specs --describe <a card> for a sample, just check, just ci.

Acceptance criteria

Every GameEvent kind, zone-change destination and screen moment maps to a sound through exhaustive types that fail tsc when a kind is missing; every user action has a sound; sounds play in step with beats; ui-test asserts logged cues for its driven actions and shown events; before/after timings recorded on the bead show slow CI grew by at most 1 s; just check and just ci pass.
