Result (staging 702bc036). Deleted: EffectCtx and effect.rs with its re-export in beryl_engine; bodies! and conditions! with their facade exports; the public Bodies, Body, Condition and Conditions of layout; Condition::Custom (no custom condition exists, and the contract says how to write the first); Ability::pauses and its may_pause field; TargetSpec::removal, ::counter and the takes field, with Malformed::Declared, which only refused them on data; effect::kicked; beryl_cards/src/tests/effect_data.rs; beryl_cards' allow(clippy::unnecessary_wraps). The escape hatch: CustomBody over Game and Resolution, registered by custom_body; its associated const SAYS (beryl_card::Says: asks, and takes for each target) is the registration that says what data says of itself, carried on beryl_card::Custom and read by Ability::asks and Ability::takes_target. interpret::resolve and Run take the game and the resolution; interpret::finish is the one place a resolution's Full becomes the engine's; Resolution::of_trigger makes a mana ability's resolution.

Counts, just bench-count rollouts corpus: before 388,988,143 (staging 44a1e43e), after 388,980,405 (-0.002%); bar 389,343,295, no bump. just test-rust (corpus and rollouts_never_change included), clippy, just smoke and the fast check pass.

Deviations and decisions. (1) No card has a Rust body, so beryl_rules' tests keep the hatch run: Test Removal (DestroyTarget, cast in many casting tests), Ponder, Binder, LootAndCast, ExileAnOpposingCreature and HumanAttacking are CustomBody types against the library; a new test holds that SAYS is read. Every other test card that had a body is effect data now (about 30 abilities). The tests' with_ctx is with_game(game, resolution), whose closures call the library directly. (2) Adeline's test card that puts a Human in attacking with no combat step stays a body: the real entry (create_attacking_tokens) rightly makes no attacker outside combat (CR 506.3b). (3) The facade exports nothing for bodies: a card file with one would import beryl_rules itself for CustomBody, custom_body and the library, as the bead allows (through the facade or directly). (4) Face and CardDef keep public fields, which beryl_rules' and beryl_session's test cards write as literals; no file under beryl_cards/src writes an Ability, Face or CardDef literal, which is what the acceptance asks. I found no separate construction path kept for body cards beyond Ability::custom, which is the hatch. (5) beryl_session gained a dev-dependency on beryl_card so promotion.rs's test cards are data. (6) rules-coverage.md's two may_pause mentions were corrected in passing.

What beryl_cards takes from the engine besides CardTable and scenario types: card files' definitions import beryl_card only; lib.rs takes CardTable, card::CardDef and coverage; decks.rs takes Decklist, card::{CardDef, Supertypes} and rng::SplitMix64; generate.rs, library/ and tests (all behind the scenarios feature or cfg(test)) take decisions, steps, zones and the engine's entry points.

Allowlists: card-names mechanics is empty (by-w8gv.9.17 emptied it); file-length has no entry under beryl_rules or beryl_engine.
