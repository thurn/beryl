Sage audit of M1a (staging 42920ed). Timings: 31 beads in ~5 h (06:02-10:59 UTC). Executors took 5-56 min each except pixel fidelity: 1.26/1.27/1.28/1.31 at 95/66/78/37 min, plus Warden 1.29 at 22 min (other Wardens 5-17 min). The pixel tail took 2.25 h of the 5 h. No reopened or released beads, no Steward beads, staging never red. Slow CI: 55 runs, all passed, 20 s rising to 46-60 s (budget 300 s). Lanes at the end: rust tests 21 s, ui-shots 24 s (it was 5 s when added), smoke 22 s, clippy 12 s. Fast check in a fresh worktree with an engine and a UI edit: 29 s cold (6.6 s of it the target clone), 8 s warm (budget 60 s). Of the rust test time, about 6 s is test execution (109 engine tests 4 s, fuzz 1.6 s); the rest is doctest binaries. Tools: ui-compare 'battlefield/*' 4.9 s; bench-count in the Lima VM 10 s from a worktree; oracle-setup 216-234 s from scratch and 1 s as a no-op; oracle-smoke 8-16 s. None of them was a bottleneck. Friction in transcripts: ordinary fast-check failures (clippy, prettier, pyre) fixed on retry, and one merge conflict between the parallel card beads (beryl_cards blue/mod.rs). Filed: by-b0q.2.3 (gate, P2, review:inline): a pixel verdict ledger, fixed reviewer packets and a changed-since-verdict report, because reviewer rounds did not converge and M1b repeats the pattern in 3.17-3.20. by-b0q.6 (top epic, P3): the target-cache clone went from 2.1 s to 11.8 s (6.1 GB, ~28k files, including 2.4 GB of target/tauri the rust lane never uses). No Enact changes: the run itself had no tooling failures.
