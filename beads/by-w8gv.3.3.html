Description

Port every card the rollout benchmark's corpus plays (the WU Control mirror, rust/crates/beryl_cards/src/decks.rs WU_CONTROL) whose abilities the vocabulary from the first bead can express, from Rust bodies to effect data, in their existing files. Leave the rest on Rust bodies; milestone 5 ports them.

  • For each ported card, its scenarios and the oracles (just oracle <names>) pass unchanged, and its old body function is deleted (never the card).
  • This is the proof for K1. Parity (plan K11) at the push: just check passes; the frozen save corpus replays (cargo test -p beryl_session --test corpus); rollouts_never_change holds; slow CI's counts lane (just count-gate, bar 405,276,238 instructions, 1% tolerance, rust/crates/beryl_bench/count-gate.toml) passes with no bump to the bar.
  • Run just bench-count before and after and record both counts and the number of cards ported in the close reason and on the milestone epic.
  • If the count gate cannot hold: profile with the count tools in docs/benchmarking.md, fix the interpreter (dispatch shape, inlining, cold paths), and try again, up to three attempts. If it still cannot hold, stop: record the measured counts and profiles on the milestone epic, mark this bead blocked and wait for the user. There is no fallback (run epic, K1): generated scenarios, derived AI knowledge, render-and-diff and the derived oracle exporters all read effect data. Never raise the bar or the tolerance for this.

Acceptance criteria

Handoff: staging 3ff04752 (commits ce826f6d perf, 3ff04752 port).

What changed

  • 20 abilities of 13 WU Control cards, plus the Treasure token's mana ability, are Behavior::Data now, in their own files; each old body fn is deleted. Fully data: Counterspell, Oust, Reprieve, Portable Hole, Damn (both), Staff of the Storyteller (all 3), The Wandering Emperor (all 3). Partly: Teferi Time Raveler (-3), Jace Wielder (+1), Jace the Mind Sculptor (-1), Teferi Hero (-3 and the emblem's trigger), Currency Converter (loot), Jace Vryn's Prodigy (+1 and the emblem's trigger). The six lands, cycling and evoke were data already.
  • Ported abilities drop may_pause and .removal(): both are read from the data.
  • beryl_engine: effect::add_produced_mana and effect::draw_a_card deleted (no card uses them; Treasure is Ability::tap_for_mana with a sacrifice cost, so it takes interpret::mana_ability's direct path). resolve.rs asks the answer source inline() before Ability::asks (a data ability's asks walks its effect; a rollout never needs it: 121k instructions). scene.rs mana_value skips the stack scan for an object not on the stack.
  • tests/effect_data.rs: each Twin now says what its body declared (may_pause, removal) instead of reading it from the card's file, which is data now.

Still on Rust bodies (vocabulary cannot say them): Snapcaster (grant flashback), Trinket Mage (search), Abhorrent Oculus (manifest dread), Beza (compares life totals and hand sizes: no Amount for a life total), Subtlety (owner chooses top or bottom), No More Lies (counter and exile), Thirst for Discovery, Stock Up, Winds of Abandon (search), Fractured Identity (each player other than its controller), Mana Crypt (coin flip, damage), and the remaining abilities of the partly ported cards (transform, cast permissions, emblems, delayed trigger, untap, win the game, choices among objects, shuffles). Mana Tithe and Get Lost are outside the corpus and left for M5, though their data sits ready in effect_data.rs.

Verification

  • A temporary test dumped every scenario's events and state hash at each action (1,633 lines over all cards) before and after: byte-identical. Not committed.
  • cargo test -p beryl_cards, -p beryl_engine, -p beryl_session --test corpus (frozen saves replay), beryl_bench rollouts_never_change: pass. just oracle on the ported cards: XMage 49 pass 0 fail, Forge 55 pass 0 fail. just check passed at the push.
  • Count gate (just count-gate, the rollouts::corpus subject): 405,672,788 before, 406,974,489 with the port alone, 406,861,374 as pushed: +0.39% against the bar of 405,276,238, +0.29% on before. No bump.

Tried and dropped: skipping Run::remember for an effect's last instruction and guarding Run::unstack by zone saved 45k together (0.01%) for a new flag in Run; not worth it.

What a reviewer should check

  • Each ported effect against the card's text, especially LibraryPosition::FromTop(2) for Oust (body said under 1) and FromTop(3) for Teferi Hero (under 2), and Duration::UntilYourNextTurn on Jace's +1.
  • That the Treasure's ability resolves through the direct AddMana path with the same Mana event (source and amount) as its body gave.
