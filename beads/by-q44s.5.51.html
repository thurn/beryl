Close reason

Phone sheet submenus replace rows with Back; on staging at e512f54e2

Description

On the phone layout, Beryl 3D's manual table context menu opens as a bottom sheet (beryl_scene src/screens/table.rs bottom_sheet), but a row with a submenu still opens it beside the menu (Anchor::Submenu, placed by place_menus in src/layout/menu.rs). The sheet is as wide as the window, so the submenu lands over the sheet. The web, and data/ui-conventions.json's 'Context menu' rule, replace the sheet's rows with the submenu's rows and offer a Back row (ui/src/table/Menu.tsx, its page state). Build that for the sheet: pressing a submenu row on the phone shows its rows in the sheet with Back first; Back shows the menu again. Rows keep their Press::Menu numbering (widgets/menu.rs menu). Test in beryl_scene tests/all/manual.rs with client_in(view, 393.0, 759.0), as a_long_press_on_a_phone_opens_a_bottom_sheet_with_the_card does.

Acceptance criteria

A test opens the phone sheet on a permanent, presses a submenu row and finds its rows and a Back row in the sheet and no Anchor::Submenu list; Back shows the first rows again; just b3d lane passes.
