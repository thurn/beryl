Close reason

The stage plays six effects from data on its clock, under or over a card, on desktop and phone; appear, landing, vanish and flip use them in the verb suite from both seats; flip passed the motion review, appear and fly pass every effect line with their one fix each on by-w8gv.21.47, vanish did not pass in three rounds (by-w8gv.21.48); staging 9e697fdf

Description

The stage has no effect layer (by-w8gv.21.20's notes, gap 2): nothing can glow, flash, dissolve or cover a card, so appear, vanish, flip and every landing are bare. The user (2026-11-11): animations are not only about duration, and Beryl's cards need their own aesthetic judgement (policy bead filed beside this one).

Build an effect layer in ui/src/stage: effects attached to an object node or a place, played on the stage's clock (clock.ts, playback.ts, timing.ts), drawn at the right depth relative to cards (stage.ts, dom.ts, pose.ts), on desktop and phone, with reduced motion honoured. Effects are data in data/ui-conventions.json beside the verbs that use them, in Beryl's skin (stage.css and the existing colors and frames, never Arena's art).

First uses, designed for Beryl's full cards and reviewed by the motion review (the review bead filed beside this one):

  • appear: a token or a card that appears in place arrives with a glow or settle, so it is noticed;
  • landing: a card that finishes a flight settles (a flash, shadow drop or both) so arrival reads;
  • vanish: a permanent that leaves breaks up or dissolves in place (today's ui/src/game/dissolve.ts is the list of cases, not the design), a resolved spell leaves the stack visibly, and a vanish that takes time gets the end hook it lacks (by-w8gv.21.20 gap 5);
  • flip: a transform or face-down card turning face up is covered or turned, then shows its new face.

Each first use plays in the verb suite (ui/scripts/verbs.mjs, ui/verbs.json) from both seats and both layouts, and passes the motion review before this bead closes. Script snapshots in Rust for any new step kind (beryl_session's script builder, by-w8gv.21.9).

Read first: by-w8gv.21.13, .14, .15, .16 handoffs; by-w8gv.21.20's comments; docs/ui-conventions.md.

Acceptance criteria

The stage draws effects from data on its clock at the right depth on both layouts; appear, landing, vanish and flip use them from both seats in the verb suite; each passed the motion review (verdict on this bead); just check passes.

Decisions and deviations (by-w8gv.21.44, the effect layer).

Built. An effect is on one node, begun by a step as it starts or ends on what the step is about (tracks.ts subjectOf), and plays on the stage's clock for its own time or, with no duration, for as long as its step shows its card (a vanish: until GONE of it). It moves nothing, holds up no step (Shown.busy and idle() do not count it) and outlives its step. The model is ui/src/stage/effects.ts (Effects: begin, on, gone, write, clear); the stage asks stage.effects(act, table) as it asks stage.timings, and stage.effect(name) reads what is under way as stage.pose reads a pose. The surface draws each as an element of its own inside the node, under or over the face (the node is a stacking context, so under is over what lies beneath the card), and the stage writes how far through it is on the node each tick as a custom property named as the effect; each look is a rule of stage.css read from that number. No CSS animation, so films and the stepped capture see them.

Data. data/ui-conventions.json has a third closed list, effects (name, shows, layer, values of duration, delay and curve), and each verb lists the effects its steps begin (effect, when, and a case or the zone its card lands in); a case with effects of its own begins none of its verb's. timing.ts effectsOf reads them; by conventions checks and renders them (a new Effects section of docs/ui-conventions.md). Six looks: glow (token appears; bounced), settle (any flight that lands on the battlefield), dissolve (destroyed, countered, sacrificed, and any uncased vanish), dim (resolved instant or sorcery), blanch (exiled), cover (flip).

Deviations from the bead's text.

  • No effect at a place: every first use has a node for the whole of its effect, so a place form would have been code nothing calls. What by-w8gv.21.21 wants at a place (a strike's impact, a life total's burst) adds it with its first use: Effects' on is the one thing to widen.
  • No Rust: no new step kind. Which step begins which effect is the design language's, by verb and case, as timing is.
  • Reduced motion: no step begins an effect (mount.ts), as every step snaps.
  • Time passed over by skip() begins none; a reset clears them; settle() does not wait for one.
  • The vanish track's fade is no longer even: it holds and goes late (tracks.ts dissolve), so the effect is what is seen. A flip's size now eases and peaks at 1.2x, not 1.3x (the reviewer's fix and note). A counterspell waits until what it countered is gone (timing.ts, from GONE, per the settled entry "A countered spell").
  • stage.ts was at the 800-line limit: Timing and SNAP moved to tracks.ts (re-exported), sameFace and sameDress to layout.ts.
