Session stops only where the human could act: no end-step halt, own triggers and own combat declarations auto-pass; pass caption matches; staging 4fbb5aa

Description

Follows 3bf9060 (smart stops) and by-isv.12 (full control).

  • Opponent's end step: seed 101 vs Expert: every opponent turn (1, 3, 5, 7, ...) stops at "Continue / To Your Turn"; the only option is Pass and the decision is halt: true (D1/012-g1-t1-oppend.png, D1/050-g1-t5-pb-0.png). About 15 extra clicks in a 30-turn game; Arena does not stop here.
  • Own trigger: your own Mana Crypt upkeep trigger stops you ("Resolve / Mana Crypt") because a flash card was castable (turn 24, D1/349-g1-t24.png).
  • Every combat step: with The Wandering Emperor in hand you stop at declare attackers, declare blockers and later steps (turn 24, D1/375-g1-attack-after.png, D1/381-g1-enter-pass-after.png).

Decide stops the way a strong player would: stop at the opponent's end step only when the player holds something they could and plausibly would use there; with instant-speed cards, stop where Arena's default stops do (and at the opponent's actions), not at every step. Full control keeps every stop.

Found by the by-isv.13 ui-review on staging 4c67340 (WU Control mirror both seats; seeds and opponents per item). Screenshots: D1 = ~/Library/Caches/beryl/ui-live/by-isv.13/20261105-224254 (1600x900), D2 = ~/Library/Caches/beryl/ui-live/by-isv.13/20261105-233541 (1280x800). Reproduce each item first with just ui-live (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md) or the named fixture; drop any that does not reproduce and say so in the close reason.

Design rules (epic by-isv): follow the skill's design philosophy (one gesture for the common case, the client does the bookkeeping, ask once in context, Arena first, show don't make them infer, few words) and add no standing words to the table. Every acceptance check is an agent's: just ui-test, just ui-shots, Rust tests or ui-live probes.

Acceptance criteria

  • beryl_session tests: with default stops and nothing castable at instant speed, no stop at the opponent's end step; holding a flash creature does not stop at each combat step of your own turn nor at your own trigger with nothing to respond with; full control still stops at each.
  • A ui-live or ui-test run counts passes per turn cycle with an empty hand of instants: 1.

Reproduced all three in new beryl_session tests (tests/stop_places.rs fail on the old game.rs). Fix is session-level only; the engine's smart stops, shared with search, are unchanged. (1) Removed the session's halt at the opponent's end step; the engine still stops there when the human has a legal action (e.g. a flash card). Halt button now always End Turn; dropped the game-halt-end-step shot; opponent-turn ui-test now runs from the opponent's turn straight to End Turn and asserts one pass per cycle with no instant in hand. (2) own_top treats no answer yet in the step as an empty stack (stacks are empty as steps begin), so the human's own upkeep trigger auto-passes. (3) New quiet_combat auto-pass with an empty stack: the attacker after their own attack declaration, and either player in declare blockers when no creature blocks. Deviation: the defender keeps priority after declaring blockers (combat-trick window; the combat-blockers fixture relies on it), and the attacker keeps it once blockers exist.
