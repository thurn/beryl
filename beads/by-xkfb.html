What the player wants: on the put-back screen (Jace, the Mind Sculptor 0 / Brainstorm: library column at left, top card lowest, hand fanned at right), dragging a card to a new height on the library should put it there. Acceptance: (1) a card under the top one, grabbed by its visible name bar and let go over the top card's place, becomes the top card; (2) the top card let go over the place under it goes under; (3) the result does not depend on where on the card it was grabbed; (4) dropping a hand card on the library still lands at the nearest place; both desktop and phone layouts.

Cause: tray.ts landing() compared the POINTER with the middles of the library's cards (carried card included). The cards cascade only 0.165 card heights apart, so each drop position was a ~40px strip and depended on the grab point: Plains (under Island, only its name bar shows) dragged down onto Island's place left the pointer above Island's middle -> 'no move'. That is the screenshot's state.

Fix (25cb2a1e): prompt.ts passes the carried card's pose to lands()/landing(); on the library the card takes the column place nearest its own middle once it is in the column (Laid.column gives the places). Other trays unchanged.

Tests: new stage-tray.test.mjs case 'a card on the library goes to the place nearest where it is let go' (fails on old code: 11 pass/1 fail; passes now), plus the prompt test asserts the pose is passed. npm test 233/233, typecheck clean. Shape suite's carry() now aims the card's middle at the target place (placeOnLibrary); just ui-test shape:split passes on yours/theirs x desktop/phone with takeBack skipped locally.
