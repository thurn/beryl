Measure slow CI's browser fallback rate after coverage refresh

  • Description
  • Acceptance criteria
  • Changelog

Description

What to measure

Change-aware slow CI (a5f80bd9, 2026-11-13 14:08 UTC) runs only the ui-test and shots checks a change can break, but falls back to every check of a lane when the coverage file cannot place a changed ui/src line. by-gdn3.13 made each passing hourly full sweep refresh coverage in the background (coverage.refresh in tools/src/beryl_tools/coverage.py, called from slow_ci.slow). This bead checks whether that worked. It is reported-only: it gates nothing.

How

Run no earlier than 2026-11-14 15:00 UTC. From $BERYL_CACHE/ci/runs.jsonl, take records with script: ci from host JARVIS over the 24 h since 2026-11-13 15:00 UTC (or the latest 24 h). Group by commit. For each commit and lane in (ui-test, shots) with any non-skip record, count it as a browser-lane run; count it as a fallback when any record's chosen starts with every: (exclude runs whose chosen is all, the hourly full sweep, and report those separately). Also take the median of each commit's summed seconds (check-seconds per run). Check $BERYL_CACHE/ci/coverage/latest.json times and refresh.log to confirm coverage refreshed at least hourly while staging moved.

Record

Comment the fallback fraction, the reasons in the every: labels, the median check-seconds against the baselines (3,692 s on by-gdn3.6, 4,777 s over the 24 h before 2026-11-13 14:30 UTC on by-gdn3.13) and the coverage refresh times on this bead, by-gdn3.13 and by-gdn3.6.

Acceptance criteria

The fallback fraction, the median check-seconds per slow-CI run and the coverage refresh cadence over a day are recorded on this bead, by-gdn3.13 and by-gdn3.6.
