Description

What to build

Plan: Designs G, "Restarting". CR 728 is the procedure; read all of it and the card's rulings before designing.

  • State::restart in the kernel (new.rs), built on State::deal: a new state from every card each player owns, wherever it was, including phased-out permanents and cards in exile. Tokens and copies are gone. The player who controlled the ability takes the first turn. The carried cards (for Karn: the non-Aura permanent cards exiled with him) start in exile and are put onto the battlefield under one player's control before the first turn, after mulligans, as the rule says. Nothing else survives: no effects, memos, trackers, counters, knowledge or turn count. Format supplies life and hand size.
  • The restart happens inside a resolution. The resolving ability and its replay record belong to the game that ended, so the resolution must end by replacing the state, and nothing of the body runs after. The engine then asks the new game's first question (the mulligans; nobody chooses play or draw).
  • Effect::RestartGame with what is carried, its rendering and mutation neighbour. "Exiled with Karn" across his three abilities is the linked-ability memo (Memo::ExiledWith, linked.rs).
  • Determinism and saves: the new deal draws from the game's own random stream, so a save replays through a restart; a checkpoint taken after one rebuilds (beryl_session/src/checkpoint.rs). The turn limit counts from the restart.
  • The session: one update whose script is the table's existing reset.
  • Karn Liberated, all three abilities.

Write each card with the card-writing skill. Read its rulings first (just card rulings <name>): the card joins data/rulings-ledger.json with every ruling a scenario or a reason, and joins the keystone fuzz pool (beryl_cards/src/decks.rs). Where XMage or Forge cannot play the card, its scenarios are reported as unsupported and the ledger is the outside check, so be thorough with it.

Scenarios: +4 and −3 exile cards of both players, then −14: the game restarts, the exiled permanent cards enter under Karn's controller, an exiled Aura and an exiled instant do not, hands are seven new cards, life is 20, Karn's controller goes first and draws on turn 1 as the rule says or does not; a token exiled with −3 does not come back; a card an opponent owns enters under your control and stays theirs.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Karn Liberated passes just card check and is in the ledger and the pool. A save through a restart replays under each perturbation.
