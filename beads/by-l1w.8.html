Delivered 6c02a74. Stack arrows (gold source->item, cyan item->target) are now routed (ui/src/components/route.ts): candidates are cubics from ports on every side of each end (3 per card side, more on the stack item's long sides, 12 round the life orb's oval, marked data-oval), leaving and arriving square to the edge so heads point into the card. Cost: length + pixels over other cards (AVOID: card slots, stack items, life orbs, game log) + hugging/crossing its own ends or leaving the table + under 110 px (adjacent cards loop rather than draw a stub) + running alongside/crossing earlier arrows + sharing an end point. All orders of up to 3 arrows are tried (greedy gold-first made the cyan cross it in arrow-ability). Perf: full search 6-10 ms, run once when the table is still; while cards glide each arrow retraces its chosen ports (~0.01 ms), so no per-frame search and no flicker, and the resting route doesn't depend on the glide. Combat links/planeswalker hairline keep their explicit geometry (sides given => unrouted) and the same tones/markers. New fixture conditions in beryl_cli (targeted spell / player targeted / ability targeting beside its source) and fixtures arrow-spell (Oust on Adeline beside the stack), arrow-ability (Wandering Emperor on neighbour Malcolm), arrow-player (Elite Spellbinder on you), with shots game-arrow-*; ui-test checks each arrow ties the right ids, meets each element's edge, is >=100 px, crosses other cards at <=6/61 points, ends >=24 px apart (all three fail on the old facing arrows).
