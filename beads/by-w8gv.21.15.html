Decisions and deviations (phone layout of the stage; sounds follow steps)

Stage core (stage.ts, pose.ts):

  • stage.follow(listener) tells each step's start and end inside the tick, in stage-time order, each with its own time (run.from / run.to), the table as the step found it and its beat's earlier steps. A skip tells nothing; a reset drops what played, so a begun step may get no end.
  • stage.relayout(layout) takes another layout. The page's stage starts on the desktop's; each board sets its own as it mounts (StageBoard: desktop; PhoneStageBoard: phone(viewport) once measured, and again when the board resizes).
  • Deviation from by-w8gv.21.13/.14: only a step's own card or ability (fly's object, emerge's ability) is drawn aloft; what merely makes room glides at its resting depth. Found on the phone, where every stack change re-lays the rows: the flying card was drawn under the creature row, both being aloft and the row resting deeper. Doubling ALOFT for the subject was rejected: the desktop's HUD has z-indexes from 1000 to 1500.

Phone layout (stage/phone.ts over mobile/layout.ts):

  • Every object rests somewhere so any step can move it: battlefield, your hand (the fan's turn about the bottom center carried to the pose's top-left corner) and stack as mobile/layout.ts places them; the opponent's hand as backs at the top edge (now stage nodes, where the old board drew chrome); the top card of each library and graveyard as a node on its pile, the rest hidden beneath; exile hidden under its owner's graveyard (the phone has no exile pile); no emblems (the phone draws none), so an emblem's ability has no source and appears on the stack.
  • Depths are banded under ALOFT (piles 1, backs 2+, permanents 10+rank, hand 600+, stack 700+): mobile/layout.ts's z runs to 20000, which would read as flying.
  • The old PhoneBoard now shares mobile/faces.tsx and mobile/chrome.tsx with the stage's board; it draws the same DOM but for --badge, which moved from the card's wrapper to the card's slot.
  • Under ?phone&stage the strips, piles' edges, orbs, phase icon and PhoneDock are chrome (life, piles and phase from the stage's table, the dock from the played view). No glow, no card pick and no manual waterbend payment yet: pointer input and marks are by-w8gv.21.16's and the prompt beads'. A touch on the board skips what is playing.

Sound (audio/steps.ts):

  • One map from Verb to cues, exhaustive at compile time. A cue sounds at its step's start, except a fly's place (as the card lands) and a damage streak's hit (as it lands), which sound at the step's end. A strike's hit is at its start: the stage has no strike track yet and applies the damage there.
  • A new turn rings on announce(turn); set(turn) ticks only for a step within the same turn. Life lost in a beat that has a strike or damage streak at that player is silent (the hit says it). An attacker's tap asks for tap and attack. A fly within the battlefield that changes controller is control.
  • A sound asked more than once at one stage time plays once (four lands tapped at once are one tap), as beatSounds played each once a view. With reduced motion every step of a script is at one time, so each sound plays once a script.
  • play()'s log entry gained delay, so the check can assert none is used; the old path still logs place with its 450.

Verb suite (ui/scripts/verbs.mjs, ui/verbs.json):

  • layouts: desktop and phone. The phone's windows are the sizes ui/shots.json has with ?phone (393x759, 375x647, 430x850): both ticks at the first, 100 ms alone at the others.
  • LAYOUTS.reflows says what differs on a phone: the rows are laid out again as the stack comes and goes and as a row fills, so a lift's source may glide aside (it still begins in the source's middle and the source ends at rest), and an earlier token may shift by less than a card any way.
  • Every run of every check holds the page's sounds to its steps: the log equals what the steps' edges ask for, at their stage times, with no delay; fly, emerge and appear also assert their own sound at an absolute time, and vanish that an ability's end asks for none. Two mutations were tried and caught: place at the fly's start, and a 450 ms delay.
  • 16 checks take about 12 s wall with the server (8 took about the same before: the suite is bound by its slowest check, emerge). The fast check cut the new ones by its box (no coverage record yet); I ran them and the phone-stage-respond shot by hand.
