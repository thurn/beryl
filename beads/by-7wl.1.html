Description

Part of epic by-7wl (read it first). Sets δ, the anchor's margin, before any game is played. No games are played here.

Build

A beryl_cli subcommand (or a test-only tool) that rebuilds a long-think run's games for one entrant and asks greedy at each of its searched decisions:

  • Seeds and players come from <run>/games.jsonl. Every decision's answer is in <run>/logs/<game>.jsonl (game::Line), which is what a resumed game replays through, so the positions can be rebuilt exactly as the arena built them (see rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/longthink/game.rs).
  • At each searched decision of the entrant, compute greedy's choice (RolloutPolicy::choose, from that seat's observation, at the entrant's knowledge tier) and join it with the decision's line in decisions.jsonl (root visits and mean per candidate).

Report

For pimc18/omniscient/rollout/tuned@10M and pimc72/hidden/rollout/tuned@30M in run-003, and δ in {0, 0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.04}:

  • the share of searched decisions where greedy's choice differs from the search's;
  • the share where the anchor would switch to greedy's choice: the pooled mean gap is under max(2·SE, δ). decisions.jsonl pools the trees, so take SE from the visit counts (binomial) and say that it approximates the per-tree test;
  • per game, the sum of the search's own estimated gaps over the switched decisions: the search's estimate of what the anchor costs;
  • a dozen switched decisions as transcript lines, for a human to read.

Recommend δ: the largest whose estimated cost is at most 0.01 win probability a game. Put the report's numbers and the recommendation in this bead's notes and close reason; commit the tool, not the report.

If the anchored root choice (by-7wl.2) has already landed, set its δ constant to the recommendation in the same change, with a comment citing this measurement. Otherwise that bead reads it from here.

Notes

2026-11-07 results (beryl_cli anchor-replay, run-003, pimc18/omniscient/rollout/tuned@10M, 42 games, 3,717 searched decisions; 'fresh' = re-searched from a new seed with the budget split between the two candidates, LongSearch::duel, free of the most-visited bias):

  • Greedy's answer differs from the search's choice at 64% of searched decisions.
  • Anchoring every decision: switches 37% of decisions (33 a game) at δ 0.01; fresh cost 0.068 ± 0.054 win probability a game. At priority decisions (20 a game) the search's choice leads greedy's by +0.0032 ± 0.0009 fresh: real.
  • Anchoring all non-priority decisions plus casts-over-pass: fresh cost 0.056 ± 0.030 a game at 0.01 (targets +0.0022, exile costs +0.0032, choose object +0.0015 each). Too costly.
  • Anchoring only priority decisions where the search casts a spell and greedy passes (beryl_search::guarded): 0.9% of decisions differ; at δ 0.02 it switches 0.4 a game for a fresh cost of 0.0047 ± 0.0013 a game; at 0.04, 0.7 a game for 0.013. Both of the leader's arena Ousts are in this class.
  • Decision: the anchor guards only casts over pass, δ = ANCHOR_MARGIN = 0.02 (largest within 0.01 a game). Landed with the anchor code.
