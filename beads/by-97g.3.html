Description

Part of the ladder epic (read it and docs/ai-strategy.md §5 first). The blunder suite: a regression gate from every blunder a human has caught.

Where

  • docs/incidents/*.replay.json: the positions. docs/incidents/*.md say which turn and decision each blunder was.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/review.rs: replays a game and rebuilds the agent from its seed (--agent recorded); anchor_replay.rs's walk steps a replay decision by decision.
  • rust/crates/beryl_session/src/replay.rs, UNDO_POINTS, with_stops.
  • data/blunders.json (new): the list. rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/blunders.rs (new); main.rs; the justfile and tools/src/beryl_tools for just blunders.

Build

  • An entry: the incident's name, its replay file, the decision (turn and the decision's index within the turn, or its log index), forbidden choices and an optional required choice, each named as the decision's candidates are labeled in just review. A multi-decision entry (an attack with two creatures) lists its decisions in order. The first four entries, from §5: Deep Seer turn 14 (Oust forbidden); Snapcaster turn 12 (Snapcaster Mage forbidden at the postcombat priority); Snapcaster turn 16 (attack Jace, Wielder of Mysteries with Snapcaster Mage and the Spirit token required); Trinket Mage turn 7 (Mana Crypt required).
  • beryl_cli blunders [agent] [--work 3M,30M] [--seeds 5] [--json FILE]: for each entry, replay to the position, build agent (default the champion: pimc72/hidden/rollout/tuned/anchor) at each budget from each of five seeds, search the decision, and record its choice. An entry fails on any forbidden play or any missed required one. Print a table (entry, budget, passes of 5, the choices made) and a JSON summary.
  • The champion fails some entries today: it made these plays. The gate for a promotion is comparative: a challenger must fail no entry the champion passes, and a plausibility promotion must pass every entry it claims to fix. --json from two runs is what the rung beads compare.
  • Drift: an entry whose replay no longer reproduces its position fails loudly with the incident's name, never skips. §5 says how such an entry is rebuilt as a scenario or retired.
  • Cost: four entries, five seeds, two budgets, about six minutes on a quiet machine at utility QoS.

Tests

The schema parses the four entries. A scripted agent (as boards.rs scripts seats) playing a forbidden choice fails its entry, one playing the required choice passes. A replay that drifts reports its incident. just test-rust, clippy pedantic.
