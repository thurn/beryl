Close reason

Card-record effects (settle, dissolve, dim, blanch, cover), the settle burst, shake and the shader's dissolve edge; staging 2a67fdf05

Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Effects' (the table, 'The broken edge of a dissolve', 'shake moves the picture'), 'The frame' (the card material's params; a card with dissolve, whiten or an opacity under 255 is drawn with the blended records) and 'The Baseline tier'; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/effects/card.rs (the stub look(runs, on, now, look)), src/effects/shake.rs (the stub offset(runs, now)), src/table/scene.rs (Look, Table::write, which today draws every card in Band::Opaque), src/frame/draw.rs (Draw::card's dissolve and whiten); beryl_render src/shader.wgsl (shade_card, which reads whiten and ignores dissolve; the vertex stage vs), src/renderer.rs (the uniforms), tests/render.rs. Web: ui/src/stage/effects.ts, ui/src/stage/effects.css (the keyframes of each), ui/src/design/dissolve-mask.svg (the stippled edge of a dissolve), ui/src/stage/flare.ts (SETTLE), and each effect's shows text in data/ui-conventions.json.

Build, in beryl_scene:

  • effects::card::look, by the run's eased progress p: settle: the card is as it is for two sixtieths of a second, then whiten rises to 1 by 0.3 of the run and falls back to 0 by its end. dissolve: the tint darkens to 0.35 of its color by 0.3, holds, and dissolve runs from 0 to 1 over the rest. dim: the tint darkens to 0.35 and the opacity falls to 0 over the run. blanch: whiten rises to 1 by 0.5, then the opacity falls to 0. cover: whiten rises to 1 by 0.4, holds to 0.6 and falls to 0; the face layer the card is drawn with changes to the new record's at 0.5. Put the shares under effects.<name> in space.json, set from the keyframes in effects.css.
  • settle also draws its burst: one Draw::glow with sprites::BURST, one and a half cards wide, written from effects::card so that settle is whole in one place.
  • Table::write draws a card whose look has dissolve or whiten above 0, or an opacity under 1, with the blended records: Band::TableMarks when its height is under 1 millimetre and Band::RaisedMarks otherwise. Every other card stays opaque.
  • effects::shake::offset: while a shake run lasts, an offset in physical pixels that goes side to side and a little up and down, twice, the second swing smaller, and is zero at its start and end: x = effects.shake.x x sin(4 pi p) x (1 - p), y = effects.shake.y x sin(8 pi p) x (1 - p), with 6 and 2 logical points times the window's scale. Show::frame passes it to FrameBuilder::shake.

Build, in beryl_render:

  • shade_card breaks the card up from the top down for params.x above 0: a pixel is dropped where a stipple computed from the card's own texture coordinates (a hash of the coordinates on a 24 by 34 grid, plus the coordinate down the card) is under the threshold, with a soft edge 0.05 wide. No second texture and no discard in the opaque pipeline: a dissolving card is among the blended records, and the function returns zero opacity.
  • The renderer adds Frame::shake, as a uniform, to the screen position of every record drawn with the depth test, and to nothing in the flat layer (draws[layer..]).

Do not build: the one-record effects (by-q44s.3.23); ash, vortex or bloom (milestone 6).

Tests. beryl_scene/tests/effects_card.rs: for each of the five, the card's record has the stated params and tint at the start, at each named share and at the end of the run; a dissolving card's record is at or after Frame::opaque, and the same card before the run is before it; a covered card's layer changes at half the run; Frame::shake is zero outside a shake run, non-zero inside it, and no resolved element's corners differ between a frame with shake and without. beryl_render/tests/render.rs: a card with dissolve 0.5 has a transparent pixel near its top and an opaque one near its bottom; a frame with shake (8, 0) draws a card's left edge 8 pixels to the right and a flat-layer panel where it was. tests/shader_cost.rs: lower or raise materials.card with the counts in the commit body.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_render; just b3d gate; just b3d gate --gpu; just b3d lane.
