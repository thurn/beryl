Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Effects', 'The frame' (the streak material and band 4) and 'Verbs'; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/verbs/streak.rs and src/light/streaks.rs (stubs), src/light/mod.rs (StreakRun), src/frame/draw.rs (Draw::streak, whose params are the head and the tail length; sprites::COMET), Show::reach. Web: ui/src/stage/streaks.ts (cometOf: a bright head on a low arc from where its source lay as the step began to where its target is now, with a tail that stretches out of the source and follows; StreakShape, Streaks; a streak is drawn for as long as its step plays and no longer, and is no span), ui/src/stage/flare.ts (STREAK: the ribbon of mana out of each land, in the color of the mana), ui/src/stage/light.ts (TONES: the colors), ui/src/stage/lines.ts (reachOfPool, reachOfLife, reachOfCard: where a streak leaves and lands). Cases: mana, damage.

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build:

  • verbs/streak.rs pushes a StreakRun for the step (StepOut::streak) and, where the act carries the struck object's record, replaces the table's record at the step's end. It moves nothing.
  • light::streaks::write draws each streak under way: Draw::streak records with the COMET sprite along the arc from the source's place to the target's, both read from Scene::reach on this frame, so that a target that moves is followed. The head is at the eased progress of the run and the tail's length follows cometOf. A streak with no source on the table starts at its target's player's anchor for mana out of a pool (Subject::Player), and is not drawn when the act has no from at all. Mana is tinted by its color and damage is the web's damage tone; add the six colors to src/tokens.rs from TONES. Use 8 records for a streak, placed evenly along the part of the arc between its tail and its head, in Band::Glows.
  • Show::wake is Now while a streak runs. amount draws one streak, as the web does.

Do not build: the glow on the source or the flash and impact where it lands, which are the step's effects (by-q44s.3.23); shade_streak in the renderer (by-q44s.3.22: until it lands the records are written and drawn as nothing).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/verbs_streak.rs, on views and scripts built by hand with testing::ViewBuilder, ScriptBuilder and testing::timing: for each case of the verb in data/ui-conventions.json (mana, damage), a script with that case's duration and delay plays to its end with every object at its placement (agent::steps::rest_equals_placement finds nothing), no_jumps finds nothing, and step_times finds nothing. Streak records are in the frame at every time inside the step and at none outside it; the head's record is nearer the target at each later time; a streak to a card that is in flight ends on the card's pose in that frame; mana from a pool starts at the player's anchor; a frame with 20 streaks allocates nothing.

When it is done: set BUILT = true in the verb's file, so that Client::next_unbuilt stops reporting it; run just b3d test --update and just b3d timeline-parity --update if those commands exist on staging by then, and commit the lowered lists.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d check verbs-zones; just b3d gate; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

The tests pass; Client::next_unbuilt does not report streak; just b3d gate passes, the commit body giving old and new counts if a limit rose.
