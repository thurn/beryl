Description

Symptom: on origin/staging (d892bc581 and later, checked with by-q44s.9.44's commit reverted), three Beryl 3D tests fail on every run, so every Beryl 3D push whose fast check selects beryl_scene or beryl_inspect fails beryl3d-tests:

  • beryl_scene inspect::a_graveyard_of_thirty_opens_in_the_viewer_scrolls_and_flies_back_when_closed (tests/all/inspect.rs): the helper faults(page, rules) calls page.check(), which panics on any fault, before it filters to rules. The new text-overlap check (17366ae4e) reports the pile viewer's heading over the opponent's name plate: text of text "Your graveyard" #65580 (431,48 410x70) overlaps player "Opponent 20" #65541 (774,82 51x46) at t=1000ms. Removing the page.check() line makes the helper do what its doc says and the test pass; the overlap itself belongs with by-q44s.9.52.
  • beryl_inspect differential::the_check_times_find_every_planted_fault (tests/all/differential.rs:212): "only every tick finds text-overlap", e.g. b3d:bloodghast/it-attacks-the-turn-it-returns-while-an-opponent-has-10-or-less-life/you/1920x1080 at t=1383ms, text "-2" over text "Combat". The check times (driver/check.rs) do not include the moments a life-change float crosses a notice, so either text-overlap needs check times of its own or the differential test must account for it.
  • beryl_inspect runner::the_run_log_agrees_with_the_allowance_comparison (tests/all/runner.rs:313): ("fail", 1) where ("pass", 0) was expected, probably the same new text-overlap faults missing from its allowance fixture.
