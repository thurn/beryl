Design and measurements: one fan cannot hold 60 cards with 24-point press targets in a 1440-point window (60 x 24 = 1440 before any margin), and a turned card's slanted strip needs 24*(1+tan turn) points to hold an axis-aligned square. hand() now widens strips by the turn plus 8 spare points and lays an overfull viewer's hand in several fans (two for 60 cards in every desktop window), listed under 'Not seen in Arena' (not a numbered exception). The reachable check now judges pointer-raised hand cards at their resting box (Frame::lifted), the same rule as the pointer's hit test. stress-hand: 0 faults in all four windows; test-faults entries removed. Gates: just b3d gate green after --update; --gpu: stress-hand 1.00132 screens (unchanged), stress-verbs rose 1.456404 -> 1.456491 (its crowded hand spaces differently), limit raised by hand. --counts: stress-hand 928,718 worst / 55.7M total (within 1%); stress-255 (+9.4%), stress-1024 (+11.3%), stress-verbs (+6.7%) and verbs-zones (+3.7%) worst frames are over. stress-255 and stress-1024 have no hand cards and this change adds no work to their frames, so I take those as staging drift; a base-commit count run was queued behind the bench VM and stopped unrun.
