Close reason

Show compiles a script that arrives with nothing playing over frames via Client::prepare; stress-255/1024 worst frames -44%/-33%; verbs-zones left to by-q44s.4.11

Description

Symptom: the worst frame of verbs-zones, stress-255, stress-1024 and stress-verbs is still the frame whose Client::handle takes a view with a script, because Show::push (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/play/mod.rs, push_timed and compile) compiles the whole script there. On staging, data/beryl3d/gates.json holds worst_frame_instructions at 4,816,173 (verbs-zones frame 980, 34 steps), 3,267,119 (stress-255 frame 30, 40 steps), 9,283,180 (stress-1024 frame 30) and 2,638,123 (stress-verbs frame 30, 106 steps).

What exists (by-q44s.4.3): play::compile::Compiling (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/play/compile.rs) compiles a script one step at a time, in order. advance(until, least) compiles every step that begins by until on the script's clock, then every step that begins before the latest segment under way at until ends (horizon), then more steps until it has done least (STEPS_PER_ADVANCE = 4). A step changes a segment an earlier step made only through retarget in src/play/step.rs (a card still on its way whose landing place moves), and only a segment that ends after the step begins, so these rules leave every pose up to until as the whole compile has it. tests/all/compile_in_parts.rs holds this for row flights and for draws, at least 0 and 4 steps a frame (play::parts::compile_in_parts, driver feature). compile() now runs a Compiling to its end, and Show still compiles each script whole.

What to build: have Show keep the playing script's Compiling and advance it over frames.

  1. Ownership. Motion<'a> borrows the before and after views, the timings and the Table. To keep a Compiling across frames, pass &Table into Motion::step, placement_of and place (4 uses of self.table in step.rs and step/placement.rs) instead of storing it, and keep the views alive beside it: store updates as Rc<GameUpdate> in Show (shown, Waiting::update, Playing::update) and timings as Rc<[StepTiming]>, and hold them with Motion in a self_cell (a small crate built on macro_rules!; its Cargo.toml comment says it replaces a hand-written self-referential struct, which unsafe_code = "deny" forbids). Indexing NameIds and Index by slot instead of borrowing is the alternative.
  2. Allocation. Motion::step allocates (the played table's BTreeMap, cloned ObjectViews, track and written vectors), and Client::frame must allocate nothing (allocation gate, beryl_inspect tests/all/frame_allocation.rs). So add Client::prepare(now), which runs before frame in a host's tick and may allocate. Call it from beryl_host frames.rs, drive.rs and headless.rs, and from beryl_inspect gate.rs Playback::handle, so that the gate counts its instructions with the frame (frame_counted) and its allocations as handle allocations. prepare advances the playing script with until = its clock at now, and also the waiting script that will start by now (predict the start as Show::frame does, including the catch-up rate). As a fallback for the many tests that call only frame, Show::frame also advances to now itself. Document prepare in docs/beryl-3d.md ('The client core' entry points, 'Threads').
  3. Only the playing script is ever part-compiled, and only one that arrived with nothing playing or waiting. Anything that needs the whole script finishes it first: a push while it is incomplete (the next script needs its played, and Table::reveal for the new script's showcases must not affect it), resize/scroll_row, a start that needs a relay (relay: any hold or alone timing, or a tray let-go), hand_over (the pointer holds or let go a card), hand_over_tray and place_let_go, plants (records with a planted fault), and a start where it has no tracks yet (moves decides show_playing).
  4. What the frames read while it is incomplete. finals is read every frame (src/table/scene.rs object_at and the abilities, latest_rest, coming_rows), so compute it when the compile starts from Table::placement(after, numbers), with the numbers from replay(from, script) (numbered(Played::of(after)) when that fails), and debug-assert at completion that it equals the motion's placement_of. end is unknown until completion, so the script does not end while incomplete (Show::frame's loop), and wake returns Now. Effects (affect) and notices (announce) are given at start today: give the newly compiled ones after each advance, and re-run the client's prepare_counts and NoticeFloats::prepare in Client::prepare after an advance that added counts or notices (coming_counts and coming_notices are read in handle today). Spans, streaks, counts, sounds, turns and pile counts are given by index from sorted lists: append each advance's items and re-sort with relay::settle (stable), which keeps the order a whole compile gives. History records are written at completion; record_start must create the entry when it comes first.

Measured: the stress boards' scripts are one beat of 40 staggered flights (stagger 30 ms, 420 ms each, beryl_inspect src/stress.rs), so the horizon rule compiles about 14 of the 40 in the receiving frame. stress-verbs is one beat of 106 steps with no stagger, which must compile whole in its first frame: expect no gain there.

Files: rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/play/{compile.rs,mod.rs,playing.rs,step.rs,step/placement.rs,relay.rs,tray.rs,hold.rs,wake.rs,affect.rs,notices.rs,counts.rs,scroll.rs}, src/client/mod.rs, beryl_host src/{frames.rs,drive.rs,headless.rs}, beryl_inspect src/gate.rs; tests in beryl_scene tests/all/compile_in_parts.rs; limits in data/beryl3d/gates.json; docs/beryl-3d.md 'Threads' and 'Measurements'.

Acceptance criteria

  1. A beryl_scene test plays a script of at least 30 steps in 4 or more beats through Client (handle, prepare, frame) and asserts that the frame receiving it has Work::steps at most the first beat's step count plus STEPS_PER_ADVANCE, while the poses at every frame equal those of the same script with the compile forced whole.
  2. just b3d gate --counts --board verbs-zones --board stress-255 --board stress-1024 --board stress-verbs shows verbs-zones', stress-255's and stress-1024's worst frame lower than their limits on staging, and the limits are lowered with --counts --update in the same change, with old and new numbers in the commit body.
  3. just b3d lane passes with no entry raised, and frame_allocations stays 0.
