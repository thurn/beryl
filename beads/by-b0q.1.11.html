Close reason

7 cards with scenarios for every edge case; waterbend, granted crew, scry, transform in engine (c400e76)

Description

Part of Milestone 1 (epic by-b0q): a rules-exact engine, game UI and AI opponents for a mirror match of the 40-card WU Control main deck in docs/sample-decks/2026-10-17-PrinceJools-wu-control.txt (29 distinct cards). Read the epic's description (plan, card table) and design field (decisions) first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md).

Implement these cards in beryl_cards (epic card table): Teferi, Time Raveler; The Wandering Emperor (Samurai token); Jace, the Mind Sculptor; Jace, Vryn's Prodigy // Jace, Telepath Unbound (transform, CR 701.28 and 712; the emblem); Staff of the Storyteller (Spirit token); Swift Reconfiguration; Aang's Iceberg (waterbend: tapping artifacts and creatures to pay generic mana; scry, CR 701.22).

Edge cases for scenarios: Teferi's static stopping an opponent's instant, and his +1 letting a sorcery be cast on the opponent's turn; the Emperor flashed in during combat activating at instant speed, and not the turn after; each Jace loyalty ability, including the ultimates; Jace, Vryn's Prodigy transforming at five graveyard cards and his -3 exiling the recast spell; Staff counting one counter for a two-token creation; Swift Reconfiguration on a creature (no longer a creature until crewed) and crewing it; Aang's Iceberg's exile returning when it leaves, and waterbend paid by tapping creatures.

Acceptance criteria

  • Each card listed has one file under its color directory with its CardDef, bodies and scenarios; every ability is exercised by at least one scenario, including the edge cases named; every body cites the CR.
  • All scenarios pass under the strict runner.
  • Fuzz games including these cards raise no invariant (debug, at least 1,000 games on demand; the just ci count still passes).

A back face up (Jace, Telepath Unbound; Soporific Springs) has only that face's characteristics, name included (CR 712.8e). The legend rule (sba.rs legend_group) compares t.def(card).name, and the PlayerView will need the face-up name too. When adding the second face, give Face a name and have the legend rule compare face names.
