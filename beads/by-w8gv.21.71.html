Close reason

Tags beside every kept event (cause + stack place), stack arrivals/departures in the stream, 9 CR tests; count +0.05%, gate file unchanged

Description

Step two of the presentation track (epic by-w8gv.21; docs/project-50k.md, Target architecture, Presentation, "Causes"; decision K23): "Engine events carry their cause and what they are simultaneous with. Step one infers both in the session from today's flat events. Step two takes them from the interpreter (K1), where every effect entry knows its source, and deletes the inference. Rollouts drop events at the sink, and the tags are held to the count gate with no bump."

This bead is the first half of the engine side: every recorded event says what it came from, and names which entry of the stack that is. The next bead in this lane (simultaneity) and the session's beads (lane:script-causes) build on the type settled here, so settle it carefully and write the decision down in the handoff.

The code as it stands

  • rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/event.rs: Event<L> and Sink<L>. Sink::push (counts work, keeps the event when on) and Sink::note (UI-only events) are the only two ways in; take_from and truncate drop or take the events of a body run replayed (architecture §7.3); relayout widens a sink. Sink::off() is what search and rollouts use.
  • Only eleven sites push or note, in ten files: beryl_rules/src/event.rs (2, the pipeline every zone change and settled event goes through), cast/steps.rs (2), cast/targets.rs, costs.rs, resolve.rs, sba.rs, drawing.rs, mana/add.rs, actions/shuffle.rs, actions/scry.rs, and beryl_kernel/src/knowledge.rs. So tags are stamped in the sink, from a register the rules set, not at the sites.
  • Where the engine knows the cause: beryl_rules/src/resolve.rs and resolution.rs (Resolution.entry is the StackEntry resolving; interpret/mod.rs resolve and interpret/run.rs Run carry out its effect data; a mana ability resolves through mana_ability without coming there); cast/steps.rs, cast/costs.rs, costs.rs and mana/pay.rs (a player casting or activating, and what the costs are paid for; resolution payments, Event::ResolutionPayment); engine.rs (a player's special actions, such as playing a land); turn.rs, combat.rs and sba.rs (turn-based and state-based actions: the rules, no cause); trigger.rs (a trigger put on the stack).
  • beryl_kernel/src/stack.rs StackEntry has no identity of its own, and State must not gain one: a field there is copied in every rollout.
  • What the session guesses today, which this must make unnecessary: rust/crates/beryl_session/src/script/infer.rs causes and loose (Origin: Rules, Player, Object, Ability, Top), payments and paid_for (Pays: which spell or ability a Mana event's mana pays for), and same, pair and lifts_from (which stack entry an event is about; a view's stack does not name its abilities, so script/mod.rs "Names" has the session number them). Read infer.rs's header and those functions, and script/mod.rs "Cause" and "Names"; do not change the session here beyond what compiling needs.

What to build

  1. A cause on every kept event. Recommended shape, to change with a reason: a small Copy enum in beryl_kernel's event.rs, about: the rules (a turn-based or state-based action); a player's own action, with the stack entry it casts, activates or pays into when there is one (so a cost's taps, mana and sacrifices name the spell or ability they pay for, and the session's Pays guess goes); the effect of a resolving spell or ability (the entry); and the object for what has no entry (a mana ability's source, a static ability's replacement). Keep the event enum's variants as they are: the tag sits beside the event in the sink (a struct of event and tag, or a parallel list), so trigger matching and Event::kind are untouched.
  2. A name for a stack entry that costs a rollout nothing. Recommended: the entry's index from the bottom of the stack when the event happened, read lazily only when the sink is on, with every arrival on and departure from the stack an event in the stream (today an untargeted trigger "goes unsaid", Event::Ability's doc; add a note()-only event or widen that one), so the session can follow the stack event by event and map index to its own numbering. Check the cases where an entry leaves from under another (a countered spell under the resolving counterspell; a spell whose resolving ability outlives it, by-w8gv.21.58 and .60) and say in the handoff how the session keeps its mapping right.
  3. The register. The sink holds the current cause; the rules set it for a scope (a resolution, a cast or activation with its payment, a special action, a turn-based action, the state-based check) and restore the outer one on the way out, since scopes nest (a mana ability activated while paying a cost inside a resolution, CR 608.2g). Setting it must be free with the sink off: one predictable branch at most per scope, nothing per event, nothing in State. A scope guard that borrows the sink will fight Game's borrows; a set and restore pair around the few scope sites is fine.
  4. take_from, truncate, drain, relayout and the session's paused-events bookkeeping (beryl_session/src/game.rs events and paused; game/views.rs) carry the tags with their events. beryl_rules/src/resolve.rs truncates the sink to a mark when a body run is discarded: the tags go too.
  5. Tests in beryl_rules/src/tests/events.rs (its harness records a sink): for a cast with a mana payment, an activated ability with a sacrifice cost, a resolving spell's effects, an enters trigger making a token, a state-based death during a resolution's aftermath, combat damage, the untap step, and a mana ability activated during a resolution payment, each event's cause is the one the rules say. Write them from the Comprehensive Rules (601.2, 602.2, 603.3, 608.2, 704.3), not from what the code does today.
  6. docs/engine-architecture.md §8.1 and §8.2 say what a tag is and that search pays nothing for it.

The count

Run just bench-count rollouts corpus before the first change and after the last, and put both numbers in the handoff. The bar is the gate file's and the file does not change. If the count rises by more than noise (a few thousand of 389 million), find the instruction with the Callgrind profile (docs/benchmarking.md) and remove it; typical causes are a register write outside the on branch and a sink that no longer fits its registers.

Not to read

ui/; beryl_search; beryl_session/src/script beyond infer.rs and mod.rs's doc; the card files.

THE TYPE (beryl_kernel/src/cause.rs, re-exported from beryl_rules::event): Tag { cause: Cause } beside each kept event; .72 adds simultaneity as a second field of Tag. Cause = Rules | Player { player, entry: Option<StackIx> } | Effect { entry: StackIx } | Object { source, entry: Option<StackIx> }. Deviation from the recommended shape, with its reason: Object also carries an entry, the spell or ability whose payment a mana ability was activated in, so a Mana event says both its source (CR 605.3b, the ability's effect) and what it pays for (CR 605.3a); with a Player cause on the mana the source would be lost, with a bare Object the session's Pays guess would stay. Cause::entry() reads the entry of any cause.

WHO IS WHAT: Rules = turn-based and state-based actions, declaring attackers and blockers, combat damage, cleanup discard, legend rule, play/draw and mulligans (default: the start of the game is the game's procedure, CR 103; no scope is opened). Player = land play, turning face up, a standalone mana ability's costs, every step of casting/activating (taps, sacrifices, Cast/Ability events), putting a trigger on the stack and choosing its targets (CR 603.3b), paying mana as an effect asks (the tap; entry = the resolving entry), conceding. Effect = everything a resolution does down to its StackDeparture, including a permanent spell entering and the spell going to the graveyard. Object = a mana ability's effect; the life a land's own enters-replacement costs (pay_life_to_enter).

STACK NAMES: StackIx = place among entries the stream has said arrived and not yet said left, first arrival 0. Two new note-only events: StackArrival { entry: StackEntry } (kernel put_on_stack, which now takes the sink) and StackDeparture { entry: StackIx } (kernel take_off_stack, which now takes the sink; cast/targets.rs for CR 603.3d; Sink::resolved at the end of resolve.rs run). HOW THE SESSION KEEPS ITS MAPPING: keep a Vec; push on StackArrival; remove(ix) on StackDeparture; a cause's entry indexes that Vec at the moment of the event. Three things to know: (1) the resolving entry stays in the Vec until its departure, the last event of its resolution, though State took it off; the sink maps State indices past it (Scopes::place) and lowers every open place when something below leaves (Scopes::departed), so a counterspell's own departure names place 0 after the spell under it left. (2) A spell's ZoneChange to the stack (CR 601.2a) comes before its StackArrival and names the place it is about to take (== Vec.len()). (3) A spell whose own effect moved its card still departs last (CR 608.2m); AbilityFinished is unchanged and now always directly precedes an ability's StackDeparture, so .73 may fold it away. A session that starts mid-stack (a loaded save, an undo) must seed its Vec from the view's stack: the sink says nothing of entries that arrived before it recorded. tests/events/causes.rs read() is this reader, 30 lines.

REGISTER: Sink::enter(|outer_entry| cause) / enter_action / enter_source / enter_resolution / leave; note_from tags one event as Object without opening a scope (the mana fast path). Scope sites: engine.rs act() around the player-action arms of perform_machine, perform_priority and TriggerOrder, and concede; resolve.rs run opens, its callers close through unwind() (a wrapper around run copied its large return value once per resolution: 6,646 extra memcpys); costs.rs pay; interpret/mod.rs mana_ability; event.rs pay_life_to_enter. Scratch::settle_counts debug-asserts no scope is left open. Every site is let done = f(); leave(); done: never put a ? between enter and leave.

SESSION (compiling only): Game.events and .paused are Vec<Tagged<Large>>; Game::events() returns Vec<Event>; protocol game_events drops the two stack events, so no view, fixture or script changed. Sink::drain() still yields bare events (30 test sites); drain_tagged / take_from yield Tagged.

COUNT (just count-gate; 'bench-count rollouts corpus' is not a command): before 389,325,611; after 389,527,163 (+201,552, +0.052%); count-gate.toml unchanged, gate passes. This is NOT within a few thousand. First cut was +1,790,348; profiled with Callgrind and removed: recording paths inlined into push/note (now #[cold] out of line), the run wrapper's return copy, a scope per decision (now only on action arms, passes pay nothing), a scope per mana tap (now note_from). What is left is about 4 instructions per scope and stack push plus inlining drift (combat::deal moved out of end_step, perform +217k); one more attempt to flatten perform_priority made it +1.4M, so I stopped. A reviewer who wants it lower should look at engine.rs perform_machine/take_action.

NOT DONE, for .72/.73 to weigh: costs other than mana paid as an effect resolves (may_discard, unless) stay Effect; objects returning when an until-exile ends (CR 610.3, Reason::Return) keep the cause of the move that freed them rather than Object(the exiler); pay_life_to_enter's Object names the land as it was before it entered (same slot, earlier incarnation); static draw replacements open no scope.

REVIEW: beryl_kernel/src/{cause,event,stack,relayout}.rs; beryl_rules/src/{engine,resolve,costs}.rs and interpret/mod.rs; tests/events/causes.rs (9 tests: cast with mana, activated with sacrifice, resolving spell, enters trigger and token, state-based death, combat and untap, mana in a resolution payment, counter from under the resolving spell, discarded run, CR 603.3d removal). docs/engine-architecture.md 8.1 and 8.2. just check passed every lane but ran over its 60 s budget on a machine at load 57.
