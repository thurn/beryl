Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'The frame' (Role::Arrow: an arrow's four corners are the control points of its curve), 'Interface checks' ('Lines hold'), 'Verbs' and 'Timing a script'; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Milestone 3's prompt beads depend on this bead: a prompt draws its own arrows through Client::lit_mut.

Code: beryl_scene src/verbs/point.rs and src/light/arrows.rs (stubs), src/light/mod.rs (ArrowSpan, Span, SpanChange, Lit, Open), Show::reach, src/agent/checks.rs (lines_hold, which only unit tests call), src/driver/check.rs (Page::check_all) and beryl_inspect/src/pages.rs (check_recording), which check one frame at a time. Web: ui/src/stage/lines.ts (Reach, reachOfCard, reachOfLife, reachOfPool; Shape; wayOf and shapeOf: where a line leaves one thing and meets the other, given only how the two lie; hung: the line from an ability to its source sags below both ends), ui/src/stage/light.ts (Ribbon, Curve, Tone and TONES, widthOf, SWELL, lightOf, dark, LitLine), ui/src/stage/spans.ts (Spans, keyOf, namedBy: a span is one thing from its beginning to its end), ui/src/stage/layout.ts (chevron, sag). Cases of point: source line, target arrow, stack arrow, block line, attack line, end, source line end; the five Arrow kinds in rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/script/verb.rs.

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build:

  • verbs/point.rs: Span::Begin and Span::End push a SpanChange for Span::Arrow(ArrowSpan { arrow, from, to }) that fades in or out over the step. It moves nothing.
  • light::arrows::write, for each open arrow: its curve is a cubic Bezier from the edge of the thing it leaves to the edge of the thing it meets, computed on every frame from Scene::reach of its two ends as shapeOf computes it (target arrows arc over what lies between; the source line sags by light.sag; block and attack lines run straight). An arrow with an end that has no place this frame is not drawn. It writes one resolved element, Role::Arrow, named by its kind (Arrow::name), whose four corners are the curve's control points on screen, with ElementId((1 << 22) + its index among the open spans), and a ribbon of 16 Draw::glow quads with sprites::RIBBON along the curve and one sprites::HEAD at its end for the two target kinds, in Band::Glows. Its width follows widthOf, its opacity the fade of its Open, and its color TONES (green for a friendly target and red for a hostile one, by exception 4 of UI Conventions); add the tones to src/tokens.rs. Values go under light in space.json.
  • An element with Role::Arrow is MOVING while either end is.
  • 'Lines hold' on recordings: Page::check_all and check_recording keep the arrows of the frame before (id, corners and the MOVING bit, in a Vec the page owns, since Frame is not Clone) and run the rule of lines_hold on each pair of consecutive frames they evaluate. Change lines_hold to take that kept list.

Do not build: the marks at an arrow's ends, the attacker's wedge or the blocker's crescent (by-q44s.3.20 and by-q44s.3.21); the arrow a prompt pulls toward the pointer (Aim.tsx, milestone 3); the wait for a span to have stood whole (by-q44s.3.32).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/verbs_point.rs, on views and scripts built by hand with testing::ViewBuilder, ScriptBuilder and testing::timing: for each case of the verb in data/ui-conventions.json (the seven above), a script with that case's duration and delay plays to its end with every object at its placement (agent::steps::rest_equals_placement finds nothing), no_jumps finds nothing, and step_times finds nothing. An arrow begun in one script is still in the tree after two more scripts and gone after the script that ends it; its first and last control points lie on the edges of its two cards at every time of a flight of either; lines_hold finds nothing over a recording with arrows and finds a planted reshape of 5 points; an arrow to a player ends at the anchor; a frame with 50 arrows allocates nothing.

When it is done: set BUILT = true in the verb's file, so that Client::next_unbuilt stops reporting it; run just b3d test --update and just b3d timeline-parity --update if those commands exist on staging by then, and commit the lowered lists.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_inspect; just b3d check <a recording with a target arrow>; just b3d gate; just b3d lane.
