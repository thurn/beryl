  • the opponent's cards lifting while they look at them (Cards the opponent looks at; PlayerView.handling; liftsHandling in layout.ts);
  • a creature that enters attacking, a card that enters or turns face down (not-seen).

Decisions and deviations (by-w8gv.21.20, cards changing zone).

Scope. The bead was too big for one session (common.md, Start 4): this is its first coherent part, and the rest is by-w8gv.21.38 (stepped capture of the new cases) and by-w8gv.21.39 (the cases no moment plays yet: opening hand, mill, looks' returns, cast from a pile, linked exile's place and return, enters attacking or face down, showcase, a staggered lift, when the gap closes). by-w8gv.21.40 (human, nothing depends on it) says what to look at. Both follow-ups are lane:verbs and by-w8gv.21.33 (Warden) depends on them; .30 on .39.

Built. (1) A verb's cases carry Arena's own values: by conventions derives verbs[].cases in data/ui-conventions.json from arena-motion.json's per-case summaries, and timing.ts caseOf plays a step with its case's duration, delay, stagger and curve (a draw 1050 ms, a discard 165, destroyed 965, countered 910, exiled 600, bounced 370, resolved 500), the verb's where the case has none. One rule is not Arena's data: a batch exile staggers 300 ms (settled entry "A batch exile"; Arena's instances are single cards). (2) vanish of a card: it fades where it lay, dressed as it was (a tapped creature stays tapped), never aloft, and only as the step ends is it at its new place with no flight; a token's node is removed then (the end hook by-w8gv.21.13 asked for: stage.ts #left). (3) fly: a card that leaves face down and lands face up shows its back until 0.55 of the way (Arena's opponent's spell is edge-on a little past half): Pose.face says so and the node is dressed as a back until then (Entry.covered), so the surface now draws what the pose says. Your draw of a card you see is held up 1.57x over its place in the hand (in 0.55, hold 0.2, drop 0.25 of the step), unless several are drawn in one beat (Arena's Ancestral Recall: no hold). (4) lift: the span puts the opponent's handling on the table (table.ts apply), with the cards it began with (Table.handled), so the same cards stay lifted while a scry reorders the library; desktop uses game/layout.ts's handling places, the phone lays them just under the library. (5) A flight is not taken over by a later step that re-lays the table (Track.own): the first of three draws used to lose its flight, its depth and its turn when the second began. Only a fly's subject is aloft: a flip or set whose record changes a pose glides at its resting depth.

Deviations. No effect layer yet, so every way of leaving is a plain fade (no ash, vortex, flash); the row closes up while the card fades, not after; lifted cards rise together (Arena: 0.5 s apart); the turn-over is a swap with no edge-on squeeze. A resolving spell whose own question is open is not on the view's stack (PlayerView.stack is empty while Thirst for Discovery asks its discard), so the stage has no node for it and its vanish shows nothing: left to the prompt beads (by-w8gv.21.26, .27), and verbs.mjs says so where it skips it. The builder writes no fly library>library for a bottom-bound scry card (same-pile moves are set library), so nothing flies after the local player's scry: that is the settled rule, and the lift check's yours case asserts no card leaves your library before or after the answer, citing the scry reports (by-8qq, by-2wn7, by-kyig, by-1mrh, by-pv6p, by-yfb2, by-nva).

Suite. ui/verbs.json gains more (moments zones and scry, a fixture a seat) and watches (which a verb's check also plays); verbs.mjs plays each to its recording's end and reads the steps the walk cannot see (two steps of a beat that end together) from the stage's edges and its samples (complete). 20 checks: fly, emerge, appear, vanish, lift x yours, theirs x desktop, phone; the whole suite took about 100 s wall on a machine at load 20 to 30. Two new boards in beryl_cli boards/verbs.rs (verbs-zones, verbs-scry), four fixtures. Three script expectations added in script_build.rs (draws and discards from both seats, a returned spell, an exile). No engine or session source changed: the corpus, rollouts_never_change and the count gate are untouched by construction.

Motion. just motion still captures only the cast moment and passes with the per-case timings; lift has no captured step and is waived (measure capture, a new kind of waiver in motion.py) naming by-w8gv.21.38. The new cases are held to Arena in values only until then.
