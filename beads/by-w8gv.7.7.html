Description

Milestone 4 of Project 50k (plan K4, epic by-w8gv.7). "What it exiled" is a memo by the plan's own example. Today it is two object flags and a link field: a card in exile carries EXILED_WITH or EXILED_UNTIL and Object::linked names the slot of the object that exiled it. Milestone 3 gave the link a typed mutator (State::link) only because the scope listed the flags and not the field (Mason note 2 on by-w8gv.7.1). This bead moves all of it to the Memos store.

Read first

  • docs/project-50k.md: decisions K4, K6, K11, and "State" and "Kernel rules" under Target architecture.
  • rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs: the kernel's contract. Its "Stores" section (written by by-w8gv.7.2) is the design this bead follows: kinds, shapes, capacities and byte allotments, expiry, indexing, capability bits, and what a store implements once. The same numbers are in a comment on by-w8gv.7.2, and enact brief shows that bead's handoff. Where the code proves the contract wrong, change its text in the same push and say so on the bead.
  • rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/legacy.rs: the table of what M4 deletes. Delete each Legacy method this bead replaces; the struct itself goes in by-w8gv.7.10.
  • The Memos store's file, built by by-w8gv.7.6 (same lane: its handoff is in the brief).

Need not read: cast.rs beyond Needs::ExiledWithSource, mana.rs, layers.rs, combat.rs, ui/.

Where it is (staging 08d1a377)

  • kernel: ObjectFlags::EXILED_UNTIL and EXILED_WITH, Object::linked and its getter (object.rs); State::link (mutate.rs); State::exiled_with (state.rs); forget_exiled_with and the o.linked = None of the zone move (zone.rs); Board::set_exiled_with and set_exiled_until (board.rs); the link checks in invariants.rs (an EXILED_UNTIL card's permanent is on the battlefield; a link is allowed only with one of the two flags); relayout.rs (linked: self.linked.map(slot)).

  • session and tools: beryl_session/src/checkpoint.rs (Exiled { with, until }: the save's shape stays), protocol/build.rs (exiled_by, exiled_with in a player's view); beryl_scenario (setup.rs, Permanent::exiled_with in lib.rs, script.rs); beryl_cli (blunders/board.rs, specs/auto/definition.rs); beryl_search/src/expert/mod.rs and frozen/expert/mod.rs (linked()); beryl_cards/src/tests.rs.

Scope

  1. "Exiled until this leaves the battlefield" (CR 610.3) and "exiled with this" (CR 607.2a) become memos as the contract shapes them. Object::linked, State::link, the two flags and forget_exiled_with go; State::exiled_with and exiled_until become store queries.
  2. Keep each rule exactly as the code has it, with its citation:
    • cards stay exiled with an object after it leaves its zone, so an ability of it still on the stack finds them, until a new object arrives in its slot or its token's slot is freed (CR 607.2a, 400.7): this memo does not clear with its source's zone change. The contract says how the store holds that; if it is silent, keep today's behavior and write the answer into the contract.
    • a card exiled until a permanent leaves comes back when it does, and never leaves if the permanent was already gone (CR 610.3, 610.3b);
    • a card that leaves exile forgets who exiled it (it is a new object).
  3. The invariants say the same things about the store that they say about the flags and the link.
  4. With no card in the game that exiles this way, the zone move pays one AND. Currency Converter is in the WU mirror, so the present case is on the counted path: measure it.
  5. Object loses a two-byte field. Say on the bead what size_of::<Object<Small>>() is after (24 before; 255 of them).

Numbers and decisions (by-w8gv.7.7).

COUNT (just count-gate, rollouts subject): 389,343,295 before (staging 0d557502) -> 387,776,236 after (-0.40%), no bump and within the tolerance, so the bar stays. Currency Converter is in the WU mirror: the gain is that a permanent leaving the battlefield and an object arriving there no longer walk the exile zone (event::exiled_until, zone::forget_exiled_with); each is one AND of the store's kinds, then one pass over at most 32 entries while such a memo is live.

SIZE: size_of::<Object<Small>>() is 24 after, as before (the contract predicted it: flags are a u32, alignment gives the two bytes of linked back as padding). size_of::<State<Small>>() 19,880, unchanged (allotment 0). A memo entry is still 3 bytes in Small.

SHAPE: Memo<L> gained ExiledWith(slot) and ExiledUntil(slot), entries keyed by the EXILED card naming the exiler's slot. They end with the card (keyed bit, as every memo); ExiledWith entries also end as a new object arrives in the named slot on the battlefield or in the pool, or a token's slot is freed (State::forget_exiled_with from move_object and cease_to_exist, behind one AND). ExiledUntil needs no such end: the rules return the card as the permanent leaves, and Store::check holds the named permanent to the battlefield, as the old invariant did.

PIN: beryl_bench's corpus_never_changes moved 2,007,108,660,786,994,172 -> 6,445,720,418,991,420,564 because Object's Hash no longer feeds a link. Verified it is representation only: with State's hash temporarily feeding what the old fields held (the two flag bits and the link per object, and the memos without the exile entries) the corpus gave the old pin exactly. rollouts_never_change (answers and scores of every rollout) is untouched.

OTHER: Board::set_exiled_with / set_exiled_until are gone; callers use Board::remember(slot, Memo::ExiledWith(..)). The save's Exiled shape and the protocol's exiled_by / exiled_with are unchanged. The three rules of scope item 2 each have an engine test in beryl_engine/src/tests/events.rs: an_ability_finds_the_cards_exiled_with_its_source_until_a_new_object_is_there (new: found after the source left; not by the new object in the slot), a_token_that_ceases_to_exist_unlinks_the_cards_exiled_with_it, an_object_exiled_until_its_exiler_leaves_returns_under_its_owners_control (returns; never moves if the permanent was gone, CR 610.3b).

CHECKS run: cargo test --workspace, clippy -D warnings, just citations, just oracle for Currency Converter and Aang's Iceberg (XMage 8 pass, 1 unsupported as before; Forge 9 pass), just fuzz 300 with debug assertions.
