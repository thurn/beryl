Description

  • A card castable from exile is invisible. Elite Spellbinder exiled Jace, the Mind Sculptor from the reviewer's hand, after which "Cast Jace" was offered every turn. Jace's element was 0×0 at opacity 0, so the offer could not be seen or used from the board.
  • Exiled cards elsewhere: check that 3da1898's exile pile and 548dd34's "exiled beneath" cover every exiled card, yours and the opponent's (Sink into Stupor exiled from hand, Teferi exiled by No More Lies).
  • The opponent's revealed hand is forgotten. After Elite Spellbinder looks at it, the cards revert to backs once the overlay closes. While the cards stay known, show them face up in the opponent's fan. If the view does not carry that knowledge, add it in beryl_session.

Add a general invariant to just ui-test: every object a decision's candidates name has a visible element on screen, with a non-zero box that can be pointed at.

Files: ui/src/game/Piles.tsx, ui/src/game/CardLayer.tsx, ui/scripts/interactions.mjs, rust/crates/beryl_session/src/protocol.rs.

Before changing anything, reproduce each item on current staging. Use just ui-live, as the ui-review skill describes (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md), or a fixture. The findings come from reviews of the build before the 2026-11-05 UI fixes, so some may already be fixed. Drop those, and list them in the close reason.

Design rules (see the epic): follow the skill's design philosophy, and add no standing words to the table. What a player needs at a glance is shown as icons, badges, numbers, colour or position. Everything else goes behind disclosure: hover, the inspector, a pile's browser, or the game log.

Acceptance criteria

  • Rust tests cover any protocol change.
