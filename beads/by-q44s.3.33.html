Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'What it is held to' (the stress boards), 'Finding the worst frame' and 'What is counted'; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_inspect/src/stress.rs (Stress, NAMES, stress, board, permanent, flight_timing: boards built by hand with no engine, every permanent with a name of its own), src/gate.rs (BOARDS, Playback, rank, Counted), benches/frame_counts.rs, src/recordings.rs (listed_stress), tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/gate.py and counts.py, data/beryl3d/gates.json (frames, tiers.baseline.fragments), and the count gate of rule K1 in docs/engine-architecture.md, which the instruction counts of just b3d gate --counts follow.

Build three boards in stress.rs, each a synthetic recording like the two that exist, and add each to NAMES and to gate::BOARDS:

  • stress-verbs: 100 objects, and one script of one beat in which every one of the 16 verbs plays at once: 30 cards fly (10 of them draws), 10 appear, 10 vanish, 5 abilities emerge, 5 cards flip, 10 tap, 5 strike, 5 streaks, 5 counts, 5 sets, 3 showcases, 1 dwell, 1 turn banner, 5 arrows begin, 5 marks begin and 1 lift of 3 cards, each with the timing beryl_protocol::script::timing_of gives its act, and with the effects that timing carries.
  • stress-hand: a 60-card hand for the viewer, and a pointer that crosses it from left to right and back over 4 seconds, as Input::Pointer moves given before each frame, 60 frames a second. Playback gains the inputs of a board, beside its views.
  • stress-stack: a stack of 50 items, 25 spells and 25 abilities, each with a target arrow to a different permanent of a 50-permanent battlefield, played for 120 frames at rest and then one script that resolves the top item.

Then gate them: just b3d gate --update sets each board's limits in data/beryl3d/gates.json from what is measured; just b3d gate --counts --update and just b3d gate --gpu --update add their instruction and fragment limits. For each board, compare the worst frame with the doc's targets: 2,000,000 instructions for a frame, 16 draw calls, 0.75 screens of blended area and 1.75 screens of fragments. Where a board is over a target, do not change the board: write the number in a comment, and file one bead under by-q44s.3 that names the frame (just b3d gate prints it), the counter that is high, and the function rank puts first, with the limit set at what was measured so that it cannot rise.

just b3d list stress lists all five boards, and tree, check, png and film work on each.

Tests, in beryl_inspect/tests/stress.rs: two calls of each board return equal values; each is a legal recording (Page plays it to its end); stress-verbs has a step of each of the 16 verbs and Client::next_unbuilt reports none; stress-hand's frames have 60 cards in the hand and exactly one hovered in each frame after the first move; stress-stack has 50 arrow elements at rest; each board's Client::frame allocates nothing (tests/frame_allocation.rs's counting allocator) and every interface check passes on every tick (check_recording).

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_inspect; just b3d gate; just b3d gate --counts; just b3d gate --gpu; just b3d check stress-verbs; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

The tests pass; data/beryl3d/gates.json has limits for the three boards under frames and tiers.baseline.fragments, and just b3d gate, --counts and --gpu pass; a comment gives each board's worst frame for instructions, draw calls, blended area and fragments beside the doc's targets, with the load and the median of five runs where a time is given, and names each bead filed for a number over its target.
