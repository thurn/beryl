Description

One tool that measures a motion the same way in an Arena recording and in a Beryl capture (plan K27). The motion audit asks only whether something moved, and it passed fixes the user reported again.

Read first: the epic by-w8gv.21 (the user's decisions, what was agreed with the CI track, the code map); docs/project-50k.md, Target architecture, Presentation (K22 to K31); docs/ui-conventions.md (generated from data/ui-conventions.json: edit the data). Then the arena-video skill (.agents/skills/arena-video/SKILL.md).

Build

by video measure (just video measure), in tools/src/beryl_tools/video.py beside list, index, find, film, cards and card, or a module of its own if video.py (714 lines) would pass 800.

  • Input: an Arena recording with a start and end time and a crop, or a folder of frames with their timestamps (a Beryl capture; by-w8gv.21.19 writes those in stepped stage time, and ui/scripts/film.mjs shows the frame-folder convention used today).
  • It tracks the moving card by differencing frames and prints, as a table and as JSON: duration; start delay from a reference instant the caller gives (a click, or the end of the motion before); stagger between the cards of one deal; the path, as sampled centers normalized to the table's size so Arena's 1080p and Beryl's 1600 by 900 compare; peak size relative to the size at rest.
  • It writes an overlay of the tracked path to the cache for a person to check, never into the tree.
  • When it cannot isolate one moving card it says so and exits non-zero with no numbers. Arena's table is animated and busy; that failure is expected for some verbs and by-w8gv.21.18 reads those from filmstrips.
  • Recordings are in ~/Movies/arena and their indexes in ~/arena ($BERYL_ARENA). Never crawl ~ with find. just video index takes every core: never run it inside a check; measuring needs no index.
  • Dependencies: the standard library and the ffmpeg video.py already calls, unless one earns its place with a comment saying what it replaces (AGENTS.md, Conventions).

Tests

pytest with synthetic clips made in the test: a card-sized rectangle crossing a noisy background on a known path with known timing and scale, alone and as a staggered deal of three. The numbers must come back within a frame and 2% of size. One test of the cannot-isolate exit.

Try it

Measure three Arena moments found with just video find: a card cast from hand, a token appearing, a draw. Record on this bead the numbers and what the tracker could not follow. Commit no clip, still or overlay.

Parity and policy

Parity at the push (plan K11): just check passes; the frozen save corpus replays (cargo test -p beryl_session --test corpus); rollouts_never_change holds (rust/crates/beryl_bench/src/rollout.rs); slow CI's counts lane passes with no bump (405,276,238). Nothing waits on the user (K31): where his eye is wanted, proceed on Arena or the nearest existing verb and file a bead labelled human that nothing depends on. Record decisions and deviations as comments on this bead. A bar missed after three attempts is recorded on the epic with its measured value and cause, a follow-up is filed and the work goes on.

Acceptance criteria

by video measure prints duration, start delay, stagger, path and peak size for an Arena clip and for a folder of frames; pytest on synthetic clips recovers timing within one frame and size within 2%, and covers the cannot-isolate exit; three Arena moments are measured, with numbers and failures recorded on this bead; just check passes.
