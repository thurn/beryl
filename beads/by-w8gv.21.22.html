Cases

  • Target arrows from a stack item to its targets (ui/src/components/Stack.tsx Arrows, about lines 200 to 480, and ArrowSpec; Board.tsx boardArrows): exception 4 (colored by relationship), exception 7 (the own-target warning), Two items on the stack.
  • The line from an ability on the stack to its source: A line from an ability to its source. It ships as Arena's always-on thin line. File a bead labelled human asking the user to look at how it is drawn, naming a shot that shows it, and do not wait.
  • Combat: attack lines to the player or planeswalker attacked (Attacking a planeswalker), block lines (ui/src/game/combat.ts).
  • Marks from a script, each with its one look from the data's marks list. Build the looks once here; by-w8gv.21.26 reuses them for prompts.

How

The stage draws a line from its ends' poses in the model on every tick, so a line follows a moving end exactly, and the script's invariants never leave one on a card that moves. A line is one element from its beginning step to its end step.

The two themes

Arrows on cards that moved (6 reports) and the combat line that flickers (5) are named cases here, each citing its report beads (bd list --label bug-report --all --limit 0): stepping the clock from a line's beginning to its end, the same element is present in every tick, and its ends are where the model has the cards.

For each verb case

  • The stage's track in ui/src/stage (as by-w8gv.21.14 laid the slice's verbs out), with its values in data/ui-conventions.json: Beryl's own, designed for Beryl's cards, starting from Arena's clip, numbers and prose description in data/arena-motion.json (by-w8gv.21.18) but never copying them (by-w8gv.21.42).

Positions come from spec moments (just specs --describe <card>) or scripted boards (rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/boards.rs, recorded into ui/fixtures.json). Stage time only.
  • by-w8gv.21.19's checks hold the values to Arena. A verb that is a numbered Beryl exception is marked exempt in the data. A case the recordings do not show is composed from existing verbs and listed under not-seen (K29).

Decisions and deviations (executor, 2026-11-11).

  1. A line's shape is a function of its two ends alone (ui/src/stage/lines.ts): the stage works it out every tick from the model's poses of both ends (stage.ts lines/#reach) and the surface only writes the path. No routing round other cards and no memory of the last frame (components/route.ts is not used): Arena's arcs go over what lies between, and a shape that depends on nothing else is the same however its stage time is reached. An arrow at a target is a bowed arc with a filled head; the line to a source is the same arc, thin, no head; combat lines are straight, an attack's from the chevron's tip (by-986a), a block's between facing edges (by-4jj).
  2. Not in the plan: an arrow or a line of combat stands whole for at least 400 ms before its end (data: point, case end, delay; Timing.hold, beat.ts). Found on the first film: a Counterspell that resolves in the script that casts it had its arrow cut 0 ms after it was drawn. The end itself is a cut of no time, as Arena's (by-pnm: no pause between the arrow going and what it pointed at moving).
  3. A point step is played with the step that calls for it (Timing.with), not at a turn of its own after that step's stagger: by-w8gv.21.14's comment found the source line beginning 470 ms after its lift. The source line now begins as the lift does and is drawn from the tick the ability clears its source (by-w8gv.21.43's finding on emerge). An arrow and a combat line wait for the spell or ability they leave to land (Timing.after), then draw out in 120 / 66 ms.
  4. The end of a source line keeps the 170 + 33 ms the beat took before (case source line end): the ability and its line stay that moment after what the ability did and go together. Cutting it to nothing put the tokens' arrival, the ability leaving and (on a phone) the rows closing up in one tick, and broke the appear and vanish cases that the motion review had passed.
  5. During a strike a combat line follows its lunging creature as the model has it, and draws nothing while the two overlap. The script keeps combat lines until damage is done (script/spans.rs; Arena cuts as the lunge begins). Left as built; a reviewer finding would move the end into the script, which is by-w8gv.21.5's.
  6. Stage bug found by the new check and fixed here: when a view asks the viewer something mid-resolution, its stack list leaves the resolving item out (DecisionView.resolving). The script's table keeps it (script/table.rs Table::of) but the stage took the raw view as its table when a script ended, so the ability's node, and its line, vanished for the length of the prompt. table.ts tabled() now reads a view's stack as Table::of does.
  7. No script builds a mark (script/mod.rs, Spans). The stage plays mark steps all the same, and the suite, the stepped capture and the films play one script written by hand (ui/scripts/written-marks.mjs) over the table the first moment leaves. The six looks are in ui/src/stage/stage.css and nowhere else; by-w8gv.21.26 gives the session's prompt marks to stage.marks through the same steps or Spans.play.
  8. paying: the permanent turns a quarter through its pose (stage.ts #poseOf adds the mark's turn to an untapped permanent) and carries the tap symbol; no outline.
  9. The source line is hidden while the view shown whole asks anything but priority (stage.ts lines): its element stays, drawing nothing.
  10. The phone draws no chevron on attackers (mobile/faces.tsx), so StageLayout.chevron is 0 there and an attack line leaves the card's edge.
  11. stage.ts was at 800 lines: the beat's timeline moved to beat.ts (laid, Run, Playing, Edge) and Shown/Counting to shown.ts.
