Decisions while building the furniture:

  • The life total and the mana pool stay floats (floats::LIFE, floats::POOL) drawn over the orb, rather than becoming screen elements as the bead asked. A total changes as its count step begins, mid-script, and a float shows a total laid out ahead (Layer::prepare) with no layout in the frame; the screen is rebuilt only when an update arrives, so a screen element would show the change late and lay out during play (docs/beryl-3d.md 'Floats'). The orb (hud::players) is a region 'your orb' / "opponent's orb" centred on the same anchor; the player element stays role Player named 'You 20', so picks that target a player are unchanged.
  • The pool float now writes inline mana symbols ("2{U} {G}"), as the web's ManaPool.
  • Not drawn, for want of arc and ray primitives in the flat layer: the superellipse (the core is a rounded rectangle), the active player's crescent and rays (the active bezel is gold instead), and Thinking's sweeping arc (a still gold ring under the opponent's orb). The ManaPool pulse on passing is not built.
  • Step stops are hidden on the phone layout: 22-point icons fail the 44-point touch target check there.
  • Combat's damage icon toggles FirstStrikeDamage and CombatDamage together (client::toggled), as the web's STEP_STOPS.
  • New layer rule (layout::reconcile): a new element anchored at a point of the window stands there at once, as a float does, and a region that draws nothing (element::holder) leaves each child to fade in on its own. Without it the whole HUD faded in over 180 ms on the first view and moved every recording's moments. A fully clear panel is no longer drawn (layout::write).
  • The game screen's top element is now a holder named 'game'; the prompt keeps its panel through screens::game::on_panel (padding gives back the border's room, so its box is unchanged) and comes first so Tab reaches its buttons first. The game screen now shows whenever a game has a view, not only while a prompt is open.
  • Tests: the stop toggles are tested at the client level in beryl_scene/tests/all/hud.rs (a Page cannot answer SetStops from a recording); the pay-default totals and the overlap/reachable/named checks at 1440x900 and 2560x1080 are in beryl_inspect/tests/all/hud.rs, since only beryl_inspect loads recordings. 'every step shown' for the count recordings is covered by just b3d test (8220 tests, no new fault).
