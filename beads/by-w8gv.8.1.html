Architecture audit of milestone 4 (M4: stores) of epic by-w8gv.

Audit against docs/project-50k.md, not against taste:

  • Each decision this milestone implements (K ids in the milestone's description) is implemented as decided, or the deviation and its evidence are recorded on the milestone epic.
  • The conventions hold in new code: no card name in generic code, no file over 800 lines added to the allowlist, no procedural macros, words spelled out, no runtime dependency in the kernel, rules or card crates.
  • Nothing shaped by Forge has entered the engine or the vocabulary: string-typed parameters, script-variable indirection, per-card AI hints, or an entry whose only justification is that Forge has it (K16).
  • The hot path did not gain a general hook that runs in case some card cares (docs/rules-coverage.md, Principles).
  • The next milestone's plan section still fits the code as it now stands; if not, file the correction as a child here so the decompose bead sees it. File important findings as children of this gate epic. Do not rerun tournaments.

Acceptance criteria

Derek Thurn2026-11-12

M4 architecture audit, read at staging 86ee9715 through a detached worktree. Audited against docs/project-50k.md (K4, "State", "Kernel rules"), the kernel's "Stores" contract and the Warden's twelve checks (by-w8gv.7.11).

Verdict: the stores are sound and are the design K4 asked for. One file for each store, one trait (store::Store) for empty, forget, expire, fits, check and lingering, one expiry mutator, one bit test in the zone move, no general hook on the hot path, 3.7% fewer instructions than the milestone started with and the bar never raised. I would not restructure them. Three bugs were found beside them, each confirmed with a throwaway engine test (reverted; nothing committed), and filed as two beads under the gate.

Filed, ranked:

  1. by-w8gv.8.2 (P1, fable): twins that something else names are one candidate. Two permanents of one card, one of which exiled a card until it leaves (Portable Hole, Aang's Iceberg), are interchangeable to engine::interchangeable, so a player cannot choose which to bounce or destroy. The list of who names an object is hand-kept in the engine (cast::referenced | combat::related) and misses memos that name a slot, attachments' hosts and trigger objects. Proposed: one kernel query, State::named.
  2. by-w8gv.8.3 (P1, fable): the layers drop or misdirect effects when a game outgrows a width. (a) Small: three +1/+1 effects locked in on 120 permanents leave them 3/3 or 4/4, not 5/5: the apply list of 192 holds an entry for each member and discards a failed push; M4's locked-in sets made that reachable, though no card makes a set of two yet. (b) Large: with 301 permanents a layer effect on position 300 lands on position 44 (target as u8). Proposed: one entry for each effect, a layout-wide position, an asserting push in place of 35 discarded ones, and a check in the names lane.

Conventions (audit's second bullet): hold. No file over 800 lines added and three allowlist counts lowered; no card name in generic code beyond the allowlist's nine mechanics; no procedural macro (trackers!, counter_kinds!, rules! are macro_rules over one list each, which a const fn cannot write); no runtime dependency in beryl_kernel, beryl_card, beryl_engine; words spelled out. Nothing Forge-shaped: payloads are resolved kernel types, no strings, no script variables. No general hook on the hot path (the two Trackers exceptions are documented and measured).

Not filed, noted where they will be read:

  • On by-w8gv.9.1 (M5 decompose): the scope's four stale items (count bar, allow_sorceries_as_flash, Rule::SorceriesAsFlash, four stores); and making the legal pairs of affected side and payload the only writable ones (one Effect enum in place of Affects x Payload, which deletes two invariants, a release-mode unreachable! and the dead arms in four readers). M5 rewrites every caller, so it belongs in M5's design bead and not in a session of its own.
  • On by-w8gv.7 (M4 epic): the seven places the milestone differs from the plan's text, each with its reason.
  • On by-w8gv.20.16 (weekly prune): thirteen tests of the retired long-think arena are about half the rust lane's summed check time with no failure in 217 runs.

Lower value, not filed: Memo::X and Memo::Paid are non-zero by invariant, not by type. The Trackers store's Store impl is half vacuous (keys, expire, fits). LayerChange repeats Modification variant for variant; that is the resolved form the contract asks for and will diverge once amounts are expressions, so it is fine. Timestamps are a wrapping u16 (65,536 in a game). clippy::cast_possible_truncation is allowed workspace-wide; by-w8gv.8.3 fixes the layout-dependent sites and leaves the lint.

Bug classes this milestone: the one entry in docs/bugs.md (effects not in the signature) was admitted by a comparison that lists what it compares; the Warden's Stored fixed the half about the object itself, by-w8gv.8.2 is the half about what names it.

CI budget: not timed. The load average was 58 during the audit, and both checks enforce their own load-adjusted budgets. Three Steward beads since M4 began (by-g6iw disk full, by-xppd and by-89lk browser checks timing out on a loaded or Windows machine): all in the presentation and CI tracks, none from M4's code.

Next milestone: the plan's M5 section fits the code as it stands.
