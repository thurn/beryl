Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Interface checks' (every step shown, not crowded, no blank card, no jumps) and 'Test speed'; rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md (test-faults.json). Reproduce with just b3d test <name> and just b3d tree <name> --at <t>; to see every fault of a test rather than those over the list, run with an empty data/beryl3d/test-faults.json and --json, then restore it.

Symptom: about 75 different step-shown faults in 40 recordings say a card 'was seen in N of M frames of step:... (fly), mostly outside the window'. Each is a flight between the opponent's hand, which is held mostly above the window (its cards' bottom edges 44 points down at 1440x900, as the web's heldPlacements puts them), and a place near the top edge: the opponent's graveyard, exile or library at the top right (table/pile.rs pile_base, which follows the web's PILES), or a land or spell leaving their hand. Example: verbs-piles/opponent/1440x900, step:5.1.0, Island #16 discarded from the hand at 565,-105 to the graveyard at 1201,18: at 3450ms it is 32x97 at 1086,-87, turned edge-on as it turns face up, and at 3480ms 95x78 at 1172,-46, so most of its frames have none of the five points sample() looks at inside the window. Others: choice-trigger-order/you (opponent draws, card #293 at 1004,-118), fork/copies-time-spiral-which-exiles-the-copy-first, combat-attackers/opponent (Mana Crypt #38), malcolm-alluring-scoundrel, shape-zone/opponent (Currency Converter #17 to 1286,8), time-spiral/with-empty-graveyards-six-lands-untapped-any-players/you/2560x1080, walkers/you, converter-choose and converter-exiled/opponent/2560x1080.

Code: rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/verbs/fly.rs (path_of: opponent draws and lands bow toward the middle of the table; any other flight, such as a discard, goes straight), src/table/hands.rs, src/table/pile.rs, src/agent/steps.rs (sample, step_shown). Compare with the web's paths for the same steps (ui/src/stage/bows.ts bowOf, lift.ts) and with Arena (arena-video skill) for how the opponent's discards and draws travel; the fix is a path that carries the card through the window, not a change to the check.
