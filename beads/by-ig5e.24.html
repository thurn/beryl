Close reason

Sync steps logged (Enact 9e659b2); push is 74% of sync time, gc ~1 s; 200 s syncs did not recur; findings in design.md (96e190a)

Description

On jarvis on 2026-11-16 an enact meta's opening Beads sync held the ownership lock for 227 s (lock-waits.jsonl, sync_s 226.7), and three other commands (sync, meta, start) gave up after 180 s behind it. Typical full syncs take 5-25 s. That sync ran in full because this clone did not have the remote's data tip (another machine had pushed). The log cannot say whether the pull, the push, the merge or compact_beads (dolt gc once the journal passes 64 MB) took the time.

Change (in ~/enact, scripts/enact.py, following ~/enact/AGENTS.md): record the time of each step of sync_beads (commit, pull, push, retries) and of compact_beads in the lock log line (for example pull_s, push_s, gc_s next to sync_s, written by ownership_lock from per-lock accumulators like _lock_syncs). Then, from a day of jarvis data, find what makes the long syncs long and fix it. Options include running dolt gc outside the ownership lock, or bounding the pull.

Acceptance criteria

Lock-log lines split sync_s into its steps; the cause of syncs over 60 s on jarvis is identified from data and fixed or documented in ~/enact/docs/design.md; lands through Enact's own push with slow CI green.
