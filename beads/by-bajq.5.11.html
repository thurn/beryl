Close reason

Glasspool Mimic + Assertion::Subtype: scenario shows a copy keeps its added subtype under Mind Bend; Mockingbird escalated

Description

The Warden of group 4 (by-bajq.5.7) fixed a bug in beryl_rules/src/layers/text.rs type_line: a text change that changes a subtype word on a copy rebuilt its subtypes from the copied face alone, dropping the subtype its copy exception adds (CR 707.9b). The test is a_text_change_on_a_copy_keeps_the_subtype_its_exception_adds in beryl_rules/src/tests/land_types.rs, on a test definition (Test Mimic), because no card of the pool reaches the path: Phantasmal Image is the only copy exception that adds a subtype, and it sacrifices itself when Mind Bend or Crystal Spray targets it.

Mockingbird ("You may have Mockingbird enter as a copy of any creature on the battlefield with mana value less than or equal to the amount of mana spent to cast Mockingbird, except it's a Bird in addition to its other types and it has flying.") reaches it. Write the card with the card-writing skill, and give it a scenario in which Mockingbird copies a creature, Mind Bend or Crystal Spray changes a land type word on the copy, and the copy is still a Bird. Seeing the Bird needs a reader of Bird (a card that counts or pumps Birds) or a subtype assertion in beryl_scenario_types like Assertion::Colors (by-bajq.5.1 added that one, with its XMage and Forge wiring). If the mana-spent clause needs vocabulary the card lacks, escalate it per card-writing and pick another real card whose copy exception adds a subtype.

Acceptance criteria

The card passes just card check (Beryl, XMage, Forge, or divergences recorded); a scenario shows a text change on its copy keeping the added subtype; the Test Mimic test may stay as the unit test.
