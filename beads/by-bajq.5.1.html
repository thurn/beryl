Description

What is wrong today

Layer results exist only for permanents (State::derived, indexed by battlefield position). Anywhere else an object has its printed values: State::power and toughness read the copiable values, and filters over a hand or graveyard read per-slot bitboards built from the cards. An effect that changes cards in every zone cannot be played, and a characteristic-defining ability is not read outside the battlefield (CR 604.3).

What to build

Plan: Designs 4, "Reading" and "Characteristic-defining abilities"; decision R8; principle 7.

  1. One place. beryl_kernel/src/query.rs answers colors, types, subtypes, keywords, power, toughness and "has this ability" for any slot. For a permanent it returns the layer result. For anything else: the copiable values, then the object's own characteristic-defining abilities, then the effects that reach other zones.
  2. Effects that reach other zones. Objects gains the sets such an effect names (cards that aren't on the battlefield, spells, cards in a zone that a filter accepts). The recompute puts each live effect of that kind, in the order it applies, in a list of at most four beside the layer results (a fifth is Full). A read off the battlefield tests the list's length and walks it only when it is not empty.
  3. Filters. A filter over a hand, graveyard, library, exile or the stack uses the per-slot attribute bitboards unless the list is non-empty, and then asks the query for each object.
  4. Tarmogoyf (by-bajq.2.4) is read in every zone: add its scenario in a graveyard, and one on the battlefield under Rest in Peace (by-bajq.3.1), where it is 0/1.
  5. Hidden information: an effect that colors a card in a hand does not reveal the card. Observation gives a hidden card's characteristics as it gives its identity, not at all.

Card

Painter's Servant: "As this creature enters, choose a color. All cards that aren't on the battlefield, spells, and permanents are the chosen color in addition to their other colors." The choice is by-bajq.3.3's.

Scenarios: Circle of Protection: Red, with red chosen, may choose any source, a colorless artifact among them; with blue chosen, Subtlety's evoke cost exiles any card from hand; a card in a graveyard and a spell on the stack have the chosen color (scenario assertions). Force of Will's scenario under Painter's Servant is by-bajq.7.2's, which lands after this.

Files

beryl_kernel/src/{query.rs, filter.rs, derive.rs, object.rs, observation.rs, lib.rs}, beryl_rules/src/layers/, beryl_card/src/effect/reference.rs, the card's file.

  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate; R2 in the plan). The count is exact, so report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust). A change to what the WU mirror's rollouts do is a bug in this epic.
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (root AGENTS.md); just citations.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill: just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), every instruction carried out by its own scenarios, and just specs <card> passing the generic UI checks from both seats. A disagreement with an oracle is settled by the rules and recorded in data/oracle-divergences.json.
  • Read the card's rulings before writing its scenarios (just card brief <name> prints Scryfall's). Each statement in a ruling that a scenario can show is a scenario of the card. Where one cannot be shown yet, the close reason names the ruling and why.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry.
  • If the design in the plan is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
