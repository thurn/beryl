Build, in beryl_scene src/widgets:

  1. button and primary: the looks of buttons.css, from tokens (add any token missing to data/design-tokens.json and run just tokens); hovered, pressed and disabled states drawn from the element's StateBits; a label that does not fit shrinks its type size by the rule of useTextFit, computed in handle.
  2. icon_button(icon, name): icon-button.css, the icon drawn with the Icon material from the icons bead's cells.
  3. toggle(name, on): role Toggle, a press sends the element's press; drawn as the web's mute toggle.
  4. slider(name, value): role Slider (add it to Role with the word slider), a drag along it sets the value 0 to 100 and sends Press::Setting with it; the keys left and right move it by 5 while it has focus.

Do not build: lists, menus, tooltips (the next bead), fields.

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/widgets.rs (new): a disabled button is drawn with the faint type color and a press on it sends nothing; a primary button's panel has the primary color; a label too long for a 250-point button shrinks until the 'text fits' check passes; an icon button is 44 by 44 and passes the phone sizes check; dragging a slider to its middle sends Setting::Music(50).
