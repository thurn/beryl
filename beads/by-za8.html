Cause: a product bug, not test noise. useTextFit (ui/src/components/fit.ts) refit inside its ResizeObserver callback. The priority pill is sized by its own text (max-width 320), so when its room changed (e.g. a late font swap making the First-strike label overflow), the fit inside the callback resized the observed pill again before the callback returned, and WebKit reports 'ResizeObserver loop completed with undelivered notifications'. Flaky in CI because it depends on font-load timing: 0 of 40 instrumented reps of the turn-button check reproduced it. An RO trace showed the pill notified at 245.7 then 241.5 px within one delivery. Fix: the observer schedules the fit for the next animation frame. A new ui-test check ('a long pill refits to narrower room without a resize loop') narrows the pill's max-width; it failed 3 of 3 before the fix and passed 5 of 5 after. The full just ci passes locally (76 s). No tests deleted.
