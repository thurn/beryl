Description

  • Mulligan order: after Mulligan, the player picks cards to bottom from the new 7 before deciding to keep. The text then describes a bottom that already happened ("keep these and put 1 on the bottom"). London mulligan order is draw 7, decide, then bottom N. CR 103.5: "…puts a number of cards equal to the number of times that player has taken a mulligan on the bottom of their library…" applies once the player keeps. Check whether the engine or the UI asks in the wrong order, and fix it where it is; engine code needs its CR citation.
  • Bottom count: show it as slots that fill as cards are picked, not "1 of 2" text. Picks toggle until confirmed; today one click bottoms a card with no undo.
  • The wait after a mulligan: while the AI thinks, the old hand stays on screen as if kept (46–277 s against mcts-hidden).
  • The opponent's mulligans: they are not shown. Show them on the opponent's side, by card backs or a badge.
  • The coin flip: neither the flip nor the play/draw choice is announced when the AI makes it. Use a brief coin visual and a "first" marker on the starting player's orb. Mana Crypt's flip banner should show the card and the result, not a bare "You won the flip".
  • Start screen:
    • the default opponent is random; pick a sensible default;
    • agents appear under raw ids; give them human names;
    • an invalid seed silently greys out Start; show it by the field's style;
    • Enter does not start a game;
    • the last opponent and seed are not remembered (use local storage, wrapped in try/catch).

Files: ui/src/screens/Game.tsx, ui/src/game/ChoiceOverlay.tsx, rust/crates/beryl_session/src/game.rs, rust/crates/beryl_engine/ (if the order is the engine's).

Before changing anything, reproduce each item on current staging. Use just ui-live, as the ui-review skill describes (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md), or a fixture. The findings come from reviews of the build before the 2026-11-05 UI fixes, so some may already be fixed. Drop those, and list them in the close reason.

Design rules (see the epic): follow the skill's design philosophy, and add no standing words to the table. What a player needs at a glance is shown as icons, badges, numbers, colour or position. Everything else goes behind disclosure: hover, the inspector, a pile's browser, or the game log.

Acceptance criteria

  • A Rust or fixture test: after a mulligan, the keep decision comes before choosing cards to bottom.
  • just ui-test checks: bottom picks toggle before confirm; Enter on the start screen starts a game.
  • A shot shows the first-player marker.
  • The start screen remembers the last opponent across a reload (a check).
