Deviations and decisions:

  • The png command had no scan-cache option, so the headless host's Scans::app() now honors BERYL3D_SCANS (documented in rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md).
  • The frame's colors and em lengths are constants in beryl_assets/src/frame copied from card-frame.css and card-palettes.css, not data. Rule 6 asks for data, but the web keeps these in its stylesheets, not in design-tokens.json, and moving them there would change the web's generated CSS (C1). Recorded here rather than filing a bead.
  • Plate faces: one per paragraph of a face's rules text that says a triggered or activated ability, by the same rule as the web's abilityParagraph, looked up by (source face, triggered, nth) through FaceList::plate / Faces::plate_layer. An ability with its own text (granted, or past the printed paragraphs) has no plate face and keeps showing its source's face. A plate face is cached under its own key (source key + kind + hash of the paragraph). Plate faces count against the 256-layer downlevel array limit.
  • The plate covers the printed power and toughness or loyalty on the plate face, as the web's plate covers a scan's.
  • The late-scan thread is not joined on drop; after the workers are joined, current is set to 0 so it drops what it still holds. If it is blocked on a real download at that moment, the match's result channel stays open until that download answers (the headless host uses an address that fails at once).
