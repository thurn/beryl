Description

Review group 6 as built, against Designs 6 of docs/rules-coverage.md, CR 601.2 and CR 101.2, and fix what you find.

Look hardest at:

  • Is there one function that computes a total cost, used by the prefilter, the casting machine and the human's payment preview alike? A second copy of the order is a bug waiting.
  • The order itself against CR 601.2f, with every casting option the vocabulary has: flashback, evoke, kicker, overload, impending, escape, Force of Will's.
  • Cost rules and copies: a copy of a spell pays nothing; a spell cast "without paying its mana cost" under a floor.
  • "Can't" beats "can": a permission and a prohibition on one action, in both orders of timestamp.
  • Mana that remembers: every way mana leaves a pool; the reference payability check (beryl_rules/src/reference.rs) against the solver with such mana in the pool.
  • The human's payment (Profile::taps_mana) with a Cavern on the battlefield.

Two things every Warden of this epic reports

  • The group's count. The count gate's tolerance is one total shared by the whole epic, so a group that uses most of it leaves the next bead to fail for work it did not do. From the close reasons of this group's beads, add up the change each made to the absent-cost count (just count-gate) and put the total in your close reason. A bead that raised the count by more than 0.05% without saying why is a finding: find the cost and remove it, or file it.
  • Test definitions. List each behavior a bead of this group proved with a test definition in beryl_rules/src/tests/ where it had no real card. For each, name the card scenario that now covers the same path and agrees with XMage and Forge. Where the card has landed and no scenario does, write the scenario. Where the card is in a later group, comment on that card's bead with the path its scenarios must cover. Where no card of the epic has the behavior, file a tier:sonnet partner card bead under this group.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate; R2 in the plan). The count is exact, so report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust). A change to what the WU mirror's rollouts do is a bug in this epic.
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (root AGENTS.md); just citations.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill: just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), every instruction carried out by its own scenarios, and just specs <card> passing the generic UI checks from both seats. A disagreement with an oracle is settled by the rules and recorded in data/oracle-divergences.json.
  • Read the card's rulings before writing its scenarios (just card brief <name> prints Scryfall's). Each statement in a ruling that a scenario can show is a scenario of the card. Where one cannot be shown yet, the close reason names the ruling and why.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry.
  • If the design in the plan is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.

Derek Thurn2026-11-15

Warden review of group 6 (7.1-7.5). Pushed 6fdc5277.

Found and fixed:

  • Bug: a delve spell needing restricted mana was never offered (Gurmag Angler with only a Cavern of Souls on Zombie). cast::parts::delve_pays paid for no spell (Paying::NOTHING); it now takes the face and uses Paying::casting. Scenario 'Gurmag Angler paid with delve and Cavern of Souls' mana' fails before, passes after. docs/bugs.md entry.
  • Gap: reference.rs checked payability only for no spell, so restricted mana was never compared. check_spell_payments now compares prefilter, Funds::pays_for and the solver for each card in hand, with restricted mana its restriction accepts among the reference units (spendable(), remembered pool entries). Proven live: sabotaging it fails Cavern's scenarios.

Checked, no change:

  • One total-cost function: cast::costs::spell_cost serves the legality check (legal::refused), the casting machine (total_cost) and the human's payment preview (cost_being_cast -> total_cost). mana/lookahead.rs still adds only a permission's increase to face.cost; it is the AI's land-tapping heuristic, not a legality answer, so left alone.
  • CR 601.2f order in rule_change::changed_cost: base or alternative cost (one alternative, free = NONE), additional costs, permission increase, X, then increases, reductions saturating at 0, then the greatest floor counting X; delve subtracts after the lock-in. Free casts under a floor pay the floor; X is 0 when free. Copies are never cast, so pay nothing. Flashback here is 'gain flashback' (mana cost), evoke/overload/impending/kicker/Force of Will are CastingOptions summed the same way. Escape is not in the vocabulary.
  • total_cost recomputes rather than storing the locked-in total; today nothing can change a spell's cost between lock-in and payment except a human mana activation during delve's chooser, and no implemented mana ability changes a cost rule. Recorded as a design note, not filed.
  • Can't beats can: activation_options removes named sources before any ability (including LoyaltyAsInstant planeswalkers) is listed, so order of timestamps cannot matter. CantBeCountered is a check in actions::counter, also order-free.
  • Mana that remembers leaves the pool only through spend_remembered_mana (pay.rs) and empty_mana_pools (clears remembered too); relayout copies it.
  • Human payment with a Cavern: mana::activate taps the restricted ability only for a color the plain abilities can't make; pay_with_memory spends remembered entries from the highest index down, then restricted taps from the end of the pool. Looks right.

Filed: by-bajq.7.8 (Helm of Awakening, the missing reduction card), by-bajq.7.9 (oracles can't replay restricted payments: trace lacks the color), by-bajq.7.10 (win back the group's count, with the Callgrind profile of 4b0d874a).
