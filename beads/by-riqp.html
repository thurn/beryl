Count gate creep, 2026-11-11 to 11-13: causes and guardrails

Description

Record of why the rollout benchmark's instruction count (the count gate, rust/crates/beryl_bench/count-gate.toml) crept past its bar, and what now catches that earlier.

What happened

The bar was lowered to 389,343,295 on 2026-11-11. The count then fell to 386.0M (53a5bbe6, -0.86%), which was within the 1% tolerance, so nobody lowered the bar. It rose again in steps of 0.05% to 0.64% to 393.16M, and ebe1f089 (+1.42%) failed slow CI (by-nl72). b82d8229 won back most of ebe1f089. rollouts_never_change has been unchanged since 2026-11-08, so all of the rise was cost, not new work.

Where it came from (slow CI's logged counts per tested tip, then a per-commit bisect in the bench VM)

  • coverage built into the benchmark: +1.55M. cargo bench run from the workspace turned on beryl_engine's test-only coverage feature through other members' dev-dependencies. It cost 84k at 53a5bbe6 and 1.63M by b82d8229, as cards became effect data and every instruction recorded a hit. Fixed in 63ce44b0 (-p beryl_bench).
  • e8d5e451 (tagged unions for casting and stack items): +1.68M. cast::steps::top is now out of line and clones the stack entry about 12,500 times. Forcing it inline cost +0.59M more; returning a reference is a larger refactor.
  • 0c5e3548 (seven cards toward effect data): +1.25M, mostly inlining shifts (layers::refresh, combat::attack_targets outlined) plus pointless.
  • 1000dc3c (card definitions match Oracle text): +1.03M. Of this, 258k was the CR 704.5n Equipment check compiled into the engine loop (fixed in d947a7c3); the rest are layout shifts in engine::next.
  • fa870209 (typed combat role, object flags, event kinds): +0.70M.
  • 89e1e2e6 +0.59M, b92a517b +0.40M, 077d52ed +0.26M, 59ecb864 +0.23M.

Guardrails

Built in a6bb6073 (count_trend.py), at no CI cost. Slow CI records each staging tip's count in $BERYL_CACHE/ci/counts.jsonl and prints the change since the last tip it measured. It files a count-creep bead when one tested range adds more than 0.25% or the count drifts 0.5% above its lowest since the bar moved, and a count-lower bead when the count is 0.5% below the bar. Against the history above, these would have filed beads at 1197fe04 (+0.64%) and 94e7555d (+0.59%), and from drift by bef5cc24.

Rejected:

  • The gate in the fast check: the VM build is 25 to 50 s warm, plus 15 s of Callgrind, under a VM lock that slow CI and other sessions share. That is over the 60 s budget.
  • A smaller corpus subset: the build, not the run, is the cost.
  • A native macOS instruction proxy (proc_pid_rusage ri_instructions): it needs a release build in the fast check, which has the same cost, and it is not exact.

Filed: by-1ugj (creep beads bisect their own range in the VM, in the background).
