Close reason

shots and ui-test are parallel lanes on 4 workers each; UI lanes 194 s -> ~50 s, just ci 198 s -> 73 s

Description

Measured problem (Sage audit by-0w2.15). The slow CI's shots lane sets its wall time, and it keeps growing. It took 24 s at the end of M1a, 61 s at the end of M1b, 124 s when this run started and 181 s at 23f24d3, where just ci took 186 s of its 300 s budget. Every other lane finishes in 50 s or less: rust 50, oracle 45, app 26, python 8. The parallel by-isv UI run adds interaction checks with each bead: d7fb277 alone added 31 s to ui-test. At that rate a few more UI beads push slow CI over budget.

Where the time goes (fresh worktree, load ~9 on 18 cores):

  • by ui-shots 72 s: recording all 28 fixtures takes 9 s, and the rest is 57 shots captured one at a time in a single WebKit page (ui/scripts/shots.mjs).
  • by ui-test 80 s: it re-records 21 fixtures (10 s) that ui-shots just recorded, then runs 64 checks one after another (ui/scripts/interactions.mjs). Each check already opens its own browser context.
  • tools/src/beryl_tools/checks.py shots_lane runs the two steps back to back. Both rmtree and write the same ui/public/fixtures, so as written they cannot run at the same time.
  • Prototype: the same 57 shots in 4 concurrent node scripts/shots.mjs processes against one Vite server took 22.1 s instead of about 63 s, with no failures.

Proposed change:

  1. Run shots and their checks concurrently: a worker pool of about 4 (an env var or flag to tune it) over browser contexts in shots.mjs and over check() calls in interactions.mjs, keeping output ordered or prefixed by name and errors per check.
  2. Record the fixtures once per lane run. Either ui-test reuses ui-shots' recordings, or each records into its own directory, so the two can become separate parallel lanes (shots and ui-test) in slow().

Expected gain: the shots lane drops from about 181 s to about 45 s, so just ci goes from about 186 s to about 60 s, which is under rust's 50 s plus margin. It also gives back about 2 minutes of headroom for the UI run's new checks.

Acceptance criteria

  • ui-shots and ui-test capture and check concurrently, and each records the fixtures at most once per slow CI run.
  • just ci passes with the shots work at most 60 s wall time on an idle machine; the bead records before and after timings.
  • A failing shot or check still fails the lane and names itself.

Timings. Before (slow CI log, staging ~2001256, load ~14): shots lane 194 s (ui-shots 98 s, ui-test 96 s), just ci 198 s. After (this change cherry-picked onto 2001256, the last commit whose fixtures all record; staging tip 594cda6 fails to record combat-blockers, stack-trigger, arrow-player, marks, marks-damage, which is by-0w2.20's area): just ci shots ui-test 54 s at load ~14 (shots 51 s, ui-test 50 s); again at load ~12: 48 s (shots 48, ui-test 45). Full just ci 73 s at load ~19 (shots 67, ui-test 64, oracle 73 now the longest). BERYL_UI_WORKERS=6 gave shots 49 s / ui-test 38 s, so the default stays 4. Design: shots and ui-test are now separate parallel lanes; ui-test records its 24 fixtures once into the cache (ui-test/<checkout>/) and its pages fetch them through a Playwright route, so it never touches ui/public/fixtures that ui-shots rmtrees and rewrites; capture.mjs workers() runs 4 browser contexts at a time in both scripts. Failure path verified: an injected bad shot and a bad check each failed their lane with their name, and the other 77 checks still ran.
