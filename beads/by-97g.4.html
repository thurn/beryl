Description

Part of the ladder epic (read it and docs/ai-strategy.md §4.2, "A screen before the match", first). A cheap estimate of what a challenger costs, for challengers that change few moves, before the hours of a match.

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/anchor_replay.rs: walk, --recheck, the margins table; it does this for one challenger, the anchor.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs: LongSearch::duel (fresh trees held to two candidates, the whole budget split between them, lead tree by tree).
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/game.rs: entrant names.
  • The ladder runner's --out directory: the champion's games with its searched decisions (its bead, which this one depends on).

Build

beryl_cli screen <challenger> <match-dir> [--recheck N] [--work 3M]:

  • Replay each game of the match directory through its log. At each of the champion's searched decisions, ask the challenger, built from the same seed, what it would play.
  • Where the two differ, duel the two candidates from a fresh seed at the proxy budget (LongSearch::duel) and take the challenger's lead over the champion's choice, tree by tree, as anchor-replay --recheck does. Cap the re-searches at N, spread over the games.
  • Report: differing decisions a game, by decision kind, and the summed lead a game with its standard error. A cost beyond 0.03 a game is written beside the match's result in the rung bead (§4.2); it stops nothing.
  • anchor-replay stays as it is; share walk and the duel code rather than copying it.

Tests

A challenger identical to the champion differs nowhere and costs nothing. A crafted difference at one decision is counted once and dueled once. just test-rust, clippy pedantic.
