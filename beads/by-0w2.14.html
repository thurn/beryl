Mason audit, ranked. I read the engine diff 69b8d7d..23f24d3 and the architecture doc (§5, §6, §7.4, §8, §9, §10).

Filed under by-0w2:

  1. by-0w2.18 (P1): Treasures are paid for outside the mana solver. Every priority with a Treasure out gains 5 standalone sacrifice options; spells payable only with a Treasure are not offered; this contradicts §10. No scenario pays with a Treasure.
  2. by-0w2.19 (P2): A copy of a face-down permanent holds the hidden original card, face down. That needs five TOKEN exemptions (bug c1ad83d was a missed one) and copies a status, which CR 707.2 forbids. Fix: a synthetic face-up FACE_DOWN_COPY card. Also deletes the never-set Object::copy_of.
  3. by-0w2.20 (P2): UI fixtures are seed-hunted agent games. This milestone they were reseeded or remapped 5 times, plus 2 Steward beads, and 8 fell back to the Vintage deck. Fix: start them from scenario boards.

Built as designed: overload (CastingOption + spell_ability_with), evoke (alternative cost + intervening-if trigger), granted flashback (CastPermission), FaceIx::DOWN read through CardDef::face (layer 1b for free), and delayed triggers now named by their ability's Trigger::Delayed. Fine as is.

Lower value, not filed:

  • Casting's step-dependent fields (spec, left, remaining, bent) want a payload-carrying CastStep. begin_payment's c.spec = 0 reset shows the hazard.
  • Jace's WinInsteadOfDrawingFromEmptyLibrary sits in the enters-only Replacement enum (enter_replacements skips it), and draw scans the battlefield for it. Acceptable until a second draw or zone replacement arrives. Then build §8.2's per-kind replacement index, or make it a Rule.
  • StackEntry has exile and flashback as two bools, and flashback's "exile wherever it goes" is checked at three sites (leave_stack, return_to_hand, put_spell_into_library). Better: one enum plus one destination function.
  • Object::linked is Option<Slot>, not an ObjectRef, so links into a reused slot need clean-up (forget_exiled_with scans exile on each battlefield entry). The fuzz invariant guards it.
  • Evoke and impending pair a CastingOption with its trigger only by convention. A table test could check it.
  • Two shapes of exile cost: Face::exile_from_graveyard and Evoke's colours.

CI: slow CI passes in 186 s of 300. I did not measure the fast check; its rust lane alone is about 50-55 s (clippy plus tests in slow CI), so it is near the 60 s budget. No load-sensitive checks found; the greedy win-rate tests are seeded.
