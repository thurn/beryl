Sage audit of milestone 1 (by-q44s.1, 2026-11-15 04:59Z to 17:50Z). Sources: bead timings and comments, .git/enact/ci.log, $BERYL_CACHE/ci/runs.jsonl (fast-check records), the Beryl 3D sweep's log.jsonl, and measurements in a fresh worktree at load 27 to 50.

  1. Which beads took far longer than their neighbours, and why. Most beads took 13 to 50 min from start to close. The outliers:
  2. by-q44s.1.15, the dependency probe (296 min). It built every planned dependency for macOS, iOS, the iOS Simulator and Android. The cause was the work itself, cross-target builds on a loaded machine, not a fault. It blocked 1.9 and 1.22.
  3. by-q44s.1.40, the interface test runner (241 min). Before by-q44s.1.53 landed, a full run took 1,381 s and stress-1024 alone took 1,363 s, so every measurement was slow. 1.53 fixed that in parallel.
  4. by-q44s.1.25, step timing sent with each script (212 min). It was done at 07:01, but the script lane failed on staging because of a fault elsewhere (spell copies, by-bajq.6.9). It was released and depended on that fix, which closed at 07:38. Nobody resumed it until about 09:30, and it was pushed at 09:47 after a rebase conflict in script/mod.rs.
  5. by-q44s.1.12, the instruction-count gate (199 min). It was taken over from 1.11's lane because that session's context was heavy. Callgrind in a 4 GiB VM had to build with -j 1, and a full run takes about 25 min. It found the quadratic placement (by-q44s.1.49).
  6. by-q44s.1.51 (149 min, Sonnet). Its own check could not run: about 10 attempts over 8 minutes were all skipped with "another sweep is in progress".
  7. by-q44s.1.4, 1.39, 1.47 and 1.50 (about 125 min each). These were large designs. 1.50 lost two pushes to rebase conflicts with other Beryl 3D beads (card records, png/film, the VM counts). Merge conflicts with the web interface or the manual table epic cost little. The one real collision was a main-workspace change breaking Beryl 3D's build, which happened 5 times (by-q44s.2.5, filed by the Mason).
  8. Isolation. No main-only change ran the beryl3d lane. In the fast check, 37 of 71 runs of the beryl3d lane ran nothing else, and 22 also ran the python lane (changes to tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d, which is intended). 12 ran main lanes because their commits really changed main crates: the lane:timing beads, 1.47 (beryl_cli fixtures), 1.50 (beryl_protocol split), ffa9d40db (motion math, which touched script/timing.rs), 7618d7b49 (by-q44s.18, script/table.rs) and the web's 8a1bf1cec, which had to patch beryl_scene/src/testing.rs for new view fields. Slow CI has no Beryl 3D lane. The Mason's answer in by-q44s.2.1 agrees.
  9. Edit-to-output is within target in a worktree that has built once. After a one-line edit to beryl_scene/src/lib.rs, just b3d tree verbs-zones --at 1500ms took 2.57, 2.02, 2.01, 2.16 and 2.00 s (median 2.0 s, target 3 s). just b3d png took 4.79, 4.76, 4.62, 4.47 and 4.80 s (median 4.8 s, target 6 s). With no edit they took 0.5 s and 1.8 s. The first command in a fresh worktree is over target: 43 s for tree and 47 s for png, because the cached build directory was 3.5 h behind the main workspace (by-q44s.2.6).
  10. Width. The vizier ran 5 sessions at once (the route's parallel: 5).
  11. Widest: at 06:15Z, 4 beads were in progress and 8 more were ready, so the session limit, not the graph, held the run back from 06:00 to 07:30.
  12. Narrowest: from 05:15 to 05:55 the run had 2 in progress and 0 ready, during the serial start (paths rule, then workspace, then contract). From 07:35 to 10:15 it had 0 ready while 1.4, 1.39, 1.47 and 1.15 ran and 1.25 sat idle. From 16:35 on, only the Wardens and 1.16 remained.
  13. What would have widened it: more sessions in the morning; splitting the beads that collect many dependencies (1.38 waited on 6 beads, 1.16 on 9, 1.42 on 4); keeping the probe (1.15) out of the feature path; and resuming a released bead as soon as its blocker closes.
  14. Waits for the owner: none. No comment on any milestone 1 bead asked for, or waited on, the owner.
  15. New checks that are slow, or that failed without a code fault.
  16. The beryl3d fast lane: median 109 s, 50 of 71 runs over the 60 s budget. cargo-test was cut off at the 180 s limit 15 times out of 60, and gate 11 times out of 32. Those cut-offs count as failures in runs.jsonl, but none was a code fault. Measurements are on by-wzq9.
  17. The Beryl 3D sweep grew from 496 s to 7,540 s per run. Its gpu step failed twice from tooling (the VM could not see the build path; by-q44s.1.51, then by-q44s.17, still open), and frame-counts runs for hours. Filed by-q44s.2.6.
  18. The sweep's cargo-test and clippy failures (by-q44s.15, .16) were real code faults from main-workspace commits.
  19. Slow CI went red 8 times (by-45a0, lfho, 7fd5, p3ju, ufe4, sbpt, qi91, k3fc, f672), for about 8.5 of the 17 hours. Every one was an oracle, count-gate or card fault in the main project, none Beryl 3D's. A red staging slows master but does not block pushes, so it did not delay this run.
  20. Drift between the web interface and Beryl 3D. The web added controlled_by, phased_out, part_of with ZoneName::Merged, really and hidden_pick to ObjectView, and no milestone 2 or 3 bead plans for any of them. Filed by-q44s.3.39 under milestone 2. The manual table moved to hosted, multiplayer tables with a lobby; this is noted on by-q44s.5.30. Wire-type churn breaking Beryl 3D's builders is by-q44s.2.5 (Mason).

Fixes. Nothing was cheap enough to fix in this bead without overlapping by-wzq9 or by-q44s.17, which are already claimed or filed. Filed: by-q44s.2.6 (gate, P1, sweep order and cache save) and by-q44s.3.39 (milestone 2). Commented on: by-wzq9, by-q44s.5.30, and the planning notes on by-q44s.3.1, by-q44s.7.2 and the epic. Milestone 2's and milestone 3's planning beads had already closed, so the notes also went to the epic and to the one open planning bead, 7.2.
