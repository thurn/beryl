Close reason

Storm copies a spell countered under its trigger from a kept stack entry (DepartedSpell, CopiesItself); Brain Freeze/Tendrils scenarios pass Beryl/XMage/Forge; State 20,016->20,128; count 394,440,410 (-0.26% vs staging tip)

Description

Symptom: with Brain Freeze or Tendrils of Agony on the stack, countering the spell in response to its storm trigger makes the trigger copy nothing. The rules make the copies anyway: the storm ability exists independently of the spell (CR 113.7a), and 'copy it' uses the spell's last known information (CR 608.2h) once it has left the stack.

Why: actions::copy::instruction (rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/actions/copy.rs) copies only spells whose stack entry is still on the stack, since the entry holds the targets, X and casting options paid that a copy takes (CR 707.10). When the spell leaves, State::take_off_stack drops the entry and nothing keeps it.

What to build: keep what a copy needs of a spell that leaves the stack while an ability that copies it waits or is on the stack (its card, face, copiable values, targets, X, costs paid), behind a table capability so a game without such a card pays nothing (count gate, just count-gate). Then copy from it. Scenarios: Brain Freeze countered in response to its storm trigger still mills with each copy; Tendrils likewise. Check XMage and Forge agree (just card check).

Files: beryl_rules/src/actions/copy.rs, beryl_kernel/src/stack.rs (take_off_stack), beryl_kernel/src/last_known.rs, the two card files under rust/cards/cards_2003/scg/.

Acceptance criteria

Brain Freeze countered while its storm trigger waits still makes its copies, in a card scenario that XMage and Forge agree with; the count gate moves by no more than layout noise.
