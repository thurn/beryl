Close reason

Phone proposal panel folds to a button under the log, nudge labels straddle the card top, table-assists phone faults gone

Description

On the phone layout, the manual table's proposal panel (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/manual/assists.rs, proposals) is anchored as a strip along the top of the safe area, where it covers the opponent's lands and creatures, and a state nudge's label (labels) can overlap the panel's Apply button. just b3d test --phone-only b3d:screen/table-assists reports 5 reachable faults (the opponent's Islands, Pacifism and Serra Angel are under the panel or the label) and 1 overlap fault (Apply over "Move to graveyard") in each of the three phone windows. The desktop layout has none: there the panel stands at the table's left edge between the two players' rows.

The web interface gives the panel its own strip on the phone (UI Conventions, "Proposal panel"). Find room for it that covers no card, for example by folding it to a button beside the log's icon button in the top right corner (manual::log::log, the phone branch) that opens the panel as a sheet, or by giving it a band the phone layout reserves (table::phone::layout, layout.phone in data/beryl3d/space.json). Keep the touch sizes the panel already has on the phone (touch in assists.rs). Read: assists.rs, rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/screens/table.rs, the screen/table-assists recording in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_inspect/src/screens.rs, ui/src/table/ProposalPanel.tsx and ui/src/table/assists.css for the web's phone strip.

Acceptance criteria

just b3d test --phone-only b3d:screen/table-assists reports no fault; just b3d test passes; enact push passes.

Handoff: On the phone, the proposal panel (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/manual/assists.rs, proposals) is now folded to a small 'Proposals' button under the log's icon button in the top right corner. It opens under that button (full safe width) when pressed. The open state is TableState::proposals_open: Option<u32>, the id of the first proposal it was opened for, so a later proposal starts folded. Applying a proposal also folds it (client/manual_overlays.rs). I used an Option rather than a bool because clippy's struct_excessive_bools fired once the newly landed quitting bool was added to TableState. On the phone, nudge labels (assists::labels) now use Anchor::Above(subject, -flat.table.labels.phone_overlap), a new value of 16 in data/beryl3d/space.json. That puts the row across the card's top edge, so both the card and the row above it keep 44 points uncovered. The desktop layout is unchanged. Deviations from the web: the web gives the panel a footer strip over the hand. Beryl 3D's phone table has no free band for it, so the panel folds instead, as the bead suggested. The button's horizontal padding is space::M so that it stays clear of the opponent's 'others' row at 375 wide.
