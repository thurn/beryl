Pay sacrifice and discard before escape exile

Description

Underworld Breach ruling 2020-01-24 / 8b673134fc05 says a creature sacrificed or a card discarded to pay an escaped spell’s additional casting cost can then pay its escape exile cost. Audit reproduction: add Village Rites with .additional_cost(&[CostPart::sacrifice(Filter::Types(Types::CREATURE))]), then start a scenario with Breach, Swamp and Prop::Vanilla on the battlefield, Village Rites plus only Island and Plains in the graveyard, and an opponent holding Counterspell and two untapped Islands. The table rejects Village Rites with Cost { unpayable: Part(Chosen(Sacrifice, …)) }. See rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/payable.rs::pays_for_spell and rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/cast/{legal,parts,steps}.rs. Spell cost legality checks the current graveyard before any costs move cards, and the cast must also permit paying the applicable parts in a legal order.

Acceptance criteria

Implement the additional sacrifice and discard cost path and test casting with escape when the third card enters the graveyard while paying. Assert all costs finish before the opponent gets priority and that a token or a replaced move does not supply an exilable card. Replace the untestable reason in the Underworld Breach ledger with the scenario and add the rules-bug entry naming the regression guard. Run the affected card checks and count gate.
