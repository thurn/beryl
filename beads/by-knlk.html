Acceptance criteria: (a) A picked attacker wears the attacker chevron at once, whether it is aimed at a player, aimed at a planeswalker, or still waiting for its aim. (b) The chevron stays when the attack is confirmed. Cause and fix: this is the same cause as lane-mate by-5fex. The stage drew the chevron only from the view's combat state, so it never saw the prompt's picks. Commit 26ce1d79 (on staging) lights every creature the prompt has picked, aimed or not. While it aims, the opponent's life orb and Jace glow cyan. Regression coverage already exists. ui/scripts/stage-declare.test.mjs has a case with an attacker picked and aimed at the opponent. The shape-declare fixture plays this exact sequence (one attacker aimed at a planeswalker, then one aimed at the opponent) in the shape:declare suite.
