Sage audit of M1b (staging 2532e87). M1b ran 11:49-19:24 UTC (7.6 h), 22 beads, none reopened or released, no Steward beads, staging never red. One Justiciar incident (by-ovn): the expert bar was unreachable and the user revised decision 6 (blocked about 15 min).

Where the time went. The critical path was MCTS 3.6 (152 min) -> Warden 3.12 (67) -> benchmark 3.9 (137) -> Warden 3.13 (66), 12:20-19:24. From the transcripts, those four sessions sat idle waiting on background tournaments for 118, 52, 98 and 55 min: about 323 of 422 min (77%). Tournaments run: 3.6 gates 2x200 games (76 min) plus 38 min of tuning runs; 3.12 reran both gates on fresh seeds (64 min); 3.9's 10-pairing round robin (130 min on 16 cores); 3.13 reran the three reported-only expert/MCTS pairings (62 min, 53,756 CPU-s for 300 games, ~180 CPU-s per MCTS game). About 1,800 MCTS games in all. The epic notes' cause: rollouts ~16x over the 1.5 us/decision target, ~120 rollouts per core-second. The expert work (3.5 109 min, 3.11 33, 3.22 116; ~30 review rounds) was off the critical path; its cost came from a 5/5-zero-blunder bar that the executor tuned to seeds 1-5 over 21 rounds.

Checks. Slow CI: 29 runs in M1b, all passed, 48 s rising to 72-119 s (budget 300); last run 69 s of lanes, longest lane shots 61 s (ui-shots 45 s, was 24 s at the end of M1a), rust 54 s (tests 40 s), oracle 34 s, smoke 24 s. Fast check: over its 60 s budget in 6 of 19 rust-lane runs (62, 66, 75, 88, 132, 144 s), the worst two beside running tournaments. Fresh worktree now, engine+UI edit, load ~27: 42 s of lanes, 49.5 s wall (rust tests 31 s). cargo test warm and quiet 19.4 s vs cargo nextest 5.7 s for the same 186 tests (sequential binaries are the cost; doctests 0.6 s). Lima/bench-count 11 min once in 3.9 (callgrind entry point gotcha), oracle lane 34 s: not bottlenecks.

Filed: by-b0q.4.5 (gate, P2, executor, review:inline): rust tests via nextest with a cargo test fallback, tournaments/play under nice, and an AGENTS.md line on tournament cost and Warden reruns.

Enact changed (staging 63bf36d): enact-plan's stoppage audit now flags sampled bars that demand perfection on a handful of cases (agree held-out inputs, a pass rate and a fallback up front); warden.md's new "Expensive reruns" bullet reruns long measurements only where they gate acceptance, never reported-only figures (3.13's 62-min rerun).

Not filed, for the next plan: engine speed is the lever for every future MCTS gate (a 2x faster engine halves each hour-long pairing); decision 7's fixed 100 games per pairing could be a sequential test that stops once the Wilson interval clears the gate (the gates passed at 92% and 67-72% against 60%/55%) - both are user decisions.
