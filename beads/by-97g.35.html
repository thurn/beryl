Description

Part of the ladder epic by-97g. Read docs/ai-strategy.md first: §4.3 (the gate), §5 (the suite) and §10 ("Does a fixed entry stay fixed from run to run?").

Why

On 2026-11-12 five rungs were gated and four failed at the suite on margins that look like sampling noise (docs/ladder.md and the gate beads' notes):

  • decline-both-teferi-untaps, which the champion passed 10/10 in every gate, came out 7/10 for hold (by-97g.25), 7/10 for waste (by-97g.27) and 8/10 for pimc18 (by-97g.10). pimc18 changes only the number of worlds. The re-open alone failed hold, which had fixed three claimed entries (staff-in-upkeep, subtlety-before-own-draw and one more) and moved jace-loots-before-draw from 0 to 9/10 and discard-emperor-over-spare-crypt from 0 to 10/10.
  • mid (by-97g.9) failed only on one re-opened entry.
  • combat (by-97g.29) moved beza-trinket-into-blockers from 0 to 8/10 and manifest-into-samurai from 1 to 8/10 with nothing re-opened or worse, and failed because a claim counts only at 10/10.
  • The champion's own counts drift between gates too: staff-in-upkeep was 6/10 in one gate and 5/10 in others.

An entry is "fixed" only at 10 of 10 runs. If a play is right in 97% of runs, it comes out 10/10 only about 74% of the time, so one unlucky run decides a gate. §10 names this risk and says the gate then needs a rerun rule. This bead adds one.

Step 1: measure the noise first

On Cygnus (BERYL_CACHE=D:\beryl-cache), run the champion (the family on docs/ladder.md's Champion: line) on decline-both-teferi-untaps and on staff-in-upkeep from 20 seeds: just blunders CHAMPION --only decline-both-teferi-untaps,staff-in-upkeep --seeds 20. Write the pass counts per budget in this bead's notes. If the champion itself fails decline-both-teferi-untaps at least once in the 40 runs, that confirms the entry is noisy. Either way, build the rule below; the numbers go in the commit body.

Step 2: the rule, in just gate

rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/gate.rs (compare, suite, constants SEEDS, RUNS, BUDGETS) runs the suite once per agent with 5 seeds at 3M and 30M (10 runs per entry), then marks each entry claimed-fixed, re-opened or worse. Change it to:

  1. Run the first suite as today (seeds 0 to 4).
  2. Close calls. An entry is a close call when its first-round mark is re-opened, or it is claimed and the challenger passed 7 or more of 10 without passing all 10.
  3. Second round. For the close calls only, run both agents again from seeds 5 to 9 at both budgets, so each close call has 20 runs per agent. Add a first-seed parameter to blunders::run (rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/blunders.rs; run_seed(name, k) already takes the seed index k, so loop k over first..first + seeds) rather than duplicating the loop.
  4. Judged on 20 runs (CHAMPION and CHALLENGER counts out of 20):
    • re-opened stands when the challenger passes at least 3 fewer of the 20 than the champion (for example, champion 20/20 and challenger 17/20 or fewer). Otherwise the entry gets no mark.
    • claimed-fixed holds when the challenger passes 18 or more of 20 and at least 6 more than the champion.
  5. worse keeps its first-round rule (4 or more fewer of 10). Entries that were not close calls keep their first-round marks.
  6. The printed table shows the 20-run counts for close calls (for example 17/20), marks rows judged in the second round with (rerun), and keeps the summary line and the GATE: PASS / GATE: FAIL line. The pass condition is unchanged: at least one claimed-fixed (or no claims), no re-opened, no worse.

Defaults chosen here (no one will answer questions; K31): the 7-of-10 threshold, the 20-run judgement, "3 fewer" for a re-open and "18 of 20 and 6 more" for a claim. Record any change to them, with the reason, in this bead's notes.

Step 3: docs

In docs/ai-strategy.md:

  • §4.3 step 2: describe the second round and the 20-run judgement.
  • §5 "Fixed and open": an entry the gate judged on 20 runs is fixed or open by the 20-run rule for that gate.
  • §10: replace the "Does a fixed entry stay fixed..." question with one line saying the rerun rule answered it, with step 1's numbers.
  • §5 "Growth changes no past verdict" stays true: the rule changes no row already in docs/ladder.md.

Tests

In gate.rs's tests: a 7/10 re-open that comes out 19/20 against 20/20 is not re-opened; one that comes out 16/20 against 20/20 is; a claimed 8/10 that comes out 18/20 against 2/20 is claimed-fixed; a claimed 6/10 gets no second round; non-close entries are judged as before. Keep the existing tests passing. cargo test -p beryl_cli.
