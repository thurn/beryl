Description

Why

The scenario generator (rust/crates/beryl_cards/src/derive/: mod.rs, play.rs, position.rs, witness.rs) plays only player 0 acting at the first main-phase priority, an attack, steps through turn 4, and a sorcery cast. UNREACHED in beryl_cards/src/tests/derive.rs lists 27 abilities it cannot reach: it never has the opponent attack, cast or draw, never deals damage after the card, never makes the card leave the battlefield, and never uses an ability that works from a graveyard. Plan: Designs E3, "Stimuli".

What to build

In play.rs (openings, awaited, walk):

  • A recipe for each event a card can listen to or replace: the opponent attacks with a creature; either player casts a spell of a kind the trigger's filter names; either player draws, discards, gains life, loses life; a creature dies; the card itself leaves the battlefield (a removal prop targets it, Prop::Removal); damage is dealt to a player, to a creature and by the card; a land is played; a permanent enters.
  • Openings from the graveyard and from exile for a card with an ability or a casting permission there.
  • A test: every TriggerEvent variant and every replaced event of beryl_card/src/continuous.rs has a recipe, written as an exhaustive match, so a new event without one does not compile.
  • The cap MOST_RUNS = 16 an opening stays; Generated::cut is reported by just card check.

Then shrink UNREACHED: every entry your recipes now reach is removed, and the test that holds the list to the truth must pass. Report the entries that remain and why.

Generated scenarios run in the oracles. A recipe an exporter cannot express is listed in data/oracle-exporter-gaps.json, or the exporter is taught it.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Every trigger event and replaced event has a recipe, held by a test. UNREACHED is shorter; the close reason lists what remains and why. Generated scenarios pass in every slot order and in the oracles.
