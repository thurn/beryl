Description

Part of the ladder epic (read it and docs/ai-strategy.md §6 rung 3 first). Where the agent is far ahead or far behind, every move looks alike to its rollouts and noise chooses; a human closing out a won game makes the obvious play.

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs: the root's estimate (the chosen candidate's tree-averaged mean, after TURN_DISCOUNT), guarded, AnchorCheck::overrides, ANCHOR_MARGIN, Options.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/agreement.rs: seed agreement in decided (above 0.95 or below 0.05) and contested positions, the evidence (60% to 78% after the discount, against 90% contested).

Build

A new option decided: when the root's estimate lies above 0.9 or below 0.1, the anchor holds at every decision kind, not only guarded priority casts, and its margin rises (a named constant, 0.05 to start). In contested positions the search plays as the champion does. Moves depend on the seed alone.

Bar (pre-registered)

Plausible: the suite regresses no entry the champion passes (none of the four targets this rung; the Deep Seer Oust was in a contested position); then just ladder pimc72/hidden/rollout/tuned/anchor+decided@3M --bar plausible accepts "no change" (elo0 -25, elo1 0, alpha = beta = 0.05, cap 2,000). Also report beryl_cli agreement over the match's decided positions for both agents: the rung claims higher agreement there, and the number goes in the notes beside the Elo.

Tests

Crafted root statistics: a decided position plays greedy's answer at a target decision unless the search leads by the raised margin; a contested one plays as before.

Record

Results go in this bead's notes and as a row in docs/ladder.md; commit no logs. On a pass: change deep_searcher in rust/crates/beryl_search/src/lib.rs to the challenger's settings, name this bead and the match in its doc comment, update the champion table in docs/ai-strategy.md §3, and run just blunders once more on the shipped agent.
