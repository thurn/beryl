Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Verbs' and the fly entry of data/ui-conventions.json; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/verbs/fly.rs and the shapes by-q44s.3.16 added. Web: ui/src/stage/drawn.ts (HELD, HELD_TURN, heldUp: a card you draw alone flies from the library across the table to a place beside the stack, is held up large to be read, sinking a little, and drops into the right end of the hand; swept: each of several drawn together sweeps past that place without stopping), ui/src/stage/desktop.ts (drawn(rest): where that place is; beside), ui/src/stage/tracks.ts (TURN 0.27 and TURNING 0.2: when a card that leaves face down and lands face up is edge-on, and how long it turns; DRAW_TURN at 0.16, each 0.08; turningOf, flatAt, halfTurned, draws, drawsToRead; and in glide, the rule for a card that lands the other way up the table), StepTiming::each (how long each card of several moves). Cases: draw, draw of several, opponent draw.

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build:

  • A turn in flight: Segment gains turning: Option<Turning> with Turning { at, each } as shares of the flight. With it, Pose::turn stays at its start value until at - each, is half way at at and at its end value from at + each, top over bottom, as flatAt has it. fly sets it from the act as turningOf does: DRAW_TURN for a draw, TURN and TURNING for any other flight whose face changes. Copy the four numbers to verbs.fly.turn.
  • A card that lands face down the other way up the table (the opponent's draw) leaves already turned the way it will lie, as glide's round has it.
  • Case draw: the viewer's card flies to the place drawn(rest) gives beside the stack (copy it as verbs.fly.held in normalized device coordinates with its scale; it is placed relative to the camera, as hands are), is held there sinking by verbs.fly.held_sink, and drops into the right end of the hand, in the shares of the step that heldUp uses (copy HELD).
  • Case draw of several: each card sweeps through that place without stopping (swept) and comes down into the hand, which made room for all of them as the first left.
  • Case opponent draw: from their library to their hand, face down, dropping toward the middle of the table and rising into the hand.
  • Check times: the start and end of each turn, and of the hold.

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/verbs_fly_draw.rs, on views and scripts built by hand with testing::ViewBuilder, ScriptBuilder and testing::timing: for each case of the verb in data/ui-conventions.json (draw, draw of several, opponent draw), a script with that case's duration and delay plays to its end with every object at its placement (agent::steps::rest_equals_placement finds nothing), no_jumps finds nothing, and step_times finds nothing. A drawn card's FACE_DOWN state clears at DRAW_TURN.at of its flight; it is within 1 logical point of the held place for the whole of the hold; three drawn together are never at rest between the library and the hand; an opponent's spell is edge-on at 0.27 of its flight; a track of a draw followed by a cast in one script holds every segment (Track::push never returns Full).

After this bead fly is whole: run just b3d test --update and just b3d timeline-parity --update if those commands exist on staging, and commit the lowered lists.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d film verbs-zones --from step:<a draw> --frames 12 --crop 'card #<its slot>'; just b3d check verbs-zones; just b3d lane.
