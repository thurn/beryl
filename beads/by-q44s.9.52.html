Description

Symptom: the text-overlap interface check (beryl_scene/src/agent/text_overlap.rs) finds lines of text drawn over other text in Beryl 3D. Each is listed in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json under text-overlap until fixed. Distinct cases, with a test and moment that shows each (just b3d tree <test> --at <t>, just b3d compare <recording> --at <t> --scale 2):

  • The library and graveyard counts collide: "Your library N" over "Your graveyard N" (b3d:chains-of-mephistopheles/chains-before-dredge/you/1440x900 t=4343ms), and the opponent's (b3d:combat-attackers/opponent/1440x900 t=44522ms). Pile counts: table/pile.rs and the hud counts.
  • The life change float "−3" (floats::LIFE_CHANGE, hud/counts.rs change_look) over the "Combat" notice text (b3d:adeline-resplendent-cathar/adelines-human-arrives-attacking/you/1440x900 t=1322ms). The web draws the change on the orb's other rim (.life-change, ui/src/screens/styles/table.css).
  • The life total "You N" over the lands row's count "Your lands right N" (b3d:stress-1024/you/1600x900@2 t=0ms).
  • The game log over the mana pool's "{C}" and over the opponent's library count (b3d:choice-trigger-order/you/2560x1080 t=75704ms; b3d:winds-of-abandon/overloaded-a-basic-per-creature-exiled/you/1440x900 t=4091ms).
  • Stack abilities over the "Attackers declared" notice (b3d:order-long/you/1440x900 t=2111ms), and the scry tray's "TOP" heading over the library count (b3d:verbs-scry/you/1440x900 t=3316ms).

For each, compare with the web at the same moment: fix the placement where the web keeps them apart; where the web overlaps them on purpose, keep its test-faults.json entry and say why in the commit. Finish with just b3d test --update.

Acceptance criteria

Each listed case is fixed or kept with a reason in its commit; just b3d test passes with the text-overlap entries lowered by --update; compare pictures named on the bead.
