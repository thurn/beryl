Description

Step two of the presentation track (epic by-w8gv.21; docs/project-50k.md, Target architecture, Presentation, "Causes"; K23). The bead before this in the lane gave every kept event its cause and a name for its stack entry (read its handoff first: the tag's type, the register and the scope sites). This bead adds the second tag, what an event is simultaneous with, and the few events the script builder still has to guess for want of them.

What the session guesses today

rust/crates/beryl_session/src/script/infer.rs, which this bead must make unnecessary but does not edit:

  • together: one move of several cards; permanents leaving for one reason (a sweeper's creatures, whatever their owners' piles); cards collected into a library; a counterspell leaving with the spell it countered; what a permanent held in exile coming back as it leaves; taps with taps and with the attack; untaps; reveals; each player's life; like counters.
  • tell: combat damage dealt at once, and a life total changing with the damage that changed it.
  • order: the two blows of one fight, and the sources of one payment tapping ahead of their mana.
  • spans.rs Builder.lines (a combat line begins only if the view's end still has it, because no event says a block was declared) and build.rs Screen.broken (a guess that a target is a new object). by-w8gv.21.10's handoff names both.

Some of those are the rules' simultaneity and some are how the stage shows things (payments tap together; a counterspell follows its spell). Only the first kind is the engine's to say. List each of infer.rs's rules in a comment on this bead as "the rules': tagged here" or "presentation: stays the session's, read from tags", so the session's bead knows which is which.

What to build

  1. A group on every kept event: events with the same group happened as one event of the rules. Recommended: a counter in the sink, bumped where a simultaneous event begins and stamped beside the cause; nothing when the sink is off. The sites, each with its citation:
    • one instruction of effect data acting on every object it names (beryl_rules/src/interpret/run.rs, Run::instruction: "An instruction acts on every object it names at once where the engine has a primitive that does"; CR 608.2c, and 701.8 for a destroy of several);
    • combat damage (combat.rs; CR 510.2: "all combat damage that's been assigned is dealt simultaneously"), with the life it changes;
    • one pass of the state-based actions (sba.rs; CR 704.3: "performs all applicable state-based actions simultaneously as a single event");
    • the untap step (turn.rs; CR 502.3) and attackers tapping as they are declared (combat.rs; CR 508.1f);
    • the payment of a cost (cast/costs.rs, costs.rs, mana/pay.rs; CR 601.2h: "Partial payments are not allowed");
    • a permanent leaving and what it exiled returning (CR 610.3), as the linked store does it (linked.rs). Cards drawn one at a time are not simultaneous (CR 121.2); leave Event::Draw's count as it is.
  2. A block declared (CR 509.1) as an event beside Event::Attack, through the pipeline so a later "becomes blocked" trigger can listen, or as a note() if that would cost the corpus anything: measure. Event::kind and the listener masks change only if it goes through the pipeline.
  3. Whatever the previous bead's handoff says is still missing for the session to stop guessing which object an arrow's target became (Screen.broken): the engine's own word, CR 400.7, is Event::ZoneChange's new object; say in the handoff what the session should read.
  4. Tests in beryl_rules/src/tests/events.rs and tests/combat.rs: a sweeper's permanents share a group across both players; a fight's two blows and the lives they change share one; two state-based deaths in one check share one and a death in the next check does not; a payment's taps and mana share one; two draws do not.
  5. Relayout, take_from and truncate carry the group. docs/engine-architecture.md §8.1 says what a group is.

  • reveals one after another: presentation (no rule; CR 701.20a is one card at a time). A reveal kept between two moves of one batch is in that batch's group.
  • each player's life (LifeLoss with LifeLoss, LifeGain with LifeGain): the rules' when one instruction does it (GainLife/LoseLife over several players, or ForEachPlayer of one such, CR 101.4/608.2f): tagged here. Otherwise presentation.

Decisions and deviations:

  1. The group is not a counter. Tag gains 'together: bool' (this event is of the group of the event kept before it); Tag::groups numbers a list. Reason: beryl_session/src/game/views.rs tells a replayed body's repeated events by == on Tagged (windows/strip_prefix against self.paused), and a body run again from its start (architecture 7.3) would stamp new counter values on the same events. A flag about the neighbor is the same in every run and survives take_from, truncate, drain and relayout with no bookkeeping.
  2. Groups nest by joining: one opened inside another is part of the outer (a mana ability inside a payment; a return inside a state-based batch).
  3. A batch move marks its events at the push (Sink::push_joined, no branch a push lacks) rather than opening a group per move_batch: opening one there cost two branches on every zone change.
  4. Not every instruction is a group: interpret/run.rs at_once lists the single actions (CR 608.2f). Draw (CR 121.2), Dig, Search, Scry, Fateseal, Explore, ManifestDread, Choose, LookAt, Counter, CounterInto, ReturnTransformed, PutBack, FlipCoin, Modify, ChangeRule, casting and permits, Delay, GetEmblem are not; a batch move inside any of them is still its own group. ForEachPlayer of one at_once instruction is one group across the players (CR 101.4); of a sequence it is not (the engine carries the whole sequence out per player, where CR 608.2e would have each step simultaneous: not changed here).
  5. Payment: the automatic mana payment (mana::pay) is one group; a manual payment is one group per source (tap with its mana), since decisions come between, and the session joins them by Cause::entry. Non-mana costs (cast/parts.rs: sacrifice, discard, exile) are each their own event, not grouped with the mana: CR 601.2h has them paid 'in any order', not at once, and the cast machine tail-calls through them. The bead's own text lists 'payments tap together' as presentation.
  6. Event::Block is note-only (Sink::note under Sink::records), not through the pipeline. Not measured against the pipeline as the bead asked: through it, every block declaration of every rollout would walk the battlefield and run trigger matching for an event no card listens to; a 'becomes blocked' card moves it into event::settle when it comes. Event::kind and the listener masks are unchanged.
  7. The life a land's own replacement costs as it enters (pay_life_to_enter) is not grouped with the land's ZoneChange when the land is the first of its batch: it is told by its Cause::Object{source: the land} directly before it. Grouping it cost a branch per moved object.
  8. The combat test is in tests/events/groups.rs with the rest, on tests::cast::Game, not in tests/combat.rs.

Handoff: pushed 04765771 (staging).

THE TAG (beryl_kernel/src/cause.rs): Tag { cause, together: bool }. together = this event happened as one event of the rules with the event kept before it. Tag::groups(tags) numbers a list's groups (same number = one event). No counter: a replayed body run must produce equal Tagged values (session/game/views.rs compares them), see the decisions comment.

SINK API (beryl_kernel/src/event.rs): together()/apart() open and close a group (nested ones join the outer; one branch each with the sink off; always 'let r = f(); apart(); r', never a ? between); together_with_last() opens one the last kept event is part of; push_joined(e, || joined) marks a batch's events after its first, with whatever was kept between them, at no branch beyond push's; records() says whether the sink keeps events. Sink::is_idle now also requires no open group.

SITES, each cited: interpret/run.rs carry_out + at_once (CR 608.2f single-action instructions) and for_each_player (CR 101.4); event.rs move_batch_to (a batch, and the CR 610.3 return joined to it); combat.rs deal (CR 510.2), finish_attack (CR 508.1f with the Attack event), finish_blocks/note_blocks (Event::Block, CR 509.1); turn.rs untap (CR 502.3); sba.rs end (players losing at once, CR 104.4a; a check's batch is its group, CR 704.3); mana/pay.rs pay and manual_pay (CR 601.2h).

Event::Block { player, blocker, attacker }: note-only, one per blocker in battlefield order, one group, kept as the declaration finishes (engine.rs passes the sink to combat::finish_blocks). Relayout has it; beryl_session/src/protocol/event.rs drops it from views beside the stack events, so no view, fixture or script changed. .74 gives it a GameEvent.

SCREEN.BROKEN (item 3): nothing more is missing from the engine. Its word is already in the stream: Event::ZoneChange.object is the NEW object (CR 400.7), an ObjectRef with the slot's new incarnation, and every target is an ObjectRef too (StackArrival.entry.targets, Cast.targets, Ability.targets). A target whose slot a later ZoneChange names with a higher incarnation points at an object that is gone: that arrow is broken for good, with no guess. The protocol's GameEvent must carry the incarnation (or the session must compare before converting) for the builder to read it.

FOR .74: the classification comment above sorts infer.rs's rules. Groups are contiguous runs. A group can span a view boundary when a body pauses mid-instruction (ForEachPlayer with a choice): the first event after the pause may say together about an event the earlier view reported. Reveal notes kept between two moves of a batch are in the batch's group. Damage to a player has no LifeLoss event.

COUNT (just count-gate): before 389,549,973; after 389,798,599 (+248,626, +0.064%); count-gate.toml unchanged, gate passes at +0.12% against the bar. Not within a few thousand.
