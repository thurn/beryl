Close reason

Single-use cards act on click; unpaid casts cancel (Escape, right-click, prompt Cancel) via a new session cancel command; fan hits follow the visible strips; Space/Enter/Escape keys, focus ring, no text selection, refusal shake, menu buttons. Already fixed: none. Not built: refusal reason in inspector, drag-back cancel (noted on by-isv.13).

Description

  • One-item menus: clicking a card with a single legal use opens a one-item menu ("Play Plains", "Cast Teferi"), so a play takes 2 clicks where Arena takes 1. A card with one use acts on click; menus are only for real choices.
  • Text selection: dragging a card selects the page's text and washes the table blue. The stage needs user-select: none, except in text fields.
  • No cancel: once a cast starts it cannot be cancelled, and Escape does nothing during target selection. Allow cancelling until the cost is paid: Escape, right-click, or dragging the card back.
  • Silent refusals: clicking an unplayable card does nothing. Give a brief shake or dim, and put the reason in the inspector on hover, not in a toast.
  • Fan hit-testing: the raised hand card covers its neighbour's visible strip, so clicks land on the wrong card. The visible part of each card must be what the pointer hits.
  • Keyboard: Space or Enter does the primary action (pass or confirm), and Escape cancels. The turn button and prompt buttons must be reachable by Tab, with visible focus. Escape closes the settings panel.
  • Roles: menu entries should be exposed as buttons.

Files: ui/src/game/Board.tsx, ui/src/game/CastStep.tsx, ui/src/game/CardLayer.tsx, ui/src/components/Controls.tsx, ui/src/screens/Game.tsx.

Before changing anything, reproduce each item on current staging. Use just ui-live, as the ui-review skill describes (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md), or a fixture. The findings come from reviews of the build before the 2026-11-05 UI fixes, so some may already be fixed. Drop those, and list them in the close reason.

Design rules (see the epic): follow the skill's design philosophy, and add no standing words to the table. What a player needs at a glance is shown as icons, badges, numbers, colour or position. Everything else goes behind disclosure: hover, the inspector, a pile's browser, or the game log.

Acceptance criteria

just ui-test checks that:

  • one click on a single-use land plays it;
  • Escape during target selection cancels, and the card is back in hand;
  • after a mouse drag, getSelection().toString() is empty;
  • Space passes priority;
  • a click at the visible strip of each hand card acts on that card;
  • menu entries are found by role button.

Executor plan, re-validated on staging 82fb5a9: (1) one-use cards act on click: a land plays, a single ability activates, and a castable card with no real choice (one cast, no X, one payment plan) casts at once, as a drop does; the cast step opens only for a real choice. (2) .stage has user-select:none (inputs excepted). (3) Cancel: the engine never rewinds, so beryl_session now checkpoints the game at the human's priority when they start a cast/activation and offers a new 'cancel' IPC command while the cast is unpaid (DecisionView.cancellable); it restores state and truncates the replay log. UI: Escape, right-click, and a Cancel button in the cast's prompt. Dragging the card back is not done. (4) Refused click: an own hand card offering nothing shakes. The hover reason in the inspector is NOT done: the view carries no reason why a card can't be played, and guessing (timing, mana, land drop) would be wrong for flash and alternate costs; left for the final ui-review to re-judge. (5) Fan hit-testing: the raised hand card is drawn by a ghost copy that the pointer passes through, while each card's element keeps the fan's z-order and hits by its fan footprint (plus the raised top above it). (6) Keyboard: Space/Enter pass or confirm (declaration, cast step, combat finish) unless a button has focus; Escape cancels the cast and closes settings; buttons get a focus-visible ring. (7) Action-menu entries are plain buttons in a labelled group.
