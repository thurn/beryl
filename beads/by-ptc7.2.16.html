Description

Quicksilver Gargantuan's 2011-01-01 ruling d3d1912543d6 says that a noncreature copy does not have power or toughness, and a later effect that makes it a creature sets its size. In the trial scenario, P0 controls Oko and seven Islands. P1 controls Portable Hole and two Plains and has Get Lost. Oko animates P1's Hole as a 3/3. The Gargantuan copies it as a noncreature Portable Hole. P1 destroys their original with Get Lost. On turn 3, Oko animates P0's copy. Beryl reports it as 7/7 where the layer 7b effect should set it to 3/3.

Read rust/cards/cards_2010/som/quicksilver_gargantuan.rs, rust/cards/cards_2019_h2/eld/oko_thief_of_crowns.rs and rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/layers/. Check the ordering of layer 1 copy exceptions and layer 7b power/toughness-setting effects.

Acceptance criteria

A Gargantuan scenario copies Oko's animated artifact as a noncreature and sees it become 3/3 when animated. Run the oracles, update the ledger entry and document the regression guard in docs/bugs.md. The count gate stays unchanged.
