The cast's steps take their answers from a source: casting inside a resolution

  • Description
  • Acceptance criteria
  • Blocking4
  • Changelog

Description

Why

A cast in progress is Pending::Casting and a paused resolution is Pending::Resolving (beryl_kernel/src/pending.rs), and the state has one pending. A paused resolution is also replayed from its start over the state as it was before it began (resolve.rs), so a cast that had committed anything would be lost or doubled. Today Effect::MayCast only records one offer (Resolution.offer), made as Pending::CastOffer after the resolution has finished, of the first candidate card, and Malformed::Cast requires it to be the effect's last instruction. Cascade casts in the middle of an effect, and Panglacial Wurm in the middle of a search. Plan: Designs H, "Casting inside a resolution".

What to build

  • The steps of a cast (the casting module's steps.rs: begin_cast, advance over X, target counts and targets, total_cost, begin_payment, pay_mana, the non-mana parts, finish) become functions that take each answer from an answer source:
    • at priority the source is Pending::Casting, one committed step for each answer, as now. A rollout's decisions and the count must not change.
    • inside a resolution the source is the resolution's own record (Resolution::answer, choices.rs), so the whole cast is part of the transaction and is replayed with it. The new spell goes on the stack above the resolving entry, Event::Cast is emitted and cast triggers are noted, and the body goes on with its next instruction. No player gets priority until the resolution is done.
  • Effect::MayCast may stand anywhere in an effect and offers a set of cards (the player picks one, or none). Remove Malformed::Cast's rule that it comes last; keep a rule for whatever really cannot be said. Pending::CastOffer and Resolution.offer go, or stay only as the one-answer case of the same code.
  • Mana: a cast inside a resolution pays through the solver and may ask for a payment plan through the record. A mana ability that needs a choice asks through the record too.
  • The record holds 32 answers in Small (Resolving.record). A longer one is Full.
  • Extend the replay differential (inline answers against replayed ones, a test that plays rollouts both ways and compares state hashes) to a resolution that casts a spell with a target and an X, then asks another question.
  • Malcolm, Alluring Scoundrel and every card that uses may_cast_free must behave as before: their scenarios and the oracles hold it.

No new card here. The three beads that follow in this lane write them.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

A test card casts a targeted spell in the middle of its effect and continues. Casts at priority ask the same decisions as before: rollouts_never_change holds and the count is unchanged; the close reason gives it.
