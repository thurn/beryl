Rows scroll by wheel and drag with slides, fades and +N counts; conventions entry added (57f23b438)

Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Objects and placement' ('A row that cannot give every card its strip scrolls'), 'Checks and isolation' (the one entry Beryl 3D adds to data/ui-conventions.json) and the epic's rule 17; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/table/rows.rs (lay_row: a row too long even when piled shows as many groups as fit from RowRules::scroll and leaves the rest undrawn), src/table/place.rs (Scroll, ROWS), src/table/scene.rs (Table::scroll, which nothing sets), src/pointer (from by-q44s.3.27), src/input.rs (PointerPhase::Wheel), src/layout (Layer::float, floats::ROW_COUNT, from by-q44s.3.3); beryl_inspect src/stress.rs (stress-255, whose rows scroll). Data: data/ui-conventions.json's entries and its section 'Not seen in Arena' (kind: "not-seen"); just conventions regenerates docs/ui-conventions.md.

Build:

  • The wheel over a row scrolls it by one group for each notch, toward the end the wheel turns to. A horizontal drag that begins on a card of a row that has cards off an end, and on no card that may be picked up, scrolls it by one group for each pointer.scroll_step_points (a new key, 48) the pointer travels. The scroll offset is kept by the table, for each row of each side, and stays within the row.
  • A scroll changes placement, so each card that stays in view gets a segment to its new place over slide.regroup_ms, and a card that comes into view fades in where it rests.
  • Each end of a row with cards off it shows their number as a float (floats::ROW_COUNT), "+12", just outside the row's end. A row with none shows none.
  • A script that plays while a row is scrolled moves a card that is out of view from the nearer end of the row, where placement rests it.
  • Add the entry to data/ui-conventions.json: kind not-seen, name "A battlefield row that scrolls", with the rule in one sentence, and run just conventions. This is the one change this milestone makes to that file; the push runs the web interface's checks once, which is expected. It is not a numbered exception.

Do not build: anything on the phone layout (milestone 4).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/scroll.rs: a row of 40 different permanents at 1440 by 900 shows a count at its right end; three wheel notches move the first visible card three groups on and the counts change by three at each end; a drag of 96 points scrolls two groups; scrolling past the end stops at it; every card in view keeps its uncovered strip; rest_equals_placement finds nothing after a scroll; a row that fits ignores the wheel; a frame during a scroll's slide allocates nothing.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d tree stress-255 --at end --do 'move 800,600'; just conventions; just b3d check stress-255; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

The tests pass; just b3d check stress-255 reports no reachable, on-screen or rest-placement fault; docs/ui-conventions.md lists the entry under 'Not seen in Arena' and just conventions leaves it unchanged; the push passed the web interface's lanes that the data file selects.

Decisions and deviations:

  • The bead named slide.regroup_ms, which did not exist. Added data/beryl3d/space.json slide.regroup_ms = 170, equal to the fly verb's regroup in data/ui-conventions.json, under space.json as the client-only own.* times are (the epic forbids adding to ui-conventions.json). Also added pointer.wheel_notch_points = 16 (beryl_host's LINE_POINTS now reads it) and layout.desktop.row_count_gap = 4.
  • Tests are in beryl_scene/tests/all/scroll.rs, not tests/scroll.rs, because rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md wants one test binary. They use 40 different lands: at 1440x900 the creatures row fits 39 of 40 cards, so only the shorter lands row scrolls far enough for the three-notch test.
  • 'Every card in view keeps its uncovered strip' is tested as: the spacing between cards and the reachable-fault count do not get worse after a scroll. The reachable check already faults rows packed at the least strip before any scroll, because perspective trims them below 24x24 points. by-q44s.3.43 owns that.
  • Acceptance 'just b3d check stress-255 reports no reachable fault' cannot hold until by-q44s.3.43 lands. The check reports 153 reachable, 153 blank-card and 7 step-shown at 1600x900, all at t=0 or in the recording's steps, exactly the counts data/beryl3d/test-faults.json lists. There are no on-screen or rest-placement faults, and just b3d test b3d:stress-255 passes at all four windows.
  • Drag: no battlefield card can be held (Client::holdable takes only the viewer's hand cards), so a drag may begin on any card of a row that scrolls.
  • The gate limits for stress-255 and stress-1024 rose for the four count floats; the commit body has old and new numbers. RowWindow keeps its numbers as u16: usize fields pushed each waiting script past 1024 bytes, and verbs-zones' handle_allocations rose by 2.
