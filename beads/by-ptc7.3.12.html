Description

What to do

For each of the card files under rust/cards/, run just card check <name> --no-oracles and read its generated line (the generated bar and the rewritten skill are dependencies; follow the skill).

  • A card whose generated scenarios kill every mutant and agree with the oracles: delete its hand-written scenarios, except any that data/rulings-ledger.json names for it and any marked Scenario::interaction. If none remain, delete the scenarios module.
  • A card with survivors: keep only the scenarios that kill a survivor, that the ledger names, or that are interactions. To find them, remove one scenario at a time and rerun the check; a scenario whose removal changes nothing goes.
  • Every card: cut its doc comment to card-specific rulings (decision P3 of the plan). A quotation of what "draw a card" or "exile" means goes; a note on why the definition is shaped as it is, or a ruling about an unusual interaction, stays with its citation. Then just citations.
  • Use a prelude function where it makes the card definition clearer.

Never delete a card implementation, and never change a definition's behavior: card-text must pass with no allowlist change, and the mutation check, the vocabulary test (every_entry_a_card_says_is_carried_out_by_a_hand_written_scenario) and the full oracle sweep (just oracle) must pass. Where deleting a scenario leaves a vocabulary entry with no hand-written scenario anywhere, move that scenario to beryl_cards/src/library/ under the module of the rule it shows instead of deleting it.

The keystones and partners of by-bajq keep every scenario their ledger entries name; the audit beads of milestone 2 are dependencies so that those names exist.

Work in batches of about twenty cards a push.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Every card file is at the new bar. The close reason gives the number of cards with no scenarios module, the median and total lines of card files before and after, and the cargo check --features scenarios seconds of the set crates before and after.
