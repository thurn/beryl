Description

Part of the long-think epic (its D1). The expert (rust/crates/beryl_search/src/expert/) carries the old Vintage deck's card knowledge (Timetwister, Mana Drain, draw_safe). In the cb8d5d5f mirror it is a weak bar: over 400 games on 2026-11-05 it beat greedy 60% and random 86%, against 83% and 98% in the Vintage mirror. Retune it for the 17 new cards (by-0w2's table: Counterspell, Reprieve, Stock Up, Thirst for Discovery, Portable Hole, Damn, Winds of Abandon, Subtlety, Snapcaster Mage, Trinket Mage, Beza, Currency Converter, Teferi, Hero of Dominaria, Jace, Wielder of Mysteries, Fractured Identity, Abhorrent Oculus, Floodfarm Verge), starting from the decking and attack-target causes by-0w2.17 found in greedy.

Then freeze it as a separate agent, expert-frozen (ai-strategy.md §6): its own module and registry entry that later expert tuning does not touch, with a test pinning its moves on a few seeded games so a refactor can't change it silently.

Acceptance criteria

  • In the cb8d5d5f mirror over 400 seat-alternated games on seeds chosen after the last change: expert v greedy (as retuned by by-0w2.17) at least 75%, expert v random at least 97%. If three tuning rounds don't reach both, freeze the best version so far and record its measured rates in the close reason.
  • No transcript-review bar (by-ovn: such bars don't converge). Reviews may guide tuning.
  • expert-frozen is in the agent registry, plays identically to the retuned expert, and a test pins its moves on seeded games.

Done in one tuning round. Acceptance on fresh seed 20261106, chosen after the last change (400 seat-alternated games each): expert v greedy 313/400 = 78.2% [73.9, 82.0], expert v random 395/400 = 98.8% [97.1, 99.5]. Baseline, same build before the changes, seed 100: 45.5% and 91.8%. Tuning seeds 1-3 (1200 games): 78.1% and 98.3%.

Changes, each measured on seeds 1-3:

  • New cards: Counterspell is a hard counter. Reprieve counters for tempo when they can't recast (a spare-counter threshold). Subtlety answers creature/walker spells, evoked only for a game-winner. Thirst and Snapcaster go at their end step, along with any other activation greedy would make (Converter loot). New removal thresholds: Portable Hole 25, Winds 35 (overload left to greedy's sweep rule), Fractured Identity 55. Floodfarm Verge counts as both colors. Mana Tithe logic is gated on Tithe being in the game. 45.5 -> 63.9.
  • Decking: draw_safe now follows greedy's may_draw (library > 10). Walker abilities that draw (Teferi, Hero +1) are skipped when unsafe. Teferi, Hero uses -3/-8; Jace, Wielder uses -8 only when it wins. -> 69.3.
  • Jace, Wielder: the +1 mills ourselves while the opponent's Jace, Wielder is live (in play, or in the game and not yet in their graveyard/exile). An opponent's Jace, Wielder near a decking win (their library <= 12 or ours <= 15) is worth 150 + loyalty, both as a permanent and as a spell to counter. The Telepath -9 emblem waits while their Jace is live. -> 73 -> 75.9 (with the Subtlety fix).
  • Subtlety is never evoked outside a response. A subagent's transcript review found it evoked on empty stacks in 27 of 55 losses.
  • Mana Crypt is cast only with <= 3 lands, a spell in hand costing more than lands+1, and life > 10. Never casting it, as greedy does, left the expert land-screwed. 75.9 -> 78.1. Tried and dropped as neutral: a next-ultimate walker bonus, all-in attacks at one walker (never chosen), no hold-for-counter, Subtlety at end step.

Freeze: rust/crates/beryl_search/src/frozen/ holds copies of the expert, combat.rs, rollout.rs and values.rs, so greedy tuning can't move it either. It is registered as expert-frozen. tests/frozen.rs pins an FNV hash of every choice in 4 seeded games (2 mirror, 2 v random). Before pinning, the live ExpertAgent gave the same hash, and expert-frozen reproduced 313/400 and 395/400 on seed 20261106 exactly. The bench corpus is now cut from expert-frozen games, so future expert tuning won't void baselines; it was re-pinned once. expert_beats_random asserts 38/40 (it gets 40/40).
