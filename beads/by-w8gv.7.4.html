Close reason

Permissions, cost increases and flashback's replacement are Effects-store entries; queue, CastPermission and the stack/casting riders deleted; State 19,832 bytes; count 398,897,988 -> 396,070,902, no bump

Description

Milestone 4 of Project 50k (plan K4, epic by-w8gv.7). Permissions to cast are the last queue beside the Effects store: State::permissions holds eight CastPermissions, each a permission with three card-specific riders (extra, exile, flashback). This bead makes them Effects-store entries with permission, cost-change and replacement payloads, and deletes the queue and the type.

Read first

  • docs/project-50k.md: decisions K4, K6, K11, and "State" and "Kernel rules" under Target architecture.
  • rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs: the kernel's contract. Its "Stores" section (written by by-w8gv.7.2) is the design this bead follows: kinds, shapes, capacities and byte allotments, expiry, indexing, capability bits, and what a store implements once. The same numbers are in a comment on by-w8gv.7.2, and enact brief shows that bead's handoff. Where the code proves the contract wrong, change its text in the same push and say so on the bead.
  • rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/legacy.rs: the table of what M4 deletes. Delete each Legacy method this bead replaces; the struct itself goes in by-w8gv.7.10.
  • docs/rules-coverage.md §9 (casting permissions and alternative costs) and §6 (replacements).
  • rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/floating.rs: CastPermission, with the rule each field implements.

Need not read: layers.rs, combat.rs, the Memos, Trackers and counters code, ui/.

Where it is (staging 08d1a377)

  • kernel: the permissions field (state.rs) and its lines in layout.rs, new.rs, relayout.rs, fit.rs and off_board (board.rs); Legacy::add_permission and Legacy::end_permissions_of_turn; State::permissions(); Observation::permissions() (observation.rs: public, as the effects that grant them are).
  • writers in the engine: EffectCtx::allow_cast and grant_flashback (effect.rs), the cleanup call in turn.rs. Their callers: jace_vryns_prodigy.rs (this turn, exiled instead of the graveyard), elite_spellbinder.rs (the opponent may cast it for {2} more while it stays exiled), snapcaster_mage.rs (flashback until end of turn, cost equal to the mana cost).
  • readers in the engine: cast.rs permission, cast_sources, the three reads of extra and flashback while options and costs are found, the block that builds the StackEntry and the Casting (exile, flashback, extra), exiled_instead, and the set of objects something refers to at the end of the file; mana.rs's look-ahead, which adds extra to a permitted card's cost.
  • readers elsewhere: beryl_scenario/src/run.rs, beryl_session/src/protocol/candidates.rs, beryl_search/src/mcts/tree.rs (two states are equal for search only if their permissions are), beryl_search/src/expert/mod.rs and frozen/expert/mod.rs.

Scope

  1. Permission payload: a player may cast one object from the zone it is in, until end of turn or while it stays there (CR 601.3; the entry names one incarnation, so it ends when the card leaves, CR 400.7). Expiry is the store's, not a sweep of its own.
  2. Cost-change payload: "A spell cast this way costs {2} more" is a cost increase applied as the total cost is determined (CR 601.2f). Casting::extra (beryl_kernel/src/pending.rs) becomes a read of it unless the contract says the record keeps it.
  3. Replacement payload: "If that spell would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead" is the replacement by-w8gv.7.2 built for ExileInsteadOfGraveyard, begun on the spell as it is cast. StackEntry::exile (beryl_kernel/src/stack.rs) then says nothing the store does not: remove it, and have cast::exiled_instead and event::destination agree on one path.
  4. Flashback from a permission (CR 702.34a): the grant is a permission whose cost is the card's mana cost paid as an alternative cost. StackEntry::flashback, "the flashback cost was paid", is a fact about how the spell was cast: if the contract makes it a memo of the spell, by-w8gv.7.6 moves it and this bead leaves the field, set from the permission as today.
  5. With no card in the game that can grant a permission, finding what a player may cast costs one AND over the hand (rules-coverage §9; a GameTable capability bit). The WU mirror has three such cards, so measure the present case too.
  6. CastPermission, the queue and what is left of floating.rs go; the session, scenario and search readers ask the store's query or Observation.

Gotchas

  • StackEntry is 60 bytes and there are 64 of them: say on the bead whether removing a field shrank it.
  • A permitted card is cast from exile or a graveyard by a player who may not own it (Elite Spellbinder): the permission names the player, not the owner.
  • Scenarios: cargo test -p beryl_cards jace, snapcaster, spellbinder; just oracle "Jace, Vryn's Prodigy" "Snapcaster Mage" "Elite Spellbinder".

Acceptance criteria

State has no permissions queue and CastPermission, Legacy::add_permission and Legacy::end_permissions_of_turn are gone: a permission to cast, its cost increase and its "exile it instead" are Effects-store entries with the contract's payloads, expiring through the store. StackEntry::exile and Casting::extra are removed, or the bead records the contract's reason each stays. cast.rs, mana.rs, beryl_scenario, beryl_session and beryl_search read permissions through the store's query or Observation. With no permission-granting card in the game the cast-source check is one capability-bit AND. Kernel tests cover a permission's two durations, its end when the card leaves its zone, and relayout; the Jace, Snapcaster Mage and Elite Spellbinder scenarios and just oracle for them pass; just bench-count before and after and the state size are recorded on the bead; parity holds with no bump to the count gate.

Measurements and decisions (by-w8gv.7.4), base staging 9e697fdf (engine code as at 0f9c2c03).

COUNT (just count-gate, rollout_counts rollouts subject, as .2 and .3 measured; not the full just bench-count):

  • before: 398,897,988 (the bar, which .3 set to its own measurement of this engine code)
  • after: 396,070,902 (-0.71%, inside the 1% tolerance, so the bar stays; no bump) The WU mirror has all three permission-granting cards, so this is the present case.

SIZE: size_of::<State<Small>>() 20,040 -> 19,832 (-208; allotment -200 or lower). The permissions queue gave back 80. StackEntry DID shrink, 60 -> 58 bytes, without exile and flashback: 128 over the stack's 64 entries. Casting is 17 bytes without extra; Pending is still 416.

DECISIONS

  1. Payloads (beryl_kernel/src/effects.rs): Payload::Cast(Permission { player, increase, replacement }) on the card; Payload::Cost(CostChange::Increase(n)) and Payload::Move(replacement) on the spell. MoveReplacement::Flashback is new: CR 702.34a's "exile this card instead of putting it anywhere else". A permission "has flashback" when that is its replacement (Permission::is_flashback), which is also what makes its cost an alternative cost. this_turn is Duration::EndOfTurn; "for as long as it remains exiled" is Duration::Indefinite on the card's object, which ends with it.
  2. Removed, none kept: State::permissions, CastPermission, floating.rs, Legacy::add_permission, Legacy::end_permissions_of_turn, StackEntry::exile, StackEntry::flashback, Casting::extra. The cost is read of the permission before the cast (cast::refused, mana's look-ahead) and of State::cost_increase(spell) from the moment the spell is on the stack (cast::total_cost).
  3. One path for "exiled instead": event::exiled_instead(s, object, to), which event::destination applies to every move. cast::exiled_instead is gone; cast::leave_stack just moves; cast::unstack and Run::unstack ask the same function to keep their batching as it was.
  4. "Was a flashback cost paid" is whether MoveReplacement::Flashback is on the spell (beryl_engine::flashback_paid): Event::Cast's flag and the session's stack item read it.
  5. Capability bit: as .2's contract decided, no GameTable bit. With no permission live, cast_sources, the mana look-ahead and referenced() each stop at one AND of the store's kinds; with none ever granted that is every read.
  6. Permissions end eagerly now, as the card's object ends. The queue kept a stale entry until cleanup, and two readers saw it: cast::referenced (the slot stayed un-collapsible for the rest of the turn) and the expert's counter_known. Neither moves a rollout: rollouts_never_change holds. referenced() now takes State::told_apart_by_effects: every object with an effect that is no layer change (a permitted card, a spell with a cost change or replacement, an object with a rule change), since nothing on the object shows those.
  7. mcts shows_the_same compared the permissions; it now compares the effects no object shows (not layer changes), as (affected, payload, duration), without source or timestamp. That covers what the stack entry's two flags said, and a player's rule change, which .3 moved out of Player and so out of that comparison.
  8. corpus_never_changes re-pinned (2,007,108,660,786,994,172): a state no longer hashes the queue, the two flags or Casting::extra, and a permission takes a timestamp as every effect does. rollouts_never_change holds unchanged and the frozen save corpus replays; a checkpoint still says "player N's permission to cast X" in the same place (State::cast_permission_lingering).
  9. beryl_session's promotion test filled the 8-entry queue with nine Snapcaster Mages. Permissions share the 64 effects now, so it uses a test spell that grants 65 permissions and fills the Effects store.
  10. File lengths: the permission code left cast.rs and effect.rs for two new files, beryl_engine/src/permission.rs (lookup, the terms begun on the spell, flashback_paid) and effect/permit.rs (EffectCtx::allow_cast, grant_flashback). cast.rs 1772 -> 1766, effect.rs 1212 -> 1170, mana.rs 1586 -> 1585; allowlist lowered.

KNOWN DIFFERENCE IN A VIEW: a GameEvent::ResolutionPayment's stack item is built from the game as it stands when the view is built. If the paying spell was cast with flashback and has left the stack by then, its item's paid no longer lists Flashback (the entry used to carry the flag). The script matches that item by slot alone, and the UI uses it to find the stack entry, so nothing drawn changes.
