Baseline audit of origin/staging 950e3c614 (2026-11-16). Read the contracts, the foundations Mason's by-bajq.4.1 audit and the eight Warden close reasons (1.7, 2.9, 3.12, 5.7, 6.6, 7.6, 8.7, 9.5). This is an as-built snapshot. The Part II module passes retain design ownership.

  1. Decisions R4–R13
  2. R4 shipped. The shared last-known table freed 56 bytes (by-bajq.1.2). At the foundations gate there were 136 bytes of headroom. by-bajq.4.3 changed Object alignment from four bytes to two and reduced the enclosing state from 20,344 to 19,840. The audit's compiled size probe measured 20,240 bytes, 240 below the cap. state.rs asserts the cap at compile time.
  3. R5 shipped. beryl_rules/src/layers/mod.rs refresh/recompute rebuilds a stale cache from base in layer order. layers/dependency.rs uses may_depend to avoid unrelated trials, then applies the candidate to scratch and checks changed applicability or effect. The Blood Moon/Urborg and Opalescence/Humility/Dress Down scenarios cover ordering and cycles.
  4. R6 shipped as typed per-kind paths. replace.rs moving and its Move, replace/draws.rs, replace/damage.rs Damage and replace/destruction.rs destruction run before the write. Destruction takes a subject set and a regeneration policy. event.rs settles actual mutations and triggers. engine/paused_step.rs and resolve.rs replay choices on scratch state. Keeping typed, kind-specific rewrite code avoids an all-events adapter. The replacement replay test exercises both a turn step and a resolution. The foundations audit's question about returning what happened is still pertinent to mutations with replacements. Outcomes that are recomputed from the requested write can attribute a replacement to the wrong object or seat.
  5. R7 shipped. State::copiable reads the definition/face from Object and additions from Effects (Flag::Copy). Memo::Really preserves the physical card. Copies of copies, spell copies and merged faces use Copiable. The representation is suitable, but State::mana_value confuses a non-token copy with an original back face. The scratch probe read 2 for both an original transformed Jace and a nontoken copy. The documented rule requires 2 and 0. Filed by-ptc7.1.14.
  6. R8 shipped with characteristic-defining power/toughness computed when read. beryl_kernel/src/query.rs, defined.rs and elsewhere.rs separate base/copied values from sparse applicable effects, including Painter's Servant's color, Underworld Breach's escape and Tarmogoyf's off-battlefield P/T. This avoids storing layer results for off-battlefield slots. The separate constructors for a layer change are a real duplication, as shown by the Painter's Servant simultaneous-entry panic in by-bajq.9.4. The existing continuous-effects pass owns their consolidation.
  7. R9 shipped with one adaptation: words.rs keeps word types private, effects/text.rs composes live words, and StackEntry::words captures an ability's source text when it goes on the stack. That separation preserves independence from the source; Fork's copy reads its own current text. The backing map is incomplete: the fifth distinct basic-land source is silently ignored (confirmed by probe). Filed by-ptc7.1.13. The fixed-size color representation suggests a compact total map for the five basic land types.
  8. R10 shipped. stack.rs Target identifies an ability by its position. State::remove_from_stack repairs references when a lower entry is removed. stack/tests.rs a_removal_below_an_ability_target_repairs_it checks this. Reuse the position representation while one stack owns both mutation and repair.
  9. R11 shipped. mutate/merge records merged parts in Zone::Merged with Memo::PartOf. phasing.rs synchronizes the battlefield list and membership bitboard without changing zone or incarnation. The Warden added the attached, unattached and previous-controller cases. Keep this representation with a single mutator boundary.
  10. R12 shipped. Decision::player and decider distinguish who owns and answers the decision. Resolving records hidden picks by slot and Observation::unseen_hidden_picks constrains determinization; the redeterminization test checks the same question and visible view. ChosenValue::Name(None) is the "Another card" outcome. The Warden found five callers that confused the two Seats. Part II should keep the seat roles explicit at protocol boundaries. The option cap is 63, keeping Decision<Small> at 312 bytes.
  11. R13 shipped for mandatory resolutions. State::note_resolution in repeats.rs uses Brent sampling over a canonical hash, after a quiet interval, and resolve.rs ends the game on a repeat. It normalizes timestamps and incarnations while retaining other game state. Worldgorger Dragon has the draw scenario. An intervening choice resets the check; optional repetition uses the ordinary budget.
  12. One-card logic. The sole thing I would remove as a card-shaped abstraction now is Animate Dead's linked-entry cluster: PutOnto::linked, TargetSpec::put_with_this, Objects::PutOntoBattlefieldWithThis, Memo::PutOntoBattlefieldWith and Kinds::PUT_WITH, the memo write/read, and the renderer's fixed "that creature". Card search finds only animate_dead.rs constructing these. One typed source/kind relation could share expiry with ExiledWith and carry the noun at the rendering boundary. This preserves the linked rule while removing dedicated read/write paths. Show and Tell is the sole present user of the simultaneous hidden-pick protocol. Keep the rule; the committed filter is currently recovered from the running effect, so the kernel decisions pass has the exact site to review. No one-card-only prompt purpose found. Mutate, dredge, remembered mana, chosen-name prohibitions, permanent and player protection and Player/Decider state describe rules with further uses. Posted the inventory or a negative finding on each of the 18 module passes by-ptc7.4.1–.18.
  13. Principle 7
  14. Does it have the ability? Ask State::has for an ObjectAbility and State::abilities for enumeration. State::has_ability answers only for an indexed ability on the object's own face, including the trigger-listener fast path. Layer trials and prospective-entry evaluation have explicitly provisional inputs. The object and copies passes should keep final readers off the face arrays and keep provisional computations separate; grant kinds the engine cannot read are refused at the table boundary.
  15. What are its copiable values? Ask State::copiable. State::definition is for definition/face facts independent of exceptions, and State::card_behind is the physical card. The copied-back-face finding is an example of a derived characteristic needing the copy's provenance.
  16. What color is it? Ask State::colors, which reads the battlefield cache or composes the off-board answer. Copiable::colors can correctly differ under a continuous effect. Layer application legitimately reads working derived values while the cache is being built.
  17. Census. just census reports 6,552 / 32,177 cards (20.4%), up from 6,107. Ranked by sole uncovered kind, then total cards in parentheses: trigger:ChangesZone 1,945 (6,641); effect:ChangeZone 1,237 (5,155); static:Continuous 1,188 (4,199); effect:Effect 322 (1,622); effect:PumpAll 311 (919). The first three already work in narrower forms. These are counts of Forge kinds, so they are upper bounds for complete kind coverage, not estimates that a single new variant enables every card in a row.
