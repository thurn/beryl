Description

Regression of by-isv.6's "a card with one use acts on click".

  • Repro: seed 101 vs Expert. Play to turn 10 with your Jace, Vryn's Prodigy the only creature on the battlefield (the opponent has none) and Oust in hand. Oust glows as playable. Click it once.
  • Actual: one choose (Cast Oust) is sent with its announced target already chosen; Oust resolves on your own Jace (into your library, you gain 3). No target step, no chance to cancel. Log: "You cast Oust targeting Jace, Vryn's Prodigy". Shots: D1/167-g1-click-oust-before.png, D1/169-g1-click-oust-600ms.png, D1/171-g1-oust-target.png.
  • Expected: a targeted spell whose only legal target is yours (or any target choice that a strong player would not make the same way every time) goes through the on-board target step, cancellable, even when there is one candidate. One-click acting stays for untargeted single-use cards and for targets that cannot hurt you only if that rule is principled; the simplest safe rule is: a click never picks a target for the player.

Look at how Board.tsx (acts.length === 1) and the session's announced target preview (beryl_session preview.rs, protocol.rs first-target field) combine.

Found by the by-isv.13 ui-review on staging 4c67340 (WU Control mirror both seats; seeds and opponents per item). Screenshots: D1 = ~/Library/Caches/beryl/ui-live/by-isv.13/20261105-224254 (1600x900), D2 = ~/Library/Caches/beryl/ui-live/by-isv.13/20261105-233541 (1280x800). Reproduce each item first with just ui-live (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md) or the named fixture; drop any that does not reproduce and say so in the close reason.

Design rules (epic by-isv): follow the skill's design philosophy (one gesture for the common case, the client does the bookkeeping, ask once in context, Arena first, show don't make them infer, few words) and add no standing words to the table. Every acceptance check is an agent's: just ui-test, just ui-shots, Rust tests or ui-live probes.

Acceptance criteria

  • A just ui-test check (fixture or scripted board) clicks a targeted spell whose only legal target is the player's own permanent and finds a target step open, nothing sent to the engine beyond starting the cast, and Escape cancels it.
  • Untargeted single-use cards still cast in one click (existing checks pass).

Derek Thurn2026-11-06

Reproduced at the engine level: advance() applied any one-candidate decision silently, so Oust's lone target (the human's own Jace) was announced without a Target decision; the UI never saw one. Board.tsx's acts.length===1 path was not at fault. Fix: Profile::asks_targets (Option<Seat>), set to the human by beryl_session::with_stops; engine::advance keeps a lone Target decision when the player is named and pending is a non-triggered Casting (casts and activations; trigger targets stay silent, CR 603.3d). MCTS smart() and bench rollouts clear it like full_control. corpus_never_changes pin moved only because State's hash now feeds the new Profile field (rollouts_never_change unchanged). New board/fixture target-own + ui-test check; engine tests in tests/casting.rs. No docs/bugs.md entry: no rule was wrong, the silent pick was legal.
