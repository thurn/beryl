Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Timeline parity' and 'Checks and isolation'; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/cli.py (how a command is declared), lane.py (steps(): each check the lane gains is appended there, and the sweep runs the lane's steps), gate.py (lowered, the model for a limit that only goes down), and the two programs this joins: just b3d timeline --all and just b3d export (by-q44s.3.5), and ui/scripts/timeline.mjs (by-q44s.3.4). Read both of those beads for the line format.

Build, in tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/timeline.py and data/beryl3d:

  • just b3d timeline-parity [prefix] exports the recordings, runs the web script over them with node in ui/ (after warm.ensure_node_modules(checkout) in tools/src/beryl_tools/warm.py, which copies ui/node_modules from the cache as just ui-unit does), runs b3d timeline --all, and compares the two recording by recording and seat by seat. Two timelines agree when they hold the same steps in the same order with the same view, beat, step, verb, case, effects and own, and every start and end is within 1 millisecond.
  • data/beryl3d/timeline-parity.json: {"recordings": {"<name>/<seat>": <steps that differ>}}, sorted by name. A step counts once however many of its fields differ, and a step one side has and the other lacks counts once. The check fails for a recording that differs and is not listed, and for one that differs by more steps than its entry. --update rewrites the file to what was measured, and refuses to raise or add an entry unless --raise is given with it; a commit that raises one says why in its body.
  • The web side's lines are kept in $BERYL_CACHE/b3d/web-timelines-<hash>.jsonl, where the hash covers the recordings pack's own hash and every file under ui/src/stage, ui/src/game and ui/scripts/timeline.mjs. A run that finds the file does not start node.
  • A fault prints the recording, the seat, the first differing step from each side, and the two commands that print each side's timeline. At most 20 are printed, then a count.
  • The check is the last step of the beryl3d lane, and so a step of the sweep. Measure it five times with the web lines cached and five times without, and report the medians with the load in a comment. If the cached run takes more than 20 seconds on the clock, the lane step compares fixtures only (the names without a /) and the sweep compares everything.
  • One paragraph in rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md under 'Data files' saying what the file holds and what the check holds, and a line for the command under 'Commands'.

It is never a lane on ui/ paths (decision C17): when the web interface's timeline moves, the sweep's bead names the recordings that now differ, and Beryl 3D follows.

Tests, in tools/tests (beside the tools package's existing tests of beryl3d): two equal timelines agree; a start 2 ms apart is one differing step and 0.5 ms apart is none; a missing step counts once; an unlisted differing recording fails; --update lowers an entry and refuses to raise one.

Run: just b3d timeline-parity verbs-zones; just b3d timeline-parity; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

just b3d timeline-parity passes with every recording that differs listed in data/beryl3d/timeline-parity.json; adding 50.0 to from in beat in beryl_scene/src/beat.rs makes it fail and name a recording (shown in a comment, then reverted); the five tests pass; the lane runs the check; the measured times are in a comment.
