Description

Part of Milestone 1 (epic by-b0q). Seen while reviewing expert-system transcripts (by-b0q.3.5), round 13, beryl_cli play expert expert --seed 5 on that bead's code: on turn 16, P1 had six untapped lands (Island, Spara's Headquarters, Seachrome Coast, Plains, Island, Plains), cast Overlord of the Mistmoors for impending ({2}{W}{W}), and P0 answered with Mana Tithe. The Overlord was countered with no YesNo decision asked of P1, although two lands were left to pay {1}. Mana Tithe's scenarios cover only a controller paying with one untapped land after a free spell, so the case where the spell's own payment left lands untapped is untested.

Reproduce as a scenario: P1 with six lands casts a four-mana spell, P0 casts Mana Tithe on it; P1 must be asked to pay. Find whether EffectCtx::unless_pays (mana::payable) misjudges what is left untapped (payment plans, the SMART profile's KeepMostPayable), or whether the transcript misreports. CR 118.12a governs; No More Lies uses the same path.

Acceptance criteria

  • A scenario in mana_tithe.rs (and one in no_more_lies.rs) where the countered spell's controller still has lands after paying for it is asked to pay and keeps the spell by paying.
  • The cause is fixed, or the transcript is shown to be wrong and that is fixed.
  • just test-rust passes.

Cause: the transcript was right and the engine correct. Replaying beryl_cli play expert expert --seed 5 on enact/by-b0q.3.5 at 61c350f (round-13 code) reproduces it: on turn 4 P0's Elite Spellbinder exiled P1's Overlord of the Mistmoors, so on turn 16 P1 recast it from exile for impending plus Spellbinder's {2} tax ({4}{W}{W}), tapping all six lands (turn 17's board shows every P1 land tapped). With nothing left, CR 118.12a's 'unless pays' rightly asks nothing. The reviewer missed the tax. No engine change. Added scenarios in mana_tithe.rs and no_more_lies.rs: an impending Overlord from six (seven) lands, the controller asked, pays and keeps it. Assertions sit at the end step because the XMage exporter does not turn silent priority passes over a non-empty stack into resolveTop; XMage and Forge oracles agree on both.
