Description

Part of the AI ladder epic by-ix1t. Read docs/ai-plan.md first: §2.3, §3 and §6.3.

When the search prefers a move other than the default, it decides between them from the trees' own means, and those means are biased. At beza-trinket-into-blockers the trees rated the attack 0.066 above holding back; 2,000 paired rollouts rated it 0.006 below, with a standard error of 0.009 (by-97g.34's notes hold the full just why --tree output for four entries). The bias is the same in every tree, so the present test, a lead of 0.02 and two standard errors across the trees, cannot see it. That is why the search anchored to the expert kept only 8 of the expert's 18 right answers.

This bead adds the option paired: the override is decided outside the trees, by playing both moves out in the same worlds with the same seeds.

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs:
    • grow: an anchored search spends rounds 1 to FREE_ROUNDS (three quarters of ROUNDS) freely. If its pick differs from the default and guards holds, it sets tree.allowed = Some([pick, default]) and spends the last quarter on those two inside the trees.
    • check and AnchorCheck { greedy, search, lead, error, trees }, and AnchorCheck::played: the present test, with ANCHOR_MARGIN (0.02) and ANCHOR_ERRORS (2.0).
    • value and discounted: how a finished game is scored, with the turn discount counted from the root's turn.
    • deal, WORLD_STEPS, ITERATION_STEPS: how a world is dealt from what the player sees and how work is charged.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/rollout.rs: soft_rollout_within, Softmax, ROLLOUT_TEMPERATURE.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/why.rs: compare, play, deal. This is a working paired comparison of two candidates at a suite position. Move its core into beryl_search and have why.rs call it, so there is one implementation.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long/turn.rs, score_worlds and reward: the turn search already scores lines in fresh worlds by rollouts.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/match_log/mod.rs: AnchorRecord, which a match logs for each decision. Rung 6 reads it.

Build

  1. Add Options::paired, named paired, after sure in the fixed order. It requires anchor.
  2. Put the test in a new module, long/paired.rs. With paired set, where grow would restrict the trees to the two candidates, it instead spends the last quarter of the decision's work on pairs:
    1. Deal a world from what the player sees.
    2. On two copies, play the search's pick on one and the default on the other. Questions asked while that action is completed (targets, payments) are answered by the rollout policy, as in why.rs.
    3. Play both to the end with the rollout policy from the same rollout seed, and score each with value.
    4. The pair's difference is the pick's score less the default's.
  3. The lead is the mean of the differences and its error their standard error. The pick is played only if the lead is at least ANCHOR_MARGIN and at least PAIRED_ERRORS (3.0) standard errors. Otherwise the default stands.
  4. AnchorCheck carries the paired lead, its error and the number of pairs. trees is a u16 and 30M steps buy about 6,500 pairs, so widen it or add a field, and carry the change through AnchorRecord and its readers (screen.rs, anchor_replay).
  5. Work is charged as the trees charge it, so a decision still spends settings.work. The result must not depend on the number of threads: pairs are numbered, each pair's world and seed come from its number, and threads take pairs by index.
  6. Tests:
    • A move compared with itself has a lead of exactly zero.
    • The same seed gives the same lead on one thread and on eight.
    • At beza-trinket-into-blockers, manifest-into-samurai, spirits-stay-home and subtlety-main-phase-empty-stack, a seeded search with anchor+guard+paired (with combat or hold where the entry's default needs it) at 300,000 steps plays the default.
    • The family name round-trips, and the champion's moves do not change.

What to expect, for your notes

At 30M steps a rollout is about 580 steps, so the quarter buys about 6,500 pairs and a standard error near 0.005. At 3M it buys about 650 pairs and an error near 0.015, so at 3M the search overrules only on leads of about 0.045 or more. Measure the pairs and the error at both budgets at one suite entry and write them in the notes; the measure bead reads them.

Acceptance criteria

paired parses and is off by default; the paired test is deterministic and independent of thread count; the four named entries keep their default under it in tests; why.rs uses the shared implementation; just test-rust and just check pass.
