Description

What to build

Plan: Designs H, "Banding". CR 702.22 is long and exact: read all of it before designing, and cite each part where it is implemented.

  • Keywords::BANDING and Capability::Banding. A game without a bander stores no band and asks nothing new.
  • Declaring attackers: as attackers are declared, the active player may put them in bands: any number of creatures with banding and up to one without. Opt::Attack gains the band an attacker joins (a new band, or an existing one it may legally join), and the bands of this combat are a short list of sets in the combat state, kept only with the capability (a Fixed queue in Small; Full widens). A band attacks one player or planeswalker.
  • Blocking: a creature that blocks one member of a band blocks every member (CR 702.22h); evasion is checked against the creature actually blocked.
  • Damage: combat::assigner (the dependency) answers the defending player for an attacker blocked by a creature with banding, and the active player for a blocker of a creature in a band with banding (CR 702.22j and 702.22k). The division decision must then let the chooser put damage where they like among the legal recipients, including all of it on one creature: check that Opt::Lethal can express that, and widen the decision if not.
  • A creature that leaves a band, or loses banding, during combat (CR 702.22f and 702.22g).
  • Benalish Hero and Benalish Infantry (both "Banding"; complete from the card store once the keyword exists, in which case their scenarios go in beryl_cards/src/library/ under keywords/banding.rs).

Write each card with the card-writing skill. Read its rulings first (just card rulings <name>): the card joins data/rulings-ledger.json with every ruling a scenario or a reason, and joins the keystone fuzz pool (beryl_cards/src/decks.rs). Where XMage or Forge cannot play the card, its scenarios are reported as unsupported and the ledger is the outside check, so be thorough with it.

Scenarios: two banders attack in a band and are blocked by one creature (it blocks both; the attacking player divides the blocker's damage); a bander and a non-bander in a band; a band blocked by a creature that could not block one member because of flying; a blocker with banding (the defending player divides the attacker's damage); a member removed from combat before damage; trample in a band.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Both cards pass their scenarios in Beryl and, where an oracle implements banding, in it. A game without banding reads no new field: the count is unchanged.
