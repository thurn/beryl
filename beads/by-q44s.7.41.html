Close reason

Phone peeks of attached cards keep a 44-point strip next to tapped hosts and in crowded rows; a hidden-end card is no longer drawn after an in-place change

Description

Symptom: on Beryl 3D's phone layout, an Aura or Equipment attached to a permanent, which peeks out from under it, has no 44-by-44 point area that takes a press. just b3d test --phone-only reports these reachable faults:

  • shape-pay/you/393x759 t=6553 (and 3 more tests): Swift Reconfiguration under Snapcaster Mage and Jace, Vryn's Prodigy.
  • table-drawn/you/393x759 t=18421 (and 1 more): Swift Reconfiguration under Adeline, Resplendent Cathar (tapped).
  • vigilance/dress-down-after-the-aura-takes-vigilance-away/you/393x759 t=5923 (and 2 more): Vigilance under Geist of the Moors (tapped).
  • teferis-protection/a-phased-out-phantasmal-image-is-not-sacrificed/you/393x759 t=3290 (and 5 more): Leonin Scimitar under a Plains.

On the phone, a peek is narrower than 44 points, or a tapped host or a neighbouring group covers it. by-q44s.7.36 kept crowded rows with Aura peeks at the least strip (lay_row in table/rows.rs); these cases are rows that are not crowded, or a host that is tapped.

Read: rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/rows.rs (peeks, place_group, lay_row), src/table/rows/ (submodules), src/table/phone.rs, src/agent/checks.rs (reachable), data/beryl3d/space.json (rules.strip, peek sizes), tests/all/table_phone.rs; docs/beryl-3d.md "The table" and "Interface checks". The web's phone layout (ui/src/stage/phone.ts) shows how far an attached card peeks out there.

Fix: on the phone, make a peeking card show at least a 44-point strip that nothing covers, including next to a tapped host. Do not add phone fault allowances; desktop tests must stay clean.

Acceptance criteria

just b3d test --phone-only and --phone-only --every-tick report no reachable fault in shape-pay, table-drawn, vigilance or teferis-protection; desktop just b3d test, just b3d cargo-test and just b3d gate pass.

Handoff: commit 'fix: keep phone attachment peeks reachable' (staging 01143334e).

  • rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/rows.rs: peek_step adds half the width difference between a permanent and a narrower card under it (an untapped Aura under a tapped creature), so every tucked card shows at least the row's least strip past the permanent's edge. In lay_row's crowded branch, a group with attached cards (they peek to the right) now takes its whole width and is never overlapped. Only the other groups share the spacing. On the desktop this means a crowded row with Auras shows fewer groups.
  • play/step/hidden.rs hides_in_place and play/step.rs follow: the teferis-protection fault was not an attachment. A tapped Plains scrolled off the lands row's hidden end got a segment from the phase-out 'set' step and was drawn over the Leonin Scimitar until a later step flew it into view. In-place (still) changes now call hide_on_arrival when the card rests out of view afterwards. An earlier version applied this to slides too and gave stress-verbs a 1 mm no-jumps fault (pile copies), so it is limited to the still path.
  • Tests: two new tests in tests/all/table_phone.rs (Aura on a tapped creature, Aura early in a crowded row). Both fail without the rows.rs change.
  • Verified: the four recordings pass just b3d test --phone-only and --every-tick. All phone tests went from 158 failing to 116, with the rest unrelated (buttons under the phase icon and others). Desktop just b3d test is clean, cargo-test passes (809), and the gate passes.
  • Surprise: the push hit a rustc incremental link failure (missing _anon...llvm symbols) after rebasing onto kernel changes. Worked around with a clean and CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0. Filed by-q44s.92.
