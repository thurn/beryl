Forge plays Lion's Eye Diamond's mana ability at priority: LED Forge 9/9 pass, Breach still passes

Description

Lion's Eye Diamond's scenarios activate its mana ability at priority (Beryl offers an instant-only mana ability as Opt::Activate, then asks the color: Pending::ManaColor). The XMage exporter plays them (card_facts.instant_mana_text, tapSource). The Forge exporter reports all 7 of them unsupported: tools/src/beryl_tools/forge.py ability_selector raises 'activating an ability that is not an activated ability' for kind Mana, and tools/oracles/BerylForgeHarness.java activate() skips sa.isManaAbility().

Make forge.py select the Diamond's ability (its first cost part, 'Discard your hand') and the harness play it at priority with the color the following answer names (purpose 'color', as chooseColors reads it), e.g. through ComputerUtil.playNoStack with the mana part's express choice, as floatTaps does for combo mana.

Acceptance criteria
