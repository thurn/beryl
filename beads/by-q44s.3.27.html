Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Motion the pointer drives', 'Interface checks' ('Reachable') and 'The test driver' (hover, move); the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. The Warden of milestone 1's core left this for milestone 2: every card is written with interactive: false, so the card half of the 'reachable' check (an uncovered square of 24 logical points on every interactive card at rest) has run on no frame of any recording. This bead turns it on, and will meet its first real faults.

Code: beryl_scene src/pointer/mod.rs (Pointer and the stub look, from by-q44s.3.2), src/client.rs (Client::point, mouse, pressed, and the hit test at the end of Client::frame), src/agent/hit.rs (hit), src/agent/checks.rs (reachable), src/table/scene.rs (Look), build.rs (the pointer object of space.json is empty). Web: ui/src/stage/raised.ts (RISE_MS and DROP_MS: up in a tenth of a second, down in about a sixth, each fast at first; Easing, raisedAt), ui/src/game/raise.ts (RAISE, raisedPose: how far up and how much larger a raised hand card is), ui/src/game/handLayout.ts (RAISED_HAND, handLifts), ui/src/stage/heft.ts (HELD_HEIGHT, MAX_GAP, LIGHT), ui/src/stage/pointer.ts (Pointer, hover).

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build:

  • data/beryl3d/space.json pointer: rise_ms 100 and fall_ms 160 (RISE_MS, DROP_MS), raise and raise_scale from RAISE, held_height from HELD_HEIGHT, max_gap_ms from MAX_GAP, and the spring's values for by-q44s.3.28 from Heft (stiffness, damping, lean), each with the unit in its name. The build script writes them as data::space::pointer.
  • Move the pointer's state from Client into pointer::Pointer: its latest position and phase, set in handle; the card it is over, found in frame by a hit test on the frame being built, because a card can move under a pointer that is still.
  • Every card the viewer can see at rest is interactive: Look::interactive is true for a face-up card on the battlefield, in the viewer's hand or on the stack, and for the top of no pile (a pile is one element). HOVERED is set on the card under the pointer.
  • When the card under the pointer changes, the hand card it left gets a segment back to its place (fall_ms) and the one it entered a segment up by raise and raise_scale (rise_ms), both easing out, both starting at that frame's now from the pose the card has then. These are ordinary segments on a track the pointer owns for each hand card, which pointer::look applies over the card's resting pose; a script that moves the card takes over from that pose. A raised card is drawn over its neighbours, and the hit test still finds cards by their resting places in the fan, as the web's does, so the pointer moving across a raised card's upper part does not flicker between two cards.
  • A touch does not hover.
  • Client::wake is Now while a rise or fall is under way.
  • Run just b3d check on verbs-zones and pay-default from both seats. For each reachable fault: fix it here if the cause is in src/pointer or in which cards are interactive; otherwise list it in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json with just b3d test --update --raise if that exists (by-q44s.3.8), name the placement function at fault in a comment, and file one bead under by-q44s.3 for each such function.

Do not build: picking a card up (by-q44s.3.28), the inspector that shows a hovered card large (milestone 3).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/pointer.rs with Page: hover on a hand card gives it the hovered state and raises its box within 100 ms, and moving off it returns it within 160 ms to its placement; the pose at 50 ms of a rise is the same whether the frames between were drawn or not; sweeping the pointer across a 7-card hand hovers each card once, in order; a card that flies under a still pointer becomes hovered when it arrives; a touch press hovers nothing; reachable runs on at least one card of verbs-zones (count the cards it judged); a frame while a card rises allocates nothing.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d tree verbs-zones --at end --do 'hover card "Island"'; just b3d check verbs-zones; just b3d lane.

Deviations and decisions:

  • The rising card is not a Segment on a pose track but a 0..1 lift per hand card (Lift in src/pointer/mod.rs) eased with Curve::EASE_OUT, applied over the card's resting pose by pointer::look. It starts at the frame's now from its value then, and a part rise or fall takes that part of the time, as raisedAt in ui/src/stage/raised.ts. This lets a script take over from the resting pose: a card a script moves drops its lift and is hovered again only once it lands.
  • Raised pose: upright, raise_scale x about its foot, raise_mm of its own millimetres up, then moved along the eye ray to nearer (0.6) of its distance and scaled by as much, so it covers the hand without moving on screen. nearer is a new pointer value the bead did not list.
  • Hit test by resting places: pointer::update swaps each raised card's resting corners and depth into the frame for the hover hit test and restores them, so the frame keeps the raised box (tests see it rise) while hover follows the fan. The checks' area search still uses drawn boxes.
  • pointer::look takes (now, camera) beside the stub's arguments; HOVERED is set by update after the hit test, not by look.
  • Interactive also requires a known name: a face-up nameless card (copy of a face-down creature) faulted named.
  • Checks changed (doc corrected): on-screen asks a card for only a target square inside the safe area (the hand is held past the bottom edge, as in Arena and the web); reachable judges cards only on frames where nothing moves (a card in flight covering a resting one tripped the_check_times_find_every_fault_that_every_tick_finds).
  • beryl_inspect: check_recording allows faults of a rule listed for that test in test-faults.json; cli tests run check on pay-default (no faults) since check exits 1 on any fault.
  • The bead asked for tests in tests/pointer.rs; they are in tests/all/pointer.rs (one test binary, per rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md) and the allocation check is in tests/frame_allocation.rs (counting allocator).
  • src/play/mod.rs is at 799 lines.
