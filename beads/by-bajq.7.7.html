Close reason

XMage and Forge replay delve's chosen cards; Cavern scenario still unsupported (by-bajq.7.9)

Description

Gurmag Angler's delve scenarios are reported unsupported by both oracles, so Beryl's delve payment (beryl_rules cast/parts.rs: DELVE, substitute_options, end_substitutes) is checked against neither. XMage pays delve as a special action (DelveAbility / DelveSpecialAction in Mage/src/main/java/mage/abilities/keyword/DelveAbility.java) that its test player asks as a choice before the cast's targets, and the exporter raises Unsupported for any card with delve (tools/src/beryl_tools/xmage.py, Exporter.cast, via CardFacts.delves). Forge's AI chooses delve's cards itself, so tools/src/beryl_tools/forge.py raises Unsupported for an answer whose purpose is 'delve'; scenarios where Beryl's delve is forced already pass in Forge. Teach the XMage exporter to activate the delve special action and target the cards the trace exiled (TestPlayer special-action and addTarget commands), and the Forge harness (tools/oracles/BerylForgeHarness.java) to take delve's cards from the script where its AI cost decision asks for them.

Acceptance criteria

just card check 'Gurmag Angler' reports each hand-written scenario as pass in XMage and in Forge (none unsupported), including 'Gurmag Angler: the caster chooses which cards to exile'.
