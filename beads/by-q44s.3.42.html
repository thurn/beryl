Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Interface checks' ('Reachable') and 'The table' (the stack); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md (test-faults.json). Symptom: since cards became interactive, the 'reachable' check faults spells on the stack: at 1600x900 a spell under another is left a strip about 14 by 19 points (verbs-zones/you t=1983ms: Thirst for Discovery at 1326,319 117x122 under Snapcaster Mage at 1312,338). It is the most common reachable fault in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json (188 of 261 faults sampled across the 1600x900 tests). The same place also faults cards that have left the stack in the view but still wait there for their own step while earlier steps play (a creature resolving to the battlefield, a countered spell going to the graveyard: cavern-of-souls/creature-spell-cant-be-countered/you t=733ms, combat-attackers/opponent t=11767ms). A few cards also wait on a graveyard pile under its top (animate-dead/destroyed-under-rest-in-peace-exiles-both/opponent t=617ms). Code: rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/stack.rs (the stack's placement, stack_step in data/beryl3d/space.json layout.desktop), src/agent/checks.rs (reachable). Compare the web's stack spacing in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Fix: space the stack so each item below the top keeps at least audit.target_points square uncovered, from the web's values. Decide for the waiting cards whether the stack's spacing removes them; if a card waiting for its own step is better judged as moving, change the check instead and say why in the doc. Lower test-faults.json with just b3d test --update. Run: just b3d check verbs-zones; just b3d test --update; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

just b3d check verbs-zones has no reachable fault on a stack card from either seat; just b3d test --update lowers the reachable entries and raises none; the tests pass.

Handoff: no code change. While this bead was open, ba64a25c5 (feat: Beryl 3D hand fans, stack places and shadows) replaced the stack placement with the web's stackPlace (space.json 'stack': step, reach, lean, relative to the camera). On origin/staging, just b3d check verbs-zones has no reachable fault at 1440x900, 1600x900, 1920x1080 or 2560x1080 from either seat, and just b3d test --update leaves test-faults.json unchanged (reachable 1315). Before the rebase I measured the old layout: layout.desktop.stack_step x had to be at least -24 world units for 1440x900 to pass, and -26 cut reachable faults from 1967 to 1317. That is the same result the new placement gets. Decision on waiting cards: the cards that waited on the stack for their own step no longer fault. The 1600x900 reachable faults that remain are cards that wait under a pile's top card (combat-attackers Currency Converter under the exile and graveyard piles, fork Plains #28 and life-from-the-loam lands under your graveyard at 151,795) and the stress boards. None of them is on the stack, so I left the check unchanged. by-q44s.3.34 (every fixture with no fault) owns them.
