Close reason

Nightly prune of gone checkouts' cache folders (by prune-caches); ran once: 78 GB in 1006 folders

Description

$BERYL_CACHE/ui-shots/<checkout name> and $BERYL_CACHE/ui-test/<checkout name> (shots.py: paths.cache_root() / 'ui-shots' / paths.REPO.name, and ui_test()'s recorded fixtures) are written per checkout and never removed. Every slow-CI tree (ci-<sha>), Enact worktree, bisect tree and coverage tree leaves one behind: on jarvis on 2026-11-10 ui-shots held 70 GB in 428 ci-* folders and ui-test 14 GB, with the disk 98% full (77 GB free). Remove them: a nightly step (nightly.py STEPS, the entry point later nightly work joins) that deletes per-checkout folders under the cache's per-checkout roots (ui-shots, ui-test, and any other cache_root()/<x>/<REPO.name> users: grep paths.REPO.name in tools/src/beryl_tools) whose checkout is not a live worktree of the main repo (git worktree list) and that were not modified for a day; and/or remove a tree's folders where the tree is removed (coverage.remove_tree, bisect.Tree, the by ci tree). Found by warden by-w8gv.20.18.

Acceptance criteria

A nightly step (or tree removal) deletes the per-checkout cache folders of checkouts that no longer exist, older than a day, and leaves live checkouts' folders alone; pytest covers both; run once on jarvis and the freed space reported on this bead.
