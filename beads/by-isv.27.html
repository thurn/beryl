Description

Follows by-isv.1 (sickness), by-isv.2 (tokens, owners) and by-isv.7 (tracker).

  • Summoning sickness: desaturated art only, which fails on muted art: seed 101 vs Expert, your Snapcaster Mage on turn 22 (sick) and 24 (ready) look nearly the same (D1/325-sick-snap-vs-exiled-snap-compare.png, D1/350-snap-unsick-t24-compare.png; Trinket Mage turn 8: D1/118-sick-trinket-t8-compare.png). Use a signal independent of the art (Arena: a sleeping glyph or dimmed frame).
  • Tokens: a pewter frame instead of black is the only mark, hard to see on a busy board (turn 22, the Jace token from Fractured Identity next to Teferi, D1/303-token-vs-card-compare.png).
  • Stolen permanents: the violet inner edge is distinct but nothing on hover says what it means (D1/531-tokens-stolen-time-attached-compare.png).
  • Lethal damage: a "1/-3" P/T just before death reads oddly (D1/495-g3-staff-after.png); clamp the drawn toughness at 0.
  • Full control: nothing on the board shows it is on (seed 202, Settings -> Full control, D1/449-g2-next-fullcontrol-after.png); a small icon by the turn button.
  • Phase icons: besides their hover tips, the shield for the second main phase is not obvious, and beginning of combat and declare attackers share the swords.
  • Coin: the landed coin shows "1" next to "On the draw", which reads as "first" (seed 101 opening, the opponent wins the flip, D1/006-g1-start-after.png).

Found by the by-isv.13 ui-review on staging 4c67340 (WU Control mirror both seats; seeds and opponents per item). Screenshots: D1 = ~/Library/Caches/beryl/ui-live/by-isv.13/20261105-224254 (1600x900), D2 = ~/Library/Caches/beryl/ui-live/by-isv.13/20261105-233541 (1280x800). Reproduce each item first with just ui-live (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md) or the named fixture; drop any that does not reproduce and say so in the close reason.

Design rules (epic by-isv): follow the skill's design philosophy (one gesture for the common case, the client does the bookkeeping, ask once in context, Arena first, show don't make them infer, few words) and add no standing words to the table. Every acceptance check is an agent's: just ui-test, just ui-shots, Rust tests or ui-live probes.

Acceptance criteria

  • probe.compare shots (in just ui-shots) of a sick and a ready copy of a muted-art creature, and a token beside its nontoken twin, that differ by a mark independent of the art.
  • A stolen permanent's hover names its owner; drawn toughness never goes below 0; full control shows a mark on the board; the coin shows the marker only beside the player going first; distinct phase icons for each tracked step (shots).
