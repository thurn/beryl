Close reason

Staged 249960ad9. Three cards build without scenario modules and pass card check with both oracles. Fast checks passed, and the rollout count held at 379,552,664 instructions.

Description

Why

Scenarios are 71% of card-file lines (a median of 83 lines a card) and three quarters of card compile cost. Decision P2 of the plan: a card needs no hand-written scenario when its generated scenarios kill every mutant of its definition and agree with XMage and Forge. Three tests and a build script still require hand-written ones. Plan: Designs E2.

What to build

  • just card check <name> (tools/src/beryl_tools/card_check.py) gains a generated line: the mutants generated scenarios alone kill, the survivors by node, the answers the generator cut (Generated::cut), and whether the card may be generated. It needs a small Rust entry point: a beryl_scenario subcommand (beryl_cards/src/bin/ beryl_scenario.rs) that runs the mutation check for one card with derive::all() only.
  • rust/crates/beryl_card_build/src/generate.rs (about line 354): a card file with no scenarios module builds. crate::scenarios() yields an empty list for it.
  • beryl_cards/src/tests.rs: every_card_has_scenarios_and_they_pass counts generated scenarios, and fails a card with no hand-written scenario unless its generated scenarios kill every mutant. every_ability_and_casting_option_is_exercised_by_a_scenario reads generated and rules-library scenarios as well as the card's own. every_entry_a_card_says_is_carried_out_by_a_hand_written_scenario (tests/vocabulary.rs) stays: it is the gate that every vocabulary entry has a hand-written scenario somewhere.
  • The oracle half of the bar: just card check fails a generated card whose generated scenarios are all unsupported by both oracles, because then nothing outside Beryl has played it. Such a card keeps a hand-written scenario the oracles can play.
  • beryl_cli script-check and the specs (beryl_cli/src/specs/auto.rs) derive a card's moments from its scenarios: check they still get moments for a card with generated scenarios only.

Prove it on three cards in this bead: remove the scenarios module of Counterspell and of two other cards whose generated scenarios kill every mutant, and leave the rest to the conversion bead.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.
