From the M1 core Warden (by-q44s.1.17), for whoever plans M2: on verbs-zones, the card half of the 'reachable' check (the 24-point uncovered strip) ran on 0 frames from both seats. The cause is that table/scene.rs writes every card with interactive: false. When cards become hoverable, that check runs on recorded frames for the first time, so expect its first real faults then. Also, agent::checks::lines_hold is called only by unit tests: Page::check_all and pages::check_recording check one frame at a time and never compare consecutive frames. Wiring it in needs the previous frame's arrows, because Frame is not Clone. The 'interface checks include lines hold' exit check needs that.

Derek Thurn2026-11-15

From the milestone 1 architecture audit (by-q44s.2.1). What milestone 2's beads should know; each of the three gate beads below blocks this milestone through the gate.

  1. Step timing still chosen in the clients: by-q44s.2.3. The web stage times the cards that make room itself (ui/src/stage/tracks.ts REGROUP 170 ms, WAITS with tap's TURNED) and reads strike's phases from data/ui-conventions.json. by-q44s.2.3 adds StepTiming::room and StepTiming::phases in rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/script/timing.rs and makes the web read them. So by-q44s.3.16 must take the regroup time from timing.room, not a new slide.regroup_ms in space.json, and by-q44s.3.15 must take the phases from timing.phases; beryl_scene's build script never reads data/ui-conventions.json. by-q44s.3.13's tap and by-q44s.3.11's vanish use timing.room for what makes room around them. Note that step timing lives in beryl_protocol now, not beryl_session; the epic's text is corrected.
  2. The desktop layout becomes data first: by-q44s.2.4. DESKTOP in beryl_scene/src/table/mod.rs moves into data/beryl3d/space.json under layout.desktop, read by one generic build-script rule (objects become modules, numbers f32, two- and three-number arrays Vec2 and Vec3). After it, by-q44s.3.2's players anchors, by-q44s.3.7's camera, by-q44s.3.25's hands and by-q44s.3.26's piles change values in space.json and fields of Layout, and none of them edits build.rs.
  3. Test views: by-q44s.2.5. Five Beryl 3D build breaks in two days came from struct literals of ObjectView and Script in testing.rs, stress.rs and tests/common. After by-q44s.2.5, build every test object with beryl_scene::testing and ..ObjectView::neutral(..). That includes by-q44s.3.33's stress boards.
  4. The lane is over budget: by-wzq9, now under the gate. The beryl3d lane takes a median of 109 s (60 s target); compiling under three profiles and linking 48 test binaries is most of it. Add new integration tests as modules of a crate's existing test binary, not as new files under tests/, once that bead has merged them.
  5. scene-purity is stricter (bbc704dd2): it bans HashMap, HashSet, Instant, the locks, OnceLock, LazyLock, thread_local and Atomic* however they are imported, and the modules std::time, thread, fs, net, env, process, io and os inside braced groups too. A new dependency of beryl_scene must be added to SCENE_DEPENDENCIES in tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/purity.py, after checking that it reads no clock, starts no thread and touches no file.
  6. Shader cost. In beryl_render/src/shader.wgsl every fragment samples the picture array before the material switch, because textureSample needs uniform control flow, so the table and panels pay a texture sample they do not use. When by-q44s.3.22 and .3.23 add shade_streak and the light materials, sample the atlas with textureSampleLevel (no derivatives) inside the branch that needs it if the shader_cost limits get tight.
  7. by-q44s.3.2 already gives Frame::sounds its capacity; today it is Vec::new() and the first sound pushed would allocate in frame.
