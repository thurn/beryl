Close reason

Phone graveyard sheets list exile, emblems draw and lift abilities, and phone checks pass

Description

The phone draws no exile pile and no emblem. An exiled card "rests unseen under its owner's graveyard" and cannot be read; a player's emblem is not drawn at all, and the screen audit says so (screen: you's emblem Teferi, Hero of Dominaria Emblem not drawn, from screenFaults in ui/scripts/capture.mjs, on the fixture emblems with ?phone). Make both readable on a phone.

Where the code is

  • ui/src/stage/phone.ts: the phone's layout of the stage. Its header comment says what it leaves out. pile(side, zone) answers where a pile rests (the Layout interface in ui/src/stage/layout.ts); for "exile" it answers the graveyard's place.
  • ui/src/stage/desktop.ts: the desktop's layout. It draws each emblem as a card (emblemObjects(view) and emblemSlot(side, index) from ui/src/game/zoneViews.ts: an emblem is an ObjectView under a negative slot), and lifts an emblem's ability out of that card (its function near line 167 that finds an ability's source). ui/src/game/layout.ts near line 227: "Emblems stand after their player's other permanents."
  • ui/src/mobile/layout.ts: phoneLayout(view, viewport) places the battlefield in rows per side (Rows: lands, other, creatures; the function that sorts a side's permanents into them is near line 167).
  • ui/src/stage/PhoneChrome.tsx: phonePiles(table) lists the piles a touch opens and filters exile out (pile.zone !== "exile"); PileButtons puts a button over each such pile; PileSheet lists a pile's cards under its name (pileName); landed.
  • ui/src/mobile/chrome.tsx: Piles draws each strip's library and graveyard edges (.phone-pile-edge).
  • ui/src/game/zoneViews.ts: zonePiles(table, order) gives each side's piles, exile among them, and each pile's name.
  • ui/scripts/stage-audit.mjs: the stage's audit. by-w8gv.21.31 taught it that a phone draws no exile (commit "test: the audit knows the opening and the phone's exile"): find that allowance with grep -n -i "phone" ui/scripts/stage-audit.mjs.

What to build

  1. Exiled cards are listed in their owner's graveyard sheet. No exile pile is added to the strip: it has no room beside the mana pool.
    • PileSheet for a graveyard shows the graveyard's cards as now and then, if that player's exile holds any card the viewer may see, a second title with the exile pile's own name (from zonePiles, as the desktop names it) and those cards, newest first, each inspected by a touch as the graveyard's are.
    • Cards a permanent holds in exile are not listed there: they stay tucked under that permanent, as now (held from ui/src/game/zoneViews.ts tells which).
    • The graveyard's button (PileButtons) is there whenever the graveyard or the exile list is non-empty. When the graveyard is empty and the exile list is not, Piles draws the pile's edge (.phone-pile-edge) at the graveyard's place so that there is something to touch.
    • The resting pose of an exiled card does not change (unseen under the graveyard), so no verb changes.
  2. Emblems stand after their player's other permanents, as on the desktop.
    • In phoneLayout, add emblemObjects(view) for each side to that side's other row, after its permanents, so that each emblem gets a Spot under its emblemSlot and the stage draws it as a card there.
    • In phone.ts, an emblem's ability lifts out of its emblem's card, as desktop.ts does it: share that function rather than copying it (export it from desktop.ts, as phone.ts already imports drawn, emerging, half and middle from there).
    • A touch on an emblem shows it whole, as a touch on any card the prompt has no use for does. Nothing else can be done with one (isEmblem).
  3. Update phone.ts's header comment, and remove the audit's allowance for a phone's exile only if the audit can now find the cards (it reads the page; cards listed in a closed sheet are not on it, so the allowance probably stays: say which in a comment on this bead).
