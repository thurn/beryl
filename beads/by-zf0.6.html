Close reason

Frozen save corpus (9 gzipped saves) held at 3 levels by tests/corpus.rs; just freeze-saves generator

Description

Part of the durable-saves epic (read it first). Depends on the migrations bead.

A corpus of frozen saves that a test keeps playable, cheap enough for just test-rust.

Build

  • rust/crates/beryl_session/tests/saves/: save files committed and never regenerated. New saves are only added.
  • A generator (beryl_cli subcommand, just freeze-saves): plays seeded games with cheap agents (no MCTS) and keeps a small set that covers every card in data/arena-powered-cube-2025-10-30.csv cast or activated and every DecisionKind at least once, plus decks::VINTAGE_WU_CONTROL. Prints the coverage it reached. Can also freeze a given replay (a bug report's, a game played in the app).
  • Freeze new saves regularly (say weekly) so the corpus has saves of every age; the recipe makes that one command.
  • The test, per save, at three levels: (1) it replays to the end, (2) every checkpoint matches, (3) the same winner on the same turn. A save in the breaks list is held to its recorded step.
  • Budget: replay needs no search; measure it, keep the whole corpus well under a second, and compress the files if their size matters.

Tests

The corpus test itself; plus a test that a deliberately edited save fails at the right level with a readable message.
