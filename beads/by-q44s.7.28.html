Handoff: pushed as staging 20e08e233 (feat: lay out phone tray spreads without overlap).

Cause: the phone layout used the desktop TraySpace, whose across width is max(0, middle - stack_side) = 0 on a phone stage, so every tray card was piled on the column's middle.

What changed (beryl_scene):

  • table/tray_layout.rs: TraySpace gains across: Across (Middle for the desktop, Column for the phone), second: Option<Second> (a band for the second of two piles, the web's TraySpace.second) and least_step (0 on the desktop). rows closes up no further than least_step and overlaps rows instead (row pitch). fan keeps least_step between neighbours and falls back to rows in deal order when one row has no room. New stacked lays out two piles one box over the other when second is set.
  • table/tray_layout/put_back.rs (moved out for file length): with a second band, the hand lies there in a straight row (no turn or drop: tilted neighbours 52 points apart failed reachable), spaced by the cards it holds now, and the cards shrink below least to fit a short band (375x647). The library's cascade is at least least_step.
  • table/phone.rs tray/tray_in: the web's phoneTray in logical points: over the opponent's rows to the middle band, and a second band from the middle band to just above the prompt's dock line. space.json layout.phone.tray holds the sizes (least_step 52, as the phone stack's step).
  • table/mod.rs Layout::tray_for(window); prompts/split.rs and choose.rs use it instead of DESKTOP.tray.
  • prompts/bar.rs: while a prompt holds cards up and puts no button forward, its stacked buttons ("more", e.g. a disabled Done) stand on the dock's line instead of above the strip, so that they don't cover the second band. Deviation from the bead's text: needed because that button sat over the put-back's hand at both sizes.
  • tests/all/table_phone.rs every_card_held_up_on_the_phone_keeps_a_square_to_press.

Results: phone matrix at check times went from 1049 to 1011 failures (reachable 444 to 399), and every tick from reachable 825 to 407. A classification of each remaining reachable fault by its card's place (tree --json) found no tray card: they are battlefield cards, stress-hand's 60-card hand, table-stack's 14-card hand fan, and one card bouncing mid-flight. The 2 choice-trigger-order panics (hud/rows.rs row counts) were in the baseline. Desktop just b3d test passes 8264/8264, and just b3d gate passes. The remaining phone faults belong to by-q44s.9.21.

Reviewer: check put_back's own-band sizes() fallback, and the bar.rs dock-line rule on prompts with several "more" buttons.
