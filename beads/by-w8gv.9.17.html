Description

Milestone 5 of Project 50k (plan K5 and K3; epic by-w8gv.9): the rules library and the card port. Line numbers and counts below were read at staging f5034c58; the code as it stands wins over this text, and a deviation is a comment on this bead.

Third of three port beads, after the first (see its handoff in enact brief for the entries it added and the pattern it set) and beside the second. These cards grant permissions to cast, copy objects, transform and remember what they exiled. All paths are under rust/crates/beryl_cards/src.

The cards

  • blue/snapcaster_mage.rs: "target instant or sorcery card in your graveyard gains flashback until end of turn. The flashback cost is equal to its mana cost" (CR 702.34).
  • blue/jace_vryns_prodigy.rs: "Draw a card, then discard a card. If there are five or more cards in your graveyard, exile Jace, Vryn's Prodigy, then return him to the battlefield transformed under his owner's control" (CR 701.27, CR 712); the back face's -3 "You may cast target instant or sorcery card from your graveyard this turn. If that spell would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead" (a permission with a replacement, CR 614); and its -9 emblem (CR 114). The +1 and the emblem's trigger are data already.
  • white/elite_spellbinder.rs: "look at target opponent's hand. You may exile a nonland card from it. For as long as that card remains exiled, its owner may play it. A spell cast this way costs {2} more to cast": a look and a choice, then a permission with a cost increase and a duration tied to the card staying exiled.
  • blue/mana_drain.rs: "Counter target spell. At the beginning of your next main phase, add an amount of {C} equal to that spell's mana value": a delayed triggered ability (CR 603.7) that carries a number locked in as the spell resolves (CR 608.2h), and mana added by a triggered ability that is not a mana ability (the M2 Mason audit's item 4: where the kind of mana comes from outside a mana ability).
  • azorius/fractured_identity.rs: "Exile target nonland permanent. Each player other than its controller creates a token that's a copy of it" (CR 707, CR 608.2h for what it last was, CR 101.4 for the order).
  • white/adeline_resplendent_cathar.rs: "Whenever you attack, for each opponent, create a 1/1 white Human creature token that's tapped and attacking that player or a planeswalker they control" (CR 508.4).
  • blue/abhorrent_oculus.rs: "At the beginning of each opponent's upkeep, manifest dread" (CR 701.62).
  • colorless/currency_converter.rs: "Whenever you discard a card, you may exile that card from your graveyard"; "{T}: Put a card exiled with Currency Converter into its owner's graveyard. If it's a land card, create a Treasure token. If it's a nonland card, create a 2/2 black Rogue creature token" (linked abilities, CR 607); and its loot. Needs::ExiledWithSource is the last one-card entry on data/card-names-allowlist.json's mechanics list this milestone owns: an activated ability whose effect chooses among objects needs one to exist, which is read from the data.

New entries this bead owns

A permission to cast an object, with its options (who, how long, at what extra cost, where the spell goes afterwards); a keyword granted to a card outside the battlefield; transforming; a copy of an object as it last existed, made by a set of players; a token that enters attacking; manifest dread; "the cards exiled with this" as objects, and exiling linked to the source; a number locked in for a delayed trigger; a condition on the type of the object an instruction acted on. The second port bead writes the delayed triggered ability and the emblem and pushes them first: use them, and if it has not pushed them when you reach Mana Drain and Jace, take the other cards first.

Gotchas

  • Permissions and the flashback grant are entries of the Effects store that the library lane typed; cast.rs reads them for every priority decision, on the counted path.
  • Jace's transformation is in the benchmark's decks, and so is Currency Converter: watch just bench-count after each.
  • Adeline's tokens ask which planeswalker or player each attacks only when there is a choice: keep the question exactly as asked today.

How a card is ported

  • Start from just card brief <name>: Oracle text, rulings, matching vocabulary entries, the nearest implemented cards, the XMage class.
  • The card file takes the form the vocabulary lane's last bead set (see its handoff in enact brief and the fifteen data-only card files it converted): built with the const builders, every ability's behavior effect data, the definition check beside it, the vocabulary imported from beryl_card. Its Rust bodies, bodies!, conditions!, may_pause, needs and .removal() go, since the data now says them.
  • A new vocabulary entry is designed from its rule, not from the card's sentence: it takes Objects, Players, Amount, Condition or a Filter wherever the rule has a parameter, and it says what the card says (K7: "its controller", "each opponent"). It carries its Comprehensive Rules citation with a quotation, on the entry and on the library function. The interpreter's arm and the library function go where the contract at the top of rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/lib.rs says; Effect::asks, Effect::takes and the definition check are extended; one hand-written, CR-cited scenario for the entry goes where the contract says library scenarios live (K3). The library lane already made the functions general: most entries only name one.
  • A body stays only where the vocabulary cannot say the card and an entry for it would be one card's sentence again. Write it against Game and Resolution as the contract says, and say why in a comment on the body and on this bead. If a second card needs it, it moves into the library (plan, A card file).
  • Two cards that say the same thing use the same entry. Helpers shared by two cards live in the library, never in a card file (AGENTS.md).
  • The card's existing scenarios are the proof of parity: they pass unedited. Do not change a scenario to fit the port. Saves and rollouts replay each decision, so a ported ability asks the same questions in the same order with the same options, makes the same events in the same order and draws the same random numbers.
  • Port what the body does today. Where a body does less than the Oracle text (a clause the engine has no mechanic for), the data does the same and no more (K14: no new mechanic): list each such clause on this bead.
  • Delete each EffectCtx shim method that no body calls any more.
  • just oracle <each card> passes (the fast check runs it for changed card files).

What the reader of this bead should know

  • The WU Control mirror the benchmark plays holds many of these cards: interpreting data where a body ran is on the counted path. just bench-count before and after, and after each group of cards if the count moves.
  • The M2 Mason audit, item 6: beryl_cards/src/tests/effect_data.rs keeps five Rust bodies as a reference for the interpreter. Leave it; the closing bead deletes it.
  • The readings of each card below were taken from its body at staging f5034c58 and are a starting point, not a specification: the Oracle text in the card file's header and the brief decide.
