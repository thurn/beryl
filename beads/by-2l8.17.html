Description

Sage finding (by-2l8.12). Two gaps in tools/src/beryl_tools/checks.py that let one failure cost far more than it should.

  1. Slow CI has no deadline. run_lanes only compares elapsed time with the 300 s budget after every step has returned, and run_lane calls subprocess.run with no timeout. On 2026-11-06 the shots lane's target/debug/beryl_cli record-fixture hung inside _dyld_start (macOS sample: every sample at dyld_start, 0.10 s CPU, exec never finished). Slow CI at 1b7e575 ran 2588 s instead of ~75 s, and master stayed 13 commits behind until the Sage killed the process by hand (Steward by-5f0). Enact now kills a slow check after [checks] slow_timeout (default 3600 s, Enact 0d0cbab), but that is a coarse backstop, and its failure log cannot say which step hung. Change: give each run a hard deadline of 2x its budget (fast 120 s, slow 600 s; CI_BUDGET_S scales it). Run every step in its own process group (start_new_session=True). When the deadline passes, os.killpg every running step with SIGKILL and fail its lane with <lane> <step>: TIMED OUT after N s (deadline D s). Also set slow_timeout = 900 in .enact.toml as a second line of defense. Expected gain: a hang costs at most 10 minutes of slow CI and a Steward bead naming the step, instead of an unbounded stall that holds every run's delivery.
  2. Nothing stops baked-in build paths from coming back. env!("CARGO_BIN_EXE_...") and env!("CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR") fix the compiling worktree's absolute path, while the warm cache (warm.py) clones rust/target into other worktrees with test binaries cargo counts as fresh. Tests then run a deleted worktree's binary, or read its files, and pass against the wrong code. This has been fixed twice in two days (26a8535; 59ec509 after by-2l8.3 reintroduced it in the longthink tests). No uses remain. Change: a step in the rust lane, like card-names, that fails on either macro under rust/crates, pointing at beryl_cli/tests/exe/mod.rs (std::env::current_exe) and run-time std::env::var("CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR"). Expected gain: the next reintroduction fails the fast check instead of producing false greens.

Acceptance criteria

  • A step that hangs (test it with a lane step that runs sleep, with the deadline lowered by environment variable) fails its lane within the deadline, and the output names the step. No process from that step survives, grandchildren included (pytest).
  • Neither the fast check's nor slow CI's normal results or timings change.
  • .enact.toml sets slow_timeout = 900.
  • Adding env!("CARGO_BIN_EXE_beryl_cli") or env!("CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR") to any file under rust/crates fails just check with a message naming the run-time alternative (shown in a pytest).

Delivered in 5005be6. Each check step runs in its own session; the run's deadline is 2x budget (fast 120 s, slow 600 s), overridable by CHECK_DEADLINE_S (the bead didn't name a variable). At the deadline kill_tree SIGKILLs the step's group plus every descendant found by ps, because cargo-nextest puts each test in its own process group, which a killpg alone would miss; the pytest covers a grandchild that setsid'd and fails if the tree walk is removed. Output goes to a temp file instead of a pipe, so a straggler can't block reading it. Ctrl-C now kills running steps explicitly (they no longer share the terminal's foreground group); checked by hand: exits in 0.2 s and leaves no child. Limit: if Enact's slow_timeout SIGKILLs by ci itself, steps in their own sessions are orphaned, which is why slow_timeout (900) sits above the 600 s deadline. build-paths is the rust lane's first step; lines that start with a // comment are skipped. Fast check and by ci rust ui: normal results, usual timings (rust 38 s, build-paths 0 s).
