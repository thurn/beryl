Saves (v3) record each first-seen card and coin flip; replay binds them and no longer needs the seed

Description

Part of the durable-saves epic (read it first). Depends on the semantic-actions bead.

Make saves independent of the RNG: record what each random event produced, and never use the seed on replay.

Build

  • One replaceable source of hidden-card identities in beryl_engine, used at the knowledge chokepoints (architecture §13: draw, look at, reveal, cast, play, face-down exile, search and tutor; see where knowledge is set in effect.rs, resolve.rs, face_down.rs, start.rs, state.rs).
    • Live play: unchanged, plus the session records each reveal (which slot became known, to whom, and the card's name).
    • Replay: when a slot is first revealed, bind the recorded card to it by swapping identities among the unknown slots of that hidden group, the move Observation::determinize (observation.rs) and the shuffle's rebinding (effect.rs shuffle) already make. A recorded card that no unknown slot can hold stops the replay at that step.
  • Other randomness on the other stream, recorded by outcome and matched in order per kind: coin flips (effect.rs, around line 1349), the play-first coin flip (start.rs), random discards and other random picks. Random bottom orders need nothing: they settle on reveal like a shuffle.
  • Object ids drawn from the RNG (zone::rebind) must not appear in the save.
  • No cost on the search hot path: search copies of State don't record or replay. just count-gate must hold (bar in rust/crates/beryl_bench/count-gate.toml); check just bench-count.
  • The save's reveal entries can name card instances for the semantic actions' disambiguation (see that bead).
  • Update architecture §13/§14 to say how replays get their randomness.

Tests

  • A recorded game replays identically under a different seed: the reveal log alone drives it.
  • Brainstorm then shuffle, a tutor, Thoughtseize, a mulligan, a random discard and a coin flip each round-trip.
  • The information-independence test still passes.
