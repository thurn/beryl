Description

Read first: the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md; docs/beryl-3d.md 'The Enhanced tier', 'Choosing a tier', 'The frame' (bands, the material table) and 'Performance gates'.

This is milestone 6's contract and its one check. Every other bead of the milestone depends on it. It builds no shading: nothing here changes a pixel of either tier.

What exists: Input::Tier and Tier { Baseline, Enhanced } (beryl_scene src/input.rs), stored as Client::tier and set by Client::set_tier (src/client/mod.rs), which low memory pins to Baseline. Nothing reads it yet. Material::Particle and Draw::particle(transform, tint, sprite, age, seed) exist in src/frame/draw.rs; nothing writes one. Contact shadows are written by write_shadow in src/table/shadows.rs.

Build, in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene:

  • Frame::tier: Tier (src/frame.rs), written by Client::frame from Client::tier before anything is drawn (FrameBuilder::new takes it), and FrameBuilder::tier() so that table and effect code can read it. The renderer reads Frame::tier to choose its passes; nothing else in the renderer may ask the client.
  • Band::Particles, after Band::Glows and before Band::Layer, in src/frame.rs and in order.rs (band, BANDS becomes 9, the band's place in the sort key). Particles are blended, drawn with the depth test like the other table bands (Pass::Table), farthest first. Add the band to the doc's list of bands in 'The frame'.
  • Frame::bands: [u32; 10]: the index in draws where each band starts, by Band value, and the end as the last entry, filled by FrameBuilder::finish. The bloom bead draws the Glows band's records again from this.
  • Load::particles: u32, the particle records of the frame, counted where Load is counted.
  • PARTICLES: usize = 2000 in src/frame.rs, the most particle records a frame holds, added into DRAWS so that the renderer's instance buffer has room for them. The gates' limit tiers.enhanced.particles (a later bead) is the same number.
  • The tier's records: write_shadow writes nothing when builder.tier() is Tier::Enhanced (the shadow map replaces contact shadows), and FrameBuilder::draw debug-asserts that no Material::Particle record is pushed in Tier::Baseline. That is the only use of the tier in the client core.

The check, in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_inspect:

  • Playback::with_tier(self, tier) -> Self in src/gate.rs, which hands the client Input::Tier(tier) before its first frame.
  • tests/all/tiers.rs, every_gated_board_resolves_and_draws_the_same_in_both_tiers: for each name of gate::BOARDS, play one Playback in each tier in lockstep (the same frames gate::play draws). At each frame, require resolved and names to be equal, and require the draw lists to be equal after removing every Material::ContactShadow record from the Baseline list and every Material::Particle record from the Enhanced list. On a difference, the message names the board, the frame, the index and both records. It runs in the lane's cargo-test step; it starts no renderer and no engine (rule 7a). Report its time in a comment; if it takes more than 3 seconds in the lane's profile, compare only at the frames gate::rank picks and at each view's first frame, and say so.
  • A test in beryl_scene/tests/all that a frame of a lifted card holds a ContactShadow record in Baseline and none in Enhanced, and that Frame::bands brackets each record's band.

Must not build: shading, the renderer's Enhanced passes, particles, choosing the tier.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_inspect; just b3d gate; just b3d lane.

Beads named above: the contract bead (the tier input and the check) is by-q44s.11.2; the renderer skeleton bead is by-q44s.11.3; the shadow map bead by-q44s.11.4; the lighting bead by-q44s.11.5; bloom by-q44s.11.6; particles by-q44s.11.7; the tier choice bead by-q44s.11.8; the settings row by-q44s.11.9; the limits bead by-q44s.11.10; the settings screen bead by-q44s.5.27.
