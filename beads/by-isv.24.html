Reproduced in ui-test before the fix: 10-70% of both Back to buttons hit pile-button / pile-at (matches the report). Decision: rather than raise the button over the piles (which would hide most of the opponent's top cards while the player is peeking at the battlefield), moved .overlay-return to the turn button's spot (bottom right, 250x48, z 600 over the hand). That spot is empty in both states: turn-controls are hidden while a prompt is up (inert) and there is no turn action once the game is over. Deviation from the old 'where View Battlefield was' placement, since the top right belongs to the opponent's piles. Added: game-over board gives the opponent a graveyard and exile pile; ui-test assertReachable (5 points via elementFromPoint, asserts both opponent piles present) in the result check and a new choice-scry View Battlefield check; shot game-choice-scry-battlefield.
