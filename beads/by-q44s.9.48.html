Description

Parts of two no-equivalent UI Conventions entries that Beryl 3D's manual table still does not follow (left from by-q44s.9.23, which made both entries recording entries for what it built):

  • "Manual counters and untap flags": a named counter on a permanent shows only its count on the card's badge (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/badges.rs counters), not its shortened name. The web's NamedCounterBadge (ui/src/components/Card.tsx) shows the name cut to five characters and an ellipsis past six, then the count. A permanent flagged doesnt_untap shows nothing; the web shows a lock badge in its corner (card-untap-lock). Badges preshape only digits once (Badges::shape) and have a fixed record budget per card (BUDGET, RECORDS_PER_OBJECT), so names need shaping as views arrive and room in the budget; check just b3d gate and move limits in the same commit if work rises.
  • "Manual table: a click acts": a refused table action plays the refusal sound (Manual::take_refusal in src/client/manual.rs) but does not shake what was clicked, as the web's shakeRefusal does. Remember the element or card a click sent each action from (Sent in src/manual/mod.rs) and shake it when take_table_reply gets Refused.
  • On the phone, the web opens a sheet of mana and player counters from the mana total beside the life orb; Beryl 3D shows no counters on the phone layout (manual::pools::pools returns none there).

Read first: docs/ui-conventions.md for both entries; rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/badges.rs; src/manual/pools.rs; src/client/manual.rs; ui/src/components/Card.tsx; ui/src/stage/PhoneChrome.tsx. Test through beryl_scene's tests/all/manual_controls.rs or a frame check.

Acceptance criteria

Named counters on permanents show shortened names, doesnt_untap permanents show a lock, a refused click shakes what was clicked, and the phone layout reaches mana and player counters; tests cover each; just b3d test and just b3d gate pass.
