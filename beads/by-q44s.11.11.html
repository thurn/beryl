Derek Thurn2026-11-17

Warden review of the Enhanced tier (0c8926dbc, 943fe103d).

What each Enhanced feature was looked at with:

  • Shadow map: verbs-zones at 700-1000ms, film --tier enhanced --scale 2 (the stack's Thirst for Discovery and the flying Island), and the destroyed vanish of Trinket Mage at 9600-10150ms (film --tier enhanced and baseline, cropped to the card). Faint shadows only, as the lighting handoff said; checked further by the two new pixel tests below.
  • Lighting: verbs-zones --at 900ms, png --scale 2 in both tiers. Positions identical; flat battlefield cards a little lighter from the sheen.
  • Bloom: same png. The flying Island's glow has a wider, brighter halo in Enhanced; text and panels unchanged.
  • Multisampling: same png and film. Tilted hand cards and the flying card have smooth edges; the flat layer (life totals, side buttons) matches Baseline.
  • Particles: Trinket Mage's ash at 9600-10150ms and Mana Crypt's vortex (exiled) at 14600-14980ms, film --tier enhanced cropped to each card.

Found and fixed:

  1. A fading or dissolving card cast the shadow of its whole rectangle into the shadow map, so a card that was vanishing left a solid shadow while it broke up, where Baseline's contact shadow fades with opacity. vs_shadow now reads tint and params: a card under half opaque casts nothing (phased-out cards are 0.4), and a dissolving card's vertices above the middle of its breaking edge are moved down onto it. Tests: a_card_less_than_half_opaque_casts_no_shadow, a_half_dissolved_card_casts_only_its_lower_half (tests/all/shadow.rs). Doc updated.
  2. Bloom made its two half-size targets and three bind groups on the first frame with a glow, usually the first frame of a script, and after low memory; the probe draws stress-255, which has no glows, so this happened in play. Enhanced::render now sizes bloom's targets with the main pass's on every frame (a size check), and Bloom::apply only reads them. Test: the_first_enhanced_frame_makes_bloom_targets_without_a_glow (unit test in enhanced/mod.rs).
  3. The shadow map's bind groups were made the first time each slot was drawn. They are made in Shadow::new now; Shadow no longer holds a device or an Option per slot.
  4. Added Playback::set_tier and a_step_down_in_the_middle_of_a_vanish_draws_what_baseline_draws (beryl_inspect tests/all/tiers.rs): stepping down on the first frame with particles, every later frame's elements and records equal the Baseline playback's.

Checked and left alone: shake is added to clip only, never to light_position, so shadows move with cards; the client reads the tier only in write_shadow and effects::particles::write; --tier and --probe are tools-only; choose() and set_graphics never hand Enhanced to a renderer without it; low memory pins Baseline (set_tier ignored after it); particles are a pure function of the run and now, seeded by slot and step, capped at PARTICLES with an equal share; the tier check's BOARDS already include stress-verbs, stress-hand and stress-stack; the Baseline shader and limits are unchanged.

Ran: just b3d cargo-test (811 passed), just b3d test (8312 tests, 0 failed), just b3d gate (pass), just b3d gate --gpu (pass), just b3d lane (pass), and the fast check in enact push. gate --gpu warns that every board is slightly over its fragment limit in both tiers (e.g. pay-default Baseline 1.0498 against 1.0126); Baseline moved by the same amounts and this change does not touch Baseline drawing, so the drift comes from earlier staging changes and only warns during the build-out.
