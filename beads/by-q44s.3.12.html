Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Verbs', 'Objects and placement' (cards held up to be read) and 'Interface checks'; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/verbs/flip.rs, showcase.rs and lift.rs (stubs), src/table/place.rs, src/table/scene.rs, src/light/mod.rs (Span::Lift). Web, for flip: ui/src/stage/tracks.ts (FLIP, turnedOver), ui/src/stage/enter.ts (coverOf). For showcase: ui/src/stage/revealed.ts (fan, PileSpace: the Revealed Cards pile at the top left, the last revealed on top), ui/src/stage/desktop.ts (DESKTOP_REVEALED), ui/src/stage/tracks.ts (SHOWN, shownUp), ui/src/stage/enter.ts (stillShown, showingOn), ui/src/stage/table.ts (Revealed), and the twice flag of showcase in data/ui-conventions.json: the card's face is shown a second time, in the pile. For lift: ui/src/game/layout.ts (handlingPlacements: where the cards the opponent is handling stand), ui/src/stage/table.ts (handledAt, and the lift branch of apply), and CardHandling in rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/protocol.

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build:

  • flip: the card turns over where it lies, its Pose::turn running from what it was to what its record says over the step, rising and growing as the web's turnedOver has it (copy FLIP's values as verbs.flip.rise and verbs.flip.scale), as a new Shape::TurnedOver. The veil over it is the cover effect, which is by-q44s.3.24's.
  • showcase: the card stays where it lies, and its face joins the Revealed Cards pile: a second scene thing, kept by the table in the order revealed, placed relative to the camera at the top left as fan lays the pile out (copy DESKTOP_REVEALED as revealed.{x, y, width, step} in normalized device coordinates), drawn as a card with the revealed face, Role::Card, the card's name, and ElementId((1 << 21) + slot). It comes up as shownUp has it: from 0.6 of its size, opaque by half its step (SHOWN), as a new Shape::Grown. The duplicates check must allow this second element: give agent::checks::duplicates the rule that an element id at or above 1 << 21 is a revealed face. A script with a reset empties the pile. The pill over the pile and the browser it opens into are milestone 3's.
  • lift: Span::Begin makes the cards the opponent is handling (handledAt's rule: the top count cards of the library, or of the pile named) come away from their pile to where handlingPlacements puts them, face down, over the step, staggered as the timing says; Span::End returns them. While the span is open, placement gives those cards their lifted places: Table::placement reads the open Span::Lift from Lit.

Do not build: the tray a prompt holds up (by-q44s.3.26).

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/verbs_shown.rs, on views and scripts built by hand with testing::ViewBuilder, ScriptBuilder and testing::timing: for each case of the verb in data/ui-conventions.json (flip; showcase; lift's tray and sort cases and its plain values), a script with that case's duration and delay plays to its end with every object at its placement (agent::steps::rest_equals_placement finds nothing), no_jumps finds nothing, and step_times finds nothing. A flipped card's FACE_DOWN state changes at the middle of its step; a showcased card has two resolved elements, and duplicates finds nothing; three showcased cards stand in the pile in the order revealed; lifted cards are back in their pile after the span ends, and a frame between the two shows them at their lifted places.

When it is done: set BUILT = true in the verb's file, so that Client::next_unbuilt stops reporting it; run just b3d test --update and just b3d timeline-parity --update if those commands exist on staging by then, and commit the lowered lists.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d check verbs-zones; just b3d lane.
