Description

Read first: tools/src/beryl_tools/motion_film.py (ARENA_FPS = 15, ARENA_WIDTH = 760, retake, arena_frames, take_lines: how just motion --film <verb> [case] cuts Arena's frames of a verb), tools/src/beryl_tools/motion.py (register, the --film options); .claude/skills/motion-review/SKILL.md and reviewer.md, and .claude/skills/arena-pixel-match/SKILL.md (what the films are used for); rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_pictures/src/film.rs (times, film, MOST_FRAMES) and docs/beryl-3d.md 'Commands' (film).

Three jobs of the parity list compare a verb's motion with Arena's on film: just motion --film, the motion-review skill and the arena-pixel-match skill. Beryl 3D can draw frames (just b3d film), but not at Arena's frame rate and size, and nothing pairs them with Arena's clips.

Rust, beryl_pictures src/film.rs: options --rate <frames a second> (frames at that rate across --from to --to, still capped at MOST_FRAMES, with the existing error naming a lower rate) and --frame-width <px> (each frame scaled to that width, keeping aspect, using shrink). --rate conflicts with --frames and --every as those two already conflict. --crop keeps working.

Python, new module tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/motion_film.py with just b3d motion-film <verb> [case]: finds a recording that plays the verb (parity.json's verbs entry, or --recording), finds the step's start and end with just b3d timeline, films it with --rate 15 --frame-width 760 from both seats and both layouts, cuts Arena's frames of the same verb and case with the main package's motion_film.retake and arena_frames (import them; do not copy), and prints the pairs of paths and each film's take_lines-style text. Arena's recordings live outside the repository ($BERYL_ARENA); when none is indexed, film Beryl 3D's side alone and say so on one line.

Skills: a "Beryl 3D" section in motion-review/SKILL.md and arena-pixel-match/SKILL.md saying to film with just b3d motion-film and, for one frame, just b3d png --tree.

Tests: in beryl_pictures (tests or a unit test module of film.rs): --rate 15 over 1 s gives 15 frame times 66.7 ms apart; --rate with --frames is a usage error. In tools/tests/test_beryl3d_motion_film.py, with programs stubbed: the film command passes --rate 15 --frame-width 760. Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_pictures, just test-py tests/test_beryl3d_motion_film.py.

Each skill keeps working for the web interface until the switch: add a Beryl 3D section, never replace the web instructions. Register any new command through MODULES in tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/cli.py with a COMMANDS tuple in your own module, --help and two examples (rule 19). When the job works, set this job's b3d in data/beryl3d/parity.json's tools to the command or skill section that does it. Rule 17: no new recipe in the justfile and no change to tools outside tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d except the skill's own files.
