Description

Cards

  • Fork: "Copy target instant or sorcery spell, except that the copy is red. You may choose new targets for the copy."
  • Tendrils of Agony: "Target player loses 2 life and you gain 2 life. Storm."
  • Brain Freeze: "Target player mills three cards. Storm."

What to build

Plan: Designs 5, "Copies of spells" (CR 707.10).

  • Effect::CopySpell: the spells copied, how many times, the copier's exception, and whether new targets may be chosen. A copy takes a slot of the token pool in the stack zone and a stack entry with the original's card, face, modes, targets, X and costs paid. Its controller is the player who made it. It is not cast: nothing that triggers on casting sees it, and it counts for no "spells cast" tracker.
  • New targets (CR 707.10c): for each target in order, the controller may change it to another legal one, through the casting machine's target step (cast/targets.rs), as the copy is made, inside the resolution (choices).
  • A copy that leaves the stack ceases to exist. A copy of a permanent spell becomes a token as it resolves (CR 608.3f); no card here does that, so prove it with a test.
  • Storm (CR 702.40): a triggered ability of the spell itself that works on the stack (by-bajq.1.3), "when you cast this spell", whose count is the spells cast before it this turn by any player (by-bajq.1.4), read as it triggers.
  • Up to twenty copies must not stall Small: the stack holds 64 entries.

Scenarios

Fork on Lightning Bolt with a new target, and with the same target; Fork on Braingeyser (by-bajq.6.7) with X of 3: the copy draws three cards for the player Fork's controller chooses and is red. If by-bajq.6.7 closed by escalating Braingeyser, this bead builds the vocabulary entry its escalation names, then the card, before this scenario; Tendrils with storm 3, each copy targeting; a copy countered; Stifle on the storm trigger is by-bajq.8.1's scenario.

From the events-and-listeners Warden (by-bajq.1.7): storm is the epic's real "when you cast this spell" trigger, which works on the stack (Ability::on_the_stack, trigger::away), and it reads Amount::SpellsCast (by-bajq.1.4). Both were proved only by test definitions (Test Herald in tests/abilities_away.rs, Test Tally in tests/trackers.rs). Tendrils' and Brain Freeze's scenarios must cover: the count is written in cast::steps::finish before the Cast event, so the storm trigger sees its own spell in the count and must subtract one; copies are not counted; and the count is per player and resets each turn (a spell the opponent cast this turn counts for storm, since storm counts every player's spells).
