Count creep beads bisect their range in the bench VM

Description

Slow CI's count gate now files a count-creep bead when one tested staging range adds more than 0.25% to the rollout benchmark's instruction count, or the count drifts 0.5% above its lowest since the bar moved (tools/src/beryl_tools/count_trend.py). The bead names the range, often 3 to 12 commits under rust/, and the session that picks it up must bisect by hand: on 2026-11-13 that took 44 builds of about 30 to 60 s each in the beryl-bench VM.

Do the bisect automatically, in the background like the browser sweep's (sweep.py, its bisect over failed checks), so it costs slow CI nothing:

  • After count_trend.report files or comments on a creep bead for a range of more than one commit, start a detached background job (utility priority) that checks each commit under rust/ in the range out in one reused worktree (so builds stay incremental) and runs by bench-count -p beryl_bench --bench rollout_counts -- --output-format=json rollout_counts::cube_rollout::rollouts::corpus. Close stdin for each run: limactl reads it.
  • Comment the per-commit counts on the bead, naming the commits that add more than 0.1%.
  • Keep it under the VM's lock (bench.locked), and skip it when another bisect is running.

Tests: unit-test the range selection and the comment text in tools/tests/test_count_trend.py; the VM run itself is not tested.
