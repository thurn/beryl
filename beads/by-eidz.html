What they want: the prompt buttons are always on top of cards. Scope: every prompt, at every moment resting cards could reach the dock's area. Interpretation: 'UI' = the dock (title text and buttons); 'always on top' includes over cards that spill into the dock's corner when a row is wide. Fix once: give the dock a depth above everything at rest and above the tray shade, below flying cards, and check the pointer still reaches the buttons. Group these three beads in one lane; fix in one change. Reproduce: just review "<folder>/replay.json" --turn 16 or the fixture route.
