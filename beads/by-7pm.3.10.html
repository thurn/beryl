Description

Play complete games through the phone UI in the iOS Simulator and the Android emulator, fix what breaks, and show that the epic's whole-run acceptance holds. Read the epic's "Whole-run acceptance" and the close reasons of every bead under by-7pm.3 (bd list --parent by-7pm.3 --status closed).

Tools

  • iOS: just ios, then the iOS Simulator tool (screenshot, tap, swipe, touch_path, text). Coordinates are device points; take a screenshot before every tap you are not sure of.
  • Android: just android, then adb -s emulator-5584 shell input tap|swipe|text and adb -s emulator-5584 exec-out screencap -p > <file>. Screenshot pixels are not input's units only if the density is scaled: check one known button first.
  • Never the user's phone (D2).

What to do

  1. Fresh builds: just mobile-setup, just ios-smoke, just android-smoke.
  2. On each platform play two whole games to game over by touch: one against the default opponent (expert) and one against mcts-hidden, with seeds you choose after the last code change (write them down). Do not concede to end a game early. Across the two games on each platform, do each of these at least once and tick it in a table on this bead (rows: the items; columns: iOS, Android; cells: the game and turn):
    • keep a hand; mulligan once and put a card on the bottom;
    • play a land and cast a spell by carrying the card out of the hand;
    • cast a spell with a target, and one with a mode or an X if the hand offers it;
    • pay a cost where more than one payment is possible;
    • activate an ability of a permanent;
    • attack with two creatures; block; take and deal combat damage;
    • respond to the opponent's spell with your own;
    • answer a scry, a search or another look at cards off the table;
    • inspect a card on the table, in the hand and on the stack;
    • open your graveyard's sheet and the opponent's; open the log; open the menu and change a volume;
    • undo an answer where the game offers it;
    • kill the app mid-game and resume it;
    • reach game over, view the battlefield, go back to the result, start a new game.
  3. On each platform file one bug report by touch with a region selected, Share it (see the share sheet, dismiss it), then just mobile-bugs and just bugs: the report is listed with all its files. Mark the two reports done.
  4. Fix each defect you find, in this bead, with a phone shot or a fixture where one can hold it (AGENTS.md, Checks). A rules bug gets its entry in docs/bugs.md. A defect too large for this session: a bead under by-7pm.3 with repro steps, the seed, the files, a lane label and tier:sonnet.
  5. just ci, whole, once at the end, in the worktree (it is the slow check: run it in the background).
