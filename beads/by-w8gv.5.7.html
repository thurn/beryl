Close reason

Players, turn, rng, profile, budgets, flags and outcome are kernel-private behind mutators and getters; engine and its tests write through them or the board door; counts 404,167,248 -> 404,243,458

Description

Milestone 3 (plan K6): only the kernel writes a field. This bead does it for the players, the turn and the game-level fields. It runs beside the by-w8gv.5.3 (objects) bead and touches different fields of the same files; rebase over it.

Read first

docs/project-50k.md: Decisions K6, K7, K11 and K12, and "Crates", "State" and "Kernel rules" under Target architecture. rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs: its rustdoc is the kernel's API contract (who writes, mutator and getter naming, events, the board door, the legacy accessor, sets and orders); follow it, and change it in the same push when the code proves it wrong. enact brief shows the handoffs of the beads this one depends on.

Scope

  • Player<L>: life, mana (ManaPool), lands_played, drew_from_empty, mulligans, mulligan, to_bottom, emblems. player_mut calls in rust/crates/beryl_engine/src when this was decomposed: start.rs 4, event.rs 3, effect.rs 2, mana.rs 2, setup.rs 2, engine.rs 1, zone.rs 1, turn.rs 1, interpret/mod.rs 1, state.rs 1; tests: payment.rs 3, rules.rs 2, events.rs 1, invariants.rs 1. Mutators by what the rules do: gain, lose and set life (CR 119), add and spend mana and empty the pool (CR 106.4, 500.4), play a land (CR 305.2), the mulligan steps (CR 103.5), draw from an empty library (CR 704.5b), get an emblem (CR 114).
  • Turn: number, active, starting_player, step, priority, passes, cleanup_check, attacked. Raw .turn uses: engine.rs 22, turn.rs 20, setup.rs 15, combat.rs 11, cast.rs 4, start.rs 4, profile.rs 4, resolve.rs 3, and a few more. Most are reads (turn() exists). Mutators: advance to a step, begin a turn, pass priority and reset passes (CR 117.3, 117.4), give priority, note an attack, the cleanup check (CR 514.3a).
  • rng (chance.rs 1, effect.rs 2, resolve.rs 1, start.rs 3, zone.rs 3), profile (engine.rs 10, effect.rs 4, cast.rs 3, face_down.rs 3, mana.rs 2), budgets (sba.rs 3, effect.rs 2, engine.rs 2, turn.rs 1), outcome (interpret/run.rs 6, engine.rs 6, effect.rs 3, interpret/scene.rs 3, resolve.rs 2, sba.rs 2 and others; ending the game is sba::end_game), and what is left of flags after the by-w8gv.5.4 (zones) bead's dirty flag.
  • Getters on Player and Turn, #[inline]. Fields stay pub for readers in other crates until the by-w8gv.5.9 (readers) bead flips them: with player_mut private no &mut Player leaves the kernel.
  • Player::sorceries_as_flash (milestone 4 deletes it; effect.rs:551, cast.rs:130, mana.rs:998, turn.rs:76, start.rs:33, relayout.rs:216, setup.rs:142 when by-w8gv.7.1 was written) goes behind the legacy accessor, raw.
  • The tests in beryl_engine/src/tests set life, mana and the step directly: they use the board door.

Constraints

  • players: [Player; 2], Seat::BOTH and .opponent() stay as they are: the by-w8gv.5.13 (player API) bead removes the two-player shortcut once this bead has made the fields private.
  • engine.rs's priority loop and mana.rs's payment are hot. Run just bench-count before and after and record both.
  • effect.rs's ask sites are being edited by the prompts milestone (by-w8gv.11): keep the diff there to the writes.

Parity and checks

Parity (plan K11) at the push: just check passes; the frozen save corpus replays (cargo test -p beryl_session --test corpus); rollouts_never_change holds (cargo test -p beryl_bench rollouts_never_change); slow CI's counts lane (just count-gate, bar 405,276,238 instructions, 1% tolerance, rust/crates/beryl_bench/count-gate.toml) passes with no bump to the bar. No behavior changes: no rules fix, no new card, no new mechanic. Work the card-names and file-length allowlists down as files are touched (data/card-names-allowlist.json, data/file-length-allowlist.json; an entry moves with its file and its count only falls; a new file is under 800 lines). Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules (AGENTS.md); keep the citations of code you move.

Acceptance criteria

State's players, turn, rng, profile, budgets, flags and outcome fields are private and State::player_mut is not callable outside beryl_kernel; every write to a Player or the Turn from beryl_engine (non-test and test) goes through a kernel mutator or the board door; Player and Turn have #[inline] getters for every field the rules give them; Player::sorceries_as_flash is reachable only through the legacy accessor; the handoff lists each mutator and getter with the field it replaces; just bench-count before and after is recorded on the bead; parity holds.

Handoff: staging e7f068a4.

State's players, turn, rng, profile, budgets, flags and outcome are pub(crate); State::player_mut is pub(crate) (player.rs). Player's and Turn's own fields stay pub to read for by-w8gv.5.9. Kernel files: player.rs (Player, ManaPool, getters, player mutators), turn.rs (new: Turn moved here, getters, turn and game-level mutators), legacy.rs, board.rs, zone.rs, mutate.rs, lib.rs (contract amended: two table rows, Events, Entities, The legacy accessor).

Mutators on State, with what each replaces in beryl_engine:

  • gain_life(player, amount) -> Event::LifeGain; lose_life -> Event::LifeLoss; damage_player(source, player, amount, combat) -> Event::Damage: Player::life (event.rs lose_life, gain_life, deal_damage).
  • add_mana(player, source, color, amount) -> Event::Mana (interpret/mod.rs add_mana, which notes it); spend_mana(player, color, amount) (mana.rs pay and manual_pay); empty_mana_pools() (turn.rs end_step): Player::mana.
  • count_land_played(player): Player::lands_played (engine.rs play_land); begin_turn zeroes it.
  • declare_mulligan(player), keep_hand(player, to_bottom), count_bottomed(player), count_mulligan(player): Player::mulligan, to_bottom, mulligans (start.rs).
  • add_emblem(player, card) -> Result: Player::emblems (effect.rs create_emblem; Board::add_emblem calls it).
  • Player::drew_from_empty is written by State::draw alone, already kernel code.
  • Legacy::set_sorceries_as_flash(player, on) via State::legacy_mut(): Player::sorceries_as_flash (effect.rs allow_sorceries_as_flash, turn.rs begin_turn).
  • set_starting_player(seat): Turn::starting_player and active (start.rs).
  • begin_turn() -> Seat (the new active player): Turn::active, number, Budgets::decisions, every Player::lands_played (turn.rs begin_turn; the turn-limit stall stays in the engine).
  • begin_step(step) -> Event::StepBegins: Turn::step, priority, passes (turn.rs begin_step).
  • give_priority(player): Turn::priority with passes = 0 (engine.rs play_land, cast.rs, face_down.rs, resolve.rs).
  • pass_priority() -> bool (all have passed): Turn::passes and priority (engine.rs pass). reset_passes(): Turn::passes = 0 alone (engine.rs twice, resolve.rs).
  • declare_attack(attackers) -> Event::Attack: Turn::attacked = true (combat.rs); State::end_combat now clears it.
  • set_cleanup_check(bool): Turn::cleanup_check (turn.rs finish_cleanup, engine.rs).
  • count_decision(): Budgets::decisions (engine.rs, effect.rs ask). count_state_based_check() -> u8 and reset_state_based_checks(): Budgets::state_based_iterations (sba.rs). budgets() reads them.
  • end_game(outcome): State::outcome (engine.rs stall, effect.rs stall, turn.rs, sba.rs end and its iteration stall). It overwrites: a capacity stall must replace a win (scratch.rs).
  • rebind_at_random(slots): State::rng.other (effect.rs put_on_bottom_in_random_order).
  • Existing, now #[inline]: player(), turn(), outcome(), profile(), rng().

Getters: Player::life, mana, lands_played, drew_from_empty, mulligans, mulligan, to_bottom, emblems (a slice), sorceries_as_flash; Turn::number, active, starting_player, step, priority, passes, cleanup_check, attacked (Turn's take self by value). Engine reads of the turn use them (s.turn().step()); engine reads of Player fields are untouched, for by-w8gv.5.9.

Board door additions (tests only): set_mana(player, ManaPool), set_lands_played, set_active, set_step, set_priority (which also zeroes passes). beryl_engine/src/tests use them and set_life.

Gotchas: (1) begin_step and declare_attack return the event; the engine settles it, so 'active' for the step's turn-based actions is read after the settle. (2) pass_priority and begin_turn hold the two-player alternation (Seat::opponent) inside the kernel, for by-w8gv.5.13. (3) data/file-length-allowlist.json: effect.rs, engine.rs and mana.rs lowered; cast.rs is exactly at its 1833.

A reviewer should check: that no behavior moved (corpus replay, rollouts_never_change and corpus_never_changes pass; count +0.019%); the merge with by-w8gv.5.4 in legacy.rs, state.rs, zone.rs and lib.rs; the CR quotations in turn.rs and player.rs (checked against yawgatog).
