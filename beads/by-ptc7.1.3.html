Description

Why

Compile time is the main obstacle to CI and to working speed, and it grows with the pool. by-ptc7.1.2 (a dependency of this bead) gives the per-crate numbers. Plan: Designs D.

What to do

Read just efficiency compile and the cargo llvm-lines rows in that bead's close reason. Take the three largest costs and reduce each. Candidates the plan already names, to confirm or discard from the numbers:

  • every crate that uses the engine instantiates the generic rules for itself. Non-generic entry points in beryl_rules for next and apply on Small and on Large would compile the engine once (see the L: Layout decision bead, which this bead must not contradict: read its close reason first if it has one);
  • clippy runs with every feature, so it type-checks every card's scenarios. Scenarios are three quarters of card compile cost (docs/engine-architecture.md, "Measurements behind the structure");
  • beryl_session and beryl_cli have many integration-test binaries, each linking the engine. One binary a crate with modules links once.

For each: the seconds before and after from the same command under the machine's normal load (docs/benchmarking.md says how to compare under load), and an entry in data/efficiency-changes.json with the metric it moves.

Never raise a budget (docs/checks.md). A cost you cannot reduce is described in the close reason with what would reduce it.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Three costs reduced, each with before and after seconds in the close reason and an entry in data/efficiency-changes.json. The fast check and slow CI pass.
