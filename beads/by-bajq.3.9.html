Description

What to build

Plan: Designs 3, "Prevention".

  1. Damage goes through the pipeline: damage::deal and combat damage build a proposal (source, recipient, amount, combat, and "can't be prevented"), gated on the kind.
  2. Protection (CR 702.16) from a quality: a keyword with a parameter. Derived has protection: Colors and nothing reads it. Build all four parts:
    • damage from a source with the quality is prevented (702.16e), a prevention effect in the pipeline;
    • it can't be the target of spells with the quality or abilities of sources with it (702.16b): in cast/targets.rs, and again as a spell or ability resolves (CR 608.2b);
    • it can't be enchanted or equipped by permanents with the quality, a state-based action (702.16c, 702.16d, CR 704.5m, 704.5n);
    • it can't be blocked by creatures with the quality (702.16f): in combat.rs. Start with colors as the quality; the type leaves room for others (a card type, a player, everything).
  3. A choice of a color as an ability resolves (choices, the Catalog shape). The five colors are one shared list of candidates: by-bajq.3.3 asks for a color as a permanent enters and may run at the same time as this bead, so whichever lands second uses the other's list.

Card

Mother of Runes: "{T}: Target creature you control gains protection from the color of your choice until end of turn."

Scenarios with Lightning Bolt: Bolt on the stack targeting a creature, Mother gives protection from red in response, Bolt has no legal target and does nothing; an Aura of the color falls off; a creature of the color can't block it; combat damage from it is prevented.

Files

The card's file, beryl_rules/src/{damage.rs, combat.rs, cast/targets.rs, sba.rs, replace.rs, layers/}, beryl_card/src/{characteristics.rs, continuous.rs}, beryl_kernel/src/object.rs.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate; R2 in the plan). The count is exact, so report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust). A change to what the WU mirror's rollouts do is a bug in this epic.
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (root AGENTS.md); just citations.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill: just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), every instruction carried out by its own scenarios, and just specs <card> passing the generic UI checks from both seats. A disagreement with an oracle is settled by the rules and recorded in data/oracle-divergences.json.
  • Read the card's rulings before writing its scenarios (just card brief <name> prints Scryfall's). Each statement in a ruling that a scenario can show is a scenario of the card. Where one cannot be shown yet, the close reason names the ruling and why.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry.
  • If the design in the plan is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
