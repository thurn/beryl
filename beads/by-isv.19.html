All three items reproduced from the code: chooseUpTo had max = cards.length (the engine asked searches with Ask::Plain, so the view had no limit); discard() gave an empty instruction for one card, which covers Thirst's basic-land discard and a one-card cleanup; and search instructions were the source's full rules text (Winds of Abandon's ran four lines). Fix: the engine now asks library searches with Ask::Search { max }, which beryl_session shows as AskView::Search; the picker caps picks at max and says 'Choose up to N.' The cleanup discard reads 'Down to seven.' with 'Discard n/count' on its button, and any other discard reads 'Discard a (highlighted) card.' Deviation: a second click on a max-1 picker lights the second card in place of the first, the overlay's existing one-click swap for every single pick, not a refusal. Two cards are still never lit at once, and Done sends one pick, which the ui-test checks. The empty-instruction check covers card pickers only. Yes/no asks (explore, pay life) keep a blank instruction because their buttons already say what they do. New boards and fixtures: choice-search, choice-thirst, choice-cleanup.
