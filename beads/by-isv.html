Description

UI defects found by four blind runs of the ui-review skill (.agents/ui-review/), beyond the project owner's own list of 22. The runs were two Sonnet reviewers and then two Opus reviewers, on the build pinned in the local branch ui-review-baseline, which predates the 2026-11-05 UI fixes. The reports, with seeds and screenshots, were in that session's scratchpad. Each bead below restates its own findings and repro.

Already landed on staging since that build, so re-check before fixing:

  • 867fff0 and b119167: the game log and paced opponent turns
  • 3bf9060: smart stops auto-pass your own spells and order triggers
  • 3da1898: browsable graveyard and exile piles
  • f66b4ec: attackers and blockers can be declared in any order
  • b9bac28 and f5564be: a single cast step, auto-pay with highlighted lands
  • 6c02a74: stack arrows routed clear of cards
  • a85df33: full trigger names, type lines and rules text
  • 548dd34: tapped cards turn whole; exiled cards shown beneath
  • 4685165: hovering moves nothing; battlefield rows centered by-ash (the lifted stack covers the opponent's piles) is related to the crowded-board bead.

Design decisions, binding on every bead:

  1. Follow the design philosophy in .agents/ui-review/SKILL.md:
    • the common case is one gesture;
    • the client does the bookkeeping;
    • ask once, in context;
    • Arena conventions first;
    • show, don't make them infer;
    • few words.
  2. Follow AGENTS.md: at least 50% fewer words on screen. No bead adds standing text to the table. What is needed at a glance is shown as icons, badges, numbers, colour or position. Anything else is disclosed on demand: hover, the inspector, a pile's browser, the game log. Example: a library's count belongs in its browser or on hover, with pile thickness giving the size at a glance, not as a number always on screen.
  3. Every acceptance criterion is checked by an agent: just ui-test checks, just ui-shots shots, Rust tests, or the ui-live probes. Nothing waits on a human.

Whole-run acceptance:

  • every child is closed;
  • just ci passes on staging;
  • the final ui-review bead has filed whatever it still finds.
