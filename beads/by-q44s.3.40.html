Lifted cards jump when an untimed step changes a look

Close reason

Shipped 74dc5a05 to staging. Library choices no longer jump and all 8,220 Beryl 3D interface tests pass.

Description

Beryl 3D holds the cards the opponent is looking at off their library (beryl_scene src/table/lifted.rs, Look and lay). The session often begins, changes or ends a look with no lift step: only the views' handling field changes, for example as a dig takes cards (count 5, then 3, then 2) or a Jace +2 ends. Look::opening then switches to the look the next view shows at the script's first step that takes time, or at its first step when none does. When that step, or the set library step that reorders the library during a look, takes no time, the lifted cards move to their new places in zero time and no_jumps reports a jump.

Seen in just b3d test: combat-attackers/opponent (+2 no-jumps), jace-the-mind-sculptor/bounced/opponent (+1, a 100 mm jump at step:6.0.0 point), stock-up/takes-two-of-five-and-orders-the-rest-on-the-bottom*/opponent (+1 or +2, a 118 mm jump at step:5.0.0 set). Their entries in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json were raised for this bead.

Possible fixes: the session gives a step that changes PlayerView::handling the lift timing (rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/script/timing.rs, a lane:timing change), or emits lift steps for those looks; or the client slides lifted cards over a time the step's StepTiming supplies. beryl_scene must not choose a duration itself (epic rule 7b).

Acceptance criteria

The three recordings' no-jumps entries in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json are back to what they were before by-q44s.3.12 (combat-attackers/opponent 215, jace-the-mind-sculptor/bounced/opponent 12, stock-up/... 6 or 7), and just b3d test passes.
