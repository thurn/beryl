Description

What to build

Plan: Designs I. CR 717 (Class cards) and 720 (Case cards). Both are single-faced enchantments whose later abilities exist only once something has happened to the permanent.

  • Class: Ability::level(n, cost). "{cost}: Level N" is an activated ability, only as a sorcery and only at level N minus one, that makes the Class level N (CR 717.4 and after). The level is state of the permanent, kept as a memo or a counter as the rule says (cite it; a class level is not a counter). An ability printed under a level bar exists while the Class is that level or greater; "when this Class becomes level N" is a triggered ability.
  • Case: "To solve — [condition]" is a triggered ability at the beginning of its controller's end step that makes the Case solved if the condition holds (CR 720.3 and after); "Solved — [ability]" exists while it is solved. Solved is a designation of the permanent, kept as a memo.
  • Both need "an ability that exists while a condition on this permanent holds", which static abilities with a Condition already are for continuous effects: check that a triggered or activated ability can be conditional the same way in the layers (LoseAbility and Grant are layer 6), or build the gate.
  • The renderer prints level bars and the two Case keywords.
  • Wizard Class: no maximum hand size (the hand-size rule lives in Format after family G's bead; a rule change on a player reads it: if that bead has not landed, read MAX_HAND_SIZE and leave a note in this bead); level 2, when it becomes level 2 draw two cards; level 3, whenever you draw a card put a +1/+1 counter on target creature you control.
  • Case of the Crimson Pulse: when it enters, discard a card, then draw two; to solve, you have no cards in hand; solved, at the beginning of your upkeep discard your hand, then draw two cards.

Write each card with the card-writing skill. Read its rulings first (just card rulings <name>): the card joins data/rulings-ledger.json with every ruling a scenario or a reason, and joins the keystone fuzz pool (beryl_cards/src/decks.rs). Where XMage or Forge cannot play the card, its scenarios are reported as unsupported and the ledger is the outside check, so be thorough with it.

Scenarios: level up twice over two turns, each ability appearing; level 2 cannot be activated at instant speed or twice; a copy of a level 2 Class is level 1; the Case solves at an end step with an empty hand and not with one card; a solved Case that leaves and returns is unsolved.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Both cards pass just card check and are in the ledger and the pool.
