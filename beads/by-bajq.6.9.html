Close reason

Spell copies appear on and vanish from the stack in scripts; Fork and Brain Freeze script checks pass; staging 0cc03949a

Description

Symptom: beryl_cli script-check (the fast check's script lane) fails 24 spec moments on staging since 3cfaa15b4 (feat: spell copies and storm): 15 of brain-freeze and 9 of fork, from both seats. Each panics in beryl_session/src/script/build.rs verify with a rule-invariant failure such as: streak #512 > opponent damage 3 <- #512: its cause #512 is not on the table as it runs, and did not leave it in this beat. Reproduce: cd rust && cargo build -p beryl_cli && ./target/debug/beryl_cli script-check ../ui/fixtures.json fork (or brain-freeze).

Why: a copy of a spell (slots 512 and up, e.g. Fork's copy of Lightning Bolt or Braingeyser, Brain Freeze's storm copies) is created on the stack and resolves, and its effects' steps name it as their cause, but no step of the script puts the copy on the table (no appear onto the stack) or takes it off (vanish), so check.rs rule finds the cause absent. The script needs the copy's arrival on the stack and its departure as steps (see script/mod.rs 'Cause' and 'Reset', build.rs/moves.rs/stack.rs for how a spell's flight to the stack and its resolution are scripted, and Heard/Names in hear.rs for how the stack is followed). Arena shows a copy appearing on the stack beside the original; look it up with the arena-video skill before choosing the verb.

This blocks every push whose fast check runs the script lane (any change to beryl_session, beryl_cli or a card crate). by-q44s.1.25 is waiting on it.
