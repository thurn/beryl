Description

Milestone 4 of Project 50k (plan K4, epic by-w8gv.7). Memos are what an object remembers about how it was cast or entered; they clear on a zone change. This bead builds the Memos store, the first bead to do so, and moves onto it what a permanent remembers of its spell: that it was kicked, that its offspring, impending or evoke cost was paid, and the value of X.

Read first

  • docs/project-50k.md: decisions K4, K6, K11, and "State" and "Kernel rules" under Target architecture.
  • rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs: the kernel's contract. Its "Stores" section (written by by-w8gv.7.2) is the design this bead follows: kinds, shapes, capacities and byte allotments, expiry, indexing, capability bits, and what a store implements once. The same numbers are in a comment on by-w8gv.7.2, and enact brief shows that bead's handoff. Where the code proves the contract wrong, change its text in the same push and say so on the bead.
  • rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/legacy.rs: the table of what M4 deletes. Delete each Legacy method this bead replaces; the struct itself goes in by-w8gv.7.10.

Need not read: cast.rs beyond the lines a grep for the names below finds, mana.rs, combat.rs, the Effects store's internals (its file shows the pattern a store follows), ui/.

Where it is (staging 08d1a377)

  • kernel: ObjectFlags::KICKED, OFFSPRING, IMPENDING, EVOKED and the LEGACY mask (object.rs); Legacy::set_object_flags (it marks the layers stale for IMPENDING) and Legacy::remember_x; the entered_x queue (state.rs, 8 entries) with its lines in layout.rs, new.rs, relayout.rs and fit.rs; State::x_of; Board::set_impending (board.rs); the zone move, which clears every flag but TOKEN and ONE_ONE with one mask (zone.rs move_object), and cease_to_exist; State::signature (zone.rs), which compares whole Objects to tell whether two are interchangeable.
  • engine: Entry::flags and Entry::x, arrive and the remember_x call in move_batch (event.rs); resolve.rs, which turns the casting options a spell paid into flags; entered_with and kicked (the end of effect.rs) and Scene::paid (interpret/scene.rs), the two readers for an enters ability's "if it was kicked"; trigger.rs's x_of; layers.rs, where StaticCondition::Impending reads IMPENDING for every permanent with that ability, on the counted path.
  • elsewhere: beryl_session/src/checkpoint.rs (Permanent::impending: the save's field name stays), beryl_search/src/expert/mod.rs and frozen/expert/mod.rs (flags().has(ObjectFlags::IMPENDING)).

Scope

  1. The Memos store as the contract shapes it, with what a store implements once (empty value, copy, relayout, fit, invariant, save description, observation, equality and signature) in its own file.
  2. The four cost-paid marks and X become memos of the permanent, written as it enters (State::arrive's Arrival, or a mutator beside it, so what a permanent enters with is still one change) and cleared by the kernel when it changes zones. The mutator that sets a memo the layers read marks them stale itself.
  3. Readers ask a query by kind. entered_with's rule stays: once the permanent has left, the answer is what it was as the ability triggered (CR 603.4, cited there).
  4. If the contract makes StackEntry::flashback (beryl_kernel/src/stack.rs: "the flashback cost was paid", CR 702.34a) a memo of the spell, move it here: cast.rs sets it where it builds the StackEntry and cast::exiled_instead reads it. If the contract moves ObjectFlags::ONE_ONE (a token copy's 1/1 exception, CR 707.9b; mutate.rs sets it, object.rs has its getter, zone.rs keeps it across moves, checkpoint.rs saves one_one), move that too; if it says the flag is the object's copiable values and stays, leave it.
  5. With no card in the game that can make a memo of a kind, the zone move and the layers pay one AND for it (a GameTable capability bit): overlord_of_the_mistmoors.rs and aangs_iceberg.rs have impending, and the WU mirror has neither, so the counted games show the absent cost.

Gotchas

  • Two permanents that differ only in a memo are not interchangeable (§7.2 of docs/engine-architecture.md): signature must still say so once the flags leave Object.
  • X is kept only when nonzero and only while the permanent stays; x_of answers 0 otherwise.
  • Scenarios: cargo test -p beryl_cards (kicker, offspring, evoke and impending cards each have them; grep -rln 'Kicker\|Offspring\|Impending\|Evoke' rust/crates/beryl_cards/src), and just oracle on the card files you touch.

Acceptance criteria

The Memos store exists as the contract shapes it, with its empty value, copy, relayout, fit, invariant, save description, observation and signature written once in its own file. ObjectFlags::KICKED, OFFSPRING, IMPENDING and EVOKED, the entered_x queue, Legacy::set_object_flags' use for them and Legacy::remember_x are gone: each is a memo written as the permanent enters and cleared by the kernel's zone move, read through a query by kind. StackEntry::flashback and ObjectFlags::ONE_ONE are moved or left as the contract says, and the bead records which. With no memo-making card in the game the zone move and the layers pay one capability-bit AND. Kernel tests cover a memo clearing on a zone change, signature telling two permanents apart by a memo, and relayout; the kicker, offspring, evoke and impending scenarios pass; just bench-count before and after and the state size are recorded on the bead; parity holds with no bump to the count gate.

Numbers and decisions (by-w8gv.7.6).

COUNT (just count-gate, rollouts subject; the gate's VM, not the full just bench-count): at staging 13fe40fb 398,897,988 before -> 392,427,771 after (-1.62%). Rebased onto by-w8gv.7.4 (6b253808): 396,070,902 before -> 389,343,295 after (-1.70%). No bump; the bar is lowered to 389,343,295, as count-gate.toml asks for a drop past the tolerance. The WU decks hold one memo-making card (Subtlety, evoke), so the kind is live in few states; the gain is one write fewer per arrival and a smaller per-object record in event::move_batch (Entry.flags u32 -> CastingKinds u8).

SIZE: size_of::<State<Small>>() 20,040 -> 20,088 on staging 13fe40fb (+48 of the +88 allotted: Memos 100 bytes, entry 3, less entered_x 50); 19,880 after the rebase onto by-w8gv.7.4 (19,832 + 48).

SHAPE: Memos<L> = queue of (slot, Memo) x 32 + a u16 kinds mask. Memo::Paid(CastingKinds) is ONE entry per permanent holding the set of kinds of cost paid (new beryl_card::CastingKinds, a bit per CastingKind); Memo::X(u8) only when nonzero. The kinds mask has a bit for X and one per CastingKind, so State::paid(slot, kind) and State::x_of(slot) stop at one AND with nothing of the kind live. An entry names a slot only: the kernel's move ends memos eagerly (State::forget_stored), so no incarnation is kept.

DEVIATIONS from the bead text, all following by-w8gv.7.2's contract:

  • No GameTable capability bit for memos: the zone move pays the keyed-slots bit test it already paid, and the layers' read of impending is one AND of the store's kinds mask (contract, 'Indexing').
  • StackEntry::flashback: not this bead's; by-w8gv.7.4 moved it (landed, Effects Payload::Move). ObjectFlags::ONE_ONE stays an object flag (copiable values, CR 707.9b).
  • Written by a mutator beside State::arrive (State::remember), not a field of Arrival: by-w8gv.7.7's memos are written at other moments and need the same mutator. beryl_engine's event::arrive calls both, so what a permanent enters with is still one step, before the ZoneChange event and trigger matching.
  • Board::set_impending became Board::remember(slot, Memo); the save's field name (Permanent::impending) is unchanged.
  • Store::lingering is None for these memos: the checkpoint already saves impending with the permanent, and kicked/offspring/evoked/X were never saved (their readers are enters abilities, off the stack by a turn's start). No save format change; the corpus replays.
  • entered_with moved from effect.rs to trigger.rs so effect.rs (file-length list) shrinks: 1170 -> 1159.

CHECKS run: cargo test --workspace (corpus_never_changes and rollouts_never_change unchanged, session corpus replays), clippy -D warnings, just citations (0 problems), just fuzz 300 with debug assertions, just exhaust for Overlord of the Mistmoors, Thieving Skydiver and Subtlety.
