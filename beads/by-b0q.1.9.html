Description

Part of Milestone 1 (epic by-b0q): a rules-exact engine, game UI and AI opponents for a mirror match of the 40-card WU Control main deck in docs/sample-decks/2026-10-17-PrinceJools-wu-control.txt (29 distinct cards). Read the epic's description (plan, card table) and design field (decisions) first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md).

Implement these cards in beryl_cards (epic card table): Mana Tithe, No More Lies, Mana Drain, Oust, Get Lost with the Map token (explore, CR 701.44), Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs, Time Spiral, Timetwister, Strip Mine, Mana Crypt, Seachrome Coast, Spara's Headquarters. Plains, Island and Tundra exist.

Edge cases for scenarios: Mana Tithe and No More Lies when the controller can and cannot pay, and No More Lies' exile; Mana Drain's mana at the caster's next main phase; Oust on a library of zero or one card; Get Lost on a planeswalker; Sink into Stupor on a spell and on a permanent, and Soporific Springs with and without the life; Time Spiral untapping chosen lands and exiling itself; the wheels with empty graveyards; Strip Mine on a basic; Mana Crypt's coin flip both ways; Seachrome Coast with two and three other lands; cycling Spara's Headquarters.

Acceptance criteria

  • Each card listed has one file under its color directory with its CardDef, bodies and scenarios; every ability is exercised by at least one scenario, including the edge cases named; every body cites the CR.
  • All scenarios pass under the strict runner.
  • Fuzz games including these cards raise no invariant (debug, at least 1,000 games on demand; the just ci count still passes).

Derek Thurn2026-11-05

From Warden by-b0q.1.13: beryl_engine's Face has no name; only CardDef.name (the front face, the CardTable key). A back face up (Jace, Telepath Unbound; Soporific Springs) has only that face's characteristics, name included (CR 712.8e). The legend rule (sba.rs legend_group) compares t.def(card).name, and the PlayerView will need the face-up name too. When adding the second face, give Face a name and have the legend rule compare face names.

D
Derek Thurn2026-11-05

From the by-b0q.1.18 warden review, two gaps this bead will hit: (1) EffectCtx::create_tokens always creates under the ability's controller, but Get Lost's Maps go to the destroyed permanent's controller, so add a player parameter or a create_tokens_for (event::create_tokens already takes one). (2) Playing an MDFC's back face as a land is unsupported: play_land and the PlayLand filter read face 0 (Sink into Stupor, an instant), so Soporific Springs is never offered and its pay-life replacement (looked up on o.face) is never asked. Playing it needs face 1 through Entry.face and life_to_enter.
