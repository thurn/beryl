Description

Blood Artist, 0/1: "Whenever Blood Artist or another creature dies, target player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life."

Why

The look-back match (trigger::match_look_back, by-bajq.1.2) is proved only by test definitions: Test Mourner in rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/tests/triggers.rs ("whenever a creature dies", which sees itself and others die in one event) . No card of the epic says "whenever a creature dies", so nothing checks this path against XMage and Forge.

Scenarios

  • A creature of either player dies: one trigger, and its target loses 1 and Blood Artist's controller gains 1.
  • A sweeper kills Blood Artist and two other creatures at once: three triggers, since each permanent of the batch is matched before any moves (CR 603.10a).
  • A creature exiled or returned to a hand does not trigger it.
  • A token creature dying triggers it.

Follow the card-writing skill: just card brief <name>, just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), scenarios for each instruction and each ruling a scenario can show, and just specs <card> from both seats. Epic bars hold: just count-gate with no bump (report the count before and after), State<Small> within 20,480 bytes, just test-rust (frozen saves, rollouts_never_change).

Acceptance criteria

Blood Artist's scenarios pass on Beryl, XMage and Forge, including three creatures dying in one event giving three triggers.
