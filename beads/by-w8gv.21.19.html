Description

Each verb is held to Arena's numbers within a tolerance (plan K27, Motion oracle): durations, delays and stagger with no browser; path and peak size from a capture taken in stepped stage time.

Read first: the epic by-w8gv.21 (the user's decisions, what was agreed with the CI track, the code map); docs/project-50k.md, Target architecture, Presentation (K22 to K31); docs/ui-conventions.md (generated from data/ui-conventions.json: edit the data). Then by-w8gv.21.17's, by-w8gv.21.18's and by-w8gv.21.14's handoffs, and the findings on by-w8gv.21.3 (stepping the stage clock inside one page.evaluate).

The values check

A pytest in the python lane (tools/src/beryl_tools/checks.py): each verb's duration, delay and stagger in data/ui-conventions.json against data/arena-motion.json. Default tolerance: Arena's measured range across instances, widened by one frame of the recording or 15% of the median, whichever is larger. The tolerance lives in the data and a verb may override it with a reason. Exempt verbs (a numbered exception) are skipped and listed in the output.

The capture check

  • A script in ui/scripts that opens a verb's case under ?stage (the cases of ui/scripts/verbs.mjs), holds the stage clock, steps it in ticks of a sixtieth of a second, screenshots every tick and writes the frames with their stage times to the cache.
  • by video measure reads the folder, and the path (normalized) and peak size are compared with Arena's within the same kind of tolerance. Both sides go through one tool: that is the point of K27.
  • It measures stage time, never wall time, so machine load cannot change a result.
  • It runs when ui/src/stage or the values in the data change (select by changed files in checks.py) and nightly (tools/src/beryl_tools/nightly.py), not in every fast check. It logs one record per verb to the run log as motion:<verb>/<layout>.

A verb that cannot be brought in

After three attempts, a waiver in the data naming a bead keeps the check passing; the measured value and cause go on the epic and the follow-up bead (epic by-w8gv.21, Acceptance). Never tune a verb to one clip.

Not to read

The script builder; the stage's internals beyond window.berylStage.

Parity and policy

Parity at the push (plan K11): just check passes; the frozen save corpus replays (cargo test -p beryl_session --test corpus); rollouts_never_change holds (rust/crates/beryl_bench/src/rollout.rs); slow CI's counts lane passes with no bump (405,276,238). Nothing waits on the user (K31): where his eye is wanted, proceed on Arena or the nearest existing verb and file a bead labelled human that nothing depends on. Record decisions and deviations as comments on this bead. A bar missed after three attempts is recorded on the epic with its measured value and cause, a follow-up is filed and the work goes on.

Acceptance criteria

A pytest fails when a verb's duration, delay or stagger is moved out of tolerance and passes on the tree; the stepped capture measures path and peak size for the slice's verbs through by video measure and compares them with Arena's; it runs on changes to the stage or the values and nightly, logged per verb; tolerances, exemptions and waivers live in the data; results do not change with tick size or load; just check passes.

Decisions and deviations. (1) Path, normalized, is held as what Arena's data can say: whether the step's subject travels (Arena's curve 'none' = in place) and how it eases, read as progress along its path at half its time (ease-out past 0.55, ease-in under 0.45, else even). Arena has no numeric path, only words and curves, so no arc height is compared. Calibrated on synthetic clips: an uneased flight reads 0.49 to 0.50, CSS ease-out 0.61 to 0.67, ease-in 0.31 to 0.40, growing or shrinking (tests/test_motion.py). (2) Size: peak and a new 'start' (size where the motion begins over size at rest); emerge's seven starts come from 21.18's notes (by eye, 0.19 to 0.30). (3) by video measure gained 'landing' (follow only the mover that comes to rest at the subject's place: a token's neighbour slides aside as Arena's does) and keeps light frames that are a whole card clear of the crop's edge (a growing copy was trimmed, reading 0.41 for a true 0.30); Arena's two tracker instances give the same numbers as before. (4) Screenshots disable CSS animations: the time counter's glow pulses on wall time, which made peaks vary between runs; with it off, repeated runs give identical numbers and verdicts, and a 33 ms tick gives the same verdicts as 16.7 ms.
