b3d: incoming combat damage as the web's orb badge

  • Description
  • Acceptance criteria
  • Changelog

Description

Symptom: during combat, the damage unblocked attackers will deal a player (hud::players::incoming_text, e.g. "−2") is drawn as bare text right of the life orb, on top of that player's step toggles (blockers, main 2). The web draws it as a badge on the orb's rim toward the table: .life-incoming in ui/src/screens/styles/table.css (left: 50% + 30px, top 6px, 82px for the opponent; dark pill, red inset ring, 700 20px sans, #ff9a7a; .life-incoming-lethal solid red). Seen with just b3d compare adeline-resplendent-cathar/adelines-human-attacks-a-planeswalker-ask-3 --at 0ms --scale 2.

The text-overlap interface check (beryl_scene/src/agent/text_overlap.rs) reports it: "text of text "2 combat damage" overlaps toggle "Opponent's blockers"" in about 290 interface tests, listed in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json under text-overlap.

Change: place and style the incoming element as the web's badge (hud/players.rs incoming_text, data::space::hud::INCOMING_GAP), then run just b3d test --update to lower the listed text-overlap counts, and compare with just b3d compare ... --scale 2.

Acceptance criteria

The incoming damage draws as the web's badge at the orb's rim; just b3d test has no text-overlap fault naming a combat damage element; a compare picture at scale 2 is named on the bead.
