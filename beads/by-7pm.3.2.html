Phone opening uses the viewport with touch inspection, bottom slots, and opponent wait. Ten phone and four desktop opening shots, all other phone shots, touch checks at both sizes, typecheck, 194 UI tests and Enact fast checks passed. Delivered to staging as 28556df3.

Description

Give the opening a phone layout. Today, on a phone, Opening is drawn inside the desktop's scaled <Stage>: seven cards about 48px wide across the middle, and "Keep 6" and "Mulligan" at 68×13. The stage does not hold these prompts: OPENING in ui/src/stage/prompt.ts leaves the purposes play-or-draw and mulligan to this screen.

Where the code is

  • ui/src/screens/Opening.tsx (about 255 lines): Opening({ view, decision, onPick, onPlan }). It draws the coin (Coin from ui/src/components/Player.tsx) with "On the play" or "On the draw", the title, the hand as a fan of Cards, the slots for cards going to the bottom, and its buttons in a Dock. FLIP_MS = 1500 is how long the coin spins when the opponent wins the flip. While the opponent decides, a mulliganed hand lies face down (CardBack).
  • ui/src/screens/Game.tsx, the end of GameScreen: {opening && <Opening … />} inside the overlays fragment, which the phone branch wraps in <Stage>. by-7pm.3.1 left that wrapper for this bead to remove.
  • ui/src/stage/PhoneChrome.tsx: Inspected shows a card whole on a phone.
  • usePhone() from ui/src/mobile/phone.ts; phone styles in ui/src/mobile/phone.css under body.phone.

What to build

Keep Opening's state and logic (the picks, onPick, onPlan, the coin's timing). Branch its markup and styles on usePhone(). Every label and title is the desktop's, unchanged (D6). On a phone:

  1. It is drawn outside <Stage>, over the phone board, with a 60% black scrim, inside the safe areas.
  2. Coin: 64px across (56px when the viewport is under 393 wide), centered, its center 88px below the top safe area, its one label under it at 13px.
  3. Title: the desktop's, at 18px in the title face, centered under the coin's label.
  4. Hand, face up: two rows, four cards then the rest, each card 90px wide (86px when the viewport is under 393 wide), 8px between cards and between rows, the rows centered across, the block centered in the space between the title and the buttons. No fan, no tilt. A hand of fewer than five cards is one row.
  5. Keep or mulligan: a touch on a card shows it whole (Inspected), and a touch beside it puts it away, as on the table.
  6. Cards to the bottom: under the hand rows, 16px below, a row of as many dashed slots as cards must go, each 72px wide (80% of a card). A touch on a hand card sends it to the next empty slot; a touch on a card in a slot returns it. This is what the desktop's click does: reuse its handlers. A touch does not show a card whole in this step (the player read the hand at the decision before).
  7. Buttons: full width, 48px high, 8px apart, pinned above the bottom safe area, the primary one lowest, the desktop's labels and enabled states (the confirm for the bottom is enabled at the exact count).
  8. While the opponent decides: the hand face down in the same rows, no buttons, and the thinking mark (Thinking from ui/src/components/Player.tsx) where the buttons were.
  9. Remove the phone branch's <Stage> wrapper in Game.tsx: nothing on a phone uses ui/src/game/Stage.tsx after this bead. Add a dev-only guard so it stays that way: in Stage.tsx, if (import.meta.env.DEV && isPhone()) console.error("the desktop stage was drawn on a phone"). A page error fails every shot (ui/scripts/shots.mjs), so every phone shot then checks it.

Fit, to check your arithmetic against: at 375×647 with seven cards and two slots, 88 + 28 (coin) + 13 + 8 + 18 + 16 (title) + 120 + 8 + 120 (rows) + 16 + 101 (slots) + 16 + 104 (two buttons) = 656, which is 9px over. Shrink the cards, not the buttons or the text, until it fits: compute the card width as the lesser of the value in item 4 and what the height left allows.
