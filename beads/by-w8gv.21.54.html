Description

Nothing depends on this. by-w8gv.21.22 draws the arrows and lines on the stage (?stage). Two of them are yours to look at; both ship as built.

A. The line from an ability on the stack to its source, as UI Conventions settles it ("A line from an ability to its source"): always shown while the ability is on the stack, thin and faint as Arena draws it, hidden while a prompt is open.

Where it is drawn: ui/src/stage/lines.ts, shapeOf, the branch for arrow === "source" (the arc, grown out of the source's end), with its look in ui/src/stage/stage.css, .stage-line-source (pale gold, 1.5 px, 60% opacity). Its timing is data/ui-conventions.json, verbs, point, cases "source line" and "source line end".

How to see it:

  • just ui-shots stage-arrow-ability phone-stage-arrow-ability prints two stills: the opponent's planeswalker ability on the stack, the thin line from it back to the planeswalker, and its green arrow onto its target.
  • just motion --film point "source line" films it as an ability lifts: the line appears as the ability clears its source and stretches with it.
  • Or open /?stage&fixture=arrow-ability#/game after just ui-shots has recorded fixtures.

What to judge:

  1. Faintness and weight: is 1.5 px at 60% right against a 3.5 px target arrow, or too easy to miss, or still too much (your by-afh and by-110h asked for no heavy arrow on triggers)?
  2. Color: Beryl's pale gold, as the lines of combat. Arena's is pale green.
  3. Shape: a bowed arc with no head. It crosses whatever lies between (on a phone, the row between the stack and the source).
  4. Whether an enters trigger should have it at all, or only abilities that stay on the stack long enough to be read.
  5. Hidden during any prompt but priority. Arena keeps its line during a target prompt (IEXcjBn3BXk 23:13.15).

B. The line from an attacker to the player. Arena draws none, only the attacker's chevron. Beryl draws a thin line from each unblocked attacker to what it attacks whenever the defender controls a planeswalker, the player included (your by-922, by-m0wf, by-986a), and no numbered exception in UI Conventions lists it. The independent motion review of point (rounds 2 and 3, comments on by-w8gv.21.22) asked for it to be dropped or listed. just ui-shots stage-attack-lines shows three on the desktop, under the cards. On a phone they run under a blocker on their way to the life total and are read as its block line (by-w8gv.21.53 is to fix that). Say whether the line to the player stays (it then becomes an exception) or only a line to a planeswalker is drawn (ui/src/stage/lines.ts straight; which lines exist is rust/crates/beryl_session/src/script/spans.rs combat).
