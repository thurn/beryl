Names

  • CHAMPION: the family on the Champion: line of docs/ladder.md.
  • CHALLENGER: CHAMPION with the knowledge fitted in place of tuned added to its options. Options are joined by + in the fixed order the family parser prints in its error message (rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs, impl FromStr for Family).
  • If CHAMPION already has the knowledge fitted in place of tuned, close this bead with the reason already in the champion.

Steps

  1. Audit check. If docs/ladder.md shows three or more pass rows since its last audit row, or a pass row since an audit row that is seven or more days old, run docs/ai-plan.md §4.5 first and add its row.
  2. The policy alone. just blunders POLICY --seeds 1 for the policy with tuned values and with fitted values. Paste both. An entry the fitted policy newly gets wrong is a blunder the fit introduced: name it.
  3. The suite. just gate CHALLENGER --claims none. Paste the table and the totals.
  4. The match. In the background: just ladder CHALLENGER --work 3M --bar stronger --positions D:\beryl-cache\ladder\positions --cap 2000. Paste the whole report with the hours.
  5. Verdict. Promote if the match accepts the bar and the suite total has not fallen by more than 20 of 400. Blunders weigh more than strength: a fitted policy that is stronger and opens an entry is not promoted until the number behind that entry is put back and the suite run again.
  6. Write the finding in docs/ai-plan.md §9 and §10 in the same push: did a policy fitted to the search make a stronger agent, and by how much.
  7. Close with the verdict, the suite totals and the score.
