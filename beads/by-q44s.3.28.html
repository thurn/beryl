Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Motion the pointer drives' (all six points) and 'The test driver' (drag); the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/pointer (from by-q44s.3.27), src/client.rs (Client::press, commands, SessionCommand::Click), src/driver/mod.rs (Page::drag: a down, eight moves with a 16 ms step after each, and an up), src/driver/recording.rs (Responses::answer). Web: ui/src/stage/pointer.ts (Pointer, Held, Holding, DRAG_THRESHOLD, heldPose; PointerClient: holdable says which cards a press may pick up, click is a card click, drop is a held card let go and returns whether the drop was taken; Drop), ui/src/stage/heft.ts (Heft, Sample, Look, drawn: the lean toward the way the card is moved and its settling flat; MAX_GAP), ui/src/stage/prompt.ts (Prompts: its holdable is #lifts(name), a click sends { kind: "on", on }, and #dropped sends { kind: "drop", on, pile, at } when the layout says the card landed in a pile and { kind: "on", on } otherwise). Protocol: Click::On and Click::Drop in rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/prompt/view.rs, and the marks of PromptView.

Build:

  • Which cards may be picked up: a card in the viewer's hand that the open prompt marks eligible (the prompt's marks), as #lifts decides. Read #lifts and port its rule exactly; it reads only the prompt's state.
  • A press on such a card that moves more than DRAG_THRESHOLD (copy it as pointer.drag_threshold_points) holds it. A held card's position is the point under the pointer on a plane held_height above the table: a function of the stored pointer position alone. Its resolved element has the HELD bit, and the checks on cards at rest skip it (agent::checks and agent::steps: add the skip where MOVING is skipped).
  • The lean: a damped spring driven by the pointer's speed, advanced in frame in steps of 1/240 second from the previous frame's now to this one's. The speed is the distance the pointer moved between the two frames divided by the time between them, and a gap longer than max_gap_ms counts as max_gap_ms. The spring's state is the only animation state kept between frames. Port Heft's update, with the values by-q44s.3.27 put in space.json.
  • Letting go: over the battlefield or the stack, or anywhere outside the hand, the client queues SessionCommand::Click with Click::On { on } for the card, as the web's #dropped does when no pile took it; a release without a hold is a click and queues the same. Click::Drop is sent only for a card let go over a pile of a tray, which milestone 3's prompts add. The card gets a segment from its held pose to its resting place (fall_ms). If the session's reply moves the card, that script's flight starts from the held pose instead.
  • A cancelled pointer, or a release while no prompt is open, returns the card and sends nothing.

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/pointer_held.rs: the same drag of 300 logical points, stepped at 60 and at 120 frames a second, ends with leans within 0.5 degrees of each other and within 0.05 degrees after 500 ms of holding still; a held card has the held state and follows the pointer exactly at every frame; a drag of a castable hand card onto the battlefield queues Click::On for it, with the prompt's decision and revision, which is the command Responses::answer finds a recorded reply for in pay-default or in the recording named in the comment; a card that is not eligible cannot be picked up; a frame gap of 2 seconds moves the lean no more than a gap of max_gap_ms; the reply's flight starts at the held pose with no jump (no_jumps finds nothing); a frame while holding allocates nothing.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d tree <the recording> --at prompt:1 --do 'drag card "<a castable card>" to card "<a land>"'; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

The tests pass; a comment names a recording whose first prompt lets a hand card be cast and gives the just b3d diff of dragging it onto the battlefield, which shows the card on the stack; just b3d check on it reports no no-jumps fault.
