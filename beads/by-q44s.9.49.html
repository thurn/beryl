Close reason

phone catalog lists no longer drawn under phase icon or counts, 0bb602ce8

Description

Symptom: on Beryl 3D's phone layout, a prompt with a long list of choice buttons (a color choice such as Painter's Servant's or Lion's Eye Diamond's, Crystal Spray's and Mind Bend's word choices, Pithing Needle's card name) reaches into the middle band. There the phase pill (region "phase") and the opponent's lands count label (text "Opponent's lands right N") are drawn over the buttons, so part of a button is hidden though it still takes presses.

Why: screens::game::game_phone (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/screens/game.rs) puts the prompt before the furniture (hud::phone::middle), and the flat layer draws in tree order. Putting the prompt after the furniture is not the fix: each phone prompt carries its own full-window shade, which would then dim the Menu button. The table's lands label is drawn by the table's labels, not by this holder.

The "reachable" interface check now finds these (by-q44s.9.37 added the rule that no painted element may be drawn over an interactive element's centre, hit::painted_over in beryl_scene/src/agent/hit.rs). Run just b3d test --phone-only and look for faults that say "is drawn under": 27 faults over 25 tests, all at 375x647 or 430x932, for example b3d:painters-servant/the-chosen-color-is-added-to-every-card-and-permanent-0/you/375x647 and b3d:mother-of-runes/bounced/you/430x932. Compare with the web's phone layout (ui/src/stage/phone.ts, just ui-shots on a phone shot of the same fixture) for where its choice list stops, and lay the list out so it fits above the middle band or scrolls, or draw it above the pill without its shade covering the Menu.

Files: beryl_scene/src/screens/game.rs, beryl_scene/src/prompts/bar.rs (the phone dock), beryl_scene/src/hud/phone.rs.

Acceptance criteria

just b3d test --phone-only reports no fault that says 'is drawn under'; a picture of one of the named tests beside the web's phone shot of the same moment is compared and both paths named on the bead.
