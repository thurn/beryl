Description

Fork's 2004-10-04 ruling bed98cd27789 says the new target of the copy of a counterspell can be the resolving Fork. In the scenario trial, P0 casts Lightning Bolt, P1 casts Counterspell on it, then P0 casts Fork targeting Counterspell. Resolving Fork offers Lightning Bolt and Counterspell as new targets but omits Fork. Copying Counterspell and choosing Fork should put a copy on the stack that then fails to resolve, because the original Fork has finished resolving and left the stack.

Read rust/cards/cards_1993/lea/fork.rs, rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/actions/copy.rs and rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/resolve.rs. Check CR 707.10c and the lifetime of a resolving spell on the stack during replay. Keep the count gate unchanged and check that resolving spell placement preserves the existing copy and target tests.

Acceptance criteria

A Fork scenario copies Counterspell, chooses Fork itself, then verifies the copy has no legal target on resolution and the original Counterspell still counters Lightning Bolt. Beryl and both oracles pass or any oracle fault is recorded. Update the ruling in data/rulings-ledger.json and document the regression guard in docs/bugs.md.
