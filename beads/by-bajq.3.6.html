Description

Flickerwisp, 3/1 flying: "When this creature enters, exile another target permanent. Return that card to the battlefield under its owner's control at the beginning of the next end step."

Reopened 2026-11-13: the first attempt escalated the card (commit 4d906d0e) because Effect::Delay carried only a number and nothing returned a card from exile. by-bajq.3.14 builds both; this bead runs after it. Write the card with them, and remove Flickerwisp's entry from data/card-escalations.json in the same commit.

Follow the card-writing skill. Flickerwisp has no flash, so its scenarios cast it in its controller's main phase. Scenarios: exile an opponent's tapped land, and it returns untapped at the beginning of that turn's end step; exile a permanent its controller does not own, and it returns under its owner's control; the token case (a token exiled does not return).
