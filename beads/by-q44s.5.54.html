Close reason

Closed prompt's tray lets go at the answering script's beat; parity entries for opponent-turn and verbs-tuck-theirs gone (830291c7d)

Description

Symptom: just b3d timeline-parity still lists two recordings whose differing steps are all steps the client makes itself ("own": true): opponent-turn/you (2 steps) and verbs-tuck-theirs/opponent (1 step). In both, the web interface lets a closed prompt's tray go at the beat of the answer's script whose steps first concern a held card (beat 3 at 261 ms in opponent-turn view 3, where the library's cards flip as it is shuffled; beat 1 at 33 ms in verbs-tuck-theirs view 5). Beryl 3D lets it go at once, first in the view's beat (beat 0 at 0 ms).

Why: the web inserts the let-go into the answer's script (lowered in ui/src/stage/prompt.ts: beats[at] = [end, ...beats[at]], where at is the first beat with a step aboutOf a held card, or 0). Beryl 3D plays the let-go in Client::own_beat (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/client/own.rs) when the closed prompt's marks end, and docs/beryl-3d.md 'Timing a script' records that choice ('the tray letting its cards go first in that view's beat, where the web lets it go as the answer's script begins'). The two agree only when the answer's first beat concerns a held card.

Do: when a closed prompt's tray lets go, find the first beat of the answering view's compiled script (Compiled::laid, Compiled::steps in play/compile.rs) that has a step about a held card, and start the let-go at that beat's turn (Laid::turn) on the script's clock rather than at once. If the script has not started yet, place it when it starts (play::relay). Teach Table::let_go (table/tray.rs) a delay, keep Show::hand_over_tray (play/tray.rs) handing cards the script moves over to it, and give OwnRecord (prompts/mod.rs) the beat and a start on the script's clock so that driver::check::timeline prints them as the web does. Correct the doc's sentence.

Found by by-q44s.5.19, which fixed the other own-only differences.
