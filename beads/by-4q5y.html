Description

Non-blocking. by-w8gv.5.17 applied its default, neither: Small keeps two 60-card decks and a pool of 135, and a game whose deck exceeds 60 plays in Large (no flat Copy state for search). Revisit when the next project picks its Legacy decks: if a chosen list needs more than 60 deck slots per player (an 80-card companion build, or cards brought in from outside the game such as Karn, the Great Creator, wishes or a companion), choose A (a third flat layout, 80+80+95) or B (re-deal Small as 80+80+95). by-w8gv.5.17's description and comments have the costs of each. Look at rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/ids.rs (SlotId), layout.rs (Small, Large) and the test eighty_card_decks_are_refused_by_small_and_play_in_large in rust/crates/beryl_search/tests/games.rs.

Acceptance criteria

When a supported deck or format (a Legacy list with wishes or a companion, later Commander) needs more than 60 deck slots per player, an agent chooses A, B or neither from by-w8gv.5.17's measured costs, records why here, and files the bead for it. No question for the user.
