Description

Five legal Mind Bend or Crystal Spray changes on one object can silently discard the last change. In timestamp order, change Island to Swamp, Plains to Island, Swamp to Mountain, Mountain to Forest, then Forest to Swamp. The staging 950e3c614 Words::changing_subtype probe returns None on that last change, and beryl_kernel/src/effects/text.rs changed_words silently keeps the old mapping. An original Island still reads Forest instead of Swamp. A full map over the five basic land words could replace the sparse capacity-four list and make this domain total. Check beryl_card/src/words.rs, beryl_kernel/src/effects/text.rs and beryl_rules/src/text.rs. Preserve timestamp composition, the captured words of a stacked ability, frozen saves and the count and size bars.

Acceptance criteria

A deterministic scenario or focused rules test composes those five changes, checks that original Island reads as Swamp and Plains still reads as Island, and covers the final change in both the live and captured read paths where applicable. Add the bug-log entry and name the guard against silent loss.
