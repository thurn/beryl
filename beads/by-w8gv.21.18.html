Do

  • For each verb: at least three instances over at least two recordings, found with just video find and measured with by video measure. Where the tracker cannot follow the card, read the numbers once from a filmstrip at the recording's frame rate (just video film) and say so.
  • Commit data/arena-motion.json. Per verb: each instance (recording id, time, crop, the numbers, how it was taken: tracker or filmstrip, and why), and the summary (median and range of duration, start delay, stagger, peak size; the path's shape). These are measurements, not the log of a run. A header says how they were taken and how to retake one.
  • tools/src/beryl_tools/conventions.py validates the file (verb names against the verbs list, recording ids, times) with a pytest, as it validates the conventions' evidence.
  • Set each verb's values in data/ui-conventions.json from Arena's medians, correcting the first values the slice gave its verbs (by-w8gv.21.14). Mark a verb that a numbered Beryl exception covers as exempt, with the exception's number. Regenerate docs/ui-conventions.md (just conventions).
  • A verb the recordings cannot show at all goes under not-seen with what it is composed from (K29).
  • Where a measurement contradicts a settled entry or a request of the user's: apply Arena, and list every case in one bead labelled human.
  • The per-verb measuring parallelizes: use Sonnet subagents (AGENTS.md, Workflow) and check one instance of each verb yourself.
  • Commit no clip, still or filmstrip.
