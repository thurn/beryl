Description

Read first: epic by-bmdk, sections 'Rules for every bead', 'Contracts' and 'Code map'. Design: docs/manual-multiplayer.md ("the doc"), section 'Combat' under 'Assists', including its pseudo-code and worked example.

Create rust/crates/beryl_table/src/assist/combat.rs: pub fn proposal(table: &Table) -> Option<Proposal>, present once an attack arrow exists.

Input: the attack and block arrows (in the order drawn), and for each creature its shown power and toughness (facts::shown_stats), its marked damage, and its keywords from the store record. Implement the doc's pseudo-code exactly:

  • Two rounds when any creature in combat has first strike or double strike, otherwise one.
  • An unblocked attacker deals its power to the seat or planeswalker attacked.
  • A blocked attacker assigns lethal damage to its blockers in arrow order (1 is lethal from a deathtouch source); with trample the remainder goes to what it attacked.
  • A blocker deals its power to the attacker it blocks.
  • After each round: a creature dies if its damage is at least its toughness, or a deathtouch source damaged it, unless it has indestructible; a lifelink source's controller gains the damage it dealt. A creature that died deals no damage later.
  • Handle first strike, double strike, deathtouch, trample, lifelink, indestructible. Ignore everything else.

Output lines, each ending with whose card it is (Line.whose): "<name> dies", "<name> takes N" for survivors, "<seat> loses N" / "gains N", "<planeswalker> loses N loyalty", and "Go to end of combat". Their actions: damage on survivors, move of the dead to their owners' graveyards, life, counter on planeswalkers, phase.

Tests in tests/assist_combat.rs: the doc's example (a 3/3 first strike trample blocked by a 2/2 and a 1/1 gives exactly the two "dies" lines); one test per keyword; double block ordering; a planeswalker attacked; unblocked damage; a line switched off is left out of apply_proposal. Run just test-rust -p beryl_table.

Acceptance criteria

The doc's worked example produces exactly its two lines; each of the six keywords has a test; applying the proposal through apply() changes the table as the lines say.

Derek Thurn2026-11-14

The worked example has two card-result lines and the requested third line advances to end of combat. The combat phase gates the preview because attack arrows stay on the table after advancing. The proposal is computed when either seat has Combat on. The adapter should check enabled(table, seat, Assist::Combat) before showing or accepting it for that seat. When manual arrows repeat a combatant, the first valid attack or block in drawing order wins.

Derek Thurn2026-11-14

Handoff: assist::combat::proposal computes one host preview from the current combat arrows and shown card facts. Filter it per seat with assist::enabled, then use assist::apply_proposal for its checked lines. The preview withdraws at EndOfCombat even if the attack arrows remain. Arrow order, the six keywords, the two worked-example deaths, and ordinary action application are exercised in tests/assist_combat.rs. Fast checks passed on staging in 852fc80c.
