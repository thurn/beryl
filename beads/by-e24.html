Close reason

One parametric battlefield layout per side; modes differ only by gap and right edge (c3bab1f)

Description

The docs/ui_mockups images are generated references, not a pixel spec: they are not consistent with one another, and earlier pixel-matching work fitted code to each one separately. This bead removes one such fit. Prefer one rule that holds in every state over matching any single mockup. just ui-compare verdicts for the affected regions may get worse; that is expected, so do not tune values back toward the mockups.

ui/src/game/layout.ts's ROWS gives each side three separately tuned layouts for its permanents row (main, combat, stack), one per mockup. Entering combat, or putting anything on the stack, moves every permanent and changes its frame's shape: your creatures go to 0.88 width × 0.957 height while their text and bars stay full size, and your row drops about 39px. The opponent's row center moves 129px left. The sides do not match: your combat center is 856 and your stack center 802, while the opponent's is 673 in both. The opponent's land row is also drawn 4.5% larger than depthScale allows (size: 1.045) with a gap of 30 against your 3, because battlefield.png painted it that way.

Replace this with one parametric layout per side. Combat and the stack may only add room (spacing, a shorter right edge where the stack and prompt stand), applied the same way to both sides. Cards keep the field frame's aspect in every mode. Land rows follow depthScale with the same gap on both sides.

Acceptance criteria

Permanents keep the field frame's aspect in every mode; main, combat and stack differ only by spacing, the same way for both sides; land rows follow depthScale with one gap. just check and just ci pass, with no new constants tuned to a single mockup.
