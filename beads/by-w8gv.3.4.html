Review of M2 (by-w8gv.3.1 to 3.3) at staging 3ff04752; fix pushed as 9d912c27.

FOUND AND FIXED

  • Bug: the interpreter read a target twice when an instruction set out to move it and could not. Run::remember marked it moved before the move, so Scene::total added its last known value to the one it still had. Reachable today: exile_until_this_leaves(TARGET) resolving after its source left (CR 610.3b), then Amount::Power/Toughness/ManaValue/Counters of TARGET gave double; it would have surfaced for every destroy once indestructible exists. Now whether a target moved is read from the state (not current), and the register only says its last known information was noted. New test a_target_an_instruction_could_not_move_is_read_where_it_is failed with 24 life for 22 before the fix. docs/bugs.md has the entry.
  • Duplicate work: target legality was found in resolve.rs (fizzles) and again in Run::new. resolve::legal_targets finds it once as a mask; interpret::resolve takes it. Count 406,861,374 to 404,574,527.
  • Condition::Is on a target the effect has moved answered false silently (Known keeps no characteristics); it is a debug assertion now, so a card cannot come to depend on it unnoticed.

CONFIRMED, NO CHANGE

  • Dependency shape and names: beryl_card depends on nothing; beryl_engine on beryl_card; beryl_cards on beryl_card, beryl_engine and beryl_scenario, as the plan's Crates table has it.
  • K16: no vocabulary entry carries a Forge script field or idiom; references are Those / ThatMany / Did, values of their expression types. Every Effect, Objects, Players, Amount and Condition type cites its rule; I read the citations against the rule text. Variants with no rule of their own (Fixed, the Comparisons, Not/All/Any) lean on their type's.
  • Equivalence: each of the 20 ported abilities read against its Oracle text and its deleted body (Oust FromTop(2) = under one, Teferi Hero FromTop(3) = under two, Jace's UntilYourNextTurn, Treasure's direct AddMana path gives the same Mana event and layer refresh), and 3.1's keyword builders against the bodies deleted from effect.rs (evoke, offspring, impending, crew). Derived removal matches what every ported ability declared.
  • Parity at the push: just check passed, cargo test -p beryl_session --test corpus passed, rollouts_never_change passed, count gate under the bar with no bump.
  • Paper test: seed 20261110, the pool's definition, the 6/51/2/1 split and all 60 cards with what each misfit lacks are on by-w8gv.3 (comment of 04:46) and in 3.1's notes. No misfit was answered with a string-typed parameter or a named variable: the only strings in beryl_card are card names and the const mana("{1}{U}") parser, which yields a typed ManaCost. NOT reproduced: the draw itself. The dated Scryfall bulk file is not kept on this machine and the sampling script was not committed, so 'not hand-picked' rests on the recorded method (sorted by oracle_id, one Random(seed).sample) and on the result, which no one would pick: 6 of 60 fit.
  • Tests: effect_data.rs (twins keep their own body copies as the reference) and effects.rs are black-box and time nothing. Nothing pruned.

LEFT ALONE, FOR M5 TO KNOW (also on by-w8gv.9)

  • Players::ControllerOf(Those) and of All read objects where they are now, so owners once they have left; last known information is kept for targets only.
  • CopyException::PowerToughness other than 1/1 panics at run time (no_primitive) rather than failing the build.
  • Ability::produces and a written-out mana ability's AddMana effect say the same ManaSet twice; tap_for_mana builds both from one argument.
  • The fast check ran 108 s against its 60 s budget at my push, under load, with rust tests at 91 s; it passed. Not M2's doing as far as I can tell; the CI track owns it.
