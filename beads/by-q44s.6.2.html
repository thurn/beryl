Sage audit of milestone 3 (by-q44s.5): 58 closed beads, 2026-11-15 16:37Z (plan) to 2026-11-16 20:33Z. Sources: bead timings and comments, $BERYL_CACHE/ci/runs (fast-check run log), $BERYL_CACHE/cpu-tokens/leases.jsonl, .git/enact/ci.log, and my own timing runs at load 8-27.

  1. Beads that took far longer than their neighbours. The median bead took about 70 min.
  2. by-q44s.5.39 (Warden, 1242 min) is not real: it was reserved at 17:01Z, before the planner had added its dependency edges, and released at 17:04Z. The review itself took about 40 min on 2026-11-16. The started_at value kept the first reservation.
  3. by-q44s.5.11, prompts pick and zone (265 min): the work was redone. The contract (by-q44s.5.2) gave prompts::own a signature that did not work, so the pick bead and the pay bead (by-q44s.5.14) each designed a different replacement in parallel. Pay landed first, and pick was rebuilt on pay's OwnRecord design. The cause is a contract gap, not a lack of context.
  4. by-q44s.5.16, 5.18 and 5.20 (145-187 min): about 70 min of each was staging being red (point 5). 5.18 was released and resumed.
  5. Rebases were cheap. 5.6, 5.17, 5.18 and 5.35 each resolved a rebase in minutes, mostly timeline-parity.json and prompts::bar's argument list. No conflicts with ui/ or with the manual table epic (by-bmdk) were recorded.
  6. The pauses between beads were caused by the shared run's slots, not by the graph. Between the contract (22:52Z) and the first track bead (05:11Z), every slot was busy with milestone 2 beads, sweep repairs and Enact fixes. About 12 milestone 3 beads were ready during those six hours.
  7. Isolation (C17). No Beryl 3D path chose a main-project lane, and no main-project path chose a Beryl 3D lane. Of 552 fast checks in the window, 162 were Beryl 3D only, 279 mixed and 111 non-Beryl 3D. The lanes that crossed over came from two sources. First, a rebase recheck reruns the lanes of commits that landed meanwhile ('affected' in the run log). For example, tools/src/beryl_tools/cli.py ran the beryl3d lanes, and data/beryl3d/gates.json (7041e45b6) ran ui. Second, 767ef0fbe ran every lane ('all') twice. names and repo run for every change by design and took 0.4-0.5 s at the median. The real cost of C17 showed up once: b55feb411, an engine fix, changed the recordings and exposed a Beryl 3D driver bug that only the sweep could see (point 5).
  8. Edit-to-output is over its target. The target is 3 s from a one-line beryl_scene edit to just b3d tree verbs-zones --at 1500ms (docs/beryl-3d.md). I measured 23, 26, 9.3, 14.7 and 2.9 s (median 14.7 s) at load 16-20. The compile plus the command takes about 3 s. The rest was waiting in the CPU-token pool: two leases each waited 10.1 s (RECLAIM_SECONDS) because they asked for 12 tokens while a sweep held elastic ones. FIXED in e07e319fe: single-threaded b3d commands (list, info, tree, diff) now ask for one token. After the fix, the tree command's lease waited 0.0 s in all six runs. Three edits took 2.3-2.5 s, and three took 11-13 s because the cargo shim's own lease still waited about 8 s. Filed by-q44s.6.5 for that. During milestone 3, agent cargo build leases waited 44,918 s in total, against 75,214 s spent holding tokens.
  9. Width. At most 10 milestone 3 beads were in progress at once (14:52Z on 11-16), and at the narrowest 0-1 (the contract, 21:59-22:52Z, and the Wardens after 19:00Z). The run was limited by slots, not by the graph. What would have widened it: Wardens that were not reserved before their edges existed (an Enact planner race), and a contract that settled prompts::own, so the pick and pay beads would not have designed it twice. The lanes (prompts-tray, prompts-aim, furniture, table, cards) each ran 2-3 beads in order. Three lane sessions were released for heavy context, which worked as intended.

  11. Staging's Beryl 3D lane was red from 14:59Z to 16:11Z (about 72 min). 2104192d7 (the settings gear) shifted the element ids that a beryl_inspect test hard-coded. Its own fast check failed both test steps, but it landed after a retry, because the fast check cuts tests when it runs out of time. The planted-fault test was cut 5 times in the window. Filed by-q44s.6.6.
  12. Overnight, before milestone 3 (11-15 22:58Z to 11-16 04:49Z), b3d:choice-pay, choice-thirst and choice-trigger-order failed in 22 consecutive fast checks on several branches. Those failures belonged to milestone 2 and the sweep repairs, not to milestone 3.
  13. The by-q44s.5.45 live test had a 20 s wall-clock bound that failed under load (fixed).
  14. Slow CI was healthy. Of 97 runs in the window, 2 failed (by-917gp, a full disk, and by-o0uui), and master never stayed behind.
  15. Slow fast-check steps: a Beryl 3D-only fast check takes 256 s at the median and 356 s at p90, against a 180 s budget. The beryl3d-interface record step took 79 s of wall time at the median and was over 20 s in 172 of 208 runs on 11-16. It is rebuilt whenever staging's main-workspace sources change, which is most pushes. beryl3d-tests cargo-test took 126 s at the median. by-yz2gx and by-ba3oh, which are open, already cover these, so I filed no duplicate.

Beryl 3D followed within hours through catch-up beads 5.49-5.57: the lobby, the long-press sheet, sheet submenus, key symbols and the deck picker.
