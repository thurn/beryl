Handoff: staging dba19a73. The phone layout now raises the viewer's life total over their hand at rest.

What was found: the table-* recordings play on the manual table (Screen::Table), where a life total catches presses on purpose (CountFloats::passes is false there). So the total really did cover back-row hand cards. On the game screen it lets presses through and never caused these faults.

Changes:

  • table/phone.rs your_life(window, bands, hand_top): the viewer's life anchor is min(strip middle, hand_top - DOCK_GAP - HALF_LIFE), clamped to no higher than your_back.top + HALF_LIFE, so a hand of 3+ rows (which already covers the lands) does not push the total onto the creatures' row. Clamping a DOCK_GAP lower left only 46 points of a 3-row back-row card, which the 4-point hit grid failed. hand_top(camera, rests) measures the hand's top from card corners, ignoring held tray poses.
  • table/scene.rs: Table::apply (before with_held) and swap_objects (before rehold) call raise_life, which rewrites layout.players[0]. Everything that reads the anchor follows it: player_pose/pool_pose (streaks, strikes, Show::reach), the life float (CountFloats::write) and the life change float. The anchor changes at a script's end, when its objects are swapped. Scripts are compiled against the table shown before that swap, so a strike within the same script aims where the float still stands.
  • hud/players.rs: on phones the orb is Anchor::Object(Subject::Player), so it follows the anchor on every frame. The desktop keeps Anchor::Point. orb_centre is still the strip middle; on phones it is now used only for the opponent (banner), since step stops are hidden there.
  • Test: table_phone.rs the_viewers_life_total_rises_over_a_hand_that_reaches_into_its_strip (it fails without the fix).
  • docs/beryl-3d.md 'Sizes' updated.

Verification: in just b3d test --phone-only over all 6204 tests, per-test fault counts only fell (338 to 328 failing tests; no test rose). --every-tick for table-crowded and table-board: table-board is clean. The faults left in table-crowded (6 at 393x759, 8 at 375x647, 1 at 430x932) are lands and other permanents covered by the hand's back rows, with no life total involved. Desktop just b3d test (8272 tests), just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene (484 tests) and just b3d gate pass.

Reviewer: check the clamp in your_life for windows where your_back is short. Note that the life total jumps (it does not animate) at the end of the script that adds a hand row.
