Description

Symptom: on Beryl 3D's phone layout, two prompts and one screen have controls below the phone's touch and text sizes. just b3d test --phone-only reports:

  • shape-divide (393x759, 375x647, 430x932, seat you, t=90): the More and Less buttons of each card's share (region "share <id>") are 28 by 28 points, below the 44 by 44 that phone-sizes requires. They stand on the card itself, so Snapcaster Mage, Elite Spellbinder and Trinket Mage are also reachable faults ("under region share ..., button More, text 1, button Less").
  • screen/settings (all three phone windows, t=0): the graphics tier buttons "Automatic", "Baseline" and "Enhanced" (about 75x48) shrink their label to 7.47 points, under the 11-point minimum (phone-sizes).

Read: rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/prompts/divide.rs (the share steppers), src/screens/settings.rs (the tier row), src/hud/phone.rs, src/agent/checks.rs (phone_sizes, reachable), data/beryl3d/space.json (hud.phone), docs/beryl-3d.md "Sizes" and "Interface checks". Look at the web interface's phone shot of the same moments for the layout (ui/shots.json, routes with ?phone).

Fix: on phones, give each share a stepper with 44-point buttons that stands off the card (beside or below it, as the web does), and lay the tier buttons out so their labels keep at least 11 points (for example, one button per line, or a wider row). Do not add phone fault allowances.
