For parts whose placement belongs to other beads (scry tray fan, hand fan), give the reviewer an aligned copy too: crop the replica at the offset that best matches the mockup.

The ten parts still differing, with the last reviewer's notes:

  • Glows: trigger_on_stack_with_target/opponent-strix-target, trigger_on_stack_with_target/you-titan-source, ui_system/04-source. The mockups disagree: ui_system 04 wraps the rim in light; the trigger screen keeps a dark frame inside a thick saturated neon ring with a broad halo.   • ui_system/05-trigger: the compact stack item's rules text (size, weight, line breaks) and the box's darker edge.
  • ui_system/04-hand: the mockup crops the three cards at their type lines; its names are larger and bolder.
  • ui_system/06-counterspell: section 06 is the real overlay scaled down; the mockup draws that card squatter, with larger text and mana symbols. Decide whether the gallery's 06 preview should change or record it as accepted.
