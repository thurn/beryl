Description

When Show and Tell puts two Auras onto the battlefield, the nonactive player can be asked what to enchant first. Show and Tell's 2004-10-04 ruling c6418194890f gives the order: after all the hand picks, the active player makes their card's entry choices first, then the other player. See CR 101.4 and 303.4f.

Reproduce in rust/cards/cards_1998/usg/show_and_tell.rs with a scenario named "Show and Tell chooses both Auras before either attachment". Give active P0 Show and Tell and Swift Reconfiguration, three Islands, and Prop::Vanilla. Give P1 Holy Strength in hand and Prop::Flier on the battlefield. Cast Show and Tell, have P0 pick Swift Reconfiguration and P1 pick Holy Strength, then script P0 to choose Prop::Flier before P1 chooses Prop::Vanilla. Beryl asks P1 to attach Holy Strength first. The intended final state has the flier as a noncreature Vehicle and the vanilla creature at 3/3, with each player controlling their Aura.

rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/zones.rs sends the set of cards to event::move_batch. In rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/event.rs, move_batch_to orders the hand cards by name via State::in_game_order, then move_ordered prepares the replacements and Aura attachments in that order. Holy Strength sorts before Swift Reconfiguration. Review the other as-enters choices that share this path. Preserve simultaneous entry and deterministic committed events across slot numberings.

Acceptance criteria

A Show and Tell scenario with reverse-alphabetical Auras asks both hand picks before attachment and asks the active player to attach first in every supported layout and slot numbering. Link the scenario to ruling c6418194890f. The card check, rulings check, count gate, frozen saves and rollouts pass. Record the bug and regression guard in docs/bugs.md.
