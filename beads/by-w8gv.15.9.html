What to build

  1. .agents/skills/card-writing/SKILL.md with a symlink .claude/skills/card-writing -> ../../.agents/skills/card-writing like the others (see .agents/skills/specs for the format and frontmatter). Steps: just card brief <name> and read it all; just card new <name>; write the definition from the vocabulary entries the brief lists and the nearest cards' files, never from Forge (K16) and with rules comments citing the Comprehensive Rules per AGENTS.md (just citations); just card check <name> --no-oracles until it passes, then the full just card check <name>; commit and push per AGENTS.md (enact worktree, enact push). Rules for the writer: no new vocabulary, no engine code, no Rust body, no edits outside the card file (and the escalation file); never weaken a check or add an oracle divergence to pass; a failing oracle or render diff means the definition is wrong until shown otherwise.
  2. Escalation. When the brief shows the vocabulary cannot express an ability, or a check fails for a missing mechanic, the writer stops, deletes the card file, and records the card in data/card-escalations.json (create it: card name → the missing mechanic in one phrase, the CR rule it falls under, and the ability's Oracle text), then commits only that entry. just card brief prints a card's escalation if it has one (a few lines in card.py), and the census can join on it later. Say in the skill how to tell a missing mechanic from a writing error (the vocabulary entry list in the brief; vocabulary.py).
  3. Try it once. Run the skill yourself on one store-only card that today's vocabulary can express (pick a simple one: a creature with an enters-the-battlefield draw, or a burn spell; not one a pilot slot will later pick if you can tell), fix the skill where it misled you, and then delete the card file without committing it (the pilot picks and keeps the real cards). Record the card, the steps that misled and what you changed on the bead.
  4. AGENTS.md "Testing your work" item 1 mentions the skill in one sentence beside just card brief.
