Close reason

Arrows anchor on facing sides and bow from chord geometry, clamped to the stage; no fixed control vectors (101db89)

Description

The docs/ui_mockups images are generated references, not a pixel spec: they are not consistent with one another, and earlier pixel-matching work fitted code to each one separately. This bead removes one such fit. Prefer one rule that holds in every state over matching any single mockup. just ui-compare verdicts for the affected regions may get worse; that is expected, so do not tune values back toward the mockups.

ui/src/game/Board.tsx's boardArrows bends every arrow with fixed pixel control vectors fitted to where the source and target sat in combat.png and trigger_on_stack_with_target.png: the attacker-to-life arrow uses leave [-34, -138] and arrive [-100, -85] with fixed 0.173 and 0.233 offsets into the orb, the source-to-stack arrow leave [30, 175] and arrive [130, -55], and each target arrow leave [-150, -195] and arrive [0, 150]. They do not depend on where the ends actually are, so other positions draw odd curves. A target near the top of the stage (the opponent's life orb at y≈48, or the opponent's lands) puts a control point off the stage.

Derive the curves from the geometry: control points from the vector between the two ends (a bow proportional to the distance, perpendicular to the chord, turned toward a consistent side), with the ends' exit and entry sides chosen from their relative positions. components/Stack.tsx's ArrowSpec already supports bend and bow; extend or simplify it as needed. Keep arrows on the stage.

Acceptance criteria

No fixed pixel control vectors in boardArrows; arrows to targets anywhere on the stage, the opponent's life included, stay on the stage. just check and just ci pass, with no new constants tuned to a single mockup.

Design: ArrowSpec gains a 'facing' side (the point of the element's inscribed ellipse toward the other end's center) and, without bend, bows away from the arrows' parent's center by 0.3 of the chord length, fading to straight when the chord's midpoint lies within an eighth of its length of the center line (no flip). Control points (quadratic and cubic) are clamped to the parent, so curves stay on the stage. Arrows to a player now meet the life orb's oval (LifeOrb anchor id <side>-life-orb) instead of the whole winged box. Removed the unused 'bow' option and boardArrows' placements parameter. Gallery/replica leave/arrive specs left as is (by-a08 owns the static table). Verified by hand: retargeting the stack-trigger arrow to the opponent's life orb, the top-right graveyard and a box at the stage's top-left corner all drew within 1600x900 (the corner case clamps a control point to y=0). just check and just ui-test pass.
