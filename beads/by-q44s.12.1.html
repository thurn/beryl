Description

Audit the architecture of milestone 6 of this epic against docs/beryl-3d.md. Scope: the whole epic: every Beryl 3D crate, the hosts, the gates and the tools.

Questions to answer: Do the tiers share every position? Does the step down move anything? Which contracts turned out wrong and were worked around instead of fixed? What would have to change to support a third layout or a third tier?
