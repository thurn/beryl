Close reason

phone rows keep their whole area when the stack cut leaves no room; held by unit tests; android-smoke passes

Description

Symptom: just b3d android-smoke on staging at 9c38c8bff panicked in lay_row (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/rows.rs) with 'min > max. min = 1, max = 0'. by-q44s.7.15 added a guard so an empty row no longer panics, but the cause remains.

Cause: 9c38c8bff ('b3d keeps creature rows clear of the stack') made row_area in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/stack.rs end a creature row a gap left of the stack's furthest reach. On the phone's narrow portrait window that end lies left of the row's start, so the row's room is negative. A row with cards then shows a single pile (shown clamps to 1) instead of laying its cards out, and the row counts in hud/rows.rs sit at crossed ends.

Fix: make row_area keep a usable row on the phone layout (for example, apply the stack cut only where the stack's column is beside the rows, or clamp the end to leave room for the least strip), and add an interface test or check that fails when any row's right end is left of its left end on the phone sizes (393x759, 375x647, 430x932).

Acceptance criteria

No battlefield row's area has right < left at the phone sizes, held by a test in beryl_scene; just b3d android-smoke passes; the desktop stress boards keep their reachable-check result.
