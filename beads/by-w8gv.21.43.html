Description

Build the independent motion review that decides when a verb is done (the user, 2026-11-11: "independent aesthetic judgement is needed here, our cards do not work the same way"; the policy bead filed beside this one).

Independent means two things: the reviewer is a separate session from the one that built the verb, given the films and not the implementer's reasoning; and it judges Beryl's motion on its own merits for Beryl's cards, using Arena's clip for what the motion must communicate, never as a picture to match.

Build

  1. A film command, just motion --film <verb> [case]: the verb's moment from the verb suite (ui/verbs.json) played on the stage in stepped stage time, both seats and both layouts, as filmstrips at a frame rate fine enough to judge easing (30 fps of stage time and an every-tick strip of the key 300 ms), plus a contact sheet that puts Arena's instances of the verb (data/arena-motion.json commands, through just video film) beside the stage's at the same scale. Reuse ui/scripts/stepped.mjs and ui/scripts/film.mjs, tools/src/beryl_tools/video.py and motion.py; prints the paths. Never commit the images.
  2. The tracker reports more than timing: tools/src/beryl_tools/video_measure.py already follows each blob's centre per frame. Add the path's bow (largest distance of the centre from the straight chord, over the chord's length, with its side) and, where the blob's box allows, rotation and squash. Print these for Arena's instances and the stage alike as information for the reviewer; nothing is held to them.
  3. A skill, .claude/skills/motion-review/SKILL.md, the reviewer's procedure. The implementer runs it by spawning a fresh session or subagent (Opus or Fable, never the implementer's own context) with only: the verb's name and cases, its prose description in data/arena-motion.json, the films and contact sheet, and the stage's values. The rubric, each scored pass, fix or note with what was seen at which frame:
    • Readable: from either seat, a player can tell what changed, whose it was and where it went, the first time, at full speed.
    • Staged: one thing leads at a time; what should hold still holds still; the eye is led to the next thing that matters.
    • Path and easing: arcs where a thrown or lifted object would arc, easing that gives weight, no linear glides, nothing that pops.
    • Anticipation and settle: a motion starts and lands with intent (a lift before travel, a settle or flash on arrival) where it helps reading and not where it slows play.
    • Depth: what moves over what is right for the whole motion; things that only make room never cross over the subject.
    • Groups: a batch reads as one group; neighbours make room as one smooth move, not a jostle.
    • Fits Beryl: works with full cards that tap, Beryl's frames, sizes and table distances; consistent with the verbs already reviewed.
    • Pace: as quick as reading allows; a game's worth of these does not drag. Against Arena's clip, only: does Beryl's version communicate the same thing in the same order? Where Arena does something that matters for reading that Beryl lacks (a face turning mid-flight, a glow, a dissolve), it is a fix.
  4. The verdict and its findings go on the verb's bead as one comment; fixes are made and the verb reviewed again by a fresh reviewer, at most three rounds, then the remaining findings are filed as a follow-up and the bead may close.

Run it once on the first slice (by-w8gv.21.14's Overlord moment: fly, emerge, appear, vanish) and file what it finds as one bead in lane:verbs, labelled tier:fable.

Not a check in CI: judgement does not run in just check. The run-log has no record for it.
