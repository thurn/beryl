Description

just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_inspect the_check_times_find_every_planted_fault fails on staging at 2a0c49aa7 (and with the by-q44s.91.11 fix on top). The differential test plays 200 sampled interface tests at their check times and again on every tick, and every tick finds text-overlap faults that the check times miss: the life change float ("-6", "-2", "-1", floats::LIFE_CHANGE) over the "Combat" notice text, in

  • b3d:overlord-of-the-mistmoors/overlord-cast-in-full-then-attacking/you/1920x1080 t=5450ms
  • b3d:malcolm-alluring-scoundrel/malcolms-fourth-chorus-counter-casts-the-discard-ask-12/you/1440x900 t=866ms
  • b3d:adeline-resplendent-cathar/adelines-human-arrives-attacking/you/1440x900 t=1583ms

So the check times (the ticks just b3d test judges) don't sample the moment the float is visible over the notice; the text-overlap rule (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/agent/text_overlap.rs, added in 17366ae4e) needs check times that cover a float's life, or the float must stop overlapping the notice (by-q44s.9.52 owns the overlap itself: the web draws the change on the orb's other rim). Fix one of the two so the differential test passes; don't weaken the differential test.

Files: rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_inspect/tests/all/differential.rs, the check-time selection in beryl_inspect (grep Mode::EveryTick / check times), rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/hud/counts.rs (change_look), data/beryl3d/test-faults.json.
