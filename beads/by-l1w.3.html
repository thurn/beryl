Findings: (1) Off-center: the nonland row could not start left of x=580 (to clear the land piles), so any row wider than ~440 px ran right (late-game opp row 580-1185, blockers 580-1495). Now rows are centered on the stage middle with room 120-1480; with the stack/prompt at the right a too-long row slides left just enough. Rows were moved apart vertically (opp lands y112/permanents 235, your permanents 408/lands 570; LAND_WIDTH 190->180) so a row may pass over the land piles' side without covering a land. Land piles stay anchored left by design (your life orb sits where a centered land row would). (2) Hover: layout() no longer depends on hover at all. Headless WebKit never showed other cards moving on hover; the visible 'jump' was the hovered card's springy un-rotate/lift being part of its placement, which also moved its hit box (4/310 sampled points lost the card once raised, i.e. hover flicker in a real WebView). The raise is now drawn inside the card element (quick 0.14 s tween, upright about the top center, straight up the screen), so the element and its hit box never move; 0/310 points lose it. Dragged hand cards stay raised and follow the pointer exactly. ui-test: hover hand/field card asserts no other card moves (1 px), no .card-slot layout transform, hovered element stays put; centering at 1280 and 1600 on combat-blockers' first quiet crowded view (fails on old code); assertClearOfLands on that and the crowded combat table. The drag test now pauses the clock only before the drop: under a frozen clock Motion dropped renders of the dragged card once no animation was running (real-time drag verified fine). Shots game-crowded(-1280) added.
