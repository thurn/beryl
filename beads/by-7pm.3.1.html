Description

Give the start screen, the game-over screen and the small overlays a phone layout. Today, on a phone, each is the desktop's 1600×900 stage (ui/src/game/Stage.tsx) scaled to the phone's width: the "New Game" panel is about 170px wide, "Start" measures 52×13, "New Game" and "View Battlefield" on the game-over screen 68×13. The phone audit (phoneFaults in ui/scripts/capture.mjs) fails all of them.

Where the code is

  • ui/src/screens/StartScreen.tsx (about 230 lines): wraps everything in <Stage> (its line 132). It holds the agent list, the seed, the Start button, "Return to Game" and "New Game" when a game is in progress, the Credits link and the sound button.
  • ui/src/screens/GameOver.tsx: GameOver (the .game-over dialog: backdrop, title, reason with its icon, the culprit card, its buttons), resultTitle, useResult.
  • ui/src/screens/Game.tsx, the end of GameScreen: the overlays fragment and the phone branch {overlaid && <Stage>{overlays}</Stage>}. The overlays are: the "Target your <permanent>?" confirmation (FloatPrompt with Confirm and Cancel), the opening (Opening: by-7pm.3.2's, leave it), the game-over screen, the "Back to <result>" button shown after "View Battlefield", and the error toast (.game-error.game-toast). The cut-over may have removed some of these: handle the ones that are there.
  • The interstitial while a game starts ("Shuffling up and drawing opening hands", Game.tsx near its line 86).
  • Phone detection: usePhone() from ui/src/mobile/phone.ts. Styles for a phone go in ui/src/mobile/phone.css under body.phone.
  • A bottom sheet already exists: the private Sheet in ui/src/stage/PhoneChrome.tsx. Export it and reuse it; do not write a second one.

What to build

On a phone none of these may be inside <Stage>. Each is laid out in CSS pixels of the phone's viewport, inside the safe areas (env(safe-area-inset-top), env(safe-area-inset-bottom), as phone.css already does for .phone-board). Keep each component's state and logic; branch only its markup and styles on usePhone(). Every label is the desktop's, unchanged (D6).

  1. Start screen. A column filling the viewport, 16px side gutters, over the same table background.
    • Top row, 56px high: the Credits link at the left and the sound button at the right, each at least 40×40.
    • The legend the desktop shows over the agents, at 13px.
    • One row per agent, at least 56px high: the radio at 24px, the title at 16px, the description at 13px wrapping to at most two lines, and the desktop's tag for a cheating agent at 13px. The whole row is the touch target.
    • The agent list is the one part that scrolls (overflow-y: auto; touch-action: pan-y) when the screen is too short: with eight agents it is at 375×647.
    • The seed row, 44px high: its label, and the input with font-size: 16px (iOS zooms the page on focus below 16px) and inputmode="numeric", the desktop's placeholder.
    • The buttons, full width, 48px high, 8px apart, pinned above the bottom safe area: the primary one lowest. With a game in progress that is "Return to Game" primary and "New Game" secondary above it, as the desktop orders them. Keep the desktop's behavior on replacing a game in progress exactly (same clicks, same words).
    • The desktop's panel title and subtitle are left out on a phone (AGENTS.md, Workflow: half the words).
    • The interstitial: its text at 13px or more, centered.
  2. Game over. Over the phone board with a 60% black scrim, not inside <Stage>.
    • The title at 36px, centered, its top at 30% of the height.
    • The reason under it: its icon and the desktop's words at 16px.
    • The culprit card, when there is one, 160px wide (140px when the viewport is under 393 wide), centered under the reason, as the desktop draws it.
    • Its two buttons full width, 48px high, pinned above the bottom safe area, the primary one lowest.
    • After "View Battlefield" the scrim and the dialog go and the board shows; the desktop's "Back to <result>" button is then one full-width 48px secondary button pinned above the bottom safe area, over the hand.
  3. "Target your <permanent>?" As a Sheet holding the question at 16px and Confirm and Cancel as two 48px buttons side by side. Skip this if Game.tsx no longer has it.
  4. The error toast. At the top, under the top safe area, 16px gutters, its text at 13px or more, wrapping.
  5. Remove the phone branch's <Stage> wrapper once nothing but the opening needs it. Leave the opening inside it, with a comment naming by-7pm.3.2.
