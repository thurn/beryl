  1. A hang with no bound. At 1b7e575 (08:08Z) the shots lane's beryl_cli record-fixture was stuck in _dyld_start (macOS sample: 0.10 s CPU in 42 minutes; amfid/syspolicyd never finished the exec). Neither by ci nor Enact's runner had a timeout, so master sat 13 commits behind, the run's own 919b829/190578a among them. I killed the process at 08:51Z and commented the diagnosis on Steward by-5f0.
    • Fixed in Enact: staging 0d0cbab, slow CI passed, now on master. The runner kills a slow check's process tree after [checks] slow_timeout (default 3600 s) and files a failure.
    • Filed by-2l8.17 (P1): a per-step deadline in by ci/by check at 2x budget that names the hung step, plus slow_timeout = 900.
  2. ebb0eec is red on ui-test, "Space during the opponent's turn shows the rest of it at once". That comes from the parallel UI epic: by-isv.23 reproduced it on 190578a. The vizier's Steward has by-5f0. It still blocks this run's delivery to master.

The tests themselves run in 11.5 s wall at load 36. The floor is longthink stops_cost_nothing_but_time at 11.5 s; next come the upgrade test (5.4 s) and no_arguments_start_or_resume (5.3 s). The parallel UI epic's pushes reached 61-81 s under load, which the fast check only warns about. No bead: there is no headroom, but nothing in this epic is over budget.
