Description

What to build

Plan: Designs G, "Skipping a turn".

  • Skipping a turn is a replacement of beginning it. The replacement pipeline (replace.rs, CR 614) already asks a player about an optional replacement in the middle of a step (engine/paused_step.rs); beginning a turn becomes an event it can replace. An extra turn that is skipped is used up; the player whose turn would follow takes it (State::begin_turn holds after_extra for the order after an extra turn).
  • "Doesn't untap during your untap step" as a rule change on the permanent, if it is not there (Governed), and "enters tapped".
  • Time Vault: enters tapped; doesn't untap; "if you would begin your turn while Time Vault is tapped, you may skip that turn instead. If you do, untap Time Vault"; tap for an extra turn (Effect::ExtraTurn exists).
  • Final Fortune: an extra turn, and "at the beginning of that turn's end step, you lose the game", a delayed trigger tied to one particular turn, which must not fire in another turn's end step if that turn is skipped or ended early (Time Stop and Sundial of the Infinite are in this lane: write the interaction scenarios).

Write each card with the card-writing skill. Read its rulings first (just card rulings <name>): the card joins data/rulings-ledger.json with every ruling a scenario or a reason, and joins the keystone fuzz pool (beryl_cards/src/decks.rs). Where XMage or Forge cannot play the card, its scenarios are reported as unsupported and the ledger is the outside check, so be thorough with it.

Scenarios: skip a turn to untap the Vault, then tap it for an extra turn; decline the skip; two extra turns queued (the later is taken first); Final Fortune then Sundial of the Infinite in the extra turn's end step, with the trigger on the stack; an extra turn taken by a controlled player (Mindslaver is implemented).

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Both cards pass just card check and are in the ledger and the pool.
