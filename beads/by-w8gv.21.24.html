The audit holds the stage's clock, waits for that on the log, then steps a frame of stage time at a go and measures the page each tick.
  4. Deviation: the data names no verb that draws a second visual. The allowance is on the settled entry 'Choosing how to cast' as twice: 'mode', and the audit lets the cards a mode prompt is about (PromptView.about) show again while it is open. by-w8gv.21.28 builds that prompt on the stage; nothing draws it yet, so the pass is held by a test of the judge with no browser (stage-audit.test.mjs) and by the data's test.
  5. Deviation: no 'stretch' under the stage. UI Conventions, Verbs, point: a line is 'drawn every moment from where its two ends are', so one that follows a gliding end is the design; that a line ends before a target leaves is the script's check.   6. Deviation: 'ui' findings (a button sliding in on the page's own time) are not judged under the stage: no step says them and stage time does not measure them. 'crowded' is judged per beat: more than three different moves beginning within 150 ms.

Gotchas.

  • The audit holds the stage's clock from watching(page) until the action returns: an action passed to probe.audit or spec.audit sets things going and waits for nothing the stage plays.
  • A moment is over when the last view heard is deciding, stopped or over, the stage shows it idle, and no answer sent since lacks its view (rest). A view left at "thinking" for good (a manual review's other seat) is owed for ever: settled gives up after LONGEST as before, the audit throws.
  • Nearly all an audit costs is the page measured each tick (about 15 ms under load; a full run is about 15 minutes with BERYL_UI_WORKERS=6). The stage's time is immune to load; the wait for views is not, and is bounded by LONGEST.
  • record's stepped mode leaves its hit-test rule (pointer-events on everything) in between ticks; unhit() takes it out before a click.
  • Do not edit ui/src while a specs run is under way: Vite serves the working tree.
