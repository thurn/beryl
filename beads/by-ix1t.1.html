Close reason

Gate reports both 400-run suite totals and a dropped row without a veto. Verified on Cygnus and delivered to staging as 6a4988c3.

Description

Part of the AI ladder epic by-ix1t. Read docs/ai-plan.md first: §2.2, §4.3, §4.4 and §6.1.

just gate compares the blunder suite for the champion and a challenger. Today it prints GATE: PASS or GATE: FAIL from per-entry marks, and a FAIL stops the rung. That veto discarded hold (one entry at 17 of 20 against 20) and combat (two entries at 16 and 17 of 20 against 0 and 1, short of the 18 a claim needed). The session that runs the gate now judges the table itself, so the tool must show it the right numbers and decide nothing.

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/gate.rs: main, Round, Score, Mark (first_mark, is_close, rerun_mark), compare, judge and their tests.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/blunders.rs: SEEDS (5), BUDGETS (3M and 30M), run, Report, EntryReport.
  • rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/ladder/report.rs: row, which prints a docs/ladder.md row.
  • justfile: the comment above the gate recipe.

Build

  1. The gate runs both agents from 10 seeds (seeds 0 to 9) at both budgets: 20 runs an entry, 400 a suite. Leave just blunders's own default at 5 seeds.
  2. Remove the rerun round and the marks claimed-fixed, re-opened and worse, with first_mark, is_close, rerun_mark and judge.
  3. Print one line an entry: its name, the champion's runs passed of 20, the challenger's, the difference, claim if --claims names it, and look if the two counts differ by six or more in either direction.
  4. Then print each agent's total of 400, the count of entries each has fixed (19 or more of 20), and a ready docs/ladder.md row in the format row prints, with - for budget, games, score and Elo, and the verdict dropped. The session uses that row only if it drops the rung before a match.
  5. Print no GATE: line. Exit 0 unless an entry drifted, which stays an error.
  6. Keep saving both reports under $BERYL_CACHE/ladder/gates/.
  7. Replace the tests of the removed marks with tests of the totals, the look threshold and the dropped row. Update the usage string and the justfile comment.

It takes about an hour on Cygnus for both agents, twice what it took.

Acceptance criteria

just gate FAMILY --claims none prints per-entry counts of 20 for both agents, both totals of 400, both fixed counts and a dropped row, with no GATE line, and exits 0. just test-rust and just check pass.
