Description

What is missing

by-gdn3.3 added a machine-wide pool of CPU tokens (tools/src/beryl_tools/tokens.py). Heavy check steps, just test-rust, fuzz and exhaust take tokens from it and run with as many threads as they got. Its acceptance asks for before and after figures, but the after figures need the pool to have run under real load for a while.

Before the pool (jarvis, last 24 h of $BERYL_CACHE/ci/runs.jsonl up to 2026-11-13 13:40 UTC, estimated from step spans): 169 fast-check runs, median 134 s, p90 342 s, median load average 29. The epic's 3-day figures: median 95 s, p90 240 s.

Change

Once the pool has been on staging for at least 24 hours with 5 or more worktrees active, compute the same figures. Group runs.jsonl rows with script=check and host JARVIS.local by tree. The run's wall time is the latest (time + seconds) minus the earliest (time - seconds). Take the median of each run's load. Also run just cpu sample for one hour and just cpu report, and record the load average next to the cores in use. Watch just tokens for waits by the push class.

Done when

Before and after figures are recorded on this bead and on by-gdn3.3. If the median fast check got worse or the load average stayed well above 18, comment the cause and file a fix (for example a larger push reserve in tokens.ceiling, or a change to HEAVY_STEPS). The figures are reported only, never gated (they are measured on a loaded machine).
