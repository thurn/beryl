Close reason

Primary pointer gestures folded into one Gesture enum with one end_gesture; three pointer tests added

Description

Symptom class: a press that should do one thing does two, or a gesture that should have ended keeps acting, because the primary pointer's gestures are separate fields that are each reset by hand.

Current design. Client (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/client/mod.rs) keeps one independent Option per gesture of the primary pointer:

  • pressed: Option<ElementId> (a press on a flat-layer element, client/input.rs press_flat),
  • slider: Option<ElementId> (client/input.rs slide),
  • scroll_drag: Option<(u8, ElementId, Vec2)> (a finger scrolling the flat layer, client/input.rs scroll_layer),
  • carried_item: Option<carry::Carry> (an order prompt's item carried along its row, client/carry.rs),
  • viewing.down: Option<Pile> (a press on a pile that opens its viewer on release, client/viewing.rs and client/input.rs press_flat). Only one of these can be meaningful at a time, but every combination is representable, so the code keeps them apart by hand: self.pressed = None appears in client/input.rs (menu dismiss, Cancel/Leave, scroll drag move), client/carry.rs, client/bug.rs, client/manual.rs and client/table_pointer.rs, and hover_flat draws self.pressed.or(self.slider) as held. Client::point (client/input.rs) decides the gesture by a chain of early returns (table_pointer || scroll_layer || open_table_menu || carry_item, then slide, then press_layer), and each branch must remember to clear the others.

Why now. by-q44s.7.22 ("Handle phone taps, holds and drags in the scene") adds taps, 500 ms holds, a 6-point move threshold and drag pickups for touch, and must "cancel all pending state on cancel/leave or layout/screen change". With five loose fields that cancel is five more hand resets in each new path. Doing this first makes that bead add variants instead of resets.

Proposed design. One field for what the primary pointer is doing since it went down:

enum Gesture { None, Press(ElementId), // released on the same element presses it Slider(ElementId), Scroll { finger: u8, id: ElementId, last: Vec2 }, Carry(carry::Carry), Pile(Pile), // released on the same pile opens its viewer }

Set on Down by the branch that claims the pointer, matched on Move/Up, and replaced by Gesture::None in one place on Up, Cancel, Leave, a menu dismiss, a screen change and a resize (Client::end_gesture). hover_flat reads the held element from the gesture. Leave the table's held card (Show::pointer, beryl_scene src/pointer) and the manual table's touch hold (manual::Manual) where they are unless folding them in is a net reduction; say which in the handoff.

What it eliminates: the six scattered self.pressed = None resets and the possibility of a slider and a press, or a scroll drag and a carried item, being live at once.

Behavior that must not change: every existing test in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/tests/all (widgets.rs, flat_layer.rs, prompts tests, the order prompt's carry tests, the viewer tests) and just b3d test pass unchanged; Client::frame still allocates nothing (the allocation gate).

Acceptance test (black box, in beryl_scene tests/all): a mouse Down on a slider, Move off it onto a button, Up on the button presses nothing and leaves the slider at the level under the last Move; and a touch Down on a scrolling list row, a Move of more than one point, then Up on the same row presses nothing. Both must pass before and after (they pin the current behavior), and a new test that sends Cancel mid-carry leaves no gesture so a following Down on a button and Up on it presses it once.

Run: just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

The five gesture fields of Client are one enum reset in one place; the three pointer tests described pass; just b3d lane passes.

Handoff: Client's five gesture fields (pressed, slider, scroll_drag, carried_item, viewing.down) are now one Gesture enum in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/client/gesture.rs. Its variants are None, Press(Release), Slider, Scroll{finger,id,last,release} and Carry{carry,release}, where Release is Element(id) or Pile(viewed). Deviation from the plan: a touch that goes down on a scroller still starts as a press, and the bead's 'only one at a time' assumption was false, so Scroll and Carry carry an Option<Release> (cleared on the first scroll move or carry lift) instead of a separate Pile variant. Endings: Client::point calls end_gesture before a primary Down and after an Up/Cancel/Leave has passed through every branch. show_screen ends it when (screen, sheet) changes, which replaced the reset in bug.rs, and resize ends it too. hover_flat draws nothing held on an ending phase. Behavior changes, both deliberate: (1) a touch Down on a slider or order item inside a scroller now goes to the slider or carry, because the latest branch to claim the pointer wins, where before the scroll took the moves; (2) any finger lifting ends a Scroll, where before only its own finger did. Left alone: Show::pointer (the table's held card) and manual::Manual's touch hold. Folding them in would not have shrunk the code, since they have their own timers and phases. Tests: tests/all/gesture.rs (slider drag released on the settings sheet's close button, touch scroll over a list, Cancel mid-carry). The bead mentioned existing order-prompt carry tests, but there were none; the carry test here is the first at the client level. just b3d lane passed.
