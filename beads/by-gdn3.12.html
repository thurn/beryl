Removing a worktree kills the push's own pipe reader

Close reason

reap_processes spares the caller's process group (enact 82bfc519)

Description

What we see

Running enact push --bead X | python3 -c '...' from inside the bead's worktree ended with exit 143 and no JSON: the push landed, then remove_worktree (via reap_processes) ended every process whose working directory was inside the worktree, including the python3 reading the push's output. The push then hit a broken pipe writing its result. Agents commonly pipe enact push into jq or Python.

process_ancestors in ~/enact/scripts/enact.py excludes the push's ancestors, but not siblings in the same pipeline.

Change

Also exclude processes in the push's own process group (os.getpgid) on POSIX, and on Windows the processes sharing the push's parent, from processes_inside/reap_processes. A leftover background process from the bead (the original reason for reaping, a fuzz run) runs in a different group, so it is still ended.

Done when

A unit test in tests/test_unit.py (see ReapProcessesTest) shows a process in the caller's group with its cwd inside the tree surviving reap_processes, while one in another group is ended. Read ~/enact/AGENTS.md first.
