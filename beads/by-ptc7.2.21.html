Description

Forge's exporter cannot play Gurmag Angler can delve while escaping. Beryl and XMage pass the scenario with an Underworld Breach, one Swamp and nine other cards in the graveyard. tools/src/beryl_tools/forge.py casting_option looks for the bit in the spell's printed casting options, and cannot describe Castings::ESCAPE granted by Breach. Two generated Breach scenarios with the same gap are listed in data/oracle-exporter-gaps.json. Teach the exporter and harness to select the granted escape mode and pay both the chosen delve and escape exiles.

Acceptance criteria

Forge runs and passes Gurmag Angler can delve while escaping and the affected Underworld Breach escape cases. Remove the two supported generated gaps and rerun just card check for Gurmag Angler and Underworld Breach.
