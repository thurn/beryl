Description

Cards

  • Dandân, 4/1: "Islandwalk. Dandân can't attack unless defending player controls an Island. When you control no Islands, sacrifice Dandân."
  • Lord of Atlantis, 2/2: "Other Merfolk get +1/+1 and have islandwalk."

Dandân holds the word "Island" in a keyword's parameter, a restriction on attacking and a state trigger's condition. It is the creature of the DanDân format, a later target of the project, in which Mind Bend and Crystal Spray are the removal: changing "Island" to another land type makes it unable to attack and then sacrifices it.

What they need

  • Landwalk with a word (by-bajq.1.6, by-bajq.5.2).
  • An attack restriction: a rule change about the permanent with the ability, "can't attack unless [condition read for the defending player]". Governed gains attacking; the check is in combat.rs where an attacker is offered, for each player or planeswalker it could attack (CR 508.1c).
  • The state trigger of by-bajq.1.3, with a condition that counts permanents by a land type word.
  • Lord of Atlantis: a static ability over a filtered set that excludes its own permanent ("other"), giving a P/T bonus and a keyword with a parameter. It affects every player's Merfolk. Two Lords pump each other.

Scenarios

Dandân attacks a player with an Island and not one without; Mind Bend changing "Island" to "Swamp" on Dandân: it now has swampwalk, can attack only a player with a Swamp, and is sacrificed if its controller has no Swamp; Crystal Spray doing the same until end of turn; Mind Bend on Lord of Atlantis.

Files

The two card files, beryl_card/src/continuous.rs, beryl_rules/src/{combat.rs, rule_change.rs, sba.rs}, beryl_kernel/src/rule_changes.rs.
