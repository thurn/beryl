Close reason

lane runs b3d test (6 s CPU, lane 21 s), sweep runs every-tick (9 s); both pass on staging

Description

The interface tests (just b3d test, beryl_inspect's runner module) are meant to run before every push of a Beryl 3D change, as a step of the beryl3d lane, and on every tick in the Beryl 3D sweep (docs/beryl-3d.md 'Test speed' and 'Checks and isolation'). Neither step exists yet, because on 2026-11-15:

  • 1,795 of the 7,336 tests fail (client panics, no-jumps, step-shown, rest-placement, and blank-card on the stress boards). by-q44s.3.8 lists them in data/beryl3d/test-faults.json so that just b3d test passes with the faults it lists.
  • The whole run takes about 29 minutes on the clock in the debug build, far over the fast check's 180 s step limit, and stress-1024 checked on every tick would pass the sweep's 2-hour hang limit (HANG_LIMIT_S in tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/sweep.py). by-q44s.1.53 brings the cost down.

When both have landed:

  • Add Step("test", [*cargo, "test"], paths.REPO) to steps() in tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/lane.py, after gate.
  • Add SweepStep("every-tick", (*BY, "b3d", "test", "--every-tick")) to OWN in tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/sweep.py, with a comment saying it is the exhaustive form of the lane's step.
  • Add both to docs/checks.md ('Beryl 3D' and 'The Beryl 3D sweep') and say in docs/beryl-3d.md 'Test speed' that they run.
  • Run just b3d lane and just b3d sweep --step every-tick --dry-run, and give both times with the load in a comment. If the run still does not fit the fast check's budget in docs/checks.md, do not cut tests or raise the budget: comment the numbers on by-q44s.1.53 or a bead it filed, and leave this bead blocked on that.

Acceptance criteria

just b3d lane runs test and passes on staging within the fast check's budget; the sweep has an every-tick step that passes within its hang limit; docs/checks.md and docs/beryl-3d.md name both; the times are in a comment.
