Description

Code: beryl_scene src/verbs/announce.rs and dwell.rs (stubs), src/notice.rs (NoticeRun, from by-q44s.3.2), src/layout (Layer::float, Layer::set_float_look, floats::{BANNER, SCRY_PILL, COIN}, from by-q44s.3.3), src/element/mod.rs (text, row), src/client.rs, src/play/mod.rs. Web: ui/src/stage/notices.ts (announced: which notices are passing; scryWords: "Scry: 1 top, 1 bottom."; endWords; stepAnnouncement), ui/src/game/TurnBanner.tsx (TurnAnnouncement: "Your Turn" or "Opponent's Turn" across the middle of the table, in the player's color or the opponent's; NoticeSlot), ui/src/stage/overlay.ts (Overlay::passed: until when something passes over the table; sweepBox), ui/src/stage/shown.ts (NoticeSurface). Protocol: Notice in rust/crates/beryl_protocol/src/script/act.rs. Cases of announce: turn banner, scry pill, coin; of dwell: its plain value and "game's end".

Build:

  • verbs/announce.rs pushes a NoticeRun for the step (StepOut::notice). Show keeps the runs under way on real time, as it keeps effects, in a list with room for 8; a notice outlives its script when its step passes.
  • Client::frame shows each notice under way as a float and hides it when its run ends: Notice::Turn: floats::BANNER, the words "Your Turn" or "Opponent's Turn" in the title style, centred in the window, fading in over the first 0.15 of the run and out over the last 0.2. Notice::Scry: floats::SCRY_PILL for that player, the words scryWords gives, above the player's anchor. Notice::TopOrBottom: "Top" or "Bottom" in the same pill. Notice::Coin: floats::COIN, the words "You won the toss" or "You lost the toss" centred. Notice::Roll, Shuffle, Rewind, Phase, TableTurn and Game: the banner with the words announced and stepAnnouncement give for each. The words are the web's, exactly. Elements are made in handle when the update arrives (one for each notice of its script), and frame only sets looks.
  • Show::passed(&self) -> u64: the real time by which every notice under way is over, which by-q44s.3.32 gives beat as its passed.
  • dwell: nothing moves and the step holds its beat for its duration, as now; set it BUILT, and give its subject the FOCUSED state bit for the step, so the tree shows what is being read.

Do not build: the step banner over the battlefield (StepBanner.tsx), which is milestone 3's (by-q44s.5.1, item 6), or the rosettes and smoke of the web's banner, which are art.

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/notices.rs: a script whose first beat announces the turn has a text "Your Turn" in the tree from its step's start to its end, centred within 1 logical point, and none after; the banner is still shown while the next beat's card flies; a scry pill is above the right player's anchor from both seats; two notices at once do not overlap (agent::checks::overlap finds nothing); Frame::work.layout_nodes is 0 in every frame of a notice after the first; Show::passed is the banner's end; a frame during a banner allocates nothing.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d tree verbs-zones --at step:<an announce> ; just b3d check verbs-zones; just b3d lane.
