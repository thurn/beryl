What the player saw: casting Abhorrent Oculus, after picking six graveyard cards in the tray, several cards crossed the table (and one sat on the graveyard) as empty dark rectangles. Acceptance: (1) every card exiled by a multi-card cost shows its face from the tray to the exile pile; (2) no card in the source or destination pile is drawn as a bare edge with nothing over it; (3) both seats, desktop and phone.

Cause: a card under a pile's top two is drawn as only its paper edge (Dress.rim, .pile-card-rim, background #0b0a09). The stage dressed each card as it would finally rest as soon as its place changed. Three paths: (a) tray cards waiting for their own flight after the tray's end lift took the graveyard's rim dress at once (stage.ts flying/waits branch); (b) a card already in flight took the rim dress when a later card set off for the same pile; (c) a card whose own flight ended under another (carried() kept the face only for lift). Also the graveyard counted waiting tray cards as on it, so a card left there was a rim with nothing over it.

Fix (ui/src/stage): enter.ts coverOf keeps the shown face until the track ends; tracks.ts shownUntil applies to every move (face-down still only for lift, other moves turn over on the way); stage.ts #lay treats track.waits cards as leaving; #draw scales by the cover's frame (on the phone a covered card was drawn ~3x too large, which the verb suite caught).

Reproduced: report fixture plus card records from beryl_cli serve, served with real art via ui-live, desktop and phone. Before: Reprieve/Island/Thirst/Stock Up blank mid-flight. After: 0 frames with an exposed rim in 20 samples on each layout.
