Description

Card

Mindslaver, legendary artifact: "{4}, {T}, Sacrifice this artifact: You control target player during that player's next turn."

What is wrong today

Decision::player is both the player a decision belongs to and the player who answers it. The rollout loop, every agent, the session, the saves and the UI assume they are one.

What to build

Plan: Designs 8, "Who answers" (CR 724); decision R12.

  1. Decision gains decider. The size assertion on Decision<Small> (312 bytes) says whether a spare byte exists; report it.
  2. A controlled turn. The effect is an Effects entry on the controlled player naming the controller, which waits for that player's next turn that they actually take (CR 724.1). State::begin_turn makes it the turn's controller, and it ends with the turn. State::ask is the one place a Decision gets its two players.
  3. What the controller sees (CR 724.4): everything the controlled player could see. Observation for a decider joins both players' knowledge for the length of the turn, and nothing the controller learns that way is forgotten after it.
  4. What the controller cannot do: make the player concede (CR 724.6). The controller makes every other choice, payments included, and the controlled player's mana abilities and resources are theirs.
  5. Search and agents. next's caller asks the agent of the decider. In a tree, a node belongs to the decider and is valued for the decider. The greedy rollout policy, asked to decide for a player it is not, picks what its scoring ranks lowest for that player. That is enough for "legal and never stalls" (decision R14); nothing here is a strength bar.
  6. Session and saves. The human, as controller, is shown the other player's hand and answers their prompts; as the controlled player, watches. A save records both players of each answer, with a migration (beryl_session::migrate) in which older answers have decider equal to player. The frozen corpus must replay.

Scenarios

Mindslaver on the opponent: the controller casts the opponent's spells at the opponent's own permanents and attacks into their own blockers; the control ends with the turn; an extra turn taken first (the next turn the player takes is the controlled one).

Files

The card's file, beryl_kernel/src/{decision.rs, pending.rs, turn.rs, observation.rs, effects.rs}, beryl_rules/src/{engine.rs, turn.rs}, beryl_search/src/ (the rollout loop, greedy, MCTS nodes), beryl_session/src/ (sessions, save, migrate, view), ui/src/ only where the view's new field must be shown.

Decisions and deviations from the plan:

  • The decider is set where next() holds the decision (beryl_rules engine::controlled, cold), not in State::ask or each builder: State::ask takes a Pending, and setting it inside the decision function moved each 320-byte decision once more (+1.4% count). Inline answer sources asked mid-resolution go through resolve::answer_controlled.
  • CR 724.4 knowledge is write-through (State::sharer): look, a card entering its owner's hand, a face-down permanent's controller, and forget_hand tell the controller too; begin_turn merges the active player's knowledge as the controlled turn starts. A join inside State::knows cost ~0.15% of the count.
  • Greedy policy deciding for another player: RolloutPolicy::worst collects the policy's scores into a stack list and takes the lowest. A Pick::Worst variant changed inlining of Pick::choose/best (+0.3%).
  • Scenario actions are the decider's (XMage convention); the trace exports Position::controller and both oracle exporters report a controlled turn as unsupported (follow-up by-bajq.9.7).
  • Save format v12 (staging took v11): Described::decider, absent = player; migrate::v11_to_v12 is version-only.
  • Turn and Decision hash the new field only when it differs, so the benchmark corpora pins (corpus_never_changes, family_corpora_never_change) hold; rollouts_never_change unchanged.
  • Rulings not shown by scenarios: skipped turns (no skip effect exists) and extra turns (none exist; follow-up by-bajq.9.6); concede (session-level, engine::concede unchanged); sideboard/outside-game (not modeled).
