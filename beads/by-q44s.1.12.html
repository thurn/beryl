Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Performance gates' ('Finding the worst frame'); docs/benchmarking.md ('Instruction counts: Gungraun', and 'The present-cost gate', which toggles Callgrind's collection inside one function); rust/crates/beryl_bench (benches/rollout_counts.rs) and tools/src/beryl_tools/bench.py (count_gate) as the example to copy. Nothing of Beryl 3D's goes in beryl_bench or count-gate.toml. The spike bead made the virtual machine beryl3d-bench and the tools package's wrapper for it.

  • rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_inspect/benches/frame_counts.rs, Linux only like the engine's Gungraun benchmarks. For each gated board: a benchmark of the whole playback (the total), and one that collects only inside a wrapper, frame_counted, which the 8 frames from rank go through and Callgrind's --toggle-collect names (the worst frame).
  • A test on the stress boards counts every frame of a short stretch and requires the true worst frame to be among the 8.
  • beryl_scene and beryl_inspect build for the machine's arm64 Linux with no GPU.
  • Limits in data/beryl3d/gates.json under frames.<name>: worst_frame_instructions, total_instructions and tolerance_percent of 1.0. When a limit may rise is the epic's rule 8 and the doc's 'What it is held to'; write the same two reasons into rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. It needs nobody's approval.
  • just b3d gate --counts runs it in beryl3d-bench. It is a step of the sweep, not of the lane and not of slow CI.
  • Run it twice and require identical counts.
  • Set the limits from the first measurement. The doc's targets are 2,000,000 instructions for the worst frame of stress-255 and 4,000,000 for stress-1024. If the measurement is over a target, do not raise the target: file a bead under milestone 1 to bring the count down, and record the measured number on the epic.

Run: just b3d gate --counts.

Acceptance criteria

The gate runs in beryl3d-bench and as a step of the sweep; running it twice gives identical counts; a planted quadratic loop over the cards fails the worst-frame limit while the total stays within its tolerance; beryl_bench and count-gate.toml are unchanged.
