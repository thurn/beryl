Description

Implement the bounded turn search designed in by-97g.31. In the WU Control mirror, exact exploration had a median of 3,490.5 distinct leaves, with a 95th-percentile lower bound of 95,727. Exhaustive exploration cannot fit the decision budget. Read the design field of by-97g.31 and docs/ai-strategy.md sections 4 and 9.

Start in rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs at Method, Family parsing and display, LongSearch's root search, keys, and the accounting used by Tree::spent. Put the frontier and scoring in their own module. Reuse the hidden-world dealing, existing rollout and guard, combat::Sim and Plan, and Widening. Add a budget-aware combat entry point so nested planning is charged and can yield at a plan boundary. Use kernel State::hash with exact equality within a single world and observation-keyed continuations across worlds. Start from own-turn empty-stack priority. Return one legal Choice, and replan when asked again. Preserve PIMC on all other roots. Keep the champion unchanged.

The first family replaces pimc72 with turn72 and retains the other champion settings. Use the measured design's proposal/scoring split and frontier caps as initial defaults. Carry an explicit pass line and track payable responses with the engine's mana solver. Log work spent on proposals and scoring, completed and merged plans, and fallback counts so the gate result can be interpreted.

Acceptance criteria
