Description

Seen after by-drghs's delve fix (bbf80243) let just fuzz go past game 2409: random_games_keep_the_invariants panics in game 5842 with "an engine invariant broke: Violation { what: \"a public card unknown\", at: Some(Slot(75)) }" (engine.rs assert_sound after next).

What happens: on turn 22/24 P0's Abhorrent Oculus makes P1 manifest dread; slot 75 (a Swamp from P1's library) enters face down (FaceIx::DOWN), known only to P1. On turn 25 P1 casts Gemrazer for its mutate cost targeting slot 75 and chooses to put it on top. State::merge (rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/mutate/merge.rs) keeps slot 75 as the merged permanent and become_copy turns it face up with Gemrazer's values, but the card behind slot 75 is still the hidden Swamp, known only to P1. The invariant in rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/invariants.rs (~line 140) requires every face-up object in a public zone to be known by all players.

Rules: CR 731.2e "If a merged permanent contains face-up and face-down components, the permanent's status is determined by its topmost component." The Swamp stays a face-down component, hidden from P0 until it leaves the battlefield (CR 708.9). So revealing it would be wrong; the representation (one slot that is both the permanent and its original card) needs a way to keep a face-down component hidden under a face-up top, and the invariant and leave-the-battlefield reveal (708.9) must agree with it. Also check copiable.rs merged_with (already skips a face-down face's abilities) and the checkpoint code that refuses merged permanents.

Repro: BERYL_FUZZ_GAMES=5843 CARGO_PROFILE_RELEASE_DEBUG_ASSERTIONS=true CARGO_PROFILE_RELEASE_OVERFLOW_CHECKS=true cargo test --release -p beryl_search --test games -- --include-ignored random_games_keep_the_invariants (about 4 minutes). A deterministic test: a Gemrazer scenario mutating on top of a manifested card (Abhorrent Oculus or any manifest card), asserting P0 does not know the hidden card and the game stays sound. Add a docs/bugs.md entry.
