Description

Found by by-w8gv.21.74, which made the script builder read simultaneity from the engine's groups (Tag.together) alone. Two engine actions move one card per pick, each its own group, where the rules move them at once, so their scripts now show one beat per card where the old order-reading guess showed one beat (the old script was right by the rules):

  1. A counted exile cost (Abhorrent Oculus: exile six cards from your graveyard). rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/cast/parts.rs perform() Measure::Count calls pay_with -> event::move_one per pick, between ChooseObject decisions. CR 601.2h: the player pays the total cost; one cost's cards are exiled together.
  2. Effect::PutBack (Brainstorm: put two cards from your hand on top). beryl_rules/src/library.rs put_back() moves each card after its pick. CR 401.4: 'If an effect puts two or more cards in a specific position in a library at the same time, the owner of those cards may arrange them in any order.'

A group cannot stay open across a decision (beryl_kernel/src/cause.rs Scopes; by-w8gv.21.72 handoff), so the fix is likely to keep the picks (in Casting / the resolution's registers) and move the cards as one batch after the last pick (event::move_batch_to marks a batch one group). Check rollouts_never_change (rust/crates/beryl_bench/src/rollout.rs), the corpus (cargo test -p beryl_session --test corpus, no data/save-breaks.json entry) and just count-gate (bar 389,343,295, count-gate.toml unchanged).
