Description

This bead is the running log for the scheduled routine "Beryl: CI structural review" (~/.claude/scheduled-tasks/beryl-ci-steward-watch/SKILL.md). The routine runs every 4 hours. Each run looks at the whole CI system (the fast check, slow CI, worktree setup, caches, CPU sharing between sessions, the beads the checks file, Windows on Cygnus) and makes one structural improvement to the total time and workflow, rather than fixing beads one at a time.

Each run adds one comment here containing:

  • what it measured (the numbers and how it got them),
  • the bottleneck it chose and why it was the largest,
  • what it changed (commits) or filed (beads labeled ci-structure),
  • before and after numbers, where it has them,
  • what the next run should look at.

Beads for structural CI work carry the label ci-structure. Closing this bead ends nothing: keep it open.

Starting point (2026-11-14): by-npih makes slow CI the only writer of $BERYL_CACHE/target. When the cache was missing third-party crates, the first test build in a fresh worktree took 148 s wall and 1,158 CPU-seconds. With a warm cache, the same build took 13 s. Most "Audit slow steps" beads (check-budget) since 2026-11-13 were symptoms of this cold cache.
