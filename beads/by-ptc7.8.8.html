Description

What to build

Plan: Designs I. docs/rules-coverage.md says "Not built: transforming permanents, which the engine does not yet turn". actions/transform.rs can only put a card onto the battlefield with its back face up (return_transformed, for Jace, Vryn's Prodigy).

  • actions::transform (CR 701.27 and 712): the permanent's face changes in place. It is the same object with the same incarnation, counters, attachments and damage; it gets no new timestamp (CR 613.7g or the clause that says so in the pinned release: cite it); effects that apply to it keep applying. A kernel mutator sets the face (State::turn_face_up is the nearest model, mutate.rs). Effect::Transform, with rendering and a mutation neighbour. An instruction to transform a permanent that is not a transforming double-faced card does nothing; a permanent told to transform twice by one ability's resolution transforms once where the rule says so (CR 701.27f).
  • actions::flip (CR 710): a flip card's permanent becomes flipped. It reads its second face's characteristics while flipped; flipping is one-way, and it stays flipped while it remains on the battlefield.
  • The layers recompute when a face changes (Inputs bits for the fields a filter can read: the face is one).
  • Delver of Secrets // Insectile Aberration: at the beginning of your upkeep, look at the top card of your library; you may reveal it; if an instant or sorcery card is revealed this way, transform Delver.
  • Erayo, Soratami Ascendant // Erayo's Essence: "whenever the fourth spell of a turn is cast, flip Erayo" (the spells-cast tracker counts per player: this is the turn's fourth by anyone); flipped, a legendary enchantment that counters the first spell each opponent casts each turn.

Write each card with the card-writing skill. Read its rulings first (just card rulings <name>): the card joins data/rulings-ledger.json with every ruling a scenario or a reason, and joins the keystone fuzz pool (beryl_cards/src/decks.rs). Where XMage or Forge cannot play the card, its scenarios are reported as unsupported and the ledger is the outside check, so be thorough with it.

Scenarios: Delver reveals an instant and transforms, keeps its counters and an Aura; does not reveal; a copy of Insectile Aberration (the copy cannot transform); Erayo flips on the fourth spell and the fifth is not countered but an opponent's next first spell is; a flipped Erayo bounced and recast is unflipped; the legend rule with two.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

Both cards pass just card check and are in the ledger and the pool. A transformed permanent is the same object: a scenario shows its counters and Aura staying.
