Description

Read docs/beryl-3d.md “The table” (camera and Objects and placement), “Motion the pointer drives” and “Sizes”. just video list on dereks-macbook-pro found no local Arena recordings. Fit the required table rectangle to Beryl's web phone layout at 393×759. Use a fixed pitch and vertical field of view, starting from the desktop fit, then fit distance against the existing Camera::fit/required_fits contract and the phone safe area and reserved bands. Measure the projected bounds against the usable battlefield rectangle from phoneLayout with an empty stack at that size. Record the target and measured bounds in the handoff; each projected edge must agree within 4 logical points. When the stack opens, reserve its middle band in placement while preserving camera orientation and distance for the same window.

In beryl_scene src/table add phone.rs for pure placement metrics and phone-specific geometry. Expose phone bands to the game screen through a pure function of Window and the current view (coordinates in logical points from the window origin, including safe insets). Route Table::resize and all of its rest paths (placement, placement_of, placement_reusing and place_group in scene.rs) through geometry consistent with Window’s layout selector. Preserve Table::new, desktop constants and scroll/rest caches. Reuse Camera::fit and Camera::on_table, place.rs battlefield_row, rows.rs lay_row and least_strip, hands.rs hand, stack.rs place_stack and pile.rs pile_base/lay_pile. Keep portrait spatial values in data/beryl3d/space.json under layout.phone. Preserve the single ID for each object, stack abilities, hidden picks, attached and merged cards, and animation continuity through Show::resize. Keep all placement and layout preparation off the frame path.

Web references: ui/src/mobile/layout.ts phoneLayout, sideRows, placeRow, placeCreatures, placeHand, pileSpot and backSpot give the bands and zone placement; sizing.ts phoneFrame and creatureHeight give the column width and size floors. ui/src/stage/phone.ts rest, anchors, phoneTray and phoneRevealed supply the phase-aware bands, animation endpoints, tray and reveal spaces. PhoneStageBoard.tsx PhoneStageBoard uses those bands for both sides and bottom prompts. Put both players' rows, hands and piles and the centered stack in their phone bands. Use the 44-point projected touch strip, scroll oversized rows in groups after existing stacking, and keep the top hand edges reachable with the hand partly offscreen. This bead owns table geometry, including strips; screen functions and gesture semantics are separate.

Tests in beryl_scene tests/all/table.rs and scroll.rs: safe required rectangle in all three portrait sizes, 393×759 edge agreement, non-overlapping bands and tapped footprints, spell and ability stack ordering, both hands and each visible pile, reachable grouped and scrolled rows, no one-frame jump when resized mid-script, desktop place/scroll regressions. Exercise stress-255, stress-hand, stress-stack and verbs-zones at phone sizes and confirm card faults for on-screen/reachable are absent. Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d test b3d:verbs-zones --phone-only; just b3d tree b3d:verbs-zones/you/393x759 --at end. Use just b3d check for the stress fixtures at each phone size and record the table-rule results even if UI controls still fault.

Decisions: (1) Two battlefield rows per side on the phone (creatures; lands-left/others-right back row), not the web's three bands: the web's lands and others bands are 29 and 42 pt tall at 393x759, too small for the 44-pt target. (2) Phone pitch 10 deg, not the desktop's 27 (fov kept 24.29): at 27 deg, or with a 100 mm middle, a tapped card in the far row is 44-45 pt tall at 375x647, under the 52 pt least_strip needs (44 + 2 hit cells). (3) The middle band is permanent (50 mm: 44 pt at 375x647, 59 at 393x759) instead of rows moving apart when the stack opens; the stack's 68-pt items overhang the creature rows by up to 25 pt. The camera never moves while the window keeps its shape, and no row moves when a spell is cast. (4) PhoneBands depend on the window only, since the bands do not change with the view.
