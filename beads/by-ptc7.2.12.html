Description

Containment Priest exiles an uncast Clever Impersonator even when its controller would have it enter as a noncreature artifact. CR 616.1c puts the copy replacement ahead of the Priest; CR 614.12 examines the characteristics as the permanent would exist on the battlefield. In rust/crates/beryl_rules/src/replace.rs, moving calls destination before entering, so the Priest redirects the creature using its uncopied characteristics before copy_to_enter can ask. A behavior-preserving fix must handle choosing/declining the copy, merged entry characteristics, collecting the remaining replacements and replaying choices without retaining copied values for a redirected object. Reproduction: in the Containment Priest card file add a scenario named a creature entering as a noncreature copy returns through Containment Priest. P0 controls an uncopied Clever Impersonator with one +1/+1 counter, Prop::Artifact and three Plains, and casts Flickerwisp. P1 controls the Priest. Flickerwisp exiles the Impersonator. At the end step, resolving the delayed ability leaves the Impersonator in exile and produces no ChooseUpTo decision. It should offer the copy; choosing Prop::Artifact should yield two artifacts and no Impersonator in exile.

Acceptance criteria

The stated scenario passes in Beryl and runs in XMage and Forge, using just card check Containment Priest. Test a copy that would remain a creature and a declined copy as well. Cite the rules in the engine, record the regression in docs/bugs.md, update ruling 8737e516e828 in the ledger, and verify the count gate before and after.
