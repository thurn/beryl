Enhanced renderer: 4-sample main pass resolved, flat layer after, stub shadow/light/bloom files, shader cost and frozen-shader tests (0309dfe7f)

Description

Read first: the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead', rules 11 and 12); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md; docs/beryl-3d.md 'The Baseline tier', 'The Enhanced tier', 'Effects' and 'What is counted'. Check wgpu's documentation for the version in the workspace's Cargo.lock before writing against it.

The Enhanced tier's renderer, with every pipeline it needs made at startup, drawing what the Baseline tier draws but multisampled. Its shading functions are stubs that the shadow map, lighting and bloom beads fill in, each in a file of its own, so that those three run side by side and never edit the same file. The Baseline shader, src/shader.wgsl, does not change.

What exists, in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_render: Renderer (src/renderer.rs) with pipelines: [RenderPipeline; 3] from pipeline::pipelines (src/pipeline.rs) for Pass::Opaque, Pass::Table and Pass::Layer (src/runs.rs); render_view, one render pass over runs(frame); Limits { Default, Downlevel }; FragmentCounter (src/count.rs); tests in tests/all (render.rs's helpers renderer, overhead, card, draw, close; downlevel.rs; shader_cost.rs).

Build, all in beryl_render:

  • src/enhanced/mod.rs, Enhanced: made by Renderer::new when the device is not made with Limits::Downlevel and the adapter supports four samples for the target format and DEPTH; None otherwise. Renderer::enhanced_available() -> bool. It holds the three pipelines again with multisample.count = 4 (SAMPLES: u32 = 4), drawn from the Enhanced shader module, and a bind group layout 1: binding 0 the uniform Lit (below), binding 1 a depth texture (the shadow map), binding 2 a comparison sampler. Until the shadow map bead lands, binding 1 is a 1 by 1 depth texture cleared to 1.0.
  • Renderer::render and render_view choose by frame.tier: Tier::Enhanced with Enhanced present draws through enhanced::render, everything else through today's code unchanged. A frame in Enhanced on a renderer without it is drawn in Baseline.
  • enhanced::render, in order: shadow::draw (src/enhanced/shadow.rs, a stub that records nothing); the main pass over draws[..frame.layer] into a four-sample color target and a four-sample DEPTH buffer made for the target's size (remade only when the size changes, as depth_view does), resolved into the frame's view; bloom::apply (src/enhanced/bloom.rs, a stub that records nothing); then the flat layer's records, draws[frame.layer..], in a second pass of one sample that loads the resolved picture, with the Baseline Pass::Layer pipeline. Drawn::draw_calls counts the calls of every pass.
  • Lit, 112 bytes: light_view_projection: mat4x4, key: vec4 (the direction toward the key light, w unused), eye: vec4 (the camera's position in table space), fill: vec4 (the fill light's color and strength). Written each frame from shadow::light_view_projection(frame) (stub: identity) and light::lighting(frame) (src/enhanced/light.rs, stub: key straight down, eye from the inverse of frame.view_projection, fill zero).
  • The Enhanced shader module is the source of shader.wgsl followed by src/enhanced/entry.wgsl, light.wgsl, shadow.wgsl and particle.wgsl, joined with concat!(include_str!(...)). entry.wgsl holds vs_lit (as vs, and also passing the world position, the record's normal, which is the transform's third column, and the position in the light's clip space) and fs_lit, which samples the face before branching as fs does, computes let shadow = lit_by_key(in.light_position);, then calls shade_card_lit(face, mesh_uv, tint, params, normal, view, shadow), shade_table_lit(tint, normal, view, shadow), shade_particle(sprite, tint, params) and the Baseline shade_ function for every other material. Stubs: in shadow.wgsl lit_by_key returns 1.0; in light.wgsl shade_card_lit and shade_table_lit return the Baseline function's color with its rgb times shadow. particle.wgsl's shade_particle is final, not a stub: the sprite's alpha times the tint, faded by 1 - age (params.x), premultiplied as shade_glow premultiplies. Name every Enhanced shading function shade_<material>_lit or shade_particle, so that the cost test finds them.
  • tests/all/shader_cost.rs: also parse the Enhanced source and count shade_card_lit, shade_table_lit and shade_particle as materials card_lit, table_lit and particle, adding the costs of every function each one calls, transitively, and of fs_lit's own expressions. The one-sample limit stays on the Baseline materials; an Enhanced material may take at most 5 samples (the face and four shadow map taps). Add the three entries to materials in data/beryl3d/gates.json from the numbers the test prints. A test, the_baseline_shader_does_not_change, holds the FNV-1a hash of shader.wgsl's bytes to a constant written in the test, with a comment that the Baseline shader is frozen by the Enhanced tier and that a deliberate change updates the constant in the same commit.
  • just b3d png and just b3d film honour --tier enhanced (the option is parsed in beryl_inspect src/cli/options.rs and read by nothing yet): wherever those commands make the client and the renderer (beryl_pictures and the headless host in beryl_host), hand the client Input::Tier from Options::tier before its first frame, and make the renderer with Limits::Default in Enhanced. The text commands keep printing the same tree in either tier.

Tests, in beryl_render/tests/all/enhanced.rs (a test that finds no Enhanced support on the adapter fails; this Mac's Metal has it):

  • the twenty-card frame of render.rs drawn in Enhanced has no validation error, and at the centres of five cards and two table points its pixels are within 4/255 of the same frame in Baseline;
  • a card turned 30 degrees, drawn without its edge strip record, has pixels along its edge that are neither the card's nor the table's color in Enhanced, and only those two colors in Baseline (the multisampling);
  • a frame in Tier::Enhanced on a renderer made with Limits::Downlevel draws, and its pixels equal Baseline's exactly;
  • the flat layer is drawn after the resolve: a panel record over a card has the same color in both tiers;
  • a particle record of Sprite::DISC at age 0 lights its centre and at age 1 lights nothing.

Must not build: the shadow map, lighting, bloom, particles' motion, choosing the tier, any limit for the Enhanced tier.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_render; just b3d png verbs-zones --at 1.5s --tier enhanced; just b3d lane.

Derek Thurn2026-11-17

Decisions: (1) Enhanced is made when wgpu::Limits::default().check_limits(&device.limits()) holds and the target format and DEPTH guarantee 4 samples with resolve; the app's window and phone devices use downlevel limits, so they get no Enhanced until the tier-choice bead asks for full limits there. (2) Lit sits at offset 256 of each slot's uniform buffer (now 368 bytes), so the mapped Metal path writes it with no blit; Enhanced keeps one group-1 bind group per slot (Slot::index, Slots::uniforms). The shadow map bead must rebuild Enhanced::groups when it replaces the 1x1 map. (3) shade_particle follows shade_glow exactly, alpha 0 (additive), reading 'premultiplied as shade_glow premultiplies' literally; the particles bead can switch to ordinary blending for ash if it looks wrong. (4) The edge test uses a one-color table-material record on the card mesh instead of a card face, because the placeholder face has text and is not one color. (5) driver/mod.rs was at 800 lines: moved moment_distance and offset_from to driver/moment.rs to make room for Page::tier/with_tier, which keeps the tier when a seek remakes the client. (6) beryl_inspect's own Tier enum is now a re-export of beryl_scene::input::Tier. (7) The pipeline-statistics counter wraps only the Baseline pass; Enhanced frames are not fragment-counted yet (the limits bead's concern).
