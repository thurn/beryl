Derek Thurn2026-11-12

M5 result (by-w8gv.9.18, staging 702bc036).

Cards as data: all 47 cards in CARDS are effect data only. No card has a Rust body; the escape hatch (beryl_rules' CustomBody and custom_body, over Game and Resolution, saying of itself what data would through CustomBody::SAYS) is kept compiled and run by six test bodies in beryl_rules' tests. EffectCtx, bodies!, conditions!, Condition::Custom, Ability::pauses/may_pause, Needs and TargetSpec::removal/counter are deleted.

Vocabulary entries the milestone added. Effect: CounterInto, Explore, FlipCoin, DealDamage, ChangeRule, MayCast (9.15); PutBack, ShuffleInto, Dig, Fateseal, Search, UntapUpTo, Delay, GetEmblem, Unless over a Cost (9.16); Choose, LookAt, ExileLinked, PutIntoGraveyard, ReturnTransformed, ManifestDread, CreateAttackingTokens, AddManaOfKind, GainFlashback, PermitCast (9.17); LoseGame (9.7). Objects: CardsIn, TriggeredOn, ExiledWithThis, InZoneOf. Players: EachOther. Amount: Life, Most, ThoseOf, Carried. Condition: Token, WonFlip. Terms: Recipients, LibraryPosition::TopOrBottom, CastTerms, Taken, LibraryOrder, Found, CopyException. Costs are parts (CostPart, 9.12); a Trigger is event x subject x Players (9.8); LayerSpec names Objects and a Condition (9.9).

One-card entries removed: the five one-card rule, replacement and modification entries became composed data (9.7: RuleChange numbered per game, WouldDraw with a condition and an effect, SetPower(&Amount)); Whose, Affected and StaticCondition went (9.9); Needs and Ability::needing went and data/card-names-allowlist.json's mechanics list is empty (9.17).

Instruction counts, just bench-count rollouts corpus: 389,908,157 at the milestone's start (by-w8gv.9.2's before), 388,980,405 now (-0.24%). Bar 389,343,295, never bumped.
