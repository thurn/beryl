Close reason

Restricted-mana payments replay in both oracles: Cavern 8/8 and Gurmag+Cavern pass on XMage and Forge

Description

Every Cavern of Souls scenario that pays with the Cavern's colored mana is Unsupported in both oracles, so the main path of mana that remembers (beryl_rules/src/mana/remembered.rs) has never been checked against XMage or Forge. The cause is in the scenario trace, not the engines: Beryl's trace records which permanents a payment tapped (taps) but not which mana ability or color, and both oracles ask for the color of Cavern's mana and whether its restricted mana pays. tools/src/beryl_tools/card_facts.py Cards.restricted_mana / color_asked and tools/src/beryl_tools/forge.py taps raise Unsupported for any such source; the XMage side skips at xmage.py (color_asked).

Record in the trace, for each tapped source of restricted mana, the color it made and whether that mana was the restricted kind (the payment code knows it: mana/pay.rs pay_with_memory has From::Restricted). Then have the XMage exporter and the Forge harness answer the color and restriction choices from it instead of refusing. Start from beryl_scenario/src/facts.rs and the oracle harnesses tools/oracles/BerylForgeHarness.java and tools/src/beryl_tools/xmage_java.py.

Acceptance criteria

just card check "Cavern of Souls" runs all four of its scenarios on XMage and Forge with none Unsupported for the restricted payment, and they agree or are recorded divergences. The Gurmag Angler scenario paid with Cavern's mana runs on Forge too (XMage's delve gap is by-bajq.7.7).
