Description

The game stage was a fixed 1600x900 scaled to fit, leaving bands on 16:10 (the default window, MacBook screens) and squarer windows and stranding the HUD mid-screen on ultrawide.

Decision: the stage stays 900 high and takes the window's shape between 1440 (16:10) and 2134 (21:9) wide (layout.ts STAGE_WIDTHS, Stage.tsx fitStage); beyond that the cloth bleeds as before. Positions drawn for the 1600 base hold to the left edge, the middle (fromMiddle) or the right edge (fromRight); CSS uses --shift / --shift-mid. At 1600x900 nothing moves, so shots and ui-test are unchanged. Least is 1440 because the choice overlay's 1000px tray plus 338px side column needs it; going lower means a narrower tray. Window minimum 1024x600 (tauri.conf.json).

CI: ui-shots checks each fixture shot at 1180x820 (narrowest stage) and 2560x1080 (widest), waiting for card transforms (capture.mjs still), and crowdedControls now flags a table fixture (step stop, orb, dock, corner button) colliding with a permanent or spell. Cost ~0 s against the 62 s baseline. It found lands reaching x 650 past the step stops at 646 (fixed: ORB_LEFT 646).
