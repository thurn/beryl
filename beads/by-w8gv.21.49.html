Description

just motion --film appear (the whole verb) and just motion --film appear "opening hand" die in node about 35 s in: "FATAL ERROR: Zone Allocation failed - process out of memory", inside JSON.stringify (v8 FastJsonStringifier), heap about 414 MB, after "filmed: desktop/yours/cast, 1 takes" for the whole verb and before any take for the case alone. just motion --film appear token still films in 55 s. Seen twice on 2026-11-11 on a branch rebased on staging 291d7767 that touches neither ui/scripts/verb-film.mjs nor ui/scripts/film-walk.mjs.

The moments appear watches since by-w8gv.21.39 are opening and piles (ui/verbs.json watches): the opening hands' takes are what the film cannot hold. Likely where: what film-walk.mjs's walk returns from the page for a moment (every node's pose at every tick, for a table of two whole libraries) or what verb-film.mjs writes as takes.json (its JSON.stringify at line 677).

It blocks the motion review of appear as a whole, which by-w8gv.21.47 needs for its second round, and of any verb whose moments are as large. Film a moment's takes without holding every tick of every node at once (only the nodes that move in a take, or a take at a time), and add a test or a run that films appear whole.

Files: ui/scripts/verb-film.mjs, ui/scripts/film-walk.mjs, tools/src/beryl_tools/motion_film.py, tools/tests/test_motion_film.py.

Handoff: The OOM was not the walk's ticks but verb-film.mjs sheet(): it inlined every frame as a base64 data URL into board.setContent, and ticks.png of appear's opening hand (23 whole-table 2x PNG track frames, the take spans the table) came to 187 MB of HTML, which Playwright's JSON.stringify of the message could not hold. Sheets now load frames from disk through a board.route on http://frames.invalid/ (served()), so the HTML is small. Measured: opening's whole walk result is 1.8 MB, piles 2.1 MB. Test: ui/scripts/film-walk.test.mjs (reel).
