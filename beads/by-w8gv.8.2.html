Close reason

State::named replaces the engine's two lists; twins another record names are told apart (exile memos, attachments, trigger objects, spells' own entries), trigger order too; pins, corpus and count gate hold

Description

From the M4 Mason audit (by-w8gv.8.1), read at staging 86ee9715. A rules bug, confirmed with a throwaway test, and the hand-kept list that admits it.

The bug

Two permanents of one card are offered as one candidate (architecture §7.2) when they differ only in what another record of the state says about them. Confirmed: two Test Bears on the battlefield, one of which exiled a card with EffectCtx::exile_until_source_leaves (Portable Hole's and Aang's Iceberg's body): s.exiled_until(first).count() == 1, s.exiled_until(second).count() == 0, and engine::interchangeable(&s, first, second) is true. So a player choosing which of two Portable Holes to bounce or destroy is offered one, though the two lead to different games (CR 610.3: what the Hole exiled returns as it leaves). As old as exile_until; milestone 4 moved the link into the Memos store and its Warden fixed the same class from the other side (docs/bugs.md, newest entry: "what makes two objects interchangeable is everything the state holds about them").

Current design

engine::collapse_ordered (beryl_engine/src/engine.rs:796) keeps one of two objects when State::signature (beryl_kernel/src/zone.rs:266) is equal and neither is in a set the engine builds by hand: cast::referenced (cast.rs:1752: stack targets and the sources of abilities on the stack) | combat::related (combat.rs:69: what another object's combat names). trigger.rs:331 compares signatures alone. The signature covers what is stored about the object itself (store::Stored: its memos, counter rows, the effects on it). Nothing covers what is stored about another object and names this one. By reading, the list misses:

  • Memo::ExiledUntil(slot) and Memo::ExiledWith(slot) (beryl_kernel/src/memos.rs): the exiler, named by the exiled card's memo. Confirmed for the first; the second is Currency Converter's.
  • Object::attached_to (object.rs:97): the host of an Aura or Equipment, where the attachment changes none of the host's layer results. Swift Reconfiguration, the pool's one Aura, changes types, so no card shows it today.
  • TriggerInfo::object (stack.rs:217) of a stack entry, a waiting trigger (State::triggers) or a delayed one (State::delayed), and PendingTrigger::source: a creature an ability on the stack will act on without targeting it, and its twin.

Proposed design

One kernel query, written beside the fields it reads, in place of the engine's two lists:

impl<L: Layout> State<L> { /// The objects another record of the state names: never interchangeable with a twin (§7.2). pub fn named(&self) -> Bitboard<L::Slot> }

It is the union of: stack entries' sources, targets and trigger objects; waiting and delayed triggers' sources and objects; every combat and attached_to; and the slots the Memos store's entries name, which the store answers itself behind its kinds mask (one AND with no such memo live) so a new memo kind that names a slot is covered by its own Memo method, as Memo::relayout already must map it. collapse_ordered asks for it once, lazily as it does today, and cast::referenced and combat::related are deleted or reduced to callers of it. Decide and record on the bead whether trigger.rs:331 needs it too.

The stronger guard, to take if it fits the session: Relayout already visits every slot the state holds, because the types force each L::Slot to become an M::Slot. A test that renames two slots through that same walk (a slot map in place of the identity) and asserts the state is unchanged apart from the two objects' rows, for every pair collapse merges in the scenarios' end positions or a few hundred fuzz states, would hold any future field to this without a list. Record what you chose and why.

What it eliminates

The hand-kept list in the engine of who refers to an object, and the class of bug in which a new reference (a memo kind, a store, a trigger field) merges two objects that are not alike.

Must not change

Objects that are alike stay one candidate: two untapped basic lands, two Bears nothing names. rollouts_never_change and corpus_never_changes (beryl_bench/src/rollout.rs) hold unless a corpus game has such twins; if a pin moves, show the decision that changed and update the pin on purpose, as its rustdoc says. The frozen save corpus replays (cargo test -p beryl_session --test corpus); a save that this breaks goes in data/save-breaks.json with the reason. The count gate holds with no bump (bar 389,343,295, 1%, rust/crates/beryl_bench/count-gate.toml): collapse_ordered builds the set only once two candidates are of one card, so the common path must stay one OnceCell read.

Context

Read: beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs "Stores" (what a store implements once) and "Queries"; store.rs (Stored); memos.rs; zone.rs signature; beryl_engine/src/engine.rs:770-830; cast.rs:1741-1765; combat.rs:66-74; tests creatures_alike_today_by_effects_that_end_apart_are_not_interchangeable (beryl_engine/src/tests/events.rs:1363) and attackers_blocked_by_different_creatures_are_not_interchangeable (tests/combat.rs:714) as templates: game(), put, with_ctx, engine::interchangeable. Need not read: beryl_session, ui/, tools/, card files beyond portable_hole.rs. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules (AGENTS.md); add the docs/bugs.md entry with its prevention. No new card, no new mechanic (K14).

Acceptance criteria

Engine tests, each failing before the change: (1) of two permanents of one card, the one that exiled a card until it leaves and its twin that exiled none are two candidates (engine::interchangeable is false), and so are two that each exiled a different card; (2) the same for a permanent with cards exiled with it (exile_linked) and its twin; (3) a creature wearing an attachment that changes none of its layer results and its twin are two candidates; (4) a creature named by a trigger on the stack that does not target it and its twin are two candidates. Two permanents of one card that nothing names are still one candidate. The set of named objects comes from one kernel query; cast::referenced and combat::related no longer build their own lists. docs/bugs.md has the entry. Parity as the description says: just check passes, the corpus replays, the count gate holds with no bump, and any moved pin is explained on the bead.

Decisions (executor):

  1. One kernel query, State::named (beryl_kernel/src/query.rs), is the union of: every stack entry's object, targets and trigger object (StackEntry::names); every waiting and delayed trigger's source and object (PendingTrigger::names); every permanent's combat and attached_to (Object::names); and Store::names of the four stores. Store::names is a required method of the Store trait, so a new store cannot be written without answering it; three answer none, Memos answers behind Kinds::NAMING, and the Memos invariant holds a memo whose Memo::names is Some to that mask. cast::referenced and combat::related are deleted.
  2. Deviation from the description: a stack entry names its spell's own card too (the old list left spells out). Confirmed bug of the same class: two Test Removals of one caster aimed at different creatures were one candidate to a counterspell, and the target was chosen silently (test two_spells_of_one_card_with_different_targets_are_not_interchangeable fails with spells left out). Two spells with equal entries are now two candidates too: their place on the stack differs, and comparing entries was not worth a second rule.
  3. Named by slot, whatever incarnation the record meant, as the old lists did: a stale reference only keeps apart two objects that might have merged.
  4. Pending (the open question of a machine step) is not part of named: it names what it asks about or what was already answered (LegendRule kept, CombatDamage done/lethal, the attacker or blocker being aimed), never a candidate still to choose. The rustdoc says so.
  5. trigger.rs equivalent does need it, and now uses it: two waiting triggers of twins were compared by signature alone, so with one source named elsewhere (it exiled a card until it leaves) their order was never asked, though which leaves first decides when the card returns. But every waiting trigger names its own source, so named() as is would end all silent ordering. So: the sources and objects must be alike and outside State::named_apart_from_waiting_triggers (stack, delayed, combat, attachment, stores), and renaming one trigger's source and object to the other's must leave the waiting triggers the same multiset. That keeps the symmetric case merged (each twin's own trigger plus a watcher's trigger for each twin: 2 kinds to order, not 4) and tells apart the asymmetric one (a watcher's trigger for one twin alone). Tests: a_player_orders_their_own_triggers_only_when_they_differ (new third case), triggers_of_twins_are_equivalent_while_the_waiting_triggers_name_both_alike.
  6. The stronger guard (rename two slots through the Relayout walk and compare states) was not built. Relayout maps a slot by a free function at the same number, so a slot map means a new parameter through every Relayout impl (700 lines and each store), and 'unchanged apart from the two objects' rows' needs designed exemptions for everything that is an order or a membership set (battlefield order, library and graveyard order, zone bitboards, knowledge, keyed), in hash-critical code. That is a bead of its own, not a rider on a rules fix. What holds a new reference today: Store::names for stores, the Memos invariant for memo kinds, and the rustdoc of State::named for a plain field.
