Close reason

CDA power/toughness evaluated off the battlefield in the kernel; Tarmogoyf and Adeline scenarios pass in Beryl, XMage and Forge

Description

What is wrong today

State::power and State::toughness (beryl_kernel/src/query.rs) answer a card off the battlefield with its copiable values, so Tarmogoyf in a graveyard, hand or library is 0/1 by Beryl where CR 604.3 says its characteristic-defining ability works in every zone ("Characteristic-defining abilities function in all zones"). The same holds for any CDA that defines colors or subtypes.

Why it was left

by-bajq.5.1 built the off-battlefield reads (beryl_kernel/src/elsewhere.rs: copiable values, then the effects that reach other zones, behind one length test). Applying a CDA there needs an amount evaluated (Tarmogoyf's is Amount::CardTypesAmong over graveyards), and amounts are read only by beryl_rules (layers/apply.rs read, interpret/scene.rs). The kernel cannot call the rules.

What to build

  • A way for the query to apply a CDA off the battlefield: either a kernel-side evaluator for the amounts a CDA can say (static_ability::read_amounts lists them), or a rules-side query that the readers call. Keep the absent cost one test: a capability (table::Capability) for "some card has a CDA" gates it.
  • Readers off the battlefield (filters with PowerAtMost, the session's card views) get the CDA value.
  • Tarmogoyf scenario in a graveyard (rust/cards/cards_2007/fut/tarmogoyf.rs) with a scenario assertion of power/toughness off the battlefield; that needs a new assertion like Assertion::Colors (beryl_scenario_types, run.rs, json.rs, tools/src/beryl_tools/xmage_java.py + BerylScenarioTest.java, tools/oracles/BerylForgeHarness.java) — see how by-bajq.5.1 added Colors.

The battlefield half (Tarmogoyf under Rest in Peace is 0/1) landed with by-bajq.5.1.

Acceptance criteria

Tarmogoyf in a graveyard has the power and toughness its CDA defines by Beryl, XMage and Forge (just card check Tarmogoyf). Count gate holds with no bump; a game with no CDA pays at most one test per off-battlefield read.
