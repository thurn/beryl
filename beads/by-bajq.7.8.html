Helm of Awakening implemented; XMage and Forge agree on 5 scenarios

Description

Group 6 built the whole CR 601.2f order in beryl_rules/src/rule_change.rs (changed_cost: increases, then reductions saturating at {0}, then the greatest floor), but no implemented card reduces a spell's cost. SpellCost::Less is proved only by the test definition a_reduction_takes_generic_mana_down_to_none in beryl_rules/src/tests/spell_costs.rs, so the order 'reductions before the floor' has no card scenario and no oracle has checked it.

Implement Helm of Awakening (Visions, {2} artifact, "Spells cost {1} less to cast.") with RuleChange::spell_costs(Players::EachPlayer, Filter::Any, SpellCost::Less(1)) (see rust/cards/cards_2012/dka/thalia_guardian_of_thraben.rs and rust/cards/cards_2004/dst/trinisphere.rs for the same shape). Follow the card-writing skill (just card brief, just card new, just card check).

Scenarios to write, each checked against XMage and Forge:

  • Both players' spells cost {1} less (Helm says each player).
  • A reduction takes generic mana only: a {U} spell still costs {U}.
  • A {1}{U} spell costs {U}; a {U} spell is not reduced below {0} (CR 601.2f).
  • Helm with Trinisphere: a {1}{U} spell costs {U} after the reduction, and Trinisphere then makes it cost {2}{U} (the floor applies after reductions; Trinisphere's rulings say so).
  • Helm with Thalia: a noncreature {1}{U} spell costs {1}{U} (increase and reduction cancel). Once these pass, the test definition may be deleted if its scenarios cover the same path.

Acceptance criteria

Helm of Awakening passes just card check (Beryl, XMage, Forge) with the scenarios above, including the Trinisphere order scenario. The count gate holds with no bump (report before/after).
