Sweep 12..80 replays identically under another seed: effect source card, battlefield-order damage, knowledge-aware collapse, save v5 owner/shown

Description

Found while building by-zf0.3 (saves record reveals). beryl_session/tests/chance.rs replays saves under another seed (meaning() and seen() compare logs and views). 12 seeds pass; a wider sweep (seeds 12..80, random agent both seats, VINTAGE_WU_CONTROL vs WU_CONTROL alternating, 600 decisions, see played() there) still diverges in 2 of 68, each a tie broken by slot number, which the deal and shuffles choose at random:

  1. Seed 19 (WU vs VINTAGE, P0 WU): combat damage division (combat.rs division()/deal()): with every blocker chosen lethal, the attacker's excess damage lands on a blocker picked by slot, so damage marks differ (cosmetic here, but seed-dependent).
  2. Seed 15 (P0 WU): Abhorrent Oculus's exile-six cost (ExileCost decisions, cast.rs) plus a payment: after the recorded picks the rest of the cost exiles different graveyard cards, then taps differ.
  3. Seen with an earlier ordering (seed 55): engine::collapse / zone::signature treat a copy the opponent knows and an unknown copy of the same card in hand as interchangeable, so which one is played (and so what the opponent still knows) depends on slot. Collapse should keep apart cards the other player knows from ones they don't; save::Obj may then need a 'known to the opponent' field to tell the candidates apart.

Bisect technique: replay the log truncated at entries that are not Reveal/Flip under both seeds and compare seen(view); the first cut that differs names the step.

docs/bugs.md has the entry for the ties fixed in by-zf0.3 (mana classes and taps, look-ahead hand order, answers described in the shown position).
