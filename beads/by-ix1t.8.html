Description

Part of the AI ladder epic by-ix1t. Read docs/ai-plan.md first: §5 and §6.8.

At spare-jace-wielder the agent attacked Jace, the Mind Sculptor and the player and left Jace, Wielder of Mysteries alive, which then won the game (docs/incidents/2026-11-07-snapcaster-and-jace-wielder.md). Greedy, the expert and the combat planner all choose the wrong target there. The planner has a plan that kills Jace, Wielder and scores another plan higher.

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/combat.rs: Sim::choose, attack_plans, weigh, run, score, swing_back, and the General judgements that read card data in place of the expert's card names.
  • data/blunders.json: the entry, its forbidden and required choices.
  • just blunders greedy+combat --only spare-jace-wielder --seeds 1 (from the policy agent bead) shows the planner's answer in seconds.

Steps

  1. Print the planner's plans at the entry with their scores, and find the term of score that puts another plan above the one that kills Jace, Wielder. Write the plans and scores in the notes.
  2. Propose a correction that reads what a planeswalker's abilities do and its loyalty, and names no card. For example: a planeswalker whose ability can end the game from its present loyalty within its controller's next turn is worth more than its cost-based value suggests. If the fault is that the plan set never pairs the right attackers with the right target, fix the set and say so.
  3. Build it as an option walker, off by default, named last in the fixed order, that changes the planner's score or plan set only when set.
  4. Tests: at the entry, the planner with walker attacks Jace, Wielder with enough power to kill it; a test over every planeswalker in the card pool that the rule's reading of its abilities does not panic and is stable; the other combat entries (beza-trinket-into-blockers, manifest-into-samurai, spirits-stay-home, skip-beza-after-winds) keep their answers.

If no general correction fixes the entry without breaking another, close this bead with what you found. The entry then waits for docs/ai-plan.md §8's solver over every attack and block.

Acceptance criteria

The planner's plans and scores at the entry are in the notes; with walker the planner kills Jace, Wielder there and the four other combat entries keep their answers; the rule names no card; just test-rust and just check pass. Or the bead is closed with the finding that no general correction exists.
