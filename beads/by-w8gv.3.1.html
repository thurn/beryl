Close reason

beryl_card crate landed (staging ff692ba1): definition types moved and re-exported, Behavior::Data(&Effect)/Custom, Effect tree over Objects/Players/Amount/Condition with const builders and mana("{1}{U}"), keyword builders as data on a first interpreter; counts 407,892,697 vs 407,832,194 before; paper test 6 fit / 51 one more entry / 2 new kind of expression / 1 Rust body (seed 20261110)

Description

Create rust/crates/beryl_card (plan, Crates): the types a card file is written in, with no dependency on the engine's internals.

  • Move the pure definition types out of beryl_engine/src/card.rs (CardDef, Face, ManaCost, Types, Colors, Keywords, Subtype, Cost, TargetSpec, Trigger, Replacement, LayerSpec, Modification and friends) and filter.rs's Filter; beryl_engine re-exports them so no caller changes. The id types they need (Seat, CardIx, CounterKind) move with them or into a small ids module they share; the kernel crate in milestone 3 takes ownership later.
  • card.rs and layout.rs form a cycle with effect.rs through Bodies (fn pointers taking EffectCtx). Break it: an ability's behavior becomes Behavior::Data(&'static Effect) or Behavior::Custom(...), where the custom form stays in beryl_engine for now.
  • Add Effect: a Copy, const-constructible tree. First entries are the ones the 47 cards' bodies use most (audit: counter target spell, counter unless pays, return to hand, destroy, exile, draw, discard, mill, gain and lose life, create tokens, add counters, put into library at a position, exile until this leaves, scry, sequence, conditional, for-each-player) with references such as TARGET(n), YOU, EACH_OPPONENT, ITS_CONTROLLER. Design each entry from its Comprehensive Rules section and cite it (AGENTS.md); do not model them on Forge's script fields (K16). Player references say what the card says (K7): "each opponent" and "target opponent" are different values.
  • Builders are const fn, names are whole words (static_ability), and non-printed costs parse Scryfall notation at compile time: mana("{1}{U}").
  • No runtime dependencies (K12). No file over 800 lines: one module per concern from the start.
  • Paper test first (plan, milestone 2). Before the Effect types harden, write unimplemented definitions in the proposed vocabulary for 60 random in-scope cards: sample Scryfall's Oracle bulk data with a recorded seed, excluding un-sets, dexterity and Alchemy cards and the implemented cards; no hand-picking and no resampling. Sort each into: fits; needs one more entry of an existing kind; needs a new kind of expression (an amount, a reference, a condition); needs a Rust body. Watch amounts ("that many", "equal to its power"), references ("the exiled card", "that creature's controller") and conditions ("if you do", "then if"): the design says them as typed data, never as a string parameter or a named variable looked up while resolving. Record the seed, the split and every misfit with its Oracle text in this bead's notes and on the milestone epic. Reported with no bar, but if the third class is more than a handful, redesign the expression types before closing.

Acceptance criteria

beryl_card builds with no dependencies beyond ids; beryl_engine and every caller compile unchanged through re-exports; cargo test --workspace and the parity checks pass.

Notes

PAPER TEST (plan, milestone 2): 60 random in-scope cards written in the beryl_card vocabulary as built by this bead.

Sample: Scryfall Oracle Cards bulk file oracle-cards-20261110220203 (39,288 objects). In scope: legal or restricted in Vintage, available in paper, set type not funny, alchemy, token, memorabilia or minigame, and no face named in beryl_cards/src/names.rs: 31,215 cards. Sorted by oracle_id; random.Random(20261110).sample(pool, 60). One draw, no resampling, no hand-picking.

Rules of the sort: a card takes the worst class of any of its abilities. Printed characteristics (subtypes, costs) are not counted, since plan K2 generates them. "Fits" means every type and entry exists in beryl_card today, the 5 keyword bits included; the 6 that fit are compiled in rust/crates/beryl_card/tests/paper_test.rs.

SPLIT: fits: 6; needs one more entry of an existing kind: 51; needs a new kind of expression: 2; needs a Rust body: 1. New kind of expression (2, a handful, so no redesign): targets quantified over players (Age of Ultron); an amount divided among targets (Iron Fist). Rust body (1): Dead Ringers.

WHAT THE MISFITS ASK FOR (cards by number; a card may ask for several):

  • A Trigger entry, nearly always an event with a subject filter or another player's ("a Frog you control attacks", "an opponent casts", "each player's upkeep"): 17 (1, 3, 6, 12, 13, 25, 29, 39, 43, 47, 48, 49, 52, 54, 55, 57, 60). Trigger wants to become event x subject Filter x Players before milestone 5 ports the 47 cards.
  • Keyword bits and keyword builders only the engine can give meaning (haste, trample, menace, lifelink, morph, flashback, modular, enlist): about 22 cards name at least one.
  • A Cost part or additional cost paid with other objects (sacrifice, exile or reveal a [filter]): 8 (7, 8, 13, 17, 22, 23, 31, 56); "the sacrificed creature" as an Objects entry: 2 (22, 23).
  • A static ability affecting a filtered set (Affected has Itself and Enchanted): 6 (12, 13, 27, 35, 42, 44).
  • Filter entries: "[player] controls" taking a Players value: 3 (20, 28, 39), so Whose, in Filter and in Trigger::Step, should become Players; colors: 3 (26, 43, 50); keywords: 3 (3, 30, 34); power: 2 (36, 52); other-than-this: 2 (8, 13); attacking: 1 (27).
  • Effect entries: tap or untap: 5 (2, 11, 34, 47, 54); search: 3 (19, 20, 46); a reflexive "when you do" ability: 3 (3, 9, 51); a delayed trigger: 2 (2, 43); a choice between instructions: 2 (47, 51); modes: 1 (10); gain control, venture, amass, transform, additional combat, grant flashback, exile with counters, deal damage: 1 each.
  • Objects a player chooses as the instruction is reached ("a creature you control", "a land of their choice"): 4 (28, 29, 52, 54).
  • A static ability whose "as long as" is a general Condition (LayerSpec has StaticCondition): 4 (5, 26, 32, 60). An activation or trigger limit per turn: 4 (16, 18, 55, 60). Something read from a this-turn or this-game tracker: 5 (5, 11, 25, 32, 44). A Modification entry: 5 (3, 33, 37, 42, 54); a Rule entry: 4 (11, 22, 35, 49); a Replacement entry: 3 (24, 38, 51).

Decisions and deviations (by-w8gv.3.1):

  1. Behavior::Data(&'static Effect), as the bead says, and Ability::condition is Option<&'static Condition>. I first held both by value (so const builders could take an Effect built from an argument): Ability grew from 128 to 304 bytes and the rollout benchmark counted +0.28% (408,969,506 against staging's 407,832,194). By reference it is 120 bytes; #[repr(align(16))] and a const assertion keep it a power of two (128), which alone is worth 0.11%: a face's abilities are scanned for every decision. The one builder that makes an effect from an argument, Ability::tap_for_mana(produces), points into a private static table of "add [mana]" effects (64 kind sets x up to 3 mana).
  2. A first interpreter is in this bead (beryl_engine/src/interpret.rs), not only in by-w8gv.3.2: Ability's keyword builders (cycling, crew, offspring, evoke, impending_countdown, tap_for_mana) are inherent const fns, so they moved with Ability and can no longer name the engine's body functions. They are effect data now, and interpret.rs runs exactly the entries they use through the same EffectCtx calls (draw, sacrifice, remove counters, modify until end of turn, a 1/1 copy of this, add mana) plus their intervening ifs (Paid, All, Compare of a counter count). Every other entry panics with "the interpreter does not run ... yet", so nothing can depend on one before 3.2 runs it. Their old bodies in effect.rs are deleted; add_produced_mana, draw_a_card and kicked stay because tokens.rs, Staff of the Storyteller and Thieving Skydiver still name them.
  3. A mana ability whose whole effect is "you add [mana]" is carried out without an EffectCtx (interpret::mana_ability): -609,035 instructions. With (1) the change lands at 407,892,697, +60,503 (0.015%) over staging, leaving the gate's slack as it found it for by-w8gv.3.3.
  4. Callers did change in two small ways. Filter is the vocabulary's type, so the engine cannot give it inherent methods: matches/select are the trait beryl_engine::filter::Evaluate, imported by 5 card files, beryl_cli's playground.rs and the engine's tests. Ability::statik is Ability::static_ability (K2), 9 call sites.
  5. Custom is &'static (dyn Any + Send + Sync + RefUnwindSafe), read back with Custom::get::<T>(): bodies! and conditions! wrap the engine's Bodies and Conditions in it. RefUnwindSafe is in the bound so card statics stay unwind-safe for the catch_unwind callers.
  6. Left as moved, not redesigned (the paper test says where they pinch): Trigger, Whose, Cost, Affected, StaticCondition, Replacement, Rule, Modification. CardIx stayed in the engine: no definition type needs it. Seat, Zone, TurnStep and CounterKind are beryl_card::ids.
  7. References back ("it", "that many", "if you do") are Objects::Those, Amount::ThatMany and Condition::Did: the outcome of the instruction before, defined in beryl_card/src/effect/mod.rs. No names, no lookups.
  8. Not done, for milestone 5 to weigh: an Effect::Custom entry, so a card with one odd instruction keeps the rest of its ability as data rather than the whole body in Rust. Behavior::Custom is the only escape hatch today, as the bead says.
  9. beryl_card is in the card-names check's scope; card.rs left the file-length allowlist.
