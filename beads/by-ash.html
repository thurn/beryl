Close reason

Lifted/tall stack shrinks to fit under the opponent's piles; cutText exemption dropped; new game-stack-tall shot (e993f52)

Description

Found by by-l1w.11's visual review. While a prompt is open the stack rises by STACK_LIFT (ui/src/game/layout.ts stackPlace) to y 132, and each item beneath the top peeks out a further 46 px higher, so the stack (x 1205-1500) covers the opponent's library, graveyard and exile piles (y 13-160) and their counts; with four items the bottom item's frame leaves the stage top. Seen in shots game-target (Get Lost covers the exile count) and a held-out fixture (seed 21, first target decision for seat 0 from turn 6: four spells on the stack). There is no room for a full stack card between the piles' foot (~165) and a prompt's top (~560), so the fix is a layout choice: scale the stack down while lifted, move the opponent's piles, or narrow the peek step. capture.mjs cutText currently exempts stack cards (.card-el-stack) from its 'text lies under a card' rule because of this; drop the exemption with the fix.

Acceptance criteria

  • With a prompt open and three or more items on the stack, no stack item covers the opponent's library, graveyard or exile pile or its count, and every item stays on the stage.
  • cutText no longer exempts .card-el-stack, and just ui-shots passes.
  • just check passes.
