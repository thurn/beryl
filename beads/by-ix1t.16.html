Description

Part of the AI ladder epic by-ix1t. Read docs/ai-plan.md first: §4 and §6.9.

turn72 is built (rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long/turn.rs, by-97g.39). At the root of the player's own turn with an empty stack it proposes continuations of the turn with the opponent passing and tests them in fresh worlds. Measured alone on 2026-11-13 it took subtlety-main-phase-empty-stack from 4 of 10 to 10 and evoke-subtlety-empty-stack-hunt from 0 to 10, and took snapcaster-sorcery-speed from 9 to 2. That last entry stopped it at the gate and no match was played. With hold in the champion the Snapcaster cast is held by rule.

The challenger is the champion's family with turn72 where it has pimc72. It claims no suite entry.

Rules

  • Run every command on Cygnus (the Windows PC, BERYL_CACHE=D:\beryl-cache), from the repository at current staging. Long commands run in the background, and nothing else heavy runs beside a match.
  • You judge the result. docs/ai-plan.md §4.4 is guidance, not a rule that overrides your reading of the numbers. Write your reasoning with the numbers in this bead's notes.
  • Never wait for a person and never file a bead asking one to look. If a step fails in a way not written here, write what happened in the notes, choose the sensible course and go on.

Names

  • CHAMPION: the family on the Champion: line of docs/ladder.md.
  • CHALLENGER: CHAMPION with the method turn72 in place of pimc72 added to its options. Options are joined by + in the fixed order the family parser prints in its error message (rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs, impl FromStr for Family).
  • If CHAMPION already has the method turn72 in place of pimc72, close this bead with the reason already in the champion.

Steps

  1. Audit check. If docs/ladder.md shows three or more pass rows since its last audit row, or a pass row since an audit row that is seven or more days old, run docs/ai-plan.md §4.5 first and add its row.
  2. The suite. just gate CHALLENGER --claims none. Paste the table and the totals. If snapcaster-sorcery-speed still falls by six or more of 20, read by-97g.31's design field and the TurnReport counts in the saved report (defaulted, fallbacks) to see whether the turn search is proposing the cast or failing to score the pass, and write which.
  3. The match. In the background: just ladder CHALLENGER --work 3M --bar stronger --positions D:\beryl-cache\ladder\positions --cap 2000. Paste the whole report with the hours.
  4. Verdict. Promote if the match accepts the bar and the suite total has not fallen by more than 20 of 400.
  5. The last audit of this epic, whatever the verdict: just ladder CHAMPION --vs pimc72/hidden/rollout/tuned/anchor+guard --work 10M --bar audit --cap 400 --positions D:\beryl-cache\ladder\positions. Add its row, and write the three numbers of docs/ai-plan.md §1 as they stand (the champion's suite total of 400, the strength score with its interval) in §1's table in the same push.
  6. Close with the verdict, the suite totals, the score and the audit's score.
