Close reason

Desktop camera fitted to Arena: pitch 27.00, fov 24.29, rms 0.0055 over 14 cards

Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Space and camera' and decision C4; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Skill: arena-video (the recordings in ~/arena, just video). Code: beryl_scene src/table/camera.rs (CameraData, Camera::fit), src/table/mod.rs (DESKTOP, whose camera is 'a first estimate fitted by eye': pitch 24 degrees, field of view 30), build.rs (how data/beryl3d/space.json becomes constants), tests/table.rs.

Measure. In two recordings of the arena-video index, at 1920 by 1080, find one frame each in which both players have at least three untapped permanents and no card is in flight. From each frame take three cards of the near row and three of the far row, at the left, the middle and the right of the table, and record the four corners of each card in pixels. Write the 12 cards to data/beryl3d/camera-fit.json, each with its recording, its time and its corners in the order bottom left, bottom right, top right, top left.

Solve. A new command just b3d camera-fit, in tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/camera.py with the standard library only, reads that file and finds the pitch and the vertical field of view of a camera that looks at the table from the viewer's side. For each card it places a 63 by 88 rectangle on the plane z = 0 so that the middle of its bottom edge projects to the measured one, and takes two residuals: the ratio of the top edge's width to the bottom edge's, and the ratio of the card's height on screen to its bottom edge's width, each less the measured ratio. It minimizes the sum of squares over pitch from 5 to 60 degrees and field of view from 10 to 60 by a grid of half a degree and then a golden-section search in each to a hundredth of a degree. It prints both values, the root mean square residual and each card's residuals.

Apply. Put the two values in data/beryl3d/space.json under camera.desktop as pitch_degrees and fov_degrees, extend build.rs to write them as data::space::camera::desktop::PITCH_DEGREES and FOV_DEGREES, and make DESKTOP.camera read them. target and required stay as they are; if Camera::required_fits fails in any window of the matrix (beryl_inspect::pages::DESKTOP_WINDOWS), move target.y until it holds and say so in the commit body. The check of names in space.json (tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d, the data-names step) must accept the camera key.

Tests: the solver recovers a pitch of 30 and a field of view of 25 from corners projected by a camera with those values, within 0.05 degrees (a test in the tools package); a test in beryl_scene/tests/table.rs asserts that DESKTOP.camera equals the data's constants and that the required rectangle fits in each window of the desktop matrix.

Run: just b3d camera-fit; just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d check verbs-zones; just b3d gate; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

data/beryl3d/camera-fit.json holds 12 cards from two recordings; just b3d camera-fit prints the two values that data/beryl3d/space.json holds and a root mean square residual under 0.03; no literal pitch or field of view remains in src/table/mod.rs; the tests pass; a comment gives the old and new values, the residual, and a just b3d png verbs-zones --at 1500ms path for each.

Where this differs from the plan: Arena's battlefield cards are cropped art tiles (aspect about 0.85), not 63x88 cards, and their outer outline includes the tile's thickness. A fixed 63x88 rectangle would have forced a wrong, steep pitch. So the solver measures the art frame printed on each face, gives each card a free aspect ratio and a free turn when tapped, and minimizes corner reprojection error by Levenberg-Marquardt from a 10-degree grid of seeds (not a half-degree grid and golden-section search). With free proportions, untapped cards only pin a pitch/fov relation (an exact one-parameter degeneracy); Arena turns tapped cards about 8 degrees, and that is what fixes the fov, hence the ±3-4 degree uncertainty, which the command prints. Only 3 of the 14 cards are tapped (two lands and Esper Sentinel); more tapped cards on a light table would tighten the fit. The camera values were already data under layout.desktop.camera (an earlier refactor), so build.rs and data-names needed no change, and the bead's camera.desktop key was not added.

Handoff: tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/camera.py (solver; run_camera_fit), cli.py registers camera-fit; tools/tests/test_beryl3d_camera.py checks synthetic recovery of 30/25 within 0.05 and that space.json equals the fit of camera-fit.json (so re-measuring means updating both). beryl_scene tests/all/table.rs: DESKTOP.camera equals the data constants; required rectangle fits in all four desktop windows, so target did not move. gates.json stress-255/1024 limits rose (records 142->144, total 17011->17265, upload 11360->11520) because more of a crowded row is visible. The corners came from a scratch edge-finder (dark-to-bright step inward from each tile edge, line fits, intersections) that was not committed.
