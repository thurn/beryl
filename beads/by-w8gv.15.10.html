Pilot cards

Candidate default applied: docs/sample-decks/ holds no file marked Legacy, so I used all six WU Control decklists in it as of 2026-11-13 (183 distinct names), kept cards that Scryfall's bulk data ($BERYL_CACHE/scryfall/default_cards.jsonl.gz) marks legal in Legacy and for which just card path exits 1, and dropped cards needing {G} or {R} (no implemented land makes them, per the by-w8gv.15.9 trial). Each card below was checked against the vocabulary in rust/crates/beryl_card/src and its brief; none needs a Rust body or a new entry. Writers: read just card brief first; the nearest card named is the model to copy.

  1. Spellseeker (2026-11-02-NotBob-wu-control.txt) - creature, enters trigger. Ability::triggered(&Trigger::ENTERS) with may(YOU, search(YOU, Filter::All[AnyOf(instant, sorcery), ManaValueAtMost(2)], Found::RevealToHand)). Model: Trinket Mage. Difficulty: easy.
  2. Mana Leak (2026-10-30-Avatog-wu-control.txt) - instant. unless_pays(counter(TARGET), ITS_CONTROLLER, generic(3)) on a TargetKind::Spell target. Model: Mana Tithe. Difficulty: easy.
  3. Hallowed Fountain (2026-10-23-parley, 2026-11-02-NotBob) - land. Two Ability::tap_for_mana (W, U) plus Ability::replacing(Replacement::EntersTappedUnlessPayLife(2)). Models: Tundra and Soporific Springs (the back of Sink into Stupor). Difficulty: easy.
  4. Miscalculation (2026-10-30-tubitz-wu-control.txt) - instant with cycling. unless_pays(counter(TARGET), ITS_CONTROLLER, generic(2)) plus Ability::cycling(generic(2)). Model: Mana Tithe; grep cycling in rust/cards for the cycling shape. Difficulty: easy.
  5. Cathar Commando (2026-10-17-PrinceJools-wu-control.txt) - creature with an activated ability. Keywords::FLASH; Ability::activated(Cost::of(generic(1), &[CostPart::SACRIFICE])) targeting a permanent with Filter::AnyOf[artifact, enchantment], effect destroy(TARGET). Models: Strip Mine (sacrifice-this cost), Get Lost (destroy). Difficulty: easy.
  6. Path to Exile (2026-10-23-parley-wu-control.txt) - instant. sequence[exile(TARGET), may(ITS_CONTROLLER, search(ITS_CONTROLLER, basic land, Found::OntoBattlefieldTapped))]. Models: Winds of Abandon (basic-land fetch), Get Lost (its controller after the target leaves). Difficulty: medium (controller of an exiled object; scenarios need a basic land in the opponent's library).
  7. Loran of the Third Path (2026-10-30-tubitz-wu-control.txt) - creature, enters trigger and tap ability. Keywords::VIGILANCE; ENTERS trigger destroy(TARGET) with TargetSpec::up_to_one(Permanent, AnyOf[artifact, enchantment]); Ability::activated(Cost::of_parts(&[CostPart::TAP])) targeting TargetKind::Player with Filter::Whose(&Players::EachOpponent), sequence[draw(YOU,1), draw(target player,1)]. Models: Teferi, Time Raveler; Staff of the Storyteller. Difficulty: medium.
  8. Soul-Guide Lantern (2026-10-17-PrinceJools-wu-control.txt) - artifact, enters trigger and two activated abilities. ENTERS: exile(TARGET) of TargetKind::GraveyardCard; {T}, sacrifice: exile(Objects::InZoneOf{Graveyard, EachOpponent, Any}); {1}, {T}, sacrifice: draw(YOU,1). Models: Portable Hole, Staff of the Storyteller, Currency Converter. Difficulty: medium.
  9. Witch Enchanter // Witch-Blessed Meadow (2026-10-30-Avatog-wu-control.txt) - modal double-faced creature // land. Front: ENTERS destroy(TARGET), permanent with All[AnyOf(artifact, enchantment), Whose(EachOpponent)]. Back: Replacement::EntersTappedUnlessPayLife(3) and tap_for_mana(W). Model: Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs. Difficulty: medium (two faces).
  10. Boomerang Basics (2026-11-02-NotBob, 2026-10-23-parley) - sorcery. return_to_hand(TARGET) on a nonland permanent, then draw if you controlled it. Say it as if_else(Condition::Is(TARGET, Whose(You)), sequence[return_to_hand, draw(YOU,1)], return_to_hand), so the condition is read before the permanent leaves; the render diff may then differ in wording only. Model: Reprieve. Difficulty: hard (the look-back condition).

Spread: creatures with triggers (1, 7, 9), instants (2, 4, 6), a sorcery (10), activated abilities (5, 7, 8), a land with a replacement (3, 9), an artifact (8). No planeswalker or static continuous effect qualifies; see the next comment for why the rest fail.

Derek Thurn2026-11-13

Why the other candidates were excluded (for the census and the next mechanics pass):

  • Missing keywords: lifelink, protection, prowess, battle cry, infect, ward (Keywords has only flash, flying, first strike, reach, vigilance): Solitude, Ocelot Pride, Giver of Runes, Monastery Mentor, Sanguine Evangelist, Sage of the Skies, Enduring Innocence, Intrepid Adversary, Blightsteel Colossus.
  • Missing keyword actions or designations: surveil (Consider, Meticulous Archive), investigate/Clue (Thraben Inspector, Tamiyo), monarch (Palace Jailer, Ginger), connive (Ledger Shredder), stun counters (Seasoned Cryomancer), incubate (Sunfall), voting (Council's Judgment), ascend, replicate (Lose Focus), landcycling (Eagles of the North), printed flashback and escape (Echo of Eons, Woe Strider, Nethergoyf).
  • Missing destinations: Found has only RevealToHand and OntoBattlefieldTapped, so no fetch onto the battlefield untapped (Prismatic Vista, Marsh Flats, Fabled Passage) or tutor to the top of the library (Enlightened Tutor); CounterInto supports exile only (Memory Lapse); LibraryPosition::FromTop is fixed, not X (Unexpectedly Absent).
  • Missing filters: no power/toughness filter (Stern Scolding).
  • Missing costs: alternative pitch costs (Force of Will, Force of Negation); CostPart::This(Exile) has no payer (The Echoverse Fulcrum).
  • Mana abilities only add mana, so Ancient Tomb's damage and Chromatic Star's sacrifice-and-draw don't fit; "doesn't untap" is missing (Basalt Monolith).
  • Kicker changes Bloodchief's Thirst's target restriction, and a CastingOption::Kicker has no ability index to switch to (Overload has one).
  • Delayed triggers carry only a number, not an object, so exile-and-return-at-end-step (Flickerwisp, Phelia) can't name the card. Draw-limit rule change (Narset) is not a RuleChange.
  • Others need transform, copy, adventure, equipment, vehicle-token text or classes.
  • Cards costing {G} or {R}, and Scrubland (a Tundra copy, kept out because Hallowed Fountain covers the same shape with more to check).
