Close reason

Generator gives changeling faces every creature type; Keywords::CHANGELING renders it; Cavern of Souls scenarios with Universal Automaton (d3939bed8).

Description

Changeling (CR 702.73a: "This object is every creature type") has no vocabulary entry. The card-store generator (rust/crates/beryl_card_build/src/generate.rs) builds each face's printed subtypes from the store and refuses a hand-typed .subtypes(...), so a card file can't say it either. Subtypes::every(Types::CREATURE) in rust/crates/beryl_card/src/characteristics.rs already models the result.

Build: have the generator give a face with the Changeling keyword Subtypes::every(Types::CREATURE) (it is a characteristic-defining ability, so it holds in every zone, CR 604.3), and have the card-text renderer print the keyword. Then implement Universal Automaton ({1} artifact creature 1/1, changeling) with two scenarios: Cavern of Souls naming Elk pays for it and Counterspell can't counter it; and Cavern naming "Another creature type" does the same, since a changeling is every creature type. These close a gap the keystone adversarial review (by-bajq) found in Cavern of Souls' coverage.
