Description

Read first: epic by-bmdk, sections 'Rules for every bead', 'Contracts' and 'Code map'. Design: docs/manual-multiplayer.md ("the doc"), the Click table rows for life and counters, and the appendix tables 'Life and player counters' and 'Dice, coins and other player actions'.

How interface beads in this milestone fit together: ui/src/table/client.ts (bead "Table screen") is the manual table's pointer client. It owns the selection, sends actions with tableAct from ui/src/ipc.ts, and lets each interaction register handlers from its own file. Put your code in the file this bead names and register it; change client.ts only to add a registration point it lacks. Check your work on a live manual table in a headless browser: ui/scripts/table.mjs (same bead) starts one through the HTTP server and gives a Playwright page. Add one scripted check per behavior to ui/scripts/kept-table-<topic>.mjs and run it with just ui-test <title>.

File: ui/src/table/players.ts.

  • Clicking a life total opens the life prompt (AskPrompt, starting at the current value; "+3" gains three, "17" sets). Its context menu rows: −1, +1, −5, +5, "Set life…".
  • F12 and the = key add one life; F11 and the − key remove one; Cmd+L opens the prompt. Either player may change either life total; with the pointer over a life total the keys act on that one, otherwise on the viewer's own.
  • A click on a mana symbol beside a life total adds one of that counter; a right-click removes one.
  • A storm counter ("other") beside the life total, shown once nonzero: Cmd+] adds, Cmd+[ removes, Cmd+\ sets.
  • "Add counter…" on a player takes any name (text prompt), then a number.
  • Cmd+I rolls: ask for sides (2 to 1,000,000, start 6) then count (1 to 100, start 1). Cmd+Shift+I flips a coin. The result is the host's banner; nothing is drawn locally.
  • "Reduce life by power" (Cmd+Shift+L) on a creature: life on the player it does not belong to, by its shown power.

Checks (kept-table-players.mjs): the life prompt with "+3" and a plain number; the keys; a mana symbol click and right-click; storm counter appears at 1; a roll shows a banner.

Acceptance criteria

Life, mana symbols, the storm counter, named player counters, dice and coins work by click, menu and key as the appendix lists, with headless checks.
