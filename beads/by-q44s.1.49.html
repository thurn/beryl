Close reason

Placement now n log n: worst frame 118M->28M (255), 1976M->112M (1024), limits lowered; remaining per-step re-placement filed as by-q44s.18

Description

Symptom: just b3d gate --counts (the instruction-count gate for frames, tools/src/beryl_tools/beryl3d/counts.py) measured the worst frame of stress-1024 at 1,975,736,359 instructions and of stress-255 at 118,179,669, against the doc's targets of 4,000,000 and 2,000,000 (docs/beryl-3d.md, 'What it is held to'). Both are frame 30, the frame whose Client::handle takes the board's second view. Every other frame is within target: flight frames take 1.66 to 1.84 million (stress-1024) and 0.41 to 0.46 million (stress-255), rest frames 172,357 and 156,977. A board 4 times larger costs 16.7 times more, so the work is quadratic or worse in the number of permanents.

Why (callgrind_annotate of that frame, self cost): 43% is bcmp and 25% is quicksort and sort_by_key closures over &ObjectView in beryl_scene::table::place::battlefield (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/table/place.rs, the Lands row). Its sort key (object.tapped, first(object)) computes first with names.iter().position(...), a linear search with string comparisons, and sort_by_key recomputes the key O(n log n) times: O(n^2 log n) string compares. Another 18% is self cost in place with slice comparisons inlined, most likely piles in src/table/rows.rs, which searches every existing group with twins for each card (O(n x groups)).

Fix: compute each land's first-name index once (a map from name to first index, or sort_by_cached_key, or a stable sort by name group), and make piles find a card's group by a key in a map instead of a linear scan. Keep the order and piles exactly as now (the interface checks and just b3d tree output must not change).

Then run just b3d gate --counts --update (in beryl3d-bench, about 30 minutes) and commit the lowered limits in data/beryl3d/gates.json with the old and new counts in the commit body.

Acceptance criteria

The worst frame of stress-255 is under 2,000,000 instructions and of stress-1024 under 4,000,000 in just b3d gate --counts, or the bead records what stops it with the measured numbers; gates.json holds the lowered limits; placements and piles are unchanged (just b3d cargo-test and just b3d gate pass).
