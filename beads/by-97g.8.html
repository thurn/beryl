Description

Part of the ladder epic (read it and docs/ai-strategy.md §6 rung 4 first). The search cannot value what its rollouts cannot do. Four fixes, each a line or two, each read from card data or a table entry, never a card-specific code path (§1).

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/rollout.rs: Policy::cast (casts anything worth more than passing), Policy::targeting, Policy::value (planeswalkers: card value plus 4 a loyalty counter), walker_target, the counter rule (COUNTER_MIN), DecisionKind::ChooseUpTo.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/values.rs: NEVER_CAST (Mana Crypt), FRIENDLY (The Wandering Emperor's 1), CardInfo, Knowledge.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs: the tree's priority candidates (a never-cast card must be pruned from the tree too, so tree and rollouts agree).
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/expert/mod.rs: appraise (JACE_WINS: Jace, Wielder at 150 plus 6 a loyalty counter near a decking win), CRYPT_LANDS, counter_score (counters only a spell the caster cannot recast): the reference for the values, not code to call.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/frozen/: its own copies; the frozen bar does not move.
  • rust/crates/beryl_bench: rollouts_never_change and count-gate.toml move because the work changed (reason 1 of the gate's comment); re-pin them.
  • Incidents: Snapcaster, Trinket Mage, searcher mulligan, Deep Seer.

Build

A new knowledge tier Knowledge::Common (common in family names): Tuned's values plus:

  1. A creature with flash (Keywords) is held in its controller's main phases and cast at the opponent's end step, or at instant speed when a rollout's priority falls there.
  2. Jace, Wielder of Mysteries's +1 is in FRIENDLY, and its value follows the expert's appraise by table: a bonus while its controller's library is short, as a CardInfo field, not a branch on the name.
  3. Mana Crypt leaves NEVER_CAST for a cast_while_short_of_lands entry (the expert's CRYPT_LANDS), and the tree prunes nothing it cannot cast in the rollouts and casts nothing they cannot: long.rs reads the same CardInfo.
  4. A counterspell, or Reprieve, answers a spell only while its caster cannot recast it this turn, read from their untapped mana and the spell's cost.

Land each fix as its own commit so a loss bisects. greedy keeps Tuned, so expert-frozen and the UI's quick opponents play as before until a promotion.

Tests

Each fix on a scenario: a flash creature waits for the end step in a rollout; Jace, Wielder's +1 targets its controller; Mana Crypt is cast on turn one with one land and never with four; Reprieve is held while the caster has the mana to recast. The pins re-pinned with the counts named in the commit body.
