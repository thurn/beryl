Close reason

Attachments tuck behind their host whoever controls them, hover rings the link, inspector shows changed types and attachments; attached-across fixture, shot and ui-test check (2127555). No findings were already fixed.

Description

Auras and equipment (ObjectView.attachedTo) are drawn as loose cards in their controller's row, with no link to the host.

  • The opponent's Swift Reconfiguration on the reviewer's Adeline or Malcolm sat in the opponent's row.
  • The host's P/T badge vanished with no explanation. That part is rules-correct, because Swift Reconfiguration makes it a noncreature Vehicle, but the type change is shown nowhere.

Draw an attachment with its host, tucked behind it as Arena does, whoever controls it. Hovering either card should show the link. The inspector shows the host's current types and what is attached to it.

Files: ui/src/game/layout.ts, ui/src/game/CardLayer.tsx.

Before changing anything, reproduce each item on current staging. Use just ui-live, as the ui-review skill describes (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md), or a fixture. The findings come from reviews of the build before the 2026-11-05 UI fixes, so some may already be fixed. Drop those, and list them in the close reason.

Design rules (see the epic): follow the skill's design philosophy, and add no standing words to the table. What a player needs at a glance is shown as icons, badges, numbers, colour or position. Everything else goes behind disclosure: hover, the inspector, a pile's browser, or the game log.

Acceptance criteria

  • A fixture with an aura one player controls on the other player's creature draws the aura with its host. Their boxes overlap or touch inside the host's row.
  • The host's inspector shows its current types and its attachment.
  • A just ui-test check covers it.

Repro on staging 5d1d5f9: nothing in ui/src read ObjectView.attachedTo, so every attachment stood in its controller's row (confirmed in the new fixture's view: opponent's Swift Reconfiguration on your Jace, Vryn's Prodigy). Decisions: attachments tuck behind the host peeking up-right by BENEATH_STEP (mirror of exiled-with cards, which peek up-left), the host's row makes room; only for nonland hosts that are not themselves attached (lands pile by name; none of the implemented cards attach to lands). Hover rings the linked card(s) pale (.card-linked), apart from gold/cyan/ember. The inspector adds the current card types when they differ from the printed type line, printed struck out (two words, e.g. 'Creature Artifact'), and small field frames of what is attached. Protocol carries card types only, not subtypes, so 'Vehicle' is not shown; not worth a protocol change here. New fixture condition 'attached across the table' and fixture attached-across (seed 1, fuzz:1020 vs fuzz:20, greedy) plus shot game-attached and ui-test check.
