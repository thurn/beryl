Design as built (beryl_session game.rs; engine and agent profiles untouched):

  • Auto-pass: under smart stops a human Priority decision is answered Pass by the session when the top of the stack is the human's and, since the human's latest answer in this step, only human-controlled entries came onto the stack or left it (compared as a common-prefix diff of the stack). An opponent's response, or its resolution, still gives priority; a step change resets it, so e.g. an upkeep trigger after the opponent's turn is asked.
  • Trigger order: under smart stops the human's TriggerOrder is answered by a static heuristic over the ability definition: plain effects (no targets, no may_pause) resolve first, then targeted, then may_pause (questions at resolution) last; ties in trigger order. Exact stops ask both.
  • Replay: new Entry::Auto(u8). from_replay skips Auto entries, re-derives them while playing on, and fails BadReplay if its log differs from the file's.
  • Fixtures: auto-answers change recorded games. cast-kicker reseeded (2->3, at 427); pay-plans shot now Thundertrap Trainer at=84; combat-declare at 227->219; combat-double-block remapped (seed search for a double block, see next comment). stack-trigger now stops at the opponent's targeted trigger (the human's own is auto-passed).
