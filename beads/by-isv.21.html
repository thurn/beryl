Description

  • Repro: seed 303 (or 101) vs Expert. Within about 0.5 s of your decision arriving after an opponent turn, press Space or click a playable land.
  • Actual: while the UI still replays the previous views it ignores input with no feedback. A pass loop logged "space ignored ... after 3000 ms" on nearly every pass; land clicks were dropped on seed 202 (vs Searcher) turns 2 and 4. The UI became ready 1.9 s after the decision on the Searcher's turn 5; in seed 101, opponent turns replayed for 8-10 s (D1/083-g1-target-converter-1500ms.png to D1/086-g1-t7-pb-0.png). No way to skip the replay.
  • Expected: input during a replay is never lost: it finishes or fast-forwards the replay, then acts (or queues the action), as Arena lets a click skip animations. Pacing of opponent actions (867fff0, b119167) stays for a player who waits.

Design rules (epic by-isv): follow the skill's design philosophy (one gesture for the common case, the client does the bookkeeping, ask once in context, Arena first, show don't make them infer, few words) and add no standing words to the table. Acceptance criteria

  • A just ui-test check: during a paced replay (fixture with several views between decisions), a Space press or a playable-card click is acted on once the decision is reached, or immediately ends the replay; nothing is dropped.
  • No sleeps or wall-clock thresholds in the check (drive it by the replay's own events).

Derek Thurn2026-11-06

Design: while views are queued (Playback.behind), a pointer-down anywhere on the table (capture phase) or Space/Enter shows every queued view at once (playback 'skip': one view with all their events, so the log and animations lose nothing). Space only ends the replay and does not also pass: the player has not seen the decision it would answer (e.g. their own main phase), so passing blind could skip a land drop. A card pressed during the replay is released on the newest view (React flushes the discrete pointerdown before pointerup), so a playable land clicked mid-replay is played in one gesture; drags work the same. Pacing is unchanged for a player who waits. Checks in ui/scripts/interactions.mjs on Playwright's paused clock: 'Space during the opponent's turn shows the rest of it at once' (opponent-turn) and 'a land clicked while the game plays on to it is played' (greedy-turn-9); both fail without the fix. Note: the cached rust/target had stale fingerprints (Ask::Search from another tree) and needed a touch of the sources to rebuild.
