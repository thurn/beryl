Close reason

Glyph worker (swash), shelf packer and atlas pages sent as Prepared, prepare_latin; on staging at 938f2a820

Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'The flat layer' ('Text'), 'Card faces' ('Preparation': nothing is decoded or rasterized on the frame thread) and 'Hosts' (AssetReady::Glyphs, GlyphKey, Client::wanted_glyphs). The flat-layer slice committed the fonts in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/fonts and exposes their bytes.

In rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_assets:

  • A glyph rasterizer on a worker thread. Given a GlyphKey it rasterizes the glyph to an 8-bit coverage bitmap with swash (the release the cross-target bead pinned; say in Cargo.toml what it replaces).
  • A shelf packer written here, with no packing crate: rows of equal height, filled left to right, in 1,024 by 1,024 single-channel pages. It reports a full page.
  • Each new or changed page is sent with its glyphs' rectangles as a Prepared holding the page's bytes for the renderer and an AssetReady::Glyphs for the client core.
  • prepare_latin(scale): the printable ASCII glyphs of each type style in beryl_scene's tokens module at a display scale, for a host to call before its first frame.

Tests in beryl_assets/tests: the packer never overlaps two glyphs and reports a full page; a glyph asked for twice is rasterized once; the capital P of Open Sans at 28 pixels has coverage inside its stem and none in its bowl.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_assets.

Acceptance criteria

Glyphs asked for by key come back as pages with rectangles, each rasterized once; the packer's tests pass; no glyph is rasterized on the caller's thread.

Contract change from by-q44s.1.21 (flat-layer slice): GlyphKey is now { font, weight, glyph, size } (font indexes beryl_scene::text::fonts::ALL; weight is the CSS weight to set the variable font's wght axis to), and AssetReady::Glyphs is { page, glyphs: Vec<GlyphCell> } with GlyphCell { key, rect: [x,y,w,h] texels, bearing: [x,y] } where bearing is the bitmap's top-left relative to the pen position on the baseline, in physical pixels, y down (negative y rises above the baseline). The client places glyph quads from that bearing (beryl_scene/src/layout/write.rs).
