Description

Native iOS and Android builds of Beryl (Tauri 2 mobile targets) that play a full game against the AI on a portrait phone, with the in-app bug report working on phones.

Rewritten 2026-11-12. M1 (by-7pm.1: the Tauri shells, just ios-smoke and just android-smoke, the phone layout) is done and stands. Project 50k (by-w8gv) then replaced most of what the first plan's M2 built on, so M2's beads were rewritten against the code as it is. This epic is blocked on by-w8gv.21.32, the stage cut-over: every bead below assumes it has landed.

What changed since M1 (read before any bead)

  • The stage (plan K25, ui/src/stage/). One imperative stage plays the session's scripts. The desktop and the phone are two layouts of it. After the cut-over it is the only board: there is no ?stage switch, and ui/src/game/Board.tsx, ChoiceOverlay.tsx, beats.ts, ui/src/mobile/PhoneBoard.tsx's card drawing and ui/scripts/interactions.mjs are deleted.
  • Prompts (plan K26). A prompt's state lives in the session (rust/crates/beryl_session/src/prompt). The UI sends clicks (ipc::CLICK) and draws the marks and buttons it gets back. Every prompt shape (pick, split, zone, order, pay, declare attackers and blockers, yes or no, number, mode, catalog, divide) already works on the phone, with a phone-stage-* shot in ui/shots.json and shape checks in ui/scripts/shapes.mjs. No bead here builds a phone version of a prompt.
  • Already on the phone, do not rebuild: the table, the stack in the middle band, the hand, both strips with life, mana and piles, the dock (the open prompt's buttons), the menu sheet (Log, Report Bug, volumes, Full control, Concede, Leave Game), the log sheet, pile sheets (graveyard, library with a known card, Revealed Cards), undo, the thinking mark, card inspection, arrows, combat lines and marks, every motion verb, and sounds.

What is left (the beads of by-7pm.3)

  1. The start screen, the game-over screen and the small overlays on a phone are the desktop's 1600×900 stage scaled down to the phone's width: unreadable, and they fail the phone audit (by-7pm.3.1).
  2. The opening (coin flip, keep or mulligan, cards to the bottom) likewise (by-7pm.3.2).
  3. The phone draws no exile pile and no emblems (by-7pm.3.3).
  4. The phone's shots on the stage exist at 393×759 only, not 375×647 or 430×850; widths outside 375 to 430 are unhandled; crowded boards are unchecked at the small size (by-7pm.3.4).
  5. A phone has no window snapshot, so a bug report from one has no images (by-7pm.3.5); there is no way to get a report off the phone (by-7pm.3.11); the reporter's UI is the desktop's (by-7pm.3.6).
  6. Nothing has measured the AI, memory, app size or resume-after-kill on a phone (by-7pm.3.7).
  7. Nothing has checked the WebView's own behavior on a device: safe areas, the keyboard, scrolling, selection (by-7pm.3.12).

Engineering defaults

  • One UI codebase. The phone layout is chosen at runtime on a phone (AppInfo.mobile, read in ui/src/App.tsx, held by ui/src/mobile/phone.ts: isPhone(), usePhone()) and forced with ?phone in headless runs. App.tsx puts the class phone on <body> while it is on.
  • Portrait only. Widths 375 to 430, heights from 647.
  • The engine and AI run on the device: the same Rust crates. Desktop behavior must not change in any bead here, and no desktop shot may change.
  • Checks are the generic audits (plan K20; AGENTS.md, Checks). A new phone state is a shot in ui/shots.json with ?phone in its route, which ui/scripts/shots.mjs then holds to phoneFaults (no text under 12px, no control under 40×40), screenFaults, crowdedControls, clippedText and page errors. Add each phone shot at 393×759 and again at 375×647 with the suffix -se. Write a scripted check only where no audit can express the property, with a comment saying why.
  • Native builds stay out of just check. Slow CI's mobile lane (by mobile-check) type-checks the app for both phone targets and must keep passing. A bead that touches rust/crates/beryl_app runs just ios-smoke and just android-smoke before pushing.
  • Only one session drives the simulator and the emulator at a time. The beads that do are chained by dependencies (3.5, 3.11, 3.6, 3.7, 3.12, then the Wardens and 3.10). Do not remove those edges.
  • File paths in these beads are as of staging 53a5bbe6 (2026-11-12), before the cut-over. If a named file is gone, read by-w8gv.21.32's close reason, and find the named symbol with grep -rn "<symbol>" ui/src rust/crates.
