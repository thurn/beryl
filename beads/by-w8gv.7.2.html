Description

Milestone 4 of Project 50k (plan K4): struct fields hold only what the Comprehensive Rules give a player, an object or the turn; everything a card creates lives in three stores, Effects, Memos and Trackers. This bead settles the design the other nine beads of by-w8gv.7 build on, and proves it on the count gate by building the store core and the first store. It is the one design bead of the milestone: the others follow its contract and amend it only where the code proves it wrong.

Read first

  • docs/project-50k.md: decisions K4, K6, K7, K11, K12, and "State" and "Kernel rules" under Target architecture.
  • docs/rules-coverage.md: "Principles" (1 index by listener, 2 check the decks at game start, 3 rare and heavy goes to Large, 6 each mechanism lands with its numbers) and sections 6, 9 and 10, which say how replacements, casting permissions and player-level rule changes are meant to be found. docs/engine-architecture.md §6.4 (capability masks) and §9 (layers).
  • rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs: its rustdoc is the kernel's contract ("Mutators", "Events", "Entities and records", "The legacy accessor"). legacy.rs: the table of what M4 deletes and what replaces each. floating.rs: FloatingEffect, Continuous, Duration, CastPermission.
  • The Mason's six notes for this milestone: the first comment on by-w8gv.7.1 (bd show by-w8gv.7.1). Notes 3 (bytes), 4 (count), 5 (the resolved payload) and 6 (what the contract asks of a store) bind this bead.

Part 1: the contract

Write a "Stores" section in beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs as it will stand when M4 lands (M3 wrote its contract the same way: each later bead builds a part and amends the text). "The legacy accessor" section stays until by-w8gv.7.10 deletes it. The section settles, with numbers:

  1. Where everything goes. One row for each thing M4 moves, naming its store and kind, or a sentence saying why it is the rules' own and stays a field:
    • the effects queue (64 x 24 bytes) and Continuous::ExileInsteadOfGraveyard;
    • the permissions queue (8 x 10 bytes) with CastPermission's extra, exile, flashback and this_turn;
    • Player::sorceries_as_flash; the Rule mask (beryl_card/src/continuous.rs; rule_bit in beryl_kernel/src/table.rs is a u8, so 8 rules at most);
    • State::entered_x; the ObjectFlags KICKED, OFFSPRING, IMPENDING, EVOKED, EXILED_UNTIL, EXILED_WITH, LOYALTY_ACTIVATED;
    • Counters' long-tail pair (other_kind, other);
    • what the scope's list left out (Mason note 2, and one more found while decomposing): Object::linked with State::link, State::exiled_with and zone.rs's forget_exiled_with; StackEntry::exile and StackEntry::flashback (beryl_kernel/src/stack.rs); Casting::extra (pending.rs); ObjectFlags::ONE_ONE (a copy exception, CR 707.9b, part of a token's copiable values); ObjectFlags::ENTERED_THIS_TURN, which the rules never read and one static ability does (cast.rs, Rule::InstantLoyaltyTheTurnItEntered).
    • LOYALTY_ACTIVATED and ENTERED_THIS_TURN are facts about a permanent that end with the turn: say whether such a fact is a memo with an expiry or a tracker keyed by object. by-w8gv.7.8 builds whichever you write.
    • "Exiled with" outlives its source: the cards stay exiled with an object after it leaves its zone, until a new object arrives in its slot (zone.rs forget_exiled_with, CR 607.2a), so it does not simply clear on a zone change. Say how the Memos store holds that.
  2. Shapes, capacities and bytes. For each store: its shape in Small and in Large, the entry's size, the capacity and the total. The Mason measured Object 24, FloatingEffect 24, CastPermission 10, StackEntry 60; the three queues give back about 1,670 bytes and 488 are free. State the target size_of::<State<Small>>() at the end of M4 as a number at most 20,480, and give each later bead an allotment it can meet by itself in any landing order: the lanes after this bead run in parallel, and each bead frees what it replaces in its own push. A locked-in set is a 32-byte bitboard in Small: sets go in their own small table named by index, or the number of set-affecting effects is capped. Counter kinds cannot be per-object fields: a keyed table of (object, kind, count). Object may shrink when linked and the long-tail pair leave it (4 bytes x 255 slots if alignment allows): say whether the plan counts on it. A store that fills returns fixed::Full and the game stalls into Large (principle 3).
  3. Affected sides and payloads. An effect affects an object, a player, a locked-in set (CR 611.2c) or the game. Its payload is a layer change, a rule change, a replacement, a cost change or a permission. Payloads are resolved kernel types, as kernel Duration (carries the Seat) is to beryl_card::Duration: Continuous::Layer(Modification) stores the vocabulary's type today, and Modification::Control(Seat) (beryl_card/src/continuous.rs) names a seat no definition can (K7). It exists for one site, EffectCtx::gain_control in beryl_engine/src/effect.rs. The vocabulary's Modification loses that Seat in this bead.
  4. Duration and expiry, once. One expiry mutator the rules call with the duration that ended (beryl_engine/src/turn.rs: begin_turn and finish_cleanup), since the kernel moves the turn and holds none of its rules; and "while the object stays in its zone", which today is three hand-rolled sweeps (State::drop_stale_effects in mutate.rs, called from derive.rs; Legacy::end_permissions_of_turn; the retain in Legacy::remember_x). Memos clear on a zone change inside the kernel's move.
  5. Indexing and what an absent kind costs. Each store is indexed by kind, and a per-game capability bit, found once in GameTable::new (table.rs already finds listens_any, needy and rules there), makes an absent kind cost one AND. For each hot read say what it costs with the kind absent and present: the layers' loop over effects (layers.rs recompute), event::destination (event.rs), the zone move's reset (zone.rs move_object clears every memo flag with one mask today), the turn's reset (State::reset_permanents_for_turn), and State::signature (zone.rs), which decides whether two objects are interchangeable and must still tell a permanent with a memo from one without.
  6. Static rule changes resolve through the Effects store's query too (by-w8gv.7.3 builds it): one question, "does this rule change apply to this player or permanent now", whether a static ability or a resolved effect makes it. Say how a static's answer is kept current (rules-coverage §10: recomputed with the layers, not found by walking the battlefield as cast::sorcery_speed_limiter does) and how the list of rule kinds grows without a hand-widened mask.
  7. What a store implements once, as a trait or a fixed set of functions in the store's file, so that a new kind adds no code to any of them: an empty store (new.rs), the copy (layout.rs Large::clone_state, an exhaustive destructure, so the compiler finds a new field), Relayout and what does not fit a layout (fit.rs), the invariant check (invariants.rs), the save description (off_board in board.rs, and beryl_session/src/checkpoint.rs), what a player may see (observation.rs), and equality, hashing and signature.
  8. The API. Writes are State mutators, #[inline] and generic over the layout; one whose change a player sees returns its Event and pushes nothing; the mutator that changes a layer input sets derived_dirty itself. Reads are queries by kind. No &mut to a store leaves the crate.

Put the numbers of 2 and 5 in a comment on this bead as well, so the later beads' briefs show them.

Part 2: the store core and the Effects store for objects

Build what part 1's items 4, 5, 7 and 8 describe (the shared core, the capability bits) and the Effects store with the object side and two payloads, the layer change and the replacement "exile it instead of a graveyard" (CR 614.1a). It replaces:

  • kernel: the effects field of State (state.rs), FloatingEffect and Continuous (floating.rs), Legacy::start_effect and Legacy::end_effects (legacy.rs), State::drop_stale_effects (mutate.rs) and State::effects(); the field's lines in layout.rs, new.rs, relayout.rs, fit.rs and board.rs (off_board).
  • engine: EffectCtx::add_effect and gain_control (effect.rs), Run::modify (interpret/run.rs), the loop for e in s.effects() in layers.rs, destination in event.rs, the two expiry calls in turn.rs, and the tests that start effects (beryl_engine/src/tests/events.rs and effects.rs; only tests create ExileInsteadOfGraveyard).
  • beryl_cards/src/blue/thieving_skydiver.rs calls gain_control; Jace, Vryn's Prodigy and The Wandering Emperor reach modify through effect data.

Add no variant or side that nothing uses yet: players, the game and sets (by-w8gv.7.3, by-w8gv.7.5) and the other payloads (by-w8gv.7.3, by-w8gv.7.4) are designed here and built there. CastPermission and Duration stay in floating.rs for by-w8gv.7.4, or move to the store's file if that is where the contract puts Duration.

Timestamps keep their order: an effect takes the next timestamp as it starts (CR 613.7), as Legacy::start_effect does.

Acceptance criteria

beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs has a "Stores" section that answers each of the description's eight items, with a store and kind (or a reason to stay a field) for every listed field, flag and queue, byte sizes and capacities per store, a target size_of::<State<Small>>() at most 20,480 and an allotment per later bead; the numbers are also in a comment on this bead. State has no effects queue: FloatingEffect, Continuous, Legacy::start_effect, Legacy::end_effects, State::drop_stale_effects and Modification::Control's Seat are gone, and the layers, event::destination, add_effect, gain_control, Run::modify and the turn's expiry go through the Effects store's mutators and queries. The store's empty value, copy, relayout, fit, invariant, save description and observation are each written once in its own file, with a test that an effect survives Small to Large and back and that a full store returns Full. GameTable has the capability bits the contract names. just bench-count before and after and the new state size are recorded on the bead; parity holds with no bump to the count gate.

Stores contract: the numbers (beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs, "Stores", has the full text). Read at staging 08d1a377.

COUNT (rollout_counts rollouts subject, just count-gate, bar 405,276,238, no bump):

  • before: 404,858,188
  • with the Effects store, fields in the compiler's order: 406,151,164 (+0.32%). By profile the store's own cost is about 0.03% (the zone move's bit test +81k, State::expire +35k); the rest was loops in layers::recompute and engine::next that read fields whose offsets had moved.
  • after, as pushed: 402,475,964 (-0.59% against before, -0.69% against the bar). State is now repr(C) in a written order: small hot fields first, then zones, objects, derived, the queues, and LAST what M4 changes (effects, permissions, entered_x). A store that grows moves no hot field's offset, so later beads should not see that drift. A new store field goes at the end with the stores.

SIZE: size_of::<State<Small>>() 19,992 -> 20,040 (+40 the store's kinds mask and the keyed slots, +8 padding for the field order). Limit 20,480.

SHAPES in Small (entry bytes x capacity = total):

  • Effects: 24 x 64 + kinds mask = 1,552. Entry is 24 with every side and payload (asserted in effects.rs); Large 32.
  • keyed slots (one set, shared by every store): 32
  • locked-in sets (.5): 32 x 4 = 136
  • rules in force (.3): 8 x 2 players = 16
  • Memos (.6, .7): (slot, memo) 4 x 32 + kinds = 136
  • Trackers (.8): one set of slots per kind keyed by permanent, 32 x 2 = 64
  • Counters (.9): (slot, kind, count) 3 x 32 = 104

ALLOTMENTS (most a bead may change size_of::<State<Small>>() at its own push, any landing order): .3 +16 | .4 -200 or lower | .5 +136 | .6 +88 | .7 0 | .8 +64 | .9 +104 | .10 0 or lower Peak with everything but .4 landed: 20,448. Target with all landed: at most 20,256. The plan does not count on Object shrinking (24 -> 20 needs flags in a u16, so .6 to .9 all landed; -1,020 as a bonus for .10 if the count holds).

WHAT AN ABSENT KIND COSTS:

  • layers' loop over effects (State::layer_effects): one AND of the store's kinds mask; live: one pass over <= 64 entries.
  • event::destination (State::move_replacements): zone compare, then one AND; live: one pass.
  • zone move's reset: one bit test of the keyed slots (State::forget_stored); live: each store's forget, out of line (#[cold]).
  • turn's reset: Effects reads its length twice a turn; Trackers clear 8 words, no walk of the battlefield.
  • State::signature: one bit test of the keyed slots; memos compared only behind it (.6 builds).
  • rule applies: player = one AND of rules in force; permanent = one AND of the table's per-card rules.
  • tracker write: one AND of GameTable::can at the rules' call site.

DECISIONS the later beads build on:

  • Hot reads test the store's own kinds mask (exact), not a GameTable bit: a Rust body is opaque to the table, so a table bit for "can start a layer effect" could only be conservative. GameTable::can(Capability) holds what no live entry can say: RuleChanges, EnteredThisTurn, LoyaltyActivated (all three are in table.rs now).
  • LOYALTY_ACTIVATED and ENTERED_THIS_TURN are trackers keyed by permanent (a set of slots per kind), not memos with an expiry.
  • "Exiled with" is a memo of the exiled card naming the exiler's SLOT; the move ends memos naming a slot as a new object arrives there (battlefield or pool), behind one AND of the Memos kinds.
  • StackEntry::flashback and ::exile both become zone-change replacement entries on the spell (Payload::Move), started at cast from the permission's terms. Both are read at cast::exiled_instead, so .4 should move flashback with exile; .6 need not touch it.
  • ONE_ONE stays an object flag (copiable values, CR 707.9b).
  • A state's Hash is hand-written: fields in the old order, stores hash entries only (not kinds, not keyed). beryl_bench's corpus_never_changes hashes whole states, so a new store must hash only what is game state or that pin moves.
  • Effects on an object now end eagerly inside move_object / cease_to_exist / rebind (State::forget_stored), not lazily at rederive.
