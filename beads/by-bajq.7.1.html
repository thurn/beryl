Description

What is wrong today

A spell's cost is its mana cost or a casting option's, plus a CostChange::Increase kept as an Effects entry on the spell (State::cost_increase). The architecture document says cost modifiers "are reached through explicit engine hooks". No static ability changes a cost, nothing reduces one, and nothing sets a floor.

What to build

Plan: Designs 6, "The total cost".

  1. One function in beryl_rules/src/cast/costs.rs computes the total cost in the rule's order (CR 601.2f): the mana cost or the alternative cost chosen in 601.2b; plus additional costs and increases; minus reductions, which reduce generic mana and never below zero unless the effect says otherwise; then an effect that sets a floor, last, because such an effect says of itself that it applies after all others. The result is locked in. Both the payability prefilter (is this castable at all) and the casting machine (cast/steps.rs) use it.
  2. Static cost changes are rule changes (beryl_card::RuleChange): Governed gains "what casting the spells a filter accepts costs", with an increase, a reduction or a floor. The game numbers them as it numbers the others (rule_changes::RuleKind), so with none in force the check is the AND the rule sets already give (State::rules_for). A condition on the permanent itself (Trinisphere's "as long as this is untapped") is the ability's condition.
  3. The floor counts the total mana (CR 601.2f, "would cost less than three mana to cast"): a spell with X counts its X; a spell cast for an alternative cost of no mana costs three; the difference may be paid with any type of mana.
  4. The solver sees the final cost only.

Cards

  • Trinisphere: "As long as this artifact is untapped, each spell that would cost less than three mana to cast costs three mana to cast."
  • Thalia, Guardian of Thraben, 2/1 first strike: "Noncreature spells cost {1} more to cast."

Scenarios: a one-mana spell under each and under both (Thalia's increase first, then the floor: three, not four); a tapped Trinisphere; Counterspell under Thalia; flashback under both.

Files

The two card files, beryl_card/src/continuous.rs, beryl_kernel/src/{rule_changes.rs, effects.rs}, beryl_rules/src/{cast/costs.rs, cast/steps.rs, cast/legal.rs, rule_change.rs}.

Derek Thurn2026-11-15

Decisions: (1) Static cost changes are Governed::SpellCosts {spells, change: SpellCost::More|Less|AtLeast} with a new When::AsUsual (RuleChange::spell_costs), numbered as RuleKind::SpellCost. Deviation from the plan: they are asked of the permanents that make them, not kept in the players' rule sets via the layers, because Trinisphere's 'as long as this artifact is untapped' is a condition and the layers refuse conditional player rule changes. With none among the game's cards the total cost asks one test of t.rules_of(SpellCost). Plan (docs/rules-coverage.md Designs 6) and the kernel contract amended. (2) Locked in: the casting machine reads the total only before payment (mana first, whole), so no new state. (3) Free-cast floor (an alternative cost of no mana costs three) is shown by a rules unit test, not a card scenario: the only free-cast card is Malcolm's chorus-counter trigger. Count: experiments showed even a cold body reading nothing costs ~177K once reachable; reading Copiable::rules there cost ~660K more from inlining, so makers are found with maker_of. Before rebase: 392,904,161 -> 393,136,079. After rebase onto 098eb433 (Mind Bend), staging alone measures 395,314,570 (+1.29%, already over tolerance) and with this change 395,513,584 (+199,014, +0.05%).
