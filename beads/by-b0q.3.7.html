Description

Part of Milestone 1 (epic by-b0q): a rules-exact engine, game UI and AI opponents for a mirror match of the 40-card WU Control main deck in docs/sample-decks/2026-10-17-PrinceJools-wu-control.txt (29 distinct cards). Read the epic's description (plan, card table) and design field (decisions) first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md).

docs/ui_mockups/combat.png and trigger_on_stack_with_target.png. The overlays bead edits the same screen in parallel; expect to merge.

  • Combat: drag attackers forward (click fallback), choosing a planeswalker or the player when offered; block by dragging a blocker onto an attacker; attack and block lines; attacker and blocker frames; the "Combat, Blockers declared" banner; damage division when offered; the button naming the step ("Resolve Damage").
  • The stack: spells and abilities as large cards at the right (the triggered-ability frame), a gold line from the source, cyan lines to the targets, pass and respond, the priority pill while the opponent decides.
  • Crew: choose the creatures to tap.
  • Fixture states for ui-shots: attackers declared, blockers declared, a triggered ability with a target on the stack, a spell on the stack with a counterspell in hand.

Executor notes (decisions and deviations): • Combat: attack/block candidates are draggable. An attacker dragged forward (60 px) attacks; dropped on an opposing planeswalker or the opponent's life orb it also answers the following attackTarget decision with that defender. A blocker dropped onto an attacker declares the block and answers the blockTarget decision with that attacker (an 'intent' the next view consumes). Click fallback: candidates glow cyan and a click picks them; the turn button finishes the declaration (Attack / No Attacks / Block / No Blocks / Assign Damage). Damage division picks blockers by click, with the attacker glowing gold. Crew needed nothing new: the crew ability is on the stack, so the Vehicle glows gold and the creatures are cyan picks. • Attack lines (gold hairlines to the defending orb or planeswalker), block lines (attacker to blocker's arc), marks facing the other side, the COMBAT banner (Declare attackers / Attackers declared / Declare blockers / Blockers declared / Combat damage), 'Resolve Damage' / 'Combat damage' once blockers are declared. Attackers stand 16 px forward and blockers opposite their attacker (as combat.png); the rows are too close for more advance, left to pixel fidelity by-b0q.3.18. • Stack: spells and abilities at the mockup's place and size (295 wide at 1205,222), lower items peeking above; abilities use the StackItem frame labelled Triggered/Activated Ability with their own paragraph. Protocol: StackItem::Ability gains 'triggered' and 'nth' (the ability's ordinal among its kind, to find its paragraph; falls back to the whole text). Top item: gold line from an ability's source, cyan lines to targets, source glows gold and targets cyan at any priority. The stack rises 90 px while a prompt panel is open so they don't overlap. • Fixtures: beryl_cli fixture definitions take 'where' conditions (step, attacking, blocking, targeted trigger on the stack, opponent's spell on the stack, castable <card>). New shots game-attackers, game-blockers, game-trigger, game-counter; new ui-test checks for drag-to-attack, click-to-attack, the finish button, drag-to-block and click-to-block. • Arrows follow moving cards by re-measuring every animation frame while any arrow is drawn.
