Close reason

one Standings and one matchup namer for schedule, summary and panel; both views show the WLS Elo per tripling (4867adf)

Description

Mason finding (by-2l8.11), long-think runner, rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/longthink/.

Current design

Three aggregations of games.jsonl per matchup, each with its own Verdict-to-points mapping: run::tally into schedule::Tally (first pairs only, for the chain rule), summary::Row, and a private Tally inside panel::ladder. Three namers of a matchup: Spec::new's key prefix, matchup_label, panel::names (an omniscient matchup is "omniscient-3k-v-…", "omniscient 3k v …" and "3k omni"). And two different statistics both called "Elo per tripling": summary.txt prints the difference between adjacent rungs' Elo over log3 of their budget ratio, the live panel prints a weighted least-squares slope over every rung, so the two views of one run disagree under one name.

Proposed design

One Standings built once from the games (and clock), keyed by Matchup, read by the schedule, the summary and the panel:

impl Verdict { fn points(self) -> Option<f64> }   // None for Crashed
struct Standing { games: u32, scored: u32, score: f64, first_scored: u32,
                  first_score: f64, rollouts: u64, budget: u64, searched: u64,
                  seconds: Option<f64> }
struct Standings(BTreeMap<Matchup, Standing>);
impl Matchup { fn key(&self, c: &Config) -> String; fn label(&self, c: &Config) -> String }

GameRecord could carry a Matchup rather than rung and against side by side (serde-flattened, same JSON). Pick one "Elo per tripling" (the WLS slope with its margin is the better estimate) and show it in both places, or name the adjacent-rung steps differently.

Eliminates

Two of three tallies and points mappings, two of three namers, and the conflicting statistic.

Must not change

The schedule's order of matchups (schedule.rs's tests), game keys, and every number in summary.txt and the panel other than the Elo-per-tripling line chosen.

Smallest acceptance test

The schedule, summary and panel tests pass as they are; one new test builds a run's games and asserts summary.txt and the panel frame show the same Elo per tripling.

Acceptance criteria

schedule, summary.txt and the panel read one per-matchup aggregation and one matchup namer; summary and panel show the same Elo-per-tripling statistic, asserted by a test; schedule order, game keys and other summary numbers unchanged.
