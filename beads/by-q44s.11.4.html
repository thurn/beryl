Close reason

Shadow map from one key light: cards cast on table and cards below, 4-tap PCF, data in space.json (478e99d)

Description

Read first: the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md; docs/beryl-3d.md 'The Enhanced tier' (a shadow map) and 'The Baseline tier' (shadows are single quads, which this replaces in Enhanced).

Every card casts a shadow of its true shape, from one key light, on the table and on the cards below it. Edit only src/enhanced/shadow.rs and src/enhanced/shadow.wgsl in beryl_render, the tests named below, and data/beryl3d. The renderer skeleton bead made both files as stubs and calls them.

What exists: shadow::draw (records nothing), shadow::light_view_projection(frame) (identity) and, in shadow.wgsl, lit_by_key(light_position: vec4<f32>) -> f32 (returns 1.0), which fs_lit multiplies into the card's and the table's color. Binding 1 of group 1 is a 1 by 1 depth texture. The client leaves contact shadow records out of Enhanced frames. The Baseline's shadows fall along shadow.light in data/beryl3d/space.json, applied by cast in beryl_scene src/table/shadows.rs.

Build:

  • The light. Add enhanced.shadow to data/beryl3d/space.json: map: 2048 (pixels on a side), darkness: 0.55 (Baseline's shadow.alpha), bias: 2 and slope_bias: 2.0, taps: 4. The key light is directional, and points so that a card at height h casts its shadow where cast puts the Baseline's quad: read cast, and use the direction toward the light that gives the same offset per unit of height (normalize(-light.x, -light.y, 1) in the table's axes if cast adds light * height; follow cast's signs). light_view_projection is an orthographic projection along that direction fitted to the part of the table the camera sees: the eight corners of the camera's frustum clipped to the heights 0 and 60 mm, from the inverse of frame.view_projection. Write the direction into Lit::key too, through shadow::key_direction(), which light.rs's lighting reads, so the lit side and the shadow agree.
  • Shadow, made with Enhanced: the depth-only pipeline (vertex stage vs_shadow in shadow.wgsl, no fragment stage, DEPTH, the space.json biases as DepthBiasState, back faces not culled because a card turned over still casts), and the map by map depth texture that replaces the 1 by 1 one in binding 1. draw begins a depth-only pass on it and draws each run of runs(frame) whose mesh is Mesh::Card and whose records are before frame.layer: every card on the table, in flight, held up, in a hand or on the stack, opaque or blended. The table and every sprite cast nothing.
  • lit_by_key: the position in the light's clip space to texture coordinates, then the average of taps textureSampleCompareLevel taps in a 2 by 2 pattern one texel apart; returns 1 - darkness * (1 - lit). A point outside the map is lit.

Tests, in beryl_render/tests/all/shadow.rs, each drawing a hand-built frame in Tier::Enhanced and reading single pixels (C5: no whole pictures):

  • a card lifted 20 mm over an empty table darkens a table pixel where cast would put its shadow's centre to within 10% of darkness, and leaves a table pixel 40 mm the other side unchanged from a frame without the card;
  • a card lifted 20 mm over a card lying on the table darkens a pixel of the lower card's face, compared with the same frame without the lifted card;
  • a card lying flat on the table does not shadow its own face (no acne): its centre pixel equals the frame drawn with darkness 0, within 2/255;
  • the same frame drawn twice gives the same pixels. Add the numbers (each pixel and its expected value) to a comment on this bead.

Must not build: the key light's shading of colors (the lighting bead), bloom, any gate limit.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_render; just b3d png verbs-zones --at 1.5s --tier enhanced (look at the picture and say in a comment what you saw); just b3d lane.

Beads named above: the contract bead (the tier input and the check) is by-q44s.11.2; the renderer skeleton bead is by-q44s.11.3; the shadow map bead by-q44s.11.4; the lighting bead by-q44s.11.5; bloom by-q44s.11.6; particles by-q44s.11.7; the tier choice bead by-q44s.11.8; the settings row by-q44s.11.9; the limits bead by-q44s.11.10; the settings screen bead by-q44s.5.27. Every bead of this milestone depends on by-q44s.4, the review gate before it.

Derek Thurn2026-11-17

Pixel numbers (this Mac's Metal, overhead camera, light shading only by the shadow, stub light.wgsl): (1) grey table [180,180,180] at the lifted card's shadow centre (2.8, -10) mm, card 0.15x board size at 20 mm: [81,81,81], a darkening of 0.55 = darkness; table at (-10.8, 38.5), 40 mm the other way: unchanged from the frame without the card. (2) lower board card at height 1, lifted small card at 21 mm: the lower face at (2.8, -10) loses 0.55 of its brightness. (3) flat card on the table: centre and three other face points equal Baseline within 3/255 (no acne). (4) same frame twice: identical pixels over a 21x21 grid. Shader costs: card_lit 5 samples / 232 expressions, table_lit 5 / 200, particle 5 / 199 (fs_lit runs lit_by_key for every fragment); gates.json raised to these.

Picture (just b3d png verbs-zones --at 1.5s --tier enhanced): no acne or banding on any card face; the stacked library/graveyard piles now show soft shadows down and to the right of each stack, and the lower cards of each pile are darker than the top one. Flat battlefield cards cast only sub-millimetre shadows. Compared with the Baseline picture pixel by pixel, the darkened pixels are only around the piles and battlefield card edges.

Deviations: (a) the acne test's reference is the Baseline frame, not 'the frame drawn with darkness 0': darkness is the WGSL constant SHADOW_DARKNESS (the Enhanced shader is one fixed concat! source and only shadow.rs/shadow.wgsl may change, so no uniform could carry it), and no public API can draw without the map. The lighting bead's rule makes a flat unshadowed card equal Baseline within 3/255, so the test holds across both beads. A test holds SHADOW_DARKNESS and the four taps equal to space.json. (b) The table shares Mesh::Card, so vs_shadow moves non-card materials past the far plane; only card-mesh runs are drawn. (c) The map pass uses its own bind group 1 holding only Lit (the map cannot be bound while drawn into), made lazily per slot from a cloned device. (d) The light's depth range reaches from the slab up to the highest frustum corner along the light, so held and hand cards above 60 mm still cast. (e) light.rs is not touched: shadow::key_direction() exists for the lighting bead to read.
