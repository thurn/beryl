Description

The session holds a prompt's state (plan K26): the framework every shape uses, and the first shape on it, picking objects on the table.

Start from the design bead's handoff (first bead of lane:prompt-shapes) and the wire bead's (the bead before this one in this lane).

Framework

  • A prompt module in rust/crates/beryl_session/src holding the open prompt's state beside the open decision: made when a view with a decision is built (Game::next_view, game.rs about 1257; OpenDecision, about 41 to 160), changed by click commands, dropped when the prompt confirms, is cancelled or undone (game.rs cancel about 1315, undo about 1363), and made afresh when a game is resumed or replayed with a prompt open. One module per shape under it, each under 800 lines.
  • The click command, end to end: its constant and request in ipc.rs (beside CHOOSE), Sessions and Session dispatch (sessions.rs about 340, session.rs about 108 and 261, where preview_waterbend is wired today), the Tauri command (rust/crates/beryl_app/src/games.rs about 44 and its registration in lib.rs about 223), the specs and fixture servers (rust/crates/beryl_cli/src/serve.rs about 185, fixture.rs), and a typed wrapper in ui/src/ipc.ts with the fixture transport replaying its replies (as previewWaterbend does, ipc.ts about 489).
  • The prompt a seat is sent with its view carries its opening state: marks and buttons, not only shape and purpose.
  • A folded run: when a prompt covers several engine picks, the session answers them in turn as each decision arrives, and the seat sees one prompt until the run ends. The automatic answers the session already gives (trigger_to_stack, without_floating, the halts; game.rs about 2138 to 2210) are the pattern.
  • The test harness for hand-written snapshots, in rust/crates/beryl_session/tests.

The pick shape

Purposes and today's code, to be checked against the design's table:

  • Priority: eligible marks on what can be played, cast or activated, from the decision's candidates; the pass button's label and state from pass_to, pass_on and halt; a click on a card with one way to use it answers the pick. A card with several ways opens the mode shape, which a later bead of this lane builds: until then the click's reply says so and the old UI's chooser goes on working.
  • Targets: Target and TargetCount as one prompt (CandidateView::targets), with friendly-target and hostile-target marks. DecisionView::self_removal_targets is deleted; the old UI reads the marks instead (ui/src/game/Board.tsx and tablePick.ts).
  • Picking up to N objects on the table: ChooseUpTo and ChooseObject over permanents (untap up to N: ui/src/game/untapChoice.ts; the legend rule), with selected marks and a confirm button that says how many.

DECISIONS AND DEVIATIONS (by-w8gv.11.8)

  1. Where the code is. rust/crates/beryl_session/src/prompt/held.rs is the framework: Held (the open prompt's state: the PromptView as last sent plus the shape's own state), Clicked (what a click did: Same, Changed, Answers(run), PassesOn, Opens(prompt)), Run and Step (the picks a click that answers stands for), Table (the game a prompt opens in) and add_opening_state (public, for the hidden-information test). prompt/pick.rs is the pick shape (Pick::Priority | Pick::Objects), with pick/priority.rs and pick/untap.rs under it. The Game's side is a child module, src/game/prompts.rs (Game::click, hold, pick_in_run), because game.rs is at its length allowance: Game::answer, Game::pass_on and Game::preview_waterbend moved there unchanged to make room. by-w8gv.19.5 will meet src/game/ as a directory.
  2. State is keyed by decision, not dropped by hand. Game.prompt is Option<(DecisionId, Held)>: made as a decision's view is drawn (draw_for), kept while that decision is open (a stops change redraws it with its state and a reworded pass button), and replaced when a view of another decision is drawn. choose, cancel, undo and concede need no code: each ends in a new decision id.
  3. The run. Step::Index(i) is the open decision's candidate (a priority's click); Step::One(part, Number(n) | Done) and Step::Among(part, subjects) are found in each decision as it arrives. Among takes its objects in whatever order the engine offers them, the object itself first and else one the engine offers in its place (beryl_engine::interchangeable), so picks of twins go through. A step the arriving decision does not ask is passed over (the design's "a pick the engine did not ask is skipped"); a decision of another purpose, the agent's decision, a step boundary or the end ends the run. Known limit, with no card today: a step passed over stays unknown until a decision arrives, so a leftover target could answer a later spec's decision of the same part that offers it.
  4. Continuations. Game::choose keeps its guard for a client's answers (continuation only while casting). A run's later picks and a replayed entry go through answer_as, which honours the flag anywhere: the log's flag already says what the session did, so old saves replay as before (tests/corpus.rs passes unchanged) and a save made by clicks replays with its undo groups.
  5. DecisionView.self_removal_targets became ShapeView::Pick.warned, not a mark: a thing carries one mark from the closed list, and a warned target is also eligible. The session asks itself: a click on a warned target picks it (friendly-target), the title becomes "Target your <name>?" and the buttons Confirm and Cancel; Decline there takes the pick back (ButtonId::Decline's comment says so). The old UI reads warned through decisions.ts warnedTarget.
  6. PromptButton.caption is new: the pass button's second line ("Next | To Main 2"), as Arena's and today's turn button have. The words are wording.rs pass_words; PassOn's label is its destination's caption ("To End Step").
  7. Two errors: SessionError::PromptNotHeld (a click on a prompt whose state the session does not hold yet) and StalePrompt { revision, open }.
  8. A priority click on a card with several ways to use it replies Open with the act prompt (mode act, the card's name for its title, about [slot], no state and no buttons) and leaves the priority's state as it was. by-w8gv.11.10 gives act its state.
  9. Targets, beyond the design's summary, from what the old UI does (Board.tsx stackTargets, the Prompt's faces):
    • Among spells on the stack alone, with exactly one of them an opponent's, that one opens picked, the title says "Target <name>", and the prompt answers by Confirm only: a click on another spell changes the pick.
    • Targets in a graveyard, an exile or a library are laid out in tray. A target answers by its click wherever it is picked, tray or table; the design had "tray empty" for that.
    • A count is folded for any numbers offered (least and most are the least and greatest count); only {0, 1} exists today. Its title is still wording.rs's "How many targets?" past one, which no card reaches: by-w8gv.11.7's to reword.
    • Target's wording gained confirm "Confirm" and counted "Confirm {picked}/{of}".
  10. NOT in the session yet: "Counter your own spell, <name>?" (exception 7's other half). The old UI finds a counterspell by a regex over Oracle text, and the engine has no such fact: target_removes_permanent covers permanents only, and three of the four counterspells have Rust bodies, so it needs a declaration on their target specs. Left in the old UI, noted on the Warden bead by-w8gv.11.12. Also still the old UI's: the lone-target shortcut (Game.tsx canAutoPick), " for <source>" after a target's title, and the mana-lost pulse on a pass.
  11. The untap's opening picks are Rust's (pick/untap.rs) and the old UI reads them from the prompt's state; ui/src/game/untapChoice.ts and its ui-test are deleted. The score is the old one (cards castable, then colors made, then distinct colors, first wins), on the engine's check: the open decision is answered with each choice of lands on a copy of the game under Stops::Forced, played to the next decision, and a card in hand counts when beryl_engine::refusal is None or Target (payable at a time it may be cast; a counterspell with nothing to counter yet counts). Only cards in hand when the prompt opens count.
  12. Fixtures. The transport replays recorded click replies in order like any command's, and one that says answered plays the recording on as choose does. beryl_cli fixture.rs records none: its stand-in answers by picks. The specs server and serve.rs pass click through to Sessions; a spec moment is where a stage is driven by clicks.
  13. The hidden-information test (tests/sessions.rs no_view_shows_an_identity_its_viewer_does_not_know) now also builds the opening prompt state from the redealt state and still passes.
