Description

Read first: docs/beryl-3d.md 'Verbs', 'Effects' (impact, shake, trail) and 'Interface checks'; the epic by-q44s ('Rules for every bead'); rust/beryl3d/AGENTS.md. Code: beryl_scene src/verbs/strike.rs (stub), src/verbs/mod.rs (Places::pose for what is struck, a card or a player), and the step's StepTiming. Web: ui/src/stage/tracks.ts (STRIKE: how far the creature rears, how near it stops and how it drifts back; struck: the point it strikes at; lunge: the path, in the phases rear, hang, dash and drift back; depths, meets), ui/src/stage/layout.ts (StageLayout::middle and half: 'how far it reaches from its middle, which a strike at it stops by'). Data: strike in data/ui-conventions.json, with its cases blocked attacker and unblocked attacker and its phases (poise 0.32 and hit 0.376, as shares of the step), which the session sends with every strike step as StepTiming::phases (by-q44s.2.3).

Units. The web stage is 1600 by 900 stage pixels and Beryl 3D's table is in millimetres. A value the web gives as a share of a card, a share of a step's time or an angle is copied as it is. A distance in stage pixels becomes millimetres as pixels x 63 / the width in stage pixels of a battlefield card in ui/src/game/layout.ts. Every such value goes in data/beryl3d/space.json under verbs.<verb> (or the key the bead names) and is read through the constants beryl_scene's build script writes (data::space), never as a literal.

Build:

  • The strike's phases come in its StepTiming::phases: the at of the phase named poise and of the one named hit, as shares of the step. Read them there, as the web stage does (strikePhases in tracks.ts). beryl_scene reads nothing from data/ui-conventions.json.
  • strike gives its source four segments, in a new Shape::Lunge or as plain segments with the curves lunge uses: it rears back and up until poise, hangs, dashes to stop short of what it strikes by that thing's half extent (Reach::half) at hit, and drifts back to its place by the step's end. The distances are verbs.strike.{rear, lift, near}, copied from STRIKE. What is struck does not move. A strike at a player dashes toward the player's anchor.
  • When the act carries the struck object's record (Act::Strike::object), the record replaces the table's at hit.
  • The impact, shake and trail effects arrive through the step's timing and are by-q44s.3.23's and by-q44s.3.24's to draw.

Tests, in beryl_scene/tests/verbs_strike.rs, on views and scripts built by hand with testing::ViewBuilder, ScriptBuilder and testing::timing: for each case of the verb in data/ui-conventions.json (blocked attacker, unblocked attacker), a script with that case's duration and delay plays to its end with every object at its placement (agent::steps::rest_equals_placement finds nothing), no_jumps finds nothing, and step_times finds nothing. The source is farthest from what it strikes at the poise of the step's phases and nearest at its hit; its nearest point is outside the struck card's corners on screen; a strike at a player ends nearer the anchor than it began; the source's last segment ends at its placement.

When it is done: set BUILT = true in the verb's file, so that Client::next_unbuilt stops reporting it; run just b3d test --update and just b3d timeline-parity --update if those commands exist on staging by then, and commit the lowered lists.

Run: just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_scene; just b3d film <a recording with combat> --frames 12 --crop 'card "<the attacker>"'; just b3d lane.

Acceptance criteria

The tests pass; Client::next_unbuilt does not report strike; a comment names a recording with a strike step (from just b3d list and info) and gives the path of a just b3d film of that step; just b3d check on it reports no no-jumps, step-shown or rest-placement fault for the step.
