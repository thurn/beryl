Close reason

Kept hand drops in 130 ms and bottom-slot cards glide; both settled entries are recording entries

Description

UI Conventions has two settled entries about the opening screen that Beryl 3D does not yet show following: "The kept opening hand" and "Cards put on the bottom after a mulligan". data/beryl3d/conventions/settled.json marks both open with this bead's id.

  1. The kept hand: in just b3d tree opening-keep --at 2166ms .. --at 2450ms, card "Plains" #33 takes about 300 ms to drop from the opening screen's row (160,247) into the hand (405,735). The web's drop is 130 ms, eased out (KEPT in ui/src/stage/arrive.ts, ScriptStage.arrive in ui/src/stage/stage.ts). Make Beryl 3D's drop match (find where beryl_scene moves the opening hand's cards into hand.yours when the opening screen closes; the opening's look is Look::opening in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/play/step.rs).
  2. The bottom slots: opening-bottom at prompt:3 asks for a card to put on the bottom, but the recording has no response to a card click (just b3d tree opening-bottom --at prompt:3 --do 'press card "Stock Up" #8' panics with 'the recording has no response for click'). Add the clicks to the fixture (ui/fixtures.json clicks, as shape-mode's) or a new fixture, then check a clicked card glides into its slot under the hand in about 0.25 s and back up when clicked again (rule in data/ui-conventions.json), fixing beryl_scene where it does not.

Then replace both open entries in data/beryl3d/conventions/settled.json with {recording, at, shows} entries (optional do, seat, still: see assert_shows in rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_inspect/tests/all/screens.rs).

Acceptance criteria

Both entries in data/beryl3d/conventions/settled.json are recording entries; just b3d cargo-test -p beryl_inspect conventions and just b3d test pass; enact push passes.
