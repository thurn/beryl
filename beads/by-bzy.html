Close reason

Anchor skips never-cast cards (8c2feda); greedy unchanged since its Mana Crypt rule is measured. Deep Searcher's anchor cost 0.011 -> 0.003 a game.

Description

Found by by-7wl's anchor-replay (2026-11-07): beryl_search::guarded makes an anchored search play greedy's pass over casting a spell unless the search's lead clears ANCHOR_MARGIN and its noise. Greedy (RolloutPolicy, Knowledge::Tuned) passes on casting Mana Crypt, so Deep Seer and Deep Searcher hold Mana Crypt whenever their search is unsure: 2 of the omniscient leader's 10 guarded switches at 10M in run-003, and 5 of the fair pimc72/hidden@30M family's 10. A strong player casts Mana Crypt almost always, so holding it looks wrong.

Look at why greedy's priority scoring passes on it (its card value, or its damage trigger counted against it), fix it generically if the cause is general, and rerun beryl_cli anchor-replay <run> <entrant> --guarded --recheck N to confirm the switches and their cost drop. Rollouts use the same policy, so check the change in a paired A/B before keeping it.

Notes

2026-11-07: greedy's Mana Crypt rule (never cast, value 0) was deliberate: by-0w2.17 measured it worth ~6 points of greedy's strength, its coin flips killing a policy that does not plan. So greedy and the rollouts are unchanged; the anchor (beryl_search::guarded) now skips cards the policy is told never to cast. anchor-replay --guarded after the fix, δ 0.02, fresh rechecks: pimc72/hidden/rollout/tuned@30M 0.3 switches a game, cost 0.0031 ± 0.0017 a game (was 0.011 ± 0.006); pimc18/omniscient/rollout/tuned@10M 0.2 a game, 0.0007 ± 0.0005 (was 0.0047 ± 0.0013; part of that drop is the turn discount, by-a8g, shrinking leads). No Mana Crypt among the switches; the Ousts remain. Also: when the verification phase runs, the anchor now decides by that pair's check even if greedy's answer ends with more visits.
