Close reason

Object, Player and Turn fields are pub(crate); all readers use getters; slow CI green on 85b5d663

Description

Milestone 3 (plan K6): the last step of "fields are private". The beads before this one gave Object, Player and Turn an #[inline] getter for every field and removed every way to get a &mut to one outside the kernel. Their fields are still pub only because other crates read them. This bead converts those reads and flips the fields. It is mechanical: no design, no behavior change.

Do

  1. Read the handoffs of the by-w8gv.5.3 (objects) and by-w8gv.5.7 (players and turn) beads (enact brief shows them): each lists its getters with the field each replaces. Read the API contract in rust/crates/beryl_kernel/src/lib.rs.
  2. Make every field of Object, Player and Turn private (in beryl_kernel, the files split from state.rs).
  3. Fix each compile error by calling the getter: o.zone becomes o.zone(), o.flags.has(ObjectFlags::TAPPED) becomes the getter the handoff names, obs.turn().step becomes obs.turn().step(). Counts when this was decomposed: beryl_search about 96 object reads, 58 flag reads, 19 player and 26 turn; beryl_session 14, 29, 1 and 10; beryl_cli 3, 1, 3 and 17; beryl_cards 11, 2, 1 and 4; beryl_scenario 10, 5 and 3; beryl_bench 5 turn reads; plus beryl_engine's own remaining reads and its tests.
  4. Observation::object returns a masked copy of an Object: it stays, and its callers use the same getters.
  5. If a field has no getter, add an #[inline] one in the style of its neighbours with the same rule citation the field carries. Never add a setter or a &mut getter: a caller that writes a field is a bug in an earlier bead; file it under by-w8gv.5 and leave that one field pub with a comment naming the bead.

Not this bead

beryl_search/src/expert and beryl_search/src/frozen are frozen copies: change field reads to getters there and nothing else. Do not rename, reorder or reformat beyond what just fmt does. The fields milestone 4 deletes stay behind the legacy accessor.

Checks

just check passes; cargo test -p beryl_session --test corpus and cargo test -p beryl_bench rollouts_never_change pass; slow CI's counts lane holds with no bump (getters are #[inline], so the count should not move).

Acceptance criteria

Object, Player, Turn, Zones and State in beryl_kernel have no pub field (grep -n '^ pub [a-z_]*:' on their definitions finds none); the workspace builds and just check passes; no getter was added that returns &mut`; nothing but field reads changed in beryl_search/src/expert and frozen/; parity holds.
