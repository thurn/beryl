Description

What is wrong today

Replacement effects are five cases written by hand: Replacement::EntersTapped and its two siblings in event::enter_replacements, MoveReplacement in event::destination, and Replacement::WouldDraw, which may only win or lose the game (beryl_kernel/src/replacement.rs). event::destination's comment says that the ordering of CR 616.1 "never becomes a choice". A replacement of every card going to a graveyard cannot be written.

What to build

Plan: Designs 3; decision R6. This bead builds the pipeline without the ordering choice, which by-bajq.3.2 adds.

  1. Proposals. A typed value built before the state changes, for a move (object, from, to, place, reason, and how a permanent enters, today's event::Entry), one card drawn, and damage. The functions that build them are the ones that exist: event::move_batch_to, drawing::draw, damage::deal.
  2. The vocabulary. beryl_card::Replacement becomes a product, as Trigger is: what is replaced (the kind, a filter over the object or source, whose it is, and "this" for an object's own), whether it is optional, and a rewrite. Rewrites: go to another zone; enter tapped, with counters, or under a condition; skip; do an Effect instead, in which Players::ThatPlayer is the affected player. The card table refuses a product the rules have no arm for. The three entering variants and WouldDraw become entries of this form, and the four cards that use them are rewritten with no change in behavior.
  3. Sources. Static abilities of permanents (through State::replacing, by-bajq.1.1), of objects in a zone where the ability works (by-bajq.1.3), entries of the Effects store (effects::Payload::Move becomes one kind of them), and the entering object's own.
  4. The loop (CR 616.1), in a new beryl_rules/src/replace.rs: gate on the kind; collect what applies and has not applied to this event (CR 614.5), in a scratch list of at most 16; take the classes in order (616.1a self-replacement first); treat rewrites that are the same data as one; apply; collect again (616.1f). Where more than one candidate is left in a class, this bead applies them in timestamp order behind a debug_assert that by-bajq.3.2 replaces with the choice.
  5. Entering uses layers::as_it_would_exist (by-bajq.2.5) to find what applies.
  6. A replaced event never happens, so nothing triggers on it (CR 614.6): a card exiled instead of dying triggers no "dies".

Card

Rest in Peace: "When this enchantment enters, exile all graveyards. If a card or token would be put into a graveyard from anywhere, exile it instead."

Scenarios: a creature that would die is exiled and its dies trigger does not trigger; a token is exiled; a discarded card is exiled and a discard trigger still triggers (it was discarded); a spell that resolves is exiled; two Rest in Peace are one candidate. (Tarmogoyf at 0/1 under Rest in Peace is by-bajq.5.1's scenario.)

Files

beryl_card/src/{continuous.rs, check.rs, render/}, beryl_kernel/src/{replacement.rs, effects.rs, table.rs, lib.rs}, beryl_rules/src/{event.rs, drawing.rs, damage.rs, keywords/flashback.rs}, new beryl_rules/src/replace.rs. Cards rewritten: Seachrome Coast, Soporific Springs (or the cards that use the two "unless" variants), every land that enters tapped, Jace, Wielder of Mysteries.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate; R2 in the plan). The count is exact, so report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust). A change to what the WU mirror's rollouts do is a bug in this epic.
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (root AGENTS.md); just citations.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill: just card new, just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge), every instruction carried out by its own scenarios, and just specs <card> passing the generic UI checks from both seats. A disagreement with an oracle is settled by the rules and recorded in data/oracle-divergences.json.
  • Read the card's rulings before writing its scenarios (just card brief <name> prints Scryfall's). Each statement in a ruling that a scenario can show is a scenario of the card. Where one cannot be shown yet, the close reason names the ruling and why.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry.
  • If the design in the plan is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.

Acceptance criteria
