Description

Why

turn::next_step (rules) is a match over thirteen TurnSteps, and several readers compare steps with < (stops.rs, mana/lookahead.rs, the engine's Limit, next_in_step's (turn, step) pair). Nothing can add a step or a phase, skip one, or cut the turn short. docs/engine-architecture.md once said extra combat phases insert; the code never did. Plan: Designs G, "The turn as a plan".

What to build

  • The kernel's Turn (beryl_kernel/src/turn.rs) gains the rest of this turn as a plan: a short Fixed queue of steps still to come, filled from Format::turn as the turn begins, and the ordinal of the current step in the turn as played.
  • Mutators: insert a step or a phase after or before a named one (CR 500.8 and 500.9 say where: the most recently created comes first), remove one, cut the plan to the cleanup step. Each returns its event.
  • Capability::TurnStructure (table/capability.rs): set when a card of the game can change the turn. Without it the engine keeps today's match and stores no plan, so the mirror pays one AND. With it, next_step reads the plan.
  • Every reader that compared two TurnSteps, or identified a step by (turn, TurnStep), uses the ordinal or a named query: the smart stops (stops.rs), the mana lookahead, the Limit of engine.rs, next_in_step, the delayed triggers of trigger.rs ("at the beginning of the next end step" must fire once, at the next one), the draw-step trackers (drawing.rs, replace/draws.rs), and the session's script, which names a step to the UI (beryl_session/src/script): a second upkeep is announced as an upkeep.
  • The plan's bytes: take them from the kernel contract's allotment table and report the size. A turn with more inserted steps than the queue holds is Full, which widens the game to Large.
  • Saves: beryl_session's checkpoint and replay name a step. Keep the frozen saves replaying (a migration if the format must change; beryl_session/src/migrations).
  • Tests in the rules' own tests, with test cards: a step inserted after the current one, a phase inserted, a step removed, two insertions after one step (the later first), and a smart stop that fires at each of two upkeeps.

No keystone is written here. The four beads that follow in this lane write them.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

A test card can insert and remove steps and phases, and every reader tells a repeated step from the first. Without Capability::TurnStructure the count is unchanged; the close reason gives it and State<Small>'s size.
