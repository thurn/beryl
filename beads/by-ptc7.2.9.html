Description

Why

just fuzz plays a quarter of its games with decks dealt from one keystone family (decks::KEYSTONE_FAMILIES and fuzz_deck_from in beryl_cards/src/decks.rs, beryl_search/tests/families.rs), so cards of one family meet. Cards of different families rarely do: Humility with Gemrazer, Rest in Peace with Underworld Breach, Painter's Servant with Mother of Runes. Plan: Designs A, "The keystone pool".

What to build

  • A pool of every keystone and partner of every family (decks.rs): both decks are dealt from it with basic lands, weighted so that two or three keystones are in play by the middle of a game.
  • just fuzz --pool keystones (the fuzz recipe in the justfile, the fuzz tests of beryl_search/tests/games.rs and beryl_session's fuzz::), and just exhaust --pool keystones, which runs beryl_scenario::exhaust from positions cut from those games at a quiet priority (beryl_cards/tests/exhaust.rs shows how it is run from a scenario's end).
  • Every decision checks the invariants and the references (reference.rs, Scratch::assert_sound), and the both-layout comparison once that bead has landed (it is a dependency).
  • A nightly step (tools/src/beryl_tools/nightly.py, beside scale_step) that runs both for a fixed budget and files one bead for a failure with the seed, as the browser sweep does. It never blocks master.
  • A failing game shrinks to a scenario (beryl_search/tests/shrink.rs).

Then run it: at least 100,000 games and the exhaustive search to depth 4. Fix each failure that is small, with a scenario in the card's file and a docs/bugs.md entry. File each that is not as a child of this milestone (tier:opus), with the shrunk scenario.

Bars (every bead of this epic)

  • The plan is docs/project-50k-part-2.md. If its design is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
  • The absent-cost gate holds with no bump (just count-gate). When you touched the kernel, the rules or the card vocabulary, report the count before and after in the close reason. If it cannot hold after three attempts, stop and say so on the epic: this is the one thing that stops the run.
  • size_of::<State<Small>>() stays within 20,480 bytes. Report the size if you changed it.
  • The frozen saves replay and rollouts_never_change holds (just test-rust).
  • Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with a quotation (just citations). A card file cites a rule only for a card-specific ruling.
  • A new card follows the card-writing skill and passes just card check <name> (Beryl, XMage and Forge). A keystone or partner is listed in data/rulings-ledger.json once that file exists, with every ruling a scenario or a reason.
  • No UI work. beryl_cli script-check must pass for a new card from both seats with the verbs and prompt shapes that exist.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry, naming the guard that now stops its class.
  • A bead you file is tier:opus for design, keystones and reviews, tier:sonnet for partner cards, sweeps and measurements, and never tier:fable.

Acceptance criteria

just fuzz --pool keystones and just exhaust --pool keystones exist and the nightly runs them. 100,000 pool games ran. The close reason lists every failure with its fix or its bead, and the stall rate.
