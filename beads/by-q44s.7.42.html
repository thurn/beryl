b3d: phone manual Next button covers the hand

  • Description
  • Acceptance criteria
  • Changelog

Description

Symptom: on Beryl 3D's phone layout, the manual table's Next button (with the next phase's name under it, the turn action region) covers the viewer's hand, so hand cards have no 44-point square to press. just b3d test table-crowded --phone-only --every-tick reports reachable faults at 393x759, 375x647 and 430x932, e.g. at 393x759 t=117ms: card "Teferi, Time Raveler" under region "turn action" (177,661 192x74), button "Next" and text "Upkeep".

Cause: manual::turn::next (rust/beryl3d/crates/beryl_scene/src/manual/turn.rs) anchors the button at the window's lower right, inset by a fixed amount, which is the desktop's place. The phone layout has no place for it, so it stands over the hand band.

Fix: on a phone window, stand it where the prompt dock stands: at the right end of the viewer's strip, raised over a hand whose back row reaches into the strip, or moved into the middle band where that would cover a permanent of the back row. RightEnd::dock_line in src/prompts/bar.rs does this for the dock and the "Opponent's Turn" button (by-q44s.7.40), and hud::phone::Occupied gives the hand's top. The caption under the button makes the box 74 points tall, which does not fit the 44-point middle band, so the phone may need the caption beside the button or dropped there. Compare with the web's phone manual table (just b3d compare table-crowded --layout phone).

Read: src/manual/turn.rs (next) and its caller, src/prompts/bar.rs (RightEnd, dock_line, waiting), src/hud/phone.rs (Occupied, clear_at_right, phase_span), src/table/phone.rs (bands).

Acceptance criteria

just b3d test table-crowded --phone-only --every-tick reports no reachable fault; desktop just b3d test and just b3d cargo-test pass.
