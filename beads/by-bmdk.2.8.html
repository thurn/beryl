Make stage pointer ownership switchable between clients

Close reason

Staged switchable pointer client ownership and primary life events in 851c7c23. Regression, engine aim and drag, and phone inspection checks pass.

Description

A second consumer can call Pointer.listen today, but pageStage() in ui/src/stage/playback.ts always installs Prompts as the listener, and StageBoard, PhoneStageBoard and Chrome attach hover and click consumers to Prompts. Pointer.listen replaces its listener, but holdable is assigned independently and unsubscribing only clears callbacks. After manual play takes ownership, engine hover/click forwarding and drag rules cannot be recovered by simply leaving the manual screen. Also pointer.ts accepts player targets for the secondary path but drops primary player presses, even though by-bmdk.3.20 needs primary life clicks.

Give stage composition a single attach/detach point that owns both callbacks and holdability, with viewer hover/touch inspection subscribing at the shared stage or passed in by the active client. Leave engine prompt policy in Prompts, and wire the manual screen in by-bmdk.3.11 to the same lifecycle. Expose a typed player-primary event or explicit stage HUD callback with the same target classification. Work in ui/src/stage/{pointer,playback,prompt,Chrome,StageBoard,PhoneStageBoard} and the pointer tests. Preserve engine drag, prompt, hover and inspection semantics.

Acceptance criteria

A deterministic pointer/controller test installs engine, then manual, then engine listeners on one stage and checks that only the active one receives clicks and drag decisions, old cleanup cannot clear the current registration, and primary life clicks reach the manual client. The existing engine aim/drag and phone inspection checks still pass.

A client forwards a card click to those observers by invoking its unhandled callback; a reply from an earlier registration cannot forward it after a switch.
