Extra turns (CR 500.7) in the kernel and vocabulary, Time Warp, Mindslaver extra-turn scenarios; c8c23481e

Description

Mindslaver (rust/cards/cards_2003/mrd/mindslaver.rs, by-bajq.9.1) controls the next turn its target actually takes (CR 724.1, 724.1b). The engine has no extra turns and no skipped turns, so the scenario 'an extra turn taken first' that by-bajq.9.1 asked for cannot be written: no card can make the next turn the controlled player takes be anything but the one after the current.

Build extra turns (CR 500.7: 'Some effects can give a player extra turns. They do this by adding the turns directly after the specified turn.'): a turn-order queue the kernel's State::begin_turn (beryl_kernel/src/turn.rs) reads before alternating, and an Effect in beryl_card for 'take an extra turn after this one'. Implement a real card that grants one (Time Walk or Time Warp, written with the card-writing skill). State::begin_turn already takes the player-controlling effect (beryl_kernel/src/effects/control.rs, take_control) for whichever player's turn begins, so a controlled player taking an extra turn first should be controlled in that extra turn.

Scenarios to add to Mindslaver's file: the controlled player takes an extra turn first, and that extra turn is the controlled one; the controller takes an extra turn first, and the controlled player's turn after it is controlled. Bars of epic by-bajq apply (count gate, State<Small> size, citations).

Acceptance criteria

An extra-turn card passes just card check. Mindslaver has a scenario in which an extra turn is taken before the controlled turn, passing in Beryl. Count gate holds.

Handoff: extra turns are entries in the kernel Effects store (Continuous::ExtraTurn / Payload::ExtraTurn, Kinds::EXTRA_TURN; beryl_kernel/src/effects/extra_turn.rs, take_extra_turn). State::begin_turn (turn.rs) takes the last-created one (CR 500.7) before take_control, so Mindslaver controls an extra turn of its target; otherwise next_player of Turn::after_extra (the last non-extra turn's active player, set only during extra turns, hashed only when Some so corpora hashes hold). Board::begin_at/begin_before_turn clear it. Vocabulary: Effect::ExtraTurn(Players), build::extra_turn; rules: continuous::extra_turn_instruction (APNAP order, so the last player in turn order goes first). Card: Time Warp (cards_1997/tmp/time_warp.rs), oracles 4/4 pass both. Mindslaver gained two scenarios (controlled player's extra turn is the controlled one; controller's extra turn comes first) — the oracles mark them unsupported as with all CR 724 scenarios. Count gate +0.00%; State<Small> unchanged (Turn 10->12 bytes, inside padding). Pre-existing on staging, not mine: the mutation test fails on two Jace, the Mind Sculptor ability 0 Amount mutants (staging origin db31372 shows the same).
