Close reason

Fixed in 79e0f0e: a dropped hand card returns to its fan place once its view passes (twin played, or drawn again); ui-test check added. Playing the dragged twin itself not done: the engine names one slot for twins (§7.2).

Description

  • Repro: seed 101 vs Expert, turn 4, two Plains in hand (slots 31 and 33). Drag slot 33 onto the battlefield: the engine plays its twin, slot 31, and slot 33 stays raised (top 693 px against about 743 px for its fan place), covering its neighbour. Moving away or re-hovering does not reset it, through turn 6 (D1/057-g1-t6-after-rehover.png). Jace, Vryn's Prodigy (slot 29), drag-cast on turn 10, put into the library by Oust and drawn again, is still raised and upright on turn 20 (D1/198-g1-subtlety-caststep.png, D1/201-g1-dragback-end.png).
  • Expected: when a drag ends, whatever the engine did with it, every hand card returns to its fan place. A drag of one twin should play the dragged card itself where the engine allows.

Found by the by-isv.13 ui-review on staging 4c67340 (WU Control mirror both seats; seeds and opponents per item). Design rules (epic by-isv): follow the skill's design philosophy (one gesture for the common case, the client does the bookkeeping, ask once in context, Arena first, show don't make them infer, few words) and add no standing words to the table. Every acceptance check is an agent's: just ui-test, just ui-shots, Rust tests or ui-live probes.

Acceptance criteria

  • A just ui-test check drags one of two identical lands to the battlefield and then finds every remaining hand card at its fan position (within 2 px), with no card raised while the pointer is off the hand.
  • A card that leaves the hand and comes back (drawn again) is drawn in the fan like any other.

Derek Thurn2026-11-06

Cause: CardLayer kept a dropped card's held state (dragging false, the epoch it was dropped on) forever, and lifted/ghosted tested held !== undefined rather than whether it was still holding, so a card whose drop played something (its twin, or itself before it came back to hand) stayed raised and upright. Fix: lifted and ghosted follow holding, which ends with the drop's view. Repro reproduced as a ui-test check on a fixture derived from hand-pair (twin dragged, engine-named twin played, dragged twin to library and drawn again): fails at 57 px off before the fix, passes after. Deviation: 'a drag of one twin should play the dragged card itself' is not done: the engine offers interchangeable cards as one candidate naming one slot (architecture §7.2, decisions.ts interactions()), so the client cannot pick which twin moves; the acceptance criteria do not require it.
