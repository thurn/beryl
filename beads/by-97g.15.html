Description

Two Staff of the Storyteller activations in the same WU Control game (by-4bo, by-oli) spend live mana before the draw is useful. See docs/incidents/2026-11-09-staff-timing.md and data/blunders.json entries staff-in-upkeep and staff-before-combat-damage.

Build a challenger that models holding optional instant-speed activations. In rust/crates/beryl_search/src/rollout.rs, compare Policy::activate with the timing in expert/mod.rs (their_end, instant_speed). Classify effects and timing from card data, covering other optional resource abilities such as the Currency Converter loot. Preserve the ability to act when the result can help with the current stack or combat, when it will enable a play this turn, or when a threat to the source makes waiting unsafe. Check colored mana and playable interaction, so a lone white source in upkeep and the only two mana for second-main Winds of Abandon both count. In long.rs, check guarded and root selection: the current anchor covers casts only and a search pass should get the benefit of the same hold policy during rollout. Keep the change opt-in until promotion and leave frozen agents alone.

Test both entries with just blunders --only staff-in-upkeep,staff-before-combat-damage and add policy scenarios for passing early, using the activation at the last good opportunity, and responding when it can matter. Acceptance is both new entries fixed at the ladder's proxy and shipped budgets, no previously fixed entries reopened, and the plausible match bar in docs/ai-strategy.md. Record the matchup and promotion decision on this bead.
