Casting machine, mana solver, activated/loyalty/cycling, timing permissions, stack and resolution with transactional replay, differential test; pushed 1179861

Description

Part of Milestone 1 (epic by-b0q): a rules-exact engine, game UI and AI opponents for a mirror match of the 40-card WU Control main deck in docs/sample-decks/2026-10-17-PrinceJools-wu-control.txt (29 distinct cards). Read the epic's description (plan, card table) and design field (decisions) first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md).

Architecture sections 7.3, 7.4 and 10. Test with test-only CardDefs inside the engine; the real cards come in the next wave.

  • Casting (CR 601.2) as an explicit machine: announce, face (modal DFCs CR 712), alternative costs (impending, CR 702.176), additional costs (kicker with X, X can't be 0, CR 702.33 and 107.3; offspring, CR 702.175), targets, X, total cost with modifiers (the engine hook for Elite Spellbinder's {2} more), payment plan, payment. Spells that cannot be paid or targeted are never offered.
  • Mana (CR 106, 605): the pool, mana abilities, and the solver of decision D7: a payability prefilter, the plan keeping the most costs payable at tree nodes, greedy taps by flexibility rank in rollouts, decisions only for resource trade-offs, the manual_mana flag.
  • Activated abilities (CR 602), loyalty abilities (CR 606: one per turn, sorcery timing, loyalty cost), cycling (CR 702.29). Timing permissions: flash (CR 702.8), casting sorceries as though they had flash, a static that limits an opponent to sorcery timing, instant-speed loyalty abilities.
  • The stack (CR 405) and resolution (CR 608), with targets rechecked and fizzling (CR 608.2b). Transactional replay for may_pause bodies with Replay and inline answers (7.3), plus the differential test that compares them at every decision (17.4).
  • Countering (CR 701.6) and "unless its controller pays", asked of that player. Casting from a graveyard or exile under a permission, and without paying the mana cost (CR 118.9).

Acceptance criteria

  • Engine tests cover each rule listed, including a never-offered unpayable spell and a payment decision offered only for a real trade-off.
  • The inline-versus-replay differential test passes over random games.

Delivered in 1179861 (rebased over 1.5, 1.7 and 1.8). Decisions and deviations, for the Warden (by-b0q.1.17) and the card beads:

  • Casting machine: cast.rs, Pending::Casting (X -> target count -> targets -> payment). Casting options are Face::casting (Kicker{cost,min_x}, Offspring, Impending) and a cast candidate is Opt::Cast{slot,face,costs-bitmask}; at most one alternative cost. Unpayable or untargetable spells and abilities are never offered; X options stop at the most payable.
  • Triggered abilities now choose targets as they go on the stack through the same machine (CR 603.3d), removed if no legal target. 1.7's trigger::resolve is gone: resolve.rs resolves spells, activated and triggered abilities (with the 603.4 recheck) and runs may_pause bodies on Scratch's spare state.
  • Mana (mana.rs): Hall-theorem prefilter over unit kinds; plans = floating spend + tap counts per class of interchangeable sources; one plan per distinct floating remainder, best by 'keeps most later costs payable' (hand costs and permanents' activation costs incl. {T}), tie by flexibility rank. Profile.payment: KeepMostPayable (EXACT/SMART), Greedy (first plan in rank order, for rollouts), Manual (manual_mana: every tap and pool spend a decision, standalone mana abilities offered at priority). Assumes multi-mana simple sources make one kind (true of the cube's rocks; debug-asserted).
  • Cast during resolution (CR 608.2g, Malcolm): EffectCtx::offer_cast must be the body's last action; the offer (Pending::CastOffer, Pass or the cast options) is made as the resolution finishes, with no priority until the cast completes. Equivalent for Malcolm's wording.
  • Permissions: State.permissions (CastPermission: object ref, player, extra generic, this_turn, exile). Elite Spellbinder's {2} more is 'extra'; Jace -3's exile is 'exile', carried to StackEntry.exile.
  • Timing: Keywords::FLASH, Rule::OpponentsCastAsSorceries (Teferi static, beats flash), Player.sorceries_as_flash (Teferi +1, cleared at that player's turn start), Rule::InstantLoyaltyTheTurnItEntered + ObjectFlags::ENTERED_THIS_TURN. Loyalty: once a turn via ObjectFlags::LOYALTY_ACTIVATED, negative cost needs counters.
  • Permanent spells enter through event::move_batch with Entry.flags (KICKED/OFFSPRING/IMPENDING), Entry.x and Entry.attach (Aura), impending's time counters via Entry.counters; flags are set before enters triggers match, so 'if it was kicked' conditions see them. X lives in State.entered_x (read with State::x_of), not on Object: with combat's byte, an Object field would have cost 1 KiB.
  • State size: 20,264 bytes after combat, 216 under the 20 KiB assertion. Targets are packed into 4 bytes (Target::object/player). The next card work that grows State will need to argue for it or shrink something (stack TriggerInfo, Decision in Pending::Resolving).
  • Differential test (tests/replay.rs): random games, 8 seeds x 4 profiles, inline answers vs Replay, state hash compared at every decision, invariants checked; fuzzed 200 seeds debug without failures before the rebase and 60 x 4 after.
