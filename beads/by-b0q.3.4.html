Game screen from PlayerView: slot-keyed animated cards, drag/click to play, menus, targets, inspect, start/mulligan/game-over; just ui-test; reviewer: no major discrepancy

Description

Part of Milestone 1 (epic by-b0q): a rules-exact engine, game UI and AI opponents for a mirror match of the 40-card WU Control main deck in docs/sample-decks/2026-10-17-PrinceJools-wu-control.txt (29 distinct cards). Read the epic's description (plan, card table) and design field (decisions) first; docs/engine-architecture.md is the design. Rules code cites the Comprehensive Rules with yawgatog links and quotations (AGENTS.md).

Design decisions 2, 10 to 13 and docs/ui_mockups/battlefield.png, built from the design system and the fixture transport.

  • Start screen: pick the opponent from the registered agents (mcts-omniscient labeled as cheating), an optional seed, Start. Mulligan: keep or mulligan, then cards to the bottom. Game over screen.
  • The battlefield as in the mockup: the opponent's hand as card backs, life orbs, lands stacked by name with ×N, nonland permanents, libraries and graveyards, player badges, the phase tracker, the primary button naming the stop it advances to ("Next", "To Combat"), the priority pill, settings with the full-control toggle.
  • The hand fan in full frames. Drag a hand card onto the battlefield to cast or play it, with a click fallback. A card with several options (impending or full cost, offspring, kicker X, MDFC face) gets a small menu on drop. Clicking a permanent or a hand card lists its abilities (loyalty abilities, Strip Mine, cycling, Staff, Map). Targets: click the cyan-highlighted candidates; the source glows gold.
  • Motion for every move between zones, from the events in each PlayerView: draw, play, cast to the stack, resolve, die, bounce, exile, with shared layout per public slot, so cards never jump; tapping rotates; life changes animate.
  • Inspect: hovering a card shows the full Scryfall scan with the artist.
  • Fixture states for ui-shots: mulligan, a main phase like the mockup, a hand with castable cards, a cast-option menu, choosing a target.

Acceptance criteria

  • npm run typecheck, prettier and just smoke pass.
  • just ui-shots captures the states listed. A reviewer subagent compares them side by side with the named mockups and finds no major discrepancy; its verdict is a comment on this bead. No screenshots committed.
  • Named mockup: battlefield.png.
  • A Playwright test drives a fixture through a drag-to-play cast and a click fallback, and checks that a card moving zones keeps its element and animates over more than 0 ms.

Executor notes (deviations and decisions):

  • Cards are one element per public slot in a single layer (ui/src/game/CardLayer.tsx), placed by pure layout functions (game/layout.ts) and animated imperatively with Motion; a slot keeps its element across zones, so draw/play/cast/resolve/die/bounce/exile all glide. Library and exile cards stay mounted but hidden at their pile, so draws start at the library. Exile has no pile of its own yet: exiled cards fade out over the owner's graveyard.
  • Protocol: candidate labels now tell apart casts with options ('Cast Thieving Skydiver with kicker {X}', '... with offspring {4}', '... with impending 4, {2}{W}{W}') and activations by cost ('Jace, the Mind Sculptor: +2', 'Map Token: {1}, {T}, Sacrifice this', 'Spara's Headquarters: {3}, Discard this card'), so the click/drop menus are readable. No type changes.
  • Fixtures: 'from turn <t>' qualifier on the stop condition; the fixture transport takes '&at=<n>' to start at the nth view and plays the next recorded view on each choose, logging calls on window.berylFixture for tests. One fixture (greedy-turn-9, seed 1, greedy vs greedy) serves the mulligan, castable, cast-option-menu, main-phase and target shots.
  • The turn button's stop is computed in the UI from the step, stack and stops setting (Resolve <top> / Next: To Combat / To Main 2 / End Turn ...).
  • The stack is a simple cascade at the right with spells as full cards; arrows and the mockup stack frame are left to by-b0q.3.7. Decisions other than priority get a prompt panel listing every candidate (the click fallback for every board pick); combat and choice overlays stay with by-b0q.3.7/3.8.
  • Playwright test: ui/scripts/interactions.mjs, run by the new 'just ui-test' and in the slow-CI shots lane. Mutation-checked: skipping animation and remounting on zone change each fail it.
