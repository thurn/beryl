Description

Do every step of docs/beryl-3d.md 'Manual QA' that an agent can do without a window and without a device, through just b3d and the smoke test, and record each step's result on this bead as a comment: the command, its output (cut to what matters) and pass or fail.

What an agent can do, decided here:

  • 'First assembled check': steps 1, 2 (open the PNG with the Read tool and compare with the tree) and 4 against just b3d app --fixture verbs-zones --pause-at 1500ms --window none --for 30s. Step 3 needs a person; list it.
  • 'A whole game on the desktop': steps 1 and 2 through just b3d stand-in and just b3d play with live tree and live png between moves; 5 through live do 'key Cmd+B' and just b3d bug; 6 through just b3d stand-in's resume; 7 by resuming in Beryl 3D a game the web app saved under the same BERYL_PROFILE and checking the board. Steps 3 and 4 need a window and a person; list them.
  • 'Old and constrained hardware': step 4. Steps 1 to 3 need the Enhanced tier (milestone 6); do them if just b3d app --tier exists on staging, else list them. Step 5 needs a device; list it.
  • 'Phones': just b3d test --phone-only only. Every numbered step needs a device; list them.
  • 'Agent tools': steps 1 to 4, each in a worktree with the deliberate fault, recording the failure output, then discarding the worktree with enact worktree remove (no push).
  • 'Checking the parity list': step 1 with just b3d parity; for step 2, compare just b3d tree and png of each item with just specs --describe or just ui-shots of the same moment on the web side. Say what a person must still look at.

A step that fails is a bug: file a bead under by-q44s.9 with the reproducing command, and go on. Put the list of steps only a person can do in a comment on by-q44s.9 headed "Manual QA for the owner" (do not file a bead asking for them, rule 18). Make no code change.

Acceptance criteria

A comment on this bead records each step with its command, output and result; failures have beads under by-q44s.9; a comment on by-q44s.9 lists the steps only a person can do.
