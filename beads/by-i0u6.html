Scenario props named by role, with checked contracts

Description

Symptom: card scenarios kept adding comments like 'Prop::Creature is Elite Spellbinder, a 3/1 flier', so the role name hid nothing and the comments went stale whenever a prop changed.

Cause: a role name like Prop::Creature said nothing about the stats scenarios rely on (power and toughness, flying, color, mana value, creature type), and the old props had text that got in the way (Elite Spellbinder's and Snapcaster Mage's enters triggers), so writers explained them.

Decision:

  • Each prop in rust/crates/beryl_card_shared/src/props.rs is a Contract: card, cost, types, subtypes, stats, keywords and a summary of its rules text. every_prop_card_keeps_its_contract in beryl_cards/src/tests/props.rs checks each one against the card's definition.
  • The creature props are store-complete creatures with no text beyond keywords: Prop::Flier = Geist of the Moors (white 3/1 flier, {1}{W}{W}, Spirit), replacing Prop::Creature (Elite Spellbinder); Prop::Vanilla = Wetland Sambar (blue 2/1, {1}{U}, Elk), replacing Prop::SmallCreature (Snapcaster Mage); and a new Prop::FlashCreature = Hired Blade (black 3/2 flash, {2}{B}) for scenarios that need a creature castable at instant speed. Stats match the old props, so P/T assertions didn't change.
  • The other props keep their cards and names.
  • The card-writing skill now says not to name the card behind a prop or restate its stats. A scenario that needs something a contract doesn't promise names that card with Scenario::interaction, as Humility's and Abhorrent Oculus's enters-trigger scenarios now do.
