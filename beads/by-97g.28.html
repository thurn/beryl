Description

Part of the ladder epic by-97g. Read docs/ai-strategy.md first: §2.2 and §6.4.

Rung 4, build. The expert already has a combat planner. This bead lifts it out so the search can use it, behind a new option combat that is off by default. It needs judgement the other build beads do not, so it is not tier:light.

Claims: beza-trinket-into-blockers, manifest-into-samurai, spirits-stay-home.

Measured 2026-11-11 with just blunders expert --seeds 1: the expert, whose combat is this planner, gets those three right, and skip-beza-after-winds too. It gets spare-jace-wielder wrong (Snapcaster Mage at Jace, the Mind Sculptor and the Spirit at the player), so this rung does not claim that entry. The champion gets all five wrong. At three of them the search prefers the wrong play by a clear margin (0.02 to 0.07), and paired rollouts do not (just why, 2026-11-11: within one point either way, and the defender blocks correctly in them). So the lead is made above the rollouts. The suspect is the tree below the attack, where a block's few visits are spread over every way to block; the other is the turn discount. Step 1 finds out which.

Where

  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/expert/combat.rs: Sim (its fields are a table, a rollout policy, a scratch, a seat and a Plan; it does not need the rest of the expert), Sim::choose, attack_plans, block_plans, weigh, run, score, swing_back, Plan, Declare.
    • It names three cards: Jace, Vryn's Prodigy is kept out of attacks (attack_plans), Mana Crypt's flips are counted in score, and The Wandering Emperor's exile is counted in exiled_next.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/expert/mod.rs: how the expert calls it (obs.determinize(t, &mut self.rng), then sim.choose(&start, d)), and keeps one Plan across a turn's declarations.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/frozen/: its own copy of the expert. Do not touch it.
  • rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs:
    • LongSearch::choose: greedy's answer is computed there (self.policy.choose(&self.table, obs, d)) and passed to guarded.
    • guarded: where the root keeps a default answer.
    • Tree::iterate: the branch _ if !at_root && self.family.options.mid && answered_by_policy(&d) shows how one answer is forced below the root with self.only(...).
    • Options, to_name, FromStr: commit 1c43427 shows how an option is added.

Steps

  1. First, look. by-97g.34's notes hold just why --tree for these three entries. Read them. For each entry write one sentence in the notes: does the search's lead come from a node below the attack whose visits are spread over plays a player would not make (which node, and whose decision), from the score (the turns differ), or from neither.
    • If it is such a node at all three, build steps 2 to 5.
    • If it is the score at all three, build steps 2 to 4 only, and write step 5 not built: the lead is the discount's in the notes.
  2. Move the planner. Create rust/crates/beryl_search/src/combat.rs holding Sim, Plan, Declare and their helpers. It must name no card (just check's card-names lane fails otherwise). The three card judgements stay in rust/crates/beryl_search/src/expert/: give Sim a hook (a small trait or three function pointers) for "keep this creature out of attacks", "adjust this player's life" and "what a tapped attacker risks next turn". The expert supplies its three; the search supplies none. The expert must play exactly as before: its tests pass untouched.
  3. The option combat already exists (commit 8d57db7e): Options::combat, its name in to_name and FromStr, and a combat field of Rules (rollout.rs) that nothing reads. It was reserved as a policy rule; it is now an option of the search. Remove combat from Rules and from Options::rules, keep the Options field and its place in the order of names, and rewrite its doc comment to say what steps 4 and 5 do.
  4. At the root. In LongSearch::choose, with anchor and combat set and d.kind one of Attacker, AttackTarget, Blocker, BlockTarget: the default answer is the planner's, not greedy's. Deal one world from obs with a seed drawn from the search's own seed (the moves must depend on the seed alone), run Sim::choose on it, and keep one Plan in the LongSearch across the turn, as the expert does. guarded returns true at those four kinds whenever the search's choice differs from the default.
  5. In the tree. In Tree::iterate, with combat set, below the root, when the decision is one of those four kinds and the game's turn number is the turn number at the tree's root: answer it with Sim::choose in that tree's own world (either player), with a fresh Plan for each player each iteration, and take that one child with self.only(...). The planner's engine steps must count against the budget: give it the tree's own scratch.
  6. If step 1 showed a cause steps 4 and 5 do not cover, say so in the notes in one paragraph. Do not build anything for it.
