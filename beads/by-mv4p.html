Description

A checkbox on the start screen, "Fair deal", makes a new game start from a deal that the AI judges to give both players roughly equal chances.

Decisions (interview with the user, 2026-11-14):

  • Scope: the whole deal is judged together: both opening hands, both library orders and who is on the play. All of these come from the game's seed, so picking a deal means picking a seed.
  • Mulligans stay legal. The playouts make keep/mulligan choices too, and a mulligan's reshuffle comes from the same seed, so the judgment counts what happens after a mulligan.
  • Judge: soft (softmax) rollouts of the rollout policy in both seats on the full, known state. Plain rollouts are deterministic, so every playout of one deal would play the same game. Softmax temperature 10, the value the search uses.
  • Budget: up to about 10 s on the Mac. Fixed counts (not a timer), so the same typed seed always gives the same deal on any machine: about 100 candidate deals x 300 playouts on the Mac (about +/-3 win-rate points per deal), fewer on the phone.
  • Pick the candidate whose seat-0 win rate is closest to 50%.
  • Display: nothing at all. The replay records the chosen seed as usual, so replays and bug reports work unchanged.
  • Defaults chosen without asking: a typed seed seeds the candidate search; the checkbox is off by default and remembered with the last opponent; it applies to every game started with it checked.

Code: rust/crates/beryl_session/src/sessions.rs (StartRequest, StartRequest::game), replay.rs (ReplayFile::setup), beryl_search::rollout::{soft_rollout, Softmax, RolloutPolicy}; ui/src/screens/StartScreen.tsx and the start_game wrapper in ui/src/ipc.ts.
