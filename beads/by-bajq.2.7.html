Description

Card

Oko, Thief of Crowns, loyalty 4.

  • +2: Create a Food token. (Food: "{2}, {T}, Sacrifice this artifact: You gain 3 life.")
  • +1: Target artifact or creature loses all abilities and becomes a green Elk creature with base power and toughness 3/3.
  • −5: Exchange control of target artifact or creature you control and target creature an opponent controls with power 3 or less.

What it needs

  • The +1 is one resolved effect on one locked-in object with parts in layers 4 (it is a creature and an Elk, losing its other card types and creature types), 5 (green), 6 (no abilities) and 7b (3/3). Effect::Modify takes one Modification today: let it take several that begin together with one timestamp, as one entry or as entries sharing a timestamp. Equipment attached later gives its bonus and its granted abilities (a later timestamp); Equipment attached before gives only its P/T bonus.
  • Exchange of control (CR 701.12): Effect::ExchangeControl starts two layer 2 effects together. If either target is illegal as it resolves, nothing is exchanged (701.12a). Control changing resets summoning sickness, which the kernel already does for Modification::Control.
  • A Food token in beryl_card_shared/src/tokens.rs.

Scenarios

The Elk wearing Leonin Scimitar, attached before and after; a noncreature artifact made an Elk (it is a creature and, as the card's ruling says, no longer an artifact); a legendary creature made an Elk (still legendary); the exchange with one target gone.

Files

The card's file, beryl_card/src/{effect/mod.rs, effect/build.rs, continuous.rs}, beryl_rules/src/{continuous.rs, interpret/run.rs}, beryl_card_shared/src/tokens.rs.

A disagreement with an oracle is settled by the rules and recorded in data/oracle-divergences.json.
  • Read the card's rulings before writing its scenarios (just card brief <name> prints Scryfall's). Each statement in a ruling that a scenario can show is a scenario of the card. Where one cannot be shown yet, the close reason names the ruling and why.
  • A rules bug found on the way gets its docs/bugs.md entry.
  • If the design in the plan is wrong for the code, build what is right and amend the plan's section in the same push. Never wait for an answer: record the choice in this bead.
