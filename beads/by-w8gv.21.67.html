Description

Decision from the user, 2026-11-12, after a prototype (the Arrow Lab artifact): the stage's arrows, lines of combat, attack marks and block marks looked 'crummy and cartoony' as flat SVG strokes, and are to be drawn as light instead. On the prototype: 'Looks great, let's do it. No switch needed.' One note: the trace that follows the pointer felt sluggish and must follow the mouse directly.

What is built (ui/src/stage): light.ts is the model of everything lit at a stage time (ribbons along each line's curve, an attacker's wedge, a blocker's crescent, flashes and sparks), a function of stage time and of where the stage has each end; glow.ts paints a scene in WebGL on a canvas over the card layer; dom.ts makes the painter with the first light and keeps the SVG paths on the page, unpainted, as the record the checks read (and as the look where a browser has no WebGL). overlay.ts works the scene out each tick. Aim.tsx's trace is painted as the pointer moves, not as React renders.

Motion, Beryl's own (exception 8), from Arena's films of I3Te0m1bBFw 31:35 and IEXcjBn3BXk 22:16, 22:21, 35:24: an arrow strikes over its beginning step, lands with a flash, sparks and light that runs toward the target while it stands, and drains into its target over the step that ends it (point, end: 110 ms after 290 ms whole, where it was a cut after 400 ms: nothing waits longer than before, and what follows waits for the drain, so no end of a line moves under it). An attacker's wedge pops and settles in 190 ms and turns gold when blocked, as Arena's does; a blocker's line fires to the tip of that wedge with a crescent under the blocker.

Departures recorded in data/ui-conventions.json: lines of combat lie over the cards, not under them (this overrides by-4jj's 'subtle lines'); an attack mark is ember red, not pale gold.

Found on the way: WebGL renders in the checks' headless WebKit; a premultiplied color brighter than its alpha is corrupted in WebKit's snapshots, so the layer never writes one and light is soft-clipped and laid over, not added.

Not done: an arrow that follows the pointer while a target is chosen (Arena has one; the stage has none yet); the old board (game/Board.tsx) is untouched.

Notes

2026-11-12, after the first push (a7d2f9ff): the user asked whether the light had to lie over all other UI, 'it looks a bit dumb in places'. It did not: one canvas over the cards had put lines of combat across cards and the step's banner. Fixed in 83e77e3a: two canvases at the depths of the two SVG drawings, combat (lines, wedges, crescents, the pointer's trace) on the table under the cards and the banner, arrows at targets and the line to a source over what rests. data-light now names the layers painted. Slow CI passed on a7d2f9ff. 2026-11-12: the user asked for the arrow that follows the pointer while a target is chosen ('Add it'). Shipped in db3ac981: ui/src/stage/Aim.tsx draws it for a prompt of purpose 'target', from decision.source to the mouse, through light.ts sought() and dom.ts aim(); on an eligible mark it becomes the arrow the choice will draw (lines.ts wayOf, so the click's own arrow lies where it did), green or red by who controls it. stage.ts reach() is now public for it. A browser with no WebGL draws none. ui/shots.json stage-aim-target is its screenshot state; UI Conventions has it under Settled as Arena, 'An arrow to the pointer'.
