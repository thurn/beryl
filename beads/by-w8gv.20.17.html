Description

Measure the CI track's acceptance bar, two weeks after step two lands (epic by-w8gv.20, Acceptance; docs/project-50k.md, Acceptance, "Checks").

Start

If fewer than 14 days have passed since the step-two Warden (by-w8gv.20.18) closed, set this bead's defer date to 14 days after that close (bd update <this> --defer <date>), comment why, and release it with enact release. Otherwise:

Measure, over the 14 days after the Warden closed

  • Steward beads per 100 landed commits: bd list --all --label role:steward created in the window, against commits that reached master in the window (git rev-list --count --first-parent). Bar: at most 2.
  • The fast check's seconds for UI changes: runs.jsonl records with script check whose run included the ui-test or shots lane. Report the median and the worst. Bar: within 60 s.
  • Selection misses per 100 commits: just ci-report (the miss verdict). Under 1 means file a role:executor bead under by-w8gv.20 that moves the browser lanes' full run from slow CI to nightly (the epic's "Then").
  • Reported, no bar (never tune to these): failures by verdict, flakes and quarantined checks, checks deleted by the prunes, seconds per lane.

Record

Write the numbers in a comment on by-w8gv.20 and on this bead. If the Steward bar or the fast-check bar is missed, record the measured value and its cause (the Steward beads' close reasons, by verdict) on the epic, and file one role:executor bead per cause worth fixing. The plan allows three attempts: say which attempt this is (count earlier measurement beads under by-w8gv.20). After the third miss, record it and file a follow-up rather than another measurement.

Acceptance criteria

The Steward-beads-per-100-commits, fast-check seconds for UI changes and selection-miss figures over the 14 days after the step-two Warden are recorded on the epic, with follow-ups filed as the bead says.
