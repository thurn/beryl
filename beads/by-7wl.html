Description

Anchored search: leader strength without coin-flip blunders

Goal: a long-think entrant that ties the arena's leader (pimc18/omniscient/rollout/tuned) but stops making plays like Deep Seer's in docs/incidents/2026-11-06-deep-seer-misplays.md (Oust on its own Rogue token; Jace's +2 over his 0). A tie that drops these plays is a win: they shape how players see the agent. Once it is checked, it becomes the GUI's Deep Seer.

Evidence (design session, 2026-11-06)

  • Most of the leader's moves are coin flips by its own estimate. In run-003's decisions.jsonl, for pimc18/omniscient/rollout/tuned@10M (3,004 searched decisions, 34 games), the chosen move leads the runner-up's mean by under 0.01 in 62% of decisions, under 0.02 in 73%, under 0.05 in 86%. Every top entrant looks the same. Some ties are between equivalent options (two identical tokens) and are harmless.
  • Oust: pass 0.427 v cast 0.433 over ~61k visits, about 1.5 standard errors. The search called it a tie and noise broke it.
  • Jace's 0 scored 0.498 v the +2's 0.782 because the put-back after the 0 is a tree node whose children are each tried once in random order and averaged (Tree::select / Tree::iterate in rust/crates/beryl_search/src/long.rs). The 0 got 628 visits against 25,697.
  • Evaluation leaves (eval, cut1, cut2) trailed full rollouts in all 16 matched-budget comparisons across runs 001-003, mostly by 100-350 Elo, while running far more iterations. The leaf stays as it is.
  • Decision: no learned value model here (Derek is very skeptical it can work).

Design

The leader's search unchanged (PIMC 18 trees, full softmax rollouts, tuned card values, same budget), plus three changes, each switchable on its own:

  1. Anchor. Greedy's choice g (RolloutPolicy::choose, argmax) is the default at the root. The search's choice b replaces it only if the mean over trees of mean_t(b) - mean_t(g) exceeds both 2 standard errors (from the spread across the independent trees) and a floor δ. Monolith's winning agent worked this way (docs/ai-strategy.md §3).
  2. Verify. When b != g, the last quarter of the budget goes to b and g only, so the comparison is not made against a starved estimate.
  3. Mid-resolution by policy. Below the root, greedy answers questions asked while casting or resolving (targets, X, yes/no, choose object, scry, ...). Priority, attacks and blocks stay tree nodes. The root decision is always fully searched.

Family names: an optional fifth part lists the options, e.g. pimc18/omniscient/rollout/tuned/anchor+mid. anchor is changes 1 and 2, mid is change 3. No fifth part means today's behavior: old names and run configs parse unchanged, and existing families play move for move as before (longthink's --upgrade check enforces this). δ is a constant, fixed by the offline replay before the agent plays any arena or GUI game, because changing it later changes the moves of games already played.

Out of scope

Better planning, learned value, new leaves, new sights. This raises the floor to greedy's common sense, not the ceiling.

Done

The head-to-head against the leader passes its bar (or names the change that cost strength), the anchored families play in the arena, and the GUI's Deep Seer is the anchored agent.

Notes

2026-11-07: by-7wl.1 measured the broad anchor at 0.04-0.07 win probability a game (fresh re-searches), which would fail the 45% bar. The anchor now guards only priority decisions where the search would cast a spell and greedy would pass (beryl_search::guarded), δ 0.02, costing about 0.005 a game. Everything else plays the search's own choice. 'mid' is unchanged.
