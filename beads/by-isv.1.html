Close reason

Counters badged per kind, P/T inked against printed (damage off toughness), sickness fades art, inspector spells marks out; marks + marks-damage fixtures, shots and ui-test checks. None of the findings was already fixed. d14c1ef.

Description

The engine sends ObjectView fields (ui/src/generated/protocol.ts) that the table never draws:

  • counters: only loyalty is shown, not +1/+1, story, chorus or time;
  • damage: marked damage is invisible;
  • summoningSick: sick and ready creatures look identical;
  • pt after effects: a buffed creature's P/T looks printed.

The hover inspector also shows the printed card, not its current state.

Seen by the reviewers:

  • Staff of the Storyteller's story counters;
  • Malcolm, Alluring Scoundrel's chorus counters (its text depends on reaching 4);
  • Overlord of the Mistmoors' time counters while impending;
  • a Samurai token at 3/3 with a counter, whose inspector showed 2/2;
  • a sick and a ready Thieving Skydiver that looked identical.

How to show it, without words:

  • counters as a small icon badge with a number, one per kind, as Arena does;
  • marked damage as toughness drawn reduced and in red;
  • changed P/T coloured up or down against the printed value;
  • summoning sickness as a visual treatment, matching Arena's.

The inspector carries the detail: counter kinds, and printed against current.

Files: ui/src/game/CardLayer.tsx, ui/src/components/Card.tsx, ui/src/design/.

Before changing anything, reproduce each item on current staging. Use just ui-live, as the ui-review skill describes (.agents/ui-review/SKILL.md), or a fixture. The findings come from reviews of the build before the 2026-11-05 UI fixes, so some may already be fixed. Drop those, and list them in the close reason.

Design rules (see the epic): follow the skill's design philosophy, and add no standing words to the table. What a player needs at a glance is shown as icons, badges, numbers, colour or position. Everything else goes behind disclosure: hover, the inspector, a pile's browser, or the game log.

Acceptance criteria

  • A fixture shot (add a fixture if needed) shows, side by side or in one state, each of these next to an unmarked twin: a creature with +1/+1 counters, a creature with marked damage, a sick creature, and a permanent with story or time counters. Each one differs visibly in a probe.compare crop.
  • The inspector of a countered creature shows its current P/T.
  • No new words stand on the table.
  • just ui-shots, just ui-test and the typecheck pass.

Reproduced on staging (5d1d5f9) via a new seeded fixture 'marks' (seed 45, wu-control greedy mirror, turn 26): +1/+1, story and chorus counters, damage and sickness were all undrawn; a buffed P/T read as printed; the inspector showed only the scan. None was already fixed. Design: one dark badge per counter kind (icon + count) along the field frame's foot; P/T numerals green/red against printed, toughness less damage in red (Arena); sickness desaturates and darkens the art. Inspector: a strip under the scan with printed -> current P/T and each mark named. Also: sick/ready and pumped/plain tokens no longer share a token pile (they would hide the marks); fixture shots now wait for window.berylFixture, since large fixtures (game-late too) were captured as 'No game in progress'. Fixture conditions '<mark> beside a twin' added to beryl_cli. ui-test once failed an unrelated drag check at load avg ~19; passed on rerun.
