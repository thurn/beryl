Description

Milestone 5 of Project 50k (plan K5 and K3; epic by-w8gv.9): the rules library and the card port. Line numbers and counts below were read at staging f5034c58; the code as it stands wins over this text, and a deviation is a comment on this bead.

The milestone's scope: "the Waterbend, Crew and Exile cast steps become one cost-payment mechanism." The M2 Mason audit (comment on by-w8gv.9.1, item 2): "Cost has one bool for each self-cost and no 'sacrifice, exile or reveal a [filter]' (8 cards)." Three entries of data/card-names-allowlist.json's mechanics list are this bead's: Face::exile_from_graveyard, Cost::waterbend, CastStep::Waterbend.

Scope

  1. A cost is mana plus parts (CR 118.1: "A cost is an action or payment necessary to take another action"). A part says what is done to which objects: tap, sacrifice, discard, exile, reveal or remove counters from this object or from a number of objects a filter accepts in a zone. Crew (CR 702.122a: "Tap any number of untapped creatures you control with total power N or greater") and waterbend (find its rule in the pinned release; just citations checks the number) are parts whose objects are measured by a total. The self-costs are the same part with "this" as its objects. Loyalty (CR 606.4) may stay its own part.
  2. An additional cost of a spell (Abhorrent Oculus) and an alternative cost's non-mana half (evoke's exile) are the same parts on the casting option or the face: Face::exile_from_graveyard goes.
  3. One cast step pays the parts that need a choice of objects, in the order printed, asking with the purposes that exist. CastStep::Crew, Waterbend and Exile become one.
  4. What was paid is readable by the effect ("the sacrificed creature"): say how, as an Objects value, without adding state to Object or State (plan K4: a Memo or the cast in progress).
  5. Write the paper test's cost cards in beryl_card/tests/paper_test.rs.

No new rules features (run epic by-w8gv, K14): the vocabulary may now say things no implemented card says. Where the engine has no code path for an expressible value, the card table must refuse the definition when it is built (TableError, beryl_kernel/src/table.rs) or the definition check must reject it, with a test; it must never run as a silent no-op. List each such gap in a comment on this bead: they are docs/rules-coverage.md's project, not this one's.

Constraints

  • The questions asked while paying (which creatures crew, which permanents waterbend, which cards are exiled), their order and their options are replayed by saves and rollouts: unchanged for the four cards.
  • Casting is saved in checkpoints with its step. If the step's saved form changes, the frozen corpus must still replay: write the migration (beryl_session/src/migrate.rs, the save format's version, data/save-breaks.json's rules) or keep the saved form.
  • Ability holds a Cost and its size is a power of two (beryl_card/src/ability.rs: 0.11% on the gate at 120 bytes against 128): parts behind a &'static slice.
  • activatable and the mana solver read costs on the counted path for every decision. just bench-count before and after.
  • The lane lane:vocabulary's last bead (abilities are built, not written as literals) depends on this one and writes its builders over the cost type you leave: give Cost const builders for the parts (Cost::TAP exists).

Tests: beryl_rules/src/tests/payment.rs, cast.rs and casting.rs; the four cards' scenarios; just oracle "Swift Reconfiguration" "Aang's Iceberg" "Subtlety" "Abhorrent Oculus"; beryl_session/tests/paid_costs.rs.

Acceptance criteria

Cost has no field for one mechanic: crew, waterbend and the self-costs are parts, or builders that make parts. Face::exile_from_graveyard is gone and Abhorrent Oculus's additional cost and evoke's exile are cost parts. CastStep has one step for paying with objects in place of Crew, Waterbend and Exile. Face::exile_from_graveyard, Cost::waterbend and CastStep::Waterbend are off data/card-names-allowlist.json's mechanics list. An effect can name what a cost part took, with a unit test. The paper test's cost cards (7, 8, 13, 17, 22, 23, 31, 56) compile in beryl_card/tests/paper_test.rs, and a cost the engine cannot pay is refused when the table is built, with a test. The four cards' scenarios pass unedited and their oracles pass; the frozen corpus replays (with a migration, if the saved step changed); just bench-count before and after is on the bead; parity holds with no bump to the count gate.

just bench-count, rollouts corpus: before 388,633,629; after 387,190,185 (-0.37%). No bump to the count gate. On the way: parts read by iterating the slice cost 390,683,287 (+0.53%), and a one-pass summary struct in activatable 394,762,493 (+1.58%): each instruction in the per-ability check is about 1M. So Cost keeps a private byte of the kinds of its parts, computed in const by Cost::of, and the hot checks (does(Tap), does(Discard), is_loyalty, chooses) are one bit each as the old bools were. Ability stays 128 bytes: Cost grew from 16 to 24 with the parts slice, so only_if_you_control is now Option<&'static Filter>.

Design: Cost { mana, parts } (parts and kinds private; Cost::of, of_parts, mana, loyalty, crew, TAP, NONE). CostPart::This(action) | Chosen(action, Chosen { zone, filter, other, measure }) | Loyalty(n) | Life(n) | OneOf(&[Cost]); CostAction Tap, Sacrifice, Discard, Exile, Reveal, RemoveCounter(kind); Measure Count(n), TotalPower(n) (crew), ForGeneric(n) (waterbend). Face::additional_cost: &[CostPart] replaces exile_from_graveyard; CastingOption::Evoke { cost, parts } replaces its exile: Colors. Filter gained Colors(Colors) and derives PartialEq, Eq, Hash (TableError carries the refused part).

One step: CastStep::Objects with Casting::part (which part), left and graveyard_from; Casting::bent is now substituted. Order kept from the old code and written down in cast/parts.rs: an ability's parts are paid before its mana, a spell's after it, and a ForGeneric part with the mana whatever its place among the parts. Purposes are the existing three, chosen by (action, measure).

Deviations from the bead:

  1. No migration: Casting is never saved. A checkpoint is taken as the upkeep begins (beryl_session/src/checkpoint.rs) and saves no Pending, and saves replay answers, which are unchanged. The frozen corpus replays as it was.
  2. 'An effect can name what a cost part took': Objects::Paid(CostAction) is in the vocabulary with const walkers (Effect/Amount/Condition/Players/Ability::names_paid), the paper test's cards 22 and 23 use it, and the card table refuses a definition that names it (Unpayable::Named, tested). The engine does not record what a cost took: no implemented card reads it, recording it for every crew and exile would put memos on the counted path, and K14 forbids the new feature. The intended reading is in the doc comment of Objects::Paid: each object a part takes remembers, as a memo, the spell or source it paid for, written only for an ability whose data names_paid; last-known information for a moved object as targets have it (CR 608.2h, 608.2k).
  3. beryl_kernel/src/payable.rs is the list of what the engine pays; cast/parts.rs has unreachable!() for the rest, as listener.rs does for triggers.

Gaps: values the cost vocabulary can now say that the engine has no code for. The card table refuses each when it is built (TableError::Cost, tests in beryl_kernel/src/payable.rs). For docs/rules-coverage.md's project:

  • A part done to objects its player chooses, other than: tapping permanents measured by total power or by generic mana (an ability), and exiling a count of cards from the caster's hand or graveyard (a spell). So refused: 'sacrifice a [filter]' and 'sacrifice another [filter]' (paper test 8, 22, 23, 31), 'reveal a card from your hand' (7), discard a card, tap N untapped permanents, exile from the battlefield, and an ability that exiles cards from a graveyard (56).
  • A spell's part done to itself or paid by tapping (convoke; a waterbend cost on a spell, CR 701.67b), and an ability's exile or reveal of its own source.
  • CostPart::Life (CR 119.4; paper test 17).
  • CostPart::OneOf, a choice of costs (forage, CR 701.61a; paper test 7 and 13).
  • Two parts paid rather than generic mana in one cost: the cast keeps one count (Casting::substituted).
  • A mana ability whose cost is neither {T} nor {T} and sacrificing its source (Unpayable::ManaAbility). This gap predates the bead: the solver never offered such an ability, silently; now the table refuses it.
  • Objects::Paid, what a cost took (Unpayable::Named), as above. Not expressible yet: a mandatory additional cost of mana alone on a face (additional_cost is parts; a CostPart::OneOf can hold mana).

Found, not mine: the scenario dump reports Abhorrent Oculus 'Manifest dread with one card in the library manifests it' as a Beryl failure (snapshot says P0 controls 2 Islands, the replay finds 3: the face-down Island). It does so at base 53b549f7 too, so both oracles list it unsupported. cargo test passes it; only the dump's added snapshot assertions disagree.
