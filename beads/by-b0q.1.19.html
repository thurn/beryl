Derek Thurn2026-11-05

Warden review of 0d9eb55 + c3749fd. Acceptance verified by running the 20 combat tests and a code read against CR 506-511 / 702.122. Found and fixed (1b7ab76, two regression tests): (1) collapse() treated attackers blocked by different creatures as interchangeable (the blocker relation lives on the blocker, so the attacker's signature hid it): a third blocker, or a targeted removal spell, was offered only one of them. Objects named by another object's combat field are now never collapsed (combat::related). (2) CR 508.8: blockers/damage steps were skipped whenever nothing was attacking at the end of declare attackers, so removing the lone attacker at instant speed (Get Lost, Wandering Emperor -2) skipped two steps and their priority windows. Turn.attacked now records a declaration for the combat. Reviewed and left alone: damage-division candidate logic (never dead-ends: left > sum(lethal-1) implies a killable blocker), first-strike STRUCK_FIRST semantics, prune/leave combat unlinking, crew feasibility (tapping any candidate keeps the cost payable), the executor's recorded deviations (pick order transpositions, canonical survivor damage, CR 508.4 handed to by-b0q.1.10). Noted, not filed: collapse also ignores attached_to references, harmless for M1 since its only Aura changes derived types. Tests are black-box; fuzz test 1.1 s, fast check 25 s.
