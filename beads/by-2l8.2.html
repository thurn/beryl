Exact stop, auto-pass, subtree reuse and per-decision seeds in staging cd02805; saves 28.5% of rollouts at 3k, 33.7% at 30k

Description

Part of the long-think epic (its D4, D5). Three changes to rust/crates/beryl_search/src/mcts.rs that cut a decision's rollouts without making a seed reproduce a different move.

  • Exact stop in rounds. Each tree runs a fixed slice of its iterations per round (trees in parallel as now); after a round, sum the root visits across trees and stop when the leader's lead over the runner-up exceeds the iterations still unspent across all trees (mind the first-of-equals tie rule). The move is identical to the full budget's. Deterministic, since rounds are fixed and the check sums in tree order.
  • Auto-pass. A Priority decision whose top stack entry is controlled by the deciding player passes without search. Over 400 expert mirror games that is 27 of 159 decisions per seat a game, and the expert never acts there.
  • Subtree reuse within one action. When the agent's next decision follows straight from the child it just chose (a spell's targets, X, payment plan, as CR 601.2 asks them), with no other player's decision and no card changing zones in between, each tree keeps its determinization and starts from that child's subtree, topping up to the budget. A rung's budget becomes root visits, not fresh rollouts. Reuse ends at anything that could reveal hidden information.

Keep 18 trees. Record per decision how many rollouts were actually spent, for the runner.

Also, for the runner's mid-game resume (by-2l8.3): seed each search from (agent seed, decision index) instead of a stream advanced once per call, so an agent rebuilt mid-game plays exactly as the uninterrupted one. With subtree reuse, expose where a reuse chain starts, so a resume can re-search from there.

Acceptance criteria

  • A test: on at least 200 seeded decisions from mirror games, the exact stop chooses the same candidate as the full budget, at 1k and 10k iterations.
  • A test: subtree reuse is not used across another player's decision or a zone change.
  • Measured and reported in the commit body: the fraction of rollouts saved by each change at 3k and 30k over whole games against the expert.
  • The bench-count pins move only where the change explains it.
  • A test: an Mcts rebuilt from its seed mid-game chooses as the uninterrupted one at the next decision, including inside a reuse chain re-searched from its start.

Design as built (executor):

  • Exact stop: each tree grows its share in 16 rounds (slice = share/16); between rounds the threads meet at a Barrier and read one board of root visit counts, so all reach the same verdict. Stop when for every other candidate j: lead >= iterations left across trees (+1 if j precedes the leader, the first-of-equals rule). A panicking tree still meets the barriers (catch_unwind) so the others end instead of hanging.
  • The stop is exact per decision given the trees it starts from. Inside a reuse chain a stopped tree hands fewer visits on, so a chain's later moves are those of the stopped trees, not of an unstopped run with reuse. Still deterministic.
  • Auto-pass in Mcts::choose only; tree nodes still branch there (unchanged search).
  • Per-decision seeds: decision i's trees are seeded from the i-th output of SplitMix64(agent seed), i counting every choose call (unsearched included). Mcts::resumed(.., decision) rebuilds; Mcts::resume_point() says where a rebuilt agent must start (the chain start while trees are kept). Decision 0 seeds as before.
  • Reuse gate: (1) consecutive in the game: new Observation::decisions() (decisions offered this turn, an accessor on the engine's budget counter) with the turn number must be exactly one more; (2) no slot's incarnation changed (CR 400.7) except the card the choice names (the spell cast, land played, source activated, object picked), and the viewer's knowledge of every slot unchanged; (3) every tree's replay of the chosen child reaches a decision equal to the real one, with the same incarnations and knowledge per slot. All trees or none. 'No card changing zones' read as: none but the chosen card, else the cast-to-stack move (CR 601.2a) would rule out the main case.
  • Mutation checks: removing (2) fails subtree_reuse_stays_within_one_action; removing (1) is not caught in sampled games (smart or exact stops), since (3) already refuses those cases; (1) stays as a guard.
  • Search record (Mcts::last_search): decision, chain, rollouts, inherited root visits, root visits and summed rewards per candidate (D7 root stats).
  • The 1k/10k acceptance test is #[ignore]d (minutes in release); a 48-iteration version runs in the suite.
