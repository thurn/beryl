Reproduced (live game, just ui-live, staging d300d738): dragged a hand card up and released it back over the hand, sampling each frame. While it glided home the node carried data-raised and the visible raised copy stood about 90 px above the node, upright, with --raise-turn from the card's resting slot (-10.6, then -8.6 deg) while the node's own rotation went 0 to 8.6 deg. About 330 ms in, the hover ended and the raise vanished in one frame (copy at y 664 upright, node at y 730 turned 6.6 deg): that jump and twist is the kick. Cause: Pointer.release told hover of the card under the pointer at once, which is the card just let go; StageBoard raises a hovered hand card by its resting pose. The return path itself (tracks.ts home/glide, fly timing, linear lerp, no overshoot) is fine. Fix: Pointer keeps the let-go card as settling and tells no hover of it until ScriptStage#write sees it has no track; then it drops its stale #over for that card and the stage marks shown stale so the surface repoints. This also applies to taken drops that wait for their play. After the fix, the same drag shows settling=card for about 480 ms, no data-raised during the glide, a monotonic path into the slot, and then only the card actually under the pointer rises (checked with the home slot both left and right of the release point). Tests: ui/scripts/stage-pointer.test.mjs, three new cases (let go over itself, nothing to travel, taken drop waiting then going home); the first fails on staging. Arena: in Arena a card let go over the hand slides back flat into the fan, with no hover enlargement until it is there; not filmed side by side this session. No fixture or spec moment: this is pointer state, not a view the audits replay.
