# UI Conventions

## Stack and battlefield movement

Cards moving between the stack and battlefield travel directly and ease into
place. Triggers and tokens use the same quick flight. They start small at their
source and become visible as they move away.

## Paying with permanents

During a payment prompt such as Waterbend or Convoke, start with no payment
highlights. Hover over Auto Pay on desktop or long-press it on mobile to see
which permanents Beryl would use. Click or tap the button to accept. To pay
manually, click or tap each permanent you want to contribute. For a
three-creature Convoke, pick A, B, and D directly even if Auto Pay would use A,
B, and C. After each manual pick, recalculate the proposal for the remaining
cost and keep the player's picks. Let the player finish manually or accept Auto
Pay for the remainder, and stop taking picks once the cost is covered. Arena
documents the preview and acceptance gestures in its [May 23, 2019 patch notes].

## Private library looks

Lift the actual cards being examined. Show their faces throughout the trip into
the local player's chooser. From the other seat, keep the group face down and
modestly enlarged at screen right, with the rest of the deck on the table.
Return top-bound cards directly to the deck's top surface. Route bottom-bound
cards around the deck's edge and slide them under the packet.
