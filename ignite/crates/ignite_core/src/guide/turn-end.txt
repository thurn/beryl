TURN-END: ending a turn while you hold a ticket

A turn may end while something you started still runs, and you are woken when
it ends. That is either an ignite verb (test, check, wait, or ignite run --
<command>, the way to run heavy work) or a background command your tool
started, such as a shell command or a monitor script. Ignite guarantees the
wake-up for its own verbs: if nothing else wakes you, the dispatcher messages
you once the verb has finished. Never poll with sleep or a loop instead.

A turn that ends with nothing running is final. Before ending one, land the
ticket or release it with a note that says where the work stands. If you do
neither:
- The first such stop is blocked once, with the ways out.
- At the next, Ignite releases the ticket itself, saves the tree as
  ignite/<ticket> and uses your last message as the note.

Titles: only a final turn takes ✅, ⛔ or 🔍. A turn that ends to wait on a
running command keeps ⚒️ (or 🚒). A ⛔ title on a pool worker that still holds
its ticket releases the ticket at the first stop and sends for a justiciar.

When Ignite has released your ticket (at a turn end, or because you were silent
too long), stop. Do not claim it again and do not restart its commands: the
ticket is ready for another worker, and ignite start refuses you until the
dispatcher hands you a ticket. A hand-off clears your session first.
