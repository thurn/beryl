CLI-WORKER: working as a headless CLI worker

Your session ID starts with cli-. The daemon runs you as one process per turn
(claude -p or codex exec) and ends that process when your turn ends. Nobody
watches the turn live and nobody can answer you.

Rules, and why:
- Start no background shell commands, and no monitor scripts. Your process, and
  everything it started, ends with the turn, so a background command is killed
  before it finishes. Ignite blocks a turn end that leaves one running.
- Wait in the foreground. Run heavy work as ignite run -- <command> or ignite
  test <names>, then ignite wait <handle>. These run under the daemon, which
  outlives your turn, and ignite wait blocks until the operation finishes.
- Pass --class batch on multi-minute ignite run commands (such as fuzzing) so
  they do not starve builds, and never launch a second ignite run of a command
  already running.
- After an ignite check or ignite land failure, fix the failures and pass
  ignite test on every failing check (plus clippy for any crate you edited)
  before running ignite check or ignite land again.
- A turn may also end while an ignite verb of yours still runs. The daemon
  resumes you when the verb ends, with its result.
- Leave fleet settings to the operator, incident commander or justiciar.
- Never ask a person. Decide, act, and record the choice and why on the ticket.
  There is no interactive takeover. A person steers you with ignite tell, and
  the text arrives as the prompt of your next turn.
- End your turn only after ignite land or ignite release (ignite close for a
  ticket with nothing to land), or to wait on an ignite verb. Any other turn end
  is blocked once, with the ways out. A second one releases your ticket with
  your last message as the note and saves the tree as ignite/<ticket>.
- You have no session title. Skip every title rule: the dashboard shows your
  state from the daemon's records, and a title never releases your ticket.

If your turn was interrupted (the process crashed, the machine rebooted), the
daemon resumes you and says your last tool call may not have finished. Check the
tree and your last command before you repeat it.

When Ignite has released your ticket, stop. Do not claim it again and do not
restart its commands (see ignite guide turn-end).
