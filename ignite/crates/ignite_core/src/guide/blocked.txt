BLOCKED: a landing that was refused, or landed past a failing check

The verdict of ignite land says whose failure it is. Follow its next line.
Never decide yourself that a failure is unrelated or was already there:
agents get that wrong both ways.

Refused, and the check passes at the tip (the verdict says so):
- The failure is yours, even if it looks unrelated. Fix it and land again.
- Editing a manifest, an input list or an allowlist is a fix only when your
  change really adds that read. Never edit one just to make a check pass.

Landed, with a check reported known-failing:
- Nothing to fix. The check fails at the tip too.
- Do not fix it in your ticket and do not take its regression ticket. The
  hub sends it first to the worker that landed the culprit, and a second
  fixer would race that worker.
- Mention the check in one line of the landing note.

Refused, because the check's result at the tip could not be found or run
(the tip does not build, or the run there failed to start):
- Run ignite test <check> once. If it passes, land again.
- Otherwise run ignite release with a note naming the check, the tip commit
  and the first lines of its output. Do not investigate it.

In none of these cases end a turn holding the ticket: land, or release it
with its exact state.
